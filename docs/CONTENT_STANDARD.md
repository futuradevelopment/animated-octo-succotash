# Dictionary entries and diagnostic articles

The launch dictionary contains 11,786 source-attributed definitions. These are not 11,786 researched repair articles. Repeated generic preparation steps do not turn a definition into a useful diagnostic guide and have been removed from dictionary pages.

Dictionary pages provide the literal definition, source, publication scope and links to relevant authored guides when available. They use DefinedTerm structured data. Canonical URLs and publication status are retained.

Authored guides use Article structured data and a separate count. Before publication, each guide needs:

- A specific vehicle, engine and model-year boundary supported by reviewed manufacturer material.
- Concrete findings that change the diagnostic direction, including conditions that rule out a proposed repair.
- Clear separation of documented facts, historical scope and editorial questions.
- Traceable source revisions; a superseded application must not silently establish current model coverage.
- Appropriate limits on owner actions, without invented repair prices, probabilities, measurements or part recommendations.
- Substantive content that is not another article with only its title or code changed.

Automated checks resolve source/model relationships, inspect rendered content and reject identical diagnostic bodies even when titles or code strings change. They cannot certify technical correctness or completeness; source review is still required. The two initial authored guides demonstrate different diagnostic branches for Audi A4 CAEB and 2015 Porsche Macan Turbo P0299. No claim of 10,000 useful diagnostic articles is made.
