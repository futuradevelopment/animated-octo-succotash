# P0406: high-pressure EGR position feedback exceeds its upper range

The cited P0406 strategy checks high-pressure EGR SENT position feedback for a raw electrical value above 99% of the 5 V scale. The fault concerns signal validity, not a rule that the EGR valve may never approach full opening.

## Preserve the signal's communication context

The controller must be configured for a SENT position sensor, and no SENT communication-loss fault may be present. A high-range signal with healthy communication is a different problem from losing the sensor's messages altogether. That distinction should be resolved before drawing a mechanical conclusion from the reported position.

Monitoring runs outside cranking with relay supply above 11 V and the diagnostic system enabled. The filter uses 480 failed samples out of 600 on a 6.25 ms task, with Type A reporting after one trip.

## Verification and limits

A useful verification establishes that the received raw value is in range during an eligible monitored interval. A displayed valve angle alone is insufficient if its validity or source is unknown. Conversely, a normal mechanical movement does not independently prove the feedback circuit is healthy.

The threshold belongs to this specific monitor and does not supply a universal connector voltage for every P0406 application. Apply the exact vehicle's sensor and circuit procedure to locate the cause. This summary provides no evidence that a particular sensor, harness section or EGR assembly must be replaced.
