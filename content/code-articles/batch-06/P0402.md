# P0402: excessive high-pressure EGR detected through an air residual

P0402 is the excessive high-pressure EGR flow monitor in the cited model-based strategy. Its positive mean residual compares a modeled fresh-air quantity with measured fresh air after a threshold adjustment for operating conditions.

## Excess flow is an inference from several quantities

The calculation includes nominal cylinder flow and estimates of both EGR paths. The source associates the monitor with high-pressure EGR leakage, but a stored code does not independently prove a leaking valve. Checking whether the input estimates are valid is part of interpreting this model-derived conclusion.

The monitor averages 375 samples at 25 ms, compared with the different averaging window used by the insufficient-flow strategy. It requires stable speed, fueling and high-pressure EGR request, active air control and positive desired EGR rate.

## Eligibility changes a retest

The continuation gates operation by temperature, ambient pressure, fuel range and desired high-pressure EGR flow, with enabling conditions sustained for at least 0.80 second. Its combustion-mode support table enables the listed close-injection mode while disabling the other displayed modes, including regeneration.

A meaningful verification therefore needs an appropriate stable mode and normal residual, not simply a drive during an excluded aftertreatment event. Retain measured airflow, modeled flows and requested EGR context. The diagnostic table does not identify a failed component, establish a universal leakage quantity or prescribe a repair operation.
