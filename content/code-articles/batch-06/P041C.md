# P041C: low-range EGR temperature feedback depends on sensor type

P041C is an electrical low-range diagnosis for EGR temperature in this table. The dictionary calls the channel sensor B, while GM's row calls it EGR temperature sensor 1; those labels must be matched through vehicle-specific information before locating a component.

## Two supported sensing architectures

For an analog sensor, the monitor evaluates raw resistance below 10 ohms. For a digital thermocouple implementation, it evaluates a reported temperature below −72.80°C. These are alternative signal representations described in the strategy, not two quantities that a technician must measure on every vehicle.

A digital temperature beyond the low boundary is an electrical-validity finding, not proof that exhaust gas actually reached that temperature. The source says the digital approach can identify problems in the wiring between the module and probes.

## Conditions and verification

The check requires valid run/crank ignition status, run/crank active, no cranking and the diagnostic system enabled. The listed filter needs 19 failures among 25 samples, at 100 ms per sample, and uses Type B reporting over two trips.

First identify whether the vehicle provides resistance-based or module-reported data; then evaluate the matching quantity under enabled conditions. A normal reading from the wrong signal type is not useful confirmation. The page does not isolate a defective probe from its connections or prescribe a replacement procedure.
