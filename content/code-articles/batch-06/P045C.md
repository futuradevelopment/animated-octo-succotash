# P045C: low-pressure EGR short-to-ground indication during drive

P045C is the low-pressure EGR command short-to-ground diagnosis in the cited strategy. It uses excessive current through the H-bridge switches, reported by the hardware interface, rather than a standalone low-voltage comparison.

## The measured criterion

Current above 8 A while the H-bridge driver is on is the published threshold. The same current magnitude appears in the opposite short-to-power category, so magnitude alone cannot establish the direction of the electrical fault. The hardware status and circuit context must remain attached to the reading.

The test runs after cranking with relay supply above 11 V, enabled diagnostics and determinate hardware feedback. It requires 240 failed counts among 300 samples on a 12.5 ms task, with Type A reporting.

## What changes the next check

A driven-circuit overcurrent finding differs from an open-load result with the driver off. Establish which state produced the recorded fault before interpreting a resistance or current observation made under other conditions.

Verification should demonstrate normal hardware behavior during an eligible driven interval and use the proper electrical isolation procedure. This page does not distinguish a short in wiring from a fault within the actuator or electronics. It also does not provide a safe external-power test or authorize bypassing the driver's protection. The 8 A threshold applies to this documented implementation only.
