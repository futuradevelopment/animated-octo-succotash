# P0401: insufficient high-pressure EGR inferred from an air model

In this GM strategy, P0401 identifies insufficient high-pressure EGR through a model-based air-flow comparison. The controller compares estimated fresh-air mass with the mass-airflow sensor's measured fresh air, then evaluates a residual against a condition-dependent threshold.

## A flow model has several inputs

The estimate incorporates nominal cylinder flow and estimated high- and low-pressure EGR flows. The source discusses high-pressure EGR restriction, but the code is not a direct visual finding of a blocked valve. Validity of the model inputs matters before assigning the residual to one component.

The insufficient-flow criterion is a negative mean residual error, averaged over 200 samples at 25 ms. Engine speed, fuel request and EGR flow request must be sufficiently steady, with active closed-loop air control and desired EGR above zero.

## Why a drive may not rerun the test

The continuation requires valid flow estimates, a desired low-pressure EGR split below 0.62 and enable conditions sustained for at least 0.70 second. The combustion-mode table disables this monitor in numerous aftertreatment modes, including the listed regeneration modes.

For verification, establish an eligible stable operating period and review requested flow, measured fresh air and model validity together. A transient acceleration or regeneration episode cannot be assumed to exercise the monitor. The table supports a flow-diagnosis strategy, not a guaranteed valve-cleaning or replacement remedy.
