# P02D4: what cylinder 5 minimum offset learning actually measures

P02D4 concerns cylinder 5's learned injection-time adjustment, specifically a result below the minimum SQP authority limit. It should not be confused with a low injector control-circuit voltage.

## The pressure measurement supports a time calculation

SQP makes a small injection during diesel fuel cut-off and estimates its quantity from fuel-rail pressure drop. On each map update, the controller compares the learned time delta with its minimum calibration. The published unit is microseconds; no universal numeric limit appears in this table.

This measurement chain gives a useful diagnostic distinction. The stored time correction is the conclusion, while the pressure-drop observation is evidence used to reach it. The code alone does not isolate which physical element caused the conclusion.

## Completion depends on more than one good sample

The strategy stores saturation status for the SQP rail-pressure levels. A single saturated level can support P02D4, and all levels must be free of saturation for the described unset state. Review whether the affected region was actually relearned before calling a post-service result satisfactory.

The monitor also needs SQP authority diagnosis and injection management enabled; its timing follows the learning event rather than a fixed road-test duration. Type B, two-trip reporting is listed. The table supports interpretation of the monitor, while vehicle-specific professional procedures are still needed for any fuel-system test or repair.

## Source and applicability

2025 GM 25OBDG04B diagnostic parameter tables; the cited monitor only. Vehicle, engine and calibration applicability must be confirmed separately.
