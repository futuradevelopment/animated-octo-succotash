# P041D: high-range EGR temperature circuit evidence

The P041D table checks EGR temperature sensor 1 for an excessive raw value. The catalog's generic wording calls the channel sensor B, so the physical location cannot be inferred from the letter alone.

## Choose the correct raw quantity

GM documents an analog-resistance interpretation and a digital-thermocouple interpretation. The analog high boundary is greater than 20,000,000 ohms; the digital boundary is a reported temperature above 1,289.85°C. The strategy uses the representation appropriate to the installed sensor architecture.

This is an out-of-range electrical check. A high digital value does not independently establish a real exhaust overtemperature, just as the resistance criterion is not an exhaust-temperature specification. That distinction prevents a sensor-validity fault from being treated as confirmed thermal damage.

## Monitoring context

Run/crank must be active and its ignition status valid, the engine must be out of cranking and diagnostics enabled. The source lists 19 failed samples out of 25, sampled at 100 ms, with Type B, two-trip reporting.

Verification should establish an in-range signal from the correct channel during a valid monitoring period. Compare the sensor architecture and reporting path with the actual vehicle before choosing an electrical test. The summary allows sensor or connection-related integrity concerns but provides no component-isolation procedure, pinout or grounds to replace a probe solely from P041D.

## Source and applicability

2025 GM 25OBDG04B diagnostic parameter tables; the cited monitor only. Vehicle, engine and calibration applicability must be confirmed separately.
