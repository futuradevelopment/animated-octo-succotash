# P0404: high-pressure EGR position does not follow its setpoint

The documented P0404 monitor evaluates high-pressure EGR position tracking. A difference greater than 10% between requested and measured position is its failure criterion, intended to detect obstruction during valve movement.

## Evaluate a stable command, not an arbitrary snapshot

The continuation requires the setpoint rate to remain between −50 and 50% per second for at least 0.38 second. Closed-loop position control must be active, with no mechanical-stop soft approach or anti-sticking procedure underway. Those gates prevent deliberate special movements from being treated as ordinary tracking evidence.

The test also needs valid coolant and outside-air inputs, no active cold-start strategy, coolant at least 40°C or the cooling-system target reached, outside air at least −20°C and relay supply above 11 V.

## An electrical explanation remains possible

The filter is 960 failures among 1,200 samples on a 6.25 ms task. At 480 failures, it enables the P0403 open-circuit check. This links tracking difficulty with a separate check that can distinguish missing electrical loading from an assumed mechanical obstruction.

Verification should establish valid position feedback and agreement with a stable requested position under enabled conditions. The code does not by itself prove carbon buildup or justify cleaning or replacement. Review accompanying electrical evidence and the applicable service procedure before selecting a repair.
