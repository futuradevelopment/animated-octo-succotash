# P07B9: Park-button channel B low-input detection

P07B9 identifies the B channel of the Park-button input as reading low in the reviewed GM monitor. The A/B label belongs to the selector-switch diagnosis; it should not be used to infer that the mechanical parking lock itself has been tested.

## A qualified low reading

The original PDF shows the malfunction boundary as a measured switch B signal at or below 115.00. Twenty failing samples within 25 are required. This is a repeated low-range observation rather than a declaration that every momentary transition must produce a code.

The signal's unit is not printed. A raw value of 115 must not become an assumed 1.15-volt workshop threshold. Confirm the measurement scale in the vehicle's service information before comparing a meter or a converted scan-tool display with the monitor. The actual switch signal and an interpreted selector state are different pieces of evidence.

## Verification and limits

Calibration enablement is required, and the row lists Type B reporting over two trips. Unlike the adjacent switch A row, this page does not print a software-loop interval, so the sample count should not be converted to a claimed elapsed time.

A useful verification observes switch B's measured input and whether the monitor completes without a qualified low condition. Merely seeing the letter P on a display does not establish that electrical result. The source provides no part-selection or disassembly instruction. These facts are confined to the 2025 GM table until the installed shifter and calibration are confirmed.
