# P067F: cylinder 6 glow-plug output high-circuit fault

The cylinder 6 glow-plug circuit is the subject of P067F in the reviewed 2025 GM monitor. The controller checks its electrical integrity and recognizes an output connection toward a voltage source. A high-circuit code alone does not identify whether the defect lies in the plug, its connection or the control circuitry.

## The source defines two electrical cases

For load resistance greater than 0.65 ohm, the short-to-power criterion uses impedance below 0.5 ohm to a source within the normal or high operating-voltage range. When load resistance is between 0.2 and 0.65 ohm, the limit is below 0.14 ohm instead.

Those alternative limits explain why a generic “check for high resistance” interpretation is misleading. The monitored fault is a low-impedance route toward voltage, evaluated in relation to load resistance. A removed component's resistance is a different measurement.

## Conditions that affect verification

GM requires the engine to be running but not cranking, acceptable battery voltage, no glow-system supply fault, calibration enablement and an enabled diagnostic system. The counter needs 10 failing samples within 20, with a 100-millisecond task interval. The listed reporting class is Type B over two trips, accompanied by glow-lamp activation.

If supply faults remain active, absence of a fresh P067F may reflect an inhibited check. Confirm that the relevant conditions were satisfied before evaluating a repair. These facts are bounded to the published monitor; they do not establish every model using the code or provide a safe circuit-probing procedure.
