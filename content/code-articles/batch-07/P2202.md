# P2202: NOx sensor 1 reports below its usable concentration range

P2202 is labeled NOx sensor circuit low, bank 1 sensor 1. In the reviewed GM implementation, the monitored quantity is the raw engine-out NOx concentration, not a published external signal-wire voltage. The failure threshold is below −90 ppm.

## A negative out-of-range value is not low emissions evidence

The monitor checks whether the sensor's reported concentration has fallen outside its permitted lower range. It does not establish that combustion produced a physically negative NOx concentration or prove unusually effective emissions control. A raw out-of-range value is a sensor-data validity issue under this strategy.

The controller requires no current-control failure and no relevant electrical fault on sensor 1. CAN communication and frame validity must also be acceptable. Those prerequisites distinguish this concentration-range check from a separate electrical or communication diagnosis.

## Qualification and verification

The bus relay must be commanded on, relay voltage exceed 11 volts and sensor supply exceed 9.90 volts. Dewpoint must be reached, the applicable combustion-mode flag enabled and fuel-quantity request above the printed −1 mm³ boundary. Qualification uses 100 failures within 200 samples at 25 milliseconds each; reporting is Type B over two trips.

Verification should review the raw concentration after the sensor becomes eligible, together with its electrical and current-control status. A missing P2202 while another fault inhibits the check is inconclusive. The source does not identify a replacement component or establish universal sensor location. Confirm the exact vehicle and NOx system before applying these monitor-specific criteria.
