# P07B4: Park-button switch A high-signal qualification

The GM source for P07B4 monitors the Park pushbutton's switch A electrical signal. “Circuit high” describes that input's measured range. It does not mean that Park is necessarily engaged, nor does it establish a mechanical parking-lock defect.

## A signal value, not a published voltage

The table places the high boundary at 236.00 or greater. No unit is printed for the number. It should remain a controller-signal threshold rather than being converted into an invented voltage specification. Before comparing scan data with it, establish how the diagnostic tool represents this input.

The failure counter needs 20 high samples out of 25, with the software operating on a 25-millisecond loop. The listed reporting policy is Type B across two trips. This sample requirement distinguishes a qualified high condition from an isolated excursion that may be visible in a trace.

## Separating input and engagement evidence

A displayed selector request and the actual switch A electrical measurement answer different questions. The former can show a requested mode; P07B4's documented evidence concerns the latter. Preserve both if available, and verify calibration enablement before treating a no-code observation as a successful test.

After the underlying issue is addressed, confirmation should exercise the Park-button input and establish that the high-range counter no longer qualifies under the applicable diagnostic. The source does not specify a connector test or justify replacing the complete shifter. The explanation is limited to this 2025 GM parameter-file row, with exact vehicle coverage still requiring service confirmation.
