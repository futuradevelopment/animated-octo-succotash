# P2085: rapid raw-temperature changes from EGT sensor 2

P2085 is labeled exhaust-temperature sensor 2 circuit intermittent. GM's reviewed strategy gives that label a specific meaning: the raw temperature signal changes too quickly for the expected sensor behavior. This is separate from an out-of-range high/low result or a lost-communication event.

## What qualifies as excessive change

The table uses an absolute raw-temperature difference above 100°C, accumulated as 12 failures within 25 samples. The task runs every 100 milliseconds. These are change-and-persistence criteria, not an absolute exhaust overtemperature limit.

The enable window places unfiltered temperature at least at 140°C, with the continuation giving the upper 1,070°C boundary. Relevant sensor electrical faults and communication loss must be absent. Sensor initialization must also be complete, with cranking excluded and valid Run/Crank and supply conditions.

## Interpreting the fault record

The source lists sensor internal malfunction, wiring deterioration and connector electrical issues as failure modes the quick-change test can detect. It does not identify which has occurred, and a smoothed scan display may hide the raw discontinuity being assessed.

Verification should therefore focus on the sensor 2 raw signal and monitor completion within its enabled temperature range. A stable reading immediately after a cold start may not exercise the check. The listed reporting is Type B over two trips. This summary supports no generic probe-replacement decision or location assumption; exact sensor mapping and electrical isolation must be established from the applicable vehicle service information.
