# P2054: DEF valve 2 low-side ground-short supervision

P2054 is the circuit-low code for bank 1, unit 2 of the reductant injection system. The GM row reviewed here specifically monitors a low-side short to ground on DEF metering valve 2. The evidence is supplied by the control hardware rather than inferred from catalyst behavior.

## Channel and fault direction both matter

The second-valve hardware interface must report its ground-short state as faulted. Its result must also be determinate. These requirements distinguish P2054 from an open circuit, a short toward battery supply and an unavailable hardware diagnosis, even though all can concern the same dosing valve.

The table gives no fixed terminal voltage or resistance to reproduce with a meter. Applying generic limits would add a test that the source does not support. It also gives no basis for assuming that the valve itself, rather than another part of its circuit, must be replaced.

## An eligible confirmation

The monitor requires calibration enablement, battery voltage above 11 volts and key-on or engine-running operation, with cranking excluded. Thirty failures in 60 samples qualify the condition on a 100-millisecond time base. The listed reporting is Type A, one trip.

Verification should establish a valid, nonfaulted ground-short diagnosis for valve 2 after those conditions are satisfied. Pump-pressure recovery or a successful test of valve 1 cannot substitute for that channel-specific result. Component identification and electrical isolation must follow the vehicle's service information; this explanation covers only the 2025 GM monitor described in the source.
