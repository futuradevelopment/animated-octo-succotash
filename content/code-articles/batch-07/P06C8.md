# P06C8: cylinder 4 glow-plug incorrect versus an electrical short

P06C8 is the cylinder 4 glow-plug resistance rationality code in the GM source reviewed here. Unlike a circuit high or low diagnosis, it evaluates whether the powered plug's calculated resistance remains within the manufacturer's calibrated operating range.

## What “incorrect” establishes

The dictionary's “glow plug incorrect” wording should not be read as a confirmed parts-identification result. The documented evidence is a ratio of measured voltage to measured current outside the accepted resistance window. GM displays that window as 0.23 to 2.00; an explicit unit is absent beside the expression, so the page does not justify a universal bench-test limit.

The distinction from an electrical short is also visible in the enable rules. The individual glow-plug electrical fault must be absent, the glow system enabled and the plug commanded on for a calibrated time. Voltage-feedback circuitry must also be fault-free. Those are prerequisites, not additional conditions that themselves set P06C8.

## Interpreting a retest

The engine must run outside cranking with battery voltage in range and diagnostics active. The table calls for 15 failing samples in a 25-sample window at 100-millisecond updates and lists Type B, two-trip reporting.

Confirmation therefore requires more than reconnecting the plug and observing no immediate code. Establish that the powered rationality check became eligible and completed. The source does not establish a faulty part, a repair method, or universal cylinder-location information. Exact vehicle and engine applicability require service information beyond this 2025 monitor summary.
