# P2293: regulator-controlled rail pressure exceeds a speed-dependent ceiling

P2293 is a regulator 2 performance code in the reviewed GM implementation. The monitor checks whether rail pressure exceeds the maximum allowed while the pressure regulator is governing the system. It is not simply a comparison with the requested pressure, nor does it use one ceiling at every engine speed.

## The ceiling is mapped

The supporting normal map ranges from 68 to 268 MPa and varies with engine speed. It allows 268 MPa across its central speed region but lowers the ceiling toward the map's high-speed end. A separate extended-area map also applies when that calibration area is enabled.

A pressure value that appears acceptable at one engine speed may therefore exceed the limit elsewhere. Comparing a freeze-frame pressure with the map's largest number alone can miss the actual reason for qualification.

## Conditions and confirmation

Relay voltage must be at least 11 volts and pressure-regulator control must be active. The diagnostic qualifies through 121 failing samples within 242, or 121 continuous failures, at 6.25 milliseconds per sample. Reporting is Type A over one trip.

Verification should establish engine speed, selected control mode and the applicable normal or extended ceiling alongside measured rail pressure. An electrical pass on the solenoid does not independently establish hydraulic control. The source does not identify the cause of excessive pressure or a replacement part. High-pressure fuel-system testing requires the vehicle's professional service procedure; these 2025 GM limits must not be applied to an unidentified engine or calibration.
