# P0473: A high exhaust-pressure circuit signal is separate from real overpressure

P0473 is exhaust-pressure sensor A circuit high. In Ford’s diesel summary, it appears with P0472 beneath an **upstream turbine pressure signal-range heading**. The listed high electrical boundary is above 4.8 V, with a typical two-second monitoring duration.

## A high signal is not automatically high gas pressure

This electrical classification is separate from P0471’s stopped-engine pressure offset and P0474’s running model comparison. A diagnostic display may translate signal voltage into pressure, but an electrically invalid input cannot simply be treated as a trustworthy pressure measurement.

Useful evidence therefore preserves whether the fault is a circuit-range result or a plausible-range pressure disagreement. A later pressure value that appears normal does not demonstrate that an earlier intermittent high electrical excursion has been resolved.

## Confirm the exact sensor specification

The source’s main heading and DTC descriptions identify exhaust pressure, but two internal subheadings refer to reductant pressure. That apparent copy/paste inconsistency means the listed limit should be confirmed in the correct vehicle service procedure before using it as a physical test specification.

The summary does not provide connector pin identity, a universal sensor transfer function, or an automatic sensor replacement rule. It supplies a high-circuit classification and its surrounding monitor context. Determine the actual reporting sensor and applicable circuit method before attributing P0473 to genuine exhaust overpressure or selecting a component to replace.
