# P00F3: Humidity-sensor performance is checked through its temperature reading

P00F3 is humidity-sensor circuit performance. Ford’s diesel monitor uses the **temperature measurement associated with the humidity sensor**, comparing it with intake-air temperature. The listed failure is an absolute temperature difference greater than 50°C.

## This is not a humidity-percentage limit

The comparison concerns two temperatures, not a relative-humidity reading above or below a fixed percentage. A normal humidity value therefore does not directly demonstrate that the temperature plausibility check passed.

Ford lists one check per drive cycle with a typical ten-second duration. Its entry table places 30 kg accumulated airflow in the maximum column. This is a bounded monitoring opportunity; the source should not be rewritten as an instruction to wait until at least 30 kg of air has passed.

## Compare the right signals at the right time

Useful evidence pairs intake-air and humidity-sensor temperatures during the qualifying period. A temperature mismatch recorded after a different operating history may not represent the source’s stated test window.

The summary does not identify which of the two measurements is incorrect or provide a sensor-location map. It also does not authorize replacing the humidity sensor solely because its name appears in the code. The correct vehicle procedure is needed to assess signal validity and isolate the cause. P00F3’s diagnostic value here is the temperature relationship and timing context, rather than a guessed humidity fault or universal electrical threshold.

## Source and applicability

This explanation follows Ford’s 2023–2024 diesel OBD summary. Match the vehicle’s engine and calibration before applying these monitor values; a generic code alone does not establish fitment.
