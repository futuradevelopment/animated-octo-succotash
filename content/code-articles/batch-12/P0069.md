# P0069: MAP and barometric pressure are compared near engine-off conditions

P0069 is MAP/barometric-pressure correlation. Ford’s diesel monitor compares the two pressure signals during a **near-stationary engine condition**, rather than judging boost pressure under normal loaded operation.

## The correlation window is asymmetric

Ford lists the pressure difference outside a window bounded by −8 kPa and +4.5 kPa as the malfunction condition. The unequal limits should not be replaced with a symmetric tolerance or borrowed from another pressure-correlation monitor.

The check runs once per trip with a typical 1.5-second duration. Entry conditions include key-on, battery voltage of 9–16.25 V, engine speed between zero and 437.5 rpm, and a listed engine-off time of at least two seconds. MAP and BARO low/high circuit diagnostics must be satisfactory.

## Correlation does not identify which input is wrong

A disagreement between two signals does not, by itself, prove that the manifold sensor is defective. Both inputs and the conditions of comparison need to be evaluated using the applicable vehicle procedure.

Useful verification preserves the two pressure values together, their electrical validity, and the engine state when the test ran. A loaded boost reading cannot be substituted for this low-speed comparison. The summary also does not establish a universal sensor location or instruct replacing both sensors. It describes an implausible relationship that requires further source-specific diagnosis to determine which measurement or system condition is responsible.

## Source and applicability
