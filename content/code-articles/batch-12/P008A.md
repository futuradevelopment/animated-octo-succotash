# P008A: Continuous low supply pressure and repeated short drops are different triggers

P008A concerns the **low-pressure fuel supply**, even though it can appear alongside rail-pressure complaints. Ford’s diesel summary describes both a persistent low-pressure condition and a repeated-drop condition. The occurrence pattern therefore matters as much as the value seen during one workshop check.

## Two ways to meet the monitor

The documented pressure boundary is 51 psig. A code may be set when indicated low-side pressure remains below that level, or when it temporarily drops below the boundary five times within fifteen minutes. Ford gives a typical monitoring duration of 60 seconds for the listed check.

Its entry conditions also include battery voltage, fuel and coolant temperature, and an undeployed airbag state. Those prerequisites show why a pressure observation made under an unrelated operating condition is not necessarily equivalent to a completed controller test.

## Intermittent evidence needs its own timeline

Normal low-side pressure at the moment of inspection does not exclude the repeated-drop branch. Preserve event history and any available pressure-switch or supply-pressure information instead of assuming that the absence of a continuous shortage disproves P008A.

The original PDF places “10 liter” in the fuel-in-tank maximum column. That unusual entry must not be rewritten as a minimum-fuel instruction. Confirm the applicable requirement in service information before using it as a practical test condition.

The code does not, by itself, locate a restriction or prove a lift-pump defect. Keep the low-pressure detection separate from high-pressure rail diagnosis and use the correct vehicle procedure for component isolation.

## Source and applicability

This explanation follows Ford’s 2023–2024 diesel OBD summary. Match the vehicle’s engine and calibration before applying these monitor values; a generic code alone does not establish fitment.
