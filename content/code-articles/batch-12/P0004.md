# P0004: An energized fuel-volume valve exceeds its current limit

P0004 is commonly described as a fuel-volume regulator control circuit high fault. Ford’s diesel implementation supplies a more useful detail: the controller detects **excessive valve current while the valve is energized**. “High” here should not be assumed to mean a high fuel-pressure reading or an unspecified voltage at a connector.

## The electrical event

The stated malfunction threshold is current above 3.7 A. Ford contrasts that with a normal maximum operating current of 2.2 A in the described system. Monitoring is continuous, and the summary gives 0.3 second as the typical detection duration.

This powered-state test differs from P0001 and P0003, whose feedback checks occur with the valve unenergized. An off-state voltage measurement alone cannot reproduce the condition defined for P0004. Likewise, a valve that appears electrically quiet after shutdown has not demonstrated normal operation during the recorded load event.

## What should be verified

Useful evidence includes the exact code, its recurrence, and whether companion volume-control driver codes were recorded. Relate any available command and fault information to the energized condition before choosing a test from the correct vehicle procedure.

The monitor establishes an overcurrent observation, not a complete fault location. It does not say that every P0004 requires a valve, ECU, or fuel-pump replacement. The quoted current limits are calibration-specific monitor information, not instructions to energize the component on a bench. High-pressure fuel hardware and its control circuit require the appropriate service method before physical intervention.
