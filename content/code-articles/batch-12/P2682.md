# P2682: Coolant bypass control low means a reported short to ground

P2682 identifies engine coolant bypass valve A control circuit low. In Ford’s diesel summary, the power stage reports a **short-to-ground error in the actuator circuit**. The word low refers to that electrical classification, not to coolant temperature or coolant level.

## Keep the three circuit branches distinct

The monitor is continuous with a typical five-second duration. P2681 is the corresponding open-load report, while P2683 identifies short to power. A code reader may group all three as coolant-bypass faults, but the individual classifications direct different electrical questions.

Ford lists no separate sensor prerequisite for this check. A normal temperature reading therefore does not by itself demonstrate that the bypass-valve output circuit passed its short-to-ground assessment.

## What remains unknown from the code

The summary does not locate the short in the actuator, harness, connector, or control electronics. It also does not provide a universal voltage or resistance value for a manual test.

A useful investigation confirms the exact actuator and wiring for the actual engine, preserves companion circuit codes, and follows the appropriate service isolation procedure. Do not infer a failed thermostat or a particular valve position from P2682 alone. The source supports an electrical fault classification; cooling-system behavior and mechanical valve operation need their own evidence, especially before any intervention in a hot or pressurized system.

## Source and applicability

This explanation follows Ford’s 2023–2024 diesel OBD summary. Match the vehicle’s engine and calibration before applying these monitor values; a generic code alone does not establish fitment.
