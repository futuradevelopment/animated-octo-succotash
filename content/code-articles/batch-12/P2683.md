# P2683: Coolant bypass actuator high is a short-to-power classification

P2683 is engine coolant bypass valve A control circuit high. Ford’s diesel monitor sets the listed condition when the **power stage reports a short-to-power actuator error**. This is electrical evidence, not a direct statement that coolant temperature is too high or that the bypass valve is fully open.

## The driver distinguishes high, low, and open faults

Ford describes continuous monitoring with a typical five-second duration. The adjacent P2682 is short to ground, and P2681 is open load. Preserving those distinctions is more useful than reducing all three to a generic coolant-valve malfunction.

The source gives no independent sensor prerequisite and no numeric connector-voltage boundary. A workshop value should not be invented from the word high or borrowed from another actuator’s diagnostic table.

## Verification needs the actual circuit arrangement

Identify the controlled valve A in the correct engine’s wiring information, then relate the power-stage report to companion circuit faults and the commanded operation. The code alone does not determine whether the fault lies in wiring, the actuator, or output electronics.

A normal coolant-temperature trend is not a direct check of the short-to-power classification, and an abnormal trend does not identify its electrical cause. Ford’s summary does not prescribe a valve replacement or a relay bypass. The proper service procedure is needed for safe electrical isolation and any subsequent work on the coolant system.

## Source and applicability

This explanation follows Ford’s 2023–2024 diesel OBD summary. Match the vehicle’s engine and calibration before applying these monitor values; a generic code alone does not establish fitment.
