# P05A2: Grille shutter A open-circuit diagnosis includes timed coil checks

P05A2 identifies active grille shutter A control circuit/open. In Ford’s **3.0L-only** grille-shutter section, the actuator’s microcontroller detects a shorted or open motor coil. The title’s open-circuit wording therefore does not describe every internal diagnostic route listed in the source.

## Movement timing creates the test opportunities

The open-coil check occurs at the beginning of each movement and again 500 milliseconds after the movement ends. The short-coil check is continuous. Ford lists a ten-second monitoring duration for the group and initiation of a movement command as the entry condition.

These distinctions matter when interpreting an intermittent result. A static inspection with no movement command does not reproduce the beginning/end opportunities for the open-coil test, and those opportunities differ from continuous short detection.

## Preserve the internal fault detail

A useful diagnosis identifies shutter A, the commanded movement, and any available coil-fault classification reported by the actuator. A shutter that fails to reach an endstop may also have a separate position/performance issue; that observation should not replace the electrical evidence behind P05A2.

The summary does not specify an external resistance test or distinguish coil, wiring, and actuator-electronics causes. Nor does it authorize forcing the shutter blades through their travel. Use the applicable service procedure to isolate the circuit problem before selecting an actuator assembly, preserving the command timing that makes this monitor useful.
