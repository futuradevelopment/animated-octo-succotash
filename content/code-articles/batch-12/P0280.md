# P0280: Cylinder-seven injector high means a high-to-low-side short here

P0280 refers to cylinder seven’s injector A circuit high. Ford’s diesel summary clarifies the mechanism: the driver IC has detected a **short between the injector high side and low side**. That is a more precise starting point than treating “high” as excessive fuel delivery or guessing at a voltage reading.

## The monitor observes a commanded circuit

The listed check is continuous when injections are requested and has a typical duration of 0.3 second. A test taken only while the output is inactive cannot reproduce every condition present during the controller’s injection event.

P0279 is grouped under injector short-circuit detection without the same high-to-low-side wording. P0207 belongs to the open-circuit family. Retaining those distinctions helps keep the fault record aligned with the type of electrical test required by the correct service information.

## What remains to be established

The code identifies the seventh-cylinder channel and the controller’s short classification; it does not establish which physical component contains the fault. A failed injector, a wiring defect, or a driver diagnosis cannot be selected merely from the code title.

A useful follow-up preserves companion injector and driver codes, verifies the actual cylinder/channel mapping for the engine, and follows the applicable high-voltage injector-circuit procedure. A later smooth idle does not itself demonstrate that the intermittent energized short classification has been resolved. The summary does not authorize a bench-current test, give safe probe locations, or specify a replacement component.

## Source and applicability

This explanation follows Ford’s 2023–2024 diesel OBD summary. Match the vehicle’s engine and calibration before applying these monitor values; a generic code alone does not establish fitment.
