# P0100: The MAF circuit threshold is not published in this Ford summary

P0100 identifies the mass/volume airflow A circuit. Ford’s diesel summary lists a **hard-coded criterion that is not exposed in this software summary**. That is a real limit of the source: a precise P0100 voltage, frequency, or airflow threshold should not be invented from neighboring entries.

## The published monitor context is still useful

The check runs continuously with a typical 1.5-second duration. Entry conditions include key-on and battery voltage between 9 and 16.25 V. Ford lists no separate sensor prerequisite for this circuit check.

The same page gives signal-period boundaries for P0102 and P0103, with different typical durations. Those values belong to the low and high codes; they cannot be presented as the unpublished setting rule for P0100.

## Distinguish circuit evidence from airflow plausibility

The code’s monitored level is the air-meter circuit. Other Ford sections use airflow/model ratios and adaptation values for range/performance diagnostics. A physically plausible airflow reading does not necessarily demonstrate that the circuit check passed, and a circuit fault does not identify an intake restriction.

Useful verification preserves P0100’s status and companion low/high or performance codes, then uses the actual vehicle’s electrical service procedure. The source does not provide a connector pinout or decide between the meter, harness, supply, and controller. Its most important diagnostic distinction is the boundary of what is published, preventing borrowed adjacent-code limits from becoming false specifications.

## Source and applicability

This explanation follows Ford’s 2023–2024 diesel OBD summary. Match the vehicle’s engine and calibration before applying these monitor values; a generic code alone does not establish fitment.
