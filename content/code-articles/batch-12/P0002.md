# P0002: Fuel-volume regulator performance can mean a hot ECU driver

The broad P0002 label, fuel-volume regulator range/performance, can suggest a hydraulic flow problem. In Ford’s documented diesel monitor, however, the decisive observation is **temperature inside the ECM power-stage driver**. The listed failure threshold is above 170°C.

## A different kind of performance fault

This monitor is continuous, with a typical duration of 0.3 second. Ford lists no separate sensor prerequisite in this summary. The reported temperature belongs to the electronic power stage; it is not a fuel-temperature limit or permission to judge the valve by touching its housing.

The neighboring codes underline the distinction. P0001 and P0003 concern voltage feedback with the valve unenergized, whereas P0004 identifies an energized-current limit. None of those electrical observations should be substituted for the thermal criterion attached to P0002 here.

## How that changes the next check

The useful diagnostic question is whether the correct controller has reported its thermal protection condition and whether other volume-control driver faults accompany it. Record when the event occurred and the operating history available from the vehicle’s diagnostic system. A normal fuel-pressure snapshot after cooling cannot establish that the driver-temperature monitor was wrong.

Further isolation requires the vehicle-specific electrical and thermal diagnostic procedure. This summary does not distinguish an overloaded output from an internal control-unit problem, nor does it prescribe an ECU replacement. Treating P0002 as automatic proof of a restricted fuel valve would skip the very parameter Ford says triggered this implementation.

## Source and applicability

This explanation follows Ford’s 2023–2024 diesel OBD summary. Match the vehicle’s engine and calibration before applying these monitor values; a generic code alone does not establish fitment.
