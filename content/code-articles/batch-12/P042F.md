# P042F: EGR stuck closed uses actuator-position evidence rather than flow alone

P042F identifies EGR control A stuck closed. Ford’s 2024–2025 gasoline summary maps it alongside the detailed identifier P0403_73 in the **EGR control-valve electrical monitor**. The relevant evidence is observed actuator position compared with calculated position limits, not simply insufficient measured EGR flow.

## Control-position and flow checks answer different questions

Ford lists continuous monitoring during the EGR monitor and a typical four-second detection duration for the control-valve group. It requires satisfactory supporting intake, manifold-pressure, barometric, and EGR-related information.

The flow monitor described nearby uses separate insufficient/excessive-flow codes and a much longer listed duration. A flow deficit therefore should not be substituted for the actuator-position classification that P042F describes.

## A printed formula limits numerical advice

The source’s stuck-closed threshold sentence repeats actuator-position phrases and refers to an 11 mm calculated limit, while an adjacent position-transfer presentation includes its own inconsistent travel labeling. This article does not reconstruct that malformed expression into a universal travel or voltage test.

The supported diagnostic direction is to verify commanded-versus-observed actuator behavior using the actual vehicle’s EGR procedure, while preserving any position-sensor circuit faults. The code does not prove carbon buildup, a failed motor, or a particular replacement valve. Its exact numerical acceptance criteria need confirmation from applicable service information. The diesel document’s mentions of P042F are prerequisites for other checks and are not used here as primary setting evidence.
