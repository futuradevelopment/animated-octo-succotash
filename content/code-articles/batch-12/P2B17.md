# P2B17: Cylinder-four learning must be judged during the calibration function

P2B17 selects cylinder four in the injection-pulse offset learning-limit family. Ford’s monitor runs with the **zero-fuel calibration base function**, rather than treating every injection during ordinary driving as an interchangeable test event.

## The comparison that matters

The observed test-injection energizing time is compared with a target for the injector. A deviation at least 156 microseconds below target, or at least 254 microseconds above target, triggers the documented condition. Typical monitoring duration is 30 seconds.

The enabling window includes fuel temperature of 10–75°C, coolant above a stated 50°C minimum, system voltage of at least 10 V, and accelerator input no greater than 2%. The requested-versus-actual rail-pressure difference must also remain within the listed 50-bar limit. These details explain why a learning result cannot be evaluated using pulse data from an unrelated operating state.

## What to preserve in the diagnosis

Keep cylinder-four identity, adaptation status, and companion injector or pressure faults together. The summary lists satisfactory injector and other supporting inputs as prerequisites, so an untrustworthy control environment can affect interpretation of a learning complaint.

The underlying threshold is shared across the P2B1x cylinder codes; P2B17 is not evidence of a unique cylinder-four repair procedure. Ford also labels several conditions 3.0L-only, which prevents assuming universal applicability. The source does not identify a failed injector or provide a complete relearn method. Those conclusions require the correct vehicle and calibration information.

## Source and applicability

This explanation follows Ford’s 2023–2024 diesel OBD summary. Match the vehicle’s engine and calibration before applying these monitor values; a generic code alone does not establish fitment.
