# P062E: Bank-two injector-driver performance concerns the high-voltage supply

P062E identifies fuel-injector driver circuit performance on bank two. Ford’s diesel summary says the driver’s integrated-circuit logic has detected a **high-voltage supply failure**. This is broader than naming one cylinder’s injector and is not the same observation as an individual injector open or short circuit.

## Bank-level evidence versus cylinder evidence

The documented monitor runs continuously with a typical duration of 0.5 second. Ford assigns P062D to the corresponding bank-one driver classification. Separate P0201–P0208 and low/high injector-circuit codes identify individual cylinder channels under their own electrical checks.

That distinction changes how a group of codes should be interpreted. Several injector-channel complaints alongside a bank-level supply fault should be considered together; their coexistence does not establish that every injector independently failed. This is an interpretation of the shared diagnostic level, not an OEM replacement instruction.

## Preserve the system boundary

The page attributes detection to internal driver logic but does not state that the ECU itself is necessarily defective. Nor does it identify a physical bank location for every engine. Confirm engine layout and the applicable driver test information before mapping the code to hardware.

Useful verification separates high-voltage supply integrity from the individual output-circuit conditions recorded by companion codes. The source supplies no bench-energizing procedure or connector-level voltage test. Injector driver circuits may involve hazardous voltage; the appropriate service method is needed before any physical testing. P062E alone cannot justify replacing a bank of injectors or a control module.

## Source and applicability

This explanation follows Ford’s 2023–2024 diesel OBD summary. Match the vehicle’s engine and calibration before applying these monitor values; a generic code alone does not establish fitment.
