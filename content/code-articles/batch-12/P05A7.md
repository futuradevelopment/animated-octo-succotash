# P05A7: Grille shutter A reports its internal supply below the allowed level

P05A7 is active grille shutter A supply voltage circuit low. Ford’s **3.0L-only** grille-shutter section defines the failure as internal supply voltage measured by the actuator ASIC below 6.9 V. That internal quantity is distinct from the battery voltage used to enable monitoring.

## Battery and internal supply have different roles

The listed entry conditions include key-on, at least five seconds since engine start, and battery voltage between 9 and 16.26 V. The check is continuous and its detection duration is described as instantaneous.

Consequently, a battery reading within its allowed range does not by itself demonstrate that the actuator’s internal supply is above 6.9 V. Conversely, the internal threshold should not be presented as an acceptable vehicle-battery minimum.

## Identify the affected actuator before diagnosing the source

P05B6 is the corresponding shutter-B supply code. P05A7 selects shutter A, but the document does not establish a universal physical actuator location or connector arrangement.

Useful verification connects the reported internal-supply fault to the correct module and operating interval, while retaining related communication or movement faults. The source does not decide whether external supply wiring or internal electronics caused the low measurement. It also provides no safe internal probing procedure. Follow the vehicle’s service information to isolate the supply path rather than replacing a shutter solely from the low-voltage title.

## Source and applicability

This explanation follows Ford’s 2023–2024 diesel OBD summary. Match the vehicle’s engine and calibration before applying these monitor values; a generic code alone does not establish fitment.
