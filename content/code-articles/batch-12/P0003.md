# P0003: Low driver feedback with the fuel-volume valve switched off

P0003 is the low-circuit member of the fuel-volume regulator family. In Ford’s diesel summary, the low condition is assessed at the volume-control chip while the valve is **not energized**. It therefore describes a particular electrical state, not a direct finding that rail pressure or fuel quantity is too low.

## The threshold and the command state

Ford gives a feedback value below 2.8 V, with the normal secondary supply described as 5 V. The continuous check has a typical monitoring duration of 0.3 second. A diagnostic interpretation must keep the measured quantity and command state together: low feedback during this off-state test is different from checking operating current under load.

The adjacent P0001 monitor occupies a higher feedback band in the same unenergized state. P0004, by comparison, requires the valve to be energized and assesses current. This helps separate low feedback from open-circuit classification and excessive energized current.

## Interpreting the result usefully

Preserve the complete regulator fault set and identify whether the applicable controller supports this monitoring strategy. If rail-pressure data also shows a deviation, that is additional evidence to evaluate, not the definition of P0003 itself.

The summary does not identify a connector pin or distinguish every possible wiring, valve, supply, and driver failure. Vehicle-specific circuit testing is needed to locate the defect. Measuring an arbitrary terminal against the 2.8 V figure, or replacing a pump because the title contains “low,” would extend the document beyond what it establishes.
