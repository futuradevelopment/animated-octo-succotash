# P0910: Veloster gate-select motor control and source-label caution

P0910 identifies the gate-select actuator circuit in the 2012 Veloster dual-clutch transmission procedure. The select motor is part of the gear actuator and has a separate diagnostic path from the clutch motors and shift motor. Its control fault should not be translated directly into worn transmission gears.

## Select-motor evidence and connector identification

Hyundai enables this monitor when motor supply exceeds 9 V. The detecting-condition image lists driver integrated-circuit error information and feedback current below 1 A, with one occurrence required. The documented fallback holds the gear fixed. That is a fault-management response, not evidence that a particular internal gear has broken.

The scan-tool section compares select-motor voltage with behavior across operating states. A currently normal response leads toward intermittent connector trouble or an uncleared historical fault; an abnormal response leads into the harness checks. The diagnostic value lies in connecting the stored event with the actual motor-control response.

One instruction needs correction before physical testing: the power-check section says to disconnect the select motor, then names the clutch motor 1 harness connector for the measurement. The surrounding title, diagram and later signal checks concern the select motor. This article leaves that component-name conflict explicit and does not supply a guessed connector location or pinout.

The supported sequence establishes connector condition, power, signal and ground before a conditional known-good select-motor check. Qualified service must reconcile the connector wording with the correct vehicle diagram. Following repair, verification requires operation under the monitor's enable conditions and checking for recurrence. Directly energizing an unidentified actuator or treating the fixed-gear fallback as a successful repair would bypass the documented diagnostic distinction.
