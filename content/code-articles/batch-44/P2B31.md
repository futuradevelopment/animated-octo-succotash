# P2B31: fan 2 blocked-performance report on Jetta DJXA

Volkswagen's P2B31 entry concerns fan 2 performance or a stuck-off condition. The DJXA table describes an internal blocked check, with an error counter above three. It also requires fan duty above 13 percent for more than 90 seconds before the listed feedback conditions are relevant.

## The fan 2 diagnostic timing pattern

The coded ground-signal duration band is 4.7–5.3 seconds, with a delay band of 1.9–2.1 seconds. These are communication-pattern timings. They should not be described as a direct measurement of rotor stoppage lasting five seconds. Monitoring is continuous when applicable, and the table lists MIL illumination after two driving cycles.

A wording conflict remains in the source: the fan row says the ground signal is sent by the pump. The DTC title and referenced VX57 procedure identify the radiator-fan assembly, so exact vehicle information must resolve that editorial ambiguity. The article does not translate the error into a water-pump diagnosis.

The fan procedure checks electrical supply and the control path before its component-replacement branch. A blocked-performance report can therefore require distinguishing a genuine obstruction or assembly failure from a supply or control problem. The code alone does not establish which one occurred. Useful evidence includes the sustained command, actual fan behavior, related circuit faults and the reported status pattern.

Inspection around the fan requires professional precautions because it can operate unexpectedly. Do not hold a blade, insert an object or deliberately create a stall. Qualified service should correct the supported defect, then verify operation under conditions that satisfy the sustained-command requirement rather than relying on a brief idle check.
