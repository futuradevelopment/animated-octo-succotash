# P0916: Veloster shift-position low circuit and Hall-signal checks

P0916 is the gear-shift-position circuit-low entry in Hyundai's 2012 Veloster DCT procedure. The detecting-condition image describes voltage and error-information monitoring with ignition on. An out-of-range condition must persist for more than six seconds on three occasions. The source supplies no numerical low-voltage trigger for that detection rule.

## Hall feedback and the replacement-label conflict

Its diagnostic procedure is more specific than the generic circuit title. The scan-tool section examines shift-motor current, voltage and speed as operation changes. The harness section then distinguishes the Hall-sensor supply, three signal paths and Hall ground. Those feedback circuits are separate from simply confirming that the motor has battery power.

For the stated disconnected-connector test conditions, the manual lists approximately 5 V for Hall supply, approximately 3–6 V for the signal checks and approximately zero for ground. These are service-check references, not interchangeable DTC thresholds. They should not be used to invent a universal low-fault cutoff.

A genuine source inconsistency appears at the final branch: it proposes substituting a known-good shift motor, but then names the select motor as the replacement if the problem is corrected. Exact vehicle information must resolve that label before ordering or installing a part. The article remains marked for review for this reason.

Hyundai lists a fixed-gear fallback and possible signal-circuit, motor or TCM causes. Qualified diagnosis should identify the supported branch rather than assume one from the fallback. After repair, the relevant enable conditions and repeated-event logic need to be exercised, followed by a fault check. Powered actuator access and driving verification belong in controlled professional service.
