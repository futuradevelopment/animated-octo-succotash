# P039B: Jetta cylinder 1 pressure inferred from crankshaft motion

P039B is the cylinder 1 excessive-pressure entry in Volkswagen's 2020 DJXA engine table. Its measurement is relative angular crankshaft deceleration. The code therefore does not establish that a dedicated cylinder-pressure sensor has failed, and the published limits cannot be converted directly into a compression-gauge reading.

## Cylinder 1 thresholds and qualifying operation

For cylinder 1, the table gives nondynamic limits of greater than 16,000–20,700 s⁻², medium-dynamic limits of greater than 24,000–31,050 s⁻², and high-dynamic limits of greater than 32,000–41,400 s⁻². These are calibration ranges, not three universal thresholds. Dynamic classification uses engine-speed gradient and requested manifold-pressure gradient, with respective boundary ranges of 600–1,400 rpm/s and 63.28–430.31 kPa/s.

Evaluation spans 30 revolutions and requires load above 60 percent and speed below 6,000 rpm. The clutch must be closed, gear shifting and fuel cutoff inactive, and actual versus requested rail-pressure difference below 10 MPa. Tooth-angle adaptation and the permitted ignition-timing relationship are additional requirements. These filters matter when deciding whether a later drive actually exercised the monitor.

Volkswagen's diagnostic guidance includes fuel quality, plug fouling, mechanical compression and deposits that may raise compression. The referenced crankshaft-sensor procedure also distinguishes a valid speed signal from intermittent wiring or tooth-wheel problems. None of those possibilities is established by the code alone.

A useful service record preserves cylinder identity, operating conditions, fuel history and supporting ignition or crankshaft faults before clearing memory. Qualified diagnosis should resolve the supported cause and verify the original monitor. Deliberate high-load reproduction or improvised fuel draining is inappropriate.
