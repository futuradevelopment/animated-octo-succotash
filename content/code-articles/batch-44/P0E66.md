# P0E66: MX-30 charger relay fails to switch on

Mazda's P0E66 procedure describes a charger relay that remains off while the PCM commands it on. This gives the generic battery-charger control-circuit-low title a concrete command-versus-operation meaning. The source does not publish a voltage threshold or a time limit, and the fault does not directly measure the onboard charger's high-voltage output.

## Relay response and contradictory test wording

Diagnosis begins with the supply chain rather than immediate charger replacement. After saving snapshot data, the workshop checks main relay No.1, then the charger relay. The PCM connector and charger relay control circuit follow. Mazda lists an open circuit, a short to ground, and a short to supply among possible electrical causes, in addition to relay and PCM faults.

The detailed chart has wording errors that should be resolved in current service information: headings for short-to-ground and short-to-supply checks are followed by instructions saying to inspect for an open circuit. This article preserves the intended fault categories without converting those inconsistent lines into a pin-by-pin test.

A repair must be followed by a recurrence check. If the same pending P0E66 remains, the chart calls for CAN diagnosis and another pass through the diagnostic sequence. PCM replacement appears only after communication is normal and the fault recurs. Other DTCs are then reviewed.

A useful repair record identifies which relay or circuit actually failed and whether its correction restored the commanded response. Do not bridge the charger relay as a test or charging workaround. The applicable isolation and electrical-safety procedures remain necessary around this EV charging system.
