# P0D52: MX-30 charger DC output-current sensor performance

P0D52 names battery-charger output-current sensor range or performance. In Mazda's MX-30 electric manual, the onboard charger detects a characteristic malfunction of its internal DC current sensor. The stated fail-safe stops charging. The page does not define a numeric current limit, allowable error, comparison signal, or persistence timer.

## Current-sensor evidence inside the charger

The DC qualification matters. This monitor concerns the charger's internal DC current measurement, and should not be described as an AC supply-current threshold or an external battery-pack sensor test. It also does not establish that charging current was actually excessive; a characteristic fault concerns the reliability of the sensing function.

Mazda lists both charging equipment and the onboard charger as possible causes. The workshop saves the snapshot, clears the record, and performs a controlled comparison with different equipment. The source calls this quick charging, so the exact applicable setup must come from the full vehicle service information. Historical-only status after the comparison leaves the original equipment as a possible explanation.

If P0D52 remains current, CAN communication diagnosis and a repeated diagnostic sequence come before onboard-charger replacement. The replacement branch requires continuing recurrence with communication normal. A final scan addresses other DTCs.

Useful evidence includes the original charging circumstances, current-versus-historical status, and the outcome of the supervised comparison. This DTC page supplies no accessible sensor pin or separate sensor-replacement procedure. Opening the charger, bypassing its monitoring, or forcing charging to continue would exceed what the source supports and belongs outside owner-level diagnosis.
