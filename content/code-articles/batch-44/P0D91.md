# P0D91: MX-30 charging connector lock circuit

For the MX-30 electric vehicle, P0D91 identifies a circuit malfunction in the charging connector lock actuator system. The PCM detects the fault, but the page provides no numeric voltage limit or timer. The locking function concerns retention of the charging connector; it should not be confused with the separate exterior charge-lid lock.

## Connector lock and lid terminology

Mazda's diagnostic sequence first preserves the snapshot and examines the charge port-electrical supply unit connection. A connector or terminal defect is corrected before the actuator is judged. The next decision depends on the separate charge connector lock actuator inspection. A normal actuator directs the repair toward the charge port-electrical supply unit; an abnormal actuator directs it toward the charge port assembly.

There is a wording inconsistency in the source's possible-cause list: one subitem says charge-lid lock actuator even though the title, inspection, and replacement branch concern the charging connector lock. This article follows the explicit diagnostic branches while retaining that discrepancy for review. It does not treat the two locks as interchangeable.

After repair, the workshop checks whether the same pending fault returns. Persistent recurrence leads through CAN diagnosis and a repeated diagnostic sequence before a PCM replacement decision. The final check also addresses other DTCs.

Record whether the connector is retained correctly, whether the fault is current, and the actual actuator inspection result. Do not force out a locked charging plug or bypass the lock circuit. Charge-port assembly work falls under the manual's high-voltage service precautions and requires trained personnel with the correct procedure.
