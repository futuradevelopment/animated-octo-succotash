# P2D88: fan 1 module performance and coded short-circuit feedback

The DJXA table links P2D88 to fan 1 control-module performance and an internal short-circuit check. Its feedback signature is a ground-signal duration band of 2.7–3.3 seconds with a 1.9–2.1 second delay, evaluated with an error counter greater than three. This is distinct from the longer signal band assigned to fan 2 module performance.

## The fan 1 short-circuit feedback pattern

Volkswagen lists continuous monitoring, zero seconds of additional monitoring time, and MIL illumination after two driving cycles. The row supplies no universal resistance or motor-current limit. The diagnostic signal is therefore evidence of a module-reported condition, rather than a published bench test for the fan motor.

The row also describes the signal as sent by the pump despite naming a fan fault and referring to radiator fan VX57. That component wording is retained as a source-review issue. It must be checked against the correct vehicle diagram and current diagnostic text instead of silently changing the system being diagnosed.

The referenced fan procedure begins with suitable supply conditions and checks the power and control paths. A replacement decision depends on the outcome of those checks. External wiring or supply evidence should not be bypassed simply because the diagnostic title includes control module.

Service records should preserve which fan is implicated, the command and reported status, companion faults and any intermittent recurrence. Electrical tests and access around the fan belong with trained technicians; deliberate shorting or blade obstruction would create a new hazard and misleading evidence. Confirmation should show that the original fan 1 status report no longer returns during applicable operation.
