# P03B9: cylinder 4 pressure monitor and dynamic operating bands

Volkswagen's P03B9 entry assigns excessive-pressure monitoring to cylinder 4 of the DJXA engine. The table measures relative angular crankshaft deceleration, so the diagnostic title should not be read as proof of a faulty cylinder-pressure transducer. The source gives no pressure-sensor replacement instruction for this event.

## Cylinder 4 dynamic bands and confirmation

The cylinder 4 bands are greater than 15,500–22,600 s⁻² for nondynamic operation, greater than or equal to 23,250–33,900 s⁻² for medium-dynamic operation, and greater than 31,000–45,200 s⁻² for high-dynamic operation. The different inequalities and cylinder-specific values should be preserved. Speed-gradient and requested manifold-pressure-gradient conditions select the dynamic category; their listed boundaries vary by calibration.

The observation period is 30 revolutions. More than 60 percent engine load and less than 6,000 rpm are required, together with a closed clutch and no active gear change or fuel cutoff. Rail-pressure tracking, tooth-angle adaptation and ignition-timing conditions also gate evaluation. These requirements are why merely clearing the code and idling the engine cannot establish a completed repair.

The diagnostic guidance includes unsuitable or aged fuel, spark-plug fouling, mechanical compression and carbon deposits. The associated knock and crankshaft procedures assess the relevant signals and wiring before their conditional component-replacement branches. Those references support a structured investigation; they do not identify the failed part from P03B9 alone.

Record the cylinder 4 event, companion faults and operating conditions, then let qualified service investigate the supported branch. Avoid intentional high-load provocation. Repair confirmation requires the applicable monitor to run successfully under controlled conditions.
