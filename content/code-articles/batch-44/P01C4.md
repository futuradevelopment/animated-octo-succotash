# P01C4: DJXA fuel-pressure offset checked after thermal soak

P01C4 is a fuel-pressure offset plausibility check in Volkswagen's DJXA table. The negative-offset branch lists rail pressure below 0.10 MPa at engine start; the positive-offset branch lists pressure above 1.50 MPa. These are separate offset decisions, not a normal running-pressure target range.

## Thermal history and the two offset branches

The published logic uses shutdown and restart history. It includes coolant above 70°C at shutdown, or modeled coolant above 95°C, followed by start coolant no higher than 35°C and an engine-off interval of at least 25,000 seconds. Engine speed is zero for the start plausibility evaluation. Comparisons among coolant, intake-air and ambient temperatures establish whether the thermal state is credible.

The multi-page table also lists optional and uncalibrated alternatives. Its negative branch includes a summed lambda-adaptation and controller-deviation value below 0.70; the positive branch includes a value above 1.30. Those alternatives should not be presented as one universal list of simultaneous requirements. The row lists zero seconds of additional monitoring, once per driving cycle, and MIL illumination after two driving cycles.

The referenced G247 and N290 procedures distinguish pressure feedback from metering control and examine their electrical paths before replacement. A start offset can therefore call for checking whether the pressure signal is believable, not simply fitting a pump because pressure appears low or high.

Useful evidence includes shutdown temperature, elapsed off time, restart temperatures and the initial pressure trace. Qualified service should identify which calibrated branch applies and verify the signal under its actual enable conditions. High-pressure fuel-system opening is not an appropriate informal way to confirm this plausibility code.
