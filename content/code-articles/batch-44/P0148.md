# P0148: Jetta fuel-pressure rise missing during engine start

For the 2020 Jetta DJXA engine, P0148 means that the expected rail-pressure rise at engine start was not detected. The factory table describes a functional fuel-pump-control check. It does not identify the transfer pump, high-pressure metering valve or pressure sensor as the failed part by itself.

## Conditions for the monitored pressure rise

The monitor requires a detected low-pressure start, closed-loop rail-pressure control and a high-pressure start commanded on. Prestart rail pressure must be below 3.00 MPa. Coolant temperature must be above −36°C and no higher than 35°C. Volkswagen lists five seconds of monitoring, once per driving cycle, with MIL illumination after two driving cycles. These conditions distinguish the event from a pressure complaint that occurs only after a hot restart or sustained acceleration.

The diagnostic references cover fuel pressure and delivery quantity, the G247 pressure sensor, N290 metering valve, and GX1 delivery unit with J538 control. The delivery-unit procedure explains that the transfer pump can prime briefly when the door opens or ignition is switched on. Hearing that prime does not establish adequate pressure or delivery under the monitored start condition.

A sound investigation preserves start temperature, commanded state, pressure response and related electrical faults. Qualified testing then separates insufficient supply, a metering-control problem and inaccurate pressure information. The component procedures check the relevant power, control and wiring paths before conditional replacement.

High-pressure fuel must not be released as an informal diagnostic test. Professional service should establish the failed branch and verify that the specified start sequence produces the required pressure response. This table's limits apply to the documented engine calibration context, not every vehicle storing P0148.
