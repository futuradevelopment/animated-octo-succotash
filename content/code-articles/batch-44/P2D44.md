# P2D44: MX-30 compressor overcurrent has cooling-system causes

The MX-30 electric implementation of P2D44 detects current through the electric air-conditioning compressor above a specified value. Mazda does not publish that current limit or a detection timer here. Although the generic label says compressor motor performance, the diagnostic sequence recognizes that excessive load can originate outside the compressor.

## Compressor current and cooling-system context

Related high-voltage DTCs are checked first. The workshop then assesses fan control and the cooling fan motor. Reduced heat rejection from a condenser or obstructed air duct, restricted refrigeration piping, and excessive refrigerant charge are separate listed possibilities. These branches explain why a compressor-current code is not sufficient evidence of an internal motor defect.

The procedure also checks the two-way and three-way refrigerant valves and both electric expansion-valve locations: the battery side and the refrigeration-system side. Their separate service procedures determine the actual fault. Refrigerant handling and charge correction require suitable equipment and qualified personnel, not pressure release or an owner recharge attempt.

Only after those checks does the chart examine the high-voltage cable between junction box No.3 and the compressor for insulation or continuity defects. A current recurring P2D44 after the prerequisite work leads to the compressor replacement branch. That cable work requires the full EV isolation and verification procedure.

Preserve snapshot data and record the cooling, refrigerant, valve, and cable findings. Repair verification includes recurrence, CAN communication if the fault persists, and review of other DTCs. The useful conclusion is the condition that produced the overcurrent indication, rather than a compressor order based only on the code title.
