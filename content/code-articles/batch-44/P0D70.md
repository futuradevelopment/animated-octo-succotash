# P0D70: MX-30 compressor internal temperature indication

P0D70 is broadly named for an air-conditioning compressor control-module internal temperature-sensor circuit. In this MX-30 electric manual, Mazda describes high input from the compressor temperature sensor and an internal temperature above a specified value. The actual temperature threshold and detection duration are not given. A generic circuit label should therefore not be converted into an invented resistance value or a claim about an external thermistor.

## Compressor temperature and cooling interpretation

The chart lists the electric compressor and PCM among possible causes. It begins by preserving snapshot data, then asks whether P0D70 is a present malfunction. That status matters: an old record and a presently repeated abnormal temperature indication do not carry the same diagnostic weight.

For a current fault, the workshop checks recurrence after a controlled power-state cycle. Mazda specifies at least 30 seconds off and at least 10 seconds in READY before rescanning. A recurring current P0D70 leads to its electric-compressor replacement branch. These waiting periods belong to verification; they do not disclose how long the temperature must exceed its threshold to set the code.

The repair is followed by a pending-code check under the relevant recorded conditions. If the fault remains, the technician checks CAN communication and repeats the diagnostic sequence. Other stored faults are handled through their own procedures.

Useful service evidence includes the snapshot, present or historical status, and the recurrence result. This page does not establish refrigerant quantity, a cooling restriction, or an owner-serviceable sensor location. Work on the electric compressor involves high voltage and belongs to trained technicians using the complete applicable procedure.
