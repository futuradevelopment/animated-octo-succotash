# P26F7: Passat coolant-pump C live-status signal missing

Although P26F7 is generically named coolant-pump C supply-voltage circuit, the Passat CVCA2016 monitor description is a missing live signal from the electric coolant pump. The output-driver diagnostic is assessed over 60 seconds and MIL illumination is listed after two driving cycles. The table does not give a direct supply-voltage threshold.

## Missing live status and pump identification

This matters when interpreting a scan result. A missing live-status indication is not equivalent to a proven zero-volt supply, and it does not by itself identify an open ground, failed motor, or defective ECM. The source supports the missing-signal diagnosis while leaving the exact electrical cause to testing.

The row references heater support pump V488, but the detailed shared V467/V488 procedure names V467 in its connector and replacement steps. The correct component and circuit mapping must be checked against the vehicle's actual wiring and current repair information. This article preserves that ambiguity rather than publishing a potentially wrong test point.

The component procedure's broad order is supply verification, control-path evaluation, conditional assembly replacement, and recurrence testing. Those stages help distinguish an unavailable operating supply from a fault within the monitored component or its control path. The 60-second monitor interval should remain separate from any workshop test waiting period.

Record the missing status, actual supply findings, companion faults, and repair verification result. Do not bypass pump control or assume that a longer timer makes the fault insignificant. Qualified service should identify the correct pump and use the full electrical and cooling-system precautions before component access.
