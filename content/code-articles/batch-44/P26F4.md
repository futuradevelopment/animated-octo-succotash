# P26F4: Passat coolant-pump C open-control indication

P26F4 is an electrical open-control indication for coolant pump C in the Passat CVCA2016 table. Volkswagen evaluates the output driver's diagnostic signal and lists 480 milliseconds of monitoring, with MIL illumination after two driving cycles. The row supplies no numeric voltage or current threshold and no separate enabling condition.

## An open driver indication and pump identity

The source points to heater support pump V488, but the referenced section is jointly titled for V467 and V488 while its detailed test steps name V467. That component-name mismatch must be resolved against the exact vehicle wiring and current repair information before using the procedure. The article does not assume the two pumps share terminals or are interchangeable.

The general diagnostic structure checks a valid power supply and the control path before a pump replacement branch. This makes P26F4 evidence of an electrical-control problem, not proof that coolant is absent or that the impeller is mechanically blocked. Those are different faults and need their own evidence.

Useful records include the exact installed pump identity, output-driver fault status, supply and harness findings, and recurrence after correction. The shared component procedure's electrical checks and final ECM branch should be interpreted only after the naming ambiguity is resolved.

Do not bridge the pump supply or run it without coolant to reproduce the code. Cooling circuits can be hot and pressurized, and electrical tests require suitable equipment. Qualified service should establish the correct component, repair the verified defect, and confirm that the original DTC does not return under the applicable operating conditions.
