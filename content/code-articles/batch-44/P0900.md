# P0900: Veloster clutch 1 actuator's current-feedback fault

The 2012 Veloster DCT procedure associates P0900 with clutch 1 motor control. Clutch 1 operates the first, third and fifth gears; the separate second clutch handles second, fourth, sixth and reverse. That architecture explains why the detecting-condition table lists an even-gear fallback, without establishing that continued driving is appropriate.

## Current feedback and clutch 1 control

The monitor requires motor supply above 9 V. Its fault criteria list error information from the driver integrated circuit and feedback current below 1 A, with detection recorded as one occurrence. The general description also refers to output voltage outside the usable range. These are actuator-control observations, not a measured clutch-friction wear limit.

The scan-tool procedure evaluates clutch-motor voltage as operating conditions change. If the recorded behavior now matches the reference, the decision branch considers an intermittent connection or a previously repaired fault whose memory was not cleared. An abnormal response leads to harness diagnosis. Thus a normal present reading does not erase the significance of an intermittent stored event.

The electrical sequence checks connectors, power supply, signal and ground before considering a known-good clutch 1 motor. Replacement is conditional on that substitution correcting the problem. The procedure does not direct wholesale transmission replacement from P0900 alone.

Qualified service should retain the original fault and motor-control evidence, identify the correct clutch actuator and follow the applicable circuit branch. Powered clutch movement and controlled driving checks require professional precautions. After correction, Hyundai calls for operation within the DTC's enable conditions and a repeat fault check; clearing memory by itself is not repair verification.
