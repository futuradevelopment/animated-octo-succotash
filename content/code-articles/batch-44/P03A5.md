# P03A5: DJXA cylinder 2 excessive-pressure monitor limits

The important detail in the DJXA P03A5 entry is its cylinder 2 calibration. Volkswagen assesses relative angular crankshaft deceleration over 30 revolutions; this is an inference about combustion loading, not a direct pressure reading supplied in bar or psi.

## Cylinder 2 calibration and supporting checks

Cylinder 2's nondynamic threshold range is greater than 16,000–21,300 s⁻². The medium-dynamic range is greater than 24,000–31,950 s⁻², while high-dynamic operation uses greater than 32,000–42,600 s⁻². Using the neighboring cylinder 1 numbers would misstate this row. Engine-speed gradient and requested manifold-pressure gradient determine which dynamic category applies, and their boundaries are themselves calibration ranges.

The monitor is continuous when enabled and lists MIL illumination after two driving cycles. Required conditions include engine load above 60 percent, speed below 6,000 rpm, a closed clutch, no active gear shift and no fuel cutoff. Rail-pressure tracking, completed tooth-angle adaptation and ignition-timing checks further restrict evaluation. A code-free idle period does not demonstrate that these conditions were satisfied.

The factory guidance considers poor or aged fuel, fouled plugs and mechanical compression, including carbon deposits. Its knock-sensor procedure first considers fuel, ignition timing and loose engine components before following the electrical path. Its crankshaft-sensor procedure examines signal availability and, where indicated, wiring and the reference wheel.

Service should preserve the cylinder 2 event and compare those possibilities with actual evidence instead of replacing a presumed pressure sensor. Fuel-system opening, forced-load tests and invasive engine work belong with trained technicians. Confirmation should establish that the repaired vehicle completes the applicable monitor without recurrence.
