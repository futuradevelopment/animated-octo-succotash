# P0D67: MX-30 charger internal fault with overheat priority

P0D67 is the MX-30 electric onboard charger's internal-malfunction code. The monitor description does not identify a particular processor, memory location, or electrical threshold. Its stated protective action is to stop charging. An internal-fault title therefore supplies less component-level detail than it may initially appear to provide.

## Resolve charger overheating before the internal-fault branch

Mazda gives one companion fault explicit priority: P0D24, the charger's internal-temperature fault. If P0D24 is present, its procedure is followed first. This explains why the P0D67 cause list includes the cooling system as well as charging equipment and the onboard charger. An internal-performance indication should not be evaluated independently of a known thermal problem.

With that prerequisite addressed, the workshop preserves snapshot information, clears the record, and checks recurrence using different equipment. The manual calls the comparison quick charging; the complete applicable service information must determine the correct setup. A historical-only record after the comparison can support an event associated with the original equipment.

A current repeated P0D67 requires CAN diagnosis and another pass through the sequence. Only continuing recurrence with communication normal reaches the onboard-charger replacement branch. Other DTCs are checked at completion.

The useful repair evidence is whether overtemperature was present, what cooling or equipment issue was found, and whether the internal fault remained current after that issue was addressed. The source does not provide an internal electronics repair or an owner-accessible test point. Charger access and controlled charging verification require qualified personnel and the manufacturer's high-voltage safety procedure.
