# P2D87: fan 1 module overtemperature status on DJXA

P2D87 is the fan 1 control-module overtemperature entry in Volkswagen's DJXA engine table. Its fault criterion is a power-stage overtemperature function with an error counter greater than three. The document does not state a temperature in degrees for this code, so an exact thermal cutoff cannot be inferred from the title.

## A coded thermal report without a temperature cutoff

The feedback criterion uses a PWM short-to-ground diagnostic pulse in the 0.7–1.3 second band and a delay in the 1.9–2.1 second band. These are the source's coded signal timings. They are not a recommendation to ground the control wire, and they do not describe how long a technician should run an overheated fan.

The monitor is continuous, lists zero seconds of additional monitoring time and requests MIL illumination after two driving cycles. Its diagnostic reference is the radiator fan VX57 procedure. That procedure establishes the electrical prerequisites and assesses the power supply and control circuit before reaching a fan-replacement decision.

The distinction between a reported internal thermal condition and its cause is important. Actual cooling performance, commanded fan state, related electrical codes and recurrence provide context. P2D87 alone does not prove that coolant temperature exceeded a particular limit or that the engine thermostat failed. Those claims require their own evidence.

Qualified service should preserve the feedback and operating data, identify the installed fan arrangement and investigate the supported electrical or assembly branch. Fan blades can move without warning, and a hot cooling system can remain pressurized. Repair confirmation should establish normal fan operation and absence of the original overtemperature report under appropriate controlled conditions.
