# P01C8: DJXA pressure-control correction and a bank-label caveat

P01C8 is labeled fuel rail/system pressure too low, Bank 2, in the DJXA factory table. Its actual fault criterion is filtered fuel-pressure-controller output greater than 1.20 MPa for 7.5 seconds. That quantity is controller correction, so it must not be rewritten as measured rail pressure below 1.20 MPa.

## Controller output and the unresolved bank designation

The row requires more than five seconds after engine start and more than 1.5 seconds after fuel cutoff. Fuel cutoff and catalyst heating must be inactive, fuel-volume-regulator control active, and relative fuel mass within the listed 5.02–249.98 percent range. The monitor performs multiple checks and lists MIL illumination after two driving cycles.

The Bank 2 wording is an unresolved applicability issue in this DJXA document context. This source does not establish a second physical fuel rail or identify a Bank 2 component for the vehicle. A technician must reconcile the exact vehicle, software and current factory diagnostic information before choosing a component or wiring branch. The article therefore remains marked for source review.

The referenced N290 and G247 procedures provide useful distinctions between metering control and pressure feedback. Their electrical checks precede conditional replacement, but neither reference turns a large controller correction into proof that one named component is defective. Actual pressure, requested pressure, controller output and supporting faults need to be considered together.

Preserve the original scan-tool wording and operating data. Qualified service should resolve the bank designation, determine why the controller is demanding excessive correction, and confirm the repair with the applicable monitor. Opening high-pressure lines or replacing a supposed second rail without that identification would be unsupported.
