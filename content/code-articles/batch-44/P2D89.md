# P2D89: fan 1 supply-circuit diagnostic without a voltage limit

Although P2D89 is titled fan 1 control-module supply-voltage circuit, the DJXA table defines its detection through an internal-electronics check. The error counter must exceed three, and the listed ground-signal duration band is 3.7–4.3 seconds with a 1.9–2.1 second delay.

## Supply-circuit interpretation without an invented limit

No numeric supply-voltage threshold is published in this row. Nearby low- or high-circuit codes cannot supply a missing limit for P2D89. The timings describe coded diagnostic feedback, not a direct voltage measurement or a duration for a technician to short a terminal. The monitor is continuous and lists MIL illumination after two driving cycles.

The wording that the feedback comes from a pump conflicts with the fan title and VX57 diagnostic reference. That ambiguity remains explicitly flagged for review. Exact vehicle documentation should establish the correct assembly and circuit before any test or replacement decision.

The referenced radiator-fan procedure begins by checking electrical prerequisites, then the power supply and control path. This is particularly relevant to a supply-circuit title: reported internal electronics trouble does not eliminate the need to establish a reliable external feed and connection. Conversely, the code alone does not prove that the vehicle battery is defective.

Useful evidence includes the complete fan fault set, commanded operation, actual supply findings and the reported status sequence. Qualified service should perform the electrical investigation with suitable equipment and precautions against unexpected fan movement. A completed repair should restore correct operation and prevent recurrence under the applicable monitor conditions, rather than merely erase the stored report.
