# P0784: Passat DQ250 fourth gear remains engaged

P0784 in the Passat 2015 DQ-250 6F table concerns failure to disengage fourth gear during the monitored shift process. Volkswagen checks whether the fourth-gear fork remains beyond its learned synchronizing point plus 10 percent despite a disengagement command. The shift-fork valve 3 command must be at least 5 percent.

## Interpreting the fourth-gear release check

The enabling hydraulic configuration is specific: safety valve 2 is commanded at least 20 percent, multiplexer position is 1, requested main pressure exceeds 2 bar, and there is no main-pressure-loss condition. The monitoring period is 6,000 milliseconds, with MIL illumination after two driving cycles. These details distinguish the monitor from a general complaint of delayed fourth-to-fifth shifting.

Additional prerequisites include terminal 15 voltage above 4 volts, battery voltage above 9 volts, and engine speed above 600 rpm, each sustained for more than 500 milliseconds. A fault found while those conditions are not established may require separate diagnosis before this shift-response result can be evaluated.

The synchronizing point is established by basic adjustment. It is not an arbitrary physical fork location or an owner adjustment specification. A workshop should correlate the commanded valve state, fork feedback, learned reference, and pressure context rather than treating P0784 as proof of a failed transmission assembly.

The cited row supplies a monitor, not a complete mechanical repair decision. Use the applicable DQ250 troubleshooting and current service information to determine the cause. Do not force repeated shifts to recreate the fault, especially if engagement is unreliable. Preserve event data and companion DTCs so qualified service can distinguish control, feedback, and hydraulic contributions.
