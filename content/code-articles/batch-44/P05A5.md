# P05A5: Passat active-grille signal below its low threshold

Volkswagen's Passat CVCA P05A5 row describes a short-to-ground indication for the active grille shutter. Signal voltage falls below a threshold shown as 1.92–2.21 volts, with 450 milliseconds of monitoring and a two-driving-cycle MIL schedule. The range is the source's threshold band; it should not be simplified into a single universal trip voltage.

## Separating the control signal from shutter position

The referenced component is radiator shutter motor V544. Its normal function includes controlling airflow through the lower inlet to manage warm-up and aerodynamic resistance. The procedure describes the shutter staying closed during warm-up until coolant reaches its stated 80-degree-Celsius opening threshold, with later positioning based on speed and cooling demand. A closed shutter can therefore be normal; P05A5 adds electrical evidence beyond its visible position.

The component procedure first establishes proper battery supply at the motor and then checks the control connection to the ECM. It directs wiring and connector repairs when those checks fail. The motor replacement branch applies after the relevant supply and control-path checks pass, rather than from the code title alone.

The workshop should preserve the fault context and report the actual low-signal cause, such as a verified circuit or connection defect. Repair verification includes checking for recurrence and restoring readiness as required. Do not force the shutter mechanism or use a generic voltage threshold from a different vehicle. Fans and shutter mechanisms can move under control-system command, so inspection and electrical testing should follow the vehicle-specific service precautions.
