# P2531: MX-30 main-power signal disagrees with BCM status

P2531 is commonly titled ignition-switch run circuit low. The MX-30 electric implementation does not depend on assuming a conventional ignition switch: the PCM sees main power off at terminal AN while the body control module reports main power on over CAN. The reported on state may be READY off or READY on. The essential evidence is disagreement between a physical input and a network state.

## Comparing the power input with BCM information

Mazda first checks BCM faults. Those must be addressed before treating the PCM's comparison as an isolated power-feed defect. The next checks concern IG1 relay No.1 and its supply, followed by PCM connector condition. The source then identifies fuse F53, rated 15 A, and the PCM supply path as further diagnostic branches. These identifiers apply to this manual edition, not to every vehicle storing P2531.

The possible circuit faults include an open or grounded supply. The chart does not publish an input-voltage threshold or detection timer, so a generic low-voltage figure would be an invention. A blown fuse also needs its circuit context assessed; repeatedly replacing it without understanding the failure does not establish a repair.

After a documented defect is corrected, the workshop checks whether the same pending code returns under the recorded conditions. CAN diagnosis and a repeated sequence precede the PCM replacement branch. Other DTCs are reviewed at completion.

Preserve the snapshot, BCM status, relay findings, and the identity of any failed supply path. Do not bridge the relay or fuse. This fault concerns the consistency of power-state information and does not alone diagnose the high-voltage battery.
