# C0564: low supply voltage in Ford's hybrid brake-module monitoring

C0564 is the low-system-voltage member of Ford's hybrid ABS-module supply check. It concerns the voltage available to the control system, not a direct measurement of brake-fluid pressure or proof of an internal module failure. This distinction matters when several brake-related faults appear during the same event.

## Supply validity supports other diagnostics

Ford's combined low/high table identifies an acceptable battery-supply window of 9.0 to 16.9 volts with ignition on. The operation table lists continuous monitoring and a 100 millisecond monitoring duration. C0564 addresses the low side of that documented operating window; those values should not be generalized to every vehicle or every electrical circuit in the braking system.

Other monitor entries explicitly account for supply-voltage faults. For example, the ABS pump-motor circuit-high check requires that overvoltage and undervoltage codes be absent. A pump-related observation made while the module's supply is invalid therefore needs different interpretation from a result obtained with a valid supply.

## Look at the event before condemning a component

The useful evidence includes the voltage seen by the affected controller, when the event occurred and which accompanying tests were eligible to run. A satisfactory battery measurement taken later does not reconstruct the voltage at the brake module during the fault. Conversely, the code alone does not identify a battery, connection or module as the proven cause.

Ford's source describes monitor operation rather than a complete electrical repair procedure. Appropriate follow-up establishes the vehicle-specific supply and ground conditions and then confirms that the brake system's relevant checks can run normally. A code clear does not establish brake safety or substitute for the required professional system verification.
