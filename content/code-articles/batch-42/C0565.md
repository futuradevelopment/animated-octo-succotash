# C0565: Ford's brake control module reports a high-voltage condition

The high-voltage label in Ford's C0565 example refers to the brake control system's electrical supply. It is not evidence of high hydraulic brake pressure. Ford monitors the supply so that other electrical and actuator checks are interpreted within an acceptable operating range.

## Identify which voltage the code describes

The hybrid OBD summary pairs C0565 with the low-voltage code C0564 and gives a normal supply window of 9.0 to 16.9 volts. The check operates with ignition on, continuously, with a listed 100 millisecond monitoring duration. C0565 belongs to the upper boundary of that specific module-level check.

A supply reading from another module or a later stationary measurement is not automatically equivalent to the brake controller's observation at the event. The fault record and applicable electrical procedure are needed to determine whether the condition was shared across the vehicle or confined to the monitored supply path. The code itself does not resolve that question.

## High supply can change which results are usable

Ford's ABS pump-motor diagnostics include a prerequisite that neither supply overvoltage nor undervoltage be active for the circuit-high test. This makes C0565 relevant when evaluating associated pump reports: an unavailable or inhibited test should not be mistaken for a successful one.

Useful verification establishes an acceptable supply under the relevant operating conditions and confirms the subsequent availability and completion of the affected brake-system checks. It does not require deliberately producing an overvoltage condition. The summary supplies no blanket replacement recommendation, and clearing the voltage code alone cannot demonstrate that the braking system's operation or the underlying electrical condition has been fully verified.
