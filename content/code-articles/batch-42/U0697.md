# U0697: distinguish Ford's sensor-D communication loss from its circuit subtypes

Ford's sensor-D pressure monitoring uses several identifiers that can look similar on a shortened scan-tool display. U0697 is specifically the lost-communication category for the digital SENT particulate-filter pressure sensor D. It should remain distinct from the P2CEA circuit family and its analog subtypes.

## Retain the complete reported identifier

The summary lists U0697/U0697_00 alongside P2CEA/P2CEA_01 in the SENT table. The communication check operates continuously, has no preceding monitor sequence and uses a five-second monitoring duration. The table identifies the result but does not give a universal missing-pulse threshold or connector-level test.

Elsewhere, P2CEC, P2CED and P2CEE describe analog low, high and intermittent conditions. Their voltage and subtype information does not convert U0697 into an analog pressure-signal test. Establishing the actual sensor implementation is necessary before interpreting a measurement.

## Restore reliable information before judging filter behavior

Ford's sensor-D range/performance code is P2CEB. Its functional checks follow completed electrical checks and compare pressure with offset and exhaust-flow expectations. A communication loss can affect whether those later observations are usable, but it does not identify a physical blockage or prove that the filter substrate is defective.

The source does not map the letter D to a guaranteed location on every engine in the document. Diagnosis therefore needs the exact vehicle configuration, complete code and associated electrical findings. Verification should establish a reliable digital link and the required follow-on monitoring. A cleared memory or one later pressure value cannot show that the original loss of communication has been resolved, and neither observation supplies a reason to condemn a particular filter.
