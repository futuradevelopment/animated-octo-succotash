# P2630: Ford's NTK oxygen-sensor trim low code can include an open circuit

P2630 is the oxygen-sensor pumping-current trim circuit low code for bank 2 sensor 1. In Ford's published example, the relevant wire diagnostic applies to NTK UEGO sensors and the RL trim circuit. Ford explicitly includes an open circuit as well as a short to ground in the condition.

## Low does not restrict the fault to a grounded wire

The source represents the result as P2630/P2629_14. Its description of an open-or-ground condition means the word low should not be used to exclude an open connection. The controller's diagnostic classification is more specific than a general assumption about voltage direction.

Ford lists continuous monitoring with no preceding monitor sequence. The supporting condition is valid UEGO heater operation, with battery voltage between 11 and 16 volts. The UEGO interface ASIC reports the circuit fault, and the circuit failure must be present for ten seconds. Those qualifications belong to the electrical test, not to a direct measurement of exhaust mixture.

## Keep NTK and Bosch diagnostic strategies separate

The same pages discuss a different Bosch trim-circuit rationality check during deceleration fuel shutoff. Its operating sequence should not be imported into this NTK RL diagnosis. Similarly, the pump-current, reference and common wire paths listed nearby are distinct from the trim circuit.

The source does not supply a universal resistance target, terminal map or physical engine-side location for bank 2. The applicable vehicle procedure must establish those details. Verification confirms the correct sensor type, electrical integrity and qualified monitor result. A normal oxygen reading after the event or a conclusion that the engine merely ran lean does not explain the timed trim-circuit failure.
