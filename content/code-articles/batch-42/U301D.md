# U301D: Ford's charging wake-up state drops during precharge

U301D is a wake-up circuit performance code, but Ford's documented example is specific to the charging-precharge sequence. The battery energy control module detects a low charging-wake-up state at a point where other charger conditions indicate that the sequence has progressed toward contactor closure.

## Sequence position distinguishes the fault

Ford describes the event during charger precharge, with charger output already within the battery controller's charge-contactor closure threshold and before the controller has requested those contactors closed. In that state, a low CHRG_WKUP indication supports U301D.

The source does not provide a numeric closure threshold or a code-specific millisecond duration for this row. Those details should come from the applicable vehicle information rather than being borrowed from neighboring contactor-driver checks. The supported fact is the inconsistency between wake-up status and the recorded stage of the charging sequence.

## Do not treat every sleeping module as a fault

Controllers can legitimately leave active states when a charging session completes or is cancelled. U301D's description is not a general claim that a module must remain awake at all times. The diagnostic record needs to establish whether the particular precharge condition was present when the wake-up state became low.

Related charging, communication and contactor findings can help describe that event, but the summary does not identify a single failed wire or module. Professional verification should follow the correct sequence and electrical procedure without bypassing wake-up control. Clearing U301D while the vehicle is not attempting the relevant charging stage does not test the original mismatch or demonstrate that normal precharge coordination has been restored.
