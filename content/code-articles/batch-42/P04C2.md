# P04C2: Ford's fuel-door unlock circuit held high

Ford associates P04C2 with a short to power in the fuel-fill-door unlock control circuit. In the documented hybrid arrangement, that condition leaves the door latched. The code concerns the commanded unlock path, rather than the fuel level or the amount of pressure remaining in the tank.

## Place the fault within the refueling sequence

The circuit monitor runs continuously with the engine off, following a refueling request, and uses a five-second observation period. A diagnosis should therefore establish whether the request reached the controller before evaluating the unlock output. Ford treats the cabin request switch as a different monitored component.

A high circuit result is also distinct from P04C1's possibilities. Ford describes an open circuit under that neighboring low-circuit code as another way the door can remain latched. The same visible inability to refuel consequently does not tell a technician whether the output is open or held at power.

## Preserve the distinction between access and pressure control

The summary says normal system behavior resumes once the door is opened in this particular fault example. That statement does not provide a universal workaround or prove that pressure was safely relieved on an individual vehicle. The sealed fuel system also depends on its refueling vapor valve and pressure information.

Useful evidence includes the unlock request, actual latch response and accompanying switch or vapor-valve faults. Connector assignments and approved door-release instructions require the exact model's service information. A repaired circuit should respond correctly to a normal refueling request; an already-open door or the absence of a newly stored code is weaker evidence because it may not exercise the previously failing unlock event.
