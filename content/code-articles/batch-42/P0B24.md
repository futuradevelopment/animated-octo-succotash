# P0B24: Ford detects a battery cell-voltage outlier relative to the average

The word unstable in P0B24 can suggest a total battery-voltage reading that jumps around. Ford's documented hybrid criterion is different: it compares an individual cell voltage with the average cell voltage. A persistent imbalance in that comparison can satisfy the monitor without an obviously unstable total-pack display.

## The cell-to-average difference is timed

Ford specifies an absolute difference greater than 0.5 volt for 15 seconds. Because the difference is absolute, a cell can be above or below the average. A diagnosis that looks only for the lowest displayed cell would therefore be narrower than the manufacturer's stated comparison.

The 15 second requirement also separates a sustained observation from a single transient sample. The fault record and the reliability of the cell measurements matter when assessing whether later readings reproduce the original condition.

## The monitor does not assign the physical cause

The BECM monitors individual voltages while managing the battery's ability to receive and provide power. P0B24 identifies a disagreement within that information, but the summary does not establish whether it comes from cell behavior, sensing or another condition. Related voltage-sense faults need to remain part of the assessment.

A plausible overall pack voltage is not sufficient proof that every cell-to-average comparison is healthy; the individual differences can be hidden by the total. Conversely, a stored P0B24 alone does not justify identifying a replacement cell from the code name.

Verification follows the exact battery's approved diagnostic procedure and evaluates valid cell information over the relevant interval. The source supplies no safe pack-disassembly instructions. Clearing the code or observing a briefly balanced set of values does not establish that the persistent deviation has been explained.
