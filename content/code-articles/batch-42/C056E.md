# C056E: Ford's brake-pedal angle sensor receives an excessive supply report

C056E is Ford's high-supply-voltage report for the brake-pedal position sensing system in the documented hybrid architecture. It concerns sensor power, rather than a driver pressing the pedal too far or a high hydraulic-pressure condition.

## The supply check has its own timing

The OBD summary pairs C056E with C056D and states that supply above 5.13 volts or below 4.87 volts for 50 milliseconds fails the combined check with ignition on. C056E is the high-side classification. A later reading within range does not exclude a short event that satisfied that timing earlier.

Ford's position-signal comparison is a separate monitor. Its entry conditions require a valid supply, and it compares the two sensor outputs after power-up. The supply code can therefore be important context when judging whether a position mismatch represents an independent sensing fault. The two tests should not be collapsed into one generic pedal-sensor diagnosis.

## Understand where the information is used

Pedal angle contributes to requested braking torque, which the hybrid brake system divides between regenerative and friction braking as operating conditions permit. A supply fault concerns the reliability of that input; it does not directly quantify the braking force available at the wheels.

Ford's PHEV gateway table maps this high-supply case from the brake module's C100F family into C056E for OBD reporting. Recording both the module and the available subtype preserves useful detail.

The applicable diagnostic procedure must establish the electrical cause and validate the brake system after correction. This monitor explanation supplies neither a safe-driving assurance nor a connector-level test. Clearing the report alone does not demonstrate that the sensor's power remains stable through the conditions that produced it.
