# P1380: Ford variable-cam-timing solenoid has an electrical circuit concern

P1380 in the 1999 Ford description concerns the variable-cam-timing solenoid circuit. The comprehensive component monitor evaluates the electrical path between the VCT system and PCM. The row’s diagnostic aid explicitly identifies this as a circuit check involving wiring, the solenoid coil and the controller.

## Keep electrical and cam-position diagnosis separate

The source lists an open or shorted VCT circuit, an open supply feed, an open or shorted solenoid valve and PCM damage. These alternatives concern the control path. P1380 does not by itself prove that cam timing is mechanically mis-set or that the actuator mechanism is binding.

The adjacent P1381 and P1383 descriptions address over-advanced or over-retarded position and direct attention to the VCT unit. That contrast is useful when several codes appear: their names share VCT, but the documented diagnostic branches are not identical.

## Limits of the abbreviated criterion

The monitor paragraph mentions watching high and low voltage, then specifies failure below a calibrated limit for a calibrated interval. No numerical threshold or timer is provided. This draft flags the incomplete directional detail rather than inventing a high-side criterion.

A technician should use the exact electrical procedure to establish whether supply, wiring, coil or controller behavior is responsible. Verification must demonstrate correct circuit operation through the applicable test conditions. A repaired timing mechanism or a cleared code alone would not show that the original electrical monitor has been satisfied.
