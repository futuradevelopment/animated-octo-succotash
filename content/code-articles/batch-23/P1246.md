# P1246: Ford generator-load interpretation depends on the charging-system variant

The Ford 1999 table contains two P1246 descriptions. One explicitly covers the 2.0L Contour/Mystique/Cougar arrangement; the other is labeled for the manual’s remaining applications. These are alternative system descriptions, not two tests to combine indiscriminately on one vehicle.

## Select the correct charging arrangement

The 2.0L row evaluates whether the generator-load input remains within a calibrated range and calls for checking charging operation and battery charge. The other row discusses the generator/regulator load signal, its dependence on regulator temperature, and the importance of the correct generator/regulator part number.

The second cause list also includes the generator drive mechanism as well as wiring and electronic faults. That widens the investigation beyond the load wire alone. Correct part identification matters because a physically installed generator is not automatically the specified signal-producing assembly.

## Do not turn ambiguous signal units into a universal target

The broader row describes frequency but expresses load examples as percentages. The abbreviated text does not clearly explain that encoding. This draft flags the uncertainty and avoids converting those numbers into an invented frequency, duty-cycle or load test.

A technician should identify the exact arrangement, battery condition, charging behavior and applicable signal specification. The source’s single voltage aid should also be used only in its original application context. Verification after a demonstrated repair requires proper charging and credible load information for that system, rather than a cleared P1246 or an alternator chosen only by physical fit.

## Source and applicability

This explanation is limited to the applicable systems in Ford’s US-market 1999 PCED. Confirm the vehicle and test application before using its limits. [Ford diagnostic descriptions: P1246 row](https://www.fordservicecontent.dealerconnection.com/pubs/content/~WVX2/~MUS~LEN/14/VX24002.HTM).
