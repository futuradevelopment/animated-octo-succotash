# P1538: Ford bank-two intake runners appear stuck open to the monitor

P1538 is the bank-two intake-manifold-runner stuck-open result in Ford’s 1999 table. The controller evaluates the monitor input against an expected range during the stated continuous and key-on self-tests. A signal that falls too low produces the described failure.

## Observe the affected bank during a controlled command

The diagnostic aid points to active-command or output-control testing and notes that a feedback value near 1 V at closed throttle may indicate concern. Those observations need the actual bank-two mechanism and the correct scan channel. Checking only the opposite bank would not resolve this code.

The numerical clue is approximate and specific to the historical system. It should not be treated as an exact universal dividing line for all intake-runner designs.

## Separate physical position from reported position

The source lists mechanical hardware problems, an IMRC circuit short, actuator damage and PCM damage. A low feedback signal can therefore require electrical investigation even when the title says “stuck open.” Conversely, an electrically plausible signal does not eliminate the need to verify the mechanism’s actual movement.

A technician should compare commanded movement, physical response and the appropriate feedback with the engine-specific procedure. Strong springs and powered levers require keeping hands clear. After a confirmed repair, repeat the relevant evaluation on bank two. The code alone does not prove that both banks need new actuators or that a complete manifold replacement is justified.
