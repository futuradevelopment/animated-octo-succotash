# P0836: Jeep diesel transfer-case position can fail four different checks

In the 2002 WG 2.7L diesel manual, P0836 covers four transfer-case-position subtypes: two plausibility checks, signal high and signal low. The generic four-wheel-drive switch-circuit title does not reveal which check failed, so retaining the complete scan description is particularly useful.

## Plausibility can involve vehicle speed

One subtype occurs when the ECM believes the transfer case is in low range but detects a vehicle speed inconsistent with that selection. Another occurs when the switch voltage does not fit any valid position range. Those observations differ from a simple high or low electrical input. A false low-range indication can create a plausibility complaint without proving a transfer-case gear has failed.

The documented high and low limits are above three volts and below 0.12 volt. The diagnostic procedure checks unwanted voltage, continuity, a short to ground and a short to sensor ground before assessing position-specific voltage behavior. Each selected range must produce its own valid signal, rather than merely changing the scan display somehow.

## Match the actual transfer case

The source's position table includes low range, Neutral and full-time four-wheel drive. It is not a universal voltage map for every Jeep selector. Using the wrong transfer-case table can make a sound sensor appear out of specification.

A qualified workshop should verify mechanical selection and reported position together, with the vehicle secured according to service information. The repair is complete when the correct position is recognized consistently and the relevant subtype no longer returns. These details apply only to this WG diesel source.
