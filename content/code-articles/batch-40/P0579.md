# P0579: Jeep diesel cruise-switch plausibility versus voltage faults

The 2002 WG 2.7L diesel manual gives P0579 three diagnostic descriptions: cruise-switch signal plausibility, voltage too high and voltage too low. Reading only the generic multifunction-input range/performance title loses this useful distinction. The complaint must be matched to the subtype reported by the ECM.

## Plausibility is not the same as a short circuit

The plausibility procedure examines excessive resistance in the switch-signal and switch-ground paths before reaching the switches or ECM. A signal can be electrically present yet fail to represent a valid switch request. That calls for a different investigation from a line held continuously at an electrical extreme.

The high-voltage branch considers an open signal, an open ground and a short to voltage, including the ON/OFF/SET switch path. The low-voltage branch distinguishes the ON/OFF/SET switch from the RESUME/ACCEL switch and checks shorts to chassis ground and sensor ground. Isolating one switch and observing recovery identifies a different fault section from an input that remains wrong with both switches excluded.

## A complete verification checks every command

The original procedure assesses all cruise-switch buttons, which matters because one functioning button does not validate the whole assembly. Any required operational verification belongs to a qualified workshop under appropriate conditions; this overview provides no road-test instructions.

Preserve the subtype, note whether the failure affects one command or several, and verify the repaired signal path after correction. The circuit arrangement and diagnostic divisions here are specific to the cited 2002 WG diesel system.
