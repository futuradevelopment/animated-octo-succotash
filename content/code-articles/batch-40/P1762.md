# P1762: the Jeep governor-pressure baseline is out of range

P1762 is the governor-pressure-sensor offset fault in the WJ/WG gas-powertrain manual. The monitor evaluates the pressure baseline in Park or Neutral within a stated sump-temperature window. This makes selector-state information important: the factory procedure specifically gives a park/neutral fault priority over the offset investigation.

## Three observations answer different questions

The diagnostic tree compares the sensor voltage reported by the scan tool with a direct electrical measurement. Agreement checks whether the controller's displayed input matches the signal reaching it. That does not yet establish that the signal accurately represents pressure.

A separate comparison uses an independent pressure gauge. If reported pressure agrees with a genuinely elevated gauge reading, the procedure considers internal leakage or governor-pressure-solenoid behavior. If the electronic report and gauge disagree, the sensor, its reference and its wiring remain in the investigation. These are distinct interpretations of the same code.

The manual also separates external wiring from wiring inside the transmission. A signal shorted to voltage or a reference shorted to ground can shift the baseline without proving a mechanical pressure fault. The source's set-condition sentence combines several timing expressions, so this overview does not turn that wording into a new simplified threshold.

## Scope and completion

The procedure is marked without DG4 and must be matched to the installed transmission. Professional diagnosis should establish which comparison failed, repair that cause and complete the prescribed verification. An offset code alone is insufficient evidence for either a valve-body replacement or a sensor replacement.
