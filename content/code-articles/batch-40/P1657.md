# P1657: Volkswagen A/C signal short and compressor interruption

Volkswagen's Golf Mk 4 distributor-pump diesel manual describes P1657 as an air-conditioning input/output short to positive. A catalog may label it more narrowly as a clutch-relay circuit, but the factory table used here names the A/C signal path and sends diagnosis to the compressor interruption function. That broader wording should guide source matching before a particular relay is condemned.

## Why cooling performance is not the whole check

The relevant test asks whether the engine-management system can interrupt compressor operation. The documented fault can affect acceleration with the air conditioner operating. An A/C system that still produces cold air therefore does not establish that this engine-management interface works correctly.

For the factory output check, the vehicle must be warmer than 15 °C, the A/C must be selected, and the temperature and blower settings must request maximum cooling. These conditions matter when interpreting a missing response: a compressor that was never enabled cannot demonstrate normal interruption.

## Interpret the commanded cycle

At the interruption stage, the manual expects the compressor to stop within five seconds and then cycle at approximately five-second intervals. It also notes that the scan routine displays this stage on vehicles without air conditioning. Merely seeing the menu item is consequently not evidence that the hardware is fitted.

The check belongs to a workshop procedure with moving engine equipment. If interruption fails under the specified conditions, the manual directs diagnosis to signals between the A/C and engine systems. Verification should demonstrate the requested interruption after the electrical fault is corrected. Refrigerant replacement or compressor replacement is not established by this code alone. Scope is the Golf diesel documentation introduced in May 1999.
