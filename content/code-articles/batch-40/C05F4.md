# C05F4: Ford rear driveshaft speed plausibility includes mechanical evidence

C05F4:00 is a rear-driveshaft-speed plausibility fault in the cited Ford Escape AWD procedure. It differs from the neighboring low, high and supply-circuit codes. The controller is questioning whether the speed information makes sense, and the source allows both electrical noise and mechanical driveline problems as explanations.

## A plausible voltage can still carry bad information

The Hall-effect speed sensor communicates rotational speed through changing frequency. A signal can exist without representing the driveline reliably. The manual specifically includes induced noise in the harness and faults involving the PTU, driveshaft or rear unit among the possible explanations for this performance complaint.

That does not prove that all those components need repair. It explains why a performance code needs broader evidence than a single static voltage measurement. The observed speed behavior, connection condition and physical sensor mounting matter.

## Follow the performance branch

In the factory flow, a presently active C05F4 goes to a different step from the three electrical-circuit codes. The performance step checks loose connections and whether the rear-unit speed sensor itself is loose before its conditional sensor decision. A mounting problem can therefore be relevant even when the circuit has continuity.

Verification belongs to a qualified workshop using the full applicable procedure. It should demonstrate credible speed information after the identified defect is corrected, not merely a temporarily empty fault memory. This article does not instruct an owner to reproduce the fault on the road.
