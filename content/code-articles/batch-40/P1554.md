# P1554: Volkswagen throttle adaptation prerequisites and interrupted learning

In Volkswagen's Polo Mk3 4LV documentation, P1554 means the conditions for throttle-unit basic setting were not met. It should not be translated directly into a failed throttle body. The associated adaptation procedure has explicit prerequisites that need to be satisfied before its result can be interpreted.

## Conditions are part of the test

The manual specifies at least 11.5 V battery voltage, coolant between 5°C and 95°C, the throttle at its idle position and no stored faults. It also requires the installed cruise-control system to function correctly. These conditions explain why repeating adaptation without understanding a refusal may accomplish little.

Learning occurs with the ignition on and the engine stopped. The controller records positions from both throttle-angle senders and checks the spring return to the emergency position. The process is therefore more than simply commanding the plate open and closed; it evaluates position feedback and return behavior as part of learning.

## Completion and storage are separate observations

Display group 60 reports successful adaptation. The procedure then requires an ignition-off period of at least 30 seconds to store the values. A workshop should distinguish a success indication from completion of the subsequent storage step.

An interrupted routine can be associated with low voltage, defective throttle equipment or wiring. The manual also notes automatic repetition at the next ignition-on after an interruption. That behavior can explain repeated attempts without proving that a new component is needed.

Verification should document the satisfied prerequisites, the final adaptation result and any remaining faults. Automatic-gearbox versions also have a separate gearbox-adaptation requirement in this source. The values and sequence above are specific to Polo 4LV documentation and must not be transferred to a different throttle system without its own instructions.
