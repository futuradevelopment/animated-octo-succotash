# P1683: the Wrangler speed-control power path needs diagnosis

P1683 is the speed-control power-supply circuit fault in the 2003 Wrangler TJ diagnostic manual. It is monitored with the ignition on and speed control switched on; the set condition is an open circuit or a short to ground in that supply. The manual groups its tests with P1595, but gives the two codes different monitor conditions and meanings.

## Why the shared test has several branches

The first workshop test evaluates the vacuum and vent solenoids separately. If only one fails to operate, its individual command path needs investigation. If both fail, the shared power path becomes especially informative. This is a diagnostic distinction, not proof that simultaneous nonoperation always means the same failed part.

The shared supply investigation follows the feed through the brake-lamp switch to the speed-control servo. The procedure distinguishes the servo-side brake-switch output wire from the controller-to-switch power wire, and includes the switch itself. Thus, the phrase power relay or driver circuit should not be read as an instruction to buy a relay without identifying the actual installed circuit.

If both solenoids respond during testing, the manual still considers servo ground and intermittent operation. A successful command today does not fully explain an earlier supply interruption.

## Scope and completion

A technician should document the failed supply, switch, ground or control section and verify speed-control cancellation as required by the service procedure. Brake-switch and cruise-control work warrants professional testing; the code is not an invitation to reproduce a fault at road speed. This explanation covers the 2003 Wrangler TJ source only.
