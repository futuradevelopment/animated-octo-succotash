# C05F0: Ford rear driveshaft speed circuit low: open or grounded

C05F0:00 is the rear driveshaft speed-sensor circuit-low fault in the cited Ford Escape AWD procedure. Ford lists an open circuit or a ground short as the electrical conditions behind this entry. Low describes the sensor circuit; it does not mean that the vehicle or driveshaft was simply turning slowly.

## The sensor carries speed as frequency

The rear drive unit's Hall-effect sensor supplies a signal whose frequency changes with driveshaft speed. When the electrical path fails, the controller loses a trustworthy basis for interpreting that speed. Treating the fault as a low-speed operating complaint misses the monitor's purpose.

The factory sequence first confirms whether the code is still present. A presently active circuit fault follows the electrical branch, while an absent fault is handled as a possible intermittent condition. This makes the current scan status more useful than a code copied from an earlier visit.

## Check support before condemning the sensor

The professional procedure distinguishes the sensor's supply from its return path. A missing supply directs diagnosis through its wiring and controller-source branch. A valid supply leads to checks of the other path before a sensor decision. Both parts must be understood to interpret a low circuit correctly.

For verification, record the demonstrated interruption or ground fault and confirm the speed information is credible again under the applicable service check. Do not infer a wheel-speed sensor failure from the rear-driveshaft label.
