# P1597: a Wrangler cruise-control switch input held low

For the 2003 Wrangler TJ, P1597 means the cruise-control switch input remains below 0.39 volt for two minutes. The check runs with the ignition on and battery voltage above 10.4 volts. It is a sustained switch-signal fault, not a finding that the vehicle failed to maintain its selected road speed.

## More than one component shares the input path

The factory diagnostic sequence distinguishes the ON/OFF switch, the RESUME/ACCEL switch, the clockspring and the signal wiring. It observes how the scan-tool voltage changes as those parts of the path are isolated. A recovery when one branch is removed carries different meaning from a voltage that stays low after all steering-wheel-side branches are excluded.

The wiring checks also separate a short to ground from a short to sensor ground. Both can hold the input down, while neither is repaired by replacing the speed-control servo. The present-reading test uses a broader low-voltage boundary than the code's time-qualified set condition; a technician should not confuse those two criteria.

## Safe next steps

Because the diagnostic path reaches steering-wheel switches and the clockspring, inspection and electrical isolation should follow the restraint-system precautions and vehicle-specific service information. This is not a reason to probe airbag connectors or to test cruise operation on the road.

Preserve the fault record, identify the branch pulling the switch signal low and complete the prescribed speed-control verification after repair. The thresholds above belong to the 2003 TJ manual and are not a general specification for later Jeep switch assemblies.
