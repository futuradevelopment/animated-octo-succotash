# P0769: An intermittent D-channel circuit result needs a different record from a failed shift

P0769 is the intermittent electrical entry for shift solenoid D in Ford's general monitor, represented more specifically as P0765_1F. The stored result may describe a condition that is absent during a later inspection, making event timing especially important.

## Do not substitute the functional event counter

Ford separates continuous electrical monitoring from functional checks made around solenoid transitions. Electrical feedback has a typical 1.5-second fault criterion in the general description. The functional filter, by contrast, counts bad responses and correct non-shifting states. The summary does not establish that filter as P0769's intermittency threshold.

The D-channel table also has separate open-circuit, ground-short and power-short categories. Preserve a detailed scanner suffix if available so an intermittent result is not automatically treated as a persistent short or break.

## Build a record of the actual interruption

Useful evidence connects D command state, electrical fault status and any accompanying supply or channel events. If a shift disturbance occurred simultaneously, its timing is valuable; it does not by itself locate an intermittent connection. A later valid circuit reading cannot reproduce an earlier event without comparable conditions.

The source does not specify a universal connector, resistance range or movement test for the D path. Those details require the exact transmission procedure. Verification should distinguish recurrence of the electrical classification from P0766 or P0767 functional failed-off/on behavior rather than counting every irregular shift as the same fault.
