# P0A15: Active mount A circuit low is distinct from the open and high categories

P0A15 is the low-circuit classification for active engine mount A in Ford's summary. It is paired with P0A14_11 and grouped under the source's bank 1 designation. Neither that bank label nor the letter A establishes a universal physical side of the vehicle.

## Follow the driver's classification

The documented strategy receives a fault report from the low-level driver and matures it in the PCM. Monitoring is continuous, and the operation table lists five seconds to register a malfunction. It does not publish a specific external voltage boundary for the reader to apply to an unknown connector.

Ford keeps the A open-circuit and high-circuit results separate as P0A14 and P0A16. A simplified description such as 'mount fault' loses the distinction that should guide the applicable electrical procedure.

## Keep electrical evidence separate from mount performance

Preserve the full code and suffix along with available command/status information. The low classification is not a direct measurement of how effectively the mount isolates vibration. A vibration observation may provide context, but it cannot identify the electrical path that the controller classified.

The source does not locate a defect in the load, wiring or driver and supplies no universal resistance or activation test. Verification requires the exact vehicle's circuit definition and component mapping. A mount replacement cannot be justified solely by treating an electrical low result as proof of mechanical deterioration.
