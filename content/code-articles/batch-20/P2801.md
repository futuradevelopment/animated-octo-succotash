# P2801: Range sensor B frequency must be checked separately from its position duty cycle

P2801 is assigned to transmission range sensor B's out-of-range frequency in Ford's cited PWM tables. A duty cycle that decodes to a plausible selector position does not establish that the signal's repetition rate meets the controller's requirement.

## Choose the architecture's frequency criterion

Ford's general range-sensor section gives a 100–175 Hz example window. The architecture tables instead describe a nominal 125 Hz with a 25 Hz allowance. These are documented variants; the exact transmission and calibration must determine the applicable limit rather than averaging or combining them.

The same tables distinguish duty-cycle extremes from frequency faults and separately evaluate whether A and B duty cycles add to the expected total. A correlation result and P2801's B-frequency classification therefore answer different questions.

## Preserve both channels without confusing their roles

A useful capture records B frequency, B duty cycle, the A channel and the selected range together. A decoded gear value alone may hide a timing fault. If B is currently normal, retain the circumstances of the stored event rather than treating the latest value as a reproduction.

The detailed representation is P2800_38. The source does not provide a universal B-sensor location or connector procedure. Verification needs the matched range-sensor design and its electrical information before the frequency result can justify any component replacement.
