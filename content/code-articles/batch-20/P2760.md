# P2760: Converter-clutch intermittency belongs to the electrical reporting path

P2760 is the intermittent torque-converter clutch circuit classification in Ford's table, represented as P0740_1F. It is separate from the functional stuck-on and stuck-off results, which evaluate whether the converter clutch follows the intended locking or release behavior.

## Keep command-feedback and slip evidence separate

Ford's electrical check compares output-driver feedback with the commanded driver state. The typical criterion is a mismatch for 1.5 seconds, while the operation table gives a broader electrical monitoring duration. The summary does not specify a unique P2760 dropout count that can be applied to every implementation.

Mechanical monitoring instead uses torque-converter slip and eligible command conditions. A normal slip value after the event cannot demonstrate that the electrical path remained uninterrupted throughout the earlier drive.

## Preserve the interruption's context

Retain the complete suffix, fault status, commanded clutch activity and the timing of any interruption. A shift or lockup disturbance occurring at the same time can be useful context, but it cannot locate the electrical defect by itself. A later static reading may miss the condition entirely.

The source does not provide a universal connector test or justify replacing the converter. Verification requires the appropriate electrical procedure for the specific transmission, while any functional failed-on or failed-off codes remain separate findings rather than substitutes for the intermittent classification.
