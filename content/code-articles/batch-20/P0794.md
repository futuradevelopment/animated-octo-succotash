# P0794: Intermediate-speed A intermittency needs more than an averaged speed trace

P0794 labels intermittent intermediate-shaft speed A circuitry in Ford's general monitor, with P0791_1F shown as the detailed representation. An averaged speed reading can obscure the interruption that made this failure classification relevant.

## Preserve the diagnostic category

Ford assigns separate representations to noise, dropout and plausibility within the P0791 family. Its example noise and dropout counts belong to those dynamic strategies, not a published P0794 intermittency counter. Applying them indiscriminately would change the meaning of the reported code.

The source also distinguishes electrical checks that can operate without vehicle movement from comparisons requiring believable speed and gear information. A smooth moving trace later in the drive does not prove the earlier electrical condition never occurred.

## Identify which intermediate sensor is present

The summary describes one intermediate sensor in the listed eight-speed design and two in the ten-speed design. The A designation therefore needs the exact transmission's component mapping before it becomes a physical location.

Useful evidence includes the complete suffix, status changes, command or gear state and the speed signal around the event. A coincident shift disturbance establishes timing, not the defective part. The summary does not provide an intermittency-specific universal pin test or recurrence threshold. Verification must follow the applicable electrical procedure while retaining the distinction from P0792's functional speed interpretations.
