# P0017: bank 1 sensor B correlation requires confirmed phaser park

P0017 checks whether bank 1 camshaft sensor B edges arrive at the expected crankshaft positions in this GM ECM table. The controller evaluates correlation with the phaser parked, rather than judging how quickly an actively moving phaser follows a command.

## The repeat-test delay has a purpose

The documented cam-edge limits are −10.6 to +11.3 crank degrees relative to nominal. A test fails when four of five samples fail, and two failed tests out of three confirm the diagnostic. If the first test fails, the next is delayed to verify the parked state; an additional delay applies to mid-park phasers.

The monitor needs synchronized cam/crank signals and a spinning engine. Relevant crank-sensor faults, P0365 and P0366 must be absent, and the exhaust-cam low-oil-pressure condition must be false. These are prerequisites, not additional P0017 diagnoses.

## Making the next check count

Retain the event’s synchronization and phaser-state information. An angular mismatch while park has not been confirmed should not be equated with the completed diagnostic. Likewise, a sensor-circuit fault can prevent this correlation assessment from being meaningful.

The code establishes an observed relationship error; it does not identify a specific timing component for replacement. Verification should allow the parked comparison and its repeat qualification to complete after the actual cause is addressed. The exact vehicle procedure is needed before mechanical timing work or application of these calibration-specific angle limits.

## Source and applicability

This explanation covers the documented 2025 GM monitor, not every vehicle using P0017. Confirm the vehicle’s module and calibration before applying its limits. [GM diagnostic table, PDF page 84](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=84).
