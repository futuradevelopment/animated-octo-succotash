# P0016: bank 1 sensor A cam-to-crank correlation at park

P0016 evaluates the relationship between bank 1 camshaft sensor A pulses and crankshaft position in this GM ECM. The monitor operates with the phaser indicated as parked, which makes it distinct from a variable-valve-timing command-tracking test.

## How the source qualifies misalignment

The shown edge limits are below −10.6 or above +11.3 crank degrees from the expected nominal cam position. The monitor evaluates four cam edges per engine cycle. A test fails at four failures in five samples; the diagnostic requires two failed tests out of three.

Crank and cam signals must be synchronized, the engine must be spinning, and relevant crank-sensor and P0340/P0341 faults must be absent. The listed intake-cam low-oil-pressure condition must also be false. After an initial failure, another test is delayed to confirm the phaser has parked; mid-park phasers have an additional delay.

## What this means diagnostically

A correlation fault is not automatic proof of a stretched chain or a defective position sensor. The controller is observing an unexpected angular relationship after its prerequisites are satisfied. Preserve the complete event, synchronization status and parked-state evidence before selecting the mechanical or electrical service path.

Verification must include the qualified parked comparison, not just a running cam-angle display. Applying these exact angle limits to an unidentified engine would be inappropriate; they belong to this documented calibration context and must be confirmed against the vehicle’s service information.

## Source and applicability

This explanation covers the documented 2025 GM monitor, not every vehicle using P0016. Confirm the vehicle’s module and calibration before applying its limits. [GM diagnostic table, PDF page 83](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=83).
