# C0595: brake-booster internal power driver fails supervision

C0595 is the brake-booster internal power-driver range/performance fault in this GM brake controller. The monitored hardware is the B6 bridge-driver ASIC and its operating state, rather than a direct measurement of brake-fluid leakage.

## Startup and running failures differ

At initialization, the controller checks whether the driver responds correctly to a microcontroller test. Another branch continuously checks whether the driver remains fault-free and in a valid operating mode, including whether a MOSFET short-circuit flag is set.

The table also watches for motor unavailability caused by driver reinitialization or a detected bridge-driver undervoltage condition. That branch has a 0.1-second detection interval. A code stored during a voltage disturbance may therefore contain different evidence from one stored during an initial internal self-test.

## What should guide the next step

Preserve the complete failure detail and the timing relative to power-up. Review supply history when the reported branch is undervoltage or reinitialization; do not jump from the phrase “internal power driver” directly to a module replacement. Conversely, a normal external supply at the time of inspection does not erase an earlier internal-test failure.

Verification must include the original check: a continuous operating-state pass does not demonstrate that the next initialization test will pass. The source describes diagnostic behavior but does not provide component-level repair instructions. Use vehicle-specific professional brake-booster service information for testing and any assembly decision.

## Source and applicability

This explanation covers the documented 2025 GM monitor, not every vehicle using C0595. Confirm the vehicle’s module and calibration before applying its limits. [GM diagnostic table, PDF page 5](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=5).
