# C0011: left-front outlet valve electrical fault

C0011 refers to the left-front outlet-control valve in the documented GM electronic brake controller. Although the name identifies a hydraulic-control function, the listed monitors evaluate electrical current, coil resistance and driver feedback rather than directly measuring wheel-brake pressure release.

## Read the continuation page

The code starts at the bottom of PDF page 25; most criteria are on page 26. The controller checks for overcurrent in the 4–6.5 A hardware band, driver overheating, ground-path problems and interruption of the coil path. A separate resistance comparison flags a calculated value outside 4.8–13.7 Ω.

The driver also compares currents before and after a switching point. A difference above 20% is one failure condition. Internal ADC, actuation logic and register checks provide additional ways to set C0011, even when a simple coil measurement appears reasonable.

## What this changes diagnostically

Do not equate the location label with proof of a mechanically sticking outlet valve. The exact failure detail is needed to decide whether the evidence points to the electrical load, its supply path or internal electronic supervision. Several periodic checks run every 20 seconds only outside valve control and without a hydraulic request; a successful commanded valve operation alone does not cover those tests. Verification should include the relevant controller self-test and its enabling conditions. Intrusive brake-system tests and hydraulic disassembly require the matched professional service procedure, which this emissions parameter table does not supply.

## Source and applicability

This explanation covers the documented 2025 GM monitor, not every vehicle using C0011. Confirm the vehicle’s module and calibration before applying its limits.
