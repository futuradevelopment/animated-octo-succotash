# P0090: high-pressure pump solenoid low-side open circuit

P0090 is implemented here as the high-pressure fuel-pump control-solenoid low-side open-circuit diagnostic. The GM controller checks output voltage while the driver is off, rather than measuring fuel-pressure regulation performance directly.

## Electrical evidence and enable conditions

The source relates its voltage thresholds to an open path of at least 200 kΩ between the signal and controller ground. That is a controller diagnostic specification; it is not a complete coil-resistance test or a value to apply at an unidentified connector.

The engine must turn at least 50 rpm and battery voltage must be at least 11 V. Monitoring is restricted around pump device-control and code-clear states. It uses 20 failing samples from 40, with 100 milliseconds per sample, and is classified Type A, one trip.

## Distinguish it from a pressure complaint

A P0090 event supports investigation of the commanded electrical path. It does not establish a worn pump or a rail-pressure deficit without additional evidence. Record engine speed, supply voltage and the full failure status, especially if the event occurred during starting or diagnostic actuator control.

Verification should include the enabled driver-off test after the cause is corrected. A successful pressure reading alone does not demonstrate that the open-circuit monitor has passed, and a stationary engine below the speed prerequisite may not run it. High-pressure fuel components must be handled according to professional vehicle-specific procedures; no line-opening test follows from this code.

## Source and applicability

This explanation covers the documented 2025 GM monitor, not every vehicle using P0090. Confirm the vehicle’s module and calibration before applying its limits. [GM diagnostic table, PDF page 105](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=105).
