# C001D: right-rear outlet valve and driver feedback

C001D identifies right-rear outlet control in this GM brake-module implementation. The published criteria describe electrical supervision of that valve and its driver. They do not establish that brake pressure at the right-rear wheel is trapped or that the valve must be replaced.

## Interruption, protection and self-checks

The controller can detect an interrupted coil path through open-load or undercurrent feedback. It separately watches overcurrent, output-stage temperature and power-ground integrity. A switching comparison flags a current deviation greater than 20%, while gate feedback can reveal an unexpected commanded state.

The resistance branch begins on page 32 and continues on page 33, with failure limits above 13.7 Ω or below 4.8 Ω. The continuation also contains internal driver logic and register checks. Missing that page would make the code appear narrower than the OEM definition.

## How to use the distinction

An undercurrent failure and an internal register failure are different evidence, even though both belong to C001D. Retrieve the complete fault status before choosing a component test. Then consider when the check ran: continuous protection uses a short 0.03-second detection interval, but cyclic self-tests operate every 20 seconds outside active valve control and without a hydraulic request. Verification must allow the relevant branch to execute. Use the exact vehicle’s professional brake procedure for supply, circuit or actuator testing; these monitor thresholds are not instructions for external power application.

## Source and applicability

This explanation covers the documented 2025 GM monitor, not every vehicle using C001D. Confirm the vehicle’s module and calibration before applying its limits.
