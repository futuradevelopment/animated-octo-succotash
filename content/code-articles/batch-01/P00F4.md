# P00F4: humidity value received over LIN is below range

P00F4 is the humidity-sensor circuit-low diagnostic for the GM LIN-MAF arrangement described here. The ECM flags a reported relative-humidity value at or below −6.25%, rather than directly testing an analog humidity-signal voltage.

## An impossible value is diagnostic evidence

The source explains that the sensing system converts capacitance into a relative-humidity percentage, which the MAF sends over LIN. A negative threshold is an out-of-range diagnostic boundary; it is not a statement about physically negative moisture in the air.

Monitoring requires established LIN communication, powertrain-relay voltage at least 11 V for 0.9 second and no active powertrain-relay fault. The diagnostic qualifies 40 failing readings out of 50 at 100-millisecond intervals. Thus a working communication connection is a prerequisite even though the measurement carried by it may be invalid.

## What changes the next step

Record the raw reported humidity and communication status together. A generic voltage-short explanation would omit this implementation’s reported-data check. Conversely, successful LIN communication alone does not validate the humidity value.

Use vehicle-specific diagnosis to determine why the reported measurement is erroneous before selecting a sensor or MAF assembly. Verification needs a plausible humidity value and completion of the enabled low-value monitor across its sample window. The code does not provide a physical sensor location, circuit pinout or a repair conclusion by itself; these details depend on the actual vehicle configuration.

## Source and applicability

This explanation covers the documented 2025 GM monitor, not every vehicle using P00F4. Confirm the vehicle’s module and calibration before applying its limits. [GM diagnostic table, PDF page 125](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=125).
