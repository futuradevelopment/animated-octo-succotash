# P0111: intake temperature 1 is the cold-soak outlier

P0111 evaluates intake-air temperature 1 against IAT2 and IAT3 in the GM humidity/manifold-temperature configuration described here. It detects an in-range temperature that disagrees with the other sensors after a long engine-off soak.

## The comparison uses all three readings

One branch requires IAT2 and IAT3 to agree within 25°C while IAT differs from each by more than 25°C. If the three do not agree, other branches determine the middle value and whether IAT is farther from it than the other remaining sensor. This is an outlier test rather than a simple absolute temperature limit.

It runs once at the beginning of an eligible ignition cycle after more than 28,800 seconds since the engine last ran. Relay voltage must be at least 11 V for 0.9 second, LIN communication with the MAF must be established, and specified temperature, relay and timer faults must be absent.

## Preserve startup evidence

Keep the original power-up temperatures together. A warm-engine comparison uses different thermal conditions and cannot reproduce the monitor’s equalization assumption. A plausible IAT value by itself also cannot show whether it agreed with both references.

Verification needs a qualifying soak and another enabled startup comparison after the cause is diagnosed. Clearing the code followed by a warm restart is insufficient. Confirm the vehicle’s actual sensor arrangement before applying this three-sensor strategy, and use the appropriate service procedure to identify the cause rather than replacing the named sensor automatically.

## Source and applicability

This explanation covers the documented 2025 GM monitor, not every vehicle using P0111. Confirm the vehicle’s module and calibration before applying its limits.
