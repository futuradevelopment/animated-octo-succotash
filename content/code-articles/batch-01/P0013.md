# P0013: bank 1 exhaust cam solenoid driver open circuit

P0013 identifies the bank 1 exhaust camshaft actuator-solenoid open-circuit monitor in this GM ECM. The controller supervises the solenoid’s high-side output driver while it is powered on.

## Why an inactive check can miss it

The failure evidence is a voltage measurement outside the controller’s acceptable on-state range. The published design specification corresponds to impedance above 200 kΩ between the signal and controller ground. The source does not give a universal connector pinout or a complete disconnected-solenoid resistance procedure.

The output must be commanded on, supply must exceed 11 V and ignition must be in crank or run. The diagnostic uses 20 failures among 25 samples at 250 milliseconds per sample. These conditions distinguish a completed circuit test from simply observing no code immediately after an ignition cycle.

## Separate electrical control from cam timing

A P0013 event concerns the power path to the exhaust cam oil-control valve. It is not direct proof of a camshaft position-sensor failure or a mechanical timing error. Preserve the output command and voltage context, then use the matched vehicle procedure to determine where the open-circuit behavior originates.

After the cause is verified and addressed, confirm that the commanded-on diagnostic completes under adequate supply voltage. A camshaft that appears to sit at a plausible resting angle does not prove this solenoid driver can deliver its commanded output. The table supports a circuit investigation, not a predetermined parts replacement.

## Source and applicability

This explanation covers the documented 2025 GM monitor, not every vehicle using P0013. Confirm the vehicle’s module and calibration before applying its limits. [GM diagnostic table, PDF page 81](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=81).
