# P0089: high-pressure fuel control has reached its authority limit

P0089 is the high-pressure pump minimum/maximum authority diagnostic in this GM direct-injection strategy. The generic fuel-pressure regulator performance label is implemented here by monitoring how far pump control must move its delivery command.

## Saturated control is the key observation

The table flags a high-pressure pump delivery angle at or above 134°, or at or below 0°. Failure must occupy ten seconds of a 12.5-second sample window. This is evidence that control has reached an end of its permitted range, not a direct statement that rail pressure is always too high or always too low.

The engine must not be cranking. Battery voltage must be at least 11 V and low-side fuel pressure above 0.275 MPa. The source also requires valid pressure and timing-related inputs, the low-side pump on, and absence of specified device-control or low-fuel conditions.

## What changes the investigation

Capture which delivery-angle limit was reached and the associated commanded and measured pressure context. Opposite control limits can lead to the same code, so treating every P0089 as one pressure direction loses important evidence. Review the validity of the upstream supply and sensor prerequisites before blaming the high-pressure pump.

Verification needs the enabled running monitor to complete without remaining at its authority limit. A cranking-only check is insufficient. This table is not a depressurization or high-pressure fuel test procedure; fuel-system access and component decisions require qualified service using the exact vehicle instructions.

## Source and applicability

This explanation covers the documented 2025 GM monitor, not every vehicle using P0089. Confirm the vehicle’s module and calibration before applying its limits.
