# P0091: fuel-pump control low-side short-to-ground monitoring

P0091 is the high-pressure fuel-pump control-solenoid low-side short-to-ground diagnostic in the referenced GM ECM. The controller evaluates voltage outside its expected range while the output driver is off.

## Why the output state matters

A ground-side control circuit is not diagnosed by one voltage reading taken without knowing its command. This entry specifically evaluates the off state. The summary’s ancillary current specification is controller-dependent, so it should not be converted into an invented universal resistance or terminal-voltage limit.

The check needs engine speed of at least 50 rpm, battery voltage at least 11 V and the appropriate device-control/code-clear state. Twenty failures among 40 samples, each 100 milliseconds apart, qualify the diagnostic. Those conditions mean a quick key-on observation with the engine stationary may not reproduce the original test.

## Use the fault as electrical evidence

Record the complete failure detail, output command and supply context before clearing. “Circuit low” is not a statement that fuel rail pressure is low. A technician needs the vehicle’s circuit procedure to determine the source of the abnormal ground-side behavior; the table does not identify one failed regulator or pump.

After the cause is verified and addressed, confirm successful completion of the same off-state monitor under valid conditions. A good fuel-pressure reading or an actuator command alone is not equivalent. High-pressure fuel-system testing and access require the prescribed professional procedures.

## Source and applicability

This explanation covers the documented 2025 GM monitor, not every vehicle using P0091. Confirm the vehicle’s module and calibration before applying its limits. [GM diagnostic table, PDF page 106](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=106).
