# P0092: fuel-pump solenoid low-side short to power

P0092 is the high-pressure pump control-solenoid low-side short-to-power monitor in this GM strategy. It evaluates the output driver while commanded on and detects voltage outside the controller’s acceptable on-state range.

## Circuit high does not mean high rail pressure

The failure describes electrical control behavior. It does not establish that actual fuel pressure exceeded a pressure limit. The source lists selectable current-related thresholds depending on pump configuration, so the table does not support one universal external current or resistance test.

Engine speed must be at least 50 rpm and battery voltage at least 11 V. The monitor also restricts pump device-control and code-clear states. It qualifies 20 failed samples out of 40 at 100-millisecond intervals and is classified Type A, one trip.

## Evidence to preserve

Capture the commanded-on state and the exact pump/controller application. An off-state measurement may be useful elsewhere in the service procedure but cannot establish that this on-state diagnostic passed. Likewise, pressure that looks normal at one moment does not erase the electrical failure evidence.

Use the matched circuit diagnosis to locate the cause before selecting an actuator, wiring repair or controller. Verification requires the enabled on-state check to complete without failure after correction. The source provides no permission to open a high-pressure fuel line or manually energize the pump solenoid; those operations require the vehicle-specific professional fuel-system procedure.

## Source and applicability

This explanation covers the documented 2025 GM monitor, not every vehicle using P0092. Confirm the vehicle’s module and calibration before applying its limits. [GM diagnostic table, PDF page 107](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=107).
