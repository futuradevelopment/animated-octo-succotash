# C0553: longitudinal acceleration circuit-low depends on sensor polarity

C0553 is the longitudinal-acceleration circuit-low diagnostic in this GM controller table. The strategy looks for a short to ground or an open condition by comparing the raw acceleration signal with limits appropriate to the configured sensor relationship.

## Sensor configuration changes the interpretation

For the explicitly described directly proportional sensor case, the low criterion is below −3.85 g. The document also includes an inversely proportional case. A generic voltage-to-acceleration assumption cannot safely be transferred between those configurations; confirm the sensor type before applying a numerical limit.

The monitor needs battery and run/crank voltage above 11 V, diagnostic enable and no active or current-key-on U0073 communication failure. It uses more than 30 seconds of stability, failure time above 75 seconds and sample-time qualification above 120 seconds, with 50-millisecond updates.

## Avoid two common misreadings

The word “low” does not describe low vehicle acceleration under ordinary driving. It describes a circuit diagnostic expressed through a raw sensor value. Also, a displayed 0.0 g after failure may be the source’s documented substitute value, not proof that the original signal has recovered.

Retain the configured sensor relationship and complete event data before clearing the code. A technician should establish the actual electrical fault through the matched circuit procedure rather than replacing a sensor from the code alone. Verification must allow the stability and monitoring intervals to complete under valid supply and communication conditions.

## Source and applicability

This explanation covers the documented 2025 GM monitor, not every vehicle using C0553. Confirm the vehicle’s module and calibration before applying its limits. [GM diagnostic table, PDF page 76](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=76).
