# P0102: Continental MAF signal frequency is below range

P0102 is a low-frequency mass-airflow circuit diagnostic for the Continental MAF implementation in this GM table. The ECM fails a MAF output at or below 1,050 Hz.

## Low frequency is not low airflow here

This sensor sends a digital square-wave signal, which the ECM converts to airflow using a transfer function. The source explicitly associates lower frequency with higher airflow. Applying the opposite relationship from another sensor family would misinterpret this particular monitor.

The check requires engine run time above one second, speed at least 300 rpm and relay voltage at least 9.1 V. The listed conditions must persist for at least one second. Failure qualification is 150 failing samples out of 190, with one sample per cylinder-firing event. Consequently, elapsed qualification time depends on event rate rather than a fixed 100-millisecond sampling period.

## What the evidence supports

Record raw frequency as well as the converted grams-per-second value. The table describes either a continuous short to ground or a sensor output frequency that is too low; it does not uniquely identify the source of the abnormal signal. The vehicle’s circuit and signal procedure is needed before choosing a repair.

Verification should establish valid frequency and completion of the enabled event-based check. A plausible converted airflow number alone can obscure the underlying frequency behavior. Confirm that the vehicle uses the documented Continental transfer function before applying the 1,050 Hz limit or its airflow direction.

## Source and applicability

This explanation covers the documented 2025 GM monitor, not every vehicle using P0102. Confirm the vehicle’s module and calibration before applying its limits. [GM diagnostic table, PDF page 131](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=131).
