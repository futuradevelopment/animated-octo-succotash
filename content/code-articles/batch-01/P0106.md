# P0106: MAP performance through several intake-flow comparisons

P0106 is the manifold-pressure performance fault within this GM single-turbo intake-flow rationality strategy. It looks for a MAP value that is inconsistent with modeled engine behavior, including a signal that remains within its electrical range but is inaccurate.

## Multiple models support the decision

The source gives three MAP-model residual limits: the first two exceed their limits above a filtered 25 kPa difference, and the third above 30 kPa. These model results are combined with MAF, boost-pressure and throttle-model results through a failure matrix. One residual exceeding a limit is not the complete P0106 setting rule.

The monitor computes every 12.5 milliseconds within the documented running window, including 400–6,100 rpm and relay voltage at least 9.1 V for 0.2 second. Temperature conditions and residual weighting also govern whether the comparisons are valid. Several related sensor faults inhibit the assessment.

## Interpret the model inputs together

Capture measured MAP, relevant model estimates and supporting airflow/boost/throttle data. A disagreement does not independently prove that the MAP sensor hardware is defective; another input can affect the model or prevent its use. A value being electrically in range is likewise not proof of correct pressure information.

Verification requires valid supporting inputs and completion of the appropriate model combination under the exact calibration. A single idle vacuum reading or a guessed fixed kPa tolerance cannot reproduce this diagnostic. The table does not prescribe cleaning or component replacement without further diagnosis.

## Source and applicability

This explanation covers the documented 2025 GM monitor, not every vehicle using P0106. Confirm the vehicle’s module and calibration before applying its limits.
