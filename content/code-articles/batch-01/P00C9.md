# P00C9: fuel-regulator supply high-side short to ground

P00C9 is the fuel-pressure regulator solenoid supply-circuit-low diagnostic in this GM strategy. More specifically, the controller supervises the high-pressure pump solenoid’s high-side driver for a short-to-ground condition while the output is on.

## Identify the supply side correctly

The high-side driver is the monitored supply-control path. That distinguishes this code from the low-side pump-solenoid diagnostics elsewhere in the same document. Applying a low-side off-state test to P00C9 would not reproduce the state described by this monitor.

The controller compares its measured voltage with internal limits. The table lists selectable current-related thresholds that depend on pump configuration, so it does not support a universal external current limit. Engine speed must be at least 50 rpm and battery voltage at least 11 V. Twenty failures out of 40 samples qualify the fault at 100-millisecond spacing.

## What to do with the evidence

Preserve supply voltage, high-side command and complete fault status. The monitor also restricts pump device-control and code-clear states, which can explain why a diagnostic command does not immediately reproduce the event. “Circuit low” does not directly mean low fuel pressure.

Verification should show successful completion of the enabled high-side on-state test after the actual cause is identified and corrected. Component selection requires the exact circuit procedure. High-pressure fuel-system access or manual actuator energizing is outside what this table supports and should follow professional vehicle-specific service instructions.

## Source and applicability

This explanation covers the documented 2025 GM monitor, not every vehicle using P00C9. Confirm the vehicle’s module and calibration before applying its limits. [GM diagnostic table, PDF page 118](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=118).
