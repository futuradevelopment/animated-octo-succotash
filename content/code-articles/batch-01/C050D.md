# C050D: left-rear wheel-speed performance at rest and in motion

C050D is a left-rear wheel-speed range/performance fault in the documented GM brake system. The visible table distinguishes weak magnetic pickup during rotation from missing status information while the wheel is stationary.

## Why zero speed is not the whole diagnosis

For BoschVDA and ContiVdaR variants, a standstill fault requires absence of both speed pulses and the VDA standstill protocol. The sensor supply must exceed 6 V, and detection takes 3.6 seconds. A DF11i variant has its own missing speed/stop-pulse check. Thus sensor type matters when interpreting silence on the signal line.

The air-gap-related branch requires at least five wheel rotations and speed above 1.24 mph. Its detection duration depends on speed. The source identifies low magnetic flux as the observation, but the extracted unit is unclear; it does not support prescribing a numeric physical gap here.

## Using those distinctions

Determine the fitted sensor family and whether the recorded event occurred at standstill or during rotation. A correct live speed while driving does not verify the standstill protocol, and a successful stationary electrical test does not establish adequate rotating magnetic signal. The four wheel-sensor undervoltage checks also serve as prerequisites, so their status matters before interpreting a nonrepeat.

Use the specific vehicle’s brake diagnostic procedure to investigate the identified branch and confirm its completed result after repair. C050D alone neither identifies a replacement assembly nor provides a judgment that continued driving is safe.

## Source and applicability

This explanation covers the documented 2025 GM monitor, not every vehicle using C050D. Confirm the vehicle’s module and calibration before applying its limits. [GM diagnostic table, PDF page 48](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=48).
