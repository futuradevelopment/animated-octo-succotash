# P0096: intake temperature 2 is an outlier after a long soak

P0096 checks intake-air temperature 2 against IAT and IAT3 in this GM application with humidity and manifold-temperature sensing. It detects a value stuck within its electrical range after a soak long enough for the temperatures to approach equilibrium.

## Three sensors help identify the outlier

One failure branch requires IAT and IAT3 to agree within 25°C while IAT2 differs from each by more than 25°C. Other branches handle three disagreeing readings by identifying the middle value and whether IAT2 is farther from it than the remaining sensor. The monitor therefore uses relationships, not just an absolute hot or cold limit.

It runs once at the beginning of an ignition cycle after more than 28,800 seconds since the engine last ran. Powertrain-relay voltage must be at least 11 V for 0.9 second, LIN communication with the MAF must be established, and specified temperature, relay and engine-off timer faults must be absent.

## A useful diagnostic comparison

Preserve the power-up readings from all three sensors. Later readings after engine heat develops do not reproduce the long-soak comparison. Also establish that the prerequisite inputs are valid before attributing the outlier to IAT2 itself.

Verification requires another eligible power-up after a sufficient soak. A warm restart or a plausible single IAT2 reading does not show that this once-per-cycle monitor passed. Confirm the exact vehicle’s sensor configuration before applying the three-sensor logic or deciding which assembly requires service.

## Source and applicability

This explanation covers the documented 2025 GM monitor, not every vehicle using P0096. Confirm the vehicle’s module and calibration before applying its limits.
