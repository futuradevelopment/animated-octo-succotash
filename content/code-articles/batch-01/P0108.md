# P0108: MAP circuit-high can indicate a power short or an open

P0108 is the manifold absolute-pressure circuit-high diagnostic in this GM table. It evaluates MAP voltage above 90% of the stated 5 V range, or above 4.5 V on that scale.

## High signal does not prove high physical pressure

The OEM strategy explicitly includes either a short to power or an open circuit. Those different electrical conditions can produce the same high-input classification. The source’s associated 390 kPa value is a calibration conversion, not independent evidence that the manifold actually reached that pressure.

The ECM samples every 12.5 milliseconds and requires 320 failures out of 400. The diagnostic is Type B, two trips. This count-based qualification is more specific than saying any momentary high reading immediately confirms the code.

## Diagnostic implications

Record the raw signal and full current/history status, then use the exact circuit procedure to distinguish an open path from a power-related fault. Assuming that every circuit-high code is a short alone would omit one of the source’s stated possibilities. The code does not independently identify the MAP sensor as the failed component.

Verification should show a credible input and a completed passing high-circuit check after the cause is addressed. A normal-looking calculated pressure at one instant cannot establish that the full sample window passed. The 4.5 V equivalent and 390 kPa mapping belong to this particular 5 V calibration and need vehicle confirmation before use.

## Source and applicability

This explanation covers the documented 2025 GM monitor, not every vehicle using P0108. Confirm the vehicle’s module and calibration before applying its limits. [GM diagnostic table, PDF page 137](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=137).
