# C0505: left-front speed disagreement with transmission output

C0505 is a correlation fault in the transmission-control section of this GM document. The controller converts left-front wheel speed and transmission output-shaft speed into vehicle-speed values, then compares their absolute difference. It is not the brake module’s direct sensor-current test.

## What confirms the mismatch

The documented threshold is a difference above 3.5 km/h. An enabled comparison updates every 25 milliseconds; failure time exceeding one second increments a counter, and more than two counts confirms the fault. A single unequal sample is therefore not equivalent to a confirmed C0505 event.

This entry requires the front-wheel-drive calibration flag, a steady-state variator and transmission output speed selected as the vehicle-speed source. Battery and run/crank supply must each exceed 9 V for over 0.1 second. Active U0121 or specified output-speed faults inhibit the comparison.

## Diagnostic implications

Compare both original data sources and their validity rather than condemning the left-front sensor from the name alone. A disagreement does not establish which input is wrong. The inhibiting faults also matter: their presence can remove the conditions needed to assess correlation.

The table describes a default that sets the affected wheel-speed signal to zero after failure. A zero displayed after the event may therefore be a substitute value rather than direct evidence that the wheel stopped producing pulses. Verification needs the relevant comparison enabled and a completed passing result.

## Source and applicability

This explanation covers the documented 2025 GM monitor, not every vehicle using C0505. Confirm the vehicle’s module and calibration before applying its limits. [GM diagnostic table, PDF page 1064](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=1064).
