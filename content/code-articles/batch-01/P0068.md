# P0068: throttle-based airflow disagrees with MAP and MAF

P0068 is a MAP/MAF/throttle correlation diagnostic in this GM ECM. The controller estimates airflow behavior from throttle position, then checks measured manifold pressure and mass airflow against their expected values.

## Two portions contribute to failure

The MAP portion can fail when measured and estimated pressure differ beyond a throttle-dependent table, or when specified reference-voltage or MAP-circuit diagnostics have failed during the key cycle. The MAF portion similarly evaluates measured versus estimated airflow and includes MAF-circuit failures and additional maximum-airflow comparisons.

The threshold values depend on throttle position, engine speed and voltage tables; this page does not provide one universal permissible kPa or grams-per-second difference. The recorded failure requires the MAP and MAF portions to fail continuously for 0.1875 second. Engine speed must exceed 800 rpm and run/crank voltage must exceed 6.41 V.

## Why related codes matter

Review the MAP, MAF and 5 V reference failures from the same key cycle before assuming a purely mechanical airflow problem. Those electrical results can directly participate in this monitor’s failure logic. Also capture throttle position alongside both measured and estimated quantities, because an isolated MAF value omits the relationship being tested.

P0068 does not establish a specific leak, dirty component or replacement part. Verification should show that the contributing inputs are valid and that the enabled correlation check completes successfully. Apply the correct calibration tables and vehicle-specific diagnosis rather than a guessed fixed tolerance.

## Source and applicability

This explanation covers the documented 2025 GM monitor, not every vehicle using P0068. Confirm the vehicle’s module and calibration before applying its limits. [GM diagnostic table, PDF page 95](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=95).
