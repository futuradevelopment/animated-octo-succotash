# C0001: TCS channel A valve 1 electrical monitoring

C0001 identifies traction-control channel A valve 1 in the GM electronic brake control module table. The controller monitors the valve coil and its electronic driver, so this code is broader than a conclusion that the hydraulic valve is mechanically stuck.

## What the checks distinguish

A fast monitor runs with ignition on and no valve test active. It can recognize excessive current, driver overheating or loss of the power-ground path. The documented overcurrent threshold is a hardware range of 4–6.5 A, with a 0.03-second detection time. That range describes internal protection behavior; it is not a recommended workshop current injection test.

A separate silent driver test runs cyclically at 20-second intervals outside valve control, with no hydraulic request. Several checks require valve-relay supply above 6.9 V. The continuation lists measured resistance below 4.8 Ω or above 13.7 Ω as another failure route.

## Interpreting the evidence

The operating state matters: a fault recorded during quiet self-testing need not follow an actual traction-control intervention. Conversely, a driver protection flag should not be treated as proof of an open coil. Obtain the complete brake-module failure detail and supply history to separate those paths. The listed conditions also mean an uneventful scan immediately after clearing is not verification that the cyclic check has run. Brake electrical and hydraulic service should follow the vehicle-specific professional procedure.

## Source and applicability

This explanation covers the documented 2025 GM monitor, not every vehicle using C0001. Confirm the vehicle’s module and calibration before applying its limits.
