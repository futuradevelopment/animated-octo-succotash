# P0038: bank 1 sensor 2 heater on-state circuit-high

P0038 is the bank 1 sensor 2 oxygen-sensor heater circuit-high diagnostic in this GM table. The controller checks the low-side output driver while commanded on and identifies voltage behavior consistent with a short to power.

## The condition being tested

The published controller specification corresponds to less than 0.5 Ω between the output and controller power. This describes the electrical fault classification behind the voltage thresholds. It is not an oxygen-sensing voltage limit, a heater-temperature measurement or proof of a rich mixture.

The diagnostic requires voltage above 11 V, engine speed above 400 rpm and ignition in crank or run. Twenty failing observations out of 25 qualify the fault, sampled every 250 milliseconds. The table classifies it as Type B, two trips.

## Use the right operating state

An off-state electrical observation does not verify this on-state check. Preserve actual heater command and supply conditions, then follow the matched circuit procedure to distinguish the abnormal connection from driver behavior. The monitor alone cannot select a sensor, wiring repair or controller replacement.

After the cause is established and corrected, demonstrate that the heater is commanded on with the diagnostic enabled and that it completes without failure. A sensor that produces plausible exhaust readings after warming can still have a heater-control problem, so those readings are not a substitute. Exact component identification and permitted test methods remain vehicle-specific.

## Source and applicability

This explanation covers the documented 2025 GM monitor, not every vehicle using P0038. Confirm the vehicle’s module and calibration before applying its limits. [GM diagnostic table, PDF page 93](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=93).
