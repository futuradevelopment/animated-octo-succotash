# P00E9: intake temperature 3 disagrees with its reference sensors

P00E9 is an intake-air temperature 3 performance diagnostic for the GM arrangement with humidity and manifold-temperature sensing. It identifies IAT3 as a possible in-range outlier by comparing power-up IAT, IAT2 and IAT3 values.

## The power-up relationship is decisive

One branch requires IAT and IAT2 to agree within 25°C while IAT3 differs from both by more than 25°C. Other branches evaluate the middle of three disagreeing values and whether IAT3 is the farther outlier. This is more specific than checking whether IAT3 is simply hot or cold.

The monitor runs once at the beginning of an eligible ignition cycle after more than eight hours since the engine last ran. Relay voltage must be at least 11 V for 0.9 second, LIN communication must be established, and relevant temperature, relay and timer faults must be absent. The good-correlation branch also lists coolant minus outside temperature at or below 25°C.

## Capture the right readings

Keep all three original power-up values. Readings after warm-up no longer represent the thermal-equilibrium assumption behind this test. An apparently sensible IAT3 value alone does not establish agreement with its references.

Verification requires another qualifying soak and startup, plus credible reference inputs. A warm restart cannot validate the once-per-cycle comparison. The code does not independently prove which assembly is defective, so component decisions need the correct vehicle-specific sensing configuration and service procedure.

## Source and applicability

This explanation covers the documented 2025 GM monitor, not every vehicle using P00E9. Confirm the vehicle’s module and calibration before applying its limits.
