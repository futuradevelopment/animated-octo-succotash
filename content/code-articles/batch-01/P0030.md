# P0030: bank 1 sensor 1 heater open-circuit check

P0030 is the bank 1 sensor 1 oxygen-sensor heater control-circuit diagnostic in this GM table. It evaluates the heater’s low-side output driver for an open circuit while the driver is off, rather than evaluating oxygen content in the exhaust.

## An off-state electrical test

The controller compares driver voltage with an internal acceptable range. The stated open-circuit specification corresponds to impedance above 200 kΩ between the output and controller ground. This explains the detection strategy; it is not a complete resistance test for the heater element itself.

The diagnostic requires crank or run, voltage above 11 V and engine speed above 400 rpm. It confirms 20 failures out of 25 samples at 250 milliseconds per sample. The source classifies the monitor as Type B, two trips, and notes that P0031 may also set on certain controllers.

## Why the companion code matters

P0030 and P0031 appearing together do not automatically prove two independent faults. The source explicitly allows overlap between the open-circuit and low-circuit classifications. Preserve the complete failure status and use the vehicle-specific heater circuit procedure to establish the actual path involved.

Verification should include the driver-off diagnostic under its enabled conditions. A plausible oxygen-sensor reading after the exhaust warms does not demonstrate heater-circuit integrity, because exhaust sensing and heater supervision are different functions. The code supports electrical investigation but does not identify a replacement sensor by itself.

## Source and applicability

This explanation covers the documented 2025 GM monitor, not every vehicle using P0030. Confirm the vehicle’s module and calibration before applying its limits. [GM diagnostic table, PDF page 85](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=85).
