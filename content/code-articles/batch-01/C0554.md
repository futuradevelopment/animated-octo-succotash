# C0554: longitudinal acceleration circuit-high and substituted data

C0554 is the longitudinal-acceleration circuit-high fault in the referenced GM ECM table. It is intended to detect a short to power or an open condition through the raw signal’s relationship to calibrated limits.

## High is configuration-dependent

For a directly proportional sensor, the documented high criterion is greater than 3.85 g. The table also distinguishes inversely proportional sensing, so a diagnostic must identify the configured sensor type before interpreting the direction of a raw reading. The code is not a claim that the vehicle actually accelerated at that level.

Battery and run/crank supplies must exceed 11 V, the diagnostic must be enabled, and U0073 must not be active or have failed during the current key-on. The table requires stability above 30 seconds, accumulated failure time above 75 seconds and sample-time qualification above 120 seconds.

## What confirms a repair

A short observation after clearing is inadequate because the qualifying periods may not have elapsed. Preserve the original fault state and evaluate the specific electrical path under the vehicle’s service procedure. The summary does not distinguish every possible wiring or sensor cause and does not prescribe a replacement.

The documented default sets the acceleration signal to 0.0 g. That makes post-fault zero a potentially substituted value rather than direct evidence of a healthy sensor. Final verification should establish valid, nonsubstituted input information and successful completion of the enabled high-circuit monitor for the correct calibration.

## Source and applicability

This explanation covers the documented 2025 GM monitor, not every vehicle using C0554. Confirm the vehicle’s module and calibration before applying its limits. [GM diagnostic table, PDF page 77](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=77).
