# P0121: throttle-position plausibility checked against intake-flow models

P0121 is a throttle-position performance diagnostic within the documented single-turbo GM intake-flow rationality strategy. The broad dictionary title mentions throttle/pedal sensing, but this particular monitor evaluates throttle-position consistency with airflow and pressure models.

## A modeled relationship, not just a voltage limit

The table uses a filtered throttle-model error limit of 300 kPa·g/s. That compound unit shows why the value should not be reinterpreted as a throttle angle or a direct signal-voltage threshold. MAF, several MAP models and boost-pressure comparisons contribute to the failure matrix that determines when P0121 sets.

Computations occur every 12.5 milliseconds under specified running conditions, including 400–6,100 rpm and relay voltage at least 9.1 V for 0.2 second. Temperature conditions, residual weighting and absence of listed related faults also affect monitoring.

## What changes the diagnostic decision

Record throttle position alongside supporting pressure and airflow data. An electrically in-range throttle signal can still be inconsistent with the modeled system, while an invalid supporting sensor can affect or inhibit the comparison. The code alone therefore does not identify the throttle body or accelerator pedal as the failed assembly.

Verification requires valid related inputs and completion of the calibrated model combination. A stationary pedal sweep or a single plausible throttle percentage cannot reproduce the full running rationality test. Use the exact vehicle’s service procedure and failure matrix before selecting a repair; this table does not prescribe cleaning, relearning or replacement as a universal remedy.

## Source and applicability

This explanation covers the documented 2025 GM monitor, not every vehicle using P0121. Confirm the vehicle’s module and calibration before applying its limits.
