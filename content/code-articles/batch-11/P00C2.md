# P00C2: The bypass-driver high code is supported by current feedback

P00C2 names a high fault in the bank 2 turbocharger bypass-valve circuit. The GM summary describes a short to battery voltage, but the monitored quantity shown in its row is output current with the power stage energized. The short label alone therefore misses the way this implementation detects trouble.

## Commanded on, then checked for current

The stated condition combines power stage ON with output current of at least 2 amperes. The detection period is one second, and reporting is classified as two-trip. These are controller-monitor details, not instructions to connect a test power supply or force current through the actuator.

The paired low-circuit row checks voltage while the output is off. This difference matters when reading scan or measurement evidence: an on-state current condition and an off-state voltage condition are not interchangeable tests.

## Avoid jumping from “high” to a part

Preserve the energized-command state and the electrical feedback associated with the stored fault. A valve’s mechanical position or a boost-pressure response does not independently establish whether this driver-current condition occurred.

The source refers to other inhibit conditions and does not specify where a short originates. The correct service wiring and circuit procedure are needed to distinguish the control branch from the connected component and to confirm the applicable limits. P00C2 alone cannot justify replacement of the turbocharger or its bypass valve.
