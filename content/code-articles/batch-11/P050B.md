# P050B: Cold-start timing performance is compared with catalyst-heating intent

P050B concerns cold-start ignition timing performance. In GM’s example, the monitor compares actual ignition efficiency with the efficiency requested for catalyst heating while the engine is idling. A single ignition-angle reading cannot represent that whole assessment.

## The heating strategy must be active

The row requires catalyst heating to be activated and the current cold-start heating period at idle to exceed ten seconds. It evaluates a mean efficiency deviation against a mapped threshold ranging from 0.27 to 0.999 and lists a two-second assessment with two-trip reporting.

The enable logic also considers end-of-start status, homogeneous operation and initial-fueling conditions. These requirements distinguish an eligible catalyst-heating event from ordinary warm idle or an unrelated acceleration.

## Preserve the requested behavior

A useful record includes the heating request and actual operating mode alongside the ignition-efficiency or manufacturer diagnostic parameters. Retarded timing during a heating strategy is not, by itself, evidence of this fault; the question is whether actual behavior matches the intended heating operation.

The threshold is a calibration map, not a universal timing angle or an instruction to adjust ignition manually. The source does not identify a failed coil, plug, sensor or catalyst from P050B alone. Confirm that the correct heating monitor ran, then follow the applicable diagnostic procedure to investigate why the observed efficiency differed from the requested result.
