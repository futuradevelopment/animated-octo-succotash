# P0242: High boost-sensor voltage requires an electrical interpretation

P0242 concerns the high side of the bank 2 boost-pressure sensor input. GM documents a raw-voltage path and a physical-pressure path under the same code, so the short label cannot specify which measurement produced the fault.

## A high-voltage path is clearly documented

The electrical row uses voltage above 4.85 volts for 1.5 seconds. It requires ignition on and basic enable conditions, with two-trip reporting. This is an input-range judgment, not proof that the engine experienced excessive real boost pressure.

The physical-range row instead names a high-pressure fault but prints a less-than 5,000-hectopascal condition. It uses a valid raw signal, a 400-millisecond interval and one-trip reporting. The comparator is present in the rendered original and remains a source-review issue.

## Avoid mixing two thresholds

Do not compare a voltage reading with the pressure-row number or treat both reporting intervals as one combined test. Identify the active monitor variant and retain the corresponding raw voltage or converted pressure along with its validity state.

The established electrical boundary can guide the choice of the matching service diagnostic branch, but it cannot determine whether the sensing element or its circuit caused the high input. The disputed physical comparator requires clarification before use. A component replacement decision needs the vehicle’s actual wiring and pressure-sensor verification procedure, not a guessed reversal of the summary-table sign.
