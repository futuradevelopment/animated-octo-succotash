# P0652: Reference B low identifies the second TCM reference rail

P0652 concerns sensor reference voltage B. The cited transmission controller calls the affected supply VREF2, distinguishing it from the first reference rail used by P0642. That channel identity matters when several sensors or references are present.

## The second rail has its own monitored value

The documented low boundary is 4.5 volts, with 50 millivolts of hysteresis and a confirmation period of at least 40 milliseconds. The check runs continuously when the main battery supply is above 8.75 and below 18 volts, outside cranking and the STOP key state.

P0882 and P0883 disable the monitor. Their presence can therefore explain why clearing P0652 and observing no recurrence does not establish that VREF2 has passed its assessment.

## Do not substitute a healthy first rail

A normal VREF1 reading does not verify VREF2. Preserve the actual B-rail measurement, its affected input context and the main supply status. The shared numerical threshold does not make the two circuits interchangeable.

The source gives rail-level evidence and a type-A classification, but no list of connected sensors or connector pins. The vehicle’s wiring information is needed to locate the low condition. It could not be assigned to a particular component from this code alone. Follow the approved diagnostic procedure and confirm the correct rail under eligible monitor conditions before treating a clear fault memory as proof of repair.
