# P0240: Boost-sensor plausibility is checked with the engine stopped

P0240 is a range or performance fault for the bank 2 boost-pressure sensor. The GM example performs a cross-check with the engine at zero rpm, which makes it fundamentally different from a loaded-engine boost target comparison.

## A stopped-engine reference comparison

The high-side row requires engine speed to remain zero for at least five seconds. It compares bank 2 upstream-throttle pressure with a reference pressure plus a 60-hectopascal tolerance, using a 0.4-second comparison interval. It also restricts the raw pressure range and requires the ECU’s DRIVE state.

Those conditions explain why a pressure value captured during acceleration does not reproduce the documented test. Here the reference comparison, not requested boost under load, is the central evidence.

## Do not silently repair an ambiguous formula

The paired low-side row also prints a reference-plus-tolerance expression. Its intended lower comparison is not established well enough to turn into a universal service specification. The article therefore preserves the engine-off plausibility principle while leaving that formula for calibration-specific confirmation.

A useful record includes both the measured pressure and the reference used by the controller after the qualifying engine-off interval. A disagreement does not automatically identify the boost sensor as the failed part; the reference and parameter mapping must also be credible. Use the matching service information to distinguish the source of the discrepancy before considering replacement.
