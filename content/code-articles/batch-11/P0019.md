# P0019: How the bank 2 exhaust-cam correlation check is enabled

P0019 identifies the bank 2 exhaust side of the crankshaft-to-camshaft correlation assessment. That exhaust assignment matters when an engine offers several cam-position channels: an intake-cam reading is not a substitute for the signal pair evaluated by this monitor.

## Offset and eligibility are separate evidence

GM describes an averaged angular offset outside a window bounded by −8.79 and 12.59 crankshaft degrees. The table also requires successful crankshaft synchronization and four crank revolutions without the relevant signal errors. An offset number obtained during interrupted synchronization is therefore not equivalent to a completed P0019 check.

The exhaust-cam edge-adaptation request is an explicit enabling condition. Backward rotation is excluded, and the table notes that pulse length conveys rotation direction. These conditions explain why a captured waveform needs operating context rather than interpretation from one edge alone.

## What to preserve before drawing conclusions

Record which exhaust channel supplied the data, whether synchronization remained established, and whether related loss-of-signal faults were present. A trustworthy but displaced relationship and an unreliable signal trace lead to different next checks.

One-trip reporting and a two-cam-revolution evaluation window describe detection behavior, not a replacement recommendation. The source does not tell the reader to replace the cam sensor or timing drive. Vehicle-specific waveform references and timing instructions remain necessary before deciding whether the observed relationship is electrically or mechanically wrong.
