# P0643: Reference A high must be distinguished from high battery supply

P0643 is the high-reference-voltage A fault. GM’s TCM example evaluates VREF1, a sensor supply rail, and separately qualifies the main battery voltage. The two voltages answer different diagnostic questions.

## The high reference boundary

The monitor detects VREF1 above 5.5 volts. It uses 50 millivolts of hysteresis and at least 40 milliseconds of fault confirmation. A momentary sample near the threshold should therefore not be interpreted without the timing and hysteresis context.

Main supply must be above 8.75 and below 18 volts, the engine must not be cranking, and the key state must not be STOP. Existing P0882 or P0883 faults disable the assessment. The source describes continuous monitoring only within those eligible conditions.

## Follow the affected supply network

Preserve the VREF1 reading and any shared-input symptoms without assuming that one sensor is responsible. A high rail can affect interpretation of connected signals, but this summary does not map the branches or localize the source of the condition.

The appropriate service distribution diagram and circuit procedure are required before isolating loads or selecting a component. The code does not prove a failed controller, and a high battery reading alone does not recreate its reference-rail test. Verification needs the correct rail to return to an acceptable state while the qualified monitor actually runs.
