# P0250: Wastegate output 1 high identifies a short-to-supply report

P0250 identifies a high wastegate-control circuit condition. In this GM implementation, the decisive information is an H-bridge chip report of a short to battery on bank 2 output 1. It is not simply a statement that boost pressure is high.

## Eligibility matters before the report is judged

The source requires supply voltage above 10.9 volts and keeps open-load diagnosis inactive during this check. It specifies a 1.5-second detection period and one-trip reporting. These conditions tie the fault to a particular electrical diagnostic mode.

Because the report comes from output 1 of the driver, the correct actuator and output mapping must be established from service information. Generic “B” labels, bank numbers and physical connector pins should not be assumed to be interchangeable across engines.

## Keep supply faults separate from boost faults

Preserve the driver’s short-to-battery status and the conditions under which it was recorded. A pressure trace alone cannot confirm or exclude that electrical condition. Likewise, observing no actuator movement after a protective shutdown does not identify the original short location.

The monitor description does not select an actuator, wiring section or controller as the failed part. Localization requires the applicable circuit tests and connector information. Its value is in narrowing the diagnostic question to a reported supply-side fault on a specific driver output, rather than treating the entire turbocharger assembly as the immediate replacement target.

## Source and applicability
