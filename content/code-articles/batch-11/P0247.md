# P0247: An open wastegate-driver report requires a quiet diagnostic window

P0247 concerns the bank 2 turbocharger wastegate control circuit in this GM strategy. The controller does not infer an open load merely because boost differs from a target. It receives an explicit open-load error from the H-bridge driver electronics.

## Other driver activities must be inactive

The row requires no actuator-drive request, no conflicting internal driver use, and no active short-circuit protection or awaited protective test pulse. These conditions prevent the open-load assessment from being interpreted during a different driver operating mode.

The battery-voltage condition is above 10.9 volts. A diagnostic shutdown request from the actuator must be absent, and the inhibit tables apply. The documented evaluation lasts 1,500 milliseconds and has one-trip reporting.

## The driver status changes the next question

Preserve the H-bridge status and actuator operating state when the fault occurred. If the output was disabled for another protective reason, a non-moving actuator is not automatically evidence of the open-load condition described here.

This monitor reports electrical continuity evidence at the driver; it does not establish which harness segment, connector or actuator element is interrupted. The applicable circuit procedure is needed to locate the problem. A mechanical wastegate movement test answers a separate question and cannot replace verification of the open-load report. The source supplies no pinout or permission to energize the actuator outside its approved diagnostic procedure.
