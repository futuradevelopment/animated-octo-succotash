# P0392: A low bank 2 exhaust-cam signal is assessed when edges stop

P0392 is the low-circuit code for the bank 2 exhaust camshaft sensor. The GM strategy evaluates a loss of new cam edges with the signal low as it transitions into the no-signal state. It does not directly describe an incorrect mechanical cam angle.

## Crank rotation makes the missing edges significant

The row qualifies the observation with crankshaft revolutions. Ignition must be on, a crank signal containing its gap pattern must be detected, and backward rotation must not be detected. Those conditions give the controller a valid basis for expecting new exhaust-cam edges.

A low logic level when the engine is stationary is therefore incomplete evidence. The key combination is ongoing qualified rotation and a cam channel that no longer supplies its expected transitions.

## Separate circuit loss from pattern error

A pattern-performance fault evaluates the order or spacing of edges that do exist. P0392 here concerns their absence together with a low level. Preserve the crank reference and exhaust-cam signal simultaneously to retain that distinction.

The source assigns one-trip reporting and references other enable and inhibit conditions. It supplies no terminal location or resistance test and does not prove that the sensor itself failed. The correct engine circuit procedure must distinguish the signal path, sensor supply and controller input. Use the code to choose that investigation, rather than jumping from “low” to a timing-component replacement.
