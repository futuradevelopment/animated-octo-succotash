# P07C6: Intermediate speed A high includes pulse-quality checks

P07C6 is the high-circuit code for intermediate shaft speed sensor A. GM maps this input to the odd-clutch speed sensor and includes several electrical and pulse-quality checks under the same code. It is not simply a warning that the shaft rotates too quickly.

## Voltage and pulse behavior are separate paths

The average signal-voltage boundary is above 4.230 volts, with 30 millivolts of hysteresis. Additional paths assess pulse-width validity and changes in pulse width. At frequencies of at least 40 hertz, the listed jump boundary is greater than 15 microseconds within a valid range.

The supply VSS1 must be above 8.25 and below 10 volts, while battery voltage is separately qualified. Confirmation uses at least 300 milliseconds, and P0882/P0883 disable the check.

## Preserve waveform context

A speed PID alone hides pulse encoding and average electrical level. Retain the relevant input signal and supply information before deciding whether the fault concerns voltage, pulse shape or another listed branch.
