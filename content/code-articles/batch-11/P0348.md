# P0348: A high bank 2 intake-cam input is diagnosed when edges disappear

P0348 is the high-circuit code for the bank 2 intake camshaft sensor. In the cited GM monitor, “high” is not an advanced cam angle. It describes the electrical level retained when the controller stops receiving new cam edges.

## A no-edge state with a high level

The controller observes at least four crankshaft revolutions and checks that the cam signal is permanently high as it transitions into the no-signal state. Ignition must be on, the crank gap pattern must be detected, and backward engine rotation must be absent.

These conditions distinguish a missing-edge fault from a static high level when the engine is not rotating. They also distinguish it from a correlation error, where usable signals are present but their timing relationship is wrong.

## Use both signals to interpret the event

Preserve the crankshaft reference and the bank 2 intake-cam trace together. A high reading without proof of qualifying rotation cannot reproduce the monitor logic, while a cam waveform containing valid transitions would raise a different question.

The source specifies one-trip reporting and points to other enable and inhibit conditions. It does not locate the high condition within the sensor, supply, harness or controller input. That requires the matching circuit procedure and channel identification. The code alone therefore supports investigation of a no-edge high-input event, not automatic replacement of the cam sensor or timing components.
