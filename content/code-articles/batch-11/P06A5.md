# P06A5: Reference D high can be a 10.5-volt speed-sensor supply check

P06A5 is generically named sensor reference voltage D high. In the cited GM transmission controller, that code monitors speed-sensor supply VSS1. Its operating range is different from the five-volt reference rails elsewhere in the same document.

## The actual rail matters more than the letter

The high boundary is VSS1 above 10.5 volts, with 500 millivolts of hysteresis and a confirmation time of at least 75 milliseconds. Applying a five-volt-reference expectation to this rail would misinterpret the documented system.

The main battery must be above 8.75 and below 18 volts. Cranking and the STOP key state are excluded, and P0882 or P0883 disables the monitor. The check runs continuously when these conditions allow it.

## Keep supply diagnosis separate from speed plausibility

A speed value that looks implausible may motivate investigation, but P06A5 here concerns the electrical supply feeding the speed-sensor system. Preserve VSS1 voltage and eligibility information rather than relying solely on wheel or shaft-speed values.

The source does not provide a distribution map or identify which connected element raised the supply. Use the applicable transmission wiring and circuit procedure to localize the issue. A normal VREF reading on another rail cannot confirm this fault is resolved. Verification must concern the VSS1 supply under the documented qualified conditions, with the correct calibration limits.
