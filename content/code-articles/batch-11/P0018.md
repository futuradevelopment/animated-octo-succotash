# P0018: Bank 2 intake-cam correlation needs valid timing signals

P0018 is a correlation judgment about the crankshaft and bank 2 intake camshaft. In this GM table it is not simply a missing-signal alarm: the controller first requires a usable relationship between both signals before evaluating their angular offset.

## What the comparison measures

The documented average-offset boundaries are above 12.59 or below −8.79 crankshaft degrees. Those asymmetric values describe this calibration; they are not universal installation marks or specifications for every engine with P0018.

The enable column requires four crankshaft revolutions without relevant crank or cam signal error and without loss of synchronization. It also excludes backward rotation and requires the appropriate intake-cam edge-adaptation request. Thus a correlation result depends on signal validity and operating state, not just the presence of two sensor voltages.

## Separate correlation from signal failure

When reviewing a scan record, retain any signal-loss or disturbance faults alongside P0018. They affect whether this particular correlation monitor could run. A repeated synchronization problem calls for a different investigation from a stable, valid signal pair that consistently reports an offset.

The table allocates two camshaft revolutions to the comparison and one-trip reporting. Neither statement proves a timing chain has failed. Confirm the applicable engine procedure before interpreting waveform alignment, adapted positions or mechanical timing. The evidence here supports an offset diagnosis; it does not establish which component caused it.
