# P0877: Pressure sensor D low refers to the differential-pressure input here

P0877 is the low-circuit code for transmission pressure sensor D. GM maps this row to the limited-slip-differential pressure sensor. That is distinct from the main transmission system-pressure sensor elsewhere in the same table.

## Low signal voltage is not measured low pressure

The established input boundary is below 220 millivolts, with 30 millivolts of hysteresis and a confirmation time of at least 150 milliseconds. The table requires the main battery supply above 8.75 and below 18 volts and excludes cranking and STOP state.

The VREF1 enable cells are contradictory: they print a value above 5.5 volts and below 4.5 volts simultaneously. This was verified in the rendered original and is not converted into an assumed corrected window.

## Follow the correct reference rail

The row lists P0882/P0883 and reference-A faults P0642/P0643 as disabling conditions. Preserve those codes and the VREF1 context when reviewing why the pressure-input monitor did or did not run.

A generic sensor-D label does not identify a physical connector without the installed transmission mapping. Nor does a low electrical reading prove low differential clutch pressure or a failed sensing element. The applicable circuit procedure must resolve the source’s supply-window issue and distinguish the sensor, wiring and controller input. This monitor supplies a low-signal diagnosis, not a complete hydraulic repair decision.

## Source and applicability

This explanation covers the cited GM monitor, not every vehicle carrying this code. The application-matrix filename does not resolve cleanly to this file, so no model or engine fitment is assigned.
