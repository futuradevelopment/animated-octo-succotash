# U0644: Wastegate position sensor A SENT communication failure

U0644 reports loss of communication with wastegate position sensor A. This GM monitor evaluates the electronic wastegate's SENT interface, checking message pulses and timing validity. It does not directly test whether actual boost matches commanded boost or whether the wastegate linkage moves freely.

## The fault requires more than an old position reading

The criteria combine a SENT message-fault count greater than zero with a message age greater than 3.13 milliseconds. Qualification uses ten failures within twelve samples, sampled every 100 milliseconds. The message-age limit and the sampling interval are separate parameters; the latter is not the permitted age of an individual SENT message.

The monitor must be enabled and the SENT interface in use. Powertrain-relay voltage must be at least 11 volts, the engine must not be cranking, and the diagnostic system must remain enabled. The source classifies the fault as Type A with one-trip behavior.

## Identify A before investigating the circuit

The table defines turbocharger A differently by arrangement. In a series system, it is the first turbocharger in exhaust-flow order. In a parallel system, it is associated with engine bank 1. That definition does not authorize guessing the layout or component location of a specific vehicle.

Preserve the SENT fault and message-age information alongside other boost or actuator codes. Unavailable position feedback can undermine control interpretation without proving a mechanical turbocharger failure. Establish the installed arrangement, signal validity, and supply conditions before attributing a boost symptom to a stuck wastegate or replacing the turbocharger assembly.

## Source and applicability

2025 GM 25OBDG03B documented monitor only. Vehicle, engine and calibration applicability must be confirmed from the matching service information; no model fitment is inferred.
