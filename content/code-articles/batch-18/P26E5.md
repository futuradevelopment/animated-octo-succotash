# P26E5: Low pinion-relay feedback uses its own starting-voltage boundary

P26E5 concerns the starter pinion-relay high-side output being classified as shorted to ground while the output is on. The GM row evaluates controller-specific voltage feedback. It is not a direct measurement of pinion movement or evidence that the starter mechanism is mechanically locked.

## The enable voltage differs from related codes

This monitor permits run/crank voltage down to 6.41 V. The neighboring open- and high-circuit rows use an 11 V minimum. Importing their eligibility into P26E5 would therefore change when this particular diagnosis can run.

The documented electrical classification corresponds to impedance at or below 0.5 Ω toward controller ground. Because this describes the internal diagnostic model, it cannot be used as a connector resistance specification without the applicable circuit information.

## Separate fast confirmation from mechanical conclusions

P26E5 uses eight failures out of ten samples, with 50 ms per sample. That sample decision is shorter than the 40-of-50 checks printed for the adjacent open and high faults. The two-trip reporting classification is a separate matter from these sampling intervals.

Retain output command, supply voltage and the exact subtype when evaluating a starting complaint. A normal signal after the command ends does not test the same state. The monitor provides an electrical direction for investigation but does not locate a ground path or prove that the relay, harness or controller requires replacement.
