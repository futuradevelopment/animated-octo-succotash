# P2E81: Cold-start valve D remains on the closed side during a tracking error

P2E81 identifies the closed-side tracking result for coolant valve D during the documented cold-start strategy. GM requires a position mismatch together with feedback below the dividing angle. A low angle matching a low-angle command does not satisfy that combination.

## Read the two failure conditions together

The absolute target-versus-feedback difference must exceed five degrees and feedback must remain below 50 degrees. Four seconds of failure within five seconds are required. The system must be in Low Flow or Low Flow Autostop, with catalyst warm-up enabled and usable electrical feedback.

Supply, relay and calibration-state qualifications also apply. These requirements matter when distinguishing a genuine eligible tracking failure from an unavailable drive supply or an endstop-learning movement.

## Preserve a published enable ambiguity

The continuation prints a consecutive-command change greater than five degrees for more than three seconds. Related tracking rows use a smaller-change qualification. This article does not silently reverse the operator or assume the same command-stability rule for every variant; the exact calibration needs confirmation.

Retain the actual command sequence, feedback and warm-up state. The supported result is a closed-side tracking discrepancy, not direct proof of restricted coolant flow or a particular mechanical jam. Resolve the enable detail and follow the vehicle-specific procedure before using the code to choose a component or reproduce an exact test.

## Source and applicability

2025 GM 25OBDG03B documented monitor only. Vehicle, engine and calibration applicability must be confirmed from the matching service information; no model fitment is inferred.
