# P2C17: Exhaust Hall sensor B can report movement that the controller never commanded

P2C17 is the performance result for exhaust profile-position sensor B, called exhaust Hall sensor 2 in the GM row. The controller sees feedback associated with a profile-pin shift but has no matching shift command. The electrical signal can be present while its reported event remains inconsistent with control history.

## A normal endpoint does not explain the event

The source recognizes extending, extended and retracted states. An uncommanded sequence through those states can finish at a normal-looking retracted position. A snapshot taken afterward therefore cannot reconstruct the diagnostic evidence by itself.

The page’s numerical state boundaries are not accompanied by clear physical units. They describe internal feedback recognition and should not be interpreted as universal connector voltages or mechanical travel limits.

## Preserve the qualified engine-cycle context

The engine must be running, supply above 11 V and the diagnostic enabled. Relevant crankshaft and intake/exhaust cam-sensor faults must not be active. Without sound reference signals, the interpretation of a profile event relative to engine operation is weakened.

Four failed readings out of five establish the stated result. Retain the exhaust B-channel transitions, command history and reference status together. The source does not prove whether physical pin movement occurred unexpectedly or whether the Hall feedback misrepresented it. The matching service procedure must resolve that distinction before a sensor or actuator is condemned.
