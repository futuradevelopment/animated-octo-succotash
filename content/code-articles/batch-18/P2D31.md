# P2D31: The intake tuning valve’s learned closed stop exceeds its accepted range

P2D31 evaluates the learned closed-stop feedback of intake manifold tuning valve 1. GM uses a calculated average feedback voltage at the closed stop, rather than simply declaring that the valve failed to move during an ordinary command. A moving valve can still learn an unacceptable endpoint.

## Separate learning offset from a circuit fault

The published upper comparison is above 4.5 V for the averaged closed-stop value. The row also prints another comparison at 4.25 V whose equality wording is not sufficient to reconstruct a conventional lower-bound check confidently. That entry is not silently changed into a different operator here.

The continuation excludes relevant position-sensor and actuator circuit faults, including P2077, P2078, P2076 and P0660. Those prerequisites matter because a learned endpoint is only meaningful when its underlying feedback can be trusted.

## Do not apply the printed temperature bounds physically

The source prints coolant-temperature limits of ±3,000°C. These are unusable as ordinary vehicle-temperature specifications and remain unresolved. They cannot justify a test condition or a claim of universal eligibility.

The row lists a one-second monitor interval and two-trip reporting. Useful evidence preserves the learned average, closed-stop event and relevant circuit status. The supported finding is a learning-range concern, not proof that the manifold or actuator needs replacement. Matching service information must resolve the ambiguous calibration entries before an exact relearn or repair procedure is selected.
