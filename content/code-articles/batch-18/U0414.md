# U0414: Four-wheel-drive data arrives but fails integrity checks

U0414 concerns invalid data from the four-wheel-drive clutch control module. In the GM monitor, the relevant message frame is available on the bus, but its rolling-count or protection/checksum information is incorrect. Receiving a frame therefore does not guarantee that its content can be trusted.

## Separate availability from integrity

The source supervises the alive rolling count and protected secondary-axle signal, using eight failures out of eighteen samples. This is different from a simple timeout caused by a message never arriving. A scan display that refreshes can still carry data the receiver rejects.

The diagnostic executes in a 12.5 ms loop. The sampling decision should not be confused with the separate two-trip reporting classification, nor should one erroneous frame automatically be treated as the completed fault.

## Verify stable enabling conditions

The stated conditions must be met for at least five seconds, including supply eligibility and no pending accessory-to-off transition. The message frame carrying the integrity fields must be present. Those qualifications are essential when comparing a stored result with behavior during shutdown or recovery.

Useful evidence includes the receiving controller, message identifier, counter/protection error and power state. U0414 does not prove a failed four-wheel-drive clutch or mechanical engagement problem. The appropriate network procedure must determine why the received data fails integrity validation before mechanical repairs or module replacement are considered.

## Source and applicability

2025 GM 25OBDG03B documented monitor only. Vehicle, engine and calibration applicability must be confirmed from the matching service information; no model fitment is inferred. These criteria explain controller logic, not a complete repair procedure. [25OBDG03B, PDF page 1042](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03B.pdf#page=1042).
