# P2C09: Intake profile-position channel B has its own open-circuit result

P2C09 concerns the intake camshaft profile-position sensor B circuit being classified open. The cited GM strategy uses a driver-level voltage check while its output is on. This is channel-specific electrical evidence, so it should not be generalized into a failure of every profile sensor or the entire camshaft assembly.

## Identify the intended channel and command

The monitor compares voltage with controller-specific thresholds corresponding to impedance above 200 kΩ toward controller ground. The source does not provide a physical connector location or make the generic B designation a cylinder number.

The neighboring P2C0A performance row describes intake Hall sensor 2 and a command-to-state inconsistency. That distinction helps separate an electrical open result from a signal that is present but reports the wrong event sequence.

## Evaluate the powered-on observation window

The output must be commanded on, system supply above 11 V, diagnostic enablement active and ignition in crank or run. Four failed samples among five establish the code’s result. A normal off-state voltage cannot substitute for this energized assessment.

Preserve channel B’s command and electrical status rather than relying only on a decoded position display. The monitor does not establish where the interruption is located or whether the connected sensor, its circuit or the driver caused it. A vehicle-specific circuit procedure is needed before choosing a part or an invasive mechanical test.
