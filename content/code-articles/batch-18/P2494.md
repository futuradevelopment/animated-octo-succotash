# P2494: Low EGR bypass feedback is different from a closed valve

P2494 identifies low electrical feedback from the high-pressure EGR cooler-bypass position sensor in the cited GM system. The monitor evaluates raw analog voltage as a percentage of the 5 V reference. It is not simply saying that the valve has reached its closed mechanical position.

## Establish whether the feedback remains in range

The low threshold is below 1% of the reference. The neighboring high-range monitor, P2495, uses the opposite end of that scale. This distinction matters when a scan tool also displays a calculated valve percentage: a converted position value can conceal that the underlying electrical signal is outside its valid range.

Monitoring must be enabled by calibration, cranking must have ended, and the powertrain-relay supply must exceed 11 V. A signal observed during cranking or inadequate supply does not reproduce this particular running check.

## Use the sample history, not one snapshot

GM specifies 200 failed observations out of 250 samples and a 6.25 ms function task. The sample decision and two-trip reporting describe different levels of fault confirmation. Do not interpret the trip count as a sensor-response time.

For verification, retain the raw feedback, reference context and supply state together. If the feedback is electrically invalid, a mechanical-position conclusion should wait until the signal can be interpreted reliably. The table does not locate a grounded wire or establish that the sensor itself requires replacement.
