# U0688: Throttle-position sensor G SENT communication failure

U0688 is lost communication with throttle-position sensor or switch G in the generic dictionary. The cited GM implementation pairs it with U0608 and describes throttle-position sensor 2 on bank 2. These are source-specific logical identities; neither name alone establishes a physical connector pin or authorizes treating the code as an accelerator-pedal fault.

## Three signal conditions can cause communication failure

The SENT communication check can fail because no signal exists on the line, because a message pulse length is outside its allowed range, or because the calibration pulse is outside its allowed range. The table specifies 0.13 seconds and one-trip behavior for this communication check.

That is different from invalid received data. Adjacent U136E/U136F rows examine SENT driver monitoring status and use a different duration. It is also different from a throttle-position voltage being outside its permitted range. Preserve which of these observations the controller actually reported.

## Interpret the paired channels and operating state

The document includes related shared-code entries for the two bank 2 sensor channels. Their presence does not establish that both channels independently failed. The matching diagnostic subtype and live interface status are needed to locate the affected path.

The enable logic includes DRIVE or POSTDRIVE controller state, safety fuel-cutoff status, adequate voltage for throttle operation, and limp-home-position conditions. Additional inhibit and enable tables apply. Capture those states with the fault record before clearing it. A communication fault does not, by itself, prove that the throttle plate is mechanically stuck or that the complete throttle assembly requires replacement.
