# P2E82: Cold-start pump overspeed produces a negative tracking error

P2E82 concerns the coolant pump reporting more speed than requested during cold-start low-flow operation. GM forms command minus feedback, so an overspeed condition appears as a negative error. The sign is important: a large positive deficit belongs to an underspeed interpretation instead.

## Verify the stable-demand comparison

The failure criterion is a command-minus-feedback difference below −200 rpm. The strategy describes filtering that difference and requires eight seconds of failure within a ten-second window. A brief overshoot after a rapid request change is not the same evidence.

The commanded speed must change by less than 50 rpm for at least three seconds. Low Flow or Low Flow Autostop mode and catalyst warm-up must be enabled. A normal check performed after the warm-up strategy ends does not necessarily repeat the relevant diagnostic opportunity.

## Confirm that feedback can support the inference

The row includes engine-inlet temperature eligibility and pump-speed fault exclusions. If speed reporting is invalid, its numerical disagreement should not be treated as proof of actual mechanical overspeed. Preserve electrical and feedback statuses alongside the error trace.

A useful investigation retains requested speed, measured speed, operating mode and duration. The monitor does not directly detect air in the circuit, establish coolant flow or identify a defective motor. Vehicle-specific verification must distinguish real response behavior from feedback or control problems before a repair is selected.
