# P27F2: Range-position circuit high is not a request for a higher gear

P27F2 concerns excessive raw voltage on the transmission mode-valve B position-sensor circuit. Its high designation is electrical and has no direct meaning of a higher gear or a more open hydraulic valve. The cited GM diagnostic classifies the condition as a short toward voltage.

## Compare the raw signal with its own boundary

The failure limit is above 2.538 V in the documented internal electronic range-selection arrangement. P27EF supervises a separate invalid middle band, while P27F1 supervises the low end. These code distinctions are more informative than simply reporting that a range value looks implausible.

Battery voltage must exceed 8 V for more than one second and the diagnostic must be enabled. The source labels the implementation for GF9 and CVT systems; that scope cannot be extended to another transmission solely because the scanner displays the same generic code.

## Determine whether the high value persisted

The monitor requires 0.100 second within a 0.163-second observation period, with a 6.25 ms update. Retaining the signal trace and enable state is therefore more useful than comparing isolated readings taken before and after a range change.

The classification does not locate the source of unwanted voltage or establish that the position sensor itself failed. A matching circuit procedure is needed to separate wiring, supply interaction and controller interpretation before making a component decision.
