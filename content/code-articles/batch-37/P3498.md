# P3498: Cylinder 1 may fail to return from deactivation

P3498 identifies cylinder 1 deactivation/intake-valve performance. Mazda's SKYACTIV-G procedure describes a number 1 valve stuck closed after the engine returns from cylinder-deactivation operation.

## The PCM infers the problem from crankshaft behavior

The monitor evaluates excessive crankshaft speed fluctuation over 200 crankshaft rotations after recovery from deactivation. It uses valid operating conditions, including completed crankshaft tolerance learning, a stabilized engine and no fuel cut. It is not a direct electrical test of one valve wire.

Mazda warns of possible engine damage and lists rough idle, poor acceleration and stalling. Its protective actions limit intake air and can cut fuel to the affected cylinder to avoid fuel accumulating behind a closed valve. This is a reason for prompt professional assessment, not repeated attempts to reproduce the fault on the road.

## Do not jump directly to internal engine repair

The factory diagnosis checks ignition and injection, the deactivation oil-control valves, switchable hydraulic lash adjusters and the oil solenoid system. It also considers unreliable sensor inputs, intake leakage, belt or tensioner disturbances, compression loss and internal damage.

These alternatives matter because the conclusion is inferred from rotational behavior. A cylinder 1 combustion failure can disturb the same signal. Verification follows an appropriate repair and controlled comparison with the captured conditions; it is not an instruction to keep driving a rough-running engine until the code returns.
