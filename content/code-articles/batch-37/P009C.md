# P009C: Fuel relief-valve low input has operating and closed-valve branches

P009C identifies a fuel-pressure relief control circuit low condition. Mazda's SKYACTIV-D monitor uses two electrical checks rather than one fixed observation of rail pressure.

## Which state failed

One branch evaluates the drive-current control switch during valve operation. Another evaluates low downstream drive-circuit voltage while the relief valve is stopped and closed. Both use a 10–16 V battery condition. The source does not publish the individual current or voltage limits.

The circuit can be grounded, powered incorrectly, open or shorted between its positive and negative control paths. Mazda consequently checks both conductors and their relationship, as well as the valve and PCM connections. A low code does not establish that fuel pressure is physically too low.

## Protective changes can be broader than the valve

The documented responses restrict torque and maximum speed, inhibit DENOx/DESOx and EGR control, and inhibit i-stop. These may appear as several unavailable engine functions even though the initiating fault belongs to the relief-valve drive system.

The factory component inspection follows the external circuit checks. Confirmation requires the relevant warmed-engine operating cycle and a pending-code inspection. Common-rail fuel equipment demands professional pressure-control precautions; this code is not a basis for opening a line or manually powering the valve. Preserve whether the event occurred during operation or the closed-state check whenever that evidence is available.
