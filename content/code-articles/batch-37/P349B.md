# P349B: Cylinder 4 valve-performance fault after deactivation ends

P349B is the cylinder 4 deactivation/intake-valve performance code. Mazda's factory page pairs it with the cylinder 1 monitor but specifically assigns P349B to a number 4 valve that remains closed after deactivation ends.

## Understand the detection context

The PCM looks for excessive crankshaft fluctuation across 200 rotations after recovery from cylinder deactivation. The stated prerequisites include a learned crankshaft tolerance, stable engine operation, coolant at least 20 °C and no active fuel cut. A roughness event outside those conditions is not automatically the same monitor result.

The source describes a risk of engine damage if oil or fuel accumulates while a valve remains closed. It lists intake-air limitation and affected-cylinder fuel cut as protective actions. Rough idle, stalling or poor acceleration can accompany the fault, so professional evaluation should precede further operation intended merely to test it.

## Separate the cylinder fault from the inference

Mazda's diagnostic tree includes the cylinder-deactivation oil-control system and switchable lash adjusters, but also ignition, fuel injection and erroneous sensor information. Belt/tensioner disturbance, intake leakage, compression loss and coolant entering a combustion chamber are additional branches.

The number 4 assignment directs cylinder-specific investigation; it does not prove a particular valve or lash adjuster is defective. A supported repair conclusion needs the relevant combustion, oil-control or mechanical evidence. Post-repair verification uses the captured operating context under safe workshop supervision rather than code erasure alone.
