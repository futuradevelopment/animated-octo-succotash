# P009B: Diesel fuel-pressure relief valve control-circuit fault

P009B generally denotes the fuel-pressure relief control circuit or an open circuit. Mazda's SKYACTIV-D page evaluates the operation of the valve's drive-current control switch while the relief valve is being operated.

## The electrical operating state matters

The listed battery-voltage window is 10–16 V. Within that condition, an operating amount at or below the specified limit can trigger the fault. The page does not publish the numerical current-control limit, so the code cannot be converted into a universal resistance or voltage specification.

Mazda lists both positive and negative control paths. Either path can have an open, a short to ground or a short to power. Connections at the valve and PCM, an internal valve fault and the PCM are also included. The generic “open” label therefore does not mean an open wire is the only supported explanation.

## Verify the circuit before the valve

The factory sequence evaluates connections and both control conductors before the component inspection. It inhibits i-stop engine stopping as the stated response. It does not establish that rail pressure is safe merely because no additional fail-safe is listed.

Repair confirmation uses a warmed-engine operating cycle that exercises the relief-control system, followed by inspection for recurrence. This is professional common-rail work: do not loosen a fuel connection to test pressure relief or improvise an energizing test. The source's driving cycle must be performed only in lawful, controlled conditions appropriate to the vehicle.
