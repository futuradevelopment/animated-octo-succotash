# P01C5: Low pressure-sensor signal from the number 4 diesel injector assembly

P01C5 identifies fuel pressure sensor A circuit low. Mazda's SKYACTIV-D page locates this sensor inside the number 4 fuel injector assembly, an important distinction from a separate rail-mounted sensor assumption.

## What the PCM detects

The output must remain at or below 0.37 V for 0.7 seconds, after the specified ignition-on delay and with battery voltage at least 8 V. The engine may be off or running. This is an electrical threshold, not a direct finding of inadequate fuel pressure.

The listed causes include grounded supply or signal wiring, a signal-to-ground short, an open supply, connector defects and an injector/sensor assembly fault. Mazda checks the injector and PCM connections and these external circuit paths before the integrated sensor inspection.

## Component handling is part of correct diagnosis

The source specifically warns that touching injector terminals or bringing certain high-voltage-generating devices near the injector can damage it. Testing therefore needs the exact professional procedure; generic probing habits can introduce another fault.

No dedicated fail-safe is listed. That does not certify fuel-system operation or identify the required replacement. Confirmation includes one minute of ignition on with the engine off, followed by one minute after starting and another DTC inspection. Retain the injector number and the observed electrical cause so a low circuit signal is not confused with a mechanical fuel-pressure problem.
