# P0B17: Hybrid battery output monitor above its allowed voltage

P0B17 is the high-input counterpart of the hybrid battery voltage-sense B code. Mazda's M Hybrid application monitors its 24-volt circuit at battery terminal 2A.

## A pack-output limit with a relay precondition

The documented threshold is more than 40 V for three seconds in READY, with the internal battery relay on and P0A1F:09 absent. The measurement concerns the pack output monitor, not an individual battery cell or the conventional 12-volt battery.

That relay condition is essential. A reading taken in an unrelated battery state cannot be judged as if the same monitor had run. Mazda lists an M Hybrid battery malfunction as the possible cause and i-stop inhibition as the stated fail-safe for this page.

## Confirm whether it is a present event

The diagnostic flow separates current and historical records, rechecks after an ignition transition and considers P0AE3. Its repeatability guidance also notes that vertical vehicle motion can expose the condition, so an intermittent record may need its original operating context understood.

Professional diagnosis should preserve the full battery status and accompanying precharge codes before clearing them. The threshold does not authorize opening the pack, bypassing its relay or applying an external voltage to reproduce the event. Confirmation must use the exact service procedure and safe operating conditions, establishing whether the high-output report actually returns in the qualifying battery state.
