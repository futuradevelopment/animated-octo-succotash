# P0039: Air bypass valve position does not follow its target

P0039 concerns air bypass valve control range/performance. In Mazda's SKYACTIV-X engine, the PCM compares the valve's actual opening with the opening it has commanded.

## A tracking error can come from either side

The actual angle can be too large or too small for a sustained period. The page does not provide a numerical allowable angle difference or duration. It does require battery voltage above 11 V and both the number 2 intake-air and boost-air temperature readings at least 20 °C, with specified valve and temperature circuit faults absent.

The PCM disables boost and stops EGR control when this fault is detected. Those responses explain why an air-bypass problem can affect functions beyond the valve itself.

## Separate actuator movement from feedback

Mazda watches AIR_BYPASS_VLV_POS_ACT while evaluating the valve-sensor and PCM connections. It then checks the sensor supply, signal and ground alongside the actuator's positive and negative control circuits. A damaged position circuit can falsely suggest poor movement; a damaged actuator circuit can prevent movement despite correct feedback.

Only after the electrical paths are assessed does the flow inspect the valve and position sensor individually. Confirmation includes warming coolant to at least 80 °C and the specified ignition-on, engine-off condition. A cold check that does not satisfy the monitor prerequisites is weak evidence that the tracking fault has been resolved.
