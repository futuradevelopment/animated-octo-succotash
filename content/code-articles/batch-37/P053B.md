# P053B: Crankcase heater supply remains low when its relay is commanded on

P053B generally identifies PCV heater A control circuit low. Mazda's SKYACTIV-D procedure calls the system the blow-by heater and compares heater supply voltage with the commanded relay state.

## The relay must be on for this check

The monitor requires battery voltage at least 8 V, at least half a second after ignition on, and the heater relay commanded on. The monitored supply then remains below specification for five seconds. The page does not publish a numerical heater-voltage threshold.

Mazda lists the relay, F10 15 A fuse, relay supply, heater power path and monitor circuit. A relay-control wire shorted to power is also included. That combination shows why the code should not be reduced to a failed heating element: the PCM may be unable to turn on or observe the supply correctly.

## Check delivery and monitoring separately

The factory flow assesses the relay and fuse before the various power, control and monitor paths, then the heater itself. A good fuse does not establish that the relay closes, and a powered heater circuit does not establish that its monitor signal reaches the PCM correctly.

No dedicated fail-safe is listed. Repair verification uses the specified key-on or running self-test and a pending-code check. Keep the commanded-on state with the voltage evidence, because a low heater voltage when the relay is intentionally off is a different observation.
