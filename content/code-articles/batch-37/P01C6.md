# P01C6: High pressure-sensor signal in the number 4 injector

P01C6 describes fuel pressure sensor A circuit high input. In the documented Mazda diesel system, the pressure sensor is integrated with fuel injector number 4.

## A high voltage can have an open-circuit cause

The PCM threshold is at least 4.15 V for 0.7 seconds after its ignition-on delay, with battery voltage at least 8 V. Mazda lists a signal short to supply, a short between signal and sensor supply, and an open signal or ground circuit. The same high reading therefore does not uniquely identify excessive fuel pressure or a powered signal wire.

The diagnostic sequence checks injector and PCM connections before separating these cross-circuit and continuity possibilities. Only then does it evaluate the integrated pressure sensor. The component location changes the appropriate service information and parts decision, but is not proof that the whole injector must be replaced on the DTC alone.

## Protect the injector during investigation

Mazda warns against touching injector terminals and exposing the injector to nearby high-voltage-generating equipment. Qualified diagnosis should follow those handling instructions alongside common-rail fuel precautions.

The completion check includes one minute with ignition on and engine off, then one minute after starting, followed by code inspection. Preserve the original high reading and any companion fuel or communication faults. A clean record immediately after erasure is weaker evidence than a completed check under the specified electrical conditions.
