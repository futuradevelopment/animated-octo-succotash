# P213B: Low-pressure EGR motor needs excessive control duty

P213B is cataloged as EGR throttle control circuit B range/performance. Mazda's SKYACTIV-D procedure specifically identifies the DC motor control system for the low-pressure EGR valve.

## What high duty tells the controller

During feedback control, a valve duty of at least 90% for two seconds triggers the documented range/performance decision. Related motor-control, position-sensor and PCM-control faults must be absent. This is a high control-effort condition, not a direct measurement of excessive exhaust flow.

The fault list includes valve and PCM connections, a control circuit shorted to power or ground, the LP-EGR valve and the PCM. The procedure checks the connections and both short-circuit directions before evaluating the valve. High duty therefore does not establish mechanical sticking without the corresponding electrical and component evidence.

## Several restrictions can follow one valve fault

Mazda restricts torque and maximum speed, inhibits DENOx/DESOx, compulsory DPF regeneration and EGR control, and inhibits i-stop. Those responses can create a broad set of unavailable functions from one initiating control issue.

Repair verification uses the specified key-on, engine-off self-test and checks for the same pending DTC. Do not use disabled regeneration as a reason to force a regeneration attempt before the EGR fault is addressed. Keep the LP-EGR assignment explicit; the generic circuit-B label alone does not identify this valve on every diesel engine.
