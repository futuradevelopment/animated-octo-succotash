# P006A: Airflow and manifold-pressure estimates disagree

P006A identifies a MAP-to-MAF correlation problem. Mazda's SKYACTIV-X PCM compares air quantity estimated from manifold pressure with air quantity measured by the mass-airflow sensor.

## A disagreement does not name the bad sensor

The monitor applies at 500 rpm or above, outside acceleration and deceleration, with battery voltage at least 8 V and specified related DTCs absent. The source does not publish its allowable difference. A brief mismatch during a throttle transition therefore should not be judged against an invented steady-state threshold.

Mazda's cause list includes improper installation of the MAF, MAP or boost-pressure sensing assemblies, faulty connectors and defective sensors. It also includes intake leakage, purge-valve or PCV faults, and a non-genuine air cleaner or cover. The air path itself can explain disagreement even when each sensor's wiring is intact.

## How the factory diagnosis broadens the check

The sequence assesses installation and connectors before unit inspections, then investigates leakage and the purge and PCV systems. This prevents a correlation code from becoming an automatic MAF replacement recommendation.

The procedure first distinguishes a repeatable concern from an intermittent one using a prescribed warmed-engine test. Its completion check includes an engine start and an ignition-off wait of at least fifteen seconds before code review. Use the exact safe workshop procedure; a generic idle reading alone cannot establish that the modeled and measured air quantities agree throughout the relevant conditions.
