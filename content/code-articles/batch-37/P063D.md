# P063D: ISG voltage measurements disagree with an internal high signal

P063D identifies generator voltage-sense circuit A high. Mazda's M Hybrid ISG compares two internal measurements of the same supply terminal before classifying the high-input fault.

## Two different voltage quantities

The documented conditions include an absolute difference of at least 5 V between the main microcomputer's measured supply and the stator-driver control IC's measurement, together with an internal input signal of at least 4.9 V. The determination period is half a second and the stator driver must otherwise be normal.

The 4.9 V value is a sensing-signal limit, not the generator's normal operating supply. Confusing these quantities could lead to a completely wrong external charging-system test. The disagreement establishes why the controller distrusts the measurement, rather than directly proving an actual supply overvoltage.

## Current and historical faults are separated

Mazda lists an ISG malfunction, then checks whether the DTC is present, repeats an ignition-state check and assesses recurrence using recorded conditions. It also recognizes communication noise as a possible contributor to a displayed record.

The response removes hybrid supply and relies on the 12-volt battery while inhibiting i-stop, regeneration and assistance. Continued operation can deplete that battery. Internal ISG measurements require the exact professional procedure; the article's values are monitor criteria, not instructions for opening or probing the generator. Retain both module status and snapshot evidence before a replacement decision.
