# P06A2: Variable compressor valve control circuit high

P06A2 is a high-input fault in the variable A/C compressor control circuit. Mazda monitors the compressor valve's control voltage against the PCM command and requires the mismatch to persist for five continuous seconds.

## A different fault from poor cooling alone

A warm cabin does not establish P06A2, and P06A2 does not by itself establish a refrigerant shortage. This monitor evaluates the electrical control of the valve built into the A/C compressor. Mazda lists the valve connector, PCM connection, a short to supply in the control wire, the internal valve and the PCM as possible causes.

Its high-input sequence concentrates on the connectors and the control circuit's exposure to supply voltage. This differs from a missing valve power feed: the documented condition is that control voltage is too high for the command being issued.

## Avoid an early assembly diagnosis

The factory flow places external circuit inspection ahead of its compressor-valve repeatability decision. A harness fault that holds the command line high can resemble an internal valve fault at the controller, so circuit integrity changes whether the compressor is a reasonable repair candidate.

Confirmation requires a cooling demand, maximum-cold A/C operation and observation of cold vent air, followed by inspection for the same pending code. Persistent faults also pass through communication and repeat-diagnosis checks before a PCM decision. The manual lists no separate fail-safe here; it does not provide evidence that an affected A/C system will still deliver normal cooling.
