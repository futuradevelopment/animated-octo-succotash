# P0647: A/C clutch relay control stays electrically high

P0647 reports an A/C clutch relay control circuit high condition. In Mazda's SKYACTIV-G procedure, the PCM compares compressor magnetic-clutch control voltage with its own control signal for five continuous seconds.

## High voltage does not mean high refrigerant pressure

The code concerns an electrical relay-control discrepancy. The factory fail-safe releases the compressor clutch. An inoperative A/C system may therefore reflect deliberate control shutdown as well as the underlying electrical problem.

The documented cause list is focused: a faulty relay, PCM connector or terminals, a control-circuit short to supply, or a PCM malfunction. The high-input branch does not use the fuse and open-feed checks that appear in the paired low-input procedure. That distinction keeps the diagnosis centered on why the commanded circuit remains high.

## What changes the next decision

Mazda evaluates the A/C relay first, then the PCM connection, then whether the relay control circuit is shorted to a power source. The presence of a high circuit reading by itself cannot establish an internal PCM defect; the external relay and wire can hold the same circuit at an incorrect potential.

After correction, the workshop verification calls for maximum-cold A/C operation, cold-air confirmation at the vent and another PCM DTC inspection. If the pending fault returns, the manual includes communication checks and repetition of diagnosis before its final controller branch. Keep the circuit conclusion separate from any additional refrigerant-system diagnosis.
