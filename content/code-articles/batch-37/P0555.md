# P0555: Brake booster vacuum sensor signal outside its electrical range

P0555 concerns the brake booster pressure-sensing circuit. Mazda's SKYACTIV-G procedure calls the component the power brake unit vacuum sensor and monitors its signal whenever the ignition is on.

## What the monitor detects

The documented PCM sets this fault when the signal stays below 0.15 V or above 4.8 V for five seconds. Either electrical extreme can produce the same code. This makes the actual stored voltage important: P0555 alone does not establish low brake vacuum or identify which wire has failed. The listed response is inhibition of i-stop engine stopping.

## How the evidence directs diagnosis

Mazda checks the sensor connector, its supply and signal circuits for grounding faults, and the PCM connector before assessing shorts to power, cross-circuit shorts and opens. Supply, signal and ground integrity all matter; replacing the vacuum sensor cannot correct a damaged supply wire. The sensor itself is evaluated after those circuit checks.

Because this measurement belongs to brake assistance, inspection and any brake-unit work require the vehicle-specific professional procedure. An electrical code is not evidence that braking performance is acceptable. Repair confirmation includes the specified key-on or running self-test, a check for recurrence as a pending fault, and review of other DTCs. Preserve the original signal and operating-state evidence before clearing memory.
