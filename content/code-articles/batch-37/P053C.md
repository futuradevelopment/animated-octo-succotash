# P053C: Crankcase heater voltage stays high after the relay is switched off

P053C is the PCV-heater high-input code. In Mazda's SKYACTIV-D implementation, it refers to the blow-by heater supply staying above specification while the relay is commanded off.

## Command state defines the failure

The preconditions include battery voltage between 8 and 16 V and at least half a second after ignition on. The high supply must continue for five seconds. A heater receiving voltage while intentionally energized is not the same condition; the disagreement occurs during the commanded-off state.

The source lists a faulty relay, a relay-control circuit short to ground, powered heater or monitor wiring, connector trouble and a heater or PCM malfunction. A grounded control wire can keep a relay energized, so the high-input label does not imply that only the heater feed itself is shorted to power.

## Establish why the supply remains present

Mazda inspects the relay and PCM connection, then the relay control path and heater-side connection and supply before evaluating the heater. This helps distinguish a relay that remains active from unwanted power reaching the output circuit elsewhere.

The page lists no separate fail-safe. Confirmation uses the applicable self-test and another pending-code inspection. Preserve the original relay command and monitored supply relationship; merely observing heat or battery voltage at the heater without the command context cannot confirm this specific fault or prove its repair.
