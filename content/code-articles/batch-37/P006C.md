# P006C: MAP and boost pressure disagree during a no-boost condition

P006C describes disagreement between manifold absolute pressure and boost pressure. Mazda's SKYACTIV-X monitor deliberately checks this comparison under conditions where boost is not being produced.

## Why the valve position matters

The listed prerequisites are engine speed no higher than 3,000 rpm, battery voltage at least 8 V, and a no-boost state represented by an air bypass valve opening of at least 90%. The controller then compares the two measured pressures against its specified allowable difference, which this page does not publish.

These conditions are important to interpretation. Pressure differences observed during active boosting are not automatically evidence of this particular monitor failure. The factory fail-safe disables the boost system and stops EGR control.

## Diagnose the measurements separately

Mazda lists the MAP sensor, boost pressure sensor, their connections and the PCM. Its sequence inspects the MAP/IAT number 2 connector, boost-pressure/boost-temperature connector and PCM connection before testing the two pressure sensors. The DTC does not choose which sensor is inaccurate.

The relevant repair evidence is a trustworthy comparison in the defined no-boost state, not merely finding that both sensors display some pressure. Completion includes starting the engine, switching it off for at least fifteen seconds and checking for recurrence under the prescribed procedure. The documented test is specific to SKYACTIV-X and should not be transferred unchanged to another engine's turbocharger or sensor arrangement.
