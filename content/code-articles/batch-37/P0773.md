# P0773: The CW6A-EL on/off solenoid electrical circuit

P0773 is commonly labeled shift solenoid E electrical. Mazda's CW6A-EL diagnostic page identifies its component as the on/off solenoid rather than assigning it a universal E location.

## A circuit diagnosis with a specific serviceable component

The TCM evaluates the circuit with battery voltage at least 10.5 V. The listed possible causes are a short or open circuit and an on/off solenoid malfunction. The source does not provide a test resistance or identify a particular stuck gear from the DTC alone.

Emergency mode is activated, and learning, manual mode, neutral-idle control, i-stop and AAS are inhibited. These control responses can explain changes in transmission operation while leaving the underlying fault specifically electrical.

## Distinguish this repair route from neighboring solenoid codes

The factory sequence records the snapshot and considers related service information. Its unresolved branch calls for replacement of the on/off solenoid. In contrast to an assumption that every solenoid code requires the same assembly, the applicable Mazda component procedure matters here.

Verification consists of ignition-on operation with the engine off or on and another TCM DTC inspection. Any additional codes follow their own diagnostic routes. Before selecting parts, confirm the CW6A-EL application and exact on/off-solenoid service information. P0773 by itself does not establish internal clutch damage, and this source does not justify replacing the complete transaxle solely on the code.
