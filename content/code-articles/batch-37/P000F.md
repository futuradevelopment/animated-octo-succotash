# P000F: Excessive measured fuel pressure in the SKYACTIV-X system

P000F is cataloged as fuel-system overpressure relief valve activated. This Mazda page does not independently establish that a relief valve activated. Mazda's SKYACTIV-X page describes a high-pressure fuel-control malfunction and bases its decision on the high fuel pressure sensor.

## What the published monitor checks

The documented threshold is 93 MPa or more for five seconds, with a 2 MPa hysteresis. The explanation says determination continues until pressure falls to 91 MPa or below. These unusually high values belong to this exact source and engine system; they are not general test targets for gasoline injection equipment.

The monitor requires engine speed of at least 500 rpm and battery voltage of at least 8 V. Certain fuel-pressure and pump-control DTCs must be absent. That makes the complete code set important before treating the overpressure finding as a stand-alone pump fault.

## Pressure control and pressure information

The PCM uses measured pressure to regulate the high-pressure pump. The source lists both the pump and pressure sensor, plus the PCM, as possible causes. Its connector check observes FUEL_PRES for an intermittent signal before unit inspections of the pump and sensor.

Fuel cut is the listed protective action. Do not open a pressurized fuel connection, attempt to force the system to the stated limit, or treat a reset as a repair. Professional diagnosis must establish whether pressure is genuinely excessive or incorrectly reported, then perform the specified confirmation under the applicable fuel-system safety procedure.
