# P009F: Insufficient fuel-pressure reduction in Mazda diesel relief control

P009F is cataloged as fuel-pressure relief control stuck on. Mazda's SKYACTIV-D source uses different wording: relief valve malfunction, stuck closed, detected when the expected pressure reduction is insufficient.

## Keep the application-specific meaning explicit

The monitor runs during relief-valve operation with the pump not discharging fuel and the injectors in fuel cut. Battery voltage must be 10–16 V and fuel temperature at least 8.85 °C. Related pressure, relief-control, suction-control and communication faults must be absent. These conditions isolate the pressure-reduction event; they do not prove that the same code means a physically closed valve on every vehicle.

The source does not publish the required pressure drop or exact sustained duration. It identifies the relief valve, suction control valve and PCM as possible causes. Inadequate reduction can therefore require investigation of fuel supply control as well as the relief component.

## Confirm the pressure-response cause safely

Mazda checks related DTCs, evaluates the relief valve and then the suction control valve. The listed protective action inhibits i-stop. Confirmation uses a warmed operating cycle and another fault check.

This article retains the dictionary/source wording difference rather than silently equating “stuck on” and “stuck closed.” Professional diagnosis must use the exact vehicle procedure and pressure data. Never loosen a common-rail connection to investigate whether the valve releases pressure.
