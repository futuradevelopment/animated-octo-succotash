# P2712: CW6A-EL hydraulic support during i-stop

P2712 is cataloged as hydraulic power unit leakage. Mazda's CW6A-EL source describes an oil-pump shift valve that is stuck, detected through missing pressure-switch confirmation during i-stop.

## Monitor the stopped-engine hydraulic event

The documented condition is oil pressure switch number 1 remaining off for ten seconds while the electric AT oil pump operates during i-stop, with D or N selected. The pressure switch, low clutch and range sensor must be normal, and the rate-of-speed-change condition must be satisfied.

This is a hydraulic support test during an engine-stop state. It is not a direct observation of fluid leaking outside the transaxle. Mazda lists the hydraulic passage switching valve in the control valve body and the electric AT oil pump as possible causes, then inhibits i-stop.

## Why an ordinary idle check may miss it

The confirmation sequence includes driving in second gear or higher and subsequent i-stop operation, repeated under the specified conditions. A vehicle that never enters i-stop has not reproduced the relevant event merely because it idles without a code.

The factory unresolved-fault branch addresses the control valve body and electric pump. That direction should be applied only after confirming the exact transaxle, service information and repeatability. The general leakage label and Mazda's stuck-valve wording are retained as an applicability distinction, not silently treated as proof of an external hydraulic leak.
