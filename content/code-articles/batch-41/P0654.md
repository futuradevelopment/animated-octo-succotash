# P0654: GM engine-speed output fails its driver-state comparison

P0654 in the cited GM procedure concerns the controller's engine-speed output. The PCM produces pulses by switching a ground path at the frequency of its engine-speed input, then monitors the output voltage. The diagnostic asks whether the electrical result agrees with the output driver's commanded state.

## A running engine does not verify this output

The listed enable conditions are engine speed above 400 rpm and ignition voltage between 6 and 18 V, excluding the boundaries as printed. A mismatch between requested driver state and actual circuit state must persist for at least five seconds.

Because the monitor evaluates an outgoing circuit, the engine can supply speed information internally while that information is not reproduced correctly on the output path. The code alone therefore does not establish a failed crankshaft sensor or identify a particular display as the cause.

## Interpret history separately from a present failure

GM records the operating conditions when the diagnostic fails. Its diagnostic aids emphasize intermittent connections and connector protection, including sealing against moisture. A current fault can become history after the condition disappears; the history record has a separate warmup-cycle clearing policy.

A later normal output observation should be compared with those recorded conditions rather than used to dismiss the earlier event. The indexed procedure must be matched to the actual controller before electrical testing. It does not supply a universal pin, signal destination or permission to replace the PCM from P0654 alone.
