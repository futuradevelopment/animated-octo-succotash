# P0945: The hydraulic-pump relay control path must be checked directly

## The relay command is the subject of this fault

On the MR2 sequential manual transmission, P0945 concerns the hydraulic pump relay circuit. Toyota's detection table refers to absence of the MTRL output signal and an open or short in that circuit. Its listed trouble areas are the motor relay, the MTRL wiring and the transmission control ECU. This is a narrower electrical investigation than the separate code for abnormal hydraulic-pressure behavior.

A pump that does not run can be the visible symptom, but that observation alone cannot distinguish an interrupted relay command from a failed pump motor. Preserve the code and freeze-frame information before replacing a hydraulic assembly.

## The factory sequence tests switching, then wiring

The first step checks the relay identified by its HPU marking. The component procedure compares contact continuity in the unenergized and energized states. A technician should use the correct terminal map and a protected, isolated test setup; bridging the relay socket in the vehicle is not equivalent to this inspection.

If the relay passes, the next step checks the harness and connectors between the transmission ECU's MTRL terminal and the relay for an open or short. The final ECU branch follows those checks. An audible relay click alone does not establish that its controlled contacts and command path meet the inspection requirements.

## Verify commanded operation after the repair

Keep the electrical finding attached to the repair record: defective switching contacts, an interrupted command path or another demonstrated fault. After restoring the circuit, verify normal commanded pump behavior through the applicable sequential-transmission procedure. Observe the hydraulic system's stored-pressure precautions before any access. P0945 does not authorize opening an accumulator or replacing the entire HPU merely because its pump had stopped.
