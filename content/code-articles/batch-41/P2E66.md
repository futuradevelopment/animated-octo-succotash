# P2E66: GM valve E position tracking fails after the target stabilizes

The HEAT-ORV entry for P2E66 in GM's 24OBDG03A Part 1 describes engine coolant bypass valve E failing to follow its desired position. GM frames the detected behavior as a slow or stuck part, but measures it through commanded-versus-actual position rather than assuming a mechanical cause in advance.

## A sustained error under controlled conditions

The listed deviation must exceed 12 degrees and occupy eight seconds of a ten-second observation window. Before that comparison is meaningful, closed-loop position control must be active, the desired-position gradient must be stable, and the anti-sticking routine must be inactive.

Those requirements matter when interpreting actuator data. A transient difference during a commanded transition is not automatically the sustained, stabilized error described by this monitor. Similarly, observations made during an anti-sticking routine should not be treated as though the normal tracking comparison were enabled.

## Verify eligibility before judging the result

The row requires an enabled engine diagnostic system, valid run/crank ignition conditions and an engine that is not cranking. Its specified position-sensor fault conditions must be absent. The coolant-related branch uses a valid engine-outlet reading above minus 39 °C or the listed OBD coolant-enable criterion.

The publication does not give every application for this individual row, a physical valve location or a connector test. Matching the actual vehicle's valve-E strategy is therefore necessary before using these thresholds. This monitor shares tracking logic with other valve entries, but the letter still identifies the monitored channel; it does not make the assemblies interchangeable. GM assigns Type B, two-trip indication to this documented fault.
