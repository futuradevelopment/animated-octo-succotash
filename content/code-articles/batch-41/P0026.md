# P0026: Bank 1 lift feedback can fail in either direction

## What the performance code compares

For the cited 2011 Impreza 2.5-liter SOHC system, P0026 concerns bank 1 variable intake-valve lift. The ECM commands an oil switching solenoid, while a dedicated oil-pressure switch reports whether the mechanism has reached the expected lift mode. Consequently, a solenoid-named performance code can describe disagreement in the feedback path; it does not by itself establish a failed electrical winding.

## Read the duty and switch state together

Subaru separates two failures. With duty at least 62 percent, the pressure switch remaining ON is the low-side failure. With duty below 33 percent, a switch remaining OFF is the high-side failure. Those names describe unsuccessful lift transitions, not generic low and high supply-voltage codes. The low-side condition must persist for 784 milliseconds; the high-side condition requires 3,000 milliseconds.

The test starts no earlier than six seconds after engine start, with battery voltage at least 10.9 volts, engine oil temperature at least 15°C, and variable lift control operating. An observation made before those conditions exist cannot demonstrate that this particular monitor has passed.

## Preserve the failed transition

A useful diagnostic record therefore includes commanded duty, switch status, oil temperature and elapsed running time. The factory normality criteria reverse the expected switch states: OFF at the higher duty and ON at the lower duty. Their qualifying times also differ, at 208 and 3,000 milliseconds respectively. Use the matching bank-specific circuit and hydraulic procedure to investigate the disagreement. Replacing a solenoid solely from the code label discards the monitor's most useful distinction: which requested transition lacked confirming feedback.
