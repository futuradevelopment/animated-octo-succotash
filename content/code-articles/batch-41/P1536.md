# P1536: Cadillac inhibits air conditioning after receiving high coolant temperature

The 2005 Cadillac DeVille P1536 page describes the dash-integration module receiving coolant-temperature information from the PCM over serial data. When that reported temperature is too high, the module withholds the air-conditioning compressor-clutch request. The resulting loss of A/C can be an engine-protection response.

## The temperature message has an upstream source

The PCM derives coolant temperature from a thermistor circuit. If that circuit has the listed low- or high-input fault, the PCM can send a default temperature instead. Interpreting the A/C shutdown therefore requires attention to the engine-control temperature evidence, not only the compressor circuit.

The page gives a setting threshold of at least 121 °C for more than two seconds and a return below 119 °C for clearing the current condition. Those separate boundaries describe hysteresis in the reported-temperature strategy.

## Verify the conflicting descriptor before applying limits

Although the page heading and diagnostic aids identify P1536 and high coolant temperature, its descriptor line names P1540 and refrigerant overpressure. This article retains a source-review flag and does not treat that inconsistent descriptor as a second proven cause.

The supported narrative explains why cooling-system performance, airflow, coolant level and temperature-sensor faults are relevant to repeated A/C interruption. Exact service information must resolve the identifier before numerical acceptance testing. An A/C-off complaint with this history does not automatically justify compressor replacement, and an overheating condition requires appropriate professional investigation.
