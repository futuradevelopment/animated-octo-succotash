# P0812: Reverse indication must agree with both stroke sensors

## A disagreement, not simply an inoperative lamp

In the 2005 MR2 Spyder sequential manual transmission procedure, P0812 means the reverse signal does not correspond to the shift or select signal. Toyota consequently includes both stroke sensors, two back-up light switches, their circuits, the gear-shift actuator and its linkage, and the transmission ECU in the investigation. The back-up lamps alone cannot establish which input is wrong.

## Compare independent indications first

The chart begins by comparing the physical shift/select shaft position, the displayed gear and scan-tool information in Neutral, first, second and Reverse. Agreement sends the technician toward the reverse-switch branch. Disagreement sends the investigation through the stroke-sensor wiring, individual sensor checks and actuator installation. That first comparison prevents a plausible reverse-switch reading from masking an incorrectly reported gear position.

On the reverse branch, Toyota checks the ECU's reverse-switch data and then the wiring between the switches, ignition supply and controller. Both back-up switches receive a separate continuity inspection. The procedure does not treat one switch as a substitute for the other.

## Preserve context and respect the actuator

Save freeze-frame information before erasing the code, including whether the vehicle was moving and whether the engine was warm. Have a technician perform gear-command and actuator work with the vehicle secured; the sequential transmission can move its mechanism under control-system commands.

The useful repair record identifies which indications disagreed and what test restored agreement. A new switch, sensor or ECU is not proof of repair without repeating the relevant comparison. This source applies to the cited sequential system and does not define reverse-input wiring for a conventional automatic transmission.
