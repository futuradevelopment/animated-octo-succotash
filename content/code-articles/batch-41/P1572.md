# P1572: GM traction-active input remains asserted in park or neutral

The 1996 Pontiac Firebird transmission procedure describes a traction-control signal to the PCM that is normally at battery voltage and falls to zero during a traction event. P1572 sets when that active indication persists in park or neutral, where the documented strategy considers it inappropriate.

## Gear-state evidence qualifies the low signal

The source requires park or neutral, no listed fluid-pressure manual-valve position-switch fault, and the traction-active condition for 6.4 seconds. The code is therefore not simply a report that traction control operated during ordinary driving. It evaluates the asserted signal against the transmission's reported state.

The normal traction-active input also influences shifting and converter-clutch operation. Understanding that role helps explain why an electrical signal problem can produce transmission-related symptoms without proving internal transmission damage.

## Some changes follow the controller’s default policy

The procedure describes converter-clutch disengagement when traction is active, inhibited fourth gear in hot mode, frozen shift adaptations and a malfunction-lamp request. These responses can affect the complaint after the code sets; they should be distinguished from an additional mechanical failure.

The diagnostic discussion separates the PCM from the traction-control side to investigate the signal path. That requires the correct professional wiring procedure and should not be replaced by an improvised ground or bypass. Confirm this particular GM application and preserve the recorded operating conditions. P1572 alone does not prove a brake-controller failure or establish that traction-control functions remain available.
