# P1278: GM accelerator-pedal channel 1 exceeds its high-input limit

The cited GM P1278 procedure sets a high-input boundary above 4.75 V for accelerator-pedal channel 1. Its position table shows that normal full pedal travel produces a substantially lower channel-1 voltage. The fault should not be explained as merely a driver pressing the accelerator all the way down.

## Electrical range and requested acceleration differ

The three-sensor pedal assembly uses independent signal, reference and low-reference paths for its channels. Channel 1 increases with pedal travel, but that rising relationship does not make every higher voltage a valid greater acceleration request. The diagnostic identifies a value outside its permitted electrical range.

Eligibility includes crank or run, ignition voltage from 7 to 16 V and absence of the listed reference-circuit faults. A test made without confirming those conditions may not reproduce the controller's diagnostic decision. The published setting text uses less than one second and should not be silently rewritten.

## Interpret the fallback from the complete fault set

GM can continue using two remaining sensors when faults are confined to one channel. Some combinations involving multiple channels instead limit response, and all three out of range allow idle only. A guaranteed reduced-power symptom cannot be inferred from P1278 alone.

The procedure records the failure without requesting the malfunction lamp. Preserve those records and establish the applicable channel-1 circuitry before professional testing. An excessive input identifies a condition to investigate; it does not establish the responsible component or justify bypassing the redundant pedal system.
