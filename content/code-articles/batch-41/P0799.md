# P0799: Temperature matters when checking Phaeton regulator N217

## Use the temperature attached to the specification

The Phaeton manual assigns P0799, pressure-control solenoid C intermittent, to pressure control valve 3, N217. Its component procedure lists 6–8 ohms at approximately 20°C and expressly states that resistance increases at higher temperatures. A measurement made immediately after transmission operation therefore needs its temperature context before it can be compared with the reference range.

The factory DTC table does not provide a numerical hot-resistance limit or a current-monitor threshold. Extrapolating a new limit from the cold value would create a specification that this source does not establish. Use current matching service information if a different test temperature or operating test is required.

## Check the connection as well as the winding

The procedure begins with corrosion and water-entry inspection at the transmission's 16-pin connector. N217 has its own terminal mapping in the shared five-regulator table. If its winding meets the stated criteria, Volkswagen continues to the wires leading to the TCM, including a 1.5-ohm maximum continuity check and isolation between circuits.

A normal winding does not prove that the installed circuit remains sound under the conditions of the intermittent event. Conversely, an unsuitable test temperature should not be used to manufacture a failed winding result.

## Verify the actual repair

Preserve fault timing, operating temperature and the circuit results. A technician should perform the specified disconnected-circuit checks with the vehicle secured and the relevant grounds and supplies confirmed. The final decision should identify a demonstrated connector, harness, valve or controller issue. A code reset alone does not resolve the missing explanation for an intermittent pressure-control fault.
