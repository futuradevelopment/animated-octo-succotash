# P044B: EGR position performance is judged at the travel endpoints

## The endpoint comparison is the useful distinction

The cited Mitsubishi P044B procedure concerns EGR sensor C range/performance. Its detection row describes sensor output outside the specified range when the EGR volume control valve is fully closed or fully open. The qualifying delay is at least three seconds. This is a position plausibility finding at the travel endpoints, rather than an EGR-flow measurement or a direct report of insufficient exhaust-gas recirculation.

The listed component failure possibilities include the sensor, internal gear damage and valve movement that is not smooth. Those possibilities explain why the integrated valve assembly is the subject of the linked replacement procedure. The DTC page does not provide a numeric endpoint-voltage range or an invitation to force the valve through its travel by hand.

## Reproduce the proper monitoring opportunity

The confirmation sequence warms the engine, stops it, then switches the ignition on and waits at least three seconds before checking for the DTC. When repeating a previously conducted confirmation, the source specifies a separate ignition preconditioning sequence. A scan made without that operating sequence may not demonstrate that the relevant test has run.

The fail-safe description includes moderated accelerator response and disabled EGR control. Reduced response can therefore be part of the documented protective behavior, rather than proof of a second throttle fault.

## Keep replacement tied to confirmed diagnosis

The linked diagnosis page directs replacement of the EGR volume control valve. Apply that instruction only within the matching vehicle procedure and after confirming the specific P044B event. Preserve the original code and test conditions, use the complete removal/installation instructions, and repeat the prescribed confirmation after repair. Do not substitute an assumed voltage limit from a different EGR sensor.
