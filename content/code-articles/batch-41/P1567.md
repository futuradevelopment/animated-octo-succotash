# P1567: Cadillac cancels cruise control when active braking intervenes

P1567 in the 2000 Cadillac Eldorado procedure records active braking as the reason cruise control was inhibited. The cruise system must be enabled and engaged; the setting condition is the active-braking control system applying the brakes. GM calls this an information diagnostic.

## The code explains an interruption of cruise operation

The cruise module requests engagement, and the PCM uses an inhibit circuit when operating conditions do not permit cruise. In this case, the brake-system intervention supplies the reason for that inhibition. It is different evidence from a failed cruise-switch contact or a driver circuit that cannot follow its command.

A stored P1567 therefore does not establish that the cruise actuator needs replacement. The source directs attention to the conditions surrounding the cancellation and recommends verifying those circumstances with the customer. That history helps distinguish the expected response to braking intervention from an unexplained recurring complaint.

## A history record can remain without a warning lamp

The PCM disables cruise, stores failure-record conditions and does not request the malfunction indicator lamp for this diagnostic. The history record has a separate clearing policy, so its later presence is not proof that active braking is currently being applied.

The cited procedure does not identify the cause of a brake-system intervention or assess braking performance. Any unexplained braking or cruise behavior needs the matching professional diagnosis. Do not bypass the inhibit function or deliberately provoke active braking to reproduce this information code. Confirm the exact Cadillac strategy before applying the interpretation elsewhere.
