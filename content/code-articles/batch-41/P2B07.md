# P2B07: GM distinguishes a cylinder 8 pulse request from injector movement

GM's P2B07 entry in 24OBDG03B Part 1 concerns cylinder 8 injection-pulse performance. Its fault description is specific: a commanded pulse was not delivered because the injector pintle or armature did not move. The monitor uses an electrical signature of that movement rather than directly measuring fuel leaving the nozzle.

## Follow the evidence from the coil

Voltage-flux feedback is measured across the injector enable and command circuits through an analogue-to-digital path. The controller converts the measured response into an opening-magnitude quantity. An opening signature that is absent or violates the calibrated missing-pulse limit supplies the fault evidence.

A supporting table indexes the internal opening-magnitude criterion against rail pressure. The table's numbers are part of that calculated diagnostic quantity. Treating them as a voltage to apply externally would confuse an internal comparison with an electrical service specification.

## Establish that the comparison was eligible

The source names two prerequisites: the small-pulse general diagnostic is enabled, and fuel-pulse voltage-feedback data are ready. It lists continuous monitoring, cylinder-event sampling and a 50-to-100-sample decision. It does not supply enough event-rate information to convert that count into an elapsed-time limit.

GM classifies the entry as Type B, two trips. The neighboring cylinder entries share this strategy, but the stored cylinder identification still matters when matching recorded evidence to the correct circuit. Vehicle-specific service information is required before testing, and injection-system pressure and electrical work need suitable professional equipment. This monitor description alone does not establish the cause or authorize replacement of the cylinder 8 injector.
