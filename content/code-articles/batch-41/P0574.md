# P0574: Cadillac records excessive speed as a reason to cancel cruise control

The 2000 Cadillac Eldorado factory procedure describes P0574 as an information code. With cruise control enabled and engaged, the PCM detects vehicle speed above the calibration limit and inhibits cruise control. The stored code records why cruise operation was cancelled.

## Cancellation is the documented response

The source gives a threshold above 176 km/h, also printed as 110 mph. This is the controller's diagnostic boundary, not a recommended test speed. It should never be reproduced by deliberately accelerating to that speed on a road.

The cruise module requests operation through its engaged circuit, while the PCM can refuse operation through the inhibit circuit. P0574 consequently does not, by itself, demonstrate a broken cruise actuator. The first interpretive question is whether the recorded event matches the conditions under which the driver experienced cancellation.

## Use the record without assuming a component failed

GM states that the malfunction lamp is not requested and that the operating conditions are retained as failure-record data. The diagnostic aids explicitly recommend checking the circumstances with the customer. A history record can therefore remain after the cruise system is no longer experiencing the cancelling condition.

If the reported circumstances do not fit the recorded speed condition, the appropriate professional investigation is the matching vehicle's diagnostic procedure and recorded data. The source does not prove a faulty speed signal, identify a replacement part or establish this threshold for every GM model. Its useful distinction is between a stored cancellation reason and a confirmed electrical or mechanical failure.
