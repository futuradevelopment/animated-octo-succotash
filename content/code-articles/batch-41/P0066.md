# P0066: Subaru air-assist low input requires separate supply and control checks

For the 2002 Subaru Outback 2.5-litre system, P0066 identifies low input in the air-assist injector-solenoid circuit. The factory chart distinguishes the solenoid's power supply from the control wire returning to the ECM. Both paths matter before the valve becomes a replacement candidate.

## A missing feed can resemble a control-side fault

The procedure checks the ECM-side output observation, then examines the solenoid's supply from the main-relay path. Its voltage checks use a greater-than-10 V criterion in the specified ignition-on configuration. That service-test value is not a universal monitor threshold for every Subaru application.

If supply is present, the chart separately tests control-wire continuity and isolation from ground. This creates different branches for an open path, a ground short and poor connector contact. A low reading by itself does not distinguish those possibilities.

## The complete circuit precedes the component decision

The final branch examines contacts at the ECM and valve before selecting a valve replacement. The order shows why replacing the air-assist solenoid without checking its feed and harness can leave the same fault present.

Subaru lists irregular idle or stalling as possible symptoms and two consecutive failing driving cycles for detection. These are source-scoped observations, not guaranteed symptoms. Use the matching professional procedure and connector equipment; do not apply a test voltage to a guessed terminal. The prescribed post-repair inspection mode is needed to verify the system after any justified electrical repair.
