# P0076: Low bank 1 solenoid current despite a substantial command

## Low means insufficient current in this monitor

The 2011 Impreza SOHC factory criteria describe P0076 as an open-circuit diagnosis of the bank 1 oil switching solenoid valve. The important comparison is between output duty and measured control current. The controller has requested meaningful operation, but the current remains too small. This differs from the lift-mode performance monitor, which relies on an oil-pressure switch to confirm the mechanical response.

## The qualifying electrical relationship

With battery voltage at least 10.9 volts, diagnosis runs continuously. The abnormality table specifies duty of at least 30 percent together with control current below 0.026 ampere. Both conditions must remain present for 2,000 milliseconds. Subaru specifies immediate MIL illumination once the malfunction is judged.

These are controller diagnostic criteria, not instructions to apply an external voltage to the solenoid. A technician should preserve the duty/current data and inspect the appropriate bank 1 supply, connector and controlled circuit using the vehicle-specific electrical procedure. Current near zero during an intentionally low command does not meet the stated failure test.

## What establishes a normal response

The normality table does more than ask whether some current has returned. It requires the absolute difference between target current and actual current to be below 0.08 ampere, with target current at least 0.11 ampere, for 2,000 milliseconds. That distinction matters after an intermittent connector repair: an unloaded or barely commanded circuit could look quiet without demonstrating adequate operation.

Use the documented enabled test to verify the repair. Neither the generic circuit-low label nor one stored failure is sufficient evidence of a hydraulic obstruction or a defective cam mechanism.
