# P349D: GM infers cylinder 6 deactivation failure from manifold pressure

P349D is described in GM's 24OBDG03B Part 1 as cylinder 6 failing to deactivate when commanded. The narrative explains that continued intake and exhaust operation prevents the intended skipped firing cycles. Although the catalog title mentions intake-valve performance, this source describes a broader deactivation event.

## Pressure supplies indirect evidence

The monitor uses filtered, normalized manifold absolute pressure to infer that the exhaust valve failed to deactivate. It is not a direct measurement of an actuator's physical position. The failure decision requires 22 qualifying observations out of 25 opportunities, with sampling every 90 crankshaft degrees.

Eligibility includes a stable firing fraction, air per cylinder above 120 mg and the listed manifold-pressure-to-barometric-pressure window of 0.30 to 0.95, or a zero firing fraction. Transmission gear must exceed the stated calibration value, and firing fraction must be no greater than 0.95. These conditions matter because pressure behavior outside the intended operating region is not the same diagnostic comparison.

## The published inequality needs clarification

The descriptive text says the manifold-pressure signal exceeds a threshold, while the threshold cell prints a less-than comparison against combined deactivation thresholds. Those directions conflict. This article therefore retains a source-review flag and does not choose an inequality or offer an acceptance test based on one.

The supported conclusion is limited to the pressure-based strategy, eligibility and observation count. Exact calibration information is necessary to resolve the numerical comparison. The row does not locate cylinder 6 physically or prove that a particular lifter, solenoid or controller needs replacement.
