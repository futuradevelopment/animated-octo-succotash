# P0028: Bank 2 performance testing needs a valid lift-control window

## Why a cold-start observation is incomplete

P0028 in this Subaru factory description monitors the bank 2 intake-valve lift system. Before judging its feedback, the controller requires engine oil temperature of at least 15°C, at least 6,000 milliseconds since starting, battery voltage of at least 10.9 volts, and active variable valve lift control. A quiet test at idle outside that window is not equivalent to completing the diagnosis.

The mechanism switches between low-lift and high-lift cams. Its diagnostic oil-pressure switch supplies feedback about that change. The source describes two ways to fail: the switch can remain ON when a high-lift command is issued, or remain OFF when the system requests low lift. This makes P0028 a commanded-state comparison rather than a measurement of oil pressure in engineering units.

## Distinguish failure qualification from recovery

For the high-duty branch, duty must be at least 62 percent with the switch ON for 784 milliseconds. The opposite branch uses duty below 33 percent with the switch OFF for 3,000 milliseconds. The MIL is specified to illuminate when the malfunction qualifies.

Recovery is independently defined. High duty with an OFF switch must persist for 208 milliseconds, while low duty with an ON switch needs 3,000 milliseconds. A brief change in a scan-tool display is therefore not automatically a completed normality judgment.

## Make the next test answer a specific question

Identify bank 2 using the vehicle's service information, then capture the commanded mode and corresponding switch response under enabled conditions. Investigate the switch circuit, oil-control path and actuator through the matching procedure. The criteria provide no authorization to replace a cam assembly merely because the generic description includes valve control.
