# P0078: Exhaust-valve solenoid monitoring requires an engine-running event

## What the factory detection row establishes

Mitsubishi's cited P0078 row concerns the bank 1 exhaust valve timing control solenoid circuit. With the engine running, an improper voltage reaching the ECM through that circuit must persist for at least one second. The listed causes are an open or short in the harness/connectors and the solenoid itself. The row does not specify the voltage magnitude or prove that the camshaft mechanism is mechanically damaged.

The fail-safe description places valve-timing controls in their reference positions and changes runner and EGR operation. A technician should therefore distinguish the effects of that protective mode from additional independent failures before ordering parts.

## Confirmation is more demanding than clearing memory

The page describes a warm-engine confirmation under a steady, fixed-gear driving condition, followed by inspection of first-trip DTC information. That is different from simply restarting the engine and observing whether the warning remains off. Any road evaluation must be safe, legal and appropriate to the vehicle's condition; a vehicle with unreliable operation should be diagnosed in a controlled professional setting.

## Source-review boundary

The page's link labeled as the next diagnosis procedure opens a P0087 fuel-pressure procedure when followed. That is a material cross-reference error. This article remains marked for review and does not borrow the unrelated fuel-system tests as a P0078 repair sequence.

Use the verified P0078 detection facts to preserve the operating event and prioritize the correct exhaust-control circuit. Obtain the current matching factory circuit procedure before pin testing, applying a resistance specification or replacing the solenoid. The available row supports an electrical investigation, but not an invented terminal map or universal component limit.
