# P1207: Volkswagen cylinder 7 injector diagnosis follows the second ECM

## Keep the manufacturer-specific meaning and bank assignment

In this Volkswagen Phaeton BAP/BRP manual, P1207 identifies an electrical fault in the cylinder 7 fuel-injector circuit, component N85. This article applies to that Volkswagen definition. The manual places cylinders 7–12 on bank 2 and routes their injector-control wiring to engine control module 2, J624. Testing the first ECM's similarly numbered connection would not establish the condition of cylinder 7's circuit.

## Separate three electrical questions

The referenced injector procedure first establishes prerequisites including the injector fuses, power-supply relay, adequate voltage in both batteries, sound grounds and a secured vehicle. Its isolated winding test specifies 14–16 ohms. A failed resistance check supports an injector electrical defect, but a passing winding still leaves supply and activation to verify.

The next checks distinguish voltage supply from the controller's switching command. The wiring branch then traces the injector supply to its fuse and the control circuit to the correct ECM. Volkswagen specifies a maximum wire resistance of 1.5 ohms and separate isolation checks against power and ground.

## Keep fuel-system access professional

The factory procedure requires intake-system access and includes injector-seal replacement requirements if components are removed. Have a qualified technician follow the complete fuel and electrical safety procedure rather than applying power directly to an injector or probing an uncertain controller terminal.

Record the cylinder/component identity and whether the failed test involved winding, supply, command or wiring. The source's ECM replacement branch follows those circuit checks. P1207 alone does not distinguish a defective injector from a fault in its electrical path.
