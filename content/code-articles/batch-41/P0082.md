# P0082: Bank 2 current recovery must be demonstrated under load

## A returned connection is only the beginning

P0082 concerns the bank 2 oil switching solenoid on the cited 2011 Subaru SOHC engine. Its factory description identifies an open-type fault: actual current is very low despite a relatively large duty request. An intermittent terminal can therefore leave a stored code even when the circuit later appears connected. The meaningful question is whether it now carries the commanded current.

Subaru's failure criteria require duty at least 30 percent and control current below 0.026 ampere for 2,000 milliseconds. Battery voltage must be at least 10.9 volts for the continuously running diagnosis. MIL illumination is specified when the malfunction qualifies. These limits belong to this control system and should not be applied to another manufacturer's valve circuit.

## Use the normality conditions deliberately

The recovery table requires target current of at least 0.11 ampere. At that command, the absolute target-to-actual current error must remain below 0.08 ampere for 2,000 milliseconds. A zero-current reading with essentially no requested operation cannot satisfy that complete recovery test, even though target and actual might appear close.

This makes an enabled commanded-current check more informative than a static continuity result alone. Use the matching bank 2 wiring procedure to inspect connector fit, circuit integrity and the actuator/driver path, then verify operation through the documented control system.

## Keep hydraulic conclusions separate

This monitor does not measure cam lift directly. A successful electrical response does not independently prove that the oil-operated lift mechanism changes mode correctly. Conversely, a lift-performance complaint is not evidence that P0082's current threshold has been crossed. Record companion codes and follow their own diagnostic criteria when a mechanical response remains uncertain.
