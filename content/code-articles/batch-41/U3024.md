# U3024: GM transmission control compares power B with run/crank input

U3024 has a specific electrical meaning in the PCM(TCM) section of GM's 24OBDG03A Part 1. It compares the module's input-power circuit B with its run/crank ignition input. It is a correlation monitor, so a general battery-voltage observation does not supply both sides of the comparison.

## Preserve the direction of the subtraction

GM lists the criterion as power-B voltage minus run/crank voltage greater than 3 V. The table does not describe an absolute difference. Reversing the subtraction or treating either direction of disagreement as equivalent would therefore change the documented test.

The fault matures when 50 of 63 samples fail. Those are sample counts, not a published number of seconds; the row does not provide a sampling period from which a duration could be calculated. A single transient reading is consequently different evidence from the stated count-based decision.

## Cranking is explicitly excluded

Correlation monitoring must be enabled, the battery must be present and run/crank status must be active. At the same time, the starter motor must not be engaged. The name run/crank should not obscure that last condition: the monitor's stated eligibility excludes an engaged starter.

These conditions help define the evidence a professional diagnosis needs from the correct module strategy. They do not identify a universal measurement point, pin number or failed component. A sustained voltage relationship can require circuit investigation without proving that the transmission controller itself is defective. The source assigns Type A, one-trip indication and supplies no replacement instruction. Vehicle-specific power-distribution information is necessary before electrical testing.
