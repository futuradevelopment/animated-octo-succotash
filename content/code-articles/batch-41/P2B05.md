# P2B05: GM verifies cylinder 6 injector opening with coil feedback

The useful distinction in GM's P2B05 description is between requested injection and verified injector movement. In 24OBDG03B Part 1, the cylinder 6 monitor looks for the voltage-flux signature associated with movement of the injector's pintle or armature. An issued command is not sufficient evidence that the pulse occurred.

## What the controller measures

Feedback across the injector enable and command wires is measured through the controller's analogue-to-digital path. The resulting signal is converted into an opening-magnitude measure and compared with the calibrated missing-pulse criterion. A missing opening signature or an unacceptable calculated magnitude can establish the failure.

The source does not describe this as a direct fuel-flow measurement. It cannot therefore justify a statement that a particular number of milligrams was missing from cylinder 6. Nor does it identify a universal external voltage threshold that a handheld meter could reproduce.

## Separate monitor availability from a passing result

The small-pulse general diagnostic must be enabled, and the fuel-pulse feedback data must be ready. These conditions are prerequisites for interpretation. The row lists a 50-to-100-sample decision, cylinder-event sampling and continuous operation while enabled.

The supporting opening-magnitude calibration is indexed by rail pressure. It belongs to the controller's internal calculation rather than a generic wiring-test specification. GM assigns Type B, two-trip indication. A professional diagnosis should establish the matching calibration and preserve the difference between absent feedback, unsuitable test conditions and a verified failed opening event. The table alone does not determine whether the injector, its circuit or another part requires repair.
