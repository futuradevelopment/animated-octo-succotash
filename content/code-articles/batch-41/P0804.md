# P0804: GM skip-shift lamp control fails its electrical comparison

In the 2001 Chevrolet Corvette procedure, P0804 concerns the skip-shift lamp. The instrument cluster supplies battery voltage to the lamp, and the PCM illuminates it by grounding the control circuit. The lamp is commanded with the skip-shift solenoid, but this diagnostic monitors the lamp-control circuit's electrical state.

## Do not confuse two sets of conditions

The source lists specific road-speed, coolant-temperature, barometric-pressure and pedal-position conditions for enabling skip shift and its indicator. Separately, the circuit diagnostic runs above 400 rpm with ignition voltage between 6 and 18 V. The latter checks whether the driver command and actual circuit state disagree for at least five seconds.

A lamp that is not displayed when skip-shift activation conditions are absent is therefore different from a monitored electrical mismatch. Conversely, the fact that the solenoid has been commanded does not prove the indicator's supply and ground path are intact.

## Recorded voltage can explain an intermittent result

GM's diagnostic aids identify low system voltage as a possible contributor and recommend reviewing captured data for that condition. The test description also distinguishes commanded on and off observations, missing ground control and a ground applied continuously.

The procedure stores failure information without requesting the malfunction lamp. Confirm the exact indicator circuit and recorded operating state before professional testing. P0804 does not, by itself, prove that the skip-shift mechanism or transmission is mechanically defective.
