# C05B4: GM detects high voltage on EBCM temperature channel C

C05B4 is GM's high-feedback check for the third temperature channel in the 24OBDG03A Part 1 EBCM tables. The source supplies an electrical criterion, which is more useful than interpreting the generic high-input label as an automatic overheat diagnosis.

## The voltage and eligibility are separate facts

The listed failure boundary is diode feedback above 1.5 V. Maturation is 60 ms. This comparison is enabled with the power switch on and a true input-validation condition for the diode temperature sensor.

The table mentions an intended temperature range of minus 40 to 150 °C, but does not provide a conversion between feedback voltage and actual temperature. A high electrical input therefore should not be assigned an invented physical temperature. Also, a comparison made while the required enable conditions are absent does not reproduce the documented monitor.

## Distinguish a limit failure from disagreement

Other entries in the same channel family examine low feedback, changes between successive samples and disagreement with the microcontroller's die-temperature reading. C05B4 is specifically the upper-voltage boundary. A stable signal can fail that boundary without being erratic, and a plausible correlation result does not remove an excessive-voltage event.

GM identifies circuit-board, converter and microcontroller defects among the possible explanations, together with temperature-range problems. Those are possibilities in the monitor summary, not a diagnosis of a particular replacement part. Professional service must identify the matching controller and use the correct vehicle procedure; the table does not authorize opening brake electronics or applying this voltage to a guessed pin. The row lists a Type B indication and does not assess safe braking operation.
