# P0820: Eight switch signals identify the MR2 gear-lever position

## The lever has more than one position signal

The MR2 sequential-manual shift lever reports its position through eight switch signals. Four are main signals and four are check signals. At the defined lever positions, each check signal complements its corresponding main signal. P0820 concerns an invalid combination of those inputs, rather than a single analog X or Y voltage reading.

Toyota's first threshold table includes valid patterns for Reverse, Neutral, sequential mode and the upshift/downshift requests. It also includes permitted combinations while the lever moves between positions. A technician should therefore compare the captured pattern with the complete table, not assume every transitional reading is a failed switch.

## Two different ways to qualify

The monitor description identifies an unrecognized eight-signal combination as one failure route. A second route counts specific patterns listed in another table and qualifies when those occur at least five times. The strategy is continuous, specifies a duration of at least half a second and gives an immediate MIL response after qualification. These descriptions should be read together rather than converting every suspicious sample into a fault.

## Test the pattern at both ends

The diagnostic chart starts with all eight scan-tool signals at each lever position. It then checks the switch assembly's position-dependent continuity and the wiring to the controller and body ground. A switch that passes its isolated continuity table may still have a harness or connector problem in the installed circuit.

Preserve the failing position and exact signal combination before clearing memory. That information makes the subsequent continuity and circuit checks targeted, and supports any later ECU decision without replacing the entire selector merely because the generic code title mentions a position sensor.
