# P0067: Subaru air-assist high input can expose a short to supply

P0067 in the 2002 Subaru Outback 2.5-litre procedure concerns high input on the air-assist injector-solenoid circuit. Its chart focuses on the ECM output observation and how that observation changes when the solenoid is isolated. The resulting branch matters more than the code's high-input label alone.

## Isolation changes the meaning of the voltage

The chart uses a greater-than-10 V comparison at the specified ECM control point. If that high condition remains after the valve connection is removed in the prescribed sequence, the procedure investigates a battery-voltage short in the harness. A reading taken with a different connection state is not the same test.

The source also considers an intermittent supply short when the high reading appears only while the harness or connectors are disturbed during professional diagnosis. It warns through its repair branches that more than one component may be affected, rather than treating this as a simple guaranteed valve fault.

## Retain the full diagnostic context

Subaru specifies two consecutive failing driving cycles and lists irregular idle or stalling among possible symptoms. The chart can require distributor-level technical assistance when the defined electrical branches do not resolve the condition. That is a limit of this particular procedure, not permission to guess a replacement.

Confirm the exact vehicle and circuit before testing with appropriate connector equipment. The article does not recommend energizing the valve externally or bypassing its driver. A completed repair needs the manufacturer's memory-clear and inspection sequence; a single normal later voltage does not explain an intermittent high-input event.
