# P0749: Pressure-control A maps to N215 on this Phaeton transmission

## Identify the valve family before measuring it

In the Phaeton BAP/BRP diagnostic manual, P0749 identifies intermittent pressure-control solenoid A operation and maps that label to pressure control valve 1, N215. The corrective action points to the pressure-regulator test, not the neighboring ordinary shift-solenoid procedure. That distinction changes the resistance specification and the terminals involved.

Volkswagen lists 6–8 ohms for the pressure-regulator valve at approximately 20°C and notes that resistance rises at higher temperatures. The adjacent ordinary solenoid group has a different specification. A resistance reading judged against the wrong family could therefore lead to replacing a serviceable valve.

## Separate the valve from its external circuit

Before measuring, the factory sequence inspects the 16-pin transmission connector for corrosion and water ingress. The N215 regulator check uses its specific circuit pairings in that connector. A satisfactory valve result leads onward to the wiring between the transmission and TCM; it does not end the investigation.

The wire-continuity limit is 1.5 ohms, followed by an isolated check for unintended continuity between circuits. Controller replacement appears only after the wiring investigation. A qualified technician should use the actual connector drawing and test-box mapping, with the vehicle secured and circuits isolated as specified.

## Preserve intermittent-fault evidence

Record when the fault occurred and whether the circuit was hot or cold. The source does not publish a code-setting timer or commanded-current threshold for P0749, so a passing resistance check must not be described as proving that an undocumented monitor has passed. Verify the repaired circuit and transmission operation through the applicable factory process.
