# P154A: The 12-volt battery sensor reports an internal problem

## The sensor is reporting on itself

Honda P154A concerns the sensor installed between the 12-volt battery and ground. The sensor measures battery current, voltage and temperature, then communicates with the PCM over LIN. It also performs internal checks and reports their results. The monitor is therefore different from a simple measurement that the battery charge is low.

With the vehicle in ON mode, a qualifying abnormality lasting at least ten seconds can set the code. The source lists internal current, voltage and temperature detection or conversion faults, abnormal resistance-measurement or power-supply circuitry, and calibration-data problems. A single displayed battery-voltage value cannot evaluate all those internal functions.

## An internal-failure label still requires a supply check

The possible-cause section includes an open sensor power supply, including a blown fuse, as well as failure inside the sensor. This matters when planning diagnosis: replacing the sensor without establishing its supply condition could leave the original problem untouched. Conversely, installing a new battery would not prove that the sensor's electronics or calibration are operating correctly.

Retain the scan information and identify the exact Honda battery-sensor circuit before disturbing connections. The excerpt does not supply connector pinouts or authorize bypassing the sensor. Honda classifies P154A as a one-drive-cycle condition with the MIL off, while pending and confirmed DTCs and freeze data remain stored. A vehicle can consequently retain this sensor fault without the usual engine-warning-lamp indication.
