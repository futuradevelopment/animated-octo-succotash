# P171C: Two Park-position reports do not agree

## The comparison follows a completed Park action

Honda P171C compares Park-position information from the park-pawl actuator driver unit with the actual-position information supplied through the PCM from the park-position sensor. The comparison takes place after the engagement or release action is completed. A disagreement persisting for at least 40 milliseconds satisfies the documented failure criterion.

The enable conditions require battery voltage of at least 10 V and no F-CAN communication failure. That qualification matters: this monitor is intended to judge position agreement when the communication path is available, rather than using a missing network message as proof of mechanical position.

## A displayed selection is not proof of engagement

The excerpt names a parking-mechanism failure as its possible cause. It does not provide a mechanical inspection sequence, adjustment specification or conclusive component diagnosis. The appropriate Honda procedure must establish why the actuator-side and sensor-side reports disagree.

The listed confirmation involves an ON-mode transition through Neutral and Park with the brake applied and a waiting period. Because this directly involves the vehicle's restraint mechanism, such verification needs suitable securing precautions and competent personnel; it is not a reason to release the vehicle on a slope to see whether it rolls.

Honda records the fault in one drive cycle, with a blinking shift-position indicator and stored pending, confirmed and freeze-data records while the MIL remains off. Save those records before cycling power or clearing the code so the mismatch can be investigated in context.
