# P1E33: The master-cylinder cut valve operates incorrectly

## The monitor observes valve operation during brake use

Honda P1E33-00 identifies incorrect operation of the master-cylinder cut valve within the electric servo brake system. The factory summary places this check in regular diagnosis, after the initial brake-indicator self-check. The stated confirmation conditions include ON mode, a 12-volt supply of 9.5–16.7 V and several brake-pedal applications.

Unlike a check that simply waits with the pedal released, this procedure observes operation associated with pedal use. The approximately one-second duration does not reveal the internal pressure or position criterion, so a universal mechanical pass/fail threshold cannot be derived from this summary alone.

## A valve description does not settle the failed assembly

The source lists an internal pedal-feel-simulator problem involving the cut valve and an internal electric-servo-brake control-unit fault. These are separate possibilities. The document does not authorize replacing the simulator solely because the valve appears in the DTC title.

Honda describes one-drive-cycle illumination of both red and amber brake warnings. The listed fail-safe response suspends electric servo braking and multiple cooperative features, including regenerative braking, automatic hold and brake assist. Those effects can influence the driving experience even when the underlying fault is internal to one valve-control function.

Keep the full P1E33-00 record and have the current Honda brake troubleshooting sequence applied by qualified personnel. Avoid road attempts intended to provoke a brake fault, and do not regard an indicator extinguishing after a restart as proof that normal assistance and valve operation have been restored.
