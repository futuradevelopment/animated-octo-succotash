# P1E28: A low PU pressure-sensor signal does not prove low hydraulic pressure

## Keep the electrical and hydraulic meanings separate

Honda P1E28-00 identifies low voltage in the PU brake-fluid-pressure sensor circuit. The cited procedure also covers corresponding low-circuit codes on other named pressure channels. That shared diagnostic page does not establish that the hydraulic system is low on fluid or that actual braking pressure is below a particular value.

The factory confirmation uses pedal applications after an HDS clear and ignition-state cycle, then checks whether the channel-specific DTC returns. No electrical threshold, minimum fluid pressure or universal sensor-output curve is supplied in this excerpt. Those missing specifications should not be borrowed from another code or vehicle.

## An intermittent result still needs its original context

If the fault does not repeat, Honda directs investigation toward intermittent conditions and any recorded freeze or on-board snapshot. A normal reading after reset may simply mean the original condition is absent at that moment.

For a repeated fault, the page requires connector-terminal and connection checks at the electric-servo-brake controller and review of authorized service information before its controller/pedal-feel-simulator decision. That order prevents the DTC title from being used as immediate evidence for an assembly replacement.

Preserve the exact P1E28-00 record and any companion pressure-channel faults. Diagnosis and post-repair verification belong with qualified brake personnel using the matching Honda procedure. Do not alter hydraulic plumbing or substitute a sensor signal to erase an electrical plausibility fault.
