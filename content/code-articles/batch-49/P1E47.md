# P1E47: The brake controller detects a stuck-off switching stage

## The fault is detected during the initial brake self-check

Honda P1E47-00 refers to a stuck-off FET condition in the electric servo brake control system. The factory summary places the monitor in initial diagnosis, with ON mode and a 12-volt supply between 9.5 and 16.7 V. It lists an approximate one-second diagnostic duration.

Although the title names an internal control-unit circuit, the source does not limit the possible cause to the controller. It also identifies shorts to ground or power on the MU, MV and MW connections to the tandem motor cylinder, and an internal motor problem in that cylinder. Those external-load and wiring alternatives must be considered before a controller decision.

## The protective response is system-wide

Both red and amber brake indicators are listed for this one-drive-cycle fault. Honda's fail-safe description suspends electric servo assistance and several related functions, including regenerative braking, automatic hold, brake assist and brake-based driving support. An initial-check fault can therefore affect more than a single electronic component.

Save the exact suffix-bearing DTC and related brake records before clearing. The summary does not supply a transistor test, connector pinout or safe motor-powering procedure. A qualified technician needs the matching Honda diagnostic instructions to isolate wiring, motor and controller possibilities.

Do not bridge the switching stage, apply power directly to the brake motor or road-test a vehicle merely to see whether assistance returns. Brake-system service requires appropriate securing, equipment and verification of restored operation.
