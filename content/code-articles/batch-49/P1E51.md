# P1E51: A tandem-motor-cylinder linkage fault needs a physical inspection

## The confirmation includes a held pedal application

Honda P1E51-00 identifies a mechanical-linkage fault in the tandem motor cylinder's gearbox. The factory verification waits at least ten seconds after switching on with the pedal released, then uses a firm held pedal application before checking for the code. That sequence differs from simply reading a historical record with no operating confirmation.

If the fault does not return, Honda directs investigation toward intermittent conditions using the recorded freeze or snapshot information. A temporary absence should not be treated as proof that the linkage and control system are mechanically sound in every condition.

## Inspection decides between mechanical and control branches

When P1E51-00 repeats, the procedure calls for inspection of the tandem motor cylinder after the specified OFF-mode wait with the driver's door open. Damage to related parts leads to the gearbox or motor branch. If no such damage is found, the next branch considers controller-terminal connections and relevant authorized service information before a controller/pedal-feel-simulator decision.

That distinction prevents a mechanical-linkage title from automatically condemning the gearbox, while still requiring physical inspection rather than relying only on scan data. The excerpt provides no adjustment specification or repair method for internal gears.

Qualified brake personnel should perform the inspection and verify supported braking operation afterward. Preserve the diagnostic record before clearing, and do not attempt to force the linkage, operate the actuator with covers removed or reproduce the fault through aggressive road braking.
