# P1DA4: A VCU inverter-short diagnosis depends on a completed professional test

## The factory decision uses OBD status

Honda P1DA4 identifies a VCU inverter-short condition in the cited electric-powertrain procedure. After initial preparation, the source uses an HDS OBD-status result from a specified operating test. A FAILED result reaches the PCU replacement branch. PASSED directs attention to an intermittent event, while NOT COMPLETED means the monitor has not yet supplied a decisive outcome.

Those statuses must remain distinct. A code that disappears immediately after clearing is not equivalent to the monitor completing and reporting a pass. The original freeze data and any snapshot remain useful for understanding the conditions that first produced the record.

## The described operating test is specialized workshop work

The excerpt describes a running-vehicle test on a lift and warns about driveline damage from abrupt braking in that setup. This is not an owner road-test recipe or permission to spin a vehicle's wheels on ordinary supports. Any use of that method requires the current Honda procedure, approved workshop equipment and qualified personnel controlling the risks.

The document does not provide a transistor test, a safe live-terminal measurement or an individual inverter-component repair method. Its PCU decision is conditional on the prescribed diagnostic result and exact vehicle applicability.

Keep the full fault history before resetting, and have the service facility verify completion of the correct monitor. Access to the VCU/PCU and associated conductors requires the manufacturer's high-voltage isolation and verification steps.
