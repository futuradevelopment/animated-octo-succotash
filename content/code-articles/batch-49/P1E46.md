# P1E46: A brake-controller FET fault still requires motor-wiring isolation

## The controller label does not exclude the connected load

Honda P1E46-00 identifies a stuck-on FET condition in the electric servo brake controller. The full troubleshooting page first verifies recurrence after an HDS clear and power cycle, including a brief wait without pedal application. It then investigates the MU, MV and MW motor paths rather than immediately replacing the controller.

The diagnostic sequence separates shorts to power, shorts to ground and shorts between phase wires. For the power-short branch, it compares the result before and after disconnecting the tandem motor cylinder. That controlled change helps distinguish a harness fault from a problem associated with the motor.

## Wiring results determine the later branch

The source similarly uses disconnection to separate a ground short in the harness from the motor path. Only after the specified wire-to-wire checks pass does its remaining branch call for controller-terminal inspection and authorized service-information review before the controller/pedal-feel-simulator decision.

The procedure includes a three-minute OFF-mode wait with the driver's door open before connector work. Those power-state and connector conditions are integral to the measurements; this summary is not a substitute pinout or a general instruction to probe a brake actuator.

Preserve the exact P1E46-00 record and related motor-current codes before clearing. Have qualified brake personnel apply the correct Honda sequence and verify normal operation afterward. Do not bypass a switching stage or directly energize the motor in an attempt to distinguish the branches.
