# P16E3: The battery sensor stops providing usable LIN information

## Communication quality comes before battery interpretation

Honda P16E3 monitors information exchanged between the PCM and the 12-volt battery sensor on the LIN(BATT SENSOR) circuit. Either no received information or abnormal information continuing for at least five seconds meets the documented fault criterion. Monitoring begins after three seconds in ON mode, provided battery voltage is at least 10 V.

The sensor normally supplies battery-related feedback requested by the PCM. When that exchange is missing or invalid, a displayed battery measurement cannot automatically be treated as a trustworthy live sensor value. This code identifies the communication problem; it does not establish the battery's state of health.

## The fault can sit on either side of the line

The cause list includes an open or shorted LIN line, a failed battery sensor, a fuse-related interruption and an internal PCM circuit fault. Each points to a different part of the information path. Replacing a battery or disconnecting it to erase the record would not distinguish among these alternatives.

Keep the scan data and any supply-related faults, then use the correct wiring information to investigate the sensor circuit. The source's introductory LIN discussion mentions several possible slave devices, but it does not prove that every listed device shares the same wiring on every Insight configuration.

The documented fault is stored in one drive cycle with pending, confirmed and freeze-data records while the MIL stays off. Lack of an engine lamp is therefore compatible with a real stored battery-sensor communication fault.
