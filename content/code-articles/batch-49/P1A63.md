# P1A63: Two PCU voltage estimates separate beyond their tolerance

## Two voltage values serve different control functions

Honda P1A63 compares the V2M and V2S voltage measurements inside the PCU. The description associates V2M with voltage-control-unit output control and V2S with traction-motor and generator-output control. The comparison checks whether those related measurements remain consistent enough for their control tasks.

The documented malfunction threshold is a difference greater than 50 V persisting for at least 1.2 seconds. The enable table requires at least 8 V on the 12-volt supply and at least 5 V on the internal supply identified as the high-voltage-side 6 V supply. That internal supply requirement must not be confused with a safe external traction-voltage limit.

## A disagreement is not proof that either value is correct

When two measured quantities diverge, the code identifies their inconsistency; it does not establish which sensor value represents the actual voltage. The excerpt lists an internal PCU fault but does not give an independent reference measurement, connector procedure or component-replacement instruction.

Keep both available voltage readings, freeze data and companion faults for the matching Honda diagnostic process. The stated fault class illuminates the MIL in one drive cycle and includes a power-system warning, with pending and confirmed storage in the motor control module.

Inspection of the PCU or its high-voltage connections requires trained hybrid personnel and the required isolation procedure. A sensor-correlation code is not a reason to expose energized terminals simply to decide which of two scan values appears plausible.
