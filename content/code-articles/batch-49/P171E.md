# P171E: Park-actuator learning does not finish in time

## Initial learning is the monitored operation

Honda P171E is associated with the park-pawl actuator driver unit's initial learning process. The PCM commands that learning operation, and the unit reports a malfunction if it has not completed after at least 2.3 seconds. The excerpt specifies a battery-voltage enable floor of 10.8 V.

This is distinct from a timeout during an ordinary Park engagement or release after learning. Keeping that distinction in the repair history helps a technician determine whether the fault arose during initialization, following service, or during a normal operating request. The code alone cannot establish which of those events preceded the scan.

## Communication and mechanism checks both remain relevant

The factory list includes an open F-CAN A_H or A_L line, park-pawl actuator failure and parking-mechanism failure. The learning process depends on more than the actuator motor itself, so repeatedly issuing the learning command without investigating the reason for failure may simply reproduce the record.

The cited confirmation uses ON mode and a wait of at least ten seconds. Honda classifies the fault as one-drive-cycle storage with a blinking shift-position indicator and the MIL off. Turning the vehicle off can extinguish the indicator, but that is not evidence that learning has completed successfully.

Preserve the diagnostic information and use the exact Honda learning and fault-isolation instructions. Because Park restraint is involved, secure the vehicle throughout diagnosis and do not equate an extinguished warning with verified mechanical engagement.
