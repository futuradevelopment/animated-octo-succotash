# P171F: A commanded Park movement exceeds its completion time

## This timeout follows an engagement or release command

Honda P171F monitors the time taken for the park-pawl system to complete a commanded change into or out of Park. After the PCM issues the command, failure to complete the action for at least 1.5 seconds meets the documented threshold. Monitoring requires a battery-voltage reading of at least 10.8 V.

The code title refers to a stuck condition after learning, but the source's criterion is the unfinished action within the specified period. It does not directly identify which mechanical or electrical element prevented completion, nor does it prove that the actuator is receiving a correct command under all conditions.

## Distinguish a slow mechanism from lost communication

Honda lists open F-CAN A_H and A_L circuits, park-pawl actuator failure and parking-mechanism failure among the possibilities. A communication interruption and an actuator that cannot move are different diagnostic branches even though both can leave the requested action incomplete.

The source's confirmation sequence includes waiting in ON mode and making a brake-applied Neutral-to-Park selection, followed by another wait. A qualified technician should perform that verification with the vehicle secured and the matching service instructions available. Do not test Park by intentionally allowing an unsecured vehicle to move.

A blinking shift-position indicator and one-drive-cycle DTC storage are documented, while the MIL remains off. Keep the freeze data and related network records before clearing them; the warning lamp state alone cannot establish safe Park operation.
