# P1D77: Measured reactor current disagrees with the PCU estimate

## The estimate is checked in a defined control mode

Honda P1D77 compares the I1 reactor-current measurement with an estimated current value. The depicted comparison uses the magnitude of their difference. A difference greater than 50 A continuing for at least two seconds meets the stated malfunction threshold.

The eligibility conditions narrow the test considerably. Gate drive must be on, the VCU must be in direct-connection mode, and both PDU control modes must be PWM. The supply requirements are at least 8 V on the 12-volt circuit and at least 5 V on the internal supply labeled 6 V. A random current comparison outside those states does not reproduce the factory monitor.

## Disagreement does not identify which quantity is wrong

The PCU regulates VCU output using reactor-current information. If its measured and calculated values diverge, the code establishes inconsistency between those information paths. It does not prove that the measured current alone is incorrect or provide a standalone external-current test.

Honda lists internal PCU failure and specifies one-drive-cycle MIL illumination with a power-system warning. Keep freeze data, control-mode information and related current-sensor faults so the matching diagnostic procedure can be applied with the event context intact.

The excerpt contains no safe instructions for opening the reactor circuit or measuring it live. A qualified hybrid technician must follow the vehicle-specific isolation and verification requirements before accessing the PCU or its high-voltage connections.
