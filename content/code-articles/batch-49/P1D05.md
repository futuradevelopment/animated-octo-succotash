# P1D05: Internal PCU messages fail their integrity or availability check

## A communication fault inside the power electronics

Honda P1D05 monitors CAN communication between the motor control module and power drive unit within the PCU. The listed failure conditions include missing data, an ALIVE or checksum problem, and a continuing bus-off state. A qualifying condition lasting at least 500 milliseconds is sufficient for the documented monitor.

This is more specific than a generic loss of vehicle-network communication. The factory illustration places both communicating functions inside the PCU. An external CAN fault elsewhere in the vehicle should not automatically be treated as the explanation for this internal code without the matching diagnostic evidence.

## Data integrity matters even when messages exist

The ALIVE and checksum branches mean that receiving some traffic is not proof of a healthy exchange. Information can be present but fail the controller's validity checks. The monitor becomes eligible at least one second after entering ON mode and requires a 12-volt supply of at least 8 V.

The possible-cause list names internal PCU failure. It supplies no universal bus-resistance test or supported instruction to open the unit. Preserve the freeze data and all related controller records so a qualified technician can apply the proper Honda diagnostic sequence.

Honda identifies one-drive-cycle MIL illumination and a power-system indication, with pending and confirmed storage. Any access to the PCU's electrical connections must respect high-voltage isolation requirements; erasing the communication record alone does not establish that the internal exchange has recovered.
