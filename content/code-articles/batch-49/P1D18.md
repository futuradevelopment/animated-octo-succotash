# P1D18: The inverter temperature selector stops changing state

## The fault is in selecting the temperature information

Honda P1D18 concerns the selection of traction-motor-side IGBT chip-temperature information within the PCU. The control system uses a selector to obtain the parallel-phase temperature readings. The documented monitor fails when its chip-select state, communicated internally, does not switch between high and low for at least two seconds.

That mechanism differs from a straightforward high-temperature code. A selector that stops changing state can prevent the controller from obtaining the expected sequence of temperature information even if the actual chip temperatures are not excessive. The code alone therefore does not establish an overheated motor or a cooling-system failure.

## Keep the internal monitoring context

The excerpt specifies a 12-volt supply of at least 8 V and identifies internal PCU failure as a possible cause. It contains no service instruction for manually switching the selector, opening the converter or replacing an individual temperature sensor. The vehicle-specific Honda diagnostic path is needed to determine how the internal monitoring result is verified.

Honda classifies this as a one-drive-cycle MIL fault with a power-system indication. Pending and confirmed records and freeze data are stored in the motor control module. Preserve those records, including any companion internal-communication faults, before resetting the system.

Investigation involving the PCU requires qualified high-voltage service personnel. The fact that the monitor observes a logic-level state does not make the surrounding power electronics safe for live access.
