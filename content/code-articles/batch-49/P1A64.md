# P1A64: The PCU reports an internal conversion-check problem

## The description goes beyond the communication-error title

Honda P1A64 is titled as an internal PCU communication error. Its detailed explanation describes an analog-to-digital converter problem detected by the power drive unit and reported to the motor control module. The monitor runs in ON mode and uses a persistence period of at least 80 milliseconds.

That distinction matters because the title alone could suggest an external vehicle-network interruption. The cited diagram instead shows communication between functions contained within the PCU. It does not identify a CAN wiring test or establish that an external harness is the cause.

## Keep the direction-of-reporting ambiguity visible

The narrative says the motor control module receives the PDU's abnormality report, while the threshold sentence says the PDU receives the microcomputer trouble. The intended reporting direction is therefore not consistently expressed. This article keeps a source-review flag and avoids turning that wording into a detailed internal circuit diagnosis.

The sole listed cause is an internal PCU failure. That is a diagnostic possibility, not a complete replacement authorization or evidence that every PCU function is defective. Honda specifies one-drive-cycle MIL storage, a power-system warning and retained freeze data. Preserve those records and use current model-specific Honda instructions to resolve the fault description before making a hardware decision. Opening or electrically testing the high-voltage power electronics belongs with qualified personnel following the prescribed safety process.
