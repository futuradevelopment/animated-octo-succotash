# P1E63: Brake-pedal travel feedback remains stuck after a pedal event

## This monitor needs time after pedal release

Honda P1E63-00 identifies a stuck signal from the brake-pedal stroke sensor. The factory confirmation description uses ON mode, a firm pedal application followed by release, and then a wait of at least three minutes. The listed supply range is 9.5–16.7 V, and the monitor runs during regular diagnosis.

The long observation interval is a meaningful distinction. A quick ignition cycle or a momentary scan immediately after pedal release does not reproduce the stated confirmation window. The excerpt supplies no travel-distance or signal-voltage tolerance, so a universal sensor adjustment limit should not be inferred from it.

## A stuck reading is not the same as a physically stuck pedal

The source names an internal stroke-sensor failure and an internal electric-servo-brake control-unit fault as possibilities. The DTC concerns the reported signal; it does not by itself prove that the pedal mechanism cannot move or identify which electronic component is responsible.

Honda lists one-drive-cycle red and amber brake warnings. The protective response suspends servo assistance and related functions such as regenerative braking, automatic hold and brake assist. Preserve the full P1E63-00 record and associated data before clearing.

A qualified brake technician should apply the matching Honda troubleshooting process and verify function afterward. Do not repeatedly drive or perform emergency-style stops to reproduce a stuck signal. A warning disappearing after a reset does not establish that pedal-travel information and normal assistance are reliable.
