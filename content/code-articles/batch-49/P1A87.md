# P1A87: The V0 and V2M measurements diverge in the PCU

## A relative voltage check underlies the fault

Honda P1A87 compares the PCU's V0 measurement with V2M. The source diagram expresses the comparison as V0 minus V2M, and the stated threshold is a difference greater than 60 V lasting at least 1.5 seconds. The control system uses V0 information when regulating the voltage control unit.

This is a relative measurement check. The “over voltage range” wording should not be reduced to an unsupported claim that a particular battery voltage has exceeded a universal limit. Both values and the operating state are relevant to understanding why their relationship failed the monitor.

## The record does not isolate a replaceable sensor

Monitoring requires at least 8 V on the 12-volt supply. The source identifies internal PCU failure as a possible cause, but it does not say that V0 is necessarily wrong while V2M is correct, or provide separate sensor replacement instructions. The current Honda diagnostic procedure is needed to establish the next valid test.

Honda classifies the fault as a one-drive-cycle MIL event with a power-system warning. Pending, confirmed and freeze-data records are retained in the motor control module. Preserve that information before clearing so the compared voltages can be assessed in context.

The comparison involves power-electronics measurements associated with the high-voltage system. It does not authorize direct live-terminal probing. Any access to the PCU requires suitable training, the exact service information and the manufacturer's isolation and verification precautions.
