# P1E4A: Excess motor current is reported by the electric servo brake

## Current trouble belongs to the brake actuator circuit

Honda P1E4A-00 concerns an overcurrent condition in the slave-cylinder drive motor of the electric servo brake system. The cited summary assigns it to regular diagnosis with the vehicle in ON mode and a 12-volt supply between 9.5 and 16.7 V. The approximate diagnostic duration is one second.

The document does not publish the current threshold in amperes. A universal clamp-meter limit or a safe direct-power test therefore cannot be derived from this page. The available evidence identifies the monitored function and its operating context, not a complete electrical test sequence.

## The motor is only one listed possibility

Honda lists shorts to ground in the MU, MV or MW lines between the controller and tandem motor cylinder, an internal cylinder-motor fault and an internal electric-servo-brake controller problem. A motor-current code can therefore arise from the power path or control electronics as well as the actuator itself.

The stated response includes red and amber brake warnings and suspension of electric servo braking plus several cooperative functions, including regenerative braking and automatic hold. Preserve the complete P1E4A-00 record and companion faults before a reset so the correct branch can be investigated.

A qualified brake technician should use the vehicle-specific Honda procedure and verify restored brake operation after repair. Do not repeatedly command a suspected overcurrent condition, bypass motor wiring or assume a restart has restored all assistance functions.
