# P1D11: The traction motor phase-current sum has two limits

## Gate state changes the comparison

Honda P1D11 evaluates the sum of the three traction-motor phase-current sensor readings. With the IGBT gates active, the documented comparison limit depends on commanded current: the table expresses it as the absolute value of the complete expression (commanded current × 0.138 + 48.6 A). With the gates inactive, the stated limit is 157.8 A. Both branches use at least 350 milliseconds of persistence.

These are separate operating branches, not two interchangeable pass/fail values. A diagnostic capture that omits gate state or commanded current loses information needed to interpret why the sum was considered abnormal. The monitor also requires at least 8 V on the 12-volt supply.

## Preserve the calculated relationship

The code concerns the combined consistency of phase-current information, rather than naming one failed phase sensor. The excerpt lists internal PCU failure but does not identify an external probe point or provide a component-isolation procedure. The precise sign conventions and current data should be interpreted using the matching Honda service information rather than reconstructed from a single scan value.

Honda describes pending storage on the first qualifying drive cycle and confirmed storage with MIL illumination after recurrence on the next. The monitor table also identifies a power-system warning. Retain freeze data and companion faults before clearing. Work around traction-motor conductors or the PCU requires qualified hybrid personnel and the prescribed isolation process, not an improvised live-current test.
