# P1A65: Repeated data-flash faults are reported inside the inverter

## The reported fault concerns stored data

Honda P1A65 describes a data-flash problem detected within the power drive unit and communicated to the motor control module inside the PCU. The stated criterion is detection at least three times with the vehicle in ON mode. It is an event-count requirement, not a three-second delay or a demand for three separate drive cycles.

The distinction from an analog-input conversion problem is useful when interpreting related internal-PCU codes. Data-flash integrity and measurement conversion are different functions even though both can be reported through the same internal communication arrangement. The excerpt does not describe how an owner could safely verify either function independently.

## The source's reporting direction is inconsistent

The general explanation has the motor control module receiving the PDU's data-flash report, but the threshold sentence refers to the PDU receiving that trouble. This wording discrepancy remains unresolved, so the article is flagged for source review and does not present a speculative internal signal path as established fact.

Honda lists internal PCU failure as a possible cause and specifies one-drive-cycle MIL illumination with a power-system indication. Pending and confirmed records and freeze data are retained in the motor control module. Save that information before resetting the system and have a qualified technician consult the correct Honda diagnostic procedure. The document gives no supported instruction to reflash memory, open the inverter or replace an assembly solely from this code.
