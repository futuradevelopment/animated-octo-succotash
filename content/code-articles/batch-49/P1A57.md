# P1A57: Phase-current amplitudes fail their agreement check

## The monitor compares the three phases

Honda P1A57 evaluates differences in the amplitudes reported by the traction motor's three phase-current measurements. The labeled threshold says that all of the amplitude differences must exceed 141.6 A for at least 340 milliseconds. This is a relationship among phase measurements, not a claim that one particular phase has simply crossed an absolute current limit.

The monitor requires traction-motor speed of at least 500 rpm and a 12-volt supply of at least 8 V. Those eligibility conditions make an idle scan of a stationary traction motor an incomplete confirmation of the monitor. A code that remains absent in that state has not necessarily passed the comparison under its required conditions.

## Retain the phase relationship in the diagnostic record

The factory cause list names an internal PCU fault. It does not identify a separate replaceable sensor or provide a pin-level procedure. A technician needs the vehicle-specific Honda workflow and the recorded motor operating state before concluding what part of the internal sensing or control path failed.

Honda classifies the event as a one-drive-cycle MIL fault with a power-system warning, and retains pending, confirmed and freeze-data records. Avoid erasing those records before the phase-current information is reviewed. Reproducing motor rotation or accessing the power electronics is professional hybrid work; the numerical comparison is not an instruction to measure live three-phase conductors with ordinary workshop tools.
