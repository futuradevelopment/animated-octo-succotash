# P170F: The park actuator loses its PWM communication path

## There is a second communication route

Honda P170F concerns the PARKCMD and PARKBUSY PWM lines between the PCM and park-pawl actuator driver unit. The factory description says normal commands and feedback use F-CAN, while PWM provides a substitute communication path when F-CAN communication is unavailable. The explicit threshold identifies absent or abnormal PWM information lasting at least 1.5 seconds.

The monitor's listed battery-voltage floor is 10 V. The cause list covers opens and shorts to ground or power on either PWM line, as well as internal faults in the actuator driver unit or PCM. That range of possibilities requires circuit isolation rather than an assumption that the parking mechanism is jammed.

## One sentence in the source is inconsistent

The introductory paragraph says that transmitting or receiving “any signals” can trigger the fault, whereas the malfunction-threshold section specifies inability to communicate or receipt of abnormal information. This appears to omit a negation, but it should not be silently repaired into a definitive specification. The article retains a review flag and relies on the clearly labeled threshold for its bounded explanation.

Honda describes a two-drive-cycle record without MIL illumination or a blinking shift indicator. Absence of those warnings does not establish that the backup path is healthy. Preserve network and Park-related diagnostic records, secure the vehicle appropriately and have the correct Honda communication procedure applied before relying on Park engagement.
