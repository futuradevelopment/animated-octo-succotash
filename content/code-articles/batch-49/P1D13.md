# P1D13: Generator-current consistency depends on gate activity

## The generator uses its own current-sum calibration

Honda P1D13 checks the sum of three generator-motor phase-current measurements. When IGBT gate drive is active, the source defines the comparison using commanded current multiplied by 0.138 plus 34.6 A within magnitude bars. When gate drive is inactive, the listed limit becomes 111.9 A. The abnormal comparison must continue for at least 350 milliseconds.

Those values belong to the generator monitor in this Honda excerpt. Substituting a traction-motor current-sum specification would change the test and could lead to a false interpretation. The controller's gate state and commanded-current information are therefore useful parts of the diagnostic record.

## A current-sum failure is not a complete component diagnosis

The monitor operates with a 12-volt supply of at least 8 V. The factory possible-cause section names internal PCU failure, without a separate sensor-replacement instruction or a universal meter test. The code does not alone determine whether a particular phase reading, its interpretation or another internal function is responsible.

The documented storage strategy is two drive cycles: the first qualifying event creates a pending record, and recurrence produces confirmed storage and MIL illumination. Preserve the relevant freeze information and other generator-control faults before clearing memory.

The generator and its power electronics are high-voltage components. Their diagnostic data should be assessed through the correct Honda procedure by trained personnel; do not attempt to reproduce the current-sum monitor by probing energized phase connections.
