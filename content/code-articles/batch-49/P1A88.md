# P1A88: A V1-to-V0 comparison fails during PCU self-diagnosis

## The self-test state is part of the definition

Honda P1A88 compares V1 with V0 during a PCU self-diagnostic state described as C1C0 directly connected. The diagram uses the magnitude of their difference. A difference greater than 30 V lasting at least one second satisfies the documented malfunction criterion.

Although the title refers to an under-voltage range, the explicit monitor is a comparison between two measurements in a specified internal configuration. The excerpt does not establish a standalone lower battery-voltage limit. Omitting the self-test context would turn a bounded diagnostic relationship into a misleading general voltage specification.

## Use the comparison without inventing an external test

The enable condition requires at least 5 V on the supply labeled as the high-voltage-side 6 V supply. That is an internal monitor prerequisite, not a statement that the traction circuit has become safe to touch. The only listed cause is internal PCU failure; no owner-level measurement or replacement sequence is supplied.

Retain the V1 and V0 diagnostic information, freeze data and any related power-electronics faults. Honda specifies one-drive-cycle MIL illumination with a power-system indication and pending and confirmed storage in the motor control module.

A qualified hybrid technician should use the model-specific Honda procedure to evaluate the self-test result. Do not attempt to reproduce the stated internal connection externally or bypass the PCU switching arrangement to compare voltages.
