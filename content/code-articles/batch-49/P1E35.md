# P1E35: The pedal-feel simulator cut valve fails its operation check

## Identify the simulator valve rather than the master-cylinder valve

Honda P1E35-00 concerns incorrect operation of the pedal-feel simulator cut valve. The cited page separately names its stuck-off and stuck-on branches. Those simulator-valve labels should not be exchanged with master-cylinder cut-valve definitions simply because the codes are close together numerically.

The procedure observes the brake system after a power cycle, first allowing at least one second with the pedal released and then applying the pedal several times. This sequence provides the context for checking whether one of the listed simulator-valve records returns. The excerpt does not specify an internal valve-travel or hydraulic-pressure limit.

## The service decision depends on recurrence and connection checks

For a repeated fault, Honda directs inspection for loose terminals and poor connections at the electric-servo-brake controller's 22-pin connector, followed by review of relevant authorized service information. Its replacement branch concerns the controller/pedal-feel-simulator assembly after those checks. A stored historical code alone does not establish that the assembly is defective now.

When the fault does not repeat, the source calls it intermittent and uses the original freeze or snapshot conditions to guide further investigation. Preserve that information before clearing rather than treating a temporary absence as a completed repair.

Because this component belongs to the brake-control system, diagnosis and functional verification require qualified brake personnel and the correct Honda procedure. Do not force the valve, bypass the simulator or use normal pedal movement as the sole proof that supported braking behavior is restored.
