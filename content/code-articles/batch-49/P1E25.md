# P1E25: Low redundant pedal feedback requires supply and wiring checks

## The redundant channel is only part of the circuit

Honda P1E25-00 concerns low voltage on the redundant brake-pedal stroke-sensor channel. The factory procedure groups it with the primary-channel low-voltage code and first checks whether the fault returns after an HDS clear and power cycle. If it does not return, the procedure treats it as intermittent and directs attention to the original freeze or snapshot conditions.

For a repeated fault, the diagnostic path considers the ESB SVCC supply, ESB SGND reference, and BPS/BPSR signal wiring before using a known-good pedal assembly to distinguish sensor-related trouble from the controller path. That ordering prevents the word “sensor” from becoming an immediate parts-replacement decision.

## A measurement instruction in the excerpt is inconsistent

The supply and ground open-wire sections say to measure voltage with the vehicle on, yet their pass/fail question uses an ohmic continuity limit. Those instructions mix measurement modes and operating states. This article remains flagged for review and deliberately does not repeat them as a usable test sequence. The current Honda procedure must resolve the discrepancy before circuit measurements.

The document also specifies an OFF-mode waiting state with the driver's door open before connector disconnection. That requirement should not be discarded when applying corrected service information.

Preserve P1E25-00 and any companion brake faults. A qualified brake technician should perform diagnosis and verify restored operation; do not substitute signals or bypass the redundant channel to clear a safety-related fault.
