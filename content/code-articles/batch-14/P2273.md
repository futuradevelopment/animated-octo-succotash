# P2273: Interpreting a rear-sensor rich-bias check when the table wording conflicts

P2273 is the bank 2 sensor 2 biased/stuck-rich code. The GM row's malfunction column evaluates the minimum rear-sensor voltage during a commanded lean shift. Its strategy text, however, describes a maximum voltage during a rich command. Both wordings appear in the original PDF, so they are not silently treated as equivalent.

## The failure column describes inadequate lean response

The printed criterion is a minimum bank 2 secondary-sensor voltage remaining above approximately 0.2002 V while shifting lean. The continuation describes an intrusive state sequence and qualifications for exhaust flow, sensor readiness and catalyst temperature.

This is different from declaring any high rear-sensor voltage during normal operation a failure. The command and the opportunity for the exhaust response to arrive are part of the diagnostic evidence.

## Resolve the detailed procedure before applying thresholds

The continuation also contains mixed bank references in parts of the state sequence. This article therefore explains the supported lean-response criterion without presenting the entire sequence as a verified universal workshop test.

Useful observations include commanded mixture, upstream confirmation, rear-sensor minimum response and monitor eligibility. They help distinguish a response failure from an observation made before the proper test conditions existed. The code does not independently isolate the sensor, mixture cause or catalyst. Exact calibration information is needed to resolve the wording and apply the correct procedure before a component is selected.

## Source and scope

2025 GM 25OBDG07A Part 2 monitor tables only; model, engine and transmission applicability are not established by the unresolved application-matrix filename join. These are documented monitor criteria, not a universal repair procedure.
