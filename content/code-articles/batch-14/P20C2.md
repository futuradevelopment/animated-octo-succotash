# P20C2: Checking the second DEF tank heater’s resistance and power performance

P20C2 is assigned to heater 3 in this GM system, described as the second tank heater. The numbering is easy to confuse: heater 2 is the line heater, while this monitor evaluates another tank-heating circuit.

## Resistance and power tell different stories

The controller calculates resistance from voltage across the heater divided by current. For this calibration, values below 1.5 ohms or above 2.6 ohms are outside the listed resistance window and use an eight-second failure accumulation.

A second comparison checks delivered electrical power against commanded power. A difference greater than 29 watts in either direction is evaluated over ten seconds. A heater can therefore be investigated for excessive as well as insufficient power; the code is not limited to complete loss of heating.

## Confirm that heater 3 was actually eligible

A positive PWM command is required. Faults elsewhere in the temperature, heater, supply or communication system may force the command to zero and stop the intended performance assessment. No recurrence during disabled heating is not evidence that the resistance and power comparisons passed.

Preserve the heater identity, command, measured current and the two sides of the voltage measurement used by the controller. These help distinguish a resistance-window failure from a power-tracking failure. The listed values describe a specific monitor calibration and must not be applied indiscriminately to another tank design. The source does not provide a safe direct-power test or identify the replacement component.
