# P21D3: Keeping injector-channel identity separate from a cylinder 5 open-load label

The generic P21D3 definition names cylinder 5 injector B circuit/open. In the cited GM table, P21D3 appears in the group labeled injector 7. Different internal numbering can account for such distinctions, but these pages do not provide the mapping needed to identify a physical injector confidently.

## A bounded electrical observation

The row's open-load criterion uses output voltage at least 3 V and less than 5 V while the power stage is inactive. The listed maturation count is three events. Engine speed and supply conditions must also be met; an idle electrical snapshot outside that eligibility is not a completed diagnostic.

The voltage is part of the controller's test definition. It should not be converted into a universal connector-pin specification or used as an instruction to power an injector.

## What remains useful despite the mapping limitation

The source clearly distinguishes open load from its channel's low-circuit and high-circuit diagnoses. That allows the observed failure category to be preserved accurately while the physical circuit identity is verified elsewhere.

Retain controller and calibration identification together with the complete code and driver state. Those details are needed to choose the correct wiring information. The monitor does not evaluate fuel pressure, delivery quantity or spray quality, and it cannot isolate a wire, winding or driver as the failed part. Resolving the channel mapping is a necessary step before circuit testing or any component replacement decision.
