# P222B: Separating physical pressure range faults from sensor cross-check failures

P222B is the range/performance diagnosis for auxiliary barometric-pressure sensor B in the generic catalog. The documented GM implementation identifies bank 2 and groups several plausibility checks under this code, rather than assigning one fixed electrical limit.

## Four reported categories share the code

The source lists physical-range high and low faults, plus high and low sensor cross-check faults. The first pair concerns whether the pressure is physically plausible; the second concerns agreement with comparison information. A code readout alone does not reveal which category triggered.

All four rows require ignition on and refer to separate inhibition tables. The summary presents detected-fault status for these checks but does not publish a complete numeric pressure boundary or the full cross-check calculation on these pages.

## Preserve the distinction during diagnosis

An in-range raw electrical or digital signal does not establish that the pressure value is plausible. Equally, a cross-check failure does not uniquely prove that this sensor, rather than its comparison information, is wrong. Those are different questions from the raw digital limits listed for P222C and P222D.

Useful evidence includes the available subtest status, the pressure value and the relevant comparison signals with their validity. If the tool cannot identify the failed branch, the applicable workshop information is needed before selecting a test threshold. Inventing one universal pressure difference from this summary would exceed the evidence. The source supports a plausibility investigation, not an automatic sensor replacement.
