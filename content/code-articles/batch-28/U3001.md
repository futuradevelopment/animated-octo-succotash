# U3001: 2021 F-150 steering-assist loss after low-voltage starting

## U3001 needs its suffix and module

Ford SSM 51894 describes a 2021 F-150 that lacks steering assist at startup and displays a steering-assist fault message. The relevant combination is U3001:68 and/or U3001:89 together with U3000:96 in the power steering control module, or PSCM. A bare U3001 scan result from another module is not enough to identify this case.

Ford states that low battery voltage during an attempted start can cause the condition. A voltage reading taken after the vehicle has recovered is therefore not the same evidence as voltage during the failed starting event. Preserve the complete fault combination and the timing of the assist loss before clearing anything.

## The documented recovery and repair path

The message says assist is restored when battery voltage is at least 12 volts and the DTCs are cleared. That number is the bulletin’s recovery condition, not a complete battery health test or a promise that every U3001 fault will respond to clearing.

The specified service action is to update the PSCM software, clear the codes and cycle the ignition to verify normal operation. Ford explicitly says PSCM replacement is unnecessary for this described condition. If the codes return, the message directs technicians to the normal steering diagnostics in Workshop Manual Section 211-02.

Because the actual symptom involves steering assistance, restoration must be verified before treating the concern as resolved. A software-related possibility does not establish that a vehicle with continuing assist loss is suitable to drive. This case supports checking supply-event history and calibration before a module replacement decision, while retaining the fallback to full diagnosis when faults recur.

## Source and applicability

Ford SSM 51894: 2021 F-150 startup loss of steering assist with U3001:68 and/or U3001:89 plus U3000:96 in PSCM. Source-specific low-voltage/software case; not every U3001 or every steering fault.
