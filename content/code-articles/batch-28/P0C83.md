# P0C83: Sensor H circuit low must be separated from temperature direction

P0C83 identifies battery-temperature sensor H's low electrical circuit condition. In the cited GM table, the explicit failure pair is ADC below 680 and a reported temperature above 87.5°C. A low-circuit label should therefore not be described as evidence that the battery is cold.

## Interpret the reported heat carefully

The temperature is derived from the sensing signal. With this circuit code, a high display does not independently prove actual battery temperature. The correct vehicle procedure must establish whether the reading is credible before assigning a physical thermal problem.

The failure must account for 1.4 seconds of a two-second observation window, with 200-millisecond evaluations. Recovery requires temperature at or below 87.5°C and ADC at least 680 for two seconds. The duration is part of both the failed and recovered state.

## Check the supporting system

Enabled communication is required. Specified sensing-module communication and internal-performance faults must be absent, and the secondary protection self-test inactive. Preserve those prerequisites when evaluating whether a later reading constitutes a valid retest.

The source strategy sentence refers to temperature falling below a limit despite its explicit high converted-temperature criterion. That inconsistency remains flagged rather than silently corrected. Sensor H's physical location and any high-voltage battery access must come from the exact service documentation. The monitor itself provides no universal connector instruction or replacement decision.

## Source and applicability

15OBDG01 Hybrid Diagnostics, cited controller and monitor only. Historical calibration limits are not universal specifications; per-code vehicle, engine and hardware applicability must be verified.
