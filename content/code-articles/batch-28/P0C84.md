# P0C84: Sensor H high circuit can produce a cold limit reading

P0C84 is the high-circuit diagnostic for battery-temperature sensor H in the cited GM controller. The table's explicit condition is a temperature report below −40°C with ADC count above 3992. Electrical high and physical high temperature are not interchangeable descriptions.

## Keep the conversion relationship visible

An extreme cold report may arise from the signal path represented by the ADC value. This code alone cannot distinguish a physical temperature condition from an inaccurate measurement. Preserve the original reading and related electrical faults for the diagnostic technician.

The controller requires 1.4 seconds of failure within a two-second window at 200-millisecond intervals. The pass row requires at least −40°C and ADC no greater than 3992 for two seconds. One normal sample after the event does not cover that recovery interval.

## Verify the monitor's prerequisites

The shared source excludes specified communication, reference-voltage, internal-performance and bus-off faults. Enabled communication and an inactive secondary protection self-test are required. If those conditions block evaluation, no fresh code is not evidence of a passed H circuit.

The strategy sentence's temperature-direction wording conflicts with the explicit limit, which is flagged for review. No missing correction is invented. The H designation must be mapped through the exact battery documentation, and physical high-voltage work belongs to trained personnel following the appropriate vehicle service procedure.

## Source and applicability

15OBDG01 Hybrid Diagnostics, cited controller and monitor only. Historical calibration limits are not universal specifications; per-code vehicle, engine and hardware applicability must be verified.
