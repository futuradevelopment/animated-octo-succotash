# C1C21: Reverse-status information appears stuck

## Information status versus a mechanical conclusion

Honda calls C1C21-62 a reverse-information failure with a stuck condition. The receiving diagnostic context is the VSA system. The wording describes information that does not change as expected; it does not establish that the transmission is physically locked in reverse or identify a particular gear-position sensor for replacement.

The listed possible causes are a gear-system failure and an internal VSA modulator-control-unit failure. This short advanced-diagnostics sheet does not supply a wiring test, a message identifier or a transmission teardown decision. Those omissions limit what can responsibly be concluded from the code alone.

## Confirmation requires actual operating transitions

The factory confirmation calls for road operation that includes forward driving and reverse operation, repeated under specified conditions. It explicitly calls for road testing rather than testing on a lift and warns about surrounding traffic. Such a sequence belongs in a professionally controlled diagnosis; it should not be improvised by a driver trying to extinguish a warning.

The monitor runs during regular diagnosis with an approximately three-second duration. One drive cycle can illuminate both the amber brake-system indicator and the VSA indicator. Honda specifies that some electric parking-brake functions stop under the fail-safe response.

## What to report to the repairer

Preserve the exact C1C21-62 record, related gear-system faults and any observed disagreement between selected gear and displayed status. Have the parking and braking functions assessed before relying on them. A normal gear display during a later visit does not exclude an intermittent reverse-information fault.
