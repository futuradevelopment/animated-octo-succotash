# B1234: Driver air-mix feedback fault needs the right actuator path

## Keep the driver-side air-mix identity

B1234 identifies a short in the driver's air-mix control motor circuit in this Honda extract. The alternate climate indicator is 41. The named actuator and side matter when several air-door motors are fitted: a recirculation or mode-motor diagram should not be substituted because its troubleshooting pattern looks similar.

Honda also warns that several simultaneous short-circuit DTCs may point to an open or grounded 5 V power circuit. Before selecting the driver air-mix motor, preserve the full climate scan and establish whether the complaint is isolated or part of that shared-feed pattern.

## The replacement choice follows measurements

After self-diagnosis confirms recurrence, the procedure checks S5V-H at the disconnected motor for approximately 5 V. If the feed is missing, it distinguishes an open supply wire from a grounded supply circuit and then a controller fault.

With supply present, a separate position-signal voltage check determines the next branch. Further isolation checks examine AMD-P-DR for a short to ground and for a short to SENS COM-H. Only the corresponding results justify the procedure's motor or climate-control-unit replacement direction.

## Avoid a mechanical conclusion from an electrical title

An air-mix complaint can be described to the repairer, but the stored code does not by itself prove a jammed door or a failed heater component. A qualified technician should use the exact circuit layout and specified connector states. If the code does not recur, Honda directs attention to loose wiring or poor connections rather than declaring a component failed.
