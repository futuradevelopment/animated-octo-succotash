# P1011: Wastegate pressure reported above the atmospheric reference

## The relationship the controller rejects

In the cited Ford wastegate monitor, P1011 is a range/performance fault. The specified failure condition is wastegate control pressure exceeding barometric pressure by more than 3.0 inches of mercury. The monitor runs continuously with a five-second duration and relies on the pressure sensor, wastegate control and BARO inputs being usable.

This is different from the sensor's low- and high-voltage electrical limits. A voltage can fall between those electrical endpoints while the pressure it represents is implausible relative to atmospheric pressure. Consequently, the absence of P1012 or P1013 does not demonstrate that the P1011 comparison is valid.

## Use pressure and voltage together

The same factory table relates sensor output to absolute pressure. At the stated reference conditions, it gives examples including approximately 2.0 volts at 48.6 kPa and 4.5 volts at 105.5 kPa. These are sensor-characterization points, not target wastegate pressures for every operating condition.

Preserve the pressure value, BARO value and operating state from the failure before a technician evaluates the sensor circuit and pneumatic path. Comparing the two pressure channels helps distinguish a suspect reference from a suspect wastegate measurement; the code alone cannot choose between them.

Do not transfer the 20-second engine-off requirement from the neighboring P100F row to this code: Ford explicitly attaches that condition to P100F only. Correctly identifying which comparison failed avoids diagnosing this range fault as a turbocharger boost-target error.
