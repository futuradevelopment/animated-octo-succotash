# C1111: A right parking-brake circuit fault has an inconsistent line label

## Preserve the clear identity and the uncertainty

Honda defines C1111-11 as a right rear electric parking-brake actuator circuit short to ground. The source specifies a 9-to-16-volt supply window, apply/release confirmation and an approximately one-second circuit evaluation during initial or regular diagnosis.

Its possible-cause text, however, pairs a right-actuator positive-line label with a left-actuator negative-line label. That conflicts with the right-side title. This article remains flagged for source review and does not silently correct the circuit name or offer a terminal test based on the inconsistent text.

## Resolve the wiring from the exact service diagram

Record the full code and suffix, then use the vehicle-specific wiring and troubleshooting procedure to identify the right actuator's actual circuit. The page also lists actuator failure and internal VSA-modulator failure as possibilities, so the stored result is not proof that the motor alone is defective.

The complete scan should include left-side faults because Honda's stated protective response can remove parking-brake functionality when both sides are affected. Secure the vehicle independently before professional diagnosis; do not rely on an uncertain electric parking brake to prevent movement.

The practical objective is to resolve the source-label ambiguity, locate the electrical fault and confirm dependable actuation. Applying power to a guessed terminal or replacing the opposite-side actuator would not be justified by this document.
