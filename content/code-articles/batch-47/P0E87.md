# P0E87: The charger receives an excessive voltage request

## A request is different from a measured output

Ford lists P0E87 under battery-energy control-module performance as battery charger B input voltage too high. Its detailed criterion is more specific: the received battery charging voltage request exceeds 450 volts while the charger is ready. The table gives a monitoring duration below one second.

That wording matters. The source does not say that a technician measured more than 450 volts at a charging terminal, nor that the household AC supply is too high. It describes an implausible request received within the charging-control system. Treating the label as a direct mains-voltage measurement would change the diagnostic meaning.

## Preserve the requesting and receiving module information

Record charger readiness, the requested charging voltage and faults in the battery and hybrid control modules. The neighboring rows distinguish invalid battery-module data from missing communications, so a complete scan can preserve whether information was absent, marked invalid or numerically outside the accepted request range.

A qualified hybrid technician should follow the matching control-system procedure to determine why the request was rejected. The overview does not identify a failed charger or battery solely from P0E87, and it does not provide a universal replacement sequence.

Do not probe energized high-voltage connectors to try to reproduce the label literally. The useful first evidence is the communicated request and its operating state. High-voltage isolation and any physical electrical measurements require the correct factory safety procedure.
