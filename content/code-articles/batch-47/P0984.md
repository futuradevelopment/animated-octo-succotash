# P0984: The F21 checks whether solenoid E control current is stuck

## A current-control problem in a specific transmission

The Aisin F21 section of Ford's 2010 OBD summary identifies P0984 as stuck control current for variable-force shift solenoid E. The same circuit has separate codes for short-to-power and open/short-to-ground conditions. This preserves a distinction between a current response that is wrong and an obvious circuit extreme.

The general shift-solenoid monitor describes current feedback outside its range for half a second. Mechanical functional checks instead infer failure from achieved gears or shift patterns. Those different observations should not be combined into a claim that P0984 directly proves a jammed hydraulic valve.

## Preserve the current-control evidence

Record the F21 identification, solenoid command, feedback-current information and companion electrical faults. A qualified technician should then follow the correct driver, wiring and solenoid procedure. The overview does not provide an ampere target or coil-resistance limit that can safely be applied to every solenoid labeled E.

Ford also explains that the F21 has a standalone transmission controller that stores its own fault and freeze-frame information and requests the engine controller to illuminate the MIL. Reading only the engine module can therefore miss useful transmission-specific evidence.

The diagnostic objective is to explain why the controlled current fails to respond as expected. Parts replacement and any hydraulic conclusions require additional service checks. Verification should address the original electrical observation and then assess the associated transmission operation under controlled conditions.
