# C1422: Sub torque-sensor fault can share a supply problem

## Why the second torque channel matters

Honda's C1422-14 is a sub torque-sensor malfunction. The sheet names TSS as the signal path and also includes the T/S VDD power supply and T/S GND return. A healthy-looking main-channel reading alone does not resolve this diagnosis: the controller is reporting trouble with the other torque input or a circuit supporting it.

The TSS possibilities listed here are an open, a short to ground or a poor connection. The source does not list a short to power for that signal, so the separate main-channel cause list should not be copied onto this entry. Supply opens, supply shorts to ground and poor supply connections are included, together with an open or poor ground connection.

## Monitor operation and repair decisions

The confirmation conditions specify a 4.5–5.5 V torque-sensor supply and steering movement after entering READY mode. Detection occurs during regular diagnosis over about one second, with an EPS warning possible in one drive cycle. Assistance can be stopped or limited; recovery depends on a normal torque output after the next ignition-on event.

Internal steering-gearbox and EPS motor/control-unit faults remain possible after the external paths are considered. This advanced-diagnostics summary does not rank those causes or provide the connector measurements needed to distinguish them.

## Handling an intermittent warning

Record related torque codes and whether assistance changes during the event. A qualified EPS technician should assess the supply, return and sub signal using the correct vehicle procedure. Do not dismiss the warning simply because another channel still reports a plausible value.
