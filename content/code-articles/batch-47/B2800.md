# B2800: The SRS controller reports an internal fault after its power-up check

## Preserve the suffix and supply condition

The Honda extract defines B2800-49 as an internal SRS-unit failure. Its confirmation uses vehicle ON mode followed by approximately six seconds, with the 12-volt battery between 10 and 16 volts. The fault can be recorded in one drive cycle and illuminates the SRS indicator.

The source separately warns that the SRS indicator can turn on when battery voltage falls below 10 volts and that an insufficiently charged battery can make testing inaccurate. A warning lamp observed during a low-voltage event is therefore not, by itself, the complete evidence for this internal-failure diagnostic.

## Confirm the diagnostic before selecting a module

Save the full code, its status and low-voltage history before any reset. A qualified SRS technician should follow the matching restraint-system troubleshooting procedure with a suitable supply condition. The advanced-diagnostics page lists internal SRS failure as a possible cause, but explicitly says its cause list may not cover every possibility.

The page's fail-safe field says none. That should not be interpreted as a guarantee that all restraints remain available after an SRS warning. The document provides no such assurance and no owner-level deployment-circuit test.

Do not probe airbag or pretensioner circuits or substitute resistors to make the warning disappear. The useful first distinction is between a qualified, repeatable internal diagnostic and an observation compromised by low supply voltage. Module handling and any replacement or initialization belong to the exact factory SRS service process.
