# B123F: Sunlight-sensor diagnosis changes with automatic-wiper equipment

## Equipment selection is the first major decision

Honda B123F identifies an automatic-lighting-control-unit/sunlight-sensor error. After verifying it through climate self-diagnosis, the procedure asks whether the wiper/washer switch has an AUTO position. That equipment check selects materially different circuits; this is not one universal sunlight-sensor replacement instruction.

With automatic wipers, the procedure checks a separate two-pin sunlight sensor. It tests TSUN and SENSOR COM for opens, then checks TSUN for a ground short and for a short to the common wire. After satisfactory wiring checks, the separate sensor test decides between the sensor and climate control unit.

## The other branch includes a network participant

Without that AUTO position, Honda directs diagnosis toward the automatic lighting control unit-sensor and its B-CAN connection. A stored U1280 takes priority. The sequence includes fuse B10, the unit's power and ground, and continuity of B-CAN high and low paths.

After reconnection and another diagnostic run, the combination of B123F and U128D influences whether the climate controller or lighting unit-sensor is selected. That later code combination should not be skipped when following this branch.

## Why the equipment distinction matters

The two paths involve different connectors and failure checks. Applying the two-pin sensor test to the network-equipped arrangement could lead to the wrong component decision. Record the actual wiper-switch equipment and all climate/network codes, then have a technician follow the matching branch. The source does not establish one repair across every Insight trim or model year.
