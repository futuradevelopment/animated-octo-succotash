# B1077: Front-wiper auto-stop signal fails verification

## A specific wiper signal is involved

Honda defines B1077 as a front-wiper auto-stop switch signal error. The diagnosis concerns the signal associated with automatic stopping, rather than a direct measurement of blade condition or a blanket declaration that every wiper power circuit has failed.

The fault is read in the BCM diagnostic context. Honda's confirmation uses HDS to clear the record, cycles the vehicle from OFF to ON and rescans. A recurring B1077 directs replacement of the windshield wiper control unit/motor. The page does not require BCM replacement merely because the BCM reports the code.

## What a nonreturning code means

When B1077 is absent after that sequence, the factory classification is intermittent failure with the system functioning correctly at that time. The distinction is limited to the observed test result. Preserve the earlier scan and describe any inconsistent stopping or wiping behavior so the repairer can relate a later recurrence to the original complaint.

No switch voltage threshold, resistance value or standalone switch adjustment is supplied in this extract. It would therefore be unsupported to prescribe a sensor adjustment or promise that new wiper blades will correct the electronic signal fault.

## Service order and visibility

For several body-system DTCs, Honda points first to B-CAN System Diagnosis Test Mode A. Follow that order with the applicable vehicle information before authorizing an assembly. Because dependable wiper operation affects visibility, have unreliable operation assessed before travel in rain or other conditions requiring continuous windshield clearing.
