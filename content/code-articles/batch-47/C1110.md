# C1110: The left rear parking-brake actuator circuit is shorted to ground

## The circuit side and suffix are specific

The Honda extract defines C1110-11 as a short to ground in the left rear electric parking-brake actuator circuit. Its possible causes include the two left-actuator lines, the actuator itself and an internal VSA-modulator fault. The code does not decide among those locations by itself.

The listed supply window is 9 to 16 volts. Confirmation uses parking-brake apply/release operation, and the circuit monitor can register its finding in about one second during initial or regular diagnosis. A stalled or mechanically obstructed actuator should not automatically be assumed to satisfy this electrical classification.

## Keep the vehicle secured and the circuit identified

Record the exact side, suffix and any right-side or supply faults. The source describes loss of some parking-brake functionality when faults are detected on both sides, so the complete scan matters when assessing the protective response. Do not assume that the remaining actuator provides adequate holding without a service assessment.

A technician should isolate and test the correct left-actuator circuit with the vehicle secured according to the factory procedure. External power, improvised jumpers or repeated forced applications can obscure the original short and are not authorized by this advanced-diagnostics page.

The repair must explain the electrical fault before functional verification. Merely freeing a caliper or clearing the warning does not demonstrate that the left-actuator feed and control path are sound.
