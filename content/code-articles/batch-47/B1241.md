# B1241: Blower circuit diagnosis starts before the speed controller

## Establish power before blaming speed control

Honda B1241 concerns the blower motor circuit. The procedure first duplicates the fault with climate self-diagnosis, then checks fuse B3-1 and battery voltage at the blower feed. Missing feed voltage sends diagnosis to the blower relay and its supply circuits, rather than directly to the climate control unit.

That supply branch evaluates the relay, the +B HTR MTR feed, IG2 OPTION and the ground path associated with G305. Depending on the results, an open feed, relay problem or downstream BLOWER MTR wire can be the relevant repair.

## A powered blower has a different branch

When power is available, Honda uses a controlled motor-operation test. If the motor operates, diagnosis continues through the power-transistor ground associated with G351 and the BLOWER HI circuit. It then checks BLOWER V and BLOWER G for shorts to ground and opens before testing the power transistor itself.

Only after those decisions does the procedure choose the transistor or climate controller. Honda adds that a failed power transistor should prompt an inspection of the blower motor for damage, with motor replacement if necessary. That instruction helps avoid leaving an associated damaged component unexamined.

## Workshop testing and practical limits

The functional checks include prescribed jumper connections, which require the correct diagram and professional execution. This overview intentionally provides no substitute pin-bridging instructions. Preserve the code and describe the airflow complaint; a stored B1241 alone does not identify which of the power, motor or control branches has failed.
