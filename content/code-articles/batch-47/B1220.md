# B1220: Recirculation-motor fault needs a shared-supply check

## Check the pattern of HVAC faults

Honda's B1220 is a short in the recirculation control motor circuit, also represented by climate indicator 56. The factory note specifically warns that other simultaneous short-circuit codes can arise from an open or grounded 5 V power circuit. That makes the complete climate-code list useful before deciding that this motor is uniquely defective.

The recirculation actuator controls a different air-door function from the blower motor. This electrical code is therefore not a direct blower-speed diagnosis, nor does its title prove that the air door is physically stuck.

## Follow the two electrical branches

After duplicating the code with the climate self-diagnostic function, Honda checks the disconnected motor's S5V-H feed for approximately 5 V. An absent feed sends diagnosis toward an open supply wire, a short to ground or a climate-unit fault.

If supply is present, the next check evaluates the position-signal path. The ensuing isolation tests distinguish a grounded RFD-P wire from a short between RFD-P and SENS COM-H. The procedure reaches motor or climate-controller replacement only through the corresponding measured branch, not from the fault name alone.

## Handling an intermittent result

A nonreturning code directs attention to loose wires or poor connections in the recirculation circuit. Keep the original scan and report any inconsistent response to the recirculation selection. A technician should use the exact connector layout; sharing a 5 V feed with another actuator does not make their signal terminals interchangeable.
