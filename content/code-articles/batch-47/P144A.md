# P144A: A blocked EVAP line is screened and then challenged

## A two-stage restriction check

In Ford's cited EVAP strategy, P144A describes a restricted or blocked purge vapor line. The monitor first runs during phase zero of the 0.040-inch cruise test, then uses an intrusive check in phases three and four to confirm a fault. It normally executes once per driving cycle and lists a 30-second monitoring duration.

The intrusive portion needs adequate air mass and manifold vacuum while purge is active and fueling is not open loop. The printed typical minimums are 1.5 pounds per minute of air and 5 inches of mercury of manifold vacuum. Those conditions explain why a stationary glance at tank pressure is not equivalent to the completed staged test.

## Separate screening data from confirmation data

Ford provides separate Mode $06 components for screening and confirmation. They even use different units: pressure rate for the screening result and pressure for confirmation. Comparing those numbers as though both were a single pressure measurement would be misleading.

The document also warns that default zero values remain for phases that have not completed. A zero result therefore cannot automatically be read as a passed restriction check. Save completion status and the relevant result together.

P144A is listed as being replaced by P00FE in newer definitions. Confirm the installed calibration before choosing a service flowchart. The summary establishes the test strategy, not the exact location of a blockage or permission to replace a purge valve without checking the correct vapor-line arrangement.
