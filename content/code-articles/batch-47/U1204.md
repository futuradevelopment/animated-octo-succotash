# U1204: Motor controller loses PCM data on EP-CAN

## Identify the receiver and the network

In this Honda definition, U1204 is stored by the motor control module when PCM information is missing or abnormal on EP-CAN. The motor controller is part of the power control unit, alongside the MPI module and motor/generator phase-current sensor. Naming the PCU in the diagram does not establish that the entire assembly has failed.

The communication path uses EP-CAN A_H and A_L. Honda lists opens or shorts to ground on either line, an internal fault in a participating network unit, PCM failure and an internal PCU fault. That range explains why a communication code needs circuit and module-power diagnosis before an assembly is selected for replacement.

## Timing and stored evidence

The continuous monitor becomes eligible five seconds after ON mode with battery voltage at least 10 V. A missing signal or abnormal PCM information must persist for at least two seconds. This is a one-drive-cycle diagnosis: the source specifies the MIL and POWER SYSTEM indicators, with pending and confirmed records plus freeze data stored in the motor controller.

A later absence of the MIL does not necessarily erase the history. Honda allows it to clear after three consecutive trips in which the diagnostic runs without recurrence; a scan-tool clear command can also remove the stored evidence.

## Service direction

Preserve the network scan and freeze data before clearing anything. The distinction between EP-CAN here and another vehicle network is essential when selecting wiring diagrams. Hybrid power-control diagnosis should be performed by a qualified technician; this summary provides no authorization to open the PCU or probe high-voltage components.
