# B1175: Fuel-level fault checks both sender wires before the gauge

## This is a level-measurement circuit diagnosis

B1175 is Honda's fuel-level sensor or fuel-gauge sending-unit circuit malfunction. It is stored by the gauge control module. The source does not identify a fuel-pressure problem or establish that the fuel pump has stopped delivering fuel.

Verification includes clearing the code, switching OFF and then ON, and waiting more than 30 seconds before rescanning. If the fault does not return, Honda treats it as intermittent and directs attention to loose or poor connections. A scan taken too soon after switching on would not follow the documented verification sequence.

## Both sides of the sender circuit are tested

For a persistent fault, the procedure disconnects the gauge module and tank unit, then checks continuity separately through FUEL GAUGE+ and FUEL GAUGE−. An open or high-resistance path leads to wiring repair. Only after those paths are satisfactory does the procedure move to the sending unit itself.

The sender is removed and tested using its separate service test. A failed sender leads to sender replacement; a satisfactory sender leads to gauge-module replacement. The supplied extract does not contain the sender's resistance-versus-level specification, so no universal ohm value can be inferred here.

## Safe diagnosis around the tank

Have tank-unit removal and electrical testing performed by a qualified repairer with the applicable fuel-system precautions. Retain the code and describe any erratic gauge behavior. The sequence is intended to establish whether wiring, the sender or the display module is responsible before choosing a part.
