# B1036: BCM IG1 feed diagnosis separates fuse and wire faults

## Follow the feed named in the procedure

Honda B1036 is an IG1 power-supply circuit malfunction reported by the body control module. After confirming that the code returns, the factory procedure checks fuse C8. The result determines the next direction rather than immediately selecting the BCM for replacement.

With C8 intact, Honda directs attention to the BCM's IG1 FUEL PUMP feed from the under-dash fuse/relay box: an open or excessive resistance in that connection is the repair target. That circuit name is a wiring identifier; it is not evidence that the fuel pump itself is defective.

## A repeat blown fuse changes the diagnosis

If C8 is faulty, Honda calls for replacement and then repair of a short to ground in its circuit if the fuse blows again. Repeated fuse replacement without addressing the short would leave the documented cause unresolved. A technician should use the matching circuit diagram and specified fuse, not increase the fuse rating to keep the circuit powered.

The verification sequence clears the code, cycles OFF and ON, waits at least six seconds and rescans. If B1036 does not return, the procedure calls the fault intermittent and directs attention to loose or poor connections.

## Ordering the work

Multiple body codes require the B-CAN diagnostic sequence referenced by Honda. Save the original scan before clearing it, then establish whether the fault is a persistent feed loss or an intermittent connection. This page gives no numerical voltage-drop limit, so a generic invented threshold should not replace the vehicle's electrical test information.
