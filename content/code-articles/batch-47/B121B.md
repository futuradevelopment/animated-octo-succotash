# B121B: Mode-motor short code can originate in the shared 5 V feed

## Several short codes can share one cause

Honda identifies B121B, also displayed as climate indicator 4A, as a short in the mode control motor circuit. Its opening note adds an important qualification: several simultaneous short-circuit codes may result from an open or grounded 5 V power circuit. Multiple actuator codes therefore do not automatically mean multiple failed motors.

The procedure confirms the fault through the climate self-diagnostic function after clearing and cycling the ignition. If it does not recur, loose wiring or poor connections in the mode-motor circuit remain the stated concern.

## Supply and position circuits take different branches

With the motor disconnected, Honda first checks for approximately 5 V on S5V-H. If that feed is absent, the procedure works back toward the controller to separate an open supply wire, a grounded supply wire and a controller fault.

With the feed present, a second voltage check on the motor's position circuit determines whether the motor is selected for replacement or whether further wiring isolation is needed. The latter branch tests MDD-P-DR for a short to ground and for a short to SENS COM-H before the climate control unit becomes the replacement direction.

## What the code cannot decide alone

A mode-door complaint may lead the repairer to this circuit, but B121B itself does not prove a mechanically jammed door. Correct connector states and the complete wiring diagram are essential for the voltage and continuity decisions. Have a technician follow those branches rather than replacing the motor solely because its name appears in the title.
