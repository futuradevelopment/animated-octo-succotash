# C1421: Main torque-sensor circuit needs signal and supply checks

## Follow the main channel without overlooking its supply

C1421-14 identifies a main torque-sensor malfunction in Honda's EPS system. The source names the TSM signal line, the T/S VDD supply and the T/S GND return. This is more specific than a generic steering warning, but it still leaves several electrical paths to investigate before replacing the steering gearbox or controller.

For TSM, Honda lists an open, a short to ground, a short to power or a poor connection. The supply-line possibilities are an open, a short to ground or a poor connection; the ground-line possibilities are an open or poor connection. An internal gearbox fault and an internal EPS motor/control-unit fault also appear in the cause list.

## Conditions and consequences

The torque-sensor supply must be within 4.5–5.5 V for the documented confirmation conditions. Regular diagnosis has an approximately one-second duration and can illuminate the EPS indicator in one drive cycle. Assistance may stop or continue in a limited form. Normal operation can return when the torque output is normal after the next ignition-on event.

These details make supply and ground integrity material to diagnosis: a main-channel code should not automatically be treated as an isolated signal-wire defect. The sheet does not provide terminal numbers or allowable voltage-drop limits, so the matching wiring and EPS procedures remain necessary.

## Professional verification

Have a qualified repairer correlate the fault with actual steering assistance and inspect the named circuits under the prescribed conditions. Preserve the code before clearing it, especially when restarting temporarily restores normal steering effort.
