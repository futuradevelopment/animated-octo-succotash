# C1270: Brake-hold switch signal is shorted

## Why brake hold becomes unavailable

C1270-11 refers to the automatic brake hold switch signal circuit. In this Honda extract, the relevant BRAKE HOLD wire connects the switch to the gauge control module. The documented fault path therefore begins with that request signal, even though the driver sees an amber brake-system warning.

The monitor operates during regular diagnosis with the vehicle in ON mode and battery voltage between 9.5 and 16.7 V. Detection takes about one second, and the fault is a one-drive-cycle code. Honda's fail-safe stops automatic brake hold and schedules another diagnosis at the next ignition-on event.

## Interpreting the possible causes

A short to ground on the BRAKE HOLD line is one listed explanation. A defective switch or an internal gauge-module failure can also produce this diagnosis. These alternatives require different repairs; the warning does not establish a hydraulic brake defect or justify replacing a brake actuator without further evidence.

A qualified technician should preserve the fault record and identify the switch-to-gauge circuit on the vehicle's wiring diagram. Circuit isolation is needed to distinguish a grounded harness from an input held low inside a connected component. The supplied sheet does not publish a connector pinout or a voltage threshold for that decision.

## Using the vehicle safely

Do not assume the vehicle will remain stationary through automatic brake hold while this fault is active. Use the normal service brake and the appropriate verified parking procedure. An ignition cycle is a diagnostic opportunity described by Honda, not evidence that an intermittent circuit has been repaired.
