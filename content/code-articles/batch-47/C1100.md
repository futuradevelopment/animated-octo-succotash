# C1100: Parking-brake operation fails its overall monitoring check

## A system-operation check near zero speed

Honda lists C1100-48 as electric parking-brake abnormal-operation monitoring. The basic condition is wheel speed below 3 km/h. The confirmation includes several apply-and-release operations followed by at least 30 seconds, and the diagnostic duration is approximately 30 seconds.

This code evaluates overall monitored operation rather than identifying one actuator wire as shorted or open. That distinction prevents an abnormal-operation result from being treated as a specific left- or right-side motor-circuit diagnosis without supporting codes.

## Secure the vehicle before further checks

The documented response illuminates the amber brake-system indicator and stops the electric parking-brake function. Do not rely on the parking brake to hold the vehicle while investigating a fault whose protective response can disable it. A qualified technician should secure the vehicle independently and follow the exact brake-system procedure.

Preserve the command history, actuator-related faults and system-voltage information before clearing memory. Honda lists internal VSA-modulator failure as a possible cause, but the page explicitly cautions that its cause list may not include every possibility. It is not a standalone replacement verdict.

The specified near-stationary condition and waiting period are part of confirmation. A brief button press with no immediate warning does not establish that the monitor passed. Verification should explain the abnormal operation, restore the affected function and confirm reliable holding and release through the correct service checks.
