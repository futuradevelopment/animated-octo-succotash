# U1483: LKAS indicator-value check prevents lane assistance

## Lane assistance can be inhibited without an EPS lamp

Honda labels U1483-68 an LKAS malfunction involving an indicator-value check. The stated fail-safe is that LKAS function or assistance does not start. The EPS indicator is listed as off, even though the procedure appears within EPS diagnostic information. This combination matters when the complaint is unavailable lane assistance rather than a general loss of steering assistance.

The source does not describe this code as a blanket failure of the basic EPS motor. Nor does it give a universal instruction to replace a forward camera, perform an alignment or calibrate every driver-assistance sensor.

## Conditions for the check

The battery-voltage window is 10–16 V, with ignition voltage above 10 V for at least one second. Confirmation requires ON mode and a wait longer than nine seconds. Regular diagnosis then has an approximately nine-second duration and can set the fault in one drive cycle.

An internal millimeter-wave radar fault is the listed possible cause. Honda also cautions that its possible-cause lists may not cover every problem. The single listed component should therefore be treated as a diagnostic lead within the correct service procedure, not a code-only replacement order.

## Appropriate follow-up

Record whether LKAS is unavailable and retain the exact suffix and related driver-assistance faults. A qualified technician should confirm equipment applicability and follow the referenced EPS troubleshooting information. Until the function is verified, steer and supervise the vehicle normally; the absence of an EPS warning lamp does not demonstrate that lane-keeping assistance is ready.
