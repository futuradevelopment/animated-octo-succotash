# U1600: F-CAN PCM data fault has a two-cycle confirmation

## Preserve the first-cycle evidence

Honda U1600 concerns missing or abnormal PCM information received by the traction motor controller over F-CAN. On the first failing drive cycle, the motor controller stores a pending code. If the fault returns on the next drive cycle, the source describes a confirmed record and freeze data, with MIL and POWER SYSTEM indications.

This two-cycle sequence makes a pending U1600 meaningful even before the full warning response occurs. Clearing it immediately can remove the evidence needed to distinguish a recurring network interruption from a later successful trip.

## Follow the documented network route

The diagram includes the CAN gateway, F-CAN A and F-CAN C. Honda lists opens or shorts to ground on the high and low lines of both named segments, as well as internal faults in a network participant, the PCM or the PCU. The motor controller is contained within the PCU, but that packaging is not proof of an internal PCU failure.

The continuous monitor is enabled five seconds after ON mode with battery voltage at least 10 V. Either missing signals or abnormal PCM information must persist for at least two seconds. The MIL can clear after three consecutive trips in which the diagnostic runs successfully.

## Repair implications

Use the F-CAN diagram for this fault rather than borrowing the EP-CAN path from a similarly named communication code. A qualified hybrid technician should assess network integrity and module availability before replacing control hardware. This summary supplies monitor logic, not instructions for opening or probing the high-voltage power-control assembly.
