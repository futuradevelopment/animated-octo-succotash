# B1417: Telematics console input is checked separately from the antenna

## The console request circuit is the subject

Honda B1417 identifies the TELE console switch as shorted to ground. This is a switch-input diagnosis, not a cellular-antenna or GPS reception code. The factory sequence distinguishes three possible repair areas: the telematics switch module, its TELEM SW+ wire and the telematics control unit.

After confirming the fault, Honda first performs the separate telematics switch-module test. A failed switch module is replaced. If it passes, the procedure isolates both the switch and control-unit connectors and checks TELEM SW+ for continuity to body ground.

## The wire result determines the next direction

Continuity on the isolated signal path directs repair of a ground short in the wire. If that unwanted connection is absent, the final direction is replacement of the telematics control unit. The source therefore does not support selecting the controller before the switch and wiring branches have been evaluated.

Verification uses a code clear followed by OFF mode, an open driver's door and a wait longer than one minute. After ON mode, another wait of at least one minute precedes the scan. A nonreturning fault is treated as intermittent, and connector quality remains relevant.

## Appropriate functional checks

Have a qualified repairer use the prescribed switch test and connector information. The extract does not reproduce that separate switch-test specification, so no invented resistance threshold belongs here. Do not place a live emergency call merely to test the fault, and do not assume connected assistance is available until the affected function has been verified.
