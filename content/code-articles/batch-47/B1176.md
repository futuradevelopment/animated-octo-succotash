# B1176: Fuel-gauge positive circuit is checked for a ground short

## The first electrical branch is a short check

For Honda B1176, the fuel-gauge sending-unit circuit is reported shorted. The factory procedure focuses first on the FUEL GAUGE+ line after isolating the gauge control module and the fuel tank unit. Continuity from that isolated line to body ground directs repair of a short to ground in the wire.

This differs from a general continuity check along both sender wires. Preserving that distinction matters: a conductor can be continuous end to end while also having an unwanted connection to ground.

## The sender is evaluated only after the wire

If the positive line is not grounded, Honda proceeds to removal and testing of the fuel-gauge sending unit. A failed sender is replaced; if the sender passes its separate test, the procedure directs gauge-control-module replacement. The code alone does not decide between those components.

Before entering those branches, the technician clears B1176, cycles OFF and ON and waits more than 30 seconds before checking again. A fault that does not return is treated as intermittent, with loose or poor connections requiring attention.

## Limits of the supplied procedure

The extract refers to a separate sending-unit test but does not reproduce its resistance specifications. A generic resistance figure would therefore be an unsupported substitute. Fuel-tank access and circuit isolation belong with a qualified technician using the correct safety procedures. Record any gauge inconsistency for the repairer, and do not reinterpret this level-sensor code as proof of a fuel-pressure or fuel-pump delivery fault.
