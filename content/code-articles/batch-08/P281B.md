# P281B: pressure-control solenoid H electrical control path is open

P281B is the open-circuit code for pressure-control solenoid H in the cited 25OBDG04B controller family. The row maps H to nine- or ten-speed torque-converter control, an eight-speed T93 default valve, or CVT mode-valve B. Exact transmission identification is essential before testing.

## How an open path is recognized

The driver compares voltage feedback with internal circuit thresholds corresponding to an open condition. The specification is associated with signal-to-controller-ground impedance above 200 kilohms. It does not publish a universal coil-resistance test or authorize measuring resistance on a powered circuit.

Failure time must exceed 0.30 seconds within a sample period exceeding 0.50 seconds, updated every 6.25 ms. Battery voltage must be between 9 V and 32 V for more than one second. An eligible power state, enabled calibration and energized mapped high-side driver are also prerequisites. The entry is Type A, one trip.

## Diagnostic distinction

A converter that slips hydraulically and an electrically open control path are different findings. Preserve the electrical report and driver state before evaluating pressure or slip symptoms. If the relevant high-side driver is off, the monitor may not yet provide a valid circuit result.

Verification should show that this qualified open-circuit check completes after repair. The summary does not locate a broken wire or prove that an entire solenoid or valve-body assembly must be replaced.
