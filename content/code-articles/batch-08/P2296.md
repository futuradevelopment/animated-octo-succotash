# P2296: fuel pressure regulator 2 driver short-to-power detection

P2296 is listed here as fuel pressure regulator solenoid 2 control circuit high voltage. Although the generic dictionary calls it regulator B, the GM monitor identifies the rail pressure regulator valve's low-side driver. “High” describes the electrical diagnosis, not measured fuel rail pressure.

## What the controller checks

The driver is powered on when the controller compares its voltage feedback against internal limits. Feedback outside the expected on-state range indicates a short to power. The table associates this with a signal-to-controller-power impedance below 0.5 ohm; that is a controller design specification, not an instruction to resistance-test a live circuit.

The check requires powertrain relay voltage above 11 V, no engine cranking, calibration permission and usable hardware fault feedback. Its counter is 61 failed samples out of 122 at a 6.25 ms task rate. The table assigns Type A, one-trip reporting.

## Diagnostic implications

A rail-pressure value alone cannot verify this fault because the decision is made from driver feedback. Retain the electrical fault status and operating state, then use the correct wiring and diagnostic procedure to distinguish the control circuit from a pressure-regulation problem. An engine-cranking observation does not reproduce the documented enable conditions. Fuel-system work requires appropriate professional precautions; this table does not authorize opening high-pressure lines.
