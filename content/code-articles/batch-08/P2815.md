# P2815: pressure-control solenoid G circuit shorted to supply

P2815 identifies a high electrical fault for solenoid G in the cited 25OBDG04B transmission row. Its mapping covers specified line-pressure, torque-converter or CVT mode-valve A functions according to transmission architecture. The generic letter alone is not sufficient to identify the installed component.

## Controller-specific voltage feedback

The controller detects a voltage outside its acceptable circuit range and classifies a short to supply. The design specification relates that classification to impedance below 0.5 ohm between the signal and controller voltage source. It supplies no universal external pin-voltage threshold.

Failure time must exceed 0.30 seconds in a sample period exceeding 0.50 seconds. Qualification includes battery voltage between 9 V and 32 V for more than one second, an eligible run/crank or accessory state, calibration enablement and an energized mapped high-side driver. Reporting is Type A, one trip.

## Scope matters during diagnosis

This article uses the 04B monitor; the code must not be applied to another controller using its letter designation alone. Confirm architecture and compare driver feedback with the actual wiring procedure. A high commanded hydraulic pressure is not evidence of the electrical power short described here.

Verification requires the qualified short-to-voltage check to complete without failure. Do not perform resistance checks on live circuitry or replace a transmission assembly solely from this summary.
