# P24B6: particulate sensor heater remains electrically active when off

P24B6 diagnoses a short to power on the soot-sensor heater line. The distinctive evidence is electrical activity during PWM off time. The source lists heater output above 6 V, an active switch input in the off state, or off-state switch current between 0.5 A and 15 A as diagnostic alternatives.

## Why command phase matters

Voltage or current that may be expected during an on pulse can be abnormal during an off pulse. A measurement averaged across both phases does not necessarily reproduce the monitor's off-state decision. This distinction is central to interpreting the generic heater-control-high label.

The ECM requires a commanded-on sensor bus relay, no relay fault, valid communication and no sensor-control-unit undervoltage fault. Reporting uses 25 consecutive failures or 24 failures out of 96 samples at 100 ms intervals. The entry is Type B, two trips.

## Confirmation

Review the heater command phase and the sensor control unit's electrical status using the exact service procedure. Do not infer that the heater is physically too hot solely from this code. Verification should show that the off-state check completes without unintended electrical activity while supply and communication remain qualified. The table supplies internal thresholds but no universal connector pinout, direct-power test or component-replacement verdict.
