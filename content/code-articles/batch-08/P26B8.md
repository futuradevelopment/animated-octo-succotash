# P26B8: coolant bypass valve C driver reports a ground short

P26B8 is the low control-circuit diagnosis for coolant bypass valve C. The source describes a short to ground in the energized H-bridge supply/load circuitry and separates opening from closing motor direction.

## Direction affects qualification

The opening branch requires an opening command and no corresponding ground-short fault in the closing direction. The closing branch reverses those conditions. Both require valid run/crank, no engine cranking, enabled diagnostics and a determinate driver-low status. This makes direction and driver feedback useful evidence, not just whether the valve moved.

The table prints “999.9 seconds out of a 0 seconds window” for both branches. That is not a usable fault-setting duration. This article retains the supported electrical strategy and Type B, two-trip classification while leaving actual timing unresolved.

## Diagnostic interpretation

Preserve which movement direction was commanded when the shorted-low report appeared. A fault observed during one direction should not automatically be assumed to have been tested under the other branch. Use the exact service procedure to assess the circuit; the summary provides no external voltage threshold.

Verification should establish a completed applicable driver check after correction. A position response alone does not prove the electrical report is resolved, and the malformed timing cell must not be used to claim that the monitor is intentionally disabled.
