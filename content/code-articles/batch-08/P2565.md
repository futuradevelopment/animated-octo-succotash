# P2565: turbo VGT SENT position input above its electrical range

P2565 monitors the high electrical range of turbocharger position sensor A. The cited hardware is a SENT position sensor for variable-geometry turbocharger control, and the failure boundary is a raw signal above 99.00%.

## A high input is not a boost verdict

The code's circuit-high label does not independently establish excessive boost or a vane mechanism stuck in a particular position. The raw signal and its digital validity are the evidence used here. A command near a normal end position must also be distinguished from feedback outside the allowed electrical range.

The check requires calibration enablement, no cranking, relay voltage above 11 V and absence of the listed SENT out-of-range and performance fault states. Reporting follows 480 failed samples out of 600 at a 6.25 ms task rate. The source assigns Type A, one trip.

## Confirmation after diagnosis

Evaluate reported position and SENT status together, using the correct data scaling. A terminal-voltage assumption derived from the percentage would add information not provided by this table. Verify that the upper-range diagnostic actually completes after the input is restored, rather than relying only on a normal boost display. The summary establishes the monitored condition but does not choose between wiring, sensing electronics or another proven cause, and it gives no universal actuator-replacement instruction.
