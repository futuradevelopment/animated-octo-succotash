# P2627: NOx sensor 1 oxygen signal below its accepted floor

P2627 is generically called oxygen-sensor pumping-current trim circuit low. The GM row implements it as an oxygen signal-range check associated with NOx sensor 1. The documented lower boundary is −6.00% oxygen.

## What is actually too low

The threshold is a signal-validity floor, not a physical claim that exhaust can contain negative oxygen. It is also not a published pump-current or connector-voltage threshold. A low NOx ppm reading is a different parameter and cannot establish this oxygen-percent failure.

The engine must run, system voltage must exceed 11 V, the sensor must be fully operative and the applicable combustion mode must permit the test. An invalid sensor-1 condition is excluded. Reporting uses 100 failed observations out of 200 on a 25 ms task, with Type B, two trips.

## Diagnostic interpretation

Identify the oxygen parameter associated with NOx sensor 1 rather than substituting a conventional narrowband oxygen-sensor voltage. Record its validity state alongside the raw or appropriately scaled concentration. An unready sensor cannot demonstrate a qualified range test.

Verification should establish completion of the lower-range monitor after diagnosis. The code does not itself prove a failed pump-current circuit component or provide an adjustment procedure. Vehicle-specific information is required for any electrical test, sensor architecture or repair decision.
