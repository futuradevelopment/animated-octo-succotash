# P22A1: NOx sensor 2 exceeds its upper reporting limit

P22A1 is presented as the bank 1 sensor 2 NOx circuit-high monitor. Its strategy and enabling signals identify the post-catalyst sensor. The malfunction text contains a conflicting “NOx1” reference; this article follows the row heading and consistent NOx2 conditions, and does not use that isolated wording to change sensor identity.

## High concentration versus electrical high

The actual threshold given is a raw NOx reading above 2,990 ppm. The monitor counts 360 failed samples out of 720 on a 25 ms task, with Type B, two-trip reporting. This makes the code a sustained upper-range assessment in this implementation, rather than a published voltage test.

Supply, dewpoint, valid CAN communication, internal current-control status and electrical health all qualify the result. The continuation also ties operation to air-system control or a ready, uninhibited reductant system.

## Practical interpretation

First distinguish an above-range raw signal from an ordinary elevated NOx value that remains inside the sensor's range. Those observations do not invoke the same criterion. Then examine whether the conditions allowed this monitor to run, especially if other sensor or communication codes are present. An emissions-system efficiency conclusion cannot be made from this code alone. Confirm the exact vehicle's service information before selecting a sensor or applying a repair procedure.
