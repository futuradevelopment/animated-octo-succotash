# P24B5: particulate sensor heater reports a ground-short condition

P24B5 is the low heater-control code for the particulate sensor. The source describes a short to ground on the heater line, evaluated inside the sensor control unit while heater duty cycle is greater than zero.

## The failure uses more than a low voltage

The listed current criteria are below 0.5 A or above 15 A. The table also includes a ground-short error-event count above 100. These are internal diagnostic quantities; the generic circuit-low name should not be reduced to one guessed terminal-voltage test.

ECM qualification requires the bus relay on, no relay fault, no lost sensor communication and no control-unit undervoltage fault. The reporting counter is nine consecutive failures or nine out of 32 samples on a 100 ms task, with Type B, two trips.

## What to establish next

Keep heater command and current or fault-event status together. A heater-off observation cannot test the on-state criterion. Also distinguish the particulate sensor's own heater from the exhaust heating used for filter regeneration.

For verification, the heater must enter an eligible commanded-on state and complete the relevant electrical check without the ground-short report. The summary identifies the failure category but does not establish the physical short location or authorize applying power directly to the sensor. Follow the vehicle-specific electrical procedure to determine the remedy.
