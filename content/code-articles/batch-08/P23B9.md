# P23B9: NOx sensor 3 heater-sense path shorted to power

In this monitor, P23B9 means a short to power on NOx sensor 3's HTemp heater-sense connection. The diagnosis concerns resistance feedback from the heater, rather than the heater-control output or a maximum temperature threshold.

## What permits detection

The sensor needs a commanded-on bus relay, supply above 9.90 V and powertrain relay voltage above 11 V. It must have reached dewpoint and provide valid CAN data without a lost-communication condition. The failure counter is 80 out of 160 samples on a 25 ms task. The reporting designation is Type B, two trips.

Continental generation 3.5 units are additionally described as applying a 1.5-second internal debounce to electrical fault identification. That statement is qualified by sensor generation.

## Using the circuit distinction

A high fault in heater control would be P23B3 in this group; P23B9 points to the sensing path. Establishing that distinction before testing avoids treating both connections as one circuit. Check the exact service procedure for safe electrical assessment and the sensor's repairable boundaries. After correction, a completed, qualified HTemp check is the relevant evidence. Clearing the code before the sensor reaches dewpoint or observing an active heater command cannot alone demonstrate that this high sense-circuit fault has been resolved.

This explanation covers the cited 2025 GM monitor.
