# P23C5: coolant bypass valve C digital position signal high

P23C5 is the high-side range check of the coolant bypass valve C position signal. In this GM strategy the input is a SENT digital value, and the printed failure threshold is above 4,050 counts. “High” is therefore a diagnostic data-range condition, not a statement that coolant temperature or pressure is excessive.

## The communication qualifier

SENT communication must remain free of its communication error for this test to be accepted. The controller interprets an out-of-range reported position as a short-to-power condition in the signal circuit. A complete loss of SENT reporting answers a different diagnostic question.

The engine must not be cranking, run/crank must be valid and the diagnostic system must be enabled. Reporting requires four seconds within a five-second window and is Type B, two trips.

## What to retain and verify

Keep the digital position value and communication status from the event. Converting 4,050 counts directly into a terminal-voltage specification would require information this table does not provide. A valve-motion command also does not prove that position feedback remained in range. Confirmation should include valid communication and a completed position-range check after diagnosis. Component location, electrical probing details and the decision between circuit repair and assembly replacement require the exact vehicle's service procedure.
