# P249E: closed-loop reductant estimate indicates excessive dosing

P249E is the excessive-flow side of the documented PCS reductant-control diagnosis. The controller uses estimated SCR coverage and its disagreement with the chemical model, rather than a stated direct volumetric flow measurement.

## Direction of disagreement matters

For either applicable SCR unit, estimated coverage above 0.850 together with estimate-minus-model difference above 0.400 meets the initial failure criteria. This direction is the reverse of P249D's low-delivery comparison. When a fault is already active, different thresholds and repass logic apply. A result must persist for at least 60 seconds; reporting is Type B, two trips.

Qualification includes substrate temperatures between 210°C and 300°C, active dosing and estimator correction, and absence of relevant model or electrical faults. Architecture notes distinguish a second SCR unit from a second modeled brick and omit conditions for absent DEF hardware.

## Useful diagnostic decisions

First establish which hardware and modeled bricks are represented in the data. Then examine both the high estimated coverage and its difference from the model; one elevated value alone is not the complete initial criterion. Do not infer a leaking injector solely from the generic “flow too high” wording.

Confirmation requires the qualified comparison to run long enough and satisfy its appropriate repass logic. A code clear or momentary normal estimate does not reproduce that result.
