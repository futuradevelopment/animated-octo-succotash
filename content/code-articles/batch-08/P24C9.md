# P24C9: particulate sensor temperature circuit reports an electrical fault

P24C9 is named temperature circuit high, but the cited electrical monitor includes more than a high signal. The sensor control unit checks the temperature-meander signal below 0.3 V or above 3.5 V, and also checks its reference voltage below 4.5 V. The strategy describes detection of a power short or open circuit.

## Do not infer the fault direction from the title alone

Because the documented criteria include both low and high signal limits, the generic word “high” does not tell a technician which voltage was abnormal. The reference and measurement paths must also remain distinct; a low reference is different evidence from a high temperature signal.

ECM reporting requires the sensor bus relay on, no relay electrical fault, valid communication and no sensor-control-unit undervoltage fault. The counter is 24 consecutive failures or 24 out of 96 samples on a 100 ms task. The entry is Type B, two trips.

## Verification

Use the applicable service data to identify the temperature-meander status and distinguish signal-range from reference failure. A plausible temperature display by itself does not establish that both electrical checks passed. Confirmation should include valid supply and communication plus a completed sensor-control-unit electrical test. The table does not supply external probe locations or establish which component requires repair.
