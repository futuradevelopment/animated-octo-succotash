# P249D: closed-loop reductant estimate indicates insufficient dosing

P249D is labeled flow too low, but the cited PCS strategy infers the condition through SCR coverage estimates. It compares an extended-Kalman-filter estimate with a chemical model; no direct flow-meter reading is specified as the failure criterion.

## Two conditions per catalyst or brick

For SCR1, the estimated coverage ratio must be below 0.010 and the model-minus-estimate difference above 0.400. SCR2 uses a lower coverage boundary of 0.005 with the same initial difference threshold. Active-fault repass limits differ. The controller requires a result sustained for at least 60 seconds and uses Type B, two-trip reporting.

Both relevant substrate temperatures must be within 210°C to 300°C, dosing and estimator correction must be active, and chemical-model, electrical and component-management faults must be absent. Continuation rules omit nonexistent SCR2 or DEF2 hardware and explain dual-brick modeling.

## What to investigate

Identify the actual catalyst and dosing architecture before treating every condition as a second physical system. Review the estimate/model disagreement together with dosing eligibility. A low estimate with invalid model inputs is not a qualified version of this test.

Verification requires the sustained, enabled comparison. The code is evidence of insufficient dosing effect in this strategy, but does not by itself prove a clogged injector or justify replacing the pump.
