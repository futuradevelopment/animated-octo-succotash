# P26B9: coolant bypass valve C driver reports a power short

P26B9 identifies a short-to-power condition reported by the coolant bypass valve C H-bridge driver. The cited strategy monitors the energized circuit and includes separate opening and closing branches.

## What the two branches mean

For opening, the motor must be commanded in that direction and the closing-direction power-short fault must be absent. For closing, the opposite-direction qualification is reversed. Diagnostics, run/crank and driver-status validity must be acceptable, with no engine cranking. The decision uses the driver's shorted-high report, not coolant temperature or a published position error.

Both branches show an impossible-looking timing entry of 999.9 seconds within a zero-second window. No reliable setting duration can be obtained from that cell. The table still identifies Type B, two-trip reporting, but calibration-specific timing needs confirmation.

## Verification

Retain motor direction and the driver report at the fault. A high signal measured at an arbitrary point without command context is not the documented diagnostic test. Distinguish this motor-control fault from a high digital position-sensor value.

Use the appropriate service information to determine the physical circuit and corrective action. Confirmation requires the relevant energized-direction check to complete without a power-short report. Neither valve motion nor a cleared warning alone establishes that outcome, and the timing defect should not be interpreted as proof of normal operation.
