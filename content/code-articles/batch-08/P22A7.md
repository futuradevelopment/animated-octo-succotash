# P22A7: NOx sensor 2 heater resistance fails its performance check

P22A7 is a performance diagnosis for the heater-sense system of NOx sensor 2. The controller compares raw heater resistance with target resistance using a normalized difference. This differs from the explicit HTemp open, ground-short and power-short entries nearby.

## A qualified resistance comparison

The row prints positive and negative 0.06 bounds, but their inequality presentation conflicts with its “out of range” description. Those signs should not be turned into a workshop pass/fail formula without calibration-specific clarification. The supported point is that actual heater resistance is assessed relative to its target.

The enable timing is clearer: a delay exceeding 45 seconds after supply is in range above 10.8 V, more than 180 seconds after dewpoint, and more than five seconds after overrun. The table also requires valid communication and applicable combustion mode. It lists 125 failures out of 250 samples on a 25 ms task and Type B, two-trip reporting.

## What to verify

A cold resistance reading alone does not reproduce this operating performance test. Review target and actual heater behavior, if supported, after the qualifying delays and operating conditions. Also distinguish an incomplete monitor from a completed successful comparison. This source does not establish that every P22A7 requires a sensor replacement, and its ambiguous inequality prevents treating the printed numerical bounds as universal service limits.
