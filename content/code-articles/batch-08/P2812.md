# P2812: pressure-control solenoid G electrical circuit is open

P2812 is the open-circuit diagnosis for solenoid G. In this GM row the monitored function varies: line-pressure control in the named nine- and ten-speed systems, torque-converter control in the eight-speed system, or CVT mode-valve A. Identify the installed architecture before locating a component.

## Voltage feedback establishes the fault class

The controller compares circuit voltage with internal thresholds corresponding to an open path. The specification is associated with impedance above 200 kilohms between signal and controller ground; this is not an instruction to resistance-test an energized circuit.

Failure time must exceed 0.60 seconds within a sample time exceeding 0.65 seconds, with a 6.25 ms update rate. The table is Type B, two trips. Battery voltage must be between 9 V and 32 V for more than one second, an eligible power state must be present and the mapped high-side driver must be on.

## Diagnosis and confirmation

An inactive high-side supply may leave the monitor ineligible, so absence of a new code does not independently prove continuity. Review power-state and driver qualification alongside the open-circuit status. A hydraulic shift complaint is different evidence from this electrical diagnosis.

Use the exact service circuit procedure, then verify a completed qualified open-load check. The summary does not establish a universal solenoid location or require valve-body replacement.
