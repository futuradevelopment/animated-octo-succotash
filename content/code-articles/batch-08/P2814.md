# P2814: pressure-control solenoid G circuit shorted to ground

P2814 is an electrical low-circuit code for pressure-control solenoid G. The cited controller uses voltage feedback to identify a ground short, associated with less than 0.5 ohm between signal and controller ground. That impedance is a design criterion, not a live-circuit resistance-test procedure.

## The letter does not identify one universal valve

This row covers line-pressure control for its named nine- and ten-speed systems, eight-speed torque-converter control, or CVT mode-valve A. Diagnosis needs the actual transmission mapping before selecting wiring or parts.

The fault timer must exceed 0.30 seconds within a sample period exceeding 0.50 seconds, updated every 6.25 ms. Battery voltage must remain between 9 V and 32 V for more than one second. An eligible power state, calibration enablement and the applicable energized high-side driver are also required. Reporting is Type A, one trip.

## What confirms the concern

Retain electrical fault status and power/driver state together. A low hydraulic-pressure reading does not establish this electrical short, while a good pressure result does not necessarily clear an intermittent circuit issue.

After vehicle-specific circuit diagnosis, verify that the enabled ground-short check completes successfully. The source does not specify a connector pin, rank possible failure locations or prove that the solenoid itself is the defective component.
