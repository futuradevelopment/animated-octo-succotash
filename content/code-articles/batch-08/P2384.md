# P2384: EGR differential-pressure signal changes too quickly

P2384 is an intermittent-signal diagnosis for the low-pressure EGR differential-pressure sensor. The monitor compares consecutive raw readings and fails when the magnitude of their difference exceeds 10.00%. The word “intermittent” therefore has a concrete meaning here: an excessively rapid signal step.

## How the controller qualifies it

The source specifies 300 failed samples out of 600 on a 6.25 ms task. Calibration and the diagnostic system must enable the test, run/crank must be active, cranking must have ended and relay voltage must exceed 11 V. Electrical high and low faults for this sensor must not be present. Reporting is Type A, one trip.

## Why an average can miss the evidence

A steady scan average can appear reasonable while brief sample-to-sample jumps exceed the monitor's threshold. Retain the fastest useful recorded signal and the fault's operating context when supported by the service equipment. A constant bias, by contrast, is not the specific change-rate mechanism described by this code.

The electrical-range exclusions also matter: a clipped high or low signal may direct the diagnosis to another code and inhibit this one. Verification needs a completed dynamic-signal check under enabled conditions, not merely a plausible single pressure value. The table establishes the detection method but no connector location or proven failure cause.

## Source and application limits

This explanation covers the cited 2025 GM monitor.
