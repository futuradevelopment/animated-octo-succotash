# P0B84: a high block 15 report needs the correct monitor context

P0B84 is the block 15 high-voltage sensing branch in this GM document. The generic circuit O label should be interpreted through the actual vehicle's controller and battery configuration. A matching code string is not proof that all published calibration limits apply to every hybrid.

## The high branch has a narrow meaning

The source sets the range criterion at block 15 above 23 V. It requires fifteen failures out of twenty samples, with a 100 ms interval. This is evidence of a repeated high reported measurement under the monitor's conditions.

The neighboring performance test compares scaled block values with total pack voltage; the low test requires a pair of low readings. Those are separate rules. P0B84 should not be diagnosed by borrowing the criteria of either neighboring code.

## Confirm an eligible observation

The BPCM must be in RUN, its low-voltage supply must lie between 9 and 18 V, and P0A1F must not be active. If those prerequisites are missing, a code-free scan cannot establish successful completion of this test.

Retain the block 15 trace and associated faults so a technician can distinguish a real-voltage condition from inaccurate sensing using the proper circuit procedure. The table does not prescribe a replacement battery or authorize direct high-voltage measurements. Physical access and testing require the vehicle's service precautions and qualified personnel.

## Source and applicability

GM 2010 10OBDG10 Hybrid Diagnostics, cited BPCM or hybrid-controller monitor only. Historical calibration values are not universal specifications; no per-code model, engine or hardware fitment has been established.
