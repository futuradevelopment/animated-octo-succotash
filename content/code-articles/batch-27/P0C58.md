# P0C58: resolver circuit A high needs the motor B source-label caveat

P0C58 is generically motor B position-sensor circuit A high. In the GM document it appears under the motor B resolver-circuit section, but the individual row heading says motor A. That inconsistency matters before assigning the code to physical hardware and is retained for review.

## The supported electrical criterion

The row's measured quantity is resolver S13 circuit reference voltage. Its high threshold is above 3.0 V with the wakeup signal on. GM lists twenty qualifying counts among thirty at a 10.4 ms rate and one-trip reporting.

Those facts describe a reference-voltage range check. They do not establish excessive rotor angle, motor overspeed or a completed resolver-learning failure. Preserve related code states rather than collapse those different diagnostic categories.

## Confirm the channel before physical testing

The motor B section and generic code identity support the intended context, while the conflicting row heading prevents treating this summary as a definitive wiring map. Use the exact vehicle and controller service information to confirm the motor and S13 circuit assignment.

The table does not prescribe replacement or provide a safe connector test. A trained hybrid technician must establish proper high-voltage isolation for physical work and verify a credible reference signal during eligible operation. The published threshold should not be reproduced by voltage injection, and deleting the fault record does not resolve the source-mapping uncertainty.
