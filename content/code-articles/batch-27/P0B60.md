# P0B60: two low block readings define this circuit H diagnosis

P0B60 is labelled block 8 measurement out of range low in the GM hybrid diagnostic table. The code's generic H designation and the manual's block number are descriptions of the same cited monitor context, not permission to assume identical wiring on every hybrid vehicle.

## Read the complete low condition

The threshold column places block 8 below 2 V and joins it with block 9 below 2 V. The conjunction changes the diagnostic question: the evidence should show what both neighboring measurement channels were reporting. Looking only at the channel named in a generic code title can miss that distinction.

Fifteen failures out of twenty samples are needed, with a 100 ms sampling frequency. The controller consequently filters the readings instead of acting on one instantaneous sample. Its two-trip Type B classification is a separate reporting rule and should not be confused with the sample counter.

## Verify operation of the monitor

The BPCM must be in RUN, its 12 V supply must be within 9–18 V, and P0A1F must not be active. Review those conditions when a fault appears intermittent or fails to return after memory is erased.

The documented logic does not identify an individual failed cell or prescribe battery replacement. Save the paired channel values and associated faults for the applicable circuit diagnosis. Physical testing inside the traction battery requires the vehicle's high-voltage isolation procedure and qualified personnel.

## Source and applicability

GM 2010 10OBDG10 Hybrid Diagnostics, cited BPCM or hybrid-controller monitor only.
