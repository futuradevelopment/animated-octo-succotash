# P1513: Subaru idle-air signal 2 high input

P1513 is high input on idle-air control solenoid valve signal 2 in Subaru's 2001 Forester SOHC manual. It uses Subaru's shared high-input chart, but the channel-2 control wire remains separate from the first, third and fourth signals.

## A group of codes can reveal a common fault

When P1511, P1513, P1515 and P1517 occur simultaneously, the factory procedure checks the ECM's ground connection. That common-path branch is important: replacing an idle valve without evaluating the controller ground can leave the reason for multiple high-input reports untouched.

After that decision, the procedure checks the affected control line with the valve disconnected. Battery-level voltage on the isolated channel directs attention to an external power short. The chart's controller-replacement endpoints follow the specified electrical assessment, including repair of an identified harness fault, rather than following directly from P1513.

## Do not exchange high and low diagnostic routines

P1512 identifies low input on the same second channel. Its supply and continuity checks differ from the high-input chart. Likewise, a good reading on signal 1 cannot demonstrate that signal 2 has no unwanted voltage connection.

The manual associates this family with abnormal idle and stalling. Use the matching diagram and service precautions for electrical diagnosis. Following the repair, Subaru requires memory clearing and inspection mode. Verify the original channel-2 complaint and any simultaneous high-input codes, particularly when the common ECM ground was implicated.
