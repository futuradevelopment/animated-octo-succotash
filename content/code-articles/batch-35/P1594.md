# P1594: Subaru automatic-transmission diagnostic signal fault

Subaru's 2001 Forester SOHC manual defines P1594 as an automatic-transmission diagnosis input-signal circuit malfunction. This is an inter-controller diagnostic signal complaint, not a direct identification of a failed clutch or gearset.

## Shift behavior is part of the factory decision

The published chart first assesses whether automatic-transmission shift control operates correctly after warm-up. A malfunctioning shift-control result leads to its transmission-controller repair endpoint. Because that is a conditional result of the prescribed assessment, P1594 alone should not be treated as proof that the TCM must be replaced.

When shift control behaves properly, Subaru asks whether a car phone or CB radio has been installed. If such equipment is present, the chart directs attention to its grounding. This unusually specific branch matters on a vehicle with added communications equipment: an aftermarket installation can be relevant even when ordinary shifting appears normal.

## Keep the scope of the evidence narrow

The source does not say that every accessory causes P1594 or supply a universal noise threshold. Its concern is the named equipment and ground arrangement in this documented system. Record the installed accessories and observed shift behavior instead of substituting a general “transmission failed” interpretation.

A technician should assess abnormal shifting safely; do not continue a road test if vehicle control is compromised. Subaru describes detection across two consecutive faulty driving cycles and calls for memory clearing and inspection mode after repair. Verification should include the original operating complaint and signal-fault status.
