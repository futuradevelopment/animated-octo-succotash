# C0075: GM brake-pedal and booster travel disagree

C0075 is titled extended brake-pedal travel in GM's cited BSC2 table, but its exact monitor compares two travel measurements: brake-pedal stroke and booster-body stroke. The mechanical coupling means their difference should remain within a defined range.

## The measured difference gives the title context

The source flags pedal stroke minus booster-body stroke below −5.0 mm for approximately 0.04 seconds. This is a signed differential measurement. It should not be reinterpreted as a universal rule that the driver pressed the pedal more than 5 mm too far.

That distinction is diagnostically useful. The controller is assessing whether two linked movements remain mechanically plausible, not merely reading absolute pedal position. A pedal-position value that looks reasonable in isolation cannot prove that it agrees with booster-body travel.

## Detection is not a complete mechanical diagnosis

The table does not identify a specific adjustment, failed linkage, sensor or replacement assembly. The actual vehicle service procedure must establish the reason for the mismatch and evaluate related brake-system faults. Do not alter pedal linkage or attempt hydraulic disassembly from the monitor description alone.

The documented check can run in running or shutdown software states and has a one-trip Type A classification. Brake warnings, abnormal pedal feel or changed assistance require qualified inspection before further operation or dynamic testing.

Repair verification should demonstrate a credible relationship between both travel measurements and a successful diagnostic result. Simply clearing C0075 or observing one normal pedal reading does not establish that the mechanical correlation is restored.
