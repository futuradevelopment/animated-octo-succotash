# P1318: Honda detection-module reset fault: RL left-bank checks

## A reset-circuit code has its own electrical path

In the 2000 Acura RL chart, P1318 identifies the spark-plug voltage detection module's reset circuit for the left bank, Bank 2. The general Honda dictionary calls this the front bank. The factory terminology for the actual vehicle must control circuit identification; those orientation labels should not be silently interchanged.

The reset conductor is distinct from the module's spark-detection signal. For this application it is the white PHRSTL wire between PCM terminal F7 and terminal 7 of the module connector. The first electrical branches determine whether that wire is grounded unintentionally or interrupted between its endpoints. Replacing spark plugs does not test either condition.

## Why power supply remains part of the diagnosis

After the reset conductor passes, the factory chart checks the module's ignition feed. P1318 uses the black/yellow supply at terminal 5 of the eight-pin module connector. Battery voltage is expected under the specified key-on test condition.

An absent supply leads toward the wiring from the under-dash No. 20 ECU/PCM fuse, rated 20 A, to the detection module. This is a supply-path investigation, not a conclusion that the module itself has failed. Fuse numbers and connector terminals belong specifically to this RL diagram and should not be transferred to another Honda model.

## Use recurrence to interpret the result

The confirmation routine warms the engine before checking for the code at idle. If the fault cannot be reproduced, the chart identifies C451 under the right side of the dashboard, C214 at the detection module and the PCM connections for an intermittent-contact investigation.

A fault that returns after the wiring and supply checks leads to a known-good detection-module comparison. PCM comparison is the subsequent branch. The repair record should identify which failed measurement or substitution changed the result, rather than treating the reset-code wording as a parts recommendation.
