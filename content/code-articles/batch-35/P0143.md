# P0143: Honda third oxygen sensor remains low during enrichment

P0143 is generally a low-voltage fault for oxygen sensor 3 on bank 1. Honda's cited implementation adds useful context: its third heated oxygen sensor sits downstream of a NOx-adsorbing catalyst and is used to assess that catalyst. The page is specifically labeled for a manual-transmission strategy.

## A rich command gives the low reading meaning

Honda checks whether the third sensor responds toward the rich side during a rich-running phase. A signal remaining low can indicate a sensor failure or a short in the sensor circuit according to the source. The controller is not merely noticing the naturally lean exhaust of ordinary lean-burn operation.

This distinction matters when looking at a live voltage. A low value without the corresponding operating state does not reproduce the failed test. The warm-up, load and lean-burn prerequisites must also be satisfied before the monitor's result is meaningful.

## The system is more specific than a generic rear sensor

The enable conditions include a sufficient lean-burn period, selected higher gears and an IMA battery state requirement. Those features should not be generalized to every vehicle with a sensor-3 code. Nor should the sensor's catalyst-monitoring role be confused with the upstream air/fuel sensor's feedback function.

The documented strategy stores a temporary fault on the first qualifying failure and confirms the MIL on a subsequent faulty cycle. Verify the appropriate rich-phase response and completed monitor after the diagnosed repair. The source does not prove that a catalyst must be replaced simply because its downstream monitoring sensor failed this test.
