# P1366: Honda TDC sensor 2 pulse relationship fault

P1366 is labeled intermittent interruption of top-dead-center sensor 2 in Honda's Advanced Diagnostics document. Its detailed monitor is more specific than the word “intermittent”: it checks the relationship between crankshaft-position pulses and TDC sensor 2 pulses.

## An incorrect count differs from no signal

The documented criterion is a count other than eight crankshaft pulses per TDC sensor 2 pulse on 30 consecutive evaluations. Honda describes abnormal pulse counts associated with noise. Thus the monitored problem can be an unreliable pulse pattern, rather than complete absence of the sensor signal.

P1367 is the neighboring no-signal report. Treating both codes as identical loses useful evidence about whether the controller sees a corrupted relationship or no timing pulses at all. The distinction can guide signal-quality investigation under the correct vehicle procedure.

## Verify the relationship under eligible conditions

This variant runs continuously above its stated minimum engine speed of 500 rpm and excludes relevant active crank/TDC faults. The monitor can illuminate the MIL in one qualifying drive cycle and records freeze-frame information.

The page does not specify a universal sensor location, connector resistance or replacement remedy. Preserve the full code set and operating conditions, then use model-specific testing to examine both timing inputs and their paths. Confirmation should show that the crank/TDC relationship remains correct under the conditions that exposed the fault. A momentarily visible TDC waveform alone is insufficient if the relative pulse count is still wrong.
