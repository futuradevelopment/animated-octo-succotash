# P1095: Subaru right TGV actuator overcurrent

The 2001 Forester turbo manual calls P1095 tumble generator valve circuit number 1 overcurrent. This right-side actuator fault belongs to the motor-control circuit, rather than the separate position-sensor feedback circuit.

## Why the harness is checked before the assembly

The diagnostic chart isolates the actuator connection and evaluates voltage on both actuator conductors relative to chassis ground under its stated test conditions. Its 5 V decision separates an unwanted battery-voltage connection in the harness from the branch that reaches valve-assembly replacement.

That distinction matters because a motor-related code does not automatically mean an internal motor fault. If external voltage is entering the actuator circuit, replacing the intake assembly without correcting the wiring leaves the electrical cause in place. Equally, passing the external-voltage check does not mean the controller has measured a valve blockage; the factory branch identifies an assembly repair endpoint after that circuit assessment.

## Avoid confusing current and position faults

P1094 is the neighboring open-circuit fault for this actuator. P1088/P1089 instead concern its right-hand position sensor. Keeping these categories separate helps explain why the same valve assembly can generate different reports with different test priorities.

Electrical isolation and connector identification should follow the vehicle-specific diagram and procedure. The published chart is not permission to apply voltage to the actuator directly. Subaru's required after-repair memory-clear and inspection routines provide the final check that the electrical fault no longer recurs.
