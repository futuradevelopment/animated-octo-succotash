# P1346: Lexus RX300 bank-1 cam/crank relationship fault

The RX300 RM785E manual defines P1346 as bank-1 VVT/camshaft sensor range or performance. Its specific detection condition is deviation between the crankshaft-position signal and the bank-1 VVT signal over two qualifying trips.

## A sensor title can describe a mechanical relationship

The VVT pickup observes a signal plate on the intake camshaft, providing angle feedback to the engine ECU. If camshaft timing shifts relative to the crankshaft, their relationship can become wrong even though both sensors still produce signals.

That is why Lexus's inspection begins with valve timing, including a loose belt or jumped timing-belt teeth. The listed trouble areas emphasize the mechanical system and ECU. P1346 is therefore not, by itself, a command to replace the camshaft sensor.

## Distinguish deviation from signal absence

A sensor-circuit fault and an incorrect cam/crank relationship are different observations. The technician should retain the entire fault set and freeze-frame data so that electrical signal faults and mechanical timing evidence can be assessed in the proper sequence.

The source assigns this code to bank 1, with a separate bank-2 counterpart. Mechanical inspection requires correct timing references and the applicable engine service procedure; guessing belt alignment can damage an engine.

After the demonstrated timing or other fault is corrected, verification must establish the proper cam/crank relationship and successful diagnostic operation. A newly installed sensor or a cleared warning lamp does not establish that the mechanical timing relationship has recovered.
