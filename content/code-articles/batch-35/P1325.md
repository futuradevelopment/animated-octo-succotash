# P1325: Lexus RX300 coil 6 ignition-confirmation fault

In the Lexus RX300 RM785E manual, P1325 selects the number-6 ignition coil with igniter. It shares a diagnostic procedure with the other individual coil codes, but its circuit identity remains specific.

## Missing IGF is not the same as a proven failed spark plug

The source sets this fault when the ECU receives no ignition-confirmation signal during engine operation. The ECU's IGT command and the igniter's IGF response serve different purposes. A fault in either relevant signal path can affect the diagnosis, while the code itself only establishes the failed confirmation condition.

Lexus branches according to whether spark is present. It then checks the appropriate command/confirmation wiring, controller-side signals and coil supply. That sequence helps separate an igniter or wiring problem from a simple assumption that every rough-running cylinder needs a coil.

## Verify the sixth circuit, not only the shared system

A normal supply or a good signal at another ignition coil does not prove that coil 6 receives its own command and returns usable confirmation. Use the exact cylinder and connector identification in the manual, without assuming bank numbering from the code's last digit.

Testing operating ignition systems involves hazardous voltage and should use the prescribed professional methods. Preserve the original fault data and any companion codes. After correcting the established cause, verify ignition operation and the missing-confirmation monitor under the relevant conditions. Replacing all coils or clearing memory without that evidence is not a demonstrated diagnosis.
