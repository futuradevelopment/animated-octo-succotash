# P1088: Subaru right TGV low-input diagnosis

Subaru assigns P1088 to low input from tumble generator valve position sensor number 1 on the right-hand side in the 2001 Forester turbo procedure. This bank identification matters: the adjacent P1086 chart concerns the left sensor and has different signal wiring.

## A low signal has several explanations

The diagnostic sequence starts by comparing the current sensor value with 0.1 V. A value that has recovered shifts attention to intermittent contact at the valve sensor, controller or coupling connectors. The warning lamp can remain illuminated after the original electrical condition has disappeared.

If the low signal is present, Subaru's later checks distinguish a missing supply, interrupted signal continuity and a signal wire shorted toward ground. Only after those paths and connector contact have been assessed does the chart reach replacement of the tumble generator valve assembly. These branches explain why cleaning the intake valve cannot be assumed to fix an electrical low-input code.

## What the source does and does not establish

The factory table contains inconsistent throttle-related wording within the TGV procedure. This article uses its clear right-bank identification and circuit-test categories, without treating the ambiguous intermediate wording as a transferable pin-testing recipe. The exact vehicle diagram remains necessary.

The manual associates this fault family with irregular idle, stalling and poor performance, but does not establish that every vehicle will show them. Complete its inspection-mode verification after the identified circuit repair. A satisfactory result includes stable feedback, not simply removal of P1088 from scan-tool memory.
