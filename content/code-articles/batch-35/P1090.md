# P1090: Subaru right tumble valve stuck open

P1090 means the right-hand tumble generator valve system is judged stuck open in the 2001 Forester turbo manual. The system is numbered 1. Although the description sounds mechanical, Subaru deliberately checks related electrical faults before examining the valve body.

## Resolve contradictory electrical evidence first

The procedure gives priority to P1088 and P1089, the right-position sensor low/high faults, and P1094 and P1095, the right actuator open-circuit/overcurrent faults. If one of these accompanies P1090, its diagnosis comes first. Untrustworthy position feedback or a motor circuit that cannot operate normally can undermine a simple “stuck open” interpretation.

If those companion faults are absent, the chart turns to whether the valve moves smoothly and whether dirt or foreign material obstructs it. Its repair branches distinguish cleaning an obstructed valve from replacement when movement is smooth yet the diagnosed fault remains. Therefore the code alone cannot establish either a cleaning-only solution or a failed motor.

## Verify more than free movement

Mechanical inspection involves intake-component service and should follow the correct assembly procedure. A valve that feels free during inspection still needs its controlled operation verified after reassembly. Conversely, clearing the code without addressing observed obstruction does not demonstrate a repair.

The factory instructions require memory clearing followed by inspection mode after correction. Record the original related codes and the final result so that an electrical fault temporarily absent during disassembly is not mistaken for a successful mechanical repair.
