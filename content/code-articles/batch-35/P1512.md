# P1512: Subaru idle-air signal 2 low input

Subaru identifies P1512 as idle-air control solenoid valve signal 2 circuit low input in its 2001 Forester SOHC manual. The code points to the second control channel, while the factory directs it to the same low-input diagnostic framework used by the other idle-air channels.

## Common power and individual control need separate evidence

The first two branches evaluate the valve's two supply feeds from the main-relay circuit. Poor contact at the intermediate connection or an open power path is addressed there. A missing feed can compromise operation before the signal-2 conductor is even tested.

The subsequent checks use the channel-2 ECM-to-valve path for continuity and ground-short evaluation. Subaru distinguishes this conductor from those assigned to P1510, P1514 and P1516. The common procedure therefore must still be applied to the correct channel.

## Use the connector branch before replacing the valve

Once the external paths have passed, the chart examines contact at the ECM and idle-air valve connectors. Only the alternate result reaches replacement of the valve. A clean-looking valve body is not proof that its connector is electrically reliable, and an idle symptom is not proof of contamination.

The source lists irregular idle and stalling among the possible effects of this circuit family. Confirm the repair by restoring the tested signal path and carrying out the specified memory-clear and inspection routines. If several channel codes were stored, keep their identities in the repair record rather than recording only an unspecified idle-control problem.
