# P1698: Subaru torque-cut signal circuit low

In the 2001 Forester SOHC manual, P1698 means engine torque-control cut signal circuit low input. It concerns the electrical connection between the engine controller and transmission controller. The title does not measure how much torque the engine can produce.

## Separate a recovered output from a grounded wire

The factory sequence checks the ECM-side signal after a prescribed warm-up and ignition sequence. If the output is above its 3 V comparison, the chart directs attention to ECM connector contact. A normal measurement during the visit can therefore support an intermittent-contact investigation rather than disprove the stored event.

If the signal remains low, Subaru disconnects the relevant controller connections and evaluates the line for a short to ground. The next branch tests continuity between the controllers. An unwanted ground and an open connection are distinct findings, although either can prevent the intended coordination signal from being interpreted correctly.

## The endpoint is often in the connections

When continuity meets the stated criterion and no ground short is found, the chart returns to contact at the ECM or TCM connector. It does not require a blanket transmission overhaul or automatic module replacement from the code alone.

Subaru describes detection over two consecutive faulty driving cycles. Use the correct diagram and isolation procedure for electrical testing, then complete its memory-clear and inspection routines after repair. The useful confirmation is a stable, correctly transmitted torque-cut signal under the relevant conditions, together with absence of the original fault report.
