# P1091: Subaru right tumble valve stuck closed

The 2001 Forester turbo manual defines P1091 as tumble generator valve system number 1, right-hand side, stuck closed. This is a system-performance conclusion. Subaru separates it from the low/high position-sensor codes and actuator circuit codes for the same side.

## Why a closed-valve report may need electrical diagnosis

Before inspecting valve movement, the P1091 chart asks whether P1088, P1089, P1094 or P1095 is also present. These respectively concern right-side feedback and actuator circuitry. The manual directs the technician to those faults first; a mechanical conclusion is weaker while the controller's ability to move or observe the valve is compromised.

With that branch resolved, the documented physical check is smooth valve movement and freedom from dirt or foreign material. Cleaning is associated with obstruction. The alternate branch selects assembly replacement when the mechanism moves smoothly but the diagnosed malfunction remains. Neither outcome follows from the P1091 label alone.

## Distinguish the direction of failure

P1090 is the companion stuck-open report. Both charts share the preliminary electrical checks, but the recorded failed position differs. Preserve that distinction in the repair record instead of combining both as an unspecified intake-valve fault.

Service requires the correct intake and actuator procedures, including reassembly checks. Subaru calls for its memory-clear and inspection routines after repairs. Confirm that the system completes the relevant operation without returning the stuck-closed report; an empty fault list immediately after clearing is only the start of that verification.
