# P1773: Honda solenoid B circuit: resistance, supply and ground checks

## Start with the correct solenoid circuit

Honda's 1999–2000 Odyssey procedure assigns P1773 to the A/T clutch pressure control solenoid valve B circuit. Its opening note identifies an electrical fault and rules out a mechanical transmission problem as the cause of this particular code. The letter B identifies the tested circuit; it does not mean that every service operation replaces only one separately available valve.

The factory chart first measures the disconnected solenoid's resistance, expecting approximately 5 ohms. A failed result leads to the A/B solenoid assembly in the documented repair instruction. This specification and assembly arrangement are application-specific.

## Three different electrical failure paths

If the component passes, the controller connector is isolated and both solenoid conductors are checked for an unintended connection to ground. The next comparison measures resistance through the reconnected solenoid and harness from the controller end. A good component measurement combined with a failed complete-path measurement points toward an open wire or loose terminal.

P1773's later branch also checks the controller's solenoid supply. If battery voltage is absent under the stated key-on condition, Honda directs inspection of the driver's under-dash No. 6, 15-amp fuse and its supply wiring. A fuse finding should lead to diagnosis of the supply circuit, not installation of a higher-rated fuse.

## Why grounds remain part of the diagnosis

The chart checks the controller ground paths and G101 before its final controller-related branch. Poor grounding can invalidate an otherwise convincing solenoid test. Loose terminal fit is also considered before substituting a known-good controller.

The strongest repair explanation therefore includes the direct valve resistance, the isolated short/open findings, and the supply and ground results. Those observations distinguish a failed assembly from wiring or controller-support faults without treating P1773 as evidence of internal clutch damage.
