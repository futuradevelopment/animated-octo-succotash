# P1510: Subaru idle-air signal 1 low input

P1510 is idle-air control solenoid valve signal 1 circuit low input in the 2001 Forester SOHC manual. Subaru explicitly refers this code to its shared low-input procedure for signals 1 through 4. That is a documented common procedure, not evidence that the channels are interchangeable.

## Check the shared supply before channel 1

The chart first checks two power-feed connections at the idle-air valve. An open supply from the main relay or poor coupling contact can affect valve operation without proving that the signal-1 winding has failed. Only after those feeds are assessed does the sequence isolate the affected control conductor.

For signal 1, the manual identifies a particular ECM-to-valve path and tests it for continuity and an unwanted connection to ground. The separate low-input codes identify different paths through the same assembly. Testing another channel successfully does not establish that signal 1 is intact.

## Cleaning cannot repair an electrical open

The last branch checks contact at both the controller and valve connections before reaching valve replacement. This explains why an idle complaint and P1510 should not automatically be treated as a dirty throttle body. The reported problem is electrical, and supply, wiring and terminal contact are explicit alternatives.

The manual associates this circuit family with irregular idle and stalling. After the demonstrated fault is corrected, use Subaru's memory-clear and inspection routines. Confirm the affected circuit and idle operation together; a temporarily smooth idle does not rule out a connector that loses contact intermittently.
