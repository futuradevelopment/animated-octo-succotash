# P1663: Lexus RX300 bank-2 oil-control valve circuit fault

The Lexus RX300 RM785E manual assigns P1663 to the bank-2 oil-control valve circuit. Its detection condition is an open or short in the OCV circuit. This differs from a broader VVT performance fault in which commanded timing fails to change despite apparently available electrical control.

## Electrical control and hydraulic response both inform diagnosis

The factory procedure assesses the VVT response during a controlled active test, then separates valve operation, the ECU drive signal and the harness. A response when the valve is tested independently can point investigation away from the valve itself and toward its normal control path.

A normal response during inspection can also lead to the intermittent-problem branch. That is why an old P1663 does not automatically prove a permanently open solenoid winding.

## Bank identification must match the vehicle

The cited manual calls P1663 bank 2 and includes a side-specific instruction for its RX300 layout. Do not transfer the physical side designation to another Toyota or Lexus engine without confirming its bank layout. P1656 is the companion bank-1 circuit diagnosis.

The source's controlled valve-actuation procedure can disturb idle or stall the engine and requires the specified service setup. It should not be improvised by applying battery voltage to unidentified terminals.

Preserve freeze-frame information, repair the demonstrated electrical or component fault and verify both control response and diagnostic status. A cleared code alone does not demonstrate that an intermittent OCV connection has recovered.
