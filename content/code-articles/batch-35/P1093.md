# P1093: Subaru left tumble valve stuck closed

P1093 identifies the left-hand tumble generator valve system, numbered 2, as stuck closed in Subaru's 2001 Forester turbo diagnostics. It should be read together with the other recorded faults rather than translated directly into a replacement order.

## What must be resolved before a mechanical verdict

The chart first looks for left-position sensor faults P1086/P1087 and left actuator faults P1096/P1097. If any is present, Subaru sends the technician to that electrical diagnosis. A closed-position report is less decisive when the sensor circuit is unreliable or the actuator circuit cannot command normal motion.

With those faults excluded, the source considers valve movement and contamination. Obstructed movement directs attention to cleaning; smooth movement with the diagnosed fault leads to the alternate assembly-replacement branch. This explains why merely finding a stuck-closed code does not establish that deposits caused it.

## Confirm the side before intake service

There is a meaningful editorial error in the manual: this left-system page labels its mechanical check “RH.” Its heading and electrical companion codes consistently point left. Do not resolve that conflict by guessing which intake assembly to remove; confirm against correct vehicle service information.

The distinction from P1092 is the reported failed position: closed instead of open. After the actual fault is corrected, Subaru specifies memory clearing and inspection mode. Verification should show the left system operating without the same closed-position report, with any accompanying electrical codes also addressed.
