# P1768: Honda clutch pressure solenoid A electrical fault

## An electrical fault with a transmission name

The 2002 Civic HX procedure identifies P1768 as a fault in the A/T clutch pressure control solenoid valve A circuit. Honda expressly classifies this code as electrical and states that a mechanical transmission fault cannot cause this particular diagnostic code. That distinction helps prevent a circuit complaint from becoming an unsupported overhaul recommendation.

The first measurement is taken directly at the disconnected solenoid connector. The specified resistance is approximately 5 ohms for this documented application. If the direct component measurement fails, the service instruction refers to replacement of the A/B solenoid assembly. The value should not be transferred to other Honda transmissions without checking their own specifications.

## Compare the component with the complete path

A satisfactory solenoid measurement leads to harness testing with the controller connectors isolated. An unwanted path to ground identifies a wiring short. The solenoid is then reconnected and resistance is measured through the harness from the controller end. This comparison is useful: a solenoid that measures correctly at its own connector can still be unreachable through an open wire or loose terminal.

Honda next checks the controller's ground circuits, including the G101 ground connection. A failed ground path is a repair target even when the solenoid and its two circuit wires appear normal. A controller depends on those grounds to operate and assess the circuit reliably.

## When controller work becomes relevant

Software review or known-good controller substitution comes after the relevant circuit and ground checks in this flowchart. A code description alone does not establish a defective PCM. Ask for the direct solenoid measurement, the through-harness comparison and the ground result; those findings make the proposed repair much easier to assess than a parts list based only on P1768.
