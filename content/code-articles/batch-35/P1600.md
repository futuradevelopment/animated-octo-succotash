# P1600: Toyota-family ECU backup-memory supply fault

Toyota-family P1600 is illustrated here by the Lexus RX300 RM785E factory procedure. In this application, the ECU's BATT terminal receives power even with ignition off, supporting trouble-code memory and learned air/fuel-control values. The code identifies an open backup-power circuit.

## Missing memory is relevant evidence

The manual warns that when this fault appears, other trouble codes may not be retained. An otherwise sparse scan report therefore cannot be assumed to prove that no other malfunction occurred. The retention supply itself is part of the diagnostic problem.

The first check evaluates voltage at the ECU's always-live BATT input. If the expected supply is absent, the procedure checks the EFI fuse and the wiring from battery to fuse and fuse to ECU. A failed fuse leads to investigation of shorts in the circuits it supplies, rather than treating repeated fuse replacement as a repair.

## Separate backup power from normal running power

An engine that starts or an ECU that communicates can still have a defective memory-retention feed. Conversely, this source does not identify every low-voltage complaint as P1600. Match the actual Toyota or Lexus model's circuit before applying this RX300 example.

After correcting the demonstrated supply, connection or controller problem, verify reliable BATT power and retention through the relevant ignition-off cycle. Preserve whatever original data remains. A cleared lamp or a normal charging voltage measured elsewhere cannot establish that the ECU's backup-memory circuit is sound.
