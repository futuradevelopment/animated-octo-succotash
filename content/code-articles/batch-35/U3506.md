# U3506: GM control-module input B voltage high

U3506 is control-module input power B circuit high in GM's cited BSC2 brake-control table. Multiple supply-monitor branches can produce it, so the number is not equivalent to one universal overvoltage threshold.

## Duration and measurement location both matter

The source includes a sustained battery-supply condition above 17 V, a driver-module supply condition above 18 V, and a lower 16.5 V level associated with reduced performance. Their qualifying durations differ substantially: the sustained battery branch uses 90 seconds, while the other listed branches use about 1.1 seconds.

A brief spike and a prolonged supply excursion are therefore different pieces of evidence. Comparing one later battery reading against only the highest value can miss the branch that actually failed.

## The monitor does not name the failed part

These rows establish what the controller measured. They do not independently distinguish an external supply problem from wiring or measurement-related causes, and they do not authorize replacement of a brake module from the code alone. Use vehicle-specific service information and preserve related voltage data.

The documented classification is Type B with two trips, and the source gives a waiting period for its pass check. Successful verification should address the implicated supply and event duration, rather than just confirm that the scan tool can communicate.

Because this is brake-control evidence, active warnings or changed assistance require qualified service assessment. Do not use the calibration values as operational safety limits or improvise electrical tests on the brake actuator.

## Source and applicability
