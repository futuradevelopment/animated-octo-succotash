# P1596: Subaru transmission diagnostic signal high

P1596 is high input on the automatic-transmission diagnosis signal circuit in the 2001 Forester SOHC manual. Subaru's chart treats several different electrical states separately, which makes “high signal” more informative than a generic transmission-fault label.

## Excessive voltage and a missing signal differ

The first branch checks for voltage above 10 V on the ECM-side line. That finding directs attention to a battery-voltage short and possible ECM damage. Lower readings lead into further signal-state checks rather than the same repair conclusion.

When the ECM-side value is high within the later branch, Subaru compares the TCM end. A mismatch between the ends points toward an open harness connection. If the signal reaches the TCM connector, the next decisions concern connector contact and the TCM's power supply and grounds.

## A changing signal can identify an intermittent condition

The procedure also distinguishes a line changing through the expected intermediate range from one that remains abnormal. A normal response at inspection can coexist with a stored fault, prompting attention to controller connections rather than indiscriminate module replacement.

These measurements depend on the chart's particular connection states and cannot be applied as generic thresholds for other control networks. Subaru specifies two consecutive faulty driving cycles for detection, then memory clearing and inspection mode after repair. Verify the fault's original electrical behavior and the transmission controller's supply integrity when that branch was implicated.
