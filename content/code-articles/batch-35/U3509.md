# U3509: GM control-module input C voltage low

The cited GM BSC2 implementation defines U3509 as control-module input power C circuit low. Its specific measurement is voltage sensor 2. That identity matters because nearby input-A and input-B codes monitor different supply observations.

## A qualifying low reading includes operating conditions

The source combines a value below 6 V with vehicle speed above 4 mph for a 90-second qualifying interval. A momentary stationary measurement is not equivalent to this monitored event. The voltage, duration and movement conditions must be interpreted together.

This does not establish a universal minimum battery voltage for safe operation. It is the detection logic for one brake-control calibration, with vehicle fitment not established by the row alone.

## Do not verify only by cycling the ignition

The documented pass condition includes straight-ahead vehicle travel. A technician therefore needs the correct service confirmation procedure and a suitable, safe vehicle condition before performing a dynamic check. Active brake warnings or changed assistance should be assessed first.

The table does not identify the root cause as a battery, wire or module. A normal later reading can also miss a supply problem present only under the original load or operating conditions. Preserve related codes and supply information to support the diagnosis.

This variant is classified Type A, one trip. After the established fault is corrected, verify the affected input-C measurement and completion of its monitor. Removing the fault from memory or observing a stationary normal voltage is incomplete evidence of repair.
