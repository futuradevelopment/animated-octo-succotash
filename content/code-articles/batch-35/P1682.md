# P1682: Integra A/T–FI signal A high input

P1682 is high input on automatic-transmission/fuel-injection signal A in the cited 1998 Integra manual. Honda identifies the circuit as AFSA and uses a shared diagnostic chart for the A- and B-signal low/high faults.

## A shared chart still identifies a specific line

P1682 selects signal A. The B-signal codes use the separate AFSB conductor and different controller connections. Testing the B line successfully cannot demonstrate that the reported AFSA path has continuity or proper isolation.

The source first confirms the fault after operation that includes transmission upshifts and downshifts. If the code does not recur, the chart points toward loose wiring or poor connector contact at the transmission and engine controllers. A stationary normal reading may therefore be insufficient to recreate the reported event.

## Wiring checks precede the controller conclusion

For a persistent fault, Honda isolates the relevant controller connections and checks the affected line for a short to ground and an open. Its shared flowchart should be followed as written rather than replacing it with an assumed high-voltage-only parts diagnosis.

A known-good ECM comparison appears at the later endpoint after the circuit checks. The page does not establish a universal voltage threshold or prove that P1682 requires a transmission rebuild.

Verification should reproduce the relevant operating conditions safely and confirm that the AFSA fault does not return. Preserve the original combination of A/B codes, since it determines which dedicated paths require assessment.
