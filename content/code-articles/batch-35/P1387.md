# P1387: Honda CYP sensor B fault: a scoped Acura circuit example

## Match the sensor naming to the vehicle

The general Honda dictionary names P1387 as a CYP sensor B no-signal fault. The Acura RL KA9 factory pages examined here describe it as a malfunction in the CMP, also called CYP, sensor 2 circuit. Those labels identify the second camshaft/cylinder-position pickup in this documented assembly; they are not instructions to replace whichever modern sensor a parts catalog calls bank B.

The manual explains that cylinder-position information identifies the number-one cylinder for sequential injection. It locates the combined CKP/CMP assembly behind the front camshaft pulley. A circuit fault therefore needs careful identification before access or part ordering, especially because several pickup circuits share the assembly and connector.

## Compare two resistance measurements

Following confirmation of a returning code, the chart measures the indicated pickup directly at the disconnected sensor connector. The documented coil range is 650–850 ohms. It then checks that the pickup terminals are isolated from body ground. These checks address an open coil and unwanted grounding separately.

The sensor is subsequently reconnected and the corresponding circuit is measured from the disconnected controller connectors. The same resistance range is expected through the complete path. A satisfactory direct measurement accompanied by a failed controller-end measurement directs attention to the intervening wiring. The remaining branches inspect grounded conductors before controller substitution is considered.

## Handle an intermittent result accurately

If the fault does not recur, the manual identifies the right-shock-tower harness connections, sensor connector and controller terminals as inspection points. That is a documented connection investigation, not proof that the pickup is healthy under every operating condition. Exact terminal pairs must come from the matching diagram; this explanation intentionally does not infer them from a text transcription. Confirm the service edition and sensor arrangement before applying the resistance value.
