# P1592: Subaru manual-transmission neutral signal low

P1592 identifies neutral-position switch circuit low input for the manual-transmission model in Subaru's 2001 Forester SOHC manual. The transmission distinction is essential: its expected signal behavior differs from the automatic-transmission P1590/P1591 procedure.

## Neutral and engaged gear have opposite states

The factory chart expects a high ECM input in neutral and a low input with a gear selected. Its specified checks use more than 10 V in neutral and less than 1 V in other positions. Applying the automatic model's approximately 5 V non-neutral expectation would therefore misread this circuit.

The switch assessment similarly looks for an open path in neutral and continuity outside neutral. Subaru then separates the switch/transmission harness from the external ECM wiring. An unwanted ground, an interrupted wire and an unreliable connector are different possible findings, not interchangeable explanations.

## Verify the whole installed circuit

The procedure checks both signal continuity and the ground path, with different connector arrangements identified for left- and right-hand-drive configurations. That variation is a reason to use the matching diagram rather than copying terminal numbers from a general code article.

Subaru lists erroneous idle as a possible symptom and requires two consecutive faulty driving cycles for detection. Testing should be performed with the vehicle safely secured; bypassing the switch is not a repair. After correcting the demonstrated fault, verify the neutral-to-gear transition and complete the prescribed memory-clear and inspection routines.
