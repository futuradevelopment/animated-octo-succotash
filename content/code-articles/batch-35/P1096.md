# P1096: Subaru left TGV actuator open circuit

Subaru identifies P1096 as an open circuit in tumble generator valve actuator circuit number 2 in the 2001 Forester turbo manual. Number 2 is the left system, with its own actuator connector and controller paths.

## Follow the break in the control path

The first diagnostic branch measures continuity along each of the two actuator conductors after the specified disconnections. Subaru uses a below-1-ohm comparison for those paths. An unsuccessful check directs attention to an open wire or poor coupling connection between the actuator and ECM.

The next branch examines contact at the valve actuator connector. This is a separate question from conductor continuity: a harness can test intact while its assembled terminal connection is unreliable. Assembly replacement is the later outcome when the preceding wiring and contact checks do not reveal the fault.

## Related left-valve reports need this context

P1092 and P1093 describe the left valve stuck open or closed. Their charts give priority to P1096 when it is also recorded. That ordering is sensible because the controller cannot reliably assess mechanical operation while the actuator's electrical path is interrupted.

Do not substitute the right-bank P1094 terminal assignments merely because its diagnostic structure is similar. Use the correct left-system diagram and perform resistance checks only in the isolated state specified by the manual. Confirm the completed repair through Subaru's memory-clear and inspection modes, including the absence of the original actuator fault and any dependent stuck-position complaint.
