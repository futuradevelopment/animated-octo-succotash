# P1092: Subaru left tumble valve stuck open

Subaru's 2001 Forester turbo diagnostic heading identifies P1092 as tumble generator valve system number 2, left-hand side, stuck open. The useful first question is whether this position report is accompanied by a left-side electrical fault.

## Use the companion codes to choose the next branch

The factory procedure prioritizes P1086 and P1087 for left-position feedback, plus P1096 and P1097 for the left actuator circuit. Those faults can affect whether the controller moves the valve correctly or receives credible position information. Investigating them first avoids interpreting every stuck-open report as intake contamination.

The subsequent procedure distinguishes obstruction or poor movement from a valve that moves smoothly. It points toward cleaning where dirt or foreign material obstructs the mechanism, and toward assembly replacement at the alternate endpoint. These are conditional results of inspection, not a universal parts recommendation.

## A source inconsistency limits physical instructions

The P1092 heading and companion-code group identify the left system, but the printed mechanical-check row says “RH.” That conflict must not be used to justify servicing the opposite bank. Verify the vehicle's current factory instructions and assembly identification before removal. The source establishes the diagnostic priority reliably, but its copied side label needs confirmation for hands-on work.

Subaru requires memory clearing and inspection mode after repair. Verify the originally affected left system and retain the before/after codes, particularly if electrical and mechanical faults appeared together.
