# P1544: Subaru detected high exhaust temperature

P1544 reports detected high exhaust temperature in Subaru's 2001 Forester turbo manual. The procedure is broader than a sensor-circuit check: it treats other engine faults and physical exhaust defects as essential evidence.

## Related engine codes take priority

The chart first checks a substantial group of companion faults involving crank/cam signals, exhaust-temperature circuitry, airflow, misfire, mixture and oxygen-sensor systems. If one is present, Subaru directs diagnosis there rather than continuing a separate P1544 investigation. Those systems can affect combustion or the credibility of the temperature report.

This priority means a P1544 accompanying cylinder misfire should not be reduced to “replace the temperature sensor.” The misfire and related exhaust consequences require assessment in their own right.

## Inspect the exhaust when the prerequisite faults are absent

The next branch examines the exhaust manifold for insecure installation, cracks or holes, and checks installation of the front air/fuel sensor. A demonstrated exhaust-system fault leads to correcting it and addressing the pre-catalytic converter. If that inspection does not establish the cause, the source calls for further distributor-level diagnosis.

The manual lists irregular idle and poor performance as possible symptoms, but supplies no universal overtemperature number on this page. Avoid operating a vehicle showing severe misfire or exhaust-overheating symptoms. Professional diagnosis should confirm both the cause and possible heat damage. Subaru's required after-repair memory-clear and inspection routines should then be completed without recurrence of the relevant fault group.
