# P1135: Lexus RX300 front A/F sensor heater fault

In the Lexus RX300 RM785E manual, P1135 is the bank-1, sensor-1 air/fuel sensor heater circuit fault. It concerns current through the heater, not how quickly the sensor reports a change in fuel mixture.

## Either too much or too little current can trigger it

The documented conditions include heater current above 8 A or at or below 0.25 A while heating is commanded. Both electrical extremes can produce P1135. A description that assumes every case is an open heater would omit the excessive-current possibility.

The source lists heater-circuit wiring, the sensor heater and the engine ECU as trouble areas. Its inspection sequence includes the heater-related ECU voltage, sensor-heater resistance, the dedicated A/F heater relay and the connecting harness. Those are distinct checks; the code alone does not select the sensor as the failed part.

## Keep the bank and fault type clear

P1155 is the equivalent heater diagnosis for bank 2 in this manual. P1135 specifically selects bank 1 sensor 1. A normal bank-2 heater cannot establish that the bank-1 path is intact, although shared-power information may assist the full factory diagnosis.

Record freeze-frame conditions before resetting memory. Heater testing involves hot exhaust components and electrical control circuits, so use the correct service method and allow safe access. After correction, verify heater operation and the diagnostic result. Improved idle or a cleared lamp alone does not demonstrate that the original heater-current fault is repaired.
