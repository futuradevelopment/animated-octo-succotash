# P2158: vehicle-speed signal two remains unchanged inside a qualified range

P2158 is a stuck-signal check for vehicle-speed signal 2 in the cited GM 2009 hybrid table. The controller is looking for an unchanged raw signal. That is a different observation from the high-range speed value evaluated by the adjacent P2159 diagnostic.

## The unchanged signal must be qualified

The table lists vehicle speed above 6.215 mph and below 170.9 mph, with an eleven-second unchanged-signal condition. The important point is the raw signal's lack of change under the documented conditions, not an allegation that the vehicle was physically travelling at the upper boundary.

The source does not identify a universal wheel location, sensor connector or raw-data scaling for signal 2. A displayed dashboard speed that appears steady is not automatically equivalent to the internal signal the controller monitors.

## Separate a frozen report from a sensor disagreement

Record the signal identity and raw-data behavior available through the correct diagnostic tool. The nearby P215B comparison between two speed signals answers another question: whether their values are plausible together. A stuck record and an inter-signal mismatch should therefore be interpreted separately.

The parameter table supplies no road-test instruction or component replacement rule. A technician must use the applicable vehicle procedure to identify the actual source of signal 2 and determine whether its reporting path is frozen or otherwise faulty. Do not attempt to recreate the printed upper speed boundary; it is a monitor limit, not a safe test target.
