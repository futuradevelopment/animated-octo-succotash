# P2125: APP2 is out of range on the secondary processor only

P2125 is described as accelerator-pedal sensor 2 in the cited GM table, despite the broader generic sensor-E circuit title. The monitor specifically identifies a secondary-processor APP2 reading outside range while the primary-processor evaluation of that sensor remains in range.

## Keep both processor views in the diagnosis

The documented secondary limits are below 0.325 V or above 4.75 V. The row also excludes the listed five-volt-reference faults and applies supply and reduced-power qualifications. That context is necessary before treating the stored code as a simple pedal-voltage failure.

Nearby APP2 low/high rows use different combinations of primary and secondary evaluations. A displayed pedal value from one scan parameter may therefore omit the discrepancy that P2125 records. The correct vehicle data definitions determine which information is available.

## Do not assign hardware from the generic letter

The source does not establish that a universally located fifth sensor or a particular connector pin is involved. Its APP2 naming and processor split must be mapped through the actual vehicle's wiring and diagnostic information.

Retain the original code, supply/reference state and any available paired readings. The table does not prove whether the cause is in the pedal, wiring or controller evaluation. Its counter notation also does not supply a universal external test duration. Professional electronic-throttle diagnosis must isolate the cause and verify the relevant processor path under the matching vehicle procedure.
