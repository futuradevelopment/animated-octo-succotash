# P2258: secondary-air relay high circuit is separate from pump-flow performance

P2258 is the high-circuit diagnostic for the secondary-air injection relay in this GM table. The source identifies the electrical condition as a short to battery voltage. That is distinct from the system's separate pressure and air-flow performance tests.

## Preserve the electrical classification

The listed evaluation uses the stated ignition conditions, battery voltage above 11 V and absence of the specified powertrain-relay faults. The table gives a one-second condition and a two-driving-cycle classification. A power-supply problem recorded elsewhere may therefore affect how this relay report should be interpreted.

The paired P2257 row includes grounded or disconnected-circuit conditions. Those are different from P2258's high-side classification, even though both codes refer to the AIR relay circuit. The exact code should remain attached to the diagnostic evidence.

## Do not jump from relay feedback to a pump replacement

A technician needs the relevant circuit behavior and supply context, followed by the vehicle-specific wiring checks. The monitor table does not determine whether the cause is in wiring, a connector, relay circuitry or control electronics.

It also does not prove that air flow is inadequate or that a switching valve is stuck. Those questions require the applicable functional diagnostics. The source contains no relay-bypass procedure or universal terminal measurements. Professional verification should establish correct circuit operation under its enabling conditions and then address any remaining functional AIR-system faults, rather than assuming that a reset or an audible pump resolves the electrical finding.

## Source and applicability

GM 2009 09OBDG06 Engine Diagnostics, cited secondary-air relay high circuit monitor only. Document-level evidence does not establish per-code vehicle, engine or wiring applicability.
