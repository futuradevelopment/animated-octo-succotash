# P204F: reductant pressure did not build after repeated attempts

P204F is a broad reductant-system performance code, but this GM calibration gives it a specific pressure-buildup meaning. The monitored event is unsuccessful pressure development in the reductant dosing system, not a direct declaration that the fluid quality or NOx sensor is faulty.

## The control sequence qualifies the pressure

The cited failure boundary is pump-module pressure at or below 375 kPa. The controller must be in its pressure-buildup state with the corresponding status running. It also requires the documented defrost check and environmental conditions.

The table lists at least 20 buildup attempts across the pressure-buildup and ventilation states, plus minimum dwell periods in those states. Consequently, a low pressure reading before the commanded sequence has had its specified opportunity to build pressure does not reproduce the documented failure.

## Use the pressure trace with its state history

A technician needs pressure, requested state, attempt count and dwell information together. That record distinguishes a failed buildup sequence from a system that was not yet commanded or enabled to reach its working condition. Defrost and heater-related conditions may change whether the test is eligible.

The parameter row does not isolate a pump, restriction, leak or electrical supply defect. It gives no instruction to replace the complete dosing system. Follow the vehicle-specific reductant diagnostic procedure to distinguish those causes and verify successful buildup under the correct conditions. Do not improvise pressurization or disconnect a pressurized dosing line using this summary as a service method.

## Source and applicability

GM 2015 15OBDG04 Engine Diagnostics, cited ECM reductant pressure-buildup monitor. Exact vehicle, engine and calibration applicability is not established per code.
