# P2510: ECM relay faults can mean power ends too late or too early

P2510 is an ECM power-relay performance code. In the cited Duramax implementation, the controller can detect a relay that does not switch off when commanded or one that opens too early during power-down. Those opposite timing failures share this code, so “relay stuck on” is only part of its meaning.

## Two histories produce different evidence

A relay remaining on is inferred from timer values longer than would be possible if the relay opened at the end of after-run. Premature opening is indicated by a missing EEPROM write from the previous after-run. The controller is evaluating whether its shutdown sequence completed correctly, not simply checking for battery voltage at one moment.

This explains why normal operation after restarting does not exclude a power-down fault. An interrupted prior shutdown may leave diagnostic evidence even if the supply is presently restored. It also explains why a relay that switches once during inspection is not automatically proven healthy across the whole sequence.

## Preserve the transition information

GM lists checking after the engine transitions from start to run or after the power-down event, with two checks per drive cycle and first-failure detection in this calibration. Event history and companion supply faults can therefore be more useful than an isolated running-voltage snapshot.

Vehicle-specific diagnosis must determine whether the relay, its control path or power continuity caused the abnormal sequence. The summary supplies no pin test or replacement instruction. Verification needs a correctly completed shutdown and subsequent monitor assessment; clearing the record without that transition does not reproduce the test.

## Source and applicability

GM 2009 09OBDG01 Duramax Engine Diagnostics, only the cited ECM monitor. This historical calibration does not establish applicability to every Duramax vehicle or provide a complete service procedure.
