# P3409: Cylinder 2 deactivation circuit fails its startup continuity check

P3409 identifies an open control circuit for the cylinder 2 deactivation mechanism. The cited GM monitor describes the component as a gas-exchange valve and classifies low voltage during the driver's off state as the open-circuit observation. It does not diagnose cylinder compression or identify a mechanical valve that has broken.

## A commanded sequence qualifies the electrical test

The source requires the valve to be turned on and off during the beginning of the driving cycle. Engine speed must be at least 80 rpm and no more than 255 rpm, with the ECU in its specified drive state. Those conditions place the check in a narrow startup-related sequence rather than an arbitrary fully running idle observation.

Battery voltage must exceed 10.9 V and remain below 25.5 V. The listed failure must persist continuously for 0.5 seconds, with a two-trip strategy. A later normal circuit reading does not reconstruct whether the fault occurred during that qualified sequence.

## Preserve the exact electrical branch

The same source distinguishes P3411 for a low or ground-side fault and P3412 for a high or power-side fault. Although P3409 and P3411 both mention low voltage with the driver off, their internal classifications are different. A single low reading cannot substitute for the vehicle-specific procedure that separates them.

The threshold cell's resistance notation is incomplete, so this article does not turn it into a universal connector test. Verification should confirm the appropriate continuity result across the required command states. The code alone does not justify replacing a cylinder head, lifter assembly or engine controller.

## Source and applicability

GM 2020 20OBDG07 ECM Summary Tables, cylinder 2 gas-exchange/deactivation valve open-circuit monitor on physical page 418. Model fitment and external circuit-test limits are not established by this summary.
