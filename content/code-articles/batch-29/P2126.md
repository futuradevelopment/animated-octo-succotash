# P2126: pedal channel two has an unresolved range classification

P2126 is the accelerator-position channel-two rationality code in this GM 2009 table. It is distinct from P2127 and P2128, the ordinary low- and high-circuit records. The source describes a monitoring path that detects an out-of-range signal but cannot resolve the normal minimum or maximum fault classification.

## Channel two uses its own encoded range

The printed range is below 5 percent or above 50 percent, with a 100-millisecond evaluation. P2127/P2128 must not be set, pending or confirmed for the stated rationality condition. These percentages describe the table's signal representation, not a recommended physical pedal position.

The channel-one range in the same document is different. Applying its numbers to channel two would erase a meaningful distinction in this controller's evaluation. The correct scan-data and wiring definitions must establish which representation is being viewed.

## Retain classification and controller context

A technician should preserve the exact code, companion-code states and available processor information. The table's abbreviated reference to a main-processor problem does not prove that controller hardware requires replacement. It describes a diagnostic classification, not a component-isolation result.

Likewise, a driver report of poor acceleration cannot establish this specific electrical event. The matching electronic-throttle procedure must determine whether the signal path and controller evaluation are trustworthy. The source provides no pinout, pedal replacement instruction or safe operating assurance. Verification needs a qualified evaluation of the affected channel, rather than only clearing its stored record.

## Source and applicability

GM 2009 09OBDG06 Engine Diagnostics, cited accelerator channel-two rationality example only. Per-code vehicle, engine and calibration applicability has not been established; use the matching vehicle procedure.
