# P0E70: Prius charging precharge-control circuit open or high

## The fault concerns the CHRP control path

Toyota maps P0E6D15 to SAE P0E70. In the cited Prius PHEV procedure, it detects an **open circuit or short to auxiliary-battery voltage in the charging precharge-relay control circuit**. The relay is CHRP, housed with the charging relays in the No. 1 traction battery device box.

One-trip detection can command the MIL and master warning. The code does not independently prove that the precharge contact is welded or that the entire traction battery needs replacement. Its exact monitor boundary is not published.

## Live relay status changes the diagnostic route

Toyota begins by reading AC Charging Precharge Relay Status with ignition ON; the expected value is OFF. An OFF result leads to connector checks and, if those are normal, intermittent-fault diagnosis. An ON result enters a longer circuit-testing branch.

That branch checks the battery-ECU connector, control-line voltage, wiring continuity and isolation, device-box grounding and the CHRP component. Repair can involve the ECU, harness/connectors or device box according to the measured results. The component test specifies 140–290 Ω over its stated −40 to 80°C condition, but only within the exact isolated factory setup. It is not a resistance test to perform on a connected high-voltage battery.

## Confirmation differs from the low-circuit variant

For P0E6D15, Toyota specifies an ignition-off wait and ignition-on observation before HV Battery All Readiness; the page does not require the plug-in charging sequence used by P0E6D11. NORMAL is a completed pass, while INCOMPLETE or N/A needs further qualifying judgment. Verify the mirror's revision and VIN applicability, preserve initial scan data, and leave battery-enclosure access and powered measurements to a trained high-voltage technician.
