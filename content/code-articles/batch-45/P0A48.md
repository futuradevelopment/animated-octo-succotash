# P0A48: high rear-position signal does not identify a failed motor alone

The high signal associated with P0A48 is position-feedback amplitude in Toyota's documented Corolla HV application. Detailed code P0A4522 reports that the rear traction motor's resolver signal is outside its normal range on the large-amplitude side. It should not be interpreted as a high battery state of charge or an engine-speed condition.

## Use the direction without inventing a threshold

Toyota assigns one-trip detection and requests both the MIL and master warning. Its published material does not provide the voltage boundary or qualification duration, so the code description cannot supply a universal scope setting or pass/fail value.

The fault-area list includes the rear motor with its transaxle, the inverter with converter assembly, and their wiring or connectors. This is why a high signal alone cannot decide which expensive assembly, if any, requires replacement. The opposite low-amplitude entry uses the same component-level diagnostic route.

## The preceding checks are part of the diagnosis

The factory tables prioritize relevant hybrid insulation problems before processing, communication and reference-power faults. The dedicated procedure then separates a connection that is simply insecure from one with damaged contacts, water or foreign matter. Those observations change the repair direction before the resolver circuit is evaluated.

Only after the referenced rear-resolver inspection is normal does the flow lead to inverter replacement. Toyota also recognizes that diagnostic vibration or heat checks can cause a detection boundary to be crossed, requiring the test conditions to be considered when interpreting a newly stored code.

Completed All Readiness evaluation after the prescribed operating pattern is the repair check. High-voltage component access and any reproduction work belong within a qualified technician's complete manufacturer procedure.
