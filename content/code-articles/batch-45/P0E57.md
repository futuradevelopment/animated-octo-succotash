# P0E57: Toyota pre-boost overvoltage system fault

## Overvoltage before the boost stage

Toyota maps P0E5717 to SAE P0E57. The cited Corolla HV document describes **overvoltage detected before boosting**, at the VL side of the boost converter. It treats this as a system malfunction rather than the simple stuck-high voltage-sensor condition covered by another code.

Potential causes span the inverter, motor or generator coils and resolvers, battery high-voltage supply path, junction block, fuse and associated wiring. Rear-motor components are additionally listed for 4WD. One-trip detection applies with MIL and master-warning indications; the precise overvoltage threshold is not disclosed.

## The diagnosis depends on context and configuration

Toyota first examines diagnosis-related communication information and freeze frame. U11B300/U11B387 information redirects the chart; an ON Emergency Shutdown Signal can instead send diagnosis to P321E9F. The next stages check the inverter connector and generator/front-motor resolver circuits.

The procedure then separates 2WD and 4WD vehicles. Both branches check the relevant high-voltage supply circuits and inverter low-voltage circuit; 4WD adds rear-resolver and rear-motor circuit checks. Inverter replacement comes only after those inspections are normal. The broad fault label does not select an inverter, battery or motor by itself.
