# P0A91: Toyota rear-motor performance and magnetic-force loss

## Drive motor B is the rear traction motor

Toyota P0A9100 maps to SAE P0A91. For this Corolla HV configuration, drive motor B is **rear motor MGR**. The motor generator control ECU detects reduced magnetic force in that motor. Toyota's monitor description includes demagnetization or a same-phase short, while its possible-cause list also includes inverter, cable, connector and control-system faults.

The rear traction motor with transaxle assembly is therefore one diagnostic area, not the only possible repair. The fault uses one-trip detection and can command both the MIL and master warning; numerical detection thresholds remain unpublished.

## The rear-motor conclusion comes after other branches

Toyota first examines diagnosis-related information and freeze frame. U11B300/U11B387 information takes a communication-fault route. An ON Emergency Shutdown Signal, without that communication result, directs diagnosis to P321E9F instead.

The procedure then uses controlled operating checks to see whether the result remains P0A9100/P0C021D or changes to other faults needing their own charts. It next checks the inverter connector, shutdown-signal circuit and rear-motor high-voltage circuit. If those inspections are normal, the flow directs replacement of the rear traction motor with transaxle assembly. Defective connection conditions have separate repair branches before that endpoint.

## Recording a completed recovery

Toyota verifies repair with its specified operating pattern and Motor Generator All Readiness. A completed NORMAL judgment is the endpoint; INCOMPLETE needs further qualifying operation. Retain the original full scan and freeze frame, especially if the problem followed cable or connector work. This diagnosis involves high-voltage components and vehicle movement, so the detailed tests belong to a trained hybrid technician using the exact model procedure.
