# P0A30: Ariya rear-motor sensor fault and overheating use different suffixes

## The suffix changes the diagnostic question

Ariya's rear-inverter documentation separates **P0A30-11**, an open or short in the rear-motor temperature-sensor circuit, from **P0A30-4B**, a detected rear-motor stator overheat error. Both are enabled in READY, but they do not justify the same initial repair.

For suffix 11, Nissan's protective response sends the VCM a rear-motor temperature of **180°C**. That is a substituted fault value, not proof that the motor physically reached 180°C. The repair chart directs diagnosis of the rear-motor temperature-sensor circuit, with harness, sensor and inverter among possible causes.

## The overheat branch checks cooling first

Suffix 4B limits commanded rear-motor torque to zero. Its diagnosis starts with related high-voltage cooling-system codes, then checks coolant quantity or leakage, restricted or twisted cooling hoses, and rear-motor oil leakage. Only after those branches does it return to the temperature-sensor circuit.

This order prevents a temperature-sensor replacement from concealing an actual cooling problem. Conversely, treating the suffix-11 substitute temperature as a measured overheat could send diagnosis down the wrong path.

## Confirmation also differs

The circuit fault uses a short READY-state confirmation. The overheat procedure calls for a warmed vehicle and a prescribed loaded driving pattern. That pattern should be performed only by a qualified technician in a suitable controlled setting, not copied into an owner road test.

Retain the complete suffix, scan temperatures, associated cooling codes and drivetrain configuration. These procedures concern an Ariya equipped with a rear traction motor. Use the exact VIN-specific manual and EV precautions for sensor, cooling or motor work.
