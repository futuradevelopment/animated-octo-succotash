# P0C66: Toyota generator resolver sine signal below threshold

## Low signal does not mean low generator output

Toyota maps SAE P0C66 to P0C6416 in the cited Corolla HV system. The motor generator control ECU sees the **generator resolver sine-phase signal below its low-side threshold**. The generator is MG1 and the resolver measures position. This code therefore concerns a position-signal circuit, not proof that the generator's electrical output or traction-battery charge is too low.

The distinction from a resolver open-circuit fault is important. Toyota's summary for this particular low/high signal family suspects the MG ECU circuit board or an incorrectly installed low-voltage connector. It specifically says that a motor-resolver inspection is unnecessary for this branch.

## How the service path is bounded

The P0C6416 row uses one-trip detection and commands the MIL and master warning. Monitoring is continuous, but the page does not disclose the actual low-side voltage limit or duration. A numerical threshold from another model would not be supported here.

The diagnostic priority tables address insulation faults first, then related controller, communication and reference-power issues. The procedure itself checks the inverter connector. A loose connector is secured, while damaged terminals, poor contact or foreign material lead to harness/connector repair. When the connection is normal, Toyota directs replacement of the inverter with converter assembly. The source does not direct transaxle replacement for this code's branch.

## Checking the result

Toyota's prescribed operating sequence ends with Motor Generator All Readiness. A NORMAL completed judgment confirms the monitor passed; an INCOMPLETE judgment needs another qualifying sequence. Preserve detailed codes before clearing them, because the suffix separates this low-signal fault from other resolver problems. The low-voltage wording does not remove the hazards of inverter access; professional high-voltage precautions remain necessary.
