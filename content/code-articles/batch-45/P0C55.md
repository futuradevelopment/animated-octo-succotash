# P0C55: Toyota rear-motor resolver sine circuit open

## Drive motor B and circuit A identify different things

Toyota maps P0C5513 to SAE P0C55 in the documented Corolla HV configuration. The affected machine is **rear motor MGR**, called drive motor B. Within its resolver, circuit A is the sine-phase position signal. Toyota detects an open in that signal circuit.

Keeping those two labels separate matters: this is neither the front MG2 resolver nor the cosine channel of the rear resolver. Toyota lists the rear traction motor with transaxle assembly, inverter with converter assembly, and the connecting harness or connectors as possible fault areas.

## How the manufacturer narrows those possibilities

The ordinary open variant uses one-trip detection and commands the MIL and master warning. Its intermittent counterpart P0C551F maps to P0C59 and has additional companion-code conditions. Detailed threshold and duration values are not published for the shared monitor.

Toyota gives priority to listed insulation faults and then associated controller, communication and rear-motor reference-power problems. The actual procedure checks the inverter connector before following the rear-motor resolver circuit inspection. Insecure engagement is corrected; damaged or contaminated terminals lead to harness/connector repair. A normal resolver-circuit inspection is required before the flow reaches inverter replacement.

## Confirming restored position information

The manual notes that resolver faults may affect smooth driving and that diagnostic heat or vibration can influence detection. Those observations should be recorded without treating an induced code as an automatic component verdict. The professional confirmation uses the specified operating sequence and All Readiness judgment. An incomplete monitor is not a pass. Rear-motor and inverter access require the vehicle's high-voltage precautions and exact configuration-specific instructions.
