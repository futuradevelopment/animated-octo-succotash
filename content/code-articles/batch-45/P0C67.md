# P0C67: Toyota generator resolver sine signal above threshold

## What is high in this fault

P0C67 is Toyota's SAE mapping for P0C6417. In the documented Corolla HV, it means the **generator resolver sine-phase signal exceeds its high-side threshold**. The measured quantity is a position-sensor signal evaluated by the motor generator control ECU. It is not a direct report of high generator speed, charging voltage or battery state of charge.

Toyota groups this code with three other generator resolver low/high signal faults. For this family, its summary points to an internal inverter circuit-board problem or an incorrectly installed low-voltage connector. It expressly states that inspecting the motor resolver is not necessary in this branch.

## The evidence needed before assembly replacement

One-trip detection applies, with the MIL and master warning commanded on. The monitor runs continuously, although its exact upper boundary and detection duration are not published. The word high cannot be converted into a reliable meter specification from this page alone.

The factory priority tables first address isolation problems and then relevant controller, communication and reference-power faults. The direct inspection concerns the inverter connector: secure an improperly engaged connector, or repair the harness/connector if contact damage or contamination is present. A normal connection sends the factory flow to inverter with converter assembly replacement. That sequence leaves no basis for claiming the code itself proves a defective transaxle.

## Verifying the repaired circuit

Toyota requires its normal-judgment operating sequence and All Readiness assessment. NORMAL, ABNORMAL and INCOMPLETE must be interpreted separately; clearing memory is not a substitute for a completed passing monitor. Full Toyota suffixes and companion faults should be retained in the work record. Any inspection at the inverter requires the model-specific high-voltage safety process and qualified personnel.
