# P011A: Honda coolant-temperature comparison after a long soak

## The comparison needs temperatures that have stabilized

Honda's P011A extract identifies an **ECT sensor 1 range/performance problem** within a comparison involving two coolant-temperature sensors and intake-temperature inputs. After a sufficiently long shutdown, these temperatures should approach the surrounding air temperature. The diagnostic table distinguishes possible sensor disagreements rather than treating any difference as proof of overheating.

The monitor runs once per drive cycle after at least six hours of engine-off time. Two-drive-cycle detection applies, with the first event pending and a repeated qualifying event illuminating the MIL and storing confirmed fault and freeze data.

## Conditions outside the sensor can distort the comparison

Honda lists several temperature sensors and a non-genuine engine-block heater among possible causes. Its confirmation warns against using a block heater, because it prevents the temperatures from equalizing. It also calls for suitable ambient conditions and notes that abrupt environmental temperature changes may prevent a valid judgment.

The separate troubleshooting page checks connections and loose terminals at ECT sensor 1, ECT sensor 2 and the MAF/IAT sensor 1 connector. A connection defect is repaired and rechecked. If those connections are sound, the documented branch directs ECT sensor 1 replacement. That branch is more specific than replacing every sensor mentioned in the monitor overview.

## Record the soak and comparison data

Preserve the original temperatures, shutdown duration and snapshot before clearing. A reading taken just after a warm engine stops is not equivalent to the required cold comparison. The exact sensor-difference matrix belongs to the applicable manual and should not be reduced to a universal allowable difference. Verify VIN applicability for these third-generation Insight extracts and have a technician perform the model-specific confirmation.
