# P0C69: Toyota generator resolver B open circuit

## Meaning of circuit B

SAE P0C69 is explicitly linked to Toyota P0C6913 for this Corolla HV. Toyota detects an **open in the generator resolver cosine-phase circuit**. The generator is MG1, and circuit B denotes its cosine signal. Confusing that letter with drive motor B would send diagnosis toward the wrong machine.

The motor generator control ECU evaluates the resolver's position information. Toyota lists possible trouble in the inverter with converter assembly, hybrid transaxle assembly, or connecting harness and connectors. That range is why a scanner's open-circuit description does not justify immediately ordering a generator or transaxle.

## What the inspection sequence establishes

The monitor is continuous and uses one-trip logic. Both the MIL and master warning are commanded for P0C6913. Exact numeric thresholds and detection duration are proprietary in this page. The intermittent counterpart P0C691F is separately mapped to P0C6D and depends on companion faults.

The factory diagnostic priority starts with the listed insulation problems, then relevant control-module, communication and reference-power faults. Once those priorities are resolved, the procedure checks the inverter connector. Loose engagement, damaged contact and contamination have their own corrective branches. With that connection normal, Toyota calls for the generator resolver circuit inspection; only after that inspection passes does the flow end at inverter replacement.

## Confirming the diagnosis after repair

Toyota's normal-judgment procedure includes several operating states before All Readiness is assessed. A cleared code alone is weaker evidence than a completed NORMAL result, and INCOMPLETE calls for another qualifying sequence. The manual notes that resolver malfunction can disturb smooth driving and that diagnostic vibration or heat may influence detection. Keep those observations in the repair record and use professional high-voltage precautions throughout.
