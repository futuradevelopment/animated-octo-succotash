# P0D98: Ariya charge-port lid circuit differs from the coupler label

## The Nissan component name needs to stay explicit

The catalog calls P0D98 a **battery-charger coupler unlock control performance** fault. Nissan's Ariya procedures retain the coupler heading but diagnose the **charge-port lid actuator positive signal**. This draft is marked **needs-review** because a charge-port lid actuator should not be assumed to be the charging-connector latch without resolving that terminology in current service information.

The source distinguishes three suffixes: **11** for a short to ground, **12** for a short to the power supply, and **13** for an open circuit. Each requires more than two seconds of detection. These are different electrical findings even though the five-character base code is identical.

## Circuit diagnosis precedes the VCM decision

All three displayed procedures first direct inspection of the charge-port lid actuator circuit. A failed inspection leads to repair of the identified component or wiring. If that circuit passes, the procedure reconnects the removed parts and checks VCM output signals against the applicable physical-value information.

VCM replacement is the outcome only when that output check is abnormal. The source therefore does not justify a replacement control module simply because a charge-port mechanism failed to move.

## Verify identification before repair

Record the complete suffix, observed mechanism behavior and original fault status. Nissan's confirmation requires adequate 12-volt battery voltage and an undisturbed five-minute shutdown interval; door operations can restart the accessory-power waiting period.

Resolve the lid-versus-coupler terminology and verify VIN applicability before component selection. A qualified EV technician should follow the linked circuit procedure and charging-system precautions, without forcing the lid or connector mechanism.
