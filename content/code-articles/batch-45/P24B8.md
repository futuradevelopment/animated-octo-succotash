# P24B8: Honda leak-check pressure-sensor diagnosis has a chart discrepancy

## A low sensor signal triggers this monitor

Honda identifies P24B8 as an **EVAP leak-check-module absolute-pressure sensor range/performance** fault. The advanced page gives a sensor output of **0.87 V or less for at least six seconds**, with the vehicle on and two-drive-cycle MIL logic.

The troubleshooting chart first gives P24BA and P24BB priority if present. It then uses the HDS absolute-pressure reading, including a comparison with 373 mmHg, to separate the reproduced condition from an intermittent event. This is a pressure-sensor diagnosis, not direct proof of a fuel-vapor leak.

## Wiring checks distinguish several outcomes

The procedure investigates a grounded P/SNSR1OUT signal, an open VCC6 five-volt reference circuit and connector condition. A proven wire defect leads to wiring repair. In the appropriate branch, an available reference supply leads toward leak-check-module replacement; other branches require authorized service-information review or a known-good PCM substitution before deciding on PCM replacement.

Those conditional outcomes should not be collapsed into “replace the module.” The voltage, wiring and substitution results determine which conclusion the chart supports.

## One branch requires source review

This draft is marked **needs-review** because the final reference-circuit branch asks whether **P24BA** disappears, even though the procedure heading is P24B8. The available extract does not resolve whether that cross-reference is intentional or an editorial error. It must be checked in current VIN-specific Honda information before following that final PCM decision.

Preserve the complete EVAP fault set and pressure data. The confirmed monitor and wiring distinctions remain useful, but the inconsistent final branch is not silently corrected here.
