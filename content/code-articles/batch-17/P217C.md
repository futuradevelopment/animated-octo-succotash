# P217C: Group G high supply must be evaluated while its driver should be off

P217C is the high supply-circuit diagnostic for injector group G. The GM row identifies the channel as injector 7 high-side driver and tests its feedback during the off state. The fault classification concerns an electrical connection toward power.

## A high reading is abnormal only in the right context

The source compares signal with controller power and specifies a separation no greater than one volt. This should not be reinterpreted as a one-volt signal-to-ground test. Without the power reference and command state, an isolated measurement cannot establish the same criterion.

Battery voltage must be at least 11 V and the specified P062B diagnostic states absent. Ten failures within twenty samples are counted at a 100-millisecond interval, with one-trip reporting. One normal reading later in the drive does not reproduce or dismiss that earlier sequence.

## Retain the electrical finding without inventing fitment

Record the off command, feedback relationship and controller supply for the named output. A high signal captured during an intentional injection command may belong to normal operation rather than the documented failure opportunity.

The generic group-G label and GM's output numbering do not supply a universal physical-cylinder location. The monitor also cannot prove that the injector is mechanically leaking or select a failed harness section or driver. Use the exact system's circuit and diagnostic information to locate the power-directed path before choosing a repair.
