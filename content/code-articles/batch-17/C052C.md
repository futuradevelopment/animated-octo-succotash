# C052C: An ABS motor-control fault can concern the internal gate-drive regulator

C052C carries a generic ABS pump control-circuit/open description, but the cited GM row addresses the driver's internal regulated supply. That regulator feeds the low-side gate drive and supplies bootstrap charging current. The article's scope is this particular controller interpretation, rather than a universal open-wire diagnosis.

## The supply being assessed is inside the driver

The motor-driver ASIC sets its regulator fault indication when the regulated voltage crosses either its lower or upper limit. The threshold is represented by status bits; the table does not publish a numeric regulator-voltage limit suitable for a connector test. A satisfactory vehicle battery reading therefore answers only part of the electrical question.

The evaluation requires the power-switch self-test to have completed, active enable feedback, an enabled driver and valid serial data without a serial-interface fault. Vehicle supply must also exceed 6.8 V. Maturation is listed as 390 milliseconds.

## Separate eligibility from the fault finding

If serial communication is already invalid, the stated conditions for this regulator assessment are not met. Preserve that ordering instead of treating an absent regulator result as proof that the internal supply passed.

If the conditions are valid and the regulator status recurs, it identifies an internal operating-limit problem, not automatically an open external motor cable. The summary supplies no board-level isolation procedure or replacement decision. Because this belongs to brake control, module and motor-driver investigation should follow the matching service information.
