# P001A: An intake profile-actuator open circuit is tested during the on command

P001A identifies an open control circuit for the bank 1 intake camshaft profile actuator A in the generic reference. GM's cited implementation names this intake profile actuator 1 and evaluates its electrical driver while the output is powered on. A stored code therefore concerns the commanded circuit response, not direct proof that the camshaft's mechanical timing is wrong.

## The driver must have a valid test opportunity

The controller compares its voltage feedback with a controller-specific acceptable range. The published open-circuit specification is an impedance above 200 kΩ between the signal and controller ground. That specification explains the classification; the table does not supply connector identification or an instruction to measure resistance on an energized circuit.

The monitor requires an enabled diagnostic, supply above 11 V and an ignition state of crank or run. Four failures within five samples establish the reported result, with one-trip reporting.

## Interpret command and feedback together

The meaningful comparison is the driver response during its on command. A measurement obtained only while the output is off cannot reproduce this particular assessment. Similarly, low supply can invalidate the stated operating opportunity, so it should not be silently treated as a confirmed actuator failure.

Preserve the commanded state, supply condition and repeated failure evidence. The source does not distinguish an interrupted load path from an internal driver defect or authorize a specific replacement. Matching wiring and actuator service information is needed before moving from the monitor finding to a repair.
