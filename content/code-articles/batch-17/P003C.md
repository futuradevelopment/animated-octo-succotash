# P003C: Unexpected intake profile-pin movement needs the command history

The generic P003C description includes intake profile-actuator A performance or stuck off on bank 1. GM's cited row addresses a more particular event: intake profile actuator 1 appears to fire its pin without a controller command. The command history is therefore essential to interpreting this implementation.

## Watch the sequence, not only the final position

The system distinguishes extending, fully extended and retracted feedback states. The fault assessment asks whether feedback crossed into the extension region even though the controller had not requested that action. An extended pin following a genuine command is a different event and does not establish this failure by itself.

The table lists three numerical feedback boundaries, but does not identify a usable physical unit in the row. They should not be converted into invented millimetres, volts or duty-cycle percentages. What is clearly supported is the relationship between the states and the absent command.

## Establish a valid monitoring opportunity

The engine must be running, system supply must exceed 11 V and the diagnostic must be enabled. Four incorrect position observations out of five cylinder events confirm the condition, with one-trip reporting.

Preserve command timing and feedback transitions together. A single final-position screenshot cannot show whether movement was requested. The generic stuck-off wording and this uncommanded-motion strategy require vehicle-specific interpretation; neither establishes a broken actuator or justifies manually forcing the cam-profile mechanism.
