# P216F: A high group F supply fault is an off-state relationship to controller power

P216F names the high supply-circuit condition for injector group F. The cited GM strategy monitors the output called injector 6 high-side driver while it is switched off. The electrical classification is a connection toward power, not a direct finding of excessive injection quantity.

## The relevant comparison uses two voltages

The table specifies a difference of at most one volt between signal and controller power. That is a relative criterion. It does not instruct a reader to look for one volt from the signal to chassis ground, and it does not identify a connector terminal for an external test.

Battery voltage of at least 11 V and absence of the stated P062B fault conditions are prerequisites. The confirmation history is ten failed samples within twenty on a 100-millisecond loop, with one-trip reporting.

## Keep the off command in the record

A high signal during an intended on period may have a different meaning from the same signal during the documented off test. Capture command, output and power reference together so that the comparison is not reconstructed from unrelated moments.

The output-6 label is specific to this GM implementation and must not become a universal physical cylinder assignment for group F. The monitor does not locate the power-directed path inside the injector, harness or controller. Further circuit checks and any replacement decision require the matching vehicle service procedure.
