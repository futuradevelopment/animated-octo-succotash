# P0726: Engine-speed performance includes jump, noise and coupling checks

P0726 is an engine-speed input range/performance code. The cited GM transmission monitor distinguishes a large speed change, a noisy signal and a possible coupling-related pattern. Those branches are more specific than a generic instruction to replace an engine-speed sensor.

## Separate a jump from repeated noise

One branch detects a speed change of at least 600 rpm persisting for at least 0.15 second. Another evaluates eighty samples, incrementing separate counters for sufficiently large negative and positive changes. An isolated step and alternating excursions therefore contribute different evidence.

The noisy-signal row gives the positive and negative increment boundaries but does not display complete final counter comparisons. The coupling-related branch also contains incomplete conditions. This article does not fill those gaps with invented counter limits or claim to reproduce every branch numerically.

## Preserve the sequence and enabling fault status

A useful record includes the speed trace over time and which performance branch reported the fault. A smoothed average can conceal the excursions relevant to the noise assessment. Existing input-speed faults and per-key-cycle diagnostic states also affect the listed eligibility conditions.

The diagnostic task runs every 20 milliseconds and uses two-trip reporting, while individual branches have their own timing. The source supports distinguishing these signal patterns but cannot identify the exact electrical cause, establish a physical sensor location or justify a replacement without the matching transmission diagnostic procedure.
