# P2188: Bank 1 rich-at-idle monitoring uses a learned negative additive correction

P2188 identifies the bank 1 rich-at-idle system condition. In the cited GM monitor, the assessed quantity is the additive part of long-term fuel trim in gasoline operation. The code represents a learned correction beyond its permitted boundary, rather than direct proof of a leaking injector or another particular component.

## The negative boundary belongs to one trim component

The documented failure threshold is below −5.48 percent for bank 1's additive trim. The monitor requires additive adaptation to be active and applies stability conditions to the multiplicative adaptation process. Its continuation includes more than ten seconds of stability and restrictions on correction changes and comparable operating points.

A short-term fuel-trim spike is therefore not the same measurement. An unidentified combined trim value also cannot be judged against the additive boundary without confirming what the diagnostic parameter represents.

## Verify readiness before judging a new reading

The row's 0.2-second evaluation is separate from the longer eligibility conditions, and reporting is two-trip. A return to an apparently normal idle display before adaptation becomes stable does not demonstrate that this specific monitor passed.

Retain the additive value, bank identity, adaptation status and operating-state history. The source supports investigating the learned rich-direction correction but provides no ranked causes, connector procedure or component replacement conclusion. The applicable controller's parameter definitions and service tests are needed to connect this scoped result with a repair.
