# P2078: A high tuning-position code has an impossible printed voltage condition

P2078 is the high-circuit entry for intake manifold tuning-valve position on bank 1. The GM strategy names a maximum-range assessment of the raw position voltage. However, its printed malfunction condition cannot be used literally as a valid numerical test.

## The source does not provide a consistent high threshold

The original row requires the same raw voltage to be below 4.8 V and above 6.55 V at once. Visual inspection confirms both inequalities and the joining word. This article does not reverse a sign, substitute an OR relationship or borrow a threshold from a different sensor to hide that contradiction.

The surrounding conditions remain useful: battery voltage is above 9 V and below 17.5 V, the evaluation interval is 0.05 second and reporting is two-trip. The feedback configuration must also match the controller's listed enable conditions.

## Retain evidence without asserting the wrong failure boundary

Record raw position data, signal format, supply and the exact diagnostic status. A decoded valve angle is not interchangeable with the raw electrical quantity. The code's high-range name also does not demonstrate that the mechanism is stuck open or that the manifold needs replacement.

The matching controller calibration or service procedure must establish the intended high boundary before a reproduction test is interpreted. Until that issue is resolved, the source supports identifying the monitored signal and its operating context, but not a complete numerical diagnosis or a specific repair.
