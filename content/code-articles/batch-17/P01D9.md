# P01D9: Cylinder 8 retarded-injection evidence depends on a completed learning cycle

P01D9 describes over-retarded injection-timing performance on cylinder 8. GM's documented monitor looks for positive drift in small-quantity injection behavior, using controlled injections and rail-pressure evidence. This is a learned performance assessment rather than a simple electrical continuity check.

## How the injector is characterized

The strategy commands known small quantities during eligible fuel-cut operation and observes the pressure reduction in the common rail. It uses that response to estimate the energizing time needed for the intended quantity. It also determines the pressure signal's falling-edge timing and relates it to crankshaft position.

Multiple trial quantities contribute to the result. The source describes stopping after the target is bracketed by the last learning steps or after the allowed number of steps is exceeded. A partial trial sequence does not carry the same meaning as a completed assessment.

## Separate a stored result from present eligibility

The summary allows interrupted learning to resume on a later opportunity. Consequently, current driving conditions alone may not describe the conditions that supplied all of the evidence behind the stored result. Preserve completion status and the type of drift that was reported.

The quantity and timing diagnostic enables matter as well: pressure-drop magnitude and angular injection-start displacement are different channels of evidence. Neither should be substituted for the other when interpreting scan data. The table does not establish a replacement decision for injector 8 or authorize invasive high-pressure testing without the applicable service instructions.
