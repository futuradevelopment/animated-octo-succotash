# C052D: Read the motor-driver status before calling this battery overvoltage

C052D is labeled an ABS pump motor-control high-circuit fault in the generic reference. In this GM brake-controller table, the associated assessment watches the motor driver's external switching bridge. The diagnostic classification should not be translated automatically into an overcharging battery.

## What the bridge monitor compares

The documented driver evaluates differences between switching-node terminals and compares them with a hardware-configured threshold. Its fault and drain-source diagnostic status bits report the outcome. The summary discusses load connections toward ground or supply as possible electrical conditions that the bridge circuitry can distinguish; it does not provide a universal numerical voltage for hand-held probing.

A completed power-switch self-test, enabled driver, active enable feedback and valid serial information are prerequisites. The listed battery condition is a minimum above 6.8 V, and fault maturation is 390 milliseconds. That minimum enables the test; it is not the high-circuit failure threshold.

## Keep status and voltage evidence together

A useful record pairs the bridge diagnostic status with the controller's enable and serial-communication state. A battery trace alone cannot reveal which internal terminal comparison failed. Conversely, an invalid serial stream can prevent reliable interpretation of the driver result.

These distinctions direct investigation toward the documented bridge assessment without identifying a specific failed transistor, pump or harness section. Brake-system testing must use the exact controller's service procedure; this monitor summary is not a safe substitute for its circuit isolation instructions.
