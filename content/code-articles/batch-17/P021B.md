# P021B: The cylinder 8 injector can fail a current-rise timing check

P021B identifies a cylinder 8 injection-timing fault. In this particular GM table, the measured quantity is the pull-in duration at the beginning of the injector current pulse. A timing result in microseconds should not automatically be interpreted as an advanced or retarded combustion event.

## Recognize the electrical time window

The hardware interface measures the rise toward the stated 20 A current level. A pull-in interval shorter than 50 microseconds or longer than 90 microseconds is the documented failure condition. The controller samples by engine cycle and confirms ten failures within twenty observations, with one-trip reporting.

Battery voltage above 11 V, key-on operation and diagnostic enablement are prerequisites. Existing injector-circuit and boost-supply faults can prevent the stated test opportunity. The monitor also requires no reported dropped pulse, so interrupted pulse delivery is not equivalent to a valid but slow current rise.

## Keep request status with the measured interval

An investigation should distinguish a requested injection with an out-of-range ramp from an event without usable pulse information. That distinction prevents a missing opportunity from being labeled a failed timing measurement. The source does not establish whether a confirmed ramp problem originates in the injector or control hardware.

The row's cylinder 8 description is accompanied by some cylinder D identifiers in its enable logic. Their physical mapping remains unresolved, and the current target is not a manual energizing instruction. Consult the matching system procedure before identifying a component or attempting high-pressure fuel work.
