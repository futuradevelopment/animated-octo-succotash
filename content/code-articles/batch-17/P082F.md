# P082F: A high Y-axis duty signal is an electrical-range result

P082F selects the high-circuit assessment for gear-lever Y position sensor 1. The documented GM strategy watches the raw duty cycle on that channel. It does not infer which gear has mechanically engaged from the high percentage alone.

## Repeated readings establish the fault

The published upper limit is above ninety-five percent. The controller must first remain awake for at least 0.05 second. Ten failing observations within twelve samples are then counted on a 25-millisecond loop, with two-trip reporting.

These conditions distinguish a qualifying high signal from a brief startup transition. They also show why an isolated average value may not preserve the failure history: the monitor uses a sequence of observations after its enable delay.

## Retain Y-axis information independently

The X and Y channels represent different monitored inputs, and this code specifically names sensor 1. A satisfactory value on another channel cannot substitute for the Y duty-cycle evidence. A decoded selector label likewise answers a different question from the raw electrical-range test.

The source provides no universal duty-to-lever-position conversion, no connector pin and no direct voltage threshold. It therefore supports checking the specified channel's valid signal range without deciding that a particular mechanical range is selected. Further isolation of the sensor, connection and receiving circuit must follow the matching selector-system diagnostic information.
