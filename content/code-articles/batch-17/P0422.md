# P0422: This catalyst efficiency check depends on an eligible regeneration event

P0422 indicates catalyst efficiency below its required level. The cited GM application evaluates a second, underfloor diesel oxidation catalyst during particulate-filter regeneration. It uses heat released across that catalyst relative to exhaust-injected fuel, so a generic gasoline oxygen-sensor explanation would describe the wrong monitor.

## The aging index needs trustworthy inputs

The strategy forms an aging index from the oxidation response during a defined portion of regeneration. The primary failure threshold shown is below 0.63. The document separately identifies a repass boundary of 0.69 when its specified filtering and prior-failure conditions apply; a pass decision should not be assumed to use the failure boundary unchanged.

The check requires functioning exhaust fuel injection and valid temperature and exhaust-flow information. Faulted inputs, disabled regeneration, unsuitable ambient conditions or an already completed successful monitor can prevent another valid assessment in the same drive.

## Verification requires monitor completion, not just ordinary running

A later period of normal driving without an eligible regeneration does not demonstrate recovered catalyst efficiency. Preserve the monitor state, input validity, aging index and filtering configuration. The document supports different reporting behavior depending on whether its exponentially weighted filtering is enabled and which mode is active.

These distinctions explain why an apparent low result must be interpreted with its measurement conditions. The summary is not a vehicle-specific replacement instruction, and it does not justify inducing regeneration or disturbing hot exhaust hardware without the applicable professional procedure.
