# P2077: The tuning position low-range test uses a bounded raw-voltage window

P2077 identifies a low intake manifold tuning-valve position signal on bank 1. The GM row assesses the raw position-sensor voltage, rather than declaring that the valve is physically stuck at its closed stop. Electrical range and actual valve motion remain separate questions.

## The documented low region is not every zero-like reading

The failure condition is raw voltage below 0.7 V while still greater than zero. The source therefore describes a bounded low region; this article does not extend it to every missing, zero or invalid signal state without further documentation.

Battery voltage must be above 9 V and below 17.5 V, and the listed evaluation is 0.05 second with two-trip reporting. The enable block also references ADC, PWM and SENT feedback configurations. The applicable architecture must be established rather than assuming that a voltage test transfers unchanged to every possible interface.

## Keep the raw signal distinct from decoded position

Preserve the configured feedback type, raw measurement and monitor status. A displayed valve angle may already be scaled or substituted by the controller, so it cannot automatically stand in for the raw voltage used in this row.

The source supplies neither a connector pinout nor a definitive physical-stop mapping for the low region. It cannot identify a failed position sensor, wiring path or actuator from P2077 alone. The correct tuning-valve service information is needed before selecting a circuit test or mechanical repair.
