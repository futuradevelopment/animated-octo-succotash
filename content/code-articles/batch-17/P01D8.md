# P01D8: Advanced cylinder 7 injection needs quantity and timing evidence

P01D8 is the cylinder 7 over-advanced injection-timing performance entry. The cited GM strategy describes excessive negative drift identified through small-quantity injection learning. Its narrative is useful, but one numerical part of the row cannot be applied without further confirmation.

## Pressure drop and pressure timing answer different questions

The controller performs known small injections during eligible diesel fuel-cut operation. The magnitude of the rail-pressure drop helps estimate an energizing-time correction, while the falling-edge position provides injection-start timing after conversion using crankshaft speed. A quantity correction alone is therefore not direct proof of an equally sized angular timing error.

The strategy repeats trial injections and compares their timing with the selected start. It also requires the relevant learning conditions and quantity or timing diagnostic to be enabled. A normal drive with that learning inactive does not constitute a completed pass.

## Do not infer the missing threshold direction

The text describes negative drift, yet the displayed threshold column uses positive greater-than values also seen in the opposite-direction entry. This article does not reverse signs or publish a guessed numerical failure limit. Confirm the calibration's intended signed quantities before interpreting a result close to a boundary.

Useful evidence includes which correction failed, whether learning completed and the operating state during the trials. The monitor does not identify a physical injector defect by itself, and high-pressure fuel-system investigation requires the proper service procedure.
