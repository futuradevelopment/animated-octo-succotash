# P03F4: Exhaust profile-C performance depends on whether movement was requested

P03F4 is listed generically as exhaust profile-actuator C performance or stuck off on bank 1. The GM row for exhaust profile actuator 3 focuses on a pin that fires without a command. That narrower strategy cannot be reconstructed from the generic label alone.

## The abnormal relationship is movement without a request

The controller recognizes extending, extended and retracted feedback regions. For this monitor, entering the extension region in the absence of a command supplies the failure evidence. Movement during an intended profile change is not the same event, even if the final position is identical.

The source requires a running engine, supply above 11 V and diagnostic enablement. It counts four incorrect positions within five cylinder events and lists one-trip reporting. A static reading after the event cannot reveal the missing-command relationship or the number of eligible observations.

## Keep feedback interpretation within the source limits

The numerical region boundaries have no clearly identified physical units in the table. They are not presented here as pin displacement, signal voltage or duty cycle. The generic stuck-off wording also remains distinct from the cited uncommanded-motion strategy.

A useful capture aligns the request and feedback sequence for actuator 3 specifically. Further service checks must distinguish real unwanted movement from incorrect feedback. The monitor summary does not identify a replacement part, prove a mechanical jam or justify manually forcing the exhaust profile mechanism.
