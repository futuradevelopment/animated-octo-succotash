# P248A: Reductant heater A supply low in the GM tank-heater monitor

P248A is described here as DEF tank-heater supply undervoltage monitoring. Although a generic code label may call it heater A sense circuit low, this document connects the monitored quantity to the tank heater's supply. It is not a direct measurement of DEF temperature or heating effectiveness.

## Compare two voltages under a valid request

The failure calculation subtracts tank-heater supply voltage from system battery voltage. It fails when that difference exceeds the printed 3.00 threshold. The table omits a unit beside that number; the voltage comparison is clear, but confirm the detailed service limit before applying it as a measurement specification.

The heater must be requested on by the heating strategy. Calibration enable, valid powertrain-relay supply, active run/crank and exclusion of cranking are also required. Lost communication with the reductant control module, identified as U010E, must be absent. A heater that is intentionally off does not recreate the documented test state.

## Data that separates the likely paths

Capture battery voltage, reported tank-heater supply, heater request and reductant-module communication together. The important evidence is their difference during the same operating condition, rather than a heater-voltage reading from one moment compared with battery voltage from another.

The monitor requires 16 failures among 20 samples with a 500 ms time basis and lists Type A, one-trip reporting. Use that qualification when distinguishing sustained loss from a brief transition. Check the supply path using the vehicle's wiring and test procedure; this row alone cannot determine whether a connection, module or heater is responsible, and it does not establish that the tank assembly needs replacement.
