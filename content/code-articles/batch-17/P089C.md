# P089C: The second X-position signal needs its own low-duty evidence

P089C identifies low electrical range on gear-lever X position sensor 2. The cited GM monitor reads that sensor's X duty cycle and applies a lower signal boundary. The second-sensor identity is significant even when another selector input appears to report a plausible position.

## A duty-cycle finding, not a physical location

The failure criterion is below five percent. The controller must have been awake for at least 0.05 second, after which ten failed samples within twelve are counted at a 25-millisecond task rate. Two-trip reporting is listed separately.

Those details do not provide a direct lever-position or gear-range interpretation. The document supplies no map between duty percentage and selector travel, and an average voltage is not automatically an equivalent representation of this signal.

## Compare the correct source of information

Preserve the raw X-channel sequence from sensor 2, along with wakeup timing and fault status. Data from sensor 1 may provide context in a wider investigation, but it cannot demonstrate that sensor 2's own signal passed this monitor.

An early initialization transient differs from repeated low-duty observations after enablement. A normal decoded gear display also does not erase the recorded raw-channel finding. The table does not locate the failure inside the sensor, wiring or controller, or authorize a selector replacement. Use the applicable circuit procedure to resolve the signal path before drawing mechanical transmission conclusions.
