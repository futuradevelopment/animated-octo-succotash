# P08A1: The second Y-position channel is judged on raw low-duty evidence

P08A1 identifies the low-circuit result for gear-lever Y position sensor 2. In this GM implementation, the controller assesses the raw duty cycle on that particular input. It does not establish a physical lever position from the word low.

## Separate the signal boundary from the enable delay

The failure threshold is duty cycle below five percent. Monitoring requires the controller to have been awake for at least 0.05 second. The strategy then counts ten failing samples within twelve on a 25-millisecond loop, with two-trip reporting.

A signal transition during initialization and repeated low duty after enablement are therefore different observations. Preserve time since wakeup alongside the sample sequence rather than interpreting every momentary low percentage as the recorded fault.

## A different selector channel cannot verify this one

Sensor 2's Y signal must remain identifiable in the data. A healthy X signal or an acceptable first-sensor reading does not demonstrate that this channel passed. A plausible decoded gear display also cannot replace the underlying electrical-range evidence.

The source gives neither a duty-to-position map nor a conversion to an external connector voltage. It supports investigating the specified low-duty signal without asserting that a gear has disengaged or a selector mechanism is jammed. The applicable circuit procedure is needed to distinguish the sensor, connection and receiving-controller possibilities before choosing a repair.
