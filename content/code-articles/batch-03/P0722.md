# P0722: output speed remains low despite qualifying torque conditions

P0722 is the output-speed no-activity monitor in the cited TCM. The controller sees raw transmission output speed below 60 rpm while the applicable gear and torque conditions imply that usable activity should be present.

## A stationary zero is not the full criterion

The source rationalizes the speed signal against attained gear, accelerator position and engine torque, with separate range-related logic. Merely seeing zero output speed in Park does not establish the failure defined by this monitor.

The documented persistence requirement differs by gear group: more than five seconds for the high-gear branch and more than 3.5 seconds for the low-gear branch. Applying one universal timeout would ignore that distinction. The source uses rapid internal updates, but the decision still depends on the qualified duration.

## Sustained loss and intermittency can compete

The monitor description includes open-circuit, sensor-internal and controller-internal possibilities. It does not rank them or prove a specific failed component. After a sudden signal drop, P0722 may compete with the intermittent-output-speed diagnostic depending on whether the signal stays low or recovers.

Record input/output speed history, attained gear, torque context and companion codes. Verification should establish that output activity remains plausible through the relevant operating conditions and that the diagnostic passes. A single later nonzero reading does not exclude the earlier sustained loss. The table offers no basis for transmission teardown or sensor replacement without the appropriate circuit and signal diagnosis.
