# P0962: solenoid A control voltage indicates a ground-short signature

P0962 is the low control-circuit diagnostic for pressure-control solenoid A. The cited table uses a controller-specific voltage decision designed to detect less than 0.5 ohm between the signal and controller ground.

## Circuit low is separate from pressure low

The monitored quantity is electrical. This row does not establish low hydraulic pressure or prove that a clutch is slipping. The source maps solenoid A to different clutch circuits in geared transmissions and to secondary-pulley control in its CVT example, so the hardware assignment must be confirmed before linking symptoms.

The diagnostic counts more than 0.3 second of failure within a sample period exceeding 0.5 second. A momentary low observation is not the entire sample decision.

## The supply path must be active

Battery voltage must be between 9 and 32 volts for more than one second, and the appropriate mapped high-side driver must be on. The page also permits specified run/crank or accessory-power conditions. Thus, engine running is not the only possible test context, but an inactive solenoid supply is not a valid equivalent.

Record the controller's solenoid mapping, supply-driver state and circuit result together. Verification should establish a passing low-circuit monitor under those conditions before interpreting hydraulic performance. The impedance relationship describes detection capability, not an instruction to use an ohmmeter on a powered circuit. No particular solenoid, harness or module replacement follows from the code alone.
