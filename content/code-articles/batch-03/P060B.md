# P060B: internal analog-conversion checks are not external sensor thresholds

P060B is internal control-module analog-to-digital processing performance. The cited ECM tables label the implementation an ANDR ADC fault and evaluate a resistance-deviation percentage. The failure boundary shown is greater than six percent.

## Keep the measurement inside its context

The six-percent value belongs to an internal diagnostic comparison. The table does not identify an external resistor that should be measured or a sensor signal that should be adjusted to that tolerance. Treating this number as a general resistance specification for the vehicle would exceed the evidence.

The monitor requires run/crank voltage above 7 volts. Its decision is listed as two of fourteen counts or 1.75 seconds continuously, with 250 milliseconds per main-processor count. Supply conditions and completion of the internal decision are therefore relevant to whether a later observation actually tests the same failure.

## What to record and verify

Record the storing module, voltage context and any available internal fault detail. Multiple similar rows on the source page represent repeated internal checks; they do not establish that several separate external sensors failed simultaneously.

The two supplied ECM examples agree on the displayed threshold and decision wording. They do not provide pinouts, a controller-opening procedure, or a mandatory replacement instruction. Apply the exact module's diagnostic guidance, then verify the enabled analog-processing monitor passes. A plausible collection of live sensor values alone is insufficient evidence because the diagnostic concerns the reliability of internal conversion checking.
