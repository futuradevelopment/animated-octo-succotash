# U063E: The exhaust heat-exchanger bypass actuator stops replying on LIN

U063E identifies lost communication with exhaust heat exchanger bypass valve actuator A. The 2018 GM HPC2/BSM table monitors this actuator over LIN, so diagnosis should not automatically be redirected to a generic high-speed CAN bus.

## The missing-response counter

The failure criterion is at least 80 responses missed, with a 40-millisecond message interval and a listed 3.2-second evaluation. The controller must be on, Run/Crank true and LIN communication enabled for at least two seconds. GM lists Type B, two trips.

Supply context matters. The row includes battery voltage at least 10.2 V, plus a recovery condition associated with a transition from low voltage to at least 11 V maintained for five seconds. A silence during startup or supply recovery must therefore be interpreted with the complete enable logic.

## A communication result is not valve-position proof

The timeout does not establish that the valve is mechanically stuck or that exhaust heat flow is incorrect. It establishes absence of the expected electronic response. Retain actuator response status, LIN enable time and supply history before clearing the fault.

Use the vehicle-specific LIN and actuator procedure to distinguish power, communication-path and actuator issues. After repair, verify repeated responses under eligible conditions, not merely a warning that stays off momentarily. The table gives no connector pins or permission to command hot exhaust components by improvised wiring; inspection must follow the applicable service precautions.

## Source and applicability

This explanation follows the documented GM monitor, not a verified application to every vehicle with this code. Confirm the vehicle, reporting module and calibration in current service information.
