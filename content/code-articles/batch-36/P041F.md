# P041F: AIR valve circuit low during an off command

P041F identifies the secondary air injection switching valve A circuit as low. The 2015 GM ECM table describes a low-side driver feedback check: voltage remains low while the solenoid driver is commanded **off**. That command state is essential to understanding the fault.

## What the electrical test establishes

The table associates this response with a short to ground. It requires powertrain relay voltage of at least 11 V and evaluates 25 samples, taken 250 milliseconds apart, with 20 failing samples required. Its listed reporting classification is Type B, two trips.

This is an electrical circuit monitor. It does not establish how much secondary air reached the exhaust or prove that the valve's mechanical passage is blocked. A low voltage observed during a different command state is not equivalent evidence.

## Interpreting companion codes

GM notes that some controllers can also set P0412, the solenoid open-circuit code. Consequently, two stored circuit descriptions need not mean two separate component failures. Compare their operating context and the correct driver circuit before deciding which evidence is independent.

A useful verification record includes commanded driver state, feedback status and relay voltage when the monitor executes. The 11 V enable condition also means a period of inadequate supply voltage cannot serve as a clean passing test. Follow the vehicle-specific circuit procedure; this summary does not establish connector pins or authorize replacing the valve from the code alone.

## Source and applicability

This explanation follows the documented GM monitor, not a verified application to every vehicle with this code. Confirm the vehicle, reporting module and calibration in current service information.
