# C0575: The left-rear brake pressure signal is at its low electrical limit

C0575 identifies left-rear brake pressure sensor circuit low in the 2018 GM BSC1 table. The controller evaluates the signal line against its expected separation from ground and describes the failure as a short-to-ground response.

## Electrical low and hydraulic low are different findings

The documented signal threshold is below 0.3 V with ignition on, evaluated over 0.1 seconds. GM lists Type A, one-trip reporting. The monitor is assessing circuit voltage; it does not directly establish that the rear brake circuit lacks hydraulic pressure.

A pressure reading derived from a signal at its electrical endpoint may be unreliable. Treating that displayed value as a confirmed hydraulic measurement would skip the circuit fault that the DTC reports. The code itself also does not identify which wire, connection, sensor or controller is defective.

## What to preserve for diagnosis

Record the current circuit status and related brake-pressure faults. Compare them using the actual vehicle's sensor mapping and electrical procedure before assigning a hydraulic cause. A reading that returns temporarily to normal after an intermittent event does not demonstrate that the path is consistently sound.

Qualified brake service should confirm the signal's electrical integrity and the required brake-system function after repair. The source includes a stationary ignition-on pass observation, but that alone is not a complete brake safety assessment. This article provides no connector-probing or hydraulic repair instructions and no safe-driving conclusion from the code.

## Source and applicability

This explanation follows the documented GM monitor, not a verified application to every vehicle with this code. Confirm the vehicle, reporting module and calibration in current service information.
