# C1042: A cut-valve fault still requires ABS supply and connector checks

## The named valve is not the only diagnostic possibility

The Ariya C1042-04 entry reports a malfunction in cut valve 1, with power-on diagnosis listed as one second or less under the specified supply conditions. Current-fault possibilities include the ABS actuator and control unit, but the source also lists connectors, supply circuitry, fuses and the 12-volt battery.

Several brake and stability functions can be suspended, including ABS, EBD, regenerative cooperation and the electric parking brake. Those restrictions make a current warning a matter for professional assessment, not an invitation to road-test the vehicle casually until the code disappears.

## Connector condition and electrical support precede replacement

Nissan first checks whether the ABS connector is loose or disconnected. After any correction, it repeats self-diagnosis. It then checks the ABS unit's supply and ground before reaching the actuator-and-control-unit replacement branch.

The factory note explicitly distinguishes current from past status and does not support replacing the unit for a past record alone. Preserve that status and other supply-related codes before clearing memory so the workshop can evaluate the actual event.

The confirmation uses power-on without READY after the defined sleep interval, keeping the observation separate from an energized propulsion state. This article explains the diagnostic sequence rather than providing a valve-actuation or hydraulic-repair procedure. Brake-system work and any required post-repair verification must follow the complete vehicle-specific factory instructions.
