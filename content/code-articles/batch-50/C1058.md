# C1058: An internal ABS fault has a steering-angle setup check first

## Current and past findings have different implications

The Ariya C1058-01 monitor reports an internal malfunction in the ABS actuator and control unit, with diagnosis listed as two seconds or less under the stated power-on conditions. Yet Nissan's current-fault cause list also includes incomplete steering-angle adjustment and electrical supply problems.

The historical-event list contains additional contextual possibilities, and the manual explicitly warns against replacing the unit solely for a past DTC. Those distinctions prevent the phrase internal malfunction from being treated as an unconditional parts verdict.

## Finish the adjustment and supporting checks

The diagnostic procedure begins with steering-angle neutral-position adjustment, followed by a complete code reading. Any resulting codes receive attention before the flow proceeds to ABS supply and ground checks. The unit-replacement branch follows a repeated C1058 result after that sequence.

Several stability and brake functions can be suspended, including ABS and regenerative cooperation. Any unusual braking behavior therefore needs professional assessment before a driving evaluation. A warning disappearing after reset is not a replacement for the specified adjustment and confirmation.

Keep the original scan, current-versus-past status and any recent alignment, steering or brake-controller work for the technician. Those records help distinguish an unfinished setup operation from a recurring internal finding. Use the complete vehicle-matched procedure for calibration and post-repair verification; this article does not provide a shortcut around those safety-critical steps.
