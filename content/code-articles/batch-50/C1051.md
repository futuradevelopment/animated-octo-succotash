# C1051: Pump-motor diagnosis includes a required post-repair operating event

## The monitor covers the motor or its relay

The Ariya ABS C1051-04 entry describes a malfunction in the pump motor or motor relay. Its power-on diagnosis is listed as nineteen seconds or less under the stated supply conditions. The possible-cause list also includes wiring, connectors, power supply, fuses and the 12-volt battery.

Nissan distinguishes current and past status and warns against replacing the ABS actuator solely for a past DTC. A pump-related label therefore does not eliminate the need to establish whether the fault is active and whether the electrical support is sound.

## Post-repair history clearing is not just a stationary reset

The flow checks connector installation, supply and ground, then terminal condition before its final unit branch. It also specifies a driving event after relevant repair or replacement to clear previous fault information. That requirement explains why a historical result may remain after a correction that has not yet completed the prescribed verification.

The driving requirement is a workshop procedure, subject to the technician first establishing that the brakes and vehicle are safe. It should not be used to encourage driving a car with uncertain braking performance merely to erase a warning.

Save the original status, other brake codes and the work already performed. The distinction between repair, the required operating event and a returning current fault helps prevent unnecessary repetition of an ABS-unit replacement.
