# B248C: A refrigerant-leak label still requires pressure-sensor diagnosis

## The reported condition can depend on its sensing path

The Ariya B248C-09 entry describes refrigerant leaking or being absent, but the possible-cause list also includes the pressure sensor, VCM and their circuit. The compressor is stopped as the protective response. The table does not publish a detection timer or numerical pressure boundary.

A leak label therefore should not be treated as a direct measurement of refrigerant quantity. An incorrect pressure signal can affect the system's conclusion, which is why the source includes the electrical path as well as an actual refrigerant-loss condition.

## Validate pressure information before searching for leakage

Nissan's diagnostic flow checks the refrigerant-pressure-sensor system first. Only after that result is normal does it proceed to the leak test. This order distinguishes an unreliable indication from physical loss in the refrigerant circuit.

Confirmation operates air conditioning in READY at a full-cold setting and then reads HVAC results. That operating demand is different from assuming that a code cleared at rest proves the system has recovered. Retain any pressure-sensor or EV/HEV companion faults for the technician.

Do not add refrigerant solely because B248C appears. A qualified air-conditioning technician needs the correct refrigerant specification, service equipment and verified diagnosis. Recording recent refrigerant work and whether cooling deteriorated gradually or stopped suddenly can support the investigation without being mistaken for proof of a particular leak location.
