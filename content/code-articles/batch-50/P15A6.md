# P15A6: The charging connection signal disagrees with an active charge

## A connected cable must remain electrically recognized

The Ariya VCM's P15A6-13 monitor runs during normal charging. It detects the cable as not engaged through the EVSE connecting signal for more than three seconds. Nissan lists the signal circuit and normal charge port among possible causes and prohibits both normal and quick charging as a protective response.

The monitor is about recognition of the connection, not a published measurement of charging current. A physically inserted connector and an electrically valid connection signal are related observations, but one does not automatically establish the other.

## Separate the connection signal from the port circuit

The diagnostic sequence checks the EVSE connecting-signal circuit first and then the charge-port circuit. That order provides a way to investigate the specific feedback path rather than assuming that a charging interruption proves a failed traction battery.

Confirmation involves an actual normal-charging event. Retain whether the interruption occurred when the cable moved, immediately after connection or later in the session, without deliberately disturbing an energized connector to provoke a fault. Damaged contacts, heat or a loose connection require professional inspection. The article explains the circuit relationship; testing or repairing charging hardware must follow the applicable isolation and electrical precautions.

The listed confirmation observes normal charging for at least ten seconds before the scan. This is a workshop observation period; it should not replace the separate three-second detection criterion or be described as a cable-release timing specification.
