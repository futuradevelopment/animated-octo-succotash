# P12A8: Cruise braking information is missing or abnormal

## Cruise operation supplies the relevant context

The Ariya VCM sets P12A8-08 when brake information received from the chassis control module is abnormal or unavailable during cruise control. Despite the short scan label referring to a brake switch, the monitored evidence is communicated information. The listed possibilities span the network, intelligent brake unit, ABS control unit and VCM.

The protective response prohibits cruise operation, including the applicable ProPILOT function. Losing that assistance is different from identifying a particular failed brake component; the code alone cannot establish the condition of the hydraulic braking system.

## The factory route begins with communication diagnosis

Nissan directs this entry to its CAN communication troubleshooting flow. A complete controller scan is therefore more informative than ordering a switch from the abbreviated label. Retaining other brake and communication codes helps the technician locate the source of the bad or missing information.

The confirmation procedure uses cruise operation and brake input during a driving evaluation. That is a professional test requiring a safe vehicle and appropriate conditions, not an instruction to reproduce a braking concern in traffic. If braking performance is uncertain, arrange assessment before driving. Preserve whether the fault appeared during cruise engagement, cancellation or another brake event.

The table supplies no detection-delay value. A workshop should therefore use the specified cruise-and-brake confirmation rather than deciding that a communication path is healthy merely because a short stationary scan is clear.
