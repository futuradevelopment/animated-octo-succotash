# P163F: Charging-station compatibility can be the diagnostic endpoint

## This entry points to the charging environment

P163F-94 in the Ariya VCM is monitored during quick charging and concerns compatibility with the charging station through the PLC communication signal. The source names the charging device or power-supply environment as the possible cause. It does not provide a numerical timeout or identify a particular communication-protocol revision.

Both normal and quick charging are listed as prohibited responses. Those restrictions describe the controller's reaction, not proof of a permanently damaged onboard charger or traction battery.

## Reconstruct the station-specific event

Nissan's diagnosis asks which charging equipment produced the complaint and checks whether the event repeats there. If it does, the flow explains that the equipment may not be usable for charging that vehicle. Unlike many neighboring entries, this procedure does not end with an automatic onboard-component replacement instruction.

Retain the station identity, the point at which the session failed and any station message. Those facts make a compatibility investigation more useful than a generic statement that the car will not charge. The conclusion remains limited to the documented vehicle-and-device interaction; it does not establish that every unit from the same network is unsuitable. Repeated attempts are inappropriate if there is connector damage or another electrical safety concern.

The confirmation observes quick charging for at least 120 seconds. The procedure gives that observation time without supplying a separate numerical detection delay, and the two concepts should not be treated as interchangeable.
