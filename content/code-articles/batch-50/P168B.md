# P168B: Voltage remains high after main relay 1 is commanded off

## The failure is observed during shutdown

P168B-73 in the Ariya VCM concerns the transition from READY to off. After system main relay 1 is commanded off, the front inverter voltage remains above a specified value. The table does not state that voltage or a delay. The resulting restrictions prohibit restart and normally stop the high-voltage system.

The source identifies the relay-1 and precharge paths as possibilities. It does not prove that relay contacts have welded merely because voltage did not fall as expected. The command, its wiring and the monitored response remain separate diagnostic elements.

## Specific electrical faults come first

Nissan prioritizes the listed P168A, P168D and P168E electrical variants when they accompany this code. Its flow then examines relay-drive supply and, when required, the battery controller's supply and internal battery circuitry using battery-specific references.

Save the full group of codes and whether the complaint appeared while shutting down or attempting a subsequent start. Those details match this monitor more closely than a general no-start description. High-voltage relay and battery investigations require trained EV technicians; repeated restart attempts or improvised relay bypasses cannot establish safe isolation or resolve the shutdown condition.

The startup and shutdown directions are not interchangeable: this entry evaluates voltage remaining after an off command. A voltage reading that is appropriate in READY can be relevant precisely because it persists when shutdown was requested.
