# P1B18: The first battery interlock path includes the service plug and front harness

## Two connection areas belong to this path

P1B18-11 in the Ariya 91 kWh battery procedure concerns malfunction of interlock-detection circuit 1. The possible causes specifically include the service-plug interlock and the front high-voltage harness interlock, along with improper connection at either area. The power-on diagnosis is listed as two seconds or less.

The code is therefore broader than an assertion that the service plug is missing. A problem in the front-harness detection path can also be relevant, and a stored electrical fault does not by itself prove the state of either high-voltage connection.

## Follow the two checks in sequence

The factory flow checks the service-plug interlock circuit first, then the front-harness interlock circuit. If both inspections are normal, it directs investigation of an intermittent condition. The distinction preserves two separate places where connector engagement and the detection circuit must agree.

Inform the workshop about any recent battery or front-drive-unit work and retain the complete scan before clearing memory. Do not reseat a service plug or bridge an interlock as a trial repair. These devices are part of a hazardous high-voltage system, and trained EV personnel must use the full isolation and verification procedure before handling connections or testing their circuits.

A normal result at the service-plug circuit is not the end of the published investigation. The front-harness circuit remains a separate required branch, which is why checking only the most accessible connection can miss relevant evidence.
