# C10B5: Nissan Ariya parking-brake assembly check

## Why maintenance mode matters

The cited Nissan Ariya procedure labels C10B5-76 as a parking-brake assembly check. Its detection description mentions an internal ABS-controller malfunction in maintenance mode, with the power switch on and normal supply voltage. The listed delay is thirty seconds or more. That maintenance-mode context is essential: the page's first diagnostic action is an initial-position adjustment, not immediate replacement of the controller named in the description.

The possible-cause table includes the parking-brake actuator, harness connections, ABS power supply, fuses, fusible links, and the 12-volt battery. A current code can additionally implicate the ABS actuator and electronic controller. The manual explicitly separates a historical PAST record from a current CRNT record and says not to replace the ABS assembly for PAST memory alone.

## Restore the specified initial position first

The repair sequence begins with the factory brake-caliper initial-position adjustment. This is a referenced workshop operation; this DTC entry does not supply a substitute mechanical shortcut or a generic sequence for every electronic parking brake. The technician then completes the prescribed power-off rest, switches power on without entering READY, and repeats ABS self-diagnosis.

A current C10B5-76 that remains after that sequence leads to the ABS assembly replacement branch. A historical result instead leads to clearing the stored self-diagnostic record. Recording the result before and after adjustment helps distinguish an unfinished service state from the fault that actually survives it.

Electric parking-brake operation is listed as suspended in fail-safe. A qualified brake technician should establish that the caliper adjustment and final functional checks are complete before relying on the parking brake again.
