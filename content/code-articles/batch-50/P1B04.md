# P1B04: A subset of temperature-sensor circuits is reported shorted

## The number of affected circuits is part of the condition

The Ariya 91 kWh battery's P1B04-11 entry describes a short detected in fewer than half of its module-temperature-sensor circuits. This power-on diagnosis uses sensor voltage and is listed as two seconds or less. It should not be restated as a measured battery overtemperature; the evidence is a circuit condition.

The possible causes include the temperature sensors, their circuits and the cell controller. Nissan's protective pattern limits driving output, stops charging and illuminates the EV-system warning.

## Read status information before isolating a component

The procedure records the battery-pack temperature status and uses displayed open/short information to decide whether circuit inspection is needed. It then separates sensor inspection from the cell-controller branch. This makes a collection of abnormal readings diagnostically different from one proven defective temperature sensor.

Preserve the original status and any sensor-specific codes before clearing memory. Their distribution can help the technician follow the relevant harness and controller mapping. Access inside the pack is restricted to qualified EV service work with the correct isolation and handling instructions. Replacing modules based solely on a broad short-circuit report would skip the source's distinction between the sensing path and the measured component.

The status check explicitly distinguishes open or wire-short information from a short-to-ground record. Those displayed descriptions should be copied accurately into the service record instead of being reduced to a generic temperature-sensor failure.
