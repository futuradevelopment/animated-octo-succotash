# P1B01: Cell-circuit faults require the exact suffix and cell identity

## Three findings share the same base code

For Ariya's 91 kWh battery, P1B01-12 concerns a short in the cell-voltage measuring circuit. P1B01-62 compares maximum and minimum cell voltage under a no-load condition. P1B01-F1 concerns voltage outside the available range. These are different findings; a five-character scan alone cannot select the correct diagnostic branch.

The -12 procedure compares 96 monitored cell values and then separates the sensing circuit, module voltage and cell controller. A faulty measured value therefore does not establish a defective cell until the detection path has been assessed.

## Preserve freeze-frame cell numbers and the source limitation

The -62 flow records maximum and minimum cell identities and total battery voltage before evaluating deviation from the average. That information must be captured before erasure. Its extracted comparison branches do not retain explicit inequality labels, so this article remains marked for source review and does not reproduce a module-selection formula as a repair instruction.

The -F1 path likewise uses the recorded maximum-voltage cell identity and checks its detection circuit. All battery opening, module measurement and replacement decisions require the complete, visually verified factory procedure and qualified EV personnel. The useful first step is preserving precise diagnostic evidence, not selecting a replacement pack from the base code.

The -F1 protective pattern stops driving and charging, while the -62 pattern lists an output limit. Their different consequences reinforce the need to retain the suffix rather than treating all cell-voltage entries as equivalent.
