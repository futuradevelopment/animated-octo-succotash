# P1B12: Cell-controller status narrows an ASIC measuring-circuit fault

## The measuring circuit is the reported problem

P1B12-04 in the Ariya 91 kWh battery procedure concerns a malfunction of the cell-voltage measuring circuit, involving the cell-controller ASIC or detection wiring. Its power-on diagnosis is listed as two seconds or less. The protective pattern limits drive output, stops charging and illuminates the EV-system warning.

The term ASIC identifies part of the sensing electronics. It does not mean that every abnormal cell-voltage reading proves the physical cell is defective, nor does it provide a board-level repair specification.

## Confirm recurrence and retain the controller map

Nissan repeats confirmation after the prescribed memory-clearing sequence and a power-off wait. If the code returns, the flow checks eight cell-controller status entries. A controller reporting abnormal status leads to its own branch; a different result directs attention to the LBC.

Further replacement combinations appear only after assembly and another failed confirmation. That sequence should not be collapsed into an immediate recommendation to replace every controller. Preserve the original status records and which components have already been checked or replaced. Battery opening and controller work require qualified EV personnel with the complete 91 kWh procedure, correct isolation and configuration instructions; this code alone does not identify an interchangeable replacement part.

The repeated check includes a power-off wait of at least five minutes. That reset interval is part of the published sequence, not an invitation to interpret an immediate rescan as an equivalent restart of the monitor.
