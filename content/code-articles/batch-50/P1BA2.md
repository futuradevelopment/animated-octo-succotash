# P1BA2: Insulation diagnosis extends beyond the battery enclosure

## Activation and shutdown are relevant monitor events

P1BA2-49 in the Ariya 91 kWh battery procedure concerns a malfunction in insulation-resistance detection, checked when the LBC activates or shuts down. The entry lists a diagnosis time of twenty seconds or less and identifies high-voltage insulation loss as the possible cause. It does not isolate the battery cells as the source.

The flow begins with visual condition and maintenance history. It specifically asks whether incorrect material may have entered the A/C refrigerant system, because that can affect insulation. This makes recent climate-system service relevant to an electrical-isolation complaint.

## Isolate the affected high-voltage branch professionally

Nissan's procedure evaluates charging and drive harnesses, compressor, heater, junction box, converter, charger, inverter, motor and battery through their respective inspection references. The breadth of that sequence explains why replacing the pack from the code title alone can miss an external component.

The underlying tests use specialized insulation equipment with strict application limits and verified high-voltage isolation. Incorrect use can injure personnel or damage low-voltage electronics. This work belongs with trained EV technicians; a household continuity check is not an equivalent test. Preserve service history and the complete scan so the workshop can choose the correct branch and equipment-specific procedure.

The insulation flow also distinguishes charge-port and front-inverter harness paths before the component inspections. A charging-related history and a drive-related history may therefore lead the technician to different connections within the same overall diagnostic sequence.
