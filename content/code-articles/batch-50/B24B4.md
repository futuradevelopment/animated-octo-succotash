# B24B4: The climate control panel reports its own internal error

## A received error is different from silence on the network

The Ariya B24B4-02 entry describes an internal circuit error message received from the A/C control through LIN communication. The power-on condition must last at least thirty seconds. This is a reported internal problem, not simply the absence of a LIN message.

The source names the A/C control as the possible cause. That component identity should not be confused with the A/C auto amplifier, electric compressor or another controller merely because they all participate in climate operation. Correct naming matters before a replacement is selected.

## Reconfirm after the defined power transition

Nissan erases the HVAC result, turns power off and back on, then performs confirmation again. The A/C control replacement branch follows if the code returns. An immediately clear result after erasure is therefore not the complete observation described by the flow.

The thirty-second detection condition also means that an overly quick startup check can be inconclusive. A workshop should preserve the original result and observe the system long enough under the applicable conditions rather than interpreting a brief scan as proof of repair.

Record any control-panel behavior, such as whether commands were accepted, together with the exact suffix. The source provides no board-level repair specification or blanket instruction to replace the whole HVAC system. The component-specific service procedure is needed to complete a confirmed replacement and verify normal operation.
