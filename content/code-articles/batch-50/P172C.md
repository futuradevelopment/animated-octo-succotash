# P172C: An open pressure-sensor circuit has a code-priority rule

## The monitor concerns sensor 1's circuit

For the Ariya VCM, P172C-00 describes detection of an opening in battery-pack pressure sensor 1's circuit lasting more than one second. The possible-cause list also includes a short to ground and the sensor itself. This is electrical-path evidence; it does not by itself establish the physical pressure inside the battery.

Nissan explicitly prioritizes P0A7E-F1 when that code accompanies P172C-00. Retaining the complete scan is therefore necessary to follow the intended order. Clearing one code before recording the others can remove the reason for starting with a different procedure.

## Keep the diagnosis matched to the battery

The flow leads to the pressure-sensor inspection for the installed 66 kWh or 91 kWh battery. A technician must use that specific circuit information rather than assuming a connector map from another pack is interchangeable.

The useful distinction from a two-sensor correlation fault is that this entry focuses on the continuity-related condition of sensor 1's path. Report whether the fault followed battery service and preserve other battery warnings, without attempting to access the pack. Sensor and harness inspection inside a high-voltage battery requires qualified personnel and the complete isolation and handling instructions.

The possible-cause list includes an open circuit or short to ground. That broader list is why the detected opening should be treated as a diagnostic condition to investigate, rather than a guarantee that a wire is visibly broken.
