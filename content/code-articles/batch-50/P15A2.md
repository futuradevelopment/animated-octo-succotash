# P15A2: Front motor temperature needs an operating-event diagnosis

## The monitored assembly includes two possible origins

P15A2-98 in the Ariya VCM describes abnormal temperature in the front traction motor, front inverter or both. The entry does not provide a temperature limit or delay. Its broad wording therefore cannot distinguish a genuinely overheated assembly from the precise underlying cause without the associated controller information.

The factory flow first checks the front inverter for its own codes. A specific inverter fault receives its applicable diagnosis before the VCM confirmation is repeated. This avoids turning a system-level temperature report into an unsupported parts-replacement decision.

## Confirmation must represent the relevant load

Nissan's confirmation includes warming the motor through driving and a prescribed acceleration sequence. That indicates why a brief stationary restart may not reproduce the original event. The demanding sequence belongs in a controlled professional evaluation after the technician establishes that the vehicle can safely be tested.

Do not try to force an overheating warning to return on a public road. Instead, preserve the trip conditions, load and companion inverter codes for the workshop. The manual's later VCM replacement branch is conditional on its preceding diagnostic sequence and recurrence; it does not make the VCM the automatic cause of every front-motor temperature complaint.

The detection condition names the power-on state, whereas the confirmation deliberately adds motor warming and load. That difference explains why the entry can exist in memory even when the workshop's initial stationary inspection shows no temperature complaint.
