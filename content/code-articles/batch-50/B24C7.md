# B24C7: Heater-board overheating can originate in airflow control

## The temperature belongs to the heater circuit board

The Ariya B24C7-98 monitor detects a PTC heater circuit-board temperature of at least 115°C for two seconds or more while READY. Heater operation stops. The entry identifies the heater, blower system and air-mix door system as possible causes.

This is not a cabin-air temperature threshold. It is also not proof that the heater is the initial cause of the thermal problem. The inclusion of airflow and air-routing systems explains why a heater-temperature complaint needs more than an electrical scan of the heater alone.

## Verify airflow and door control in the published order

Nissan checks the blower motor system first, followed by the left and right air-mix door systems. The PTC heater branch comes after those checks are normal. A failed blower or a door-control problem therefore needs its own diagnosis before the heater assembly is condemned.

Confirmation operates HVAC in READY with a full-hot request, which is different from observing the system with no heating demand. That operating context should be preserved when the technician evaluates whether the condition can be reproduced safely.

Record whether airflow weakened, changed direction or stopped before heating was lost. Do not force repeated high-heat operation to provoke an overtemperature warning. High-voltage heater access and thermal-system repairs belong with qualified personnel using the complete factory procedure and correct isolation precautions.
