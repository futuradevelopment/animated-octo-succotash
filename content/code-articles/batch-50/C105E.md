# C105E: Pressure-calibration labels direct attention to the intelligent brake unit

## The source defines both suffixes through the brake-unit system

In the Ariya ABS procedure, C105E-92 and -93 share a pressure-sensor-not-calibrated label, while their detection rows describe a malfunction in the electrically driven intelligent brake unit system. Both use diagnosis listed as one second or less under the stated power-on conditions.

The source gives the same broad diagnostic route for these suffixes; it does not provide a numerical pressure offset or distinguish them with a unique calibration tolerance. Inventing such a difference from the suffix would go beyond the published evidence.

## Read BRAKE before condemning ABS

Nissan first performs self-diagnosis of the BRAKE system and follows any detected code. The procedure then checks ABS supply and ground before repeating ABS self-diagnosis. Its ABS-unit replacement branch is therefore conditional on the preceding checks and returning fault.

The listed suspended function is cooperative regenerative braking. That does not independently establish the condition of every braking function or certify that the vehicle is safe to drive with a warning. Actual pedal or stopping concerns require a technician's assessment.

Preserve both controllers' results and whether the code is current or past. The factory note rejects replacement for a past record alone. Any calibration or component work must use the verified vehicle-specific instructions; a pressure-sensor label by itself is not a complete adjustment procedure.
