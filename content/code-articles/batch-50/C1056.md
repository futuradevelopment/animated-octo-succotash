# C1056: Low switched supply must be separated from an internal ABS fault

## The measured supply is the power-on feed

In the Ariya C1056-13 entry, the power-switch-on supply is 10 V or less. Diagnosis is listed as one second or less. The possible causes include the 12-volt battery, charging system, supply circuitry, fuses, connectors and ABS unit; the code does not specifically identify a weak traction battery.

The source treats past status differently from a continuing current result. Even its past-fault branch calls for checking the 12-volt battery, so a historical supply event is not simply evidence to discard without considering the supporting electrical system.

## Follow the supply path before the module branch

Nissan checks the 12-volt battery first, then connector installation, ABS power and ground and the VCM's EV/HEV diagnostic result. Only after those stages and repeat confirmation does a current C1056 lead to the ABS-unit branch.

This sequence explains why a low-voltage code recorded under ABS can originate outside the brake controller. A repaired connection and a healthy battery are separate findings, and both may be needed to establish reliable supply.

Retain other low-voltage and charging-system codes before erasure. A workshop must use the correct battery and circuit testing procedures rather than replace the ABS unit from one low reading alone. The source's normal-control response does not substitute for assessing any real brake warning or abnormal pedal behavior.
