# P102C: Quick-charge relay wiring has priority over the VCM

## Two relay commands share this electrical monitor

For the Ariya procedure, P102C-01 concerns the drive circuits of quick-charge relays 1 and 2. The VCM monitors them during quick charging. An open circuit, short to ground or short to supply lasting more than four seconds meets the listed criterion. Quick charging is then prohibited; that protective action does not identify which wire or controller failed.

The complete suffix and companion codes matter. Nissan directs attention to P0D0A or P0D11 electrical variants first when they accompany P102C. Treating the broader relay complaint before those more specific contactor faults can obscure the diagnostic order.

## Establish circuit integrity before considering a controller

The procedure follows the wiring between the VCM and battery, including continuity and unwanted connections to ground or supply. It then refers to battery-specific relay checks before repeating confirmation. VCM replacement appears only after that sequence and recurrence, rather than as the first response to the code.

Confirmation includes a quick-charging event; an ordinary ignition cycle cannot reproduce every condition of this monitor. Because the investigation reaches battery and charging circuits, a qualified EV technician must use the correct isolation precautions and equipment-specific service information.

The documented confirmation observes quick charging for at least thirty seconds before the subsequent scan. That is longer than the fault's four-second detection criterion, separating the monitor threshold from the workshop observation period.
