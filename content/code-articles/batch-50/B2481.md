# B2481: The cabin PTC heater requires both LIN communication and a valid supply

## The received heater signal is abnormal

The Ariya B2481-02 entry concerns abnormalities in the PTC heater's LIN information while the vehicle is READY. The condition lasts at least two seconds and heater operation is stopped. The source lists the heater, A/C auto amplifier and open or shorted communication wiring as possible causes.

This communication finding should not be treated as proof that the heating element itself is open. The controller can lose valid heater information because the communication path or low-voltage supply is compromised, even though the complaint is noticed as a lack of cabin heat.

## Follow the complete low-voltage path first

Nissan checks the LIN line for continuity and an unwanted ground connection, then checks heater supply and ground. Only after reconnecting the relevant components does the flow perform an HVAC active test and assess the auto amplifier and heater branches.

The active test is an observation of commanded operation, not a replacement for the preceding circuit checks. Preserving whether the heater responds in different test modes can therefore matter more than simply noting that the cabin stayed cold.

Although the communication and supply checks involve low-voltage signals, the PTC heater is part of the EV's high-voltage equipment. Connector access and component replacement require trained personnel and the applicable isolation precautions. Save the full HVAC result and complete suffix before any reset or parts decision.
