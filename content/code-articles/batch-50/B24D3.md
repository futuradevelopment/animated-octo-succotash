# B24D3: Compressor communication diagnosis checks a shared LIN path

## The complaint is in transmitted information

For Ariya, B24D3-08 concerns abnormal information transmitted through the high-voltage-component LIN signal while READY. The condition must last at least thirty seconds, and compressor operation stops. The possible-cause list identifies the A/C auto amplifier and open or shorted communication wiring.

Despite the electric-compressor label, the diagnostic entry does not directly establish a failed compressor motor. The monitored communication path is a separate part of the system, and a controller can stop the compressor when it cannot rely on that path.

## The shared network must be isolated correctly

Nissan checks continuity between the compressor and auto amplifier, then checks for an unwanted ground connection with other specified LIN participants disconnected. The listed participants include the PTC heater, heat-pump controller and battery connection. That shared topology explains why testing one device without the prescribed isolation can be misleading.

The flow reconnects every disconnected harness before repeating confirmation. Its auto-amplifier replacement branch depends on the recurring result after the wiring checks, not merely on the original stored DTC.

Save the complete HVAC and related thermal-system scan, including whether multiple LIN devices have faults. Trained personnel must follow the correct precautions around high-voltage components even when diagnosing their low-voltage communication circuits. A brief power-on scan is also different from allowing the stated READY-state monitor time to run.
