# B24A1: Loss of accessory supply can stop the climate system

## The controller reports a supply-related event

The Ariya HVAC entry B24A1-16 concerns the A/C auto amplifier's accessory power supply dropping. It is monitored with the power switch on, with a listed diagnosis time under one second. Air-conditioning system operation stops as the protective response.

The source names the auto amplifier and an open or short in its accessory supply circuitry as possibilities. It does not publish a numerical low-voltage threshold here. A precise voltage limit from another module's DTC should therefore not be transferred to this entry.

## Verify the electrical foundation before interpreting the controller

Nissan's diagnostic direction is to check the auto amplifier's power supply and ground circuits. If those checks are normal, the controller branch follows; if they are not, the faulty circuit is repaired. A module that loses supply cannot be evaluated solely from the climate system's failure to operate.

Preserve whether the entire climate system stopped or only one function was affected, along with other low-voltage or body-controller codes. This information can help the technician relate a short supply interruption to the original complaint without assuming a cause from timing alone.

The referenced circuit procedure and vehicle wiring diagram are needed for connector locations and actual measurement limits. Replacing the climate controller simply because HVAC stored B24A1 would omit the source's required distinction between the controller and the power path that supports it.
