# B24D2: The compressor interlock identifies a connection-safety path

## An open interlock stops compressor operation

The Ariya B24D2-19 entry identifies an open high-voltage interlock circuit in the electric-compressor system. It is monitored in READY, with diagnosis listed as less than one second, and compressor operation stops. The possible causes include connector engagement, the high-voltage harness connector and compressor.

This is not a refrigerant-pressure or motor-current measurement. The interlock is intended to reflect the condition of a high-voltage connection, so bypassing it would remove the very evidence the system is checking rather than repair the underlying problem.

## Engagement, damage and continuity are separate findings

After the required high-voltage isolation and verification, Nissan checks that the compressor harness connector is installed correctly. It then examines the mating area for foreign material, cracking or terminal damage before checking the connector's interlock continuity.

These stages distinguish a connection that cannot engage properly from a damaged detection path and from the compressor branch reached after the harness-side checks. The final part choice depends on those results, not on the word compressor in the code label alone.

Tell the workshop about recent compressor, refrigerant or high-voltage harness work and preserve the original HVAC scan. Connector handling belongs with trained EV personnel using the complete isolation precautions. A non-operating compressor may be the intended protective response to an unsafe or electrically unverified connection.
