# C10B8: Nissan Ariya parking-brake motor misses its target

## A position result that was not achieved

For the cited Nissan Ariya brake controller, C10B8-77 means that a parking-brake actuator motor did not reach its target position. The monitor runs with the power switch on and normal supply voltage. Its listed delay is thirty seconds or more, which distinguishes this entry from parking-brake faults detected almost immediately.

The source lists the actuator and its wiring or connections as possible causes of both current and historical records. An ABS actuator and electronic controller problem is an additional possibility for a current code. The detection entry does not identify which rear wheel is involved, specify the target travel, or authorize replacement of an actuator based on a presumed side.

## Check what the actuator is trying to move

The diagnostic sequence begins with the rear brake components: pads, caliper assembly and parking-brake actuator, and disc rotor. Their detailed inspection limits are in separate referenced procedures. Any defective components found must be addressed before the verification stage, so an electronic position complaint does not eliminate a mechanical brake inspection.

The technician then completes the prescribed controller rest and power-on sequence and applies and releases the parking brake five times before reading ABS self-diagnosis. The final replacement branch concerns a CRNT C10B8-77 that remains after those checks. A PAST result is treated as historical memory, not sufficient evidence to replace the ABS assembly.

Because this entry suspends electric parking-brake operation, the vehicle must be secured appropriately during professional diagnosis. Repeatedly commanding a brake that cannot reach its target is not a substitute for identifying the underlying rear brake or actuator problem.
