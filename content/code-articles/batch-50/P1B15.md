# P1B15: Relay temperature sensing is separate from relay contact condition

## A thermistor circuit supplies temperature information

The Ariya 91 kWh battery's P1B15-11 entry concerns a short in the high-voltage relay thermistor circuit. The monitored quantity is thermistor voltage with the power switch on, and diagnosis is listed as two seconds or less. Its stated response limits driving output and illuminates the EV-system warning.

This electrical sensing condition does not prove that the relay contacts are stuck or that the relay is physically overheating. Those would require different evidence. The possible causes here are the thermistor, its circuit and the battery controller interpreting the signal.

## Circuit inspection precedes a controller decision

Nissan directs the technician to the relay-thermistor circuit procedure, then repeats confirmation. LBC replacement is conditional on the code returning after that sequence. The short DTC entry does not provide a temperature-to-resistance curve or a universal threshold that can be applied to another relay assembly.

Keep the full battery scan and any related relay or thermal codes. Their relationship may guide the workshop without changing this code's actual meaning. Accessing the relay and sensing wiring is high-voltage battery service, requiring trained personnel and the matching isolation instructions. Substituting a resistor or bypassing the sensor would undermine the temperature information the controller relies on.

The source limits the stated protective pattern to driving output and the warning lamp. It does not identify a charging restriction for this entry, and a separate charging complaint should retain its own supporting diagnostic evidence.
