# P008B: High fuel supply pressure and an ambiguous published threshold

Ford labels P008B as a fuel-pressure-too-high result in its normal fuel rail pressure control check. The useful distinction is between pressure control and sensor credibility: the functional monitor requires the related low-pressure sensor electrical and range/performance checks to complete first.

## Establish that the pressure data is usable

The table requires the high-pressure pump to be enabled, fuel level of at least 10 percent, no injector cutoff and operation outside cold-start emission reduction mode. It also bounds injection demand relative to pump volume. A pressure reading captured during a different state may not represent the conditions under which the functional result was evaluated.

Demanded and actual pressure should be preserved together. That comparison helps separate a control error from a pressure value that is simply high because the system requested it. The neighboring sensor range/performance monitor is a different check with its own time and pressure conditions.

## Published equation needs clarification

The source prints the same positive demand-minus-actual ratio for both P008A, labeled too low, and P008B, labeled too high. That duplication does not support a reliable directional threshold for P008B. This article therefore does not turn the printed formula into a workshop pass/fail specification.

Use the exact calibration's diagnostic procedure to resolve that limit. The supported information identifies the control-monitor category and its prerequisites; it does not justify replacing a regulator or opening a fuel system based on the code alone.

## Source and applicability

This explanation covers the cited Ford monitor variants, not every vehicle displaying this code. Match the engine, sensor architecture and calibration before applying numerical limits.
