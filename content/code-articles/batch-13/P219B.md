# P219B: Bank 2 air-fuel imbalance is evaluated after fuel adaptation

Ford identifies P219B as bank 2 air-fuel ratio imbalance. The monitor runs after the fuel system has adapted and evaluates a bank-specific imbalance ratio. It is not merely the same result as a bank-average lean or rich fuel-trim code.

## Adaptation and stable operating cells are prerequisites

The source requires closed-loop control, completed fuel adaptation and eligible engine speed/load cells. It also limits throttle-rate changes and excludes purge transitions. Those restrictions matter because changing operating conditions can obscure the imbalance evidence.

Ford lists 300–700 seconds to complete the monitor and requires healthy oxygen-sensor, fueling, misfire and related systems. A brief smooth idle or an uncompleted monitor does not reproduce this evaluation. Likewise, a reasonable average trim cannot by itself prove that the bank's imbalance ratio passed.

## Read the bank 2 result and its units

The published threshold is a bank 2 imbalance ratio above 0.75. Mode $06 monitor $82, test $80 exposes the bank 2 ratio and maximum limit as a unitless quantity. Do not reinterpret 0.75 as a voltage, pressure or direct percentage of one cylinder's fuel delivery.

Preserve the ratio, limit, completion status and operating conditions. The code identifies a monitored imbalance, but the summary does not determine which cylinder or component caused it. Further engine-specific diagnosis is needed before selecting an injector, ignition or mechanical repair. Verification should include a completed adapted-fuel imbalance check, not only cleared memory.

## Source and applicability

This explanation covers the cited Ford monitor variants, not every vehicle displaying this code. Match the engine, sensor architecture and calibration before applying numerical limits.
