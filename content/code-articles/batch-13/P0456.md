# P0456: A small EVAP leak can be detected after the engine is switched off

Ford's P0456 strategy evaluates a nominal 0.020-inch EVAP leak using engine-off natural pressure and vacuum behavior. The test can continue after key-off, which explains why running the engine at idle is not necessarily a useful attempt to verify this particular monitor.

## The preceding drive enables the parked test

The source requires eligible thermal history, fuel level, ambient conditions and sufficient operation before shutdown. During key-off, refueling-like pressure or level changes, unstable tank pressure, low battery voltage or turning the ignition back on can abort evaluation.

A test that stops for one of those reasons has not proved the system tight. Ford lists a maximum 45-minute evaluation when a fault is present, although successful checks may complete sooner. That is a monitor description, not a recommendation to repeatedly cycle the ignition or deliberately force a leak test.

## Read both pressure and vacuum evidence

The published malfunction criterion combines insufficient pressure build and insufficient vacuum build. Mode $06 therefore exposes separate positive-pressure and negative-pressure results, plus a normalized aggregate on applicable systems.

Ford warns that zero values may be defaults for phases that have never completed. Check completion status before interpreting a zero as a pass. The code identifies inadequate sealed-system behavior under the test conditions; it does not locate the leak or prove that a fuel cap, purge valve or canister is the failed component. Vehicle-specific leak diagnosis remains necessary.

## Source and applicability

This explanation covers the cited Ford monitor variants, not every vehicle displaying this code. Match the engine, sensor architecture and calibration before applying numerical limits.
