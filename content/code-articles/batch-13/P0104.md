# P0104: Why a brief MAF dropout can matter more than its average reading

Ford describes P0104 as intermittent behavior of digital mass airflow sensor A. Its malfunction criterion counts open or short events rather than judging only the average airflow shown on a scan tool. A smooth-looking display can therefore miss the evidence that caused the code.

## Count interruptions rather than assume low airflow

The documented threshold is more than 25 open/short occurrences. That differs from the neighboring low-input and high-input monitors, which compare the signal period against a sustained boundary. P0104 does not establish that the engine consistently receives too little air.

A useful diagnostic record preserves brief signal dropouts, their timing and the operating state in which they occur. This follows from the monitor's event-counting design; the summary does not prescribe a particular oscilloscope connection or connector manipulation test.

## Keep sensor architecture and channel separate

The page describes a digital signal and identifies sensor A. The corresponding sensor B intermittent result is P010E. Do not substitute the second sensor's data or apply an analog voltage specification merely because both components measure airflow.

Ford also lists general sensor checks and separate broken-element detection. Those categories require different evidence from intermittent circuit events. After a repair, verification should examine whether interruptions continue under the relevant conditions and whether the diagnostic monitor completes, rather than relying solely on one plausible airflow snapshot. The code itself cannot distinguish a sensor defect from the rest of its circuit.

## Source and applicability

This explanation covers the cited Ford monitor variants, not every vehicle displaying this code. Match the engine, sensor architecture and calibration before applying numerical limits.
