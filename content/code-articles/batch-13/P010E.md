# P010E: Sensor B airflow interruptions need time-resolved evidence

P010E is the intermittent circuit result for digital mass airflow sensor B in Ford's published check. It identifies a pattern of repeated interruptions, not a measured amount of air that is always too high or too low.

## Preserve the events that a live display can hide

The source uses more than 25 open/short occurrences as the intermittent malfunction criterion. A scan tool that updates slowly may show reasonable airflow between those events. The important evidence is therefore the recurrence of signal disruptions and the circumstances around them, not only the displayed average.

The neighboring low-input and high-input checks use signal-period boundaries and sustained durations. Passing one of those checks during stable operation does not demonstrate that the intermittent-event count has remained clear.

## Sensor B is a diagnostic identity

Ford distinguishes sensor A and sensor B in the same table. Match P010E to the correct channel in the engine's data and wiring documentation; the summary does not assign a universal physical side or intake location.

The documented strategy concerns a digital sensor. Analog MAF voltage guidance from another vehicle cannot be assumed to apply. After the underlying circuit issue is addressed, useful verification is a completed monitor with no repeated interruptions under the relevant operating conditions. The code does not by itself separate an internal sensor problem from connection, supply or circuit behavior, so a component replacement still needs additional vehicle-specific evidence.

## Source and applicability

This explanation covers the cited Ford monitor variants, not every vehicle displaying this code. Match the engine, sensor architecture and calibration before applying numerical limits.
