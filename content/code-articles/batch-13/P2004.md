# P2004: Bank 1 intake runners do not match the commanded closed state

Ford lists P2004 as intake manifold runner control stuck open on bank 1 in its vacuum-actuated V-engine strategy. The functional check compares plate behavior with the commanded position; it is not simply a test that the control solenoid receives voltage.

## Functional response is counted in events

The source enables IMRC monitoring after coolant exceeds 40°F and lists four events for stuck-position or over-travel evaluation. Electrical circuit checks use a separate five-second duration. A solenoid circuit that passes once therefore does not establish that the runners follow commands across the required functional events.

Preserve the requested runner state and the bank 1 position feedback together. An open position is only diagnostically meaningful relative to the command and the conditions under which the monitor evaluates it.

## Keep position sensing and control faults separate

Ford lists control-circuit, position-sensor low/high and over-travel codes alongside the stuck-open result. Invalid position feedback can change the interpretation of an apparent mismatch, while a valid electrical control signal does not prove mechanical response.

The cited strategy is explicitly vacuum-actuated and for a V-engine arrangement. Do not infer that every P2004 vehicle uses that hardware. Use the matching engine's service information to evaluate the command path, feedback and runner mechanism. The source supports a functional mismatch but does not identify a broken linkage, leaking vacuum component or failed actuator without further evidence.

## Source and applicability

This explanation covers the cited Ford monitor variants, not every vehicle displaying this code. Match the engine, sensor architecture and calibration before applying numerical limits.
