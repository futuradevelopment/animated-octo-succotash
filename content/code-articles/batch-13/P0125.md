# P0125: Coolant temperature has not caught up with the closed-loop model

P0125 concerns insufficient coolant temperature for closed-loop fuel control. Ford's cited monitor compares measured coolant warming with a modeled expectation during an eligible cold-start drive cycle. It is not the same monitor as the thermostat check printed above it.

## The model and measured temperature have different roles

The documented completion conditions require modeled coolant temperature to reach 80°F and remain beyond the threshold for at least three seconds. The listed malfunction is measured coolant temperature below 50°F. These are distinct quantities: the model establishes that warming should have progressed, while the measured value indicates that it has not met the expected state.

The monitor also limits how much of the running time is spent at idle or low load. A prolonged stationary warm-up therefore may not reproduce the same evaluation as an eligible drive cycle.

## Preserve cold-start context

Ford requires a sufficiently cold starting coolant state, an intake temperature of at least 20°F and, for flex-fuel vehicles, learned ethanol content. Those conditions affect whether the time-to-closed-loop test can complete.

Record starting temperatures, modeled or measured warm-up data when available, and monitor completion. Do not adopt the neighboring thermostat monitor's 172°F limit as the P0125 threshold: that is a different diagnostic strategy. The cited summary does not determine whether an individual case results from temperature sensing, thermal behavior or another cause, and it does not supply a universal thermostat replacement instruction.

## Source and applicability

This explanation covers the cited Ford monitor variants, not every vehicle displaying this code. Match the engine, sensor architecture and calibration before applying numerical limits.
