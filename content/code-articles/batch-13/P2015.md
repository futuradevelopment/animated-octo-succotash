# P2015: Bank 1 runner position can exceed its expected operating range

Ford describes P2015 as bank 1 intake manifold runner position over-travel in its vacuum-actuated V-engine strategy. The broader generic range/performance wording can hide this particular functional interpretation.

## Operational range is different from electrical range

The source separately lists position-sensor circuit low/high results and runner stuck-open/closed results. A position signal may be electrically valid yet indicate movement outside the expected operating range. Conversely, an out-of-range voltage is a different kind of evidence from a credible position showing over-travel.

Ford evaluates stuck and over-travel behavior across four events after coolant exceeds 40°F. The electrical checks use a five-second duration. A circuit pass alone therefore does not verify the functional range through the required events.

## Preserve command, feedback and architecture

Record the requested runner position and bank 1 feedback with the full fault set. These observations help distinguish a position-range result from a simple failure to reach the opposite commanded state.

The summary does not publish a universal angular or millimeter travel limit for P2015, nor does it identify a particular stop, linkage or sensor as failed. Apply the vehicle-specific procedure for the installed runner mechanism. Verification should demonstrate that reported position remains within the permitted operating range while following eligible commands. The source supports an over-travel interpretation for this documented strategy, not a blanket intake-manifold replacement rule for every P2015 vehicle.

## Source and applicability

This explanation covers the cited Ford monitor variants, not every vehicle displaying this code. Match the engine, sensor architecture and calibration before applying numerical limits.
