# P1784: The engine-to-transmission change signal is open or grounded

Volkswagen P1784/VAG 18192 describes an open circuit or short to ground in the change-up/down connection between the engine and transmission controllers. The chart identifies an intermodule signal wire, not simply a faulty manual-shift lever.

## Follow the connection named by the chart

The source directs wiring and connector checks, measured-value group 004, an electrical test, and engine-controller assessment. It references a particular transmission connection, but that pin assignment should be taken from the exact installed gearbox's diagram before any measurement.

This matters because other transmission codes refer to the Tiptronic switch itself. P1784's table instead focuses on communication through the engine-to-transmission change signal. Assuming the same fault location would mix distinct diagnostic branches.

## An open and a ground short share this entry

The code does not distinguish those two electrical conditions by title alone. The actual wiring test must determine whether continuity is missing or the signal is held toward ground. A component replacement without that distinction can leave the intermodule connection defective.

The chart reaches ECM replacement only if necessary after its preceding checks. It does not establish a failed transmission controller or internal gearbox damage from P1784 alone.

Verification should demonstrate a valid change signal in the applicable operating state and no recurrence of the open/ground-short entry. A normal selector display or a code erase is not enough to prove the intermodule path is intact. This explanation is limited to the documented transmission chart and its matching equipment configuration.
