# P1223: The second accelerator-pedal signal is too high

BMW's summary table places P1223 in the high-input accelerator-pedal position sensor family. It specifies a threshold above 4.75 V, evaluated continuously over a 60-millisecond interval. That is a signal-range criterion, rather than a statement that the driver pressed the pedal too far.

## Separate pedal position from signal validity

The pedal channels communicate a requested operating condition, but their electrical values must remain in a valid range. A channel at an excessive voltage can be invalid even if the pedal is physically at a normal position. The code therefore does not establish a mechanically sticking accelerator.

The table has a different row for disagreement between the two pedal channels, with a 0.75 V comparison criterion. P1223 is specifically the high-voltage branch; it should not be redefined as a generic correlation error.

## Do not borrow the throttle-body test

Accelerator-pedal and throttle-position sensors appear beside one another in the summary. They serve different roles, and the neighboring rows are not a substitute for the correct pedal wiring information. The source does not provide connector pin numbers or an approved resistance test for the pedal assembly.

The emissions-lamp requirement is two driving cycles. Verification needs a valid channel-2 signal across the pedal's operating travel and consideration of any related throttle-control faults. An unchanged pedal feel or an erased warning does not prove the electrical high-input condition is resolved. Component replacement should follow the full vehicle-specific diagnostic procedure, not the code title alone.
