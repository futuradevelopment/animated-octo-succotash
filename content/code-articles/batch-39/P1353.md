# P1353: Cylinder 6 misfire with protective fuel cutoff

P1353 is BMW's cylinder 6 misfire-with-fuel-cutoff entry. In the M54 summary, it is evaluated in the catalyst-damage branch rather than the starting-period emissions branch used by P1352.

## The observation has a short evaluation window

The table specifies a 200-revolution monitoring window and immediate lamp behavior. The threshold varies with engine speed and load, so a single universal misfire percentage cannot accurately describe the entire operating map.

This matters when reading live data after the event. A cylinder deliberately deprived of fuel by a protective strategy may continue to contribute poorly, even though that behavior is no longer a direct observation of the initiating fault. The original occurrence information should be preserved before attempting to interpret the post-fault state.

## Fuel cutoff is not an injector-circuit diagnosis

The code identifies the affected cylinder and the associated protection category. It does not establish an open injector wire, a failed ignition coil, or a loss of compression. The full combustion diagnosis is needed to distinguish those possibilities.

BMW's monitor also uses qualifying conditions related to load, vehicle speed, rough-road recognition, and changes in operation. A brief smooth idle after a restart does not establish that the original catalyst-damaging condition has disappeared.

Verification should demonstrate normal cylinder 6 contribution and no renewed protective intervention in the relevant operating region. Continued misfire warrants assessment rather than repeated code clearing. The numerical thresholds in this summary apply only to its listed emissions groups and are not a substitute for the exact vehicle's repair procedure.
