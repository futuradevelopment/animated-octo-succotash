# P1352: Cylinder 6 misfire detected during starting

The M54 monitor summary places P1352 in the start-period group, identifying cylinder 6. The controller uses crankshaft-speed variation to recognize uneven combustion. That gives a cylinder assignment and an operating phase, but does not identify a particular failed component.

## Start-period and running monitors differ

BMW's table allows an emissions-threshold exceedance within the first 1,000 revolutions after starting to satisfy the early branch. The later-running branch looks for repeated exceedances after that initial period. Consequently, the engine's behavior at the first start is more relevant to P1352 than an isolated observation made after several minutes of operation.

The summary states an emissions criterion greater than 1.8% over 3,000 ignitions and applies additional speed and airflow conditions. The numerical criteria are calibration-specific monitoring facts, not an instruction to deliberately induce a misfire.

## Use the cylinder assignment carefully

Cylinder 6 warrants focused combustion diagnosis, but the table does not rank ignition, fuel, or mechanical causes. Replacing a coil based solely on the cylinder number would add a conclusion not supplied by this source.

The fuel-cutoff/catalyst-protection family appears in another row. P1352 does not, by itself, establish that cylinder 6's fuel delivery was disabled. The start/emissions branch lists a two-driving-cycle lamp requirement.

A meaningful repair check repeats the relevant start conditions and confirms consistent cylinder contribution in that early period. Normal warm running is encouraging but does not substitute for verifying the phase that defines this code.
