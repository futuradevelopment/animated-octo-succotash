# P1525: Intake camshaft-actuator open-circuit monitoring

BMW P1525 identifies an open control circuit for the “A,” or intake, camshaft actuator on Bank 1. The M54 summary places it in the VANOS continuity group, distinct from the rationality and mechanically stuck monitors shown below it.

## Read the command state with the measurement

The table associates the inactive low-side driver with a voltage boundary below 2.9 V, while the active driver uses a 0.02–2.0 A current window. The electrical row is monitored continuously over a 1.25-second interval. These are state-dependent criteria, not a single resistance specification for a disconnected actuator.

That difference is useful when interpreting an intermittent fault. A circuit can look connected during a static check yet fail to behave correctly when the controller changes its command. The summary does not prescribe a direct battery test and does not identify the failed connector or winding from P1525 alone.

## Avoid a mechanical conclusion from an electrical code

Other rows in this source examine crankshaft/camshaft alignment and excessive time to reach the requested cam angle. P1525 belongs to neither of those performance checks. It should not automatically trigger a timing-chain or hydraulic VANOS diagnosis without first addressing the electrical evidence.

The lamp column specifies two driving cycles. Verification needs correct circuit behavior in the applicable driver states and appropriate confirmation of actuator operation. The source's LEV/ULEV test-group scope and the vehicle's actual wiring must be matched before using the listed values. It is a monitor explanation, not a complete replacement procedure.
