# P1452: DMTL switching-solenoid circuit high

The M54 OBD summary places P1452 in the DMTL switching-solenoid circuit-high family. It appears beside the pump and solenoid electrical checks, separate from the large- and small-leak measurement rows.

## Do not confuse electrical monitoring with leak diagnosis

The electrical branch checks continuity toward battery supply and lists a current criterion above 2.4 A. Its enabling battery range is above 9.5 V and below 17 V, with immediate, continuous evaluation and a two-driving-cycle lamp requirement.

The neighboring leak tests use pump-current comparisons, fuel level, temperature, soak time, and post-shutdown operation. Those rows do not define P1452. A smoke test finding no external leak therefore cannot alone prove that the switching-solenoid circuit is healthy.

## The circuit still needs fault isolation

The code identifies an electrical category, not a demonstrated hole in the tank or a failed fuel cap. The summary does not provide connector numbers, wiring colors, or an instruction to replace the entire DMTL module without further testing.

The useful next decision is whether the solenoid control path has the electrical behavior described by the matching service procedure. That requires the correct vehicle wiring and approved measurements. The current value is a monitor fact, not a recommendation to energize the circuit directly from the battery.

Verification should address the circuit-high condition and successful DMTL operation. The leak-test prerequisites should be used only when running their respective diagnostic routines, rather than being assumed to explain every DMTL-related code.
