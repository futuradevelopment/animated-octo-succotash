# P1472: Leak-detection pump V144 circuit shorted to ground

Volkswagen P1472/VAG 17880 identifies a short to ground in the tank-ventilation leak-detection pump circuit. The component is V144 in the ATQ service information. This is not the same as a small- or large-leak result.

## Distinguish pump response from command delivery

In the output test, V144 should click while selected. If it remains silent, Volkswagen checks the command at the connector. A pulsed command present without pump response leads toward the pump; a missing command requires wiring and supply investigation.

The pump's separate electrical check uses two contact-pair resistance specifications, 640–720 ohms and 17.5–27.5 ohms. These are different internal paths, so a measurement from the wrong contacts can produce a misleading conclusion. The exact connector diagram is essential.

## The power feed still matters

The supply procedure requires fuse 29 to be intact and checks the feed at idle. It follows an absent feed toward the relay-panel wiring. P1472's ground-short label narrows the fault category, but does not identify the physical location of the short without those circuit checks.

No particular fuel-vapor leak diameter or gas-cap failure is established by this code. A smoke-test result alone therefore cannot verify the electrical repair.

Completion should demonstrate valid commanded pump operation and resolve the ground-side fault, with the appropriate separate evaporative assessment where required. The source supplies controlled workshop tests; it does not support bypassing the pump circuit or applying power to unverified terminals.
