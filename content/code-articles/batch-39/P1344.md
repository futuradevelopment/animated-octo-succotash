# P1344: Cylinder 2 misfire in the early running period

P1344 is the cylinder 2 member of BMW's start-period misfire group. In the M54 emissions summary, misfire is inferred from crankshaft-speed fluctuation. The relevant branch examines the first 1,000 engine revolutions after starting, so the event history is more specific than a general complaint that the engine sometimes runs roughly.

## Why a warm test can miss the evidence

The table separates early-start recognition from a later branch that looks for repeated threshold exceedances once those first 1,000 revolutions have elapsed. If cylinder 2 contributes poorly only during starting, the later warm-running state may not reproduce the original condition.

Its emissions-based criterion is stated as greater than 1.8% over 3,000 ignitions, together with operating limits for speed and airflow. These are controller recognition conditions; they do not supply a workshop measurement of injector flow or coil output.

## Follow cylinder 2 without assuming a part

The code narrows the assigned cylinder, but the summary does not identify which combustion input failed. Comparing the original start behavior with the cylinder's relevant ignition, fuel, and mechanical evidence requires the complete diagnostic procedure.

BMW lists the fuel-cutoff/catalyst-damage group separately. P1344 should not be represented as proof that fuel was already shut off on cylinder 2.

The emissions-lamp column for the start-period branch specifies two driving cycles. Verification should cover the appropriate starting conditions and early revolutions, not merely remove the code and confirm that the engine is smooth after it has warmed.
