# P1704: The kick-down switch F8 circuit malfunctions

The Volkswagen transmission chart maps P1704/VAG 18112 to an electrical malfunction in kick-down switch F8. It lists an open connection, a short to ground between F8 and the transmission controller, and a defective switch as possible causes.

## The command is an input to the transmission

The service direction uses measured-value group 008 and an electrical check. This means the next useful observation is whether the controller receives the intended kick-down state, rather than immediately assuming a hydraulic downshift failure inside the transmission.

The chart also mentions adjustment or replacement of an accelerator cable. That instruction identifies an important scope limitation: the repair-information collection includes transmission/equipment variants, while the ATQ engine uses electronic pedal information in other procedures. The installed switch and pedal arrangement must be verified before applying a cable-related step.

## Do not infer an internal gearbox fault

P1704 does not itself establish worn clutches, inadequate pressure, or a failed shift solenoid. A missing or incorrect kick-down input can affect the requested shift behavior without identifying an internal transmission defect.

The table supplies no switching-voltage threshold or connector pin assignment for this entry. Those details require the matching wiring diagram and transmission procedure.

Verification should demonstrate a reliable kick-down input in module data and appropriate related operation after the electrical cause is corrected. This is a source-scoped explanation of the chart, not proof that every 2003 Passat has the same F8 or accelerator-cable arrangement.
