# P1677: The EPC warning-lamp circuit is shorted to supply

Volkswagen P1677/VAG 18085 identifies a short to B+ in the EPC warning-lamp K132 circuit. The lamp normally reports an electronic engine-load-control fault, but this code specifically concerns the reporting circuit itself.

## Lamp behavior has two checkpoints

The ATQ functional check expects the EPC lamp to illuminate with ignition on and then extinguish a few seconds after the engine starts, provided no relevant fault keeps it requested. Failure to illuminate and failure to go out are different observations with different electrical branches.

The procedure requires the lamp's fuses to be sound and checks the connection between the ECM and instrument cluster. It does not justify replacing a throttle body merely because “EPC” appears in the code description.

## Preserve the electrical distinction

P1677 is the supply-short variant; neighboring entries cover ground short and open circuit. A technician should retain that distinction when evaluating the warning-lamp wire and the actual commanded state.

The factory procedure uses a test box to separate the lamp/wiring path from the controller. Those equipment-specific tests are not a general instruction to bridge arbitrary cluster terminals.

Verification should show the expected ignition-on lamp check and correct post-start response, together with resolution of the stored supply-side circuit fault. A dark lamp alone is not proof that the engine-control system is healthy: a defective reporting path can hide its warning. Fault-memory and readiness completion remain part of the documented repair process.
