# P1743: A transmission pressure-control valve electrical fault

BMW's canonical P1743 description identifies pressure-control solenoid E electrical. The M62 TU summary labels its corresponding row as pressure-control valve 4. Those naming systems should be kept distinct: the code-to-component match must come from the actual transmission documentation, not from assuming that a letter is the same as a physical valve position.

## The source monitors electrical continuity

The row uses voltage as the malfunction criterion and a continuous 70-millisecond evaluation interval. It does not publish a hydraulic-pressure threshold or a solenoid resistance specification. Consequently, P1743 is not evidence by itself of insufficient line pressure or a mechanically worn clutch pack.

The same table separately evaluates gear-ratio behavior and torque-converter slip. Those monitors answer different questions from the electrical continuity check for this pressure-control valve.

## What the brief table leaves unresolved

No individual cause list or connector test is provided. A technician must identify the installed transmission, match its valve naming, and follow the corresponding electrical procedure. The navigation path's engine designation alone cannot determine every gearbox hardware detail.

The emissions-lamp grouping indicates two driving cycles. Verification should establish reliable electrical control of the identified valve and appropriate transmission operation under the matching procedure. Replacing a complete transmission or valve body solely because the title contains “pressure control” would exceed the evidence supplied here.

This is a source-scoped monitor explanation. It intentionally does not supply guessed pin numbers, coil resistance, or a universal valve location for P1743.
