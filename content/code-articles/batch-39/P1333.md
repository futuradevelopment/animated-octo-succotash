# P1333: Knock sensor 4 reports high input

BMW 2E6B/P1333 identifies high input from knock sensor 4 on the documented N62TU system. Its published enabling conditions include engine speed above 1,600 rpm, load factor above 25, and electrical-system voltage between 9 and 15 V. The load-factor unit is not defined in the chart.

## The service direction differs from low input

For this high-input entry, BMW directs an examination for mechanical engine defects before sensor replacement. That distinction matters because a sensor can be reporting real vibration as well as suffering an electrical or internal problem. Replacing it immediately could leave the cause of the measured high input unexplored.

The chart does not provide an upper signal-voltage threshold or identify a particular mechanical defect. P1333 therefore cannot by itself establish detonation, a bearing failure, or a required engine repair. It supplies the direction of the signal error and a reason to investigate the engine's mechanical condition.

## Reproduce the correct condition

The memory-entry section lists less than three seconds. An assessment carried out only at idle does not match the stated engine-speed condition. A qualified technician should connect the occurrence information with the appropriate engine tests rather than deliberately provoking abnormal engine noise.

The emissions lamp is enabled in the chart, but no specific apparent symptom is supplied. Verification should show that the high-input condition no longer occurs after the identified cause is corrected. The applicable repair manual is still needed for sensor location, attachment requirements, and safe mechanical testing; none of those details can be inferred solely from “sensor 4.”
