# P1477: The evaporative leak-detection pump malfunctions

P1477/VAG 17885 is the general leak-detection pump malfunction entry in the ATQ table. The source identifies V144 and directs checks of the pump and evaporative-canister system. It does not label this particular entry as a confirmed short to ground or supply.

## A general malfunction needs branch testing

Volkswagen's output test first establishes whether the pump responds when commanded. A silent pump with a valid pulsed connector signal is different evidence from a silent pump receiving no command. The latter branch includes harness and supply checks before a controller conclusion.

The dedicated component procedure also separates two internal resistance paths and the voltage supply. Its contact-pair values must be used with the exact connector information; they should not be collapsed into a generic “pump resistance” test.

## Do not substitute a leak diagnosis

P1477 does not publish a leak-size threshold or identify a fuel-cap seal as the cause. The adjacent table includes a different pump entry involving insufficient vacuum, but that wording should not be silently imported into this general malfunction code.

A technician should determine whether V144 is electrically functional and whether the wider evaporative test can operate correctly. The supplied procedure does not rank the pump, wiring, and controller by failure likelihood.

Verification should demonstrate correct command and response and completion of the relevant system checks. Clearing P1477 without establishing which branch failed can leave the underlying problem unresolved, even if the vehicle otherwise runs normally.
