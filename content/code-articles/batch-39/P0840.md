# P0840: Touareg hydraulic-pressure sender G193 circuit

In the BKW Touareg service collection, P0840 is assigned to transmission hydraulic-pressure sender 1, G193. The generic title calls it pressure sensor/switch A. Volkswagen directs the hydraulic-pressure-sensor electrical procedure rather than declaring a particular physical line pressure too high or low.

## Establish the test prerequisites

The procedure requires valid transmission grounds, appropriate fuses, battery voltage of at least 11.5 V, the selector in Park, and ignition off. These conditions define a controlled wiring assessment; they are not a pressure-operating specification.

The test checks continuity between the transmission-controller wiring and the identified eight-pin connection, using a maximum conductor resistance of 1.5 ohms. It separately checks for shorts between wires. Correct continuity alone therefore does not establish that adjacent conductors remain electrically isolated.

## Sensor replacement follows the wiring branch

Volkswagen directs replacement of the relevant pressure sender if the wiring checks reveal no fault. That sequence makes an external circuit defect a meaningful alternative to a failed sensor.

The chart does not publish a sensor signal voltage, pressure threshold, or hydraulic gauge comparison for P0840. A pressure value derived from a faulty electrical input cannot by itself establish actual hydraulic pressure.

Verification should address G193's measurement circuit using the exact installed transmission information and then complete the prescribed memory/readiness steps. Pressurized transmission-system work requires the applicable professional procedure; this code is not a reason to loosen a hydraulic connection merely to assess pressure by observation.
