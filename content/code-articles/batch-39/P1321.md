# P1321: W8 knock sensor 3 has a low signal

Volkswagen's W8 chart assigns P1321 to a low signal from knock sensor 3, G198. The matching BDP component procedure locates G198 in the Bank 2 sensor group and distinguishes it from sensor 4, G199. The code identifies a sensor channel, not cylinder 3.

## Mechanical contact affects the measurement

The procedure requires the contact surfaces between sensor and cylinder block to be free of corrosion, dirt, and grease. It specifies 20 Nm attachment torque for this engine. Those requirements make sensor seating part of the diagnostic evidence, rather than treating the device as an electrical component whose mounting is unimportant.

The manufacturer also specifies gold-plated connector terminals when servicing the sensor harness. A connector repair must preserve that requirement.

## Separate sensor resistance from harness continuity

Volkswagen tests isolation between the sensor's specified contact pairs, then checks the harness for opens and shorts. The sensor-side expectation of infinite resistance and the harness continuity maximum of 1.5 ohms describe different test arrangements; they must not be confused.

A low-signal entry does not establish a damaged combustion chamber or prove that the engine has no knock. It indicates that the expected sensor information is too low, with attachment and electrical integrity requiring assessment.

Verification should confirm the correctly identified G198, proper seating, and the applicable electrical checks before reassessing the code. The chart gives no low-signal voltage threshold, so none is inferred here. The W8-specific sensor location and procedure should not be transferred to another Volkswagen engine.
