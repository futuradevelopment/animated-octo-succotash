# P1174: Bank 1 additive adaptation exceeds its supported range

BMW's canonical P1174 definition concerns Bank 1 additive fuel adaptation. The M62 TU monitor table groups it with fuel-trim limits and states a correction magnitude beyond 24%, with short- and long-term correction named in the shared criterion.

## Preserve the limitation of the grouped row

The summary does not isolate a separate additive-only numerical threshold for P1174. It is therefore useful evidence of a trim-limit monitor, but not justification for applying the displayed percentage directly to an additive parameter expressed in another unit by a diagnostic tool.

The listed enabling condition is closed-loop fuel-system operation. A measurement captured before oxygen-sensor feedback is active cannot by itself verify this monitoring state. The row is evaluated immediately and continuously, while the lamp column specifies two driving cycles.

## Adaptation describes correction, not a failed component

The important next distinction is the direction and operating context of Bank 1's correction. An adaptation limit does not automatically identify an oxygen sensor or injector as defective. The table supplies no cause hierarchy and no replacement instruction.

A technician should use the exact BMW diagnostic representation of additive adaptation and its associated test plan, preserving the original correction values before resets. Resetting adaptation removes learned evidence and is not equivalent to fixing the condition that required correction.

Verification should show that the applicable adaptation stays within its permitted range in closed loop. This explanation deliberately retains the grouped-table limitation instead of presenting the 24% figure as a universal additive adaptation specification.
