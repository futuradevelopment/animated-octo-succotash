# P1394: Ignition output 2 has an electrical malfunction

The ATQ chart maps P1394 to VAG 17802 and an electrical malfunction in ignition output 2. Its service directions include the ignition wires and spark plugs, ignition-coil testing, and misfire recognition. It does not identify a particular cylinder simply from the output number.

## The coil and output stage form one assembly

Volkswagen's component procedure states that the coils and power output stage are a common part. It also says primary coil resistance cannot be measured by the proposed resistance check. A generic primary-resistance test is therefore not a valid shortcut for this application.

The prescribed secondary test initially includes the ignition wires, so the measured resistance also includes their suppression resistors. A failed combined measurement is followed by separate wire and coil checks. This prevents a wire problem from automatically being attributed to the coil assembly.

## Supply and activation are separate branches

The procedure requires at least 11.5 V and valid camshaft and engine-speed sensor operation. It then distinguishes voltage supply from ECM activation. The coils receive power through the fuel-pump relay and fuse 29, so the supply path is relevant to an ignition-output complaint.

These checks require appropriate ignition-system precautions and test equipment. The code alone does not justify probing a live high-voltage output or replacing the ECM.

Verification should establish proper supply, command, and ignition operation, with associated misfire evidence assessed. The source supports a structured distinction between the output assembly, wiring, and control signal; it does not provide a universal failure likelihood for any one of them.
