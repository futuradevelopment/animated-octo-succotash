# P0200: Touareg V10 pump-injector circuit diagnosis

For the 2004 BKW V10 diesel source, P0200 is an injector-circuit/open entry. Volkswagen directs the pump-injector unit procedure, which distinguishes the injector valves, cylinder-head connections, and wiring to the engine controllers.

## Two banks use two controller paths

The procedure identifies cylinders 1–5 with ECM J623 and cylinders 6–10 with ECM J624. That division matters because an injector-circuit investigation must follow the correct bank's wiring. A generic P0200 does not identify a particular cylinder without additional fault information.

The check requires at least 11.5 V, sound fuses and grounds, and undamaged central cylinder-head connectors. It also specifies silver-plated terminals when those connectors are serviced. That connector requirement differs from the gold-terminal instruction found in some other Volkswagen sensor procedures.

## Separate injector resistance from the harness

The isolated pump-injector valve specification is approximately 0.1–1.0 ohm. If the valve meets that check, the procedure continues to control wiring, including opens and shorts. A valid valve resistance therefore does not establish that the complete command circuit is sound.

The source does not identify a failed injector from P0200 alone or publish a fuel-delivery imbalance threshold for this code. Mechanical fuel-delivery questions are separate from this electrical diagnosis.

Access and testing involve engine components and specialized equipment and should be performed professionally. Verification must address the identified circuit and the corresponding controller/bank, then complete the prescribed fault-memory and readiness checks.
