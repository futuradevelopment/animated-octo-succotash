# P1434: The secondary-air pump relay circuit is shorted to B+

Volkswagen P1434/VAG 17842 identifies a short to B+ in the secondary-air injection pump relay J299 circuit. The ATQ table directs the output diagnostic test rather than an immediate air-pump replacement.

## Observe relay control and pump response separately

In the output test, J299 activates pump motor V101 in intervals. If the motor does not run, the procedure checks whether the expected pulsed supply reaches the pump connection. A valid supply with no motor response points to a different branch from a missing supply.

When the supply is absent, Volkswagen checks the relay fuse, the relay's power feed, and the relay/control wiring. The source therefore does not equate a silent pump with a failed motor. P1434's supply-short description makes electrical isolation especially important.

## The test has a defined operating context

Output testing is a controlled sequence that also operates the fuel pump and other actuators. The manual states that an engine-speed signal aborts the routine and that the sequence times out after ten minutes. Those details matter when distinguishing a genuinely missing response from a test that was never active or was interrupted.

The code does not prove blocked secondary-air passages or a particular exhaust-flow deficit. Those would require their own functional evidence.

Verification should demonstrate that J299 commands the expected intermittent pump operation and that the B+ circuit fault does not recur. The full electrical procedure is needed for connector measurements and safe access; the DTC title alone is insufficient to choose a relay, pump, or ECM replacement.
