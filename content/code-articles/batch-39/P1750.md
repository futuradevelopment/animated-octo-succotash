# P1750: Transmission supply voltage is outside the usable range

The Volkswagen chart pairs P1750 with VAG 18158 and a low-supply-voltage description. Its cause column is broader: it lists an open or ground short and supply to the solenoids or transmission controller outside 9–16 V. That wording should remain visible rather than being reduced to “replace the battery.”

## Measure the supply at the affected system

The service direction uses measured-value group 003 and checks voltage at transmission controller J217. A reasonable battery reading at another moment does not establish that the controller and solenoids received adequate voltage during the event.

The chart also includes the permanent-B+ path and wiring connections. Those separate feeds make connection integrity relevant alongside the state of the charging system. The source does not establish which physical connection failed from P1750 alone.

## Distinguish an electrical cause from shift symptoms

A transmission that lacks a valid supply can behave abnormally without having a primary hydraulic or mechanical defect. The chart therefore places wiring, connector, and supply checks before its controller-replacement outcome.

The low-voltage title and broader outside-range cause text are a limitation of this particular table; they should not be turned into an invented universal setting threshold. Use the complete fault record and measured conditions for the installed transmission.

Verification should demonstrate stable supply at the relevant controller and solenoid circuits and no repeated supply fault. Clearing the entry after charging the battery is not enough if an intermittent feed or ground problem remains.
