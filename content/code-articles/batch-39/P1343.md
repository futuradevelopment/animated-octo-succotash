# P1343: Cylinder 1 misfire in the fuel-cutoff family

BMW P1343 is the cylinder 1 entry in the misfire-with-fuel-cutoff family. The M54 summary groups it under the catalyst-damage criterion, separate from the ordinary emissions and start-period misfire branches.

## A protective response changes the interpretation

The monitor evaluates crankshaft behavior over a 200-revolution window. Its permitted misfire percentages vary with engine load and speed, rather than using a single fixed threshold. The table lists immediate lamp behavior for this group.

These facts make the distinction from P1342 useful: P1342 concerns the starting-period emissions branch, while P1343 belongs to the catalyst-protection group. The fuel-cutoff label describes a protective response associated with recognized misfire; it is not evidence that the injector itself failed electrically.

## Diagnose the initiating combustion problem

Cylinder 1 is the controller's assigned location, but the summary does not identify whether the initiating problem is ignition, fuel, or mechanical. Once fuel has been cut, observations made afterward can reflect the protection strategy as well as the original failure. The original fault context is therefore valuable.

The source includes qualifying limits for engine load, vehicle speed, rough-road recognition, and changing operating conditions. Those restrictions are part of the calibrated misfire assessment and should not be replaced by an arbitrary idle-only check.

A vehicle with continuing misfire needs appropriate professional assessment. Repeatedly clearing the code to restore fueling does not correct the initiating problem. Verification should establish stable combustion and absence of renewed catalyst-protection intervention under the applicable conditions.
