# P1324: W8 knock sensor 4 has an excessive signal

Volkswagen maps P1324 to a high signal from knock sensor 4, G199, on the BDP W8. The matching procedure places it in Bank 2 and distinguishes its connector from G198, the third sensor. The number identifies the sensing channel rather than cylinder 4.

## Preserve component allocation during service

Volkswagen instructs the technician to mark the connectors before disconnecting them. That requirement is useful when several similar sensors are accessible together: a valid electrical test on the wrong channel does not diagnose the P1324 entry.

Correct mounting also matters. The contact surfaces must be free of grease, dirt, and corrosion, and the securing bolt uses a specified 20 Nm torque in this application. These are part of the supported measurement path, not optional cosmetic checks.

## Test the sensor and harness as different circuits

The procedure checks isolation across the sensor contacts, followed by harness continuity and interwire shorts. Infinite resistance at the isolated sensor test and low resistance through a harness conductor are different expected results. They should not be combined into one generic “knock sensor resistance” specification.

The chart provides no actual high-input voltage threshold or proof of a particular combustion problem. Its service direction supports investigating G199's attachment and electrical path before selecting a failed part.

Verification should show the correct sensor is connected, seated, and electrically assessed, with no recurring high-signal fault. Apply this W8 procedure only to the matching engine configuration.
