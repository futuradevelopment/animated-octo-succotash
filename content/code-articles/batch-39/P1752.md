# P1752: Transmission supply voltage fluctuates excessively

Volkswagen P1752/VAG 18160 identifies an implausible supply-voltage signal at transmission controller J217. The chart specifically describes strong voltage fluctuations and lists the generator voltage regulator C1 as a possible source.

## Stability matters as well as voltage level

A single acceptable voltage reading cannot rule out the behavior described by this monitor. The useful evidence is whether the controller's supply remains stable during the relevant event. The service direction uses measured-value group 003 and a direct supply assessment at the TCM.

The adjacent P1751 row describes supply above 16 V, but P1752 does not publish a numerical fluctuation amplitude. That neighboring threshold should not be presented as P1752's own setting criterion.

## Check the path before replacing a controller

Volkswagen directs inspection of wiring and connectors, an electrical test, and evaluation of the regulator. The fault is therefore not an automatic TCM replacement instruction. A supply instability originating elsewhere can affect the transmission controller even when its internal electronics are functional.

The chart gives no particular mechanical transmission symptom or hydraulic-pressure conclusion. Such symptoms, if present, need interpretation alongside the power-supply evidence.

Verification should demonstrate stable supply at J217 and no recurrence of the implausible-voltage entry under relevant conditions. A recently charged battery or a code cleared while stationary does not establish that a regulator or intermittent connection problem has been resolved. Use the installed transmission's wiring and charging-system procedures for the actual measurements.
