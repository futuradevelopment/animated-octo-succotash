# P1175: Bank 2 additive fuel adaptation reaches a limit

The BMW P1175 definition identifies Bank 2 additive fuel adaptation. In the M62 TU OBD summary, P1175 appears in a grouped fuel-trim row whose criterion is correction magnitude beyond 24% and whose enabling state is closed-loop operation.

## The table does not separate every adaptation parameter

Both short- and long-term correction are named in the shared row. The summary does not publish an individually labeled additive threshold for P1175. That matters when a BMW scan tool displays adaptation in units different from percent: the table's number cannot simply be substituted for the tool's own allowable range.

The monitor is described as immediate and continuous, with an emissions-lamp requirement of two driving cycles. Recognition and lamp timing are therefore separate aspects of the record.

## Bank and correction direction guide the next question

Bank 2 is the affected adaptation channel, but the general malfunction label does not identify whether fuel correction is positive or negative. Retaining the actual adaptation values and operating state helps distinguish those possibilities before selecting a diagnostic branch.

The source does not prescribe an injector, airflow meter, or oxygen-sensor replacement. It explains the correction-limit observation; the detailed vehicle test plan must establish why that correction was needed.

A useful verification evaluates the correct Bank 2 adaptation parameter in closed-loop operation after the underlying cause is addressed. Clearing learned values alone can temporarily change what the tool displays without demonstrating that the mixture-control problem has been corrected. Keep the M62 TU source scope and its grouped-row limitation explicit.
