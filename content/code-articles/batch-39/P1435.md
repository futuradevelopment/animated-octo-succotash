# P1435: Secondary-air pump relay control is shorted to ground

Volkswagen assigns P1435/VAG 17843 to a short to ground in the secondary-air pump relay J299 circuit. This differs from an inadequate-airflow code: the stored entry concerns electrical relay control.

## A missing pump sound needs one more distinction

The ATQ output test commands the relay and expects pump V101 to run in intervals. When that response is absent, the procedure checks the pump's electrical supply. A pulsed supply reaching a nonresponsive motor and no pulsed supply reaching the connector are separate diagnostic outcomes.

For a missing supply, the source directs attention to the relay fuse, terminal-30 feed, relay, and control wiring. That sequence is useful because replacing the pump would not repair an upstream ground short that prevents its normal activation.

## Confirm the test is actually running

The output sequence is terminated by a detected engine-speed signal and has a ten-minute limit. It also runs the fuel pump. A technician must use the full procedure and observe its state, rather than infer a component fault after an aborted actuator test.

P1435 does not identify a restricted air passage or establish whether the secondary-air system supplies enough air once electrically operational. Those questions remain distinct from the relay-circuit fault.

Verification should demonstrate normal commanded relay/pump response and absence of the ground-short entry. The chart does not provide a universal short-circuit location or failure probability, so harness and component conclusions must come from the actual electrical checks.
