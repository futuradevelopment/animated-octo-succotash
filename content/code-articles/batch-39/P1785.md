# P1785: The change-up/down signal is shorted toward supply

The Volkswagen transmission chart pairs P1785 with VAG 18193 and a short to positive in the change-up/down connection. It identifies wiring between the engine and transmission controllers as the relevant path.

## Differentiate the signal wire from the selector switch

The service direction uses measured-value group 004 and electrical checks of the intermodule connection. This is different from a chart specifically diagnosing the Tiptronic up/down switch. The presence of “shift” in a generic description is not enough to select a replacement selector assembly.

The source's cause column also includes an open connection in this branch. That broad wording means the actual electrical test, not the title alone, must establish how the signal path has failed.

## Use the matching diagram

The table names a controller connector position, but gearbox variants can have different arrangements. The correct transmission identification and wiring diagram must accompany any pin-level test. This article does not turn the isolated chart reference into a universal probing instruction.

Volkswagen lists an engine-controller check and replacement only if necessary after wiring assessment. The entry does not prove a failed ECM, damaged transmission clutch, or mechanical shift linkage.

Verification should establish that the signal is no longer held toward supply and that the intended engine/transmission information is present. A code that disappears immediately after clearing remains unverified until the relevant connection and operating behavior have been assessed. Keep the P1785 supply-side branch distinct from P1784's open/ground category.
