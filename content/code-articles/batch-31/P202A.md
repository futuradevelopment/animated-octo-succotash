# P202A: reductant tank-heater openness is evaluated when heating starts

P202A concerns the reductant tank-heater control circuit being open. Audi's CPNB chart evaluates conductance at the start of heating and lists a value at or below 0.10 reciprocal ohm. The timing and quantity are both part of the criterion.

## Conductance is not resistance

The table uses inverse ohms, not an ohm reading. As diagnostic interpretation, copying 0.10 into a resistance test as though the units were interchangeable would be incorrect. Conductance expresses the ability of the path to carry current; the applicable repair information determines how the controller's criterion relates to a workshop measurement.

The start-of-heating condition also means that an unrelated reading taken at another operating stage may not reproduce the monitor event.

## Keep the heater identity clear

This row concerns the tank heater. The same book describes other reductant heating circuits separately, so the named circuit should be confirmed before applying a measurement. The chart gives no universal connector arrangement or complete disassembly procedure. A useful diagnosis establishes why the relevant heating path appears open under its commanded conditions. These criteria are scoped to the CPNB section of Audi's 2014 A6/S6 book and are not a blanket instruction to replace the reductant tank.
