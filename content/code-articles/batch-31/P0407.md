# P0407: low G212 EGR-position feedback and scoped software notes

P0407 concerns a low EGR-position signal in Ross-Tech's VAG entry. The feedback device is identified as G212. The source's electrical alternatives are the wiring or connections and the potentiometer itself; a low signal is therefore not proof that the EGR passage is blocked.

## Position feedback is a separate question from gas flow

A technician needs to establish why the reported position is low using the correct circuit information. As diagnostic interpretation, that evidence should be gathered before treating the code as confirmation of a mechanical flow restriction. The page does not provide a universal sensor voltage or an EGR cleaning procedure.

## Do not generalize the campaign note

Ross-Tech mentions software updates and recalls for particular rest-of-world common-rail diesel applications, including certain 1.2, 1.6 and 2.0 litre systems. That is a reason to check applicability for the actual vehicle, not evidence that every P0407 is covered by a recall or needs a flash update. Market, engine and vehicle identification remain decisive.

The useful result separates an applicable software or campaign issue from a verified G212 circuit or component defect. This article follows an independent diagnostic-tool publisher's guidance. It neither establishes current recall eligibility nor supplies a replacement instruction for every Volkswagen or Audi displaying the code.
