# P1422: interpret the secondary-air valve's short-to-positive fault

P1422 identifies a short-to-positive condition for secondary-air solenoid N112 in Ross-Tech's VAG entry. This is an electrical description. It should not be read as a claim that secondary-air pressure is too high or that excessive air is flowing through the system.

## A named valve does not settle the cause

The source lists N112 and secondary-air relay J299 as possible causes. That provides two component branches to assess rather than a single mandatory replacement. The relay's inclusion is especially relevant when the valve's apparent fault cannot be explained by findings at the valve itself.

The practical interpretation is to reconcile the positive-short indication with the actual circuit design and measured behavior. This is editorial reasoning; the source does not publish a universal voltage test, wiring route or direct-power procedure.

## A repair should explain the electrical finding

Restoring airflow without resolving the recorded circuit condition would leave the original evidence unexplained. Equally, a stored P1422 alone is not proof that the pump or a carbon-blocked passage needs replacement. Ross-Tech is an independent diagnostic-tool publisher, and its fault description is useful only when the complete VAG controller text matches the installed system. Vehicle-specific repair information is needed to turn the named alternatives into a supported repair.
