# P20B8: high metering-heater conductance is not high tank temperature

P20B8 concerns a high condition in the reductant metering-unit heater control circuit. In Audi's CPNB diagnostic chart, the criterion is conductance above 1.00 reciprocal ohm at the start of heating. The source is describing an electrical property of the evaluated heating path.

## High needs its unit

It would be incorrect to restate the threshold as one volt, one ohm or a high fluid temperature. The quantity is conductance. As diagnostic interpretation, increased conductance is not interchangeable with increased resistance, so the unit must remain attached to the number when reviewing test evidence.

The row's start-of-heating condition also limits what an observation can establish. A measurement from a different operating state may not explain why this particular monitor reported a fault.

## Confirm the metering-unit circuit

Audi lists multiple reductant heating functions in this section. The metering-unit heater should not be assumed to be the tank heater or another heating element simply because the warning concerns the same emissions system. The quick-reference row supplies no pinout, direct-power test or required replacement. Use CPNB repair information to identify the path and explain the high-conductance result before choosing a repair. These criteria are scoped to this Audi monitor.
