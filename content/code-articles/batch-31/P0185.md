# P0185: the G248 low-input interpretation needs the full fault text

The general P0185 label identifies fuel-temperature sensor B's circuit. Ross-Tech's VAG entry is more specific: the G248 signal is too low. Those descriptions differ in detail, so retain the complete controller fault text when deciding whether this interpretation applies. A bare code cannot supply the missing vehicle-specific circuit information.

## What low input does and does not establish

Ross-Tech lists open or shorted wiring, connection problems, the sensor and the controller as possibilities. It also includes low ambient temperature. Consequently, a low electrical input is not sufficient evidence to condemn G248, and it should not be translated into a claim that fuel pressure is low. Temperature sensing and fuel delivery are different diagnostic questions.
