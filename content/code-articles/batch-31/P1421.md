# P1421: N112 short-to-ground indication also warrants relay assessment

P1421 is the short-to-ground fault for secondary-air solenoid N112 in Ross-Tech's VAG reference. The electrical qualifier is important: it points to the controlled circuit rather than directly measuring how much air reaches the exhaust. A secondary-air flow diagnosis cannot be substituted for the electrical question.

## N112 is not the only named possibility

Ross-Tech includes J299, the secondary-air relay, as well as N112 in its cause list. The article therefore does not equate a valve-named code with a confirmed bad valve. The available circuit evidence needs to distinguish these possibilities using information for the actual vehicle.

As diagnostic interpretation, the full fault text should be preserved when comparing electrical findings. A ground-short indication is more specific than a general secondary-air warning and should not be reduced to an assumption that the air pump is weak.

## What the reference does not supply

There is no universal relay-terminal assignment, test voltage or solenoid-resistance limit on this source page. Improvised bypassing would not be a supported diagnostic method. Ross-Tech's independent guidance supports checking the named valve and relay, while the manufacturer's circuit procedure determines the measurements and whether a component or wiring repair is justified.
