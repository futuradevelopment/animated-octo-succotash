# P2711: DSG gear disengagement needs speed and fault-subtype evidence

P2711 describes unexpected or implausible gear disengagement in Ross-Tech's VAG reference. For the DSG context, possibilities include the double clutch, gear shafts, output-speed sensors G501/G502 and mechatronic unit J743. The fault does not identify one of these as conclusively failed.

## Preserve the subtype

Ross-Tech distinguishes a no-signal or communication interpretation from a no-or-incorrect-basic-setting interpretation. For the latter on the 02E gearbox, it points to basic setting. That distinction matters: an adaptation issue should not silently become a mechanical-clutch diagnosis.

For 02E mechanical assessment, the source discusses comparing output and input shaft speeds with the clutch engaged during a controlled test. This is a professional diagnostic observation, not an instruction to road-test a malfunctioning transmission without assessment.

## The clutch bulletin has an important condition

A Volkswagen bulletin cited for specified 2009–2012 DSG applications may call for clutch and cover replacement when P2711 is the only transmission fault. Other faults and different applications change the context. As diagnostic reasoning, retaining the complete transmission scan is therefore necessary before relying on that note. Ross-Tech's independent page supplies no universal clutch replacement requirement and does not authorize applying the bulletin beyond its stated vehicles and fault condition.
