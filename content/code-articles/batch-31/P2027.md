# P2027: the high-voltage label is paired with a current criterion in Audi's chart

P2027 carries a high-voltage label for the EVAP fuel-vapor temperature-sensor circuit. Audi's CAED quick-reference row, however, lists signal current above 2.20 A as its criterion. This article preserves that distinction rather than silently changing the number into volts.

## The label and measurement quantity are different

A code title is not a substitute for reading the criteria column. As diagnostic interpretation, comparing a voltage measurement with a limit expressed in amperes would not evaluate the published condition. The actual circuit and professional procedure are needed to determine how this current criterion is assessed.

The row also does not provide a vapor-temperature value or establish that the tank is too hot. It concerns electrical monitoring, not a direct thermal diagnosis.

## Confirm the applicable diagnostic information

The source is a quick-reference table, with no connector assignment, test-equipment setup or complete isolation sequence. Confirm the CAED repair information before applying the threshold to a real measurement. This article does not supply a direct-current probing instruction or infer a failed sensor from the generic label alone. The stated criterion is scoped to Audi's 2014 A6/S6 CAED section, and other engines must use their own documented evaluation.
