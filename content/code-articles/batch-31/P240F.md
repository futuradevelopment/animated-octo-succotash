# P240F: EGR slow response is evaluated using changing air-mass targets

P240F identifies slow EGR response. Audi's CPNB chart describes calculated characteristic values associated with negative and positive gradients of target air-mass flow. The listed boundaries are greater than 35 for the negative-gradient characteristic or at least 30 for the positive-gradient characteristic, with dimensionless notation.

## The response is dynamic

A gradient describes change, so this entry should not be reduced to a fixed airflow value at idle. As diagnostic interpretation, an acceptable static reading does not independently demonstrate that the system responds correctly when the target changes. The direction of change is also relevant because the table lists different characteristic boundaries.

Those numbers are not seconds, grams per second or a permitted EGR opening percentage. Converting them into a familiar unit without the calculation would invent a specification.

## The chart is not the complete algorithm

Audi does not publish the full characteristic calculation or isolate the failed component in this row. The correct CPNB diagnostic procedure is needed to connect the dynamic fault to its cause. This article explains why changing target-air-mass evidence matters, without claiming that P240F proves a mechanically sticking valve or supplying a universal response-time test for other engines.
