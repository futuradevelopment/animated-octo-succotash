# P2403: low leak-pump sense feedback must persist in Audi's CTUA monitor

P2403 is an EVAP leak-detection pump sense-circuit or open-circuit fault. Audi's CTUA diagnostic chart lists low signal voltage persisting for more than one second. The criterion combines an electrical state with duration; it does not specify a vapor-leak size.

## Sense feedback is different from pump control

The table separately lists pump-control faults, while this entry concerns the sense path. As diagnostic interpretation, confirming that the pump receives a command does not independently establish that its feedback is credible. Likewise, a sense-circuit fault is not direct evidence that the fuel cap or tank has a leak.

The one-second condition is also meaningful. A momentary low sample does not, by itself, reproduce the persistence described in this particular row.

## Do not invent the missing voltage limit

Audi's CTUA entry calls the signal low but does not publish a numerical voltage boundary here. That should not be filled with a guessed value or a threshold from another engine. Use the applicable repair procedure to assess the actual circuit and test state. This monitor explanation is limited to the CTUA section of the 2014 Audi A6/S6 book and does not prescribe pump replacement simply because its sense feedback was abnormal.
