# P1559: throttle basic-setting failure can involve closure or engine-speed input

P1559 describes a fault in throttle-body J338 basic settings in Ross-Tech's VAG reference. The page lists an open idle switch or circuit, a throttle valve that sticks and will not close, and a detected signal from engine-speed sensor G28. These are different reasons the required setting may not complete.

## Failure to adapt is not the same as no adaptation attempt

The useful question is what prevented the basic-setting operation from meeting its conditions. A mechanism that cannot reach closure differs from an input indicating engine rotation. As diagnostic interpretation, simply repeating the procedure without considering those conditions may leave the original obstacle in place.

Ross-Tech recommends throttle-body adaptation, but that recommendation does not erase the listed causes. The relevant vehicle procedure determines the required operating state and how the idle-switch or closure evidence should be checked.

## Avoid an unsupported throttle replacement

This source page does not provide a universal adaptation channel, target angle or actuator replacement instruction. A repair decision should explain whether the problem was the mechanical closure, its switch or circuit, or the conditions under which basic setting was attempted. The article follows Ross-Tech's independent VAG guidance and does not imply that every throttle body uses the same setting sequence.
