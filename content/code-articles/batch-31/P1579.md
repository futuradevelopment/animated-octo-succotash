# P1579: throttle adaptation has not started

P1579 means throttle-actuator J338 adaptation has not started in Ross-Tech's VAG entry. The source lists an unperformed throttle-body alignment as the cause and recommends carrying out that procedure. The important information is the status of the setting operation, rather than a demonstrated electrical or mechanical defect.

## Establish whether alignment was actually performed

As diagnostic interpretation, a technician should distinguish an operation that was never initiated from one that was attempted and failed for another reason. Those histories lead to different questions. P1579 alone does not identify a stuck throttle, a low-voltage event or a particular failed position sensor.

The source associates the fault with an illuminated malfunction lamp. The lamp confirms that the controller has reported a problem, but it does not supply the missing adaptation history.

## Completion must be verified using the applicable procedure

Ross-Tech's code page does not provide a universal basic-setting channel or sequence. Use the alignment procedure for the actual throttle system and diagnostic equipment, then evaluate whether it completes. Merely clearing P1579 is not the same operation as performing an alignment. This article follows an independent diagnostic-tool publisher's guidance and does not establish that a throttle body needs replacement when its adaptation has not yet started.
