# P1565: the throttle's lower stop can be obstructed or held by a cable

P1565 means the throttle actuator J338 did not reach its lower stop in Ross-Tech's VAG interpretation. The source includes a dirty or carbonized throttle, a faulty actuator and an excessively tight mechanical cable among the possible causes. Those alternatives distinguish a blocked or restrained movement from an internal actuator failure.

## The cable note is not universal fitment

A mechanical cable matters only on a system that actually uses one. As editorial diagnostic reasoning, the correct throttle arrangement must be established before applying that cause. An electronically controlled system without such a cable should not receive an invented cable-adjustment procedure.

The source also names contamination, so failure to reach the stop is not itself proof that the actuator's electrical drive has failed. Checking the actual movement and obstruction evidence is more informative than the warning lamp alone.

## Cleaning and adjustment require the right procedure

Ross-Tech recommends checking or cleaning the throttle and adjusting the cable where appropriate, but the page provides no universal cleaning method, stop dimension or cable setting. Use the installed system's repair instructions and verify that the relevant closure condition is achieved. This independent reference does not justify forcing the plate against its stop or replacing J338 before the mechanical alternatives are assessed.
