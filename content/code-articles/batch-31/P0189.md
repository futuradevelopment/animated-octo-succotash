# P0189: intermittent fuel-temperature feedback from G248

Ross-Tech describes P0189 as an intermittent signal from fuel-temperature sensor B, designated G248 in its VAG reference. Intermittent is the useful qualifier: the fault record concerns signal behavior that may not still be present when the vehicle reaches the workshop. A plausible current reading therefore cannot by itself explain the stored fault.

## Why the circumstances matter

The reference includes connection defects, open or shorted wiring, the sensor, the controller and low ambient temperature among its possible causes. The diagnostic question is whether the reported temperature remained believable during the recorded event. A history of cold operation and a record of a momentary electrical interruption represent different lines of evidence.

As an editorial application of those alternatives, preserve any available event conditions before attempting to reproduce the complaint. Repeated interruptions associated with the same operating circumstances are more informative than a single normal reading afterward. This is not a source-specified wiggle test or permission to probe an unidentified connector.

## Keep the conclusion narrow

P0189 does not establish contaminated fuel, a blocked filter or inadequate fuel pressure. Ross-Tech's page concerns temperature feedback and provides no universal resistance specification. Use the vehicle's repair information to establish the G248 circuit and the appropriate checks before deciding whether the fault belongs to the sensor, its connections or the controller.
