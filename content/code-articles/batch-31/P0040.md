# P0040: opposite bank corrections can reveal swapped front oxygen signals

P0040 generally identifies front oxygen-sensor signals assigned to opposite banks. Audi's 2014 A6/S6 reference book supplies a concrete CEUC-engine monitor: one bank's lambda-control value is below 0.80 while the other's is above 1.20. The table allows either bank to be the low side, provided the other is the high side.

## The relationship is the useful evidence

This is a two-bank comparison. One unusual correction value alone does not reproduce the documented condition. As diagnostic interpretation, evaluating both banks together helps distinguish an opposing control pattern from a general mixture problem affecting them similarly.

The monitor describes the controller's evidence for the fault, not a direct visual proof that two connectors were exchanged. Correct sensor-to-bank assignment still needs verification against the CEUC wiring and component information. The chart does not prescribe a sensor replacement.

## Keep the calibration scope intact

The numeric limits belong to the CEUC section of this specific Audi book. They are not universal oxygen-sensor voltages or fuel-trim limits for every manufacturer. A useful diagnostic record preserves both lambda values, their bank identities and the relevant wiring evidence. That supports a conclusion about signal assignment without assuming that a component is defective simply because its feedback produced the opposing corrections.
