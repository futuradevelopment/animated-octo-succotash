# P00A2: a low charge-air signal can be represented as an extremely high temperature

P00A2 is a low-circuit fault for the bank 2 charge-air cooling temperature signal in the generic catalog. In Audi's CTUA-engine chart, the component is described as the intake-air temperature sensor after intercooler 2, shorted to ground. The listed detection criterion is reported intake temperature above 129°C.

## Low circuit does not mean cold air

The useful distinction is between electrical signal level and the temperature represented by that signal. The source associates this ground-short interpretation with an extremely high reported temperature, not a cold reading. As diagnostic reasoning, interpreting the word low as low air temperature would send the investigation in the wrong direction.

The temperature threshold is the controller's criterion. It does not, by itself, prove that the air actually reached that temperature; the reading is being used in an electrical-fault context.

## Identify the correct sensor position

The source specifically names the sensor after intercooler 2. Its placement and circuit must be established from CTUA service information before testing. The book does not provide a resistance curve or a repair instruction in this row. This monitor explanation is limited to the CTUA section of the 2014 Audi A6/S6 reference, not every vehicle using P00A2.
