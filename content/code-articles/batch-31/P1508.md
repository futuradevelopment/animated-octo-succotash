# P1508: the upper idle-regulation limit can point toward restricted intake air

P1508 means the idle-control system has reached its upper regulation limit in Ross-Tech's VAG interpretation. The source identifies an obstructed air filter or intake path as possible causes and lists power loss as a possible symptom. Its emphasis is therefore on the air available to the engine.

## Reaching a limit does not identify a failed actuator

A controller at its adjustment limit may be responding to a condition outside the control component itself. Here, the source specifically names restricted intake air. As an editorial diagnostic implication, checking the intake path is relevant before treating the label as proof that an idle actuator or throttle assembly is defective.

The distinction is also useful after maintenance: the relevant question is whether the intended air path is available, not simply whether a filter was recently replaced. This is reasoning from the obstruction branch, not a source claim about a particular installation mistake.

## Stay within the documented evidence

Ross-Tech does not provide an intake-flow threshold, an idle-speed target or a universal adaptation procedure for this page. The source supports investigation of the filter and intake, but it does not establish a particular obstruction on the vehicle. Use the installed engine's procedure to verify the restriction and explain why idle regulation reached its limit.
