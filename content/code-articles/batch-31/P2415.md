# P2415: bank 2 front-oxygen feedback exceeds the CTUA signal-range criterion

P2415 is cataloged as an exhaust-sample error for bank 2 sensor 1. Audi's CTUA chart describes a front oxygen-sensor signal-range check and lists a signal above 3.1 V. The source therefore frames the fault as an evaluation of sensor feedback, not a direct instruction to replace the catalytic converter.

## Bank and sensor position remain essential

The monitored feedback belongs to bank 2's front sensor in this row. As diagnostic interpretation, a plausible reading from a rear sensor or the other bank cannot independently explain why this signal-range criterion was exceeded. Correct identification must precede comparison.

The voltage threshold is not a measured air/fuel ratio or a published exhaust-temperature limit. Those quantities should not be inferred from the number without the appropriate sensor characteristic and operating information.

## Keep the conclusion narrower than the repair

Audi's quick-reference table provides no complete isolation tree, wiring pinout or mandatory replacement. It identifies the controller's criterion. A supported repair requires evidence explaining the high front-sensor signal in the CTUA system. This article is limited to that engine section of the 2014 A6/S6 book and does not treat 3.1 V as a universal oxygen-sensor threshold for every P2415 implementation.
