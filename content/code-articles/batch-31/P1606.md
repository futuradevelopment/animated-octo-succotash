# P1606: rough-road or torque information from ABS has an electrical fault

P1606 concerns an electrical malfunction in the rough-road or engine-torque signal from ABS in Ross-Tech's VAG reference. The source identifies wiring or connectors to the brake electronics as possible causes. The fault therefore describes information exchanged between systems, not a direct measurement of a failed engine component.

## The brake module may hold related evidence

Ross-Tech notes that brake-electronics fault 01203 can appear with P1606. That makes the brake module's fault record relevant to understanding the engine-side complaint. As diagnostic interpretation, examining only the engine controller can leave the other side of the reported signal problem unexplained.

The page also mentions the malfunction lamp. It does not establish that road roughness itself is abnormal or that the vehicle needs an engine-torque sensor replacement. The named electrical path is the supported starting point.

## Work from the actual signal architecture

This source contains no universal connector pin, wire color or ABS-module replacement instruction. The correct wiring diagram and professional brake-electronics procedure are needed to identify the circuit and its fault. The useful outcome is evidence that links the signal complaint to a connector or wiring defect, or shows why further diagnosis is needed.
