# P1580: bank 1 throttle-actuator malfunction includes sticking and dirt

P1580 is a bank 1 throttle-actuator malfunction in Ross-Tech's VAG reference. The component discussed is throttle-position actuator V60. The source includes a dirty, stuck or faulty actuator among its possible explanations, and it notes an illuminated malfunction lamp.

## Malfunction is broader than electrical failure

The source does not restrict the fault to a short circuit or broken motor. Its dirt and sticking alternatives make the mechanism's actual condition relevant. As diagnostic reasoning, a command that fails to produce the expected movement does not, by itself, distinguish a physical obstruction from an internal component failure.

Component identification also matters. V60 is the source's designation for this interpretation; it should not be silently replaced with a pedal-sensor name or a throttle designation from a different engine.

## Inspect within the correct throttle procedure

Ross-Tech directs checking V60 but provides no universal cleaning method, resistance specification, plate angle or replacement sequence.
