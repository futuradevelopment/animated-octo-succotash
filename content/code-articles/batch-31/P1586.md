# P1586: electrical evidence for N144/N145 engine-mount valves

P1586 describes engine-mount solenoid valves N144/N145 shorted to positive in Ross-Tech's VAG reference. The page lists defective wiring or connectors and defective valves as separate possibilities. It does not say that a mechanical mount failure has been proven.

## Keep the electrical and structural questions separate

An engine mount may be named in the component description, but this code's qualifier concerns the valve circuit. As diagnostic interpretation, a complaint of vibration would not remove the need to explain the electrical finding, and the code alone cannot establish that a mount's structural material has failed.

Ross-Tech directs checking and repairing the wiring or connectors and the valves as appropriate. The two valve designations should be identified using the actual vehicle's documentation rather than assuming their locations or connector assignments.

## A component replacement needs supporting evidence

The source supplies no universal coil-resistance limit, command voltage or mounting procedure. A useful diagnostic result distinguishes a circuit defect from a valve defect under the proper test. This independent VAG reference does not instruct replacement of both mounts merely because both valve designations appear in the fault label. Any mechanical mount assessment remains a separate part of the vehicle-specific repair process.
