# P0312: cylinder 12 misfire needs a cause beyond the cylinder number

P0312 reports a misfire on cylinder 12. Ross-Tech includes missing ignition, incorrect fuel metering and compression problems in the checks for this fault. Those are different mechanisms with different repairs; the cylinder number does not tell the technician which mechanism is present.

## Mechanical evidence remains relevant

The source explicitly includes compression and base-engine problems. As diagnostic interpretation, that means persistent cylinder-12 misfire after an ignition inspection still needs explanation rather than repeated replacement of ignition parts. The article does not specify a compression value because the reference provides no engine-specific limit.

Fuel pressure, injectors, insufficient fuel and an EVAP purge issue also appear in the source. A fault detected at one cylinder can therefore coexist with a problem affecting the engine more broadly. Review the complete combustion-fault pattern before assuming the cause belongs exclusively to one cylinder's hardware.

## What would support a repair

First establish the engine's cylinder numbering from its repair information. Then document a result that meaningfully separates ignition, fuel delivery and compression for the complaint. This is an interpretation of the diagnostic branches, not a copied workshop procedure. Ross-Tech is an independent diagnostic-tool publisher; its P0312 page supplies no universal coil layout, injector access method or replacement instruction that applies to every twelve-cylinder engine.
