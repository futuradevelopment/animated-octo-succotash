# P06E5: glow-control performance can be judged by calculated ground offset

P06E5 is a glow-plug control-module performance fault. Audi's CPNB chart gives a specific criterion: calculated ground offset at or beyond positive 1.75 V, or at or beyond negative 1.75 V. Both directions matter in the documented evaluation.

## Performance does not mean one plug failed

The monitor concerns the control system's calculated ground reference. It is not an individual cylinder's resistance test and does not count defective glow plugs. As diagnostic interpretation, replacing a set of plugs without explaining the offset would not address the evidence this row actually supplies.

The word calculated is also important. The chart does not identify a universal pair of terminals where a hand-held meter must display exactly that number. A scan-derived calculation and a direct circuit measurement need the appropriate diagnostic context.

## Evaluate the reference with the correct procedure

The quick-reference book provides no connector pinout, ground-repair instruction or module replacement requirement here. Use the CPNB electrical procedure to relate the calculated finding to the real supply and ground paths. This explanation is limited to the 2014 Audi A6/S6 monitor; another manufacturer's module-performance code may use different criteria and cannot inherit these thresholds automatically.
