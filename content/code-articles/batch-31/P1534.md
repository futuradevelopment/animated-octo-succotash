# P1534: bank 2 N208 cam-adjuster short-to-positive evidence

P1534 is the short-to-positive fault for bank 2 camshaft adjustment valve N208 in Ross-Tech's VAG reference. The source associates it with poor running and an illuminated malfunction lamp. It names the wiring, the electrical adjuster and the engine controller as possible causes.

## An electrical label is not a timing measurement

Camshaft adjustment affects engine operation, but this particular fault describes the circuit. As diagnostic interpretation, poor running plus P1534 does not by itself prove a chain, belt or mechanical camshaft timing problem. The short-to-positive evidence needs its own explanation.

Bank and component identification matter before testing. The source specifies bank 2 and N208; it does not provide a universal physical location across engines. Establish those details from the vehicle's repair information rather than transferring a layout from another engine.

## Resistance limits must come from the correct procedure

Ross-Tech includes electrical checking but gives no universal resistance value on this page. A measurement without the proper specification cannot justify replacing the valve or controller. The useful finding separates a wiring defect from a component fault and supports the chosen repair. This article follows an independent diagnostic-tool publisher's guidance, not an OEM instruction to replace the engine controller whenever P1534 appears.
