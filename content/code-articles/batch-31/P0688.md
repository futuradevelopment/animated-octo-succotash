# P0688: J271 load-circuit failure can explain fan or no-start complaints

P0688 concerns an open load circuit for engine-control power relay J271 in Ross-Tech's VAG reference. The source includes fuse, relay and wiring problems. It also describes the relay's terminal 87 behavior, showing that the diagnosis concerns the power delivered through the circuit rather than the relay name alone.

## An unusual fan symptom can be relevant

Ross-Tech lists cooling fans running at full speed with a cold engine and ignition off, potentially discharging the battery. In the context of this fault, that symptom should not automatically be treated as proof of an overheating engine. The source ties it to the power-control investigation.

## A specific no-start example

For North American 2005–2007 Jetta vehicles with the 2.5-litre engine, the page highlights a damaged power wire near the engine-controller connector as a possible cause of no communication and no start. That is a bounded application note, not a universal harness location for P0688.

As an editorial diagnostic implication, confirm supply-path evidence before replacing a controller that cannot communicate. A relay or wire defect can leave the module unavailable without demonstrating internal module failure. This article does not reproduce pin probing instructions or supply a universal relay test; the actual vehicle wiring diagram and professional electrical procedure remain necessary.
