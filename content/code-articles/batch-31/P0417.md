# P0417: the CTUA valve-B fault points to a low electrical signal

P0417 carries a generic secondary-air switching-valve-B shorted-circuit label. Audi's CTUA chart describes an air-valve short to ground and uses signal voltage below 2 V as the criterion. This narrows the documented fault to electrical behavior rather than directly identifying an air-delivery deficit.

## Preserve the distinction between the two valve branches

The generic valve-B identifier is meaningful only after it has been mapped to the installed engine. As diagnostic reasoning, a healthy result at a different air-control valve would not clear this branch of suspicion. The quick-reference table does not provide that physical mapping or connector assignment.

The threshold is not a universal command voltage. It is the condition the listed monitor treats as abnormal, and its interpretation depends on the proper CTUA circuit and test state.

## A short indication still needs isolation

The row does not identify the failed wire or component, prescribe a replacement or supply a bypass method. Establish why the evaluated path has the low signal using the vehicle's electrical procedure. This scoped monitor explanation avoids treating the generic word shorted as proof of a particular valve failure or treating P0417 as interchangeable with a secondary-air flow code.
