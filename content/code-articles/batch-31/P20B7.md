# P20B7: the metering-unit heater's low-conductance start check

P20B7 describes the low control-circuit condition for the reductant metering-unit heater. Audi's CPNB table evaluates conductance at the start of heating and lists a value below 0.30 reciprocal ohm. The monitor's low label therefore should not automatically be interpreted as a published low-voltage limit.

## Use the right quantity and timing

Conductance and voltage are different quantities, and the source expressly uses inverse ohms. As diagnostic reasoning, a voltage reading alone cannot be compared numerically with this conductance threshold. The correct repair procedure is needed to relate the controller's evaluation to actual circuit measurements.

The start condition also differentiates this entry from the table's P20B5 open-circuit check during heating. These are separately defined observations even though both concern the metering-unit heater.

## The row does not locate the defect

Audi's quick-reference chart does not identify a particular connector, wire or failed heater element as the required repair. Establish the installed CPNB heater arrangement and why its electrical behavior falls outside the stated condition. This is an explanation of a documented monitor, not a universal resistance test or a parts-replacement instruction for other engines and manufacturers that also use P20B7.
