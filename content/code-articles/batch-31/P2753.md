# P2753: a cooler-valve circuit fault can involve coolant migration into wiring

P2753 identifies an open transmission-fluid cooler control circuit in Ross-Tech's VAG reference. Depending on the application, the relevant valve may be N82, N509 or N488. Wiring and power supply are also possible causes. The source includes reduced cabin heat and, in some cases, stalling, no-start or lost module communication.

## The electrical damage can extend beyond the valve

Ross-Tech highlights coolant migration through an N82 connector and into wiring. As diagnostic interpretation, finding coolant-related damage at the valve is a reason to assess the connected harness rather than assuming that replacing the valve resolves every electrical consequence. The extent must be established on the vehicle.

## Campaign references need identification checks

The page cites particular Audi service campaigns and technical information, including different North American and rest-of-world applications. These historical references do not establish current recall eligibility or one valve fitment for every model. Check the actual vehicle identification and applicable service information.

This independent diagnostic source supplies no universal connector pinout or required harness replacement. A useful repair assessment identifies the installed valve, explains the circuit fault and documents any migration damage that supports further work. A transmission-related code title alone does not prove an internal gearbox failure.
