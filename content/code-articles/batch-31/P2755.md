# P2755: high cooler-control circuit feedback may accompany widespread harness damage

P2755 is the high-circuit fault for transmission-fluid cooler control in Ross-Tech's VAG reference. The source names N82, N509 or N488 depending on application, together with wiring and power supply. It also explains why symptoms can extend beyond transmission cooling when coolant enters the electrical harness.

## Companion engine faults can be relevant

Ross-Tech describes an Audi A8L example in which N82-related harness damage accompanied P2755 in the transmission controller, intermittent rough running, stalling and rich fuel-trim faults. This is an example of connected damage, not a universal causal rule for rich-mixture codes.

The page advises considering corroded connections at engine, transmission and climate-control modules where applicable. As diagnostic interpretation, the scope of repair should follow actual contamination evidence instead of ending automatically at the first failed valve.

## Establish vehicle-specific applicability

The reference includes service campaigns and bulletins covering different models and markets. It does not establish current campaign eligibility, a common harness layout or a requirement to replace all modules. Confirm the installed valve and the relevant manufacturer's guidance. This independent source supports a wider electrical assessment when coolant migration is found; the high-circuit label alone does not prove internal transmission damage or justify a complete harness replacement.
