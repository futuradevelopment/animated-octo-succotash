# P0322: missing G28 speed signal and the connected wiring path

P0322 is the no-signal fault for engine-speed sensor G28 in Ross-Tech's VAG reference. The page associates it with stalling, a no-start, an inoperative tachometer and a flashing glow-plug indicator. These are possible effects of missing speed information, not guarantees that every listed symptom will occur.

## No signal is the important finding

G28 provides the crankshaft reference used alongside camshaft information. The source names a loose or faulty sensor as possible causes. A useful diagnostic conclusion should therefore explain why the controller is not receiving usable G28 information rather than infer failure from an unrelated dashboard symptom.

## Contamination may extend along the harness

Ross-Tech notes oil or coolant migration through wiring toward the engine control module, with a Tiguan example. As editorial diagnostic reasoning, evidence of fluid at a connector warrants consideration of the connected path rather than assuming the sensor housing is the complete problem. The example does not establish a universal Tiguan defect or require replacing the module.

The reference supplies no model-independent connector pinout, waveform specification or sensor mounting procedure. Confirm the actual engine and circuit before testing. This article describes Ross-Tech's independent VAG interpretation of the missing-signal fault; the applicable manufacturer's repair information determines how that evidence is measured and which repair is justified.
