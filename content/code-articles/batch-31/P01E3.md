# P01E3: Audi's temperature-control sensor circuit uses a high-voltage criterion

P01E3 is cataloged as engine-coolant temperature sensor 3's circuit. Audi's CPNB diagnostic chart calls the relevant device the engine-temperature-control sensor and describes a short to battery voltage. Its detection criterion is a signal above 4.92 V. The naming difference should be resolved using the installed engine's repair information.

## Electrical high is the supported observation

The source supplies a signal-voltage criterion, not a coolant-temperature limit. As diagnostic interpretation, this row cannot be used to claim that the engine overheated or that coolant temperature exceeded a particular value. The controller is reporting an electrical condition affecting its temperature information.

A high signal also does not identify the precise physical defect without the circuit test. The row is a monitor description, not proof that the sensor itself is internally shorted.

## Identify the component before selecting a repair

The reference does not supply a connector pinout or a diagram linking the generic sensor number to a particular housing. That uncertainty is retained here rather than silently equating names. Match the complete fault text, engine and circuit before applying the threshold. The numeric criterion belongs to the CPNB section of Audi's 2014 A6/S6 book and does not establish a universal coolant-sensor replacement rule.
