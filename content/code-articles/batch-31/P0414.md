# P0414: Audi's air-valve fault is evaluated as a ground-short voltage

P0414 generally identifies a shorted secondary-air switching-valve-A circuit. Audi's CTUA chart gives a more specific interpretation: an air valve short to ground, with signal voltage below 2 V. That electrical criterion should be preserved rather than reduced to a general statement that secondary-air flow is inadequate.

## A low signal is not a pressure reading

The published number is a voltage. As diagnostic reasoning, neither low air pressure nor a quiet pump is equivalent evidence for this row. The relevant circuit behavior must be assessed with the correct wiring information and test conditions.

The row does not specify which physical part caused the low signal. A grounded path and a faulty controlled component require different repair evidence, even if either prevents normal valve operation.

## Do not borrow another engine's limits

The book contains multiple engine sections, so this two-volt criterion is scoped to CTUA. The generic valve-A name also needs to be matched to the actual engine's component layout. Audi's quick-reference row provides no connector pinout or full short-circuit isolation procedure. A supported repair should explain the low-voltage condition on the relevant air-valve path rather than assume the entire secondary-air assembly has failed.
