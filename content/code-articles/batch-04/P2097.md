# P2097: Post-catalyst fuel correction reaches the rich limit

P2097 reports excessive rich indication in bank 1 post-catalyst fuel feedback. In the reviewed GM strategy, the controller responds by reducing its integral and proportional offsets, applying a lean bias to fuel control. The fault describes correction authority reaching its lower region, not a direct fuel-pressure measurement.

## What counts as a failure

The displayed calibration requires average integral authority at or below minus 90% and average total authority at or below minus 45%. Total authority combines the integral and proportional contributions. These percentages belong to this secondary control strategy; interpreting them as ordinary upstream fuel-trim percentages would change their meaning.

Samples occur every 100 milliseconds, with an average evaluated every 50 seconds. A brief rich transition and a persistent correction-limit problem are therefore different observations.

## Interpret purge and operating state

The row refers to P2096 for the operating conditions. That companion description permits cruise and acceleration, while excluding idle and deceleration. Evaluate a completion or recurrence in that context instead of treating any engine-running interval as a valid test.

High vapor loading is another explicit complication. Purge-open vapor of at least 18% for five seconds can suspend the diagnosis; purge closure or vapor at or below 14% for five seconds permits it to resume. A comparison made before and after that change may reflect diagnostic availability.

The source specifies Type B, two-trip behavior. Verification should show that the enabled post-catalyst correction monitor passes after the underlying cause is addressed. The table does not establish a replacement part or prove catalyst damage.

## Source and applicability

This explanation is limited to the documented 2025 GM monitor; its thresholds are not universal vehicle specifications.
