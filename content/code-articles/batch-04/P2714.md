# P2714: CVT forward-clutch pressure control fails hydraulically off

P2714 describes a hydraulically failed-off forward-clutch pressure-control actuator in the reviewed GM CVT-specific row. The generic solenoid-D name covers broader applications, but this article's behavior and limits come from the CVT input-clutch monitor only.

## Two operating situations can reveal the problem

The controller can detect excessive attained-gear slip during a forward engagement, termed a garage shift in the source. One documented condition uses slip above 500 rpm, with failure time and repeated-event counting.

It also evaluates loss of power flow after steady forward operation has been attained, including torque-instability evidence. A fault appearing during initial engagement therefore need not have exactly the same recorded conditions as one appearing after steady travel. The summary contains additional torque/slip labels whose units are not sufficiently clear to turn into universal service thresholds.

## Hydraulic performance is separated from electrical validity

Related solenoid circuit faults P2718, P2720 and P2721 inhibit the documented performance diagnosis. Speed-sensor and other control faults also affect eligibility, and service fast-learn or solenoid-cleaning activity is excluded.

A passed circuit check does not demonstrate that clutch pressure produced the intended hydraulic result. Conversely, an active electrical fault can prevent a valid hydraulic-performance evaluation.

Verification should identify the applicable CVT and failing operating phase, then confirm normal engagement or steady-state power transfer under the manufacturer's procedure. The source does not establish that the solenoid alone, the clutch or the complete transmission requires replacement. It lists Type A, one-trip behavior.

## Source and applicability

These limits describe the stated monitor and require vehicle, hardware and calibration confirmation.
