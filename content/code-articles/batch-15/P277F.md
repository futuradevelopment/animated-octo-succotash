# P277F: Even-side gear loss requires more than a neutral indication

P277F is identified by the source’s heading and criteria as unexpected loss of the driving gear on the even-clutch shaft. The controller detects neutral-like synchronizer speed behavior while even-clutch slip stays below 50 rpm. This supports a gear-disengagement classification rather than ordinary clutch slip.

## The operating state must agree

The selected driving gear must belong to the even shaft and agree with its logical engaged gear. No even-side synchronizer shift may be active. The failure interval is one second. These prerequisites prevent an intentional shift transition from being treated as the same failure pattern.

One narrative sentence in the P277F row says “odd clutch,” despite the even-side heading and criteria. This apparent copied-text error remains flagged; the article does not conceal it or infer a universal clutch-letter mapping.

## Verification evidence

Keep the gear command, logical engagement, speed-derived neutral indication and even-clutch slip together. A standalone neutral display or a later normal engagement does not explain the original event. The source establishes a controller inference about lost engagement, not the particular fork, valve or internal mechanical part requiring repair. Applicable service information must resolve the exact transmission configuration and diagnostic procedure.
