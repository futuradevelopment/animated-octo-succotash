# P277E: Odd-side gear loss is detected while the clutch is not slipping

In the cited dual-clutch transmission strategy, P277E identifies loss of the driving gear on the odd-clutch side. The controller sees speed evidence corresponding to neutral at the synchronizer even though the odd clutch itself is not slipping significantly. That combination helps separate a disengaged gear from a simple clutch-slip event.

## Conditions that support the classification

Odd-clutch slip must be below 50 rpm. A driving gear must be selected, it must correspond to the odd shaft, and it must match the logical engaged gear. The synchronizer must not be busy shifting. The failure confirmation interval is one second.

These gates are important: speed disagreement during an intentional gear change is not automatically the unexpected-disengagement result described by this monitor.

## Preserve the event sequence

Useful diagnostic evidence includes the commanded driving gear, logical gear, synchronizer speed interpretation and clutch slip at the same time. A later display showing neutral does not establish which condition occurred first. The monitor infers mechanical disengagement from the combined operating evidence; it does not identify a particular fork, synchronizer or hydraulic valve as the failed part. Follow the applicable transmission service procedure before choosing a repair or attempting further loaded operation.
