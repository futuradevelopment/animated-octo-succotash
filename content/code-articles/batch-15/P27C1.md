# P27C1: Valve three stuck-off needs a second path to validate rod feedback

P27C1 corresponds to shift solenoid three hydraulically stuck off in this controller. Its integrity routine makes small test movements on rods three and four with selected hydraulic routing. The purpose is to assess actuation capability, not to perform a complete gear engagement.

## No movement is only the first finding

The relevant valve-three test cases show movement no greater than 100 micrometres. That makes ineffective hydraulic actuation suspicious, but could also resemble position feedback stuck at a value. The continuation uses valve four to produce movement above 100 micrometres on one of those rods, validating that the feedback can respond.

The alternate path is therefore part of the diagnosis, not an optional extra observation. A single flat position trace cannot establish the complete stuck-off result.

## Confirm the routine’s completion

The integrity sequence is invoked by recovery logic or a startup mismatch between retained gear state and sensed engagement. Verification needs evidence that its relevant cases actually executed. A drive without the routine is not equivalent to a completed test suite. The source supports a specific hydraulic classification but does not supply a safe generic method for commanding intrusive rod movements or isolating a replacement solenoid.
