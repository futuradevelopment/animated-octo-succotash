# P283E: Rod-three high is a waveform-quality fault on the VREF2 group

P283E is the electrical-high monitor for rod-three position. It checks for missing pulses while the pin exceeds 3.5 V, or duty cycle above 92%, with at least 150 ms confirmation. The high designation describes the digital input, not a high mechanical fork position.

## The continuation identifies the supply group

The row begins on page 913 and its inhibit list continues on page 914. P0652/P0653 identify the VREF2-related supply context, alongside controller-voltage faults P0882/P0883. Reading only the first page would miss that distinction.

The printed VREF2 enable limits conflict, requiring above 5.5 V and below 4.5 V. This article keeps those limits unresolved rather than inventing a corrected range.

## Different waveform failures need different evidence

A pulse-free high level and a still-pulsing signal with excessive duty are distinct paths to the same code. Preserve pulse activity, duty cycle and supply status together. A converted position reading or a single high-voltage sample cannot show which route failed. Confirm the vehicle calibration and service test method before evaluating the input. The summary does not isolate the sensor or another circuit component as the proven repair.
