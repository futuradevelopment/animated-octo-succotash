# P2855: Clutch-one charge performance includes excess pressure near the hold state

The cited P2855 monitor identifies clutch-one pressure above the modeled expectation. Besides general positive pressure-error branches, the source includes a check around hold pressure, the control region used while the clutch is not intended to transmit torque.

## The hold branch supplies a concrete distinction

The target must be within about 0.051 bar of hold pressure. Measured pressure exceeding the model by about 1.199 bar for at least 300 ms then meets the listed hold-state failure. These values belong to this calibration’s monitor and are not a universal clutch adjustment specification.

General branches use other model-difference thresholds and confirmation times referenced in the source attachments. They should not be replaced with the hold-branch numbers.

## Eligibility makes the comparison meaningful

The controller excludes relevant electrical faults, recovery and adaptive overrides. The redundant shutdown valve must be hydraulically on; superfill must be inactive and ended more than 80 ms earlier. Preserve these states with measured and modeled pressure when verifying the event.

A pressure value alone cannot show whether the clutch was in the hold branch or another control state. This code establishes excess pressure relative to expected behavior, not the exact failed valve, sensor or mechanical component.
