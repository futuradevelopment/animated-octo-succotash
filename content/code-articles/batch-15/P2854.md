# P2854: Clutch-two low pressure must be compared with what the model expects

P2854 corresponds to clutch-two pressure control too low in this source. The clearest branch combines a modeled pressure above 1 bar with measured pressure below 0.5 bar for at least five seconds. A low reading without the model’s expectation is not the full diagnostic condition.

## Control transitions are excluded

The redundant shutdown valve must be hydraulically on. The controller excludes relevant electrical faults, recovery requests and adaptive overrides of valve current, fast filling or flow influence. Superfill must be inactive, with more than 80 ms since it ended. These gates prevent deliberate special control phases from being interpreted as ordinary pressure-tracking failures.

Another branch rejects an excessive absolute pressure-model difference when the measured value is lower. Its precise difference and time calibrations are referenced by the source rather than guessed here.

## Retain model and sensor together

Verification should preserve pressure, modeled pressure, elapsed fault time and the qualifying clutch-two state. A later normal pressure alone cannot identify which branch originally failed. The generic discharge-performance name is narrower here than a complete hydraulic diagnosis: it reports low control performance but does not prove a particular leakage path, valve defect or clutch repair.
