# P27C5: An open valve-three circuit is evaluated before hydraulic conclusions

In this transmission, P27C5 is the electrical open-load diagnostic for synchronizer actuation valve three. The driver checks its monitored node with the low-side output off. A connected load should pull that node toward supply; the diagnostic’s ability to regulate it low with only a small current supports the open-load classification.

## The off interval is essential

The table requires more than 5 ms since the output was switched off and at least 150 ms of fault confirmation. The controller must not be in safe state. Battery-related DTCs P0882 and P0883 disable this check, while cranking and stop-key states are outside its normal enable window.

That logic means a valve failing to move a rod is insufficient evidence for P27C5. The hydraulic result and the off-state electrical path are separate measurements.

## Useful follow-up evidence

Record the driver’s open-load indication with its command state and supply voltage. A successful energized actuation may be relevant but does not automatically repeat the diagnostic off interval. Identify the actual valve-three circuit through calibration-specific wiring information. The generic solenoid-P label alone cannot locate a terminal or tell whether a coil, connector, harness or output stage caused the reported opening.
