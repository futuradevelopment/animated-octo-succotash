# P27D1: Valve five needs current-profile evidence because no alternate path exists

P27D1 is shift solenoid five hydraulically stuck off in this GM transmission. The integrity routine commands small movements of rod five with opposite selector states. Unlike the valve-three and valve-four arrangement, the source explicitly says there is no redundant valve-and-rod path available to validate this result.

## Why a flat position trace is insufficient

The listed motion criteria remain at or below 100 micrometres during the relevant cases. That can make the hydraulic path suspicious, but a position sensor stuck at a value can look similar. The controller therefore uses the valve-five current-profile check to separate the possibilities.

The source requires three failed profile results and transmission oil temperature of at least 40°C. These corroborating conditions are central to the diagnosis, not optional refinements after an assumed mechanical failure.

## A complete verification record

Retain the executed integrity cases, rod-five response, oil temperature and current-profile outcomes. Lack of code return without those tests does not demonstrate a passed diagnosis. The routine uses small test movements rather than complete gear engagement. This summary explains the specific evidence combination, but the applicable transmission service procedure is required before intrusive testing or component replacement.
