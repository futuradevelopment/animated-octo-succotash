# P2632: Tank-transfer pump open-load reporting depends on command state

The P2632 row in this GM source concerns the tank-transfer pump, narrowing the generic “fuel pump B” description. The engine controller evaluates an open-load diagnostic signal supplied by the fuel tank zone module. That is a reported electrical classification, rather than a direct fuel-pressure measurement.

## Read both diagnostic states

The source accepts one diagnostic-state value directly and another in conjunction with the transfer pump being inactive. It also lists a module crank-voltage condition below 14.52 V, relevant inhibit conditions and a 500 ms interval. The relationship with pump activation matters when comparing a stored event with present data.

An inactive pump does not make all open-load information meaningless: this monitor explicitly includes an inactive-state branch. Conversely, the DTC does not prove that fuel failed to transfer during a measured pressure or volume test.

## Verification focus

Capture the tank module’s open-load report together with the pump command and module voltage. A command-only observation cannot show whether the electrical diagnostic agreed. Identify the actual transfer circuit from vehicle-specific documentation before locating hardware. The summary does not establish whether the open path lies inside the pump, its wiring or the controlling electronics.
