# P2777: Upshift paddle circuit high is a timed electrical limit

P2777 is the electrical-high check on the upshift, or TAPUP, paddle input in this source. The row specifies voltage above 4,774 mV, 30 mV hysteresis and a failure interval of at least 150 ms. The controller is rejecting the electrical value rather than simply detecting a pressed switch.

## Separate electrical and logical faults

A prolonged valid press belongs to a different monitor. P2777 does not become a stuck-paddle diagnosis merely because the driver was using the paddle when it appeared. Its relevant evidence is the input crossing the high electrical boundary for the specified interval.

Monitoring requires battery voltage above 8.75 V and below 18 V, outside cranking and stop-key states. P0882 or P0883 disables the check, linking interpretation to the controller’s power conditions.

## A useful verification capture

Retain raw TAPUP input voltage and supply information during both release and operation. A normal switch-state label displayed later does not exclude an intermittent high electrical event. The 30 mV hysteresis belongs to the controller’s threshold handling and should not be confused with a component tolerance. Use applicable wiring information for isolation; this code alone does not prove that the paddle assembly is the failed part.
