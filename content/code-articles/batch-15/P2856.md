# P2856: Clutch-two pressure charge performance depends on its control state

P2856 is clutch-two pressure control too high in this GM transmission. The controller compares the sensor reading with modeled pressure and can diagnose excessive positive error through several branches. The generic charge-performance title does not mean every high pressure reading is abnormal regardless of the command.

## Hold pressure has its own test

When the target is within approximately 0.051 bar of the hold-pressure value, the source flags measured-minus-modeled pressure above approximately 1.199 bar for 300 ms. Hold is the region intended for a clutch not transmitting torque. Other positive-error branches use different calibration-dependent thresholds and timing.

## Exclude special control phases

Relevant sensor and valve electrical faults must be absent, and the redundant shutdown valve must be hydraulically on. Recovery and adaptive overrides are excluded. Superfill must be inactive and finished more than 80 ms earlier. Those conditions are essential when comparing a stored event with a subsequent test.

Record the actual control state, model output and pressure together, rather than treating one pressure number as a diagnosis. The source explains why the controller judged pressure excessive but does not determine whether the underlying cause is sensing, valve control or another hydraulic element. Applicable service information is required for component isolation.
