# P2835: Rod-one intermittent code checks digital frequency, not just dropouts

The P2835 implementation in this source is a frequency check on the rod-one position signal. It rejects a digital input below 1,500 Hz or above 2,500 Hz after the listed 150 ms interval. Although the generic title says intermittent, the documented criterion is more specific than “the connection sometimes disappears.”

## A changing position is not the measured quantity

Signal frequency and reported rod position are different pieces of information. A position value that looks steady cannot prove the digital carrier is within range. Likewise, the presence of some pulses does not establish that their frequency is acceptable.

The table’s VREF1 enable entries are internally incompatible: above 5.5 V and below 4.5 V. The original rendering confirms the wording, so this article does not silently replace it with an assumed normal window. P0642/P0643 and P0882/P0883 are listed as inhibits.

## Verification needs timing information

Preserve actual frequency and fault duration with supply-diagnostic status. A simple continuity observation cannot reproduce the frequency calculation. Confirm the correct calibration and reference-supply conditions in applicable service information before applying the limits to a vehicle. The DTC does not by itself prove a mechanically damaged fork or identify the component responsible for the signal timing.
