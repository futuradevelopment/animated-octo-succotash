# P25C6: Brake-booster temperature low is an electrical channel-one finding

P25C6 concerns the first brake-booster temperature-sensor channel in the cited EBCM table. The monitor looks for signal voltage below 0.2 V and interprets that state as a short toward ground. A low circuit-voltage code does not establish that the physical booster temperature is low.

## Repetition is part of the criterion

The table requires the low reading on five consecutive occasions with ignition on. It lists a 0.600-second interval, continuous checking and two-trip reporting. A lone low sample, especially without its repetition history, is therefore not the whole documented fault condition.

The second temperature channel has a different low threshold in the same table. Do not borrow its limit when assessing channel one simply because both signals measure temperature.

## Verification boundaries

A useful service record preserves the channel identity, raw electrical value and whether the consecutive-failure condition returned. A converted temperature display can obscure the actual voltage classification. Diagnosis of the brake system requires the applicable service procedure; this summary does not identify an accessible terminal or prescribe probing a powered brake assembly. The DTC describes an electrical input failure and does not establish brake-system safety or identify a replacement component.

## Document scope and source

2025 GM 25OBDG07A Part 2 documented monitor only; its application-matrix filename join is unresolved. No model, engine or universal calibration applicability is inferred.
