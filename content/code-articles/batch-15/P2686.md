# P2686: Supply C high is checked after HSO7 is switched off

The P2686 implementation documented here concerns high-side output seven. During shutdown, the controller disables HSO7 and checks whether its voltage falls to the expected state. Remaining above 70% of battery voltage for at least 60 ms produces the listed shorted-switch finding.

## Startup reporting can describe an earlier event

The fault is stored at powerdown and read on the next startup. This makes the shutdown sequence the relevant event to preserve, even if the first visible indication arrives after the vehicle wakes again. A voltage recorded only during normal powered operation does not evaluate the same condition.

Battery change during the test must stay below 1.5 V, and the source disables this check when P0882 or P0883 is present. A disrupted supply can therefore prevent a valid follow-up test.

## Keep the failure classes separate

The neighboring P2684 open-load monitor uses a different threshold and a low-side load-path test. Do not combine those criteria into a single generic supply fault. Confirm HSO7’s exact assignment and the stored shutdown result through applicable service data. The summary supports the off-state high classification but does not independently identify the failed switch or external circuit element.
