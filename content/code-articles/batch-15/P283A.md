# P283A: Rod-two intermittent feedback is monitored through its frequency

P283A is the rod-two position-signal frequency diagnostic in this GM implementation. The table rejects frequency below 1,500 Hz or above 2,500 Hz for at least 150 ms. That is a concrete timing criterion behind the generic intermittent-circuit description.

## Pulse presence is not enough

A signal can contain pulses yet fail because they arrive too slowly or too quickly. Conversely, a changing mechanical position does not itself prove a frequency failure. The carrier timing and the converted position reading should be treated as separate diagnostic information.

VREF1 enable limits in the source are internally inconsistent: the table prints above 5.5 V and below 4.5 V. This article retains that limitation and does not invent a corrected interval. Supply-related inhibit codes are also listed.

## Useful follow-up data

Preserve measured frequency, the duration of its excursion and reference-supply diagnostic state. A continuity check alone cannot verify this monitor, and a stable displayed fork position does not establish that the input frequency stayed valid. Resolve exact supply eligibility and the permitted signal-test method through the vehicle’s service information. The DTC does not identify a mechanically damaged fork or a specific defective connection by itself.
