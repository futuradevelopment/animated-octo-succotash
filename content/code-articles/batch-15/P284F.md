# P284F: Rod-three drift uses even-side gear evidence and correction history

P284F is the rod-three unrequested-movement implementation in this transmission. Its direct drift check compares position with tolerance bands for the logical gear, but only after the gear agrees with speed-derived engagement. The source uses even-clutch and output-speed information for this rod.

## Valid feedback is a prerequisite

The controller excludes rod-three electrical and consistency faults, active synchronizer shifting and position-learning routines. No rod force must have been present for the required interval. These conditions matter because commanded movement or already-untrusted feedback should not be mistaken for the same mechanical drift finding.

The direct branch confirms an out-of-band condition over 500 ms. Separate routes track at least ten drift-correction activations or correction lasting more than five seconds. The corrective control begins at narrower limits than the direct test.

## What to retain after a correction

A position brought back toward target may no longer show the original excursion. Preserve the correction counter, active time, logical gear and corroborating speeds with the event. The summary does not provide universal position limits or prove that a fork, valve or other internal part is defective. Use the applicable transmission service information to distinguish the underlying cause.
