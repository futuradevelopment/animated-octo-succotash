# P2838: Rod-two electrical low is about pulse behavior, not fork travel

P2838 is the low electrical classification for rod-two position feedback. GM’s monitor describes no pulses while the pin is below 2 V, or a signal duty cycle below 8%. These are digital-input criteria, not a measurement that the fork has moved too far in one direction.

## One low sample does not establish the fault

A healthy pulse signal has low intervals. The missing-pulse branch needs an absence of transitions as well as a low state; the duty-cycle branch concerns the proportion of the repeating signal. The confirmation period is at least 150 ms.

The source prints incompatible VREF1 enables, above 5.5 V and below 4.5 V. This article does not silently swap them. Exact supply eligibility remains a review item, with P0642/P0643 and P0882/P0883 listed as inhibiting faults.

## Preserve the original signal evidence

Record pulse presence, duty cycle and reference-supply status rather than relying only on a converted rod position. The electrical-low finding should be separated from a mechanical movement failure. Resolve the actual calibration’s supply conditions and measurement method through applicable service documentation. The code alone does not identify a failed sensor, wire or controller input.
