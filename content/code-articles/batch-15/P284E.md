# P284E: Repeated rod-two corrections can explain an unrequested-movement code

P284E identifies rod-two drift in the cited controller. The direct check compares measured position with the allowable band for the logically engaged gear, using speed evidence to confirm that gear state. It requires no rod force and excludes position-sensor faults, active shifts and learning routines.

## A returned position does not erase the evidence

The controller also counts drift-correction activations. At least ten activations, or more than five seconds of active correction, can produce a fault. Correction is triggered at narrower limits than the direct wide-band test. A position that appears acceptable after correction may therefore coexist with a legitimate drift record.

The direct out-of-band route has a 500 ms confirmation period and requires the odd-clutch/output-speed context documented for this rod.

## Verification must include correction history

Preserve the correction count and duration alongside rod-two position and validated gear state. Looking only for a large final displacement can miss a problem the controller repeatedly counteracted. Do not invent the learned position limits from another transmission or rod. The source establishes a control-system drift finding; it does not identify the particular mechanical, hydraulic or sensing cause without the applicable vehicle service procedure.
