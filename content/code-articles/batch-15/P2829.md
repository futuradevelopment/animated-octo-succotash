# P2829: Selector pilot stuck-off is distinguished from a selector spool fault

P2829 corresponds to the selector pilot valve hydraulically stuck off in this transmission. The integrity routine commands the selector toward hydraulic on, but the rod response matches the opposite routing state. The intended rod moves no more than 100 micrometres while a complementary rod moves more than 100 micrometres.

## Routing evidence does not identify the pilot by itself

The controller then uses the pilot valve’s current-profile results to distinguish that valve from the downstream selector mechanism. Repeated profile failures support the pilot-valve classification. Cooler-out temperature must be at least 40°C, and the requested integrity routine must meet its operating conditions.

The continuation’s confirmation labels include a stuck-on wording under this stuck-off row. That source inconsistency is retained as a review issue; the article does not infer a different failure direction from the isolated label.

## Preserve the completed sequence

Useful evidence includes the selector target, intended and complementary rod responses, temperature and current-profile history. A failed shift alone does not contain that comparison. The source describes controlled small movements and does not provide a universal procedure for executing them manually. Apply the actual transmission service information before attributing the fault to a replaceable pilot valve.
