# P2315: Separating ignition circuit low from a combustion misfire

P2315 is an electrical low-side ignition-driver check in the cited GM file. Its trigger is a low signal while the driver is switched off, which the table interprets as a short toward ground. That evidence differs from a misfire counter: it describes the control circuit rather than proving whether combustion succeeded.

## The continuation page matters

The row begins on page 701 and continues on page 702. Together they require synchronized ignition, speed above 1,400 rpm and more than nine ignition-counter increments. Reading only the first page would miss part of the eligibility logic. Confirmation is listed as 0.4 seconds, with two-trip reporting.

A useful verification record therefore includes whether those firing events occurred and whether the suspect low reading belonged to an off interval. An observation made before the monitor becomes eligible is not a pass of this check.

## Do not guess the coil location

The generic definition calls the channel coil F; this source labels it coil E. That disagreement prevents a reliable cylinder mapping from this article. Obtain the correct vehicle circuit identity first. The supported next distinction is between an electrical low fault and a combustion complaint, not between speculative replacement parts.
