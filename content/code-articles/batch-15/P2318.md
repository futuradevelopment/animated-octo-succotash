# P2318: Understanding the coil G off-state low-voltage check

For P2318, both the generic label and this GM row identify ignition coil G. The monitor looks for a low voltage while the primary low-side driver is off. The source associates that condition with a short to ground, rather than with a low secondary spark-voltage measurement.

## Why a running capture matters

The documented check requires synchronized ignition, more than nine ignition-counter increments and engine speed above 1,400 rpm. Those prerequisites explain why an apparently quiet electrical signal at rest is not equivalent to a successful monitored firing sequence. The fault must satisfy a 0.4-second interval; the reporting designation is two-trip.

## Use the fault class to choose the question

The first question is whether the output remains low during the interval in which the controller expects its off-state behavior. A low value during a commanded on period addresses a different condition and cannot by itself verify P2318. Record the command and feedback on the same time basis.

Although the letter agrees here, the document does not supply a universal cylinder-number mapping. Resolve that mapping for the actual engine before touching components.
