# P1717: Ford shift-solenoid D lacks its expected current signature

P1717 selects shift solenoid D in Ford's inductive-signature functional monitor. The controller looks for the characteristic current dip and rise produced as the solenoid moves after an off-to-on command. This supplies a movement-related electrical observation, rather than simply confirming that the circuit conducts current.

## Distinguish the nearby solenoid D faults

P0765 is the source's electrical-driver fault for solenoid D, and P0766 is the mechanical functional entry. P1717 occupies the separate ISIG branch. A normal resistance result does not by itself establish that the expected transition signature occurred.

Ford lists ten solenoid events, positive drive torque and a 70–225°F transmission-fluid temperature window for the signature check. Verification needs the relevant events and conditions, not only time with the engine idling.

## Preserve command and response evidence

Record companion electrical or shift-performance faults and identify the actual transmission before using a service test. The monitor explains what signature was missing but does not locate the fault within the solenoid, connections or control hardware. It also does not prove that all hydraulic functions are satisfactory. Use the application-specific electrical and mechanical diagnostic procedures to resolve the complete shift complaint.
