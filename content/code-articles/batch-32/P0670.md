# P0670: Ford 7.3L glow-plug control-line fault

P0670 identifies the control line for the glow-plug control module in the documented California 7.3L diesel system. Ford checks the output driver's internal status for an open circuit or short. This is an electrical command-path decision, so the code alone does not identify a failed glow plug or measure the heating performance of every cylinder.

## Why the operating context matters

The control-line monitor runs continuously in a 25–50 millisecond background loop, with a typical detection duration under one second and no listed entry conditions. That makes its evidence different from a heating test that needs a prolonged cold-start command. A fault can therefore be recorded without proving that the glow plugs were energized for their full operating interval.

Ford describes glow-plug on-time as dependent on oil temperature, barometric pressure and battery voltage. A short command in warm conditions is not by itself evidence of this circuit fault.

## Choosing the next diagnostic branch

Keep the control circuit, the module's communication circuit and its individual glow-plug outputs separate when reviewing accompanying codes. P0683 concerns the diagnostic message path, whereas P0670 points to the driver status of the control line. Establish the correct California-system wiring and verify that command path through the applicable service procedure before condemning all glow plugs. The summary supplies a detection category, not a component replacement verdict.
