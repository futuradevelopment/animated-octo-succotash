# P1744: Ford torque-converter clutch fails its functional slip check

Ford lists P1744 as the mechanical functional torque-converter clutch check in its 2002 diesel summary. The monitor evaluates the relationship between converter input and output during lockup. It is separate from P0743, the electrical driver fault, and P1740, the inductive movement-signature test.

## Evaluate the commanded state

The documented on-state check needs steady throttle, positive drive torque, no shift in progress and transmission-fluid temperature between 70°F and 225°F. The source gives excessive slip above 100 rpm or, for some applications, a speed ratio below 0.93. It also describes an off-state low-slip check for certain applications. Those alternatives must not be combined into one universal calibration.

Ford lists five lockup events as the monitoring duration. A single brief clutch command or an idle test does not represent that complete evaluation.

## Interpreting the mismatch

Keep commanded clutch state and measured slip together. Excessive slip while commanded on and unexpectedly low slip while commanded off describe different behaviors, even though both concern clutch function. The summary does not isolate hydraulic, mechanical or electrical causes from P1744 alone. Use the correct transmission identification and service procedure to determine which calibration applies and to investigate the failed functional result before choosing a component repair.
