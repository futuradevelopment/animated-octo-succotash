# P1263: Ford cylinder 3 injector high-side to low-side short

P1263 names an electrical short between the high and low sides of cylinder 3's injector drive circuit in Ford's 2015 diesel summary. The conclusion comes from the driver integrated circuit's internal logic. It is more specific than a general cylinder contribution fault, while still stopping short of identifying the failed physical part.

## Why a combustion check is not enough

The monitor runs continuously with a 0.3-second typical evaluation duration and no listed sensor prerequisites. It can report a drive-circuit condition independently of a fuel-balance judgment. A cylinder performance result may add context, but it does not replace the electrical diagnosis named by P1263.

For cylinder 3, the same source separately lists P0203 for an open circuit and P02F0 for implausible response. Keep those distinctions when interpreting accompanying faults or selecting a service test.

## Bound the conclusion

The correct engine wiring and cylinder-location information are essential because the manual covers multiple diesel implementations. Diagnose the complete circuit with the specified equipment before choosing an injector or module repair. High-energy injector circuits require isolation precautions; the OBD monitor's short description supplies neither safe test points nor a powered probing procedure.
