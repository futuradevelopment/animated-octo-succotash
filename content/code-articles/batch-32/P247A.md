# P247A: Ford exhaust-temperature sensor 3 exceeds its upper range

P247A identifies exhaust-temperature sensor 3 outside the high side of its engineering range in Ford's 2015 diesel summary. The listed typical limit is 1,150°C. That number is a diagnostic range boundary, not an allowable exhaust operating target.

## Why the electrical codes are different

Sensor 3's circuit-low and circuit-high codes are P242C and P242D. P247A evaluates the temperature represented by the signal. Ford notes that the transfer function cannot support a separate low-side engineering threshold, which makes this particular out-of-range check high-side only.

The monitor is continuous, with key-on and a 9–16.25 volt battery window. Its typical evaluation takes two seconds. It should not be confused with a model-comparison check that waits for an extended soak and warm running conditions.

## Interpret before replacing

Retain sensor 3's reading alongside the other exhaust-temperature and circuit information. The monitor does not establish whether an extreme reading reflects actual system heat or a measurement problem. Use the vehicle-specific exhaust layout and diagnostic procedure to investigate that distinction. The source does not identify a failed DPF or SCR assembly from P247A alone, and physical work must account for hot exhaust surfaces.
