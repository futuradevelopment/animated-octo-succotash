# P1261: Ford cylinder 1 injector high-to-low-side short

P1261 is the cylinder 1 member of Ford's high-side-to-low-side injector short family. In the 2015 diesel OBD summary, the driver integrated circuit detects the short internally. The code identifies the affected cylinder circuit and the relationship between its two drive sides, rather than reporting a fuel-balance correction.

## Separate the electrical categories

Ford assigns P0201 to cylinder 1 open-circuit detection and P02EE to an implausible injector response. P1261 therefore should not be reduced to a generic injector failure. The particular short category determines which application-specific diagnostic branch is relevant.

The source lists continuous monitoring and a typical duration of 0.3 second for this family, with no prerequisite sensor list or monitor sequence. A prolonged road load is not part of the published circuit-fault criterion.

## What remains to be isolated

A cylinder identity is not proof of a defective injector body. The appropriate procedure must distinguish the injector, its electrical path and the driver circuitry. Confirm cylinder 1's physical identification from the correct engine manual, because this summary covers more than one diesel engine. Injector actuation uses specialized electrical hardware; use the prescribed isolation method and equipment rather than probing an energized circuit.
