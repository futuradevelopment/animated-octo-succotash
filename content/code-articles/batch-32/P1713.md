# P1713: Ford transmission temperature stays implausibly low

P1713 is the stuck-low transmission-fluid temperature functional fault in Ford's 2002 diesel summary. It is distinct from P0712 and P0713, the electrical open-or-short checks on the same input. A signal can remain within its voltage range yet fail to change as expected; that is the problem this functional monitor addresses.

## A trend test rather than a single reading

Ford lists a 600-second functional evaluation and a malfunction criterion of less than 6°F of temperature rise or fall after startup. The entry table includes a long engine-off soak, time running, time moving in gear under positive torque and vehicle speed. These conditions make the temperature history important. One cold reading immediately after startup is not enough to reproduce the fault decision.

The summary also notes substitution of engine-coolant temperature when the transmission-temperature input has malfunctioned. A diagnostic display or control behavior must therefore be interpreted with awareness of fallback operation.

## What the stored code supports

Retain the startup temperature and subsequent trend, together with sensor-circuit faults. The source's hot/cold entry table is formatted ambiguously, so it should not be converted into a universal coolant-temperature test recipe. Use the exact transmission calibration and service procedure to resolve the fault. P1713 alone does not establish overheated fluid, mechanical transmission damage or a particular replacement part.
