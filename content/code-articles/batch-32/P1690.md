# P1690: Ford F-Series wastegate driver detects an electrical fault

P1690 is a wastegate electrical fault in the F-Series section of Ford's 2002 7.3L diesel OBD summary. The actuator driver's status indicates an open or short. This is evidence about the electrical control path, rather than a direct measurement of boost-control accuracy.

## Why a normal pressure reading is not the whole test

The check runs continuously, has no listed enabling conditions and typically evaluates in less than one second. It does not require the loaded, higher-speed window used by the P1249 steady-state wastegate monitor. Consequently, a normal manifold pressure at idle does not explain away a driver-status fault.

P1249 compares actual pressure with desired pressure and needs more than six seconds of qualifying disagreement. Keeping these two monitors separate prevents a circuit diagnosis from being turned prematurely into a turbocharger performance diagnosis.

## Evidence for the electrical branch

Determine whether the driver fault is currently present, and retain its relationship to command changes or other electrical faults. Follow the applicable F-Series wiring and actuator-driver procedure to identify the affected path. The source does not name a failed solenoid, connector or PCM as the inevitable cause. Its F-Series-only label also matters: a shared Ford-group code listing is not evidence that this exact implementation applies to every Lincoln, Mercury or Ford engine.
