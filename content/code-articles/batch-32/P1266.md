# P1266: Ford cylinder 6 injector high-to-low-side circuit short

P1266 is the cylinder 6 high-side-to-low-side injector short code in Ford's 2015 diesel monitor table. Internal driver logic detects the electrical condition. Because the same manual covers both five-cylinder and eight-cylinder engines, this cylinder 6 entry must not be applied indiscriminately to every engine named on the document cover.

## What the detector measures

The monitor runs continuously and typically evaluates in 0.3 second. No good-sensor prerequisite or preceding test sequence is listed. That establishes a direct circuit-monitor context rather than a conclusion inferred from cylinder balance.

For cylinder 6, P0206 is the separate open-circuit category and P02F3 is the implausible-response category. These distinctions help preserve the diagnostic meaning when a scanner displays only a broad injector-fault description.

## Isolate the path before choosing a part

Use the actual engine's cylinder numbering and injector wiring to identify the circuit, then follow its prescribed electrical diagnostic method. The code does not show where the short occurs or prove that the injector itself is defective. Injector-control circuits use specialized, potentially hazardous actuation voltages; do not use a generic powered test or unverified pinout based on this summary.
