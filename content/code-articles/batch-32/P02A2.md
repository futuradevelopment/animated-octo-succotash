# P02A2: Ford cylinder 3 reaches maximum fuel trim

Ford's P02A2 entry means cylinder 3 fuel trim has reached the maximum limit. The controller is evaluating balancing correction, not simply checking injector continuity. A maximum correction is evidence that the allowed adjustment has been exhausted in that direction, but it is not a component-level diagnosis.

## The monitor's operating window

The 2015 summary requires completed FBC wheel learning, no regeneration and acceptable crankshaft-position sensor status. It lists a 500–3,000 rpm engine-speed range, 3.5–90 mg per stroke injection quantity and a typical 7.5-second evaluation. These constraints help distinguish a valid monitor event from a scan reading obtained during a different mode.

P02A3 is the minimum-limit counterpart for cylinder 3. Keep the maximum/minimum distinction in the repair record because it preserves the direction of the balancing result.

## What further evidence is needed

The source's internal limit criterion is not a flow-bench specification or an instruction to alter injector coding. Use the applicable cylinder-balance procedure to identify why the adjustment is required. Injector electrical faults, if present, provide a different kind of evidence and should be evaluated accordingly. Do not infer a failed injector from the trim-limit code alone.
