# P0487: Ford 6.0L EGR throttle actuator circuit fault

P0487 identifies the EGR throttle position control circuit in Ford's 2004 6.0L diesel summary. Although the page calls it a functional check, its stated failure criterion is actuator-driver status indicating an open or short. The actual criterion is more useful than assuming that functional necessarily means a mechanical movement test.

## Separate command-path and position faults

The adjacent P0488 check compares desired EGR throttle position with actual position. P0487 instead evaluates the electrical driver path. A valve-position mismatch and an actuator-driver electrical fault are related diagnostic possibilities, but they are not interchangeable evidence.

Ford lists continuous execution in an eight-millisecond task, a typical duration below one second and no entry conditions or prerequisite sensors. The driver check therefore does not require the particular temperature or load window of an EGR-flow assessment.

## Use the correct electrical procedure

Retain any position-sensor or EGR performance codes and establish which faults are current. Then follow the 6.0L application wiring and actuator-driver diagnostic path to distinguish the control circuit from the valve's actual movement. The OBD summary does not specify a failed component, connector pinout or universal replacement. An EGR-flow complaint alone cannot substitute for the open/short evidence named by P0487.
