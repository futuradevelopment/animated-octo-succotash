# P029F: Ford cylinder 2 fuel-balance correction is at its minimum

P029F is cylinder 2 fuel trim at the minimum limit in Ford's 2015 diesel summary. This is a controller adjustment-limit result. Its minimum direction distinguishes it from P029E, the maximum-limit result on the same cylinder. Neither wording should be treated as a direct electrical voltage diagnosis.

## What enables the result

Ford's balancing monitor runs with acceptable crankshaft-position status and completed FBC wheel learning. It excludes regeneration and specifies an injection-quantity window as well as engine speed between 500 and 3,000 rpm. The typical monitoring period is 7.5 seconds.

These conditions make an isolated correction reading from an unrelated operating mode insufficient to explain the stored fault. Preserve the conditions under which the correction reached its boundary, rather than only its later value after the engine has changed state.

## Avoid overinterpreting the adaptation

The published threshold uses an internal balance-limit value, not a universally applicable injector flow rate. The summary does not identify which mechanical or delivery fault produced the adjustment. Follow the vehicle's cylinder-balance diagnostic procedure, using the correct cylinder numbering, and distinguish any accompanying injector circuit faults from this functional correction result.

## Source and applicability
