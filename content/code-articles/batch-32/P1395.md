# P1395: Ford bank 1 glow-plug absolute-test voltage drop

P1395 identifies the bank 1 absolute glow-plug test in Ford's 1998 7.3L California system. It is not simply a second name for the continuously monitored bank 1 fault P1391. Ford gives this test its own timing and signal criterion.

## The extended heating interval matters

The listed enabling conditions are battery voltage between 11.5 and 14 volts, 100 percent glow-plug duty and on-time greater than 30.5 seconds. The monitor's duration is greater than 30 seconds. A warm start with a short heating command may therefore provide no meaningful opportunity to verify this particular test.

The fault threshold is expressed as a signal falling below 155 A/D counts during the absolute test. That is a digital measurement criterion; it must not be relabeled as 155 volts, millivolts or amperes. The source does not supply a conversion for an improvised external meter test.

## Keep conclusions at bank level

This relay-and-shunt system monitors each bank as a group. P1395 points to bank 1's absolute-test result, while P1396 identifies bank 2. It does not isolate one cylinder. Use the correct service procedure to evaluate the bank circuit and its measurement path, and verify the required on-time when checking a repair. High-current glow-plug testing requires the specified safeguards.
