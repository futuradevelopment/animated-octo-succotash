# P02A3: Ford cylinder 3 minimum fuel-trim limit

P02A3 identifies cylinder 3 at the minimum fuel-trim limit in Ford's diesel fuel-balancing monitor. The lower correction boundary is the relevant observation. It should not be translated into a claim that the cylinder has a measured rich mixture or that its injector is electrically shorted.

## A learned balance result

Ford requires FBC wheel learning to be complete before this evaluation. Crankshaft-position sensor status must also be acceptable, and regeneration is excluded. Engine speed and injection quantity must fall within the listed balancing window; the typical monitoring duration is 7.5 seconds.

Those prerequisites matter during verification. A code that has not returned after a brief operating period is not necessarily evidence that this specific balancing test has run.

## Use the direction without inventing a cause

P02A2 selects the maximum boundary for cylinder 3; P02A3 selects the minimum. Preserve that distinction along with the actual correction and operating state. The OBD summary supplies no universal mapping from a lower-limit result to a particular injector or mechanical failure. The correct engine's contribution and balance procedure is needed to isolate the cause, and its cylinder identification must be confirmed before physical testing.
