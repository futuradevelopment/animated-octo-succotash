# P1119: Ford 7.3L manifold air-temperature signal high

In Ford's 2002 7.3L diesel summary, P1119 is the manifold air-temperature circuit high fault, specifically labeled for F-Series. The catalog wording may say manifold absolute temperature; the source calls the sensor MAT. The important distinction is that the monitor evaluates electrical input voltage. It is not an alarm based on a stated maximum air temperature.

## The electrical boundary

Ford lists a malfunction threshold above 4.6 volts. The companion low-input test, P1118, uses a different boundary at the other end of the signal range. A high circuit reading therefore deserves an electrical interpretation before anyone attributes the code to genuinely hot intake air.

The check has no listed entry conditions, runs in a continuous 25–50 millisecond background task and typically evaluates in less than one second. There is no requirement here for a boost event, a particular road speed or a lengthy engine warm-up.

## Making the result useful

The diagnostic question is whether the MAT input remains outside its allowed electrical range under the applicable circuit test. Compare scan information with the actual F-Series wiring and prescribed sensor-circuit checks. A temperature display by itself cannot distinguish the sensor from a wiring or input-path problem. Do not transfer this Ford definition to another manufacturer's P1119, or extend the F-Series-only source statement to other Ford-group vehicles.
