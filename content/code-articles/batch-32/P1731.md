# P1731: 1994 Mustang misses the expected 1–2 shift rpm drop

For the documented 1994 Mustang automatic transmission, P1731 is a 1–2 shift error. The PCM commanded the shift but did not detect the expected engine-rpm drop. That is a functional observation of the shift result, not proof that shift solenoid 1 has an electrically open winding.

## The chart keeps internal causes in scope

Ford associates the row with SS1 or internal transmission parts and notes that stuck valves or damaged friction material can also produce shift errors. The actual selected gear can depend on failure mode and manual-lever position. Preserve the commanded shift and range when recording the complaint.

Pinpoint Test A expressly diagnoses the solenoid electrical system only. Passing its electrical checks therefore does not rule out the internal causes listed in the chart.

## Use the staged diagnostic path

The procedure evaluates connector condition and circuit integrity before using a transmission tester for solenoid operation and a commanded-shift drive test. Its branch after successful commanded upshifts directs attention toward the vehicle harness or PCM diagnostic information; persistent symptoms still require the relevant symptom diagnosis. Use the correct 3.8L or 5.0L wiring variant rather than transferring pins between them. P1731 alone does not justify a transmission overhaul or one specific solenoid replacement.
