# P1393: Ford glow-plug bank 2 fails its shunt-signal check

P1393 is the bank 2 glow-plug failure monitor in Ford's 1998 7.3L diesel California summary. The system reports bank behavior through shunt-monitoring circuits. Ford sets this fault when the bank 2 shunt signal is below 0.66 volt under the qualifying electrical conditions.

## Preserve the bank identity

P1391 uses the same type of check for bank 1. Treating the two as an undifferentiated glow-plug fault discards information that can focus the investigation. The summary does not provide a cylinder-position diagram here, so establish bank location from the correct engine service information before testing.

The check requires 100 percent glow-plug duty and battery voltage between 11.5 and 14 volts. A battery-voltage or command-state observation belongs with the shunt reading; without it, a low reading may not represent the enabled monitor.

## Distinguish the absolute test

P1396 is the separate absolute-voltage-drop check for bank 2 and requires a longer heating interval. P1393 should therefore not be assigned that test's timer or A/D-count criterion. Follow the bank 2 relay/shunt circuit procedure appropriate to this California system. The code alone neither identifies one defective plug nor establishes that every plug in the bank must be replaced. Use suitable equipment for the high-current circuit.
