# P02A7: Ford cylinder 4 fuel trim reaches minimum

Ford defines P02A7 as cylinder 4 fuel trim at the minimum limit. The code records an adaptation boundary in the fuel-balancing system. It is not a direct statement about injector voltage, pressure or the physical amount of fuel that leaked from a nozzle.

## Preserve the enabling state

The balancing evaluation requires completed FBC wheel learning and acceptable crankshaft-position information. It is performed outside regeneration, within the source's engine-speed and injection-quantity ranges, and has a typical duration of 7.5 seconds. These prerequisites should accompany any attempt to reproduce or verify the event.

A correction value viewed after the operating conditions have changed may not describe the stored minimum-limit event. Record both the cylinder identity and the lower-bound direction rather than only a general injector complaint.

## Differentiate related evidence

P02A6 is the maximum-limit companion for cylinder 4. Electrical injector faults belong to separate monitors and may change the diagnostic path if also present. The summary does not say that a minimum balancing correction proves one particular part is defective. Use application-specific contribution testing and correct cylinder numbering to determine the cause before deciding on an injector or engine repair.
