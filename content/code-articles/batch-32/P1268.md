# P1268: Ford cylinder 8 injector high-side to low-side short

P1268 is the cylinder 8 high-to-low-side injector short entry in Ford's 2015 diesel OBD summary. The injector driver detects the electrical fault through internal logic. It does not measure an imbalance between cylinder 8 and the other cylinders, despite the cylinder-specific label.

## The adjacent codes answer other questions

P0208 indicates an open circuit in the same cylinder's injector path, and P02F5 indicates implausible injector response. P1268 preserves the more specific relationship between the high and low drive sides. It should not be generalized into an instruction to replace an injector whenever any cylinder 8 fault appears.

Ford describes continuous monitoring, a typical 0.3-second evaluation and no listed sensor prerequisites for this circuit family. A fuel-pressure or balance test may be relevant elsewhere, but it cannot substitute for this electrical fault classification.

## Scope and service boundary

The manual covers five- and eight-cylinder diesel systems; the cylinder 8 row does not apply to a five-cylinder engine. Confirm the exact application, cylinder position and wiring before testing. The monitor supplies no connector pinout or safe energized test method. Use the proper high-voltage injector-control diagnostic procedure to distinguish the circuit, injector and driver hardware.
