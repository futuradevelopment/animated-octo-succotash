# P2703: Ford 5R110W direct clutch on/off performance fault

P2703 is the direct-clutch failed-on or failed-off entry in Ford's documented 5R110W functional monitor. Intermediate and output shaft-speed information allow the controller to evaluate whether clutch behavior matches the required gear state. The code therefore needs more interpretation than a generic friction-element label supplies.

## Commanded engagement changes the meaning

Ford detects failed-off behavior in its specified fifth or sixth gear states and shifts into them. Failed-on behavior is checked in other listed states where unintended engagement can be identified. These are internal transmission designations and must be matched to the actual service procedure.

The clutch is cycled and retested after a bad event. Three bad events are needed, which means one apparent successful shift does not necessarily establish that the complete monitor passed.

## Keep directional evidence

P0766 is the direct-clutch failed-off companion, and P0767 is failed-on. Those codes can clarify a P2703 event, while speed-sensor faults can affect the credibility of the functional comparison. The source does not isolate the control solenoid or an internal clutch component from P2703 alone. Follow the appropriate 5R110W functional diagnosis before choosing a repair.

## Source and applicability

Ford 2004 6.0L diesel OBD summary, revision August 7, 2003, 5R110W direct-clutch/SSD functional monitor; gear numbering is source-specific. Ford source, physical PDF page [41](https://www.fordservicecontent.com/ford_content/catalog/motorcraft/DOBDSM404.pdf#page=41).
