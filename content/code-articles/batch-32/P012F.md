# P012F: Ford coolant and oil temperatures disagree after a soak

P012F is engine coolant temperature and engine oil temperature correlation. In Ford's 2015 diesel summary, the controller evaluates the absolute difference between ECT and EOT after an extended engine-off period. The listed difference threshold is 15°C.

## A startup comparison, not a running heat limit

The monitor runs once per drive cycle. Its entry conditions include at least six hours with the engine off, intake-air temperature above the listed −7°C minimum and two seconds of engine running. The decision is immediate when those conditions exist.

These details explain why later hot-engine readings are not enough to reconstruct the event. Coolant and oil can have different temperatures during operation; this monitor relies on its specific startup context.

## Neither sensor is convicted alone

Ford requires acceptable ambient, intake-air, coolant and oil temperature circuit status. The correlation code identifies disagreement, not which of the two compared measurements is wrong. Preserve both temperatures and the soak history, then use the relevant sensor-plausibility procedure. Electrical high/low codes provide different evidence and should be retained. The summary does not support replacing a coolant sensor, oil sensor or thermostat solely from P012F.
