# P1391: Ford glow-plug bank 1 shunt signal is low

Ford's documented P1391 monitor evaluates bank 1 of the 1998 7.3L California glow-plug system. The PCM observes a bank shunt signal rather than identifying each glow plug individually. A bank 1 signal below 0.66 volt is the listed malfunction threshold.

## Test conditions change the meaning

The monitor requires battery voltage between 11.5 and 14 volts and a glow-plug duty cycle of 100 percent. A low shunt reading taken without those conditions does not recreate the controller's decision. Record the supply voltage and actual command when evaluating the event, especially if the heating interval was short.

This system has one monitoring path per bank. P1393 identifies the corresponding bank 2 check, while P1395 is a separate bank 1 absolute test with additional on-time requirements. Those distinctions prevent a general glow-plug description from losing the bank and test type.

## What the bank fault proves

P1391 establishes an inadequate monitored bank signal within the documented test window. It does not count failed plugs or select a particular cylinder. The applicable relay, shunt and bank-circuit diagnostic procedure is needed to isolate the problem. Because this is a high-current heating system, do not substitute an improvised jumper or bypass for the specified measurements and protections.
