# P1316: Ford PCM reports stored injector-driver-module faults

P1316 is a signpost to injector-driver-module diagnostics in Ford's 2002 7.3L diesel system. The PCM interrogates the IDM and evaluates an extension on the engine-feedback signal. Ford lists extensions of 300, 400 or 500 microseconds as indications that the IDM has stored codes. The resulting PCM code does not identify a particular injector by itself.

## Follow the information to its source

The practical next question is which detailed faults the IDM has recorded. Replacing one injector from P1316 alone would skip the information that this code is specifically designed to announce. Use a diagnostic method capable of obtaining the applicable IDM fault information, and preserve those results alongside the PCM record.

Ford describes a continuous check, no listed entry conditions and typical evaluation in less than one second. Its existence is therefore separate from a lengthy cylinder performance test or a particular engine-load condition.

## Different from a missing response

P1670 is the adjacent engine-feedback fault for a commanded IDM response that is not received during engine running. P1316 instead describes a response indicating stored faults. That difference separates a reported internal diagnostic result from a missing feedback exchange. The summary provides neither a complete IDM repair procedure nor authorization to probe injector-driver power circuits; use the vehicle-specific service information and appropriate electrical equipment.
