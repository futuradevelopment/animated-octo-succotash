# P1716: Ford shift-solenoid C movement signature is missing

Ford's P1716 monitor evaluates the inductive current signature of shift solenoid C. During an off-to-on transition, the hardware expects the current dip and rise associated with solenoid movement. Failure to detect that pattern is distinct from a basic electrical open/short or an achieved-gear error.

## Three tests for one solenoid

The 1997 gasoline summary lists P0760 for solenoid C's electrical check, P1716 for the ISIG functional check and P0761 for mechanical function. Keeping those labels separate helps a technician choose the correct evidence instead of treating every solenoid C code as a continuity problem.

The ISIG evaluation spans ten solenoid events. The listed window includes positive drive torque and transmission-fluid temperature between 70°F and 225°F. A stationary test without the required transitions cannot demonstrate completion of the same monitor.

## A missing signature is a bounded result

Use the actual transmission's electrical and functional diagnostic procedure to determine why the expected movement-related signal was absent. The summary does not prove a particular hydraulic fault or require a complete transmission replacement. Mechanical ratio observations may add useful context, but they do not replace the current-signature evidence underlying P1716.
