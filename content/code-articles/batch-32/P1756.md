# P1756: 1994 Mustang California shift-solenoid 2 functional failure

P1756 is shift-solenoid 2 functional failure in the California-only row of Ford's 1994 Mustang transmission chart. Ford describes the condition as mechanical or hydraulic failure of the solenoid function. It is distinct from P0755, the SS2 electrical circuit-failure entry.

## Retain operating behavior

The chart says improper gear selection depends on the failure mode and manual-lever position. The actual range, commanded shift and obtained gear therefore provide useful evidence beyond the generic solenoid label. P1756 does not itself identify a broken wire or establish that the solenoid winding is open.

Pinpoint Test A covers the solenoid electrical system only. It checks the harness and control paths, then uses a transmission tester to evaluate solenoid activation and commanded upshifting.

## An electrical pass has limits

The procedure's staged checks help distinguish a circuit concern from a functional transmission complaint, but they do not eliminate all hydraulic or internal causes. Use the correct 3.8L or 5.0L wiring variant and continue through the application-specific symptom diagnosis where required. The source does not support automatic replacement of SS2 or the whole transmission from P1756 alone. Preserve the California calibration limit when applying this historical example.
