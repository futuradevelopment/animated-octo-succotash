# P1264: Ford cylinder 4 injector high-to-low-side short detection

Ford defines P1264 as cylinder 4 high-to-low-side short in the documented injection-circuit monitor. The injector driver detects that condition internally. The word short refers to the electrical actuation path; it is not a statement that injection duration is too short or that the engine needs a fuel-trim adjustment.

## Circuit identity comes first

The source distinguishes this code from P0204, an open circuit, and P02F1, an implausible response for cylinder 4. These classifications direct different diagnostic questions even though each may be displayed under an injector heading.

All P1261–P1268 checks in this document run continuously, with a typical duration of 0.3 second and no prerequisite sensor list. The shared procedure is genuine, but P1264 selects cylinder 4's circuit within it.

## Preserve the electrical evidence

Record other cylinder and bank-driver codes before clearing the event. Then use the exact engine's wiring and cylinder numbering to isolate the affected path through its service procedure. P1264 alone does not discriminate among an injector, its connections and the control hardware. Avoid interpreting successful engine running as proof that the circuit is healthy, and leave high-voltage injector measurements to appropriate equipment and trained service practice.
