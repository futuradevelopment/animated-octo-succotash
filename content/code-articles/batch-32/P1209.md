# P1209: Ford 7.3L commanded and actual ICP disagree

Ford's 2002 7.3L diesel summary calls P1209 an injection-control pressure system fault. The defining evidence is a difference between commanded and actual ICP greater than 12 MPa while the engine is running. The condition must persist for more than five seconds in the documented monitor.

## What the comparison does and does not say

This is a tracking-error test, not merely a declaration that pressure is low. The summary describes a difference without assigning P1209 exclusively to an underpressure direction. Preserve desired and actual pressure together so the direction and size of the disagreement remain visible. A single pressure reading loses the relationship that the controller actually evaluated.

The check requires acceptable ICP sensor status. Ford lists separate electrical out-of-range faults P1280 and P1281; a pressure number generated from a defective input should not be treated as an independent hydraulic measurement.

## Why nearby codes matter

P1210 evaluates unexpectedly high pressure with the engine off, while P1282 evaluates excessive actual pressure during running. Those are different questions from P1209's command-tracking error. Use that distinction to select the applicable diagnostic branch and operating conditions. The OBD summary does not isolate a pump, regulator or injector fault. Confirm the pressure-control diagnosis using the correct high-pressure service procedure rather than replacing a component from the system-fault label alone.
