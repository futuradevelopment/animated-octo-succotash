# P1742: Ford torque-converter clutch appears stuck on

Ford's 1997 gasoline summary assigns P1742 to torque-converter clutch mechanical function, stuck on. The useful diagnostic relationship is commanded release versus unexpectedly little converter slip. The source describes an off-state slip check below 20 rpm for some applications; that calibration must not be assumed universal.

## Command state is the essential evidence

Low slip is desirable when lockup is commanded, but it can indicate a problem when the clutch should be released. Preserve commanded TCC state and measured slip together. Without the command, a low slip number alone does not establish the stuck-on condition.

The same section distinguishes P0743 electrical-driver faults, P1740 movement-signature faults and P1744 mechanical functional faults. Ford lists five lockup events for the functional evaluation, so one brief observation may not establish a complete monitor result.

## Do not assign a cause from the behavior

A stuck-on functional result does not identify whether the underlying problem is electrical, hydraulic or mechanical. Use the actual transmission's service procedure and calibration to verify the clutch response. The summary's several application-dependent thresholds should not be combined into one universal road-test recipe or used to justify automatic converter or solenoid replacement.

## Source and applicability
