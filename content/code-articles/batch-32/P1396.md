# P1396: Ford bank 2 glow-plug absolute-test failure

Ford assigns P1396 to the bank 2 absolute glow-plug voltage-drop test on its documented 1998 7.3L California system. The controller evaluates a bank-level signal during a specific absolute test, rather than naming an individual failed glow plug.

## Do not confuse the two bank 2 checks

P1393 is the continuous bank 2 shunt-signal check. P1396 adds an extended operating window: glow-plug on-time above 30.5 seconds, full duty and battery voltage between 11.5 and 14 volts. Ford lists more than 30 seconds for the test duration. These conditions explain why merely cycling the key may not evaluate the same monitor after a repair.

The absolute-test failure boundary is below 155 A/D counts. That number belongs to the controller's digitized signal scale. Without a documented conversion, it is not a current specification or a voltage to reproduce with a test lead.

## A focused diagnostic conclusion

Retain the bank 2 identity, command duration and supply conditions when passing the fault to a technician. The proper diagnostic path must distinguish the bank's glow-plug load and its relay/shunt measurement circuit. The OBD summary does not declare that an entire bank of plugs is defective. The high-current nature of the circuit also makes a manual-specific test procedure essential.
