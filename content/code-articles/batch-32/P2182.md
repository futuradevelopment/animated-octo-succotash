# P2182: Ford secondary coolant temperature fails cold-soak plausibility

Although P2182 has a broad coolant-temperature sensor 2 circuit title, Ford's 2015 diesel example uses it for a secondary coolant-temperature rationality check. The controller compares ECT2 with several other temperature inputs after a long soak. It is therefore distinct from the dedicated electrical high- and low-input codes P2185 and P2184.

## The cold-soak comparison

The engine must have been off for at least six hours. Ford lists comparison thresholds of 20°C against ambient air, charge air, intake air and EGR-cooler outlet temperature. The check occurs once per drive cycle and has a typical duration of 0.5 second.

A block heater disables this check. That is a consequential exception: deliberately warming part of the engine changes the assumption that the sensors should agree after a soak.

## Interpret disagreement carefully

Preserve the startup readings together, rather than treating the ECT2 value in isolation. The monitor requires acceptable status from the supporting sensors, and disagreement does not automatically prove that ECT2 is the only inaccurate input. Use the application-specific comparison and sensor diagnostic procedure. The code alone does not establish a coolant leak or justify replacing a thermostat.
