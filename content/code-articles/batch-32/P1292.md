# P1292: Ford bank 2 injector high-side short detection

Ford's P1292 definition points to bank 2's injector high-side circuit. The monitored condition is a short to ground or battery recognized by the driver integrated circuit. This describes the electrical fault category and bank; it does not name a specific injector that must be replaced.

## Keep the numbering straight

P1291 is the corresponding bank 1 monitor. P1262, despite also ending in 2, refers to cylinder 2's high-side-to-low-side short in the same document. These are different diagnostic identities. Confirm the engine's wiring and bank assignment before translating the scan-tool label into a physical test location.

The monitor runs continuously and has a 0.2-second typical evaluation period. Ford lists no required good sensors or preceding monitor sequence. This is direct driver-circuit evidence, unlike a balance code inferred from engine-speed behavior.

## Use the associated fault pattern

Other bank and cylinder driver codes can help preserve the scope of the event, but the OBD summary does not provide a rule that identifies one failed component from their combination. Use the applicable electrical diagnostic procedure and appropriate equipment. Do not perform casual powered probing of injector circuits or substitute a resistance reading for the complete high-side fault investigation.
