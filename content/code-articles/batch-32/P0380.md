# P0380: Ford 7.3L glow-plug relay circuit check

P0380 is the glow-plug relay circuit fault in Ford's 1998 7.3L diesel California-system summary. The controller's internal driver checks for an open circuit, short to ground or short to power. This identifies an electrical control-path problem; it does not identify an individual glow plug or a particular bank's heating performance.

## Understand the system being monitored

This documented system uses a relay, shunt strips and one shunt-monitoring circuit per bank. The relay supplies glow-plug power from the battery, while the PCM determines on-time from oil temperature, barometric pressure and battery voltage. Ford describes normal commanded intervals ranging from one to 120 seconds.

Consequently, a short glow-plug interval in one operating condition is not by itself proof of P0380. The relay driver's fault status is the relevant evidence.

## Separate control and bank diagnostics

The P0380 check runs continuously with no listed entry conditions. Bank-monitor codes P1391 and P1393 use different voltage and duty-cycle conditions, so they should be interpreted as additional evidence rather than synonyms for this relay fault. Follow the correct California-system wiring and relay-control procedure to isolate the circuit problem. Glow-plug feeds carry substantial current; the source does not supply a safe bypass test, and replacing all plugs is not a conclusion supported by this code alone.
