# P0298: Ford 7.3L oil temperature that fails to fall

P0298 is commonly described as engine oil over temperature. Ford's 2002 7.3L diesel summary gives that label a more specific monitor context: it is a high-temperature rationality check that expects initially hot oil to fall below a calibrated temperature during the evaluation period. It should not be reduced to an instantaneous temperature switch.

## Read the starting conditions

The listed conditions include engine speed below 1,000 rpm, desired fuel quantity below 20 mg/s and initial oil temperature above 110°C. The fault criterion is failure to reach less than 110°C within the applicable period. Ford says the monitoring duration depends on engine oil temperature; this page does not provide one universal timer to use for every event.

The monitor also requires acceptable engine-oil and intake-air temperature sensor status. That matters because a temperature trend is only useful when its underlying inputs are credible. The adjacent P0197 and P0198 checks are separate electrical range faults.

## Interpreting a stored event

Preserve the initial temperature, engine speed and fueling context. A later normal temperature reading does not reconstruct the initial high-temperature, low-speed condition that enabled this check. Conversely, the code alone does not identify an oil cooler, thermostat or sensor as the failed part. Use the vehicle's cooling and temperature-sensor diagnostic procedures to distinguish actual retained heat from an implausible measurement.
