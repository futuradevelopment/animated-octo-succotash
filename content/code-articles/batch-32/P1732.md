# P1732: 1994 Mustang 2–3 shift lacks its expected rpm change

Ford's 1994 Mustang chart defines P1732 as a 2–3 shift error: the expected engine-rpm drop was not detected after the PCM commanded the shift. The row identifies SS1, SS2 or internal transmission parts as relevant possibilities. It does not isolate one solenoid from the code alone.

## Preserve the shift and selector context

Ford notes that actual gear selection depends on failure mode and manual-lever position. It also identifies internal concerns such as stuck valves or damaged friction material as possible sources of shift errors. These details distinguish P1732 from a purely electrical circuit fault.

The linked Pinpoint Test A is explicitly limited to the solenoid electrical system. Its successful completion cannot establish that every hydraulic or internal mechanical function is healthy.

## Electrical and functional checks have different roles

The procedure separates harness continuity and short checks from solenoid activation with a transmission tester, then evaluates whether the transmission upshifts when commanded by that equipment. That staged approach supplies evidence for choosing the next diagnostic branch. Follow the correct engine-specific wiring version and the applicable symptom diagnosis. A missing 2–3 rpm drop does not by itself establish that both solenoids, or the complete transmission, require replacement.
