# P1249: Ford wastegate control misses desired boost at steady state

Ford labels P1249 wastegate failure at steady state in its 2002 7.3L diesel summary. The monitor compares actual manifold absolute pressure with the desired value. A difference beyond the specified 15 kPa margin, lasting more than six seconds, is the relevant diagnostic evidence.

## This test needs a loaded operating window

The listed entry conditions require desired fuel quantity above 30 mg per stroke and engine speed above 1,750 rpm. A stationary idle check therefore does not reproduce the documented functional test. When reviewing a road-test record, retain the fueling request and engine speed along with both desired and actual pressure.

The page does not describe the margin as exclusively an underboost threshold. Avoid changing the definition into a claim that every P1249 proves insufficient boost. The direction of the pressure disagreement must come from the event data.

## Functional evidence versus electrical evidence

Ford separately lists P1690 for wastegate actuator-driver open or short status on F-Series. P1249 asks whether pressure control achieved its target, which is a broader system question. Neither code alone proves that the turbocharger assembly needs replacement. Diagnose the relevant air, control and measurement paths with the correct vehicle procedure; the OBD summary documents the controller's decision but does not provide a complete mechanical fault tree.
