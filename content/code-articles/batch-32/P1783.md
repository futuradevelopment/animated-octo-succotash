# P1783: Ford 6R140 transmission fluid exceeds its overtemperature limit

P1783 identifies transmission overtemperature in Ford's documented 6R140 system. The threshold is transmission-fluid temperature above 275°F for five seconds. Ford labels it a non-MIL fault, so absence of an engine warning lamp does not establish that no overtemperature event was recorded.

## Distinguish temperature from sensor plausibility

The same section separately lists P0712 and P0713 for electrical low/high inputs and P0711 for in-range temperature-sensor failures. P1783 asks whether the reported fluid temperature exceeded the overtemperature boundary. Sensor credibility and actual thermal conditions both matter when investigating that result.

For diesel applications, the external TCM processes and stores its own diagnostic information and freeze-frame data. Retrieving transmission-module information is therefore important; an engine-only code view may not preserve the full event.

## Preserve the thermal history

Record the temperature and operating circumstances before clearing the fault. The code alone does not identify a cooler, fluid condition or internal transmission component as the cause. Use the correct 6R140 cooling and temperature diagnostic procedure to establish why the limit was exceeded. The threshold is a fault criterion, not permission to keep operating at that temperature or a guarantee that damage has or has not occurred.

## Source and applicability

Ford 2015 diesel OBD summary, revision December 22, 2014, 6R140 rear-wheel-drive transmission section; this threshold is not generalized to other transmissions. Ford source, physical PDF page [176](https://www.fordservicecontent.com/ford_content/catalog/motorcraft/DOBDSM1501.pdf#page=176).
