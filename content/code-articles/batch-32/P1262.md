# P1262: Ford cylinder 2 injector drive-side short

Ford's documented P1262 monitor concerns cylinder 2's injector circuit. Internal driver logic reports a high-side-to-low-side short. This is a specific electrical relationship; it does not say that cylinder 2 has a lean mixture, insufficient compression or a measured mechanical injector leak.

## Read the exact fault family

P0202 is the source's cylinder 2 open-circuit code, and P02EF identifies implausible response. Those alternatives explain why the complete code matters more than a scan tool's abbreviated injector heading. The high/low sides in P1262 refer to the actuation circuit, not high or low fuel pressure.

The check runs continuously and typically evaluates in 0.3 second. Ford provides no sensor prerequisites for this group. Consequently, the electrical result does not depend on completing the cylinder fuel-balancing monitor.

## A focused next step

Preserve related bank-driver and cylinder codes, establish the correct engine's cylinder numbering, and apply the specified injector electrical fault tree. The monitor summary cannot locate the short within a connector, harness, injector or control module. Its short detection is not a safe instruction to bridge circuit terminals or apply external power; injector-driver testing requires suitable professional equipment.
