# P1747: Ford electronic pressure-control current is below command

The 2002 Ford diesel OBD summary identifies P1747 as an electronic pressure-control electrical fault. Its listed malfunction condition is current feedback lower than commanded current for five seconds. The immediate evidence is a discrepancy in the EPC control circuit, not a direct measurement of transmission line pressure.

## A useful distinction for diagnosis

A pressure-related code name can tempt a mechanical diagnosis. Here, however, Ford's specific criterion compares current command with current feedback. Preserve both values where the diagnostic system makes them available, and establish whether the electrical fault is present before interpreting any broader shifting or pressure complaint.

The page also contains mechanical functional entry conditions involving calculated gear ratio, fluid temperature and positive drive torque. Those entries should not be mistaken for a statement that the P1747 electrical criterion measures hydraulic pressure. The electrical check is listed as continuous.

## What this source cannot settle

The monitor does not provide a universal line-pressure specification or prove that the EPC solenoid itself is the failed component. A proper circuit diagnosis must distinguish the solenoid, its connections and the controller's command/feedback path using application-specific information. It also cannot establish a transmission overhaul requirement. After a repair, verify the relevant electrical comparison under the applicable procedure rather than relying only on whether a shift feels improved.
