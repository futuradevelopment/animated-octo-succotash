# U056B: Camera data arrives with invalid integrity or freshness

U056B is an invalid-data diagnostic for the front-view camera in GM's 2020 OBDG07 IMX6 object-controller table. Unlike a communication timeout, this monitor evaluates information that has arrived but fails its rolling-count or checksum checks. The generic image-processing sensor F label is resolved here only for the documented camera architecture.

## Corrupted and outdated are different from absent
The row supervises camera messages $350 through $356 on the front-object CAN network. Six failures out of ten establish the result; GM describes this as 0.36 second of failure within a 0.6-second window. Communication must be active, with bus voltage between nine and 16 volts.

The diagnostic must also be outside temporary reset states such as code clearing, diagnostic reenabling or end-of-trip processing. Those exclusions matter because deliberate resets can disturb counters without representing the same sustained operational problem.

## Preserve the integrity evidence
Determine whether the received information fails its checksum, its rolling-count freshness check, or both. Compare that with any separate message-loss evidence. Replacing a camera solely because its data was rejected would skip possible sending, transmission or receiving-path explanations that require actual diagnostic evidence.

The supported default action disables adaptive cruise control and/or Super Cruise. Verification should establish a stream of fresh, valid messages under enabled conditions after the cause is addressed; merely seeing network traffic is insufficient. The table provides no universal camera replacement or calibration instruction, and feature applicability must be confirmed for the exact vehicle.
