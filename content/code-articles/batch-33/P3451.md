# P3451: Cylinder-7 deactivation output remains grounded

P3451 monitors the low-circuit condition of cylinder-7 deactivation control in GM's 2015 OBDG08A table. The fault is an unwanted ground-side connection in the low-side output path. It must be interpreted with the driver state, because an intentional active low-side command also produces a low electrical state.

## The off-state comparison is decisive
GM evaluates this branch while the driver is off and describes an impedance no greater than 0.5 ohm toward controller ground. The required operating conditions include engine speed at least 400 rpm and powertrain relay voltage at least 11 volts. At least 20 failed samples out of 25 establish the result, with 250-millisecond sampling.

The distinction from P3449 matters: an open path and a grounded path have different electrical explanations even when both affect the same cylinder's deactivation control. A blanket “cylinder 7 solenoid fault” description loses that useful information.

## Determine whether release is possible
Identify the actual cylinder-7 output and evaluate whether its electrical state can change away from ground when the service procedure expects release. Separate a control-path fault from a mechanical actuator problem occurring despite correct electrical behavior. The DTC itself does not choose the failed component.

After the demonstrated cause is corrected, verification should show appropriate driver-off behavior and a completed monitor. Use application-specific electrical testing; the published impedance is not a procedure for measuring energized wiring. The source gives no terminal map or universal replacement instruction, and its GM calibration scope remains essential.
