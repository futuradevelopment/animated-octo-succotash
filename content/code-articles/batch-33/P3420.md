# P3420: Cylinder-3 deactivation control circuit high

P3420 checks the cylinder-3 deactivation solenoid control path for a short toward power in GM's 2015 OBDG08A unique-DTC table. The controller monitors its low-side output driver. A circuit-high result is not the same as a mechanical actuator stuck in a particular valve position.

## The active-command branch
GM evaluates this fault with the driver on and describes a very low impedance toward the power side. The monitor requires diagnostic enablement, powertrain relay voltage of at least 11 volts and engine speed of at least 400 rpm. It accumulates at least 20 errors out of 25 samples, checking every 250 milliseconds.

The command context distinguishes a fault from a supply-level reading that is normal in another state. The code's high branch also differs from the open-path and ground-short branches associated with the same cylinder. Preserve that direction when comparing scan information with an electrical measurement.

## Interpret the control path first
Confirm that the vehicle actually uses the documented cylinder-3 deactivation architecture and identify its output conductor from the matching diagram. Determine whether the controlled state follows the command before drawing conclusions about valve operation. The DTC does not independently identify the load, harness or driver electronics as defective.

Verification should establish restored command response and successful electrical diagnostic completion after the cause is addressed. The source provides no universal connector map or replacement instruction. Treat its impedance wording as a monitor definition rather than an instruction to test powered circuits, and retain the document's calibration limits on this generic-code interpretation.
