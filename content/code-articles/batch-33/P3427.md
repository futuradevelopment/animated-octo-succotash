# P3427: Cylinder-4 deactivation output is pulled to ground

P3427 detects the cylinder-4 deactivation control circuit shorted toward ground in GM's 2015 OBDG08A monitor. The electrical direction is important: this is the circuit-low branch, while separate codes address an open path and a supply-side short for the same cylinder.

## A low state is judged against an off command
The controller checks feedback while the low-side driver is off. GM describes the fault as an impedance no greater than 0.5 ohm toward controller ground. That does not mean every low voltage on the control conductor is abnormal; the expected state changes with the driver command.

Evaluation requires the diagnostic enabled, engine speed of at least 400 rpm and powertrain relay voltage of at least 11 volts. At least 20 failures from 25 observations establish the code, with one observation every 250 milliseconds. This repeated sampling makes the stored event more specific than a single unsynchronized voltage reading.

## Separate electrical control from cylinder operation
Establish whether cylinder 4's circuit remains grounded when it should be released by the driver. A failure of that electrical relationship is different from a valve that mechanically fails to respond despite correct control. The DTC supports the electrical branch without identifying the physical cause.

After the actual problem is corrected, verification should include the relevant off-state response and monitor completion. Use the matching circuit diagram and service method, and never interpret the impedance criterion as a request to test powered wiring with an ohmmeter. Applicability is limited to the documented GM calibration family until confirmed for the vehicle.
