# P3459: Cylinder-8 deactivation control remains low after release

P3459 is the circuit-low diagnostic for cylinder-8 gas-exchange/deactivation valve control in GM's 2020 OBDG07 table. The controller detects a low electrical state while the driver is off and treats it as a short toward ground. The important relationship is between the commanded release and the circuit's response.

## The controller actively checks switching
The monitor toggles the valve control near the start of the driving cycle. It requires ECU drive state, the documented early-cycle speed window and battery voltage above 10.9 but below 25.5 volts. The failure persists for 0.5 second and is classified as a one-trip condition.

A low reading while the driver is on would answer a different question. Similarly, an open circuit can produce abnormal feedback but is evaluated through a separate code branch. Preserve both the cylinder identity and electrical direction when comparing a scan result with measurements.

## Resolve the grounded-state evidence
Identify cylinder 8's actual output conductor and determine whether it can leave the low state under the supported test. The diagnostic does not by itself identify a harness, solenoid or controller defect. It also does not prove mechanical deactivation failure when electrical control is correct.

Verification should show appropriate off-state behavior and a passing monitor after the actual cause is addressed. No numerical short-to-ground resistance is inferred from the incomplete threshold cell in this source. Vehicle applicability, terminal identification and the electrical testing method must come from the exact calibration's service information, rather than from the generic P3459 label alone.
