# P1359: Ignition group-A control circuit open or held high

GM's 2000 4.0L L47 and 4.6L LD8/L37 table assigns P1359 to ignition-control group A, comprising cylinders 1, 7, 4 and 6. The diagnostic recognizes an open circuit or a short toward battery voltage. Its group identification is important: it is not a single-cylinder misfire code.

## Open detection has a special window
The table uses five fault observations out of ten and a 250-millisecond checking interval while Ignition 1 is powered. However, it explicitly restricts open-circuit detection to key-on with the PCM in its reset state. A normal-looking running capture therefore does not reproduce every condition used by this diagnostic.

That distinction also separates the two electrical branches. Evidence of a short toward supply during operation and evidence of an open during the key-on reset window are not interchangeable. The DTC does not independently tell the technician which branch generated the stored event.

## Follow the shared group
Use the exact ignition-system diagram to trace the group-A control relationship before drawing conclusions from an individual cylinder symptom. Compare the affected group's control behavior with its expected state, while preserving whether the observation occurred during the key-on check or later powered operation.

Verification should address the actual fault branch and include the relevant key-on sequence when an open circuit was implicated. The OEM table supplies neither a component replacement instruction nor permission to probe secondary ignition energy directly. Its cylinder grouping and sampling rules apply only to the cited historical monitor after calibration applicability is confirmed.
