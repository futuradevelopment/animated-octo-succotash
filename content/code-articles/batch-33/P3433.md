# P3433: Cylinder-5 deactivation control has an open-path fault

P3433 is the cylinder-5 gas-exchange/deactivation valve open-circuit diagnostic in GM's 2020 OBDG07 document. The controller evaluates the path between its output and the load. It uses driver feedback to identify an interrupted electrical connection, rather than directly measuring the valve's mechanical response.

## The opening-cycle test matters
The row describes switching the valve control on and off at the start of the driving cycle. ECU drive state, the listed 80-to-255-rpm window and battery voltage above 10.9 but below 25.5 volts enable the check. The failure lasts 0.5 second and has a two-trip classification.

These conditions differ from a steady running performance test. A circuit that appears normal later may have failed during the brief early-cycle evaluation. The source also separates this open-path branch from circuit-low and circuit-high branches, so a generic “solenoid electrical fault” label loses useful direction.

## Follow continuity to the actual load
Confirm the cylinder-5 control arrangement for the engine and identify the intended output path. Establish whether continuity is interrupted, rather than interpreting a low feedback state without its driver context. Keep this electrical finding separate from any pumping-air or valve-lift performance diagnostic.

Verification should demonstrate reliable switching and a completed enabled monitor after the actual cause is addressed. The document's open-path impedance is not a universal coil-resistance specification or permission to test energized wiring. It supplies no automatic solenoid, harness or controller replacement decision, and its calibration applicability must be confirmed.
