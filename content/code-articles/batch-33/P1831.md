# P1831: Transmission high-side driver 1 fails its on-state check

P1831 monitors high-side driver 1 in GM's 2000 3.0L L81 Saturn L-series transmission table. The code's catalog title refers to the pressure-control solenoid power circuit; the OEM row describes an open or short toward ground that prevents the driver's feedback state from matching the commanded state.

## Commanded on is an enabling condition
The test runs with driver 1 commanded on, system voltage between eight and 18 volts and qualifying engine operation above 500 rpm. Forty-three failures within 50 samples establish the result, taking approximately four seconds. A low reading while the driver is deliberately off cannot be interpreted as this on-state failure.

The feedback comparison also matters more than the word “voltage” alone. The diagnostic evaluates whether the controlled power path follows its command; it does not state that the whole vehicle electrical system has low voltage.

## Separate the original fault from the response
GM lists maximum line pressure, torque-converter-clutch inhibition, an immediate second-gear fallback and a command to turn driver 1 off. A later off-state measurement may therefore be a controller response to the recorded fault. Preserve the initial command and feedback evidence before confusing the fallback state with the initiating problem.

Verification should demonstrate the correct driver response under the applicable service test and sufficient passing samples after the cause is corrected. The code does not independently identify the solenoid, wiring or controller as defective. Exact circuit access and repair decisions require the matching historical transmission service information.
