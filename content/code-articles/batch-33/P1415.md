# P1415: Bank-1 secondary-air response fails while bank 2 passes

P1415 is the bank-1 secondary-air performance diagnostic in the cited GM 2002 table. The controller looks for the exhaust and fuel-control response when the AIR pump operates during closed-loop running. In this row, bank 1 fails its response checks while bank 2 produces a passing result.

## The comparison is more than pump operation
GM applies an additional eight-percent fuel offset during the check. Failure requires bank 1 to miss both a lean oxygen-sensor response and the specified fuel-integrator change, while the opposite bank passes. The sensor criterion uses a signal below 222 millivolts for at least 1.3 seconds; the integrator comparison uses a 20-percent change.

The test lasts four seconds and can run up to three times. It has detailed enabling conditions, including closed-loop operation, vehicle speed of at least 15 mph, adequate ignition voltage and no relevant sensor, fueling, misfire or engine-protection faults. Merely hearing the pump operate does not reproduce that functional comparison.

## Evidence that narrows interpretation
Compare the two banks during the commanded event. A bank-specific response difference is more informative than a general assumption that the pump cannot run. Separately verify that oxygen-sensor and fuel-control data are usable, because the diagnostic relies on their response to infer AIR performance.

The monitor does not identify a failed valve, hose or pump by itself. Verification should show the expected bank-1 response under the applicable service test. This starred row is not required for the 5.3L LM7 truck application listed within the document's broader group.
