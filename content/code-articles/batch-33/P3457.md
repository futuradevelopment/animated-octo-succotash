# P3457: Cylinder-8 valve-control circuit loses its electrical path

GM's 2020 OBDG07 P3457 monitor checks cylinder-8 gas-exchange/deactivation valve control for an open circuit. The controller evaluates output-driver feedback and the connection to its load. The code does not directly report whether cylinder 8's valves achieved their intended mechanical state.

## The test happens near cycle start
The table describes switching the control on and off at the beginning of the driving cycle. Its enabling conditions include ECU drive state, battery voltage above 10.9 and below 25.5 volts and the listed 80-to-255-rpm range. The failure condition lasts 0.5 second and has a two-trip classification.

An intermittent interruption during that early evaluation can be missed by a later steady-state observation. The open-path branch must also be distinguished from cylinder 8's circuit-low and circuit-high branches. Those represent different electrical connections even though they affect the same controlled system.

## Establish continuity without assuming a mechanical fault
Confirm the actual cylinder-8 control path and connected load for the engine calibration. Determine whether the electrical path is complete using the matching service procedure. A pumping-air performance code, noise or roughness requires its own evidence and cannot substitute for an electrical continuity finding.

Verification should show reliable output switching and successful monitor completion after the demonstrated cause is corrected. The source's open-path criterion is not a universal coil-resistance specification, and it does not justify testing energized wiring with a resistance meter. Exact terminal assignments and replacement decisions remain outside this document-scoped monitor explanation.
