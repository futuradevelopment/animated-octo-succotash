# P3401: Cylinder-1 deactivation solenoid open-circuit monitor

P3401 concerns the cylinder-1 deactivation solenoid's low-side control circuit in GM's 2015 OBDG08A table. The controller tests electrical continuity of that output path. The result does not directly establish that cylinder 1 has a mechanical valve or lifter failure.

## An open path has its own diagnostic branch
The source describes an open-circuit condition with at least 200 kilohms of impedance in its monitor definition. It evaluates feedback during the driver-off state. Powertrain relay voltage must be at least 11 volts, engine speed at least 400 rpm and the diagnostic enabled. Twenty or more errors within 25 samples establish the result, with checks every 250 milliseconds.

The separate low and high circuit codes in the same source show why the suffix matters. An open-path finding is not interchangeable with a short toward ground or supply. A low observed feedback state must be interpreted through the driver's expected state and the appropriate fault branch.

## Electrical continuity before mechanical conclusions
Use the exact application diagram to identify cylinder 1's deactivation control path. Determine whether the path is interrupted and whether the controller's feedback agrees with that evidence. This is different from proving that the commanded valve or cylinder-deactivation action actually occurs.

After the established cause is corrected, verify the output circuit under enabled conditions and confirm successful monitor evaluation. The published impedance describes the monitor; it is not an instruction to measure resistance on powered wiring. Vehicle, engine and calibration applicability remain unconfirmed beyond the cited GM document scope.
