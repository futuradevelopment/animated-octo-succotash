# P3417: Cylinder-3 deactivation circuit open-path check

In GM's 2015 OBDG08A unique-DTC section, P3417 evaluates the cylinder-3 deactivation solenoid's low-side control circuit for an open path. Its placement in a document covering multiple monitors does not establish that every engine in the group uses this cylinder-deactivation arrangement.

## Continuity is judged in a defined state
The source describes an open-circuit impedance of at least 200 kilohms and evaluates driver feedback while the driver is off. It requires engine speed of at least 400 rpm and powertrain relay voltage of at least 11 volts. At least 20 failures within 25 samples establish the code, with checks every 250 milliseconds.

These are electrical criteria. They do not directly measure whether a valve, lifter or other deactivation mechanism moves correctly. Likewise, a cylinder-specific combustion complaint does not independently prove that the output circuit is open.

## Confirm architecture before applying the threshold
Identify the actual engine's cylinder-3 control path and the applicable controller calibration. Determine whether the electrical path is interrupted using the prescribed service method. Keep the open-circuit branch distinct from low- and high-circuit branches, which represent different connections in the same general control system.

Verification should demonstrate reliable continuity and a completed enabled diagnostic after the cause is corrected. The table's high-impedance criterion is not a universal solenoid-coil resistance or a safe standalone test procedure. Do not measure resistance on energized wiring or infer terminal locations from another engine. These facts remain bounded to the cited historical GM monitor.
