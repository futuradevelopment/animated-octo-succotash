# P3460: Cylinder-8 deactivation output stays high when driven

P3460 checks the high-circuit condition of cylinder-8 gas-exchange/deactivation valve control in GM's 2020 OBDG07 document. Its failure is voltage remaining high during driver activation, consistent with a short toward power. It concerns the electrical path that delivers a command to the controlled valve system.

## Activation distinguishes normal high from faulty high
The table describes toggling control at the start of the driving cycle. It lists ECU drive state, a specified early-cycle speed window and battery voltage above 10.9 and below 25.5 volts. The high-state condition lasts 0.5 second, with a two-trip classification.

A high voltage observed when the driver is off may be appropriate, so the command must accompany the measurement. The same cylinder also has separate open and low circuit branches. Their different electrical meanings should not be collapsed into a general claim that its solenoid is defective.
