# P1656: Diesel wastegate PWM output does not follow its command

P1656 monitors the wastegate PWM solenoid driver in GM's 2000 6.5L diesel table. The row explicitly excludes the L57 application. For the supported monitor, the controller finds at least two output-driver open or short indications while the wastegate output fails to follow the software command.

## Electrical control is the measured failure
The test requires ignition voltage above eight volts, qualifying conditions lasting two seconds and continuous monitoring. It does not directly compare actual boost with a target. A boost complaint and this electrical driver fault may coexist, but they are not the same diagnostic observation.

The catalog's broad “Driver 2 Line 6” title omits the controlled wastegate circuit. Identifying that load from the exact application matters more than assuming a universal controller pin or solenoid from the numerical label. The source provides no resistance specification or short-location test.

## Compare command with output behavior
Preserve the PWM command and the corresponding electrical output evidence using the proper service method. Distinguish an output that cannot follow its command from a mechanically ineffective wastegate despite correct electrical control. The monitor supports the first concern; it does not establish the second by itself.

After addressing the demonstrated cause, verification should show that commanded control and electrical feedback agree without repeated open/short indications during enabled operation. Do not infer a turbocharger replacement from this driver code. Keep the source's L57 exclusion and historical diesel calibration scope attached to the interpretation before applying its numerical limits.
