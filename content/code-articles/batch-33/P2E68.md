# P2E68: Bank-1 fuel adaptation rises too far during deactivation

P2E68 is the bank-1 lean-mixture diagnostic during cylinder deactivation in GM's 2020 OBDG07 table. The monitored quantity is the multiplicative part of long-term fuel adaptation in the deactivation state, called ZAS in the source. Its failure boundary is above 1.230011, approximately a 1.23 multiplication factor.

## The factor is not a direct exhaust reading
A factor above unity represents an upward correction in this adaptation term. The diagnostic therefore records the controller's sustained correction demand, not a direct proof of one failed injector or one air leak. It also differs from a short-term correction that briefly changes during a transition.

GM requires bank-1 adaptation in the relevant mode to be active and stable. The row includes a ten-second stability requirement and comparisons involving engine speed, load, short-term trim and the additive adaptation term. These conditions make the operating mode and stability history important to interpreting the stored result.

## Compare mode and bank deliberately
A useful diagnostic comparison separates bank-1 adaptation during deactivation from its behavior with all cylinders active. Also compare bank 2 under equivalent conditions, without assuming that the two banks must have identical instantaneous corrections. Those comparisons can identify whether the concern follows the operating mode or is broader.

Verification should show acceptable adaptation after the actual cause is addressed and the enabled monitor has evaluated again. A normal idle snapshot in another mode is insufficient. The table supplies no root-cause ranking or replacement instruction, and its numerical factor is specific to the documented GM calibration rather than every generic-code application.
