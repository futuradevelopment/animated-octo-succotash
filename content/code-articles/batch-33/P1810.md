# P1810: Pressure-switch states conflict with operating conditions

P1810 evaluates the transmission pressure-switch assembly in GM's 2000 3.5L LX5 engine/transmission table. The controller decodes several switch inputs and compares their combined state with startup and running evidence. The code can represent more than a simple electrically open switch.

## Three different plausibility checks
One branch detects an illegal combination of switch inputs for 60 seconds. A startup branch identifies a continuous drive or reverse indication without a park/neutral indication as engine speed rises through the startup range. A moving-vehicle branch compares the reported range with the transmission ratio: park/neutral with a driven ratio, reverse with a forward ratio, or a forward range with a reverse ratio.

The different branches have different timing and enabling conditions. For example, the running ratio comparison requires valid speed and shift-solenoid information, vehicle movement, throttle opening and an engine-torque window. A stationary switch reading cannot validate that comparison.

## Identify the failed relationship
Preserve the complete decoded switch combination, actual ratio and startup history rather than reducing P1810 to one displayed range. An illegal electrical combination is a different finding from a legal combination that contradicts the gear ratio. Those observations determine which part of the matching service procedure applies.

Verification should reproduce the relevant branch after the actual cause is addressed. The monitor does not independently prove a defective switch assembly or an internal transmission failure. This source's LX5 group scope and calibration-specific conditions must be confirmed before applying its timing to another GM transmission.
