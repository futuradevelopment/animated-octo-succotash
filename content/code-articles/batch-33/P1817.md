# P1817: Reverse indication conflicts with forward drive

P1817 compares transmission pressure-switch range information with the calculated gear ratio. In GM's 2000 3.0L L81 Saturn L-series table, the switch assembly reports reverse while the ratio indicates forward drive. The failure must persist for five seconds under the monitor's running conditions.

## A legal range can still be implausible
The switch combination need not be electrically impossible to fail this check. A valid-looking reverse state is still wrong if the independent ratio evidence establishes forward drive. That distinction separates P1817 from a diagnostic that only looks for an illegal combination of switch inputs.

GM requires vehicle speed of at least five mph, throttle of at least ten percent, sufficient engine torque and valid throttle, speed and shift-solenoid information. Engine operation must also satisfy the stated speed and fuel-cutoff conditions. A reading during a stationary selector movement is not the same evidence.

## Compare states over time
A useful record tracks the reverse indication and calculated ratio together, including how long their disagreement lasts. Also establish that the speed signals used in the ratio calculation are credible before condemning the pressure-switch assembly. The table does not identify an internal transmission component as failed solely from this code.

The controller can freeze adaptations, assume a D4 pattern and inhibit the torque-converter clutch after this fault. Verification should demonstrate that range and ratio agree through the relevant service check after the cause is corrected. The source is a historical Saturn transmission monitor, not a universal five-second rule for every manufacturer's P1817 implementation.
