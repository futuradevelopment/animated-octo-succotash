# P3449: Cylinder-7 deactivation control circuit appears open

P3449 is the cylinder-7 deactivation output's open-circuit diagnostic in GM's 2015 OBDG08A document. The controller evaluates low-side-driver feedback for an electrical path that does not meet the expected continuity condition. The code's cylinder identifier points to the control channel, not automatically to a failed mechanical component.

## What counts as a qualifying observation
The source describes an open-path impedance of at least 200 kilohms during the driver-off check. Engine speed must be at least 400 rpm, powertrain relay voltage at least 11 volts and the diagnostic enabled. Twenty failed observations out of 25 are required, with testing every quarter second.

A stationary resistance check and an enabled controller diagnostic are different types of evidence. The former must follow the vehicle's safe electrical procedure; the latter includes power, speed and driver-state prerequisites. Neither should be replaced by a single generic continuity assumption.

## Establish the path before judging operation
Use the correct engine diagram to identify the cylinder-7 deactivation circuit and its intended load. Determine where continuity is lost, if an open is confirmed. Keep any separate roughness, noise or valve-response concern distinct until its relationship with the electrical fault is established.

Verification should demonstrate reliable circuit behavior and successful monitor completion after the cause is corrected. Its electrical threshold and sample window are specific to the cited GM monitor and should not be transferred automatically to another engine architecture.
