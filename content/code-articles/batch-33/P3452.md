# P3452: Cylinder-7 deactivation control short toward supply

P3452 is the cylinder-7 deactivation circuit-high diagnostic in GM's 2015 OBDG08A table. The source identifies a short toward power in the low-side driver control circuit. The code addresses electrical command delivery, not a direct measurement of cylinder pressure or valve motion.

## A repeated active-driver fault
The row evaluates the supply-side condition with the driver on and describes a very low impedance toward controller power. The diagnostic needs at least 11 volts at the powertrain relay, engine speed at least 400 rpm and enablement. It fails after at least 20 errors within 25 observations, spaced 250 milliseconds apart.

Because this is a commanded-state comparison, a high voltage observed during another state may not be abnormal. Record the control command with the electrical evidence. Also distinguish a supply-side short from the separate open and ground-short branches for the same cylinder.

## Avoid a mechanical conclusion from an electrical code
Use the actual engine wiring information to identify cylinder 7's controlled path and determine whether it can follow the required command. A deactivation mechanism may need a separate performance check once electrical control is valid; P3452 alone does not prove that mechanism has failed.

Verification should show correct output response and successful electrical-monitor evaluation after the established cause is addressed. The source supplies no universal solenoid resistance, connector assignment or automatic parts decision. Its impedance criterion is descriptive diagnostic data, not an instruction to measure powered circuits, and the GM document's applicability must be confirmed for the vehicle.
