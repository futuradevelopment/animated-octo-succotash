# P1816: Park/neutral indication conflicts with a driven ratio

In GM's 2000 Saturn L-series 3.0L L81 transmission table, P1816 indicates that the pressure-switch assembly reports park or neutral while the calculated ratio indicates fourth gear. The stated ratio boundary is below 0.72. It is a disagreement between two sources of range information.

## The ratio must be credible
The monitor requires engine operation above 500 rpm for five seconds, vehicle speed of at least five mph, throttle of at least ten percent and more than ten pound-feet of engine torque. Relevant throttle, vehicle-speed, input-speed and shift-solenoid diagnostics must not be present. The contradictory condition lasts five seconds.

These requirements help distinguish a genuine switch-versus-ratio mismatch from an unreliable ratio calculation. A park/neutral display observed with the vehicle stationary does not reproduce the failing state. Neither does an implausible ratio derived from a separately failed speed signal.

## Two evidence paths to reconcile
Compare the decoded pressure-switch range with the ratio and the input data supporting that ratio. Determine whether the range information is wrong, the ratio evidence is unreliable, or the contradiction exists only during an unqualified transition. The code does not decide the faulty component by itself.

The listed response includes an assumed D4 shift pattern, adaptation changes and torque-converter-clutch inhibition. Those commanded responses can explain subsequent transmission behavior. Verification should show consistent range and ratio information under an appropriate enabled service check. Do not recreate the road conditions casually; use the exact transmission procedure and retain the source's historical Saturn scope.
