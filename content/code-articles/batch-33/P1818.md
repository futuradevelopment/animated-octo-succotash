# P1818: Forward-range switch indication conflicts with reverse ratio

P1818 is the opposite range contradiction to a reverse-switch indication during forward drive. GM's 2000 Saturn L-series transmission table identifies D4, D3, D2 or D1 reported by the pressure-switch assembly while the ratio indicates reverse. The diagnostic concerns consistency between switch information and calculated motion through the transmission.

## Why a plausible switch combination is insufficient
Each listed forward state can be a valid decoded combination on its own. The failure appears when that state disagrees with the ratio for five seconds. Thus, checking only whether a scan tool can display a forward range misses the actual plausibility comparison.

The monitor requires vehicle movement, at least ten-percent throttle, sufficient engine torque and qualifying engine operation. Throttle, input-speed, vehicle-speed and shift-solenoid diagnostics inhibit the comparison because those inputs affect its credibility. A transient selector change or invalid speed signal must not be treated as equivalent to an enabled contradiction.

## Reconcile the two histories
Preserve the switch range and ratio together, including their transition timing. Determine whether the forward indication remains asserted when reverse-ratio evidence is stable, or whether another input undermines the calculation. The DTC does not establish a broken mechanical gear solely from the word “reverse.”

GM lists adaptation freezing, an assumed D4 pattern and torque-converter-clutch inhibition as responses. Verification should establish consistent range and ratio information after the documented repair under the correct service procedure. The table's historical 3.0L L81 Saturn scope must be confirmed before its response strategy is applied elsewhere.
