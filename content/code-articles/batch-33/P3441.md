# P3441: Cylinder-6 deactivation output has an open-path indication

GM's 2015 OBDG08A P3441 monitor evaluates continuity of the cylinder-6 deactivation solenoid control circuit. It uses low-side-driver feedback to identify an open-path condition. The result concerns how the controller reaches the solenoid electrically, rather than directly measuring whether the cylinder's valves deactivate.

## Repeated evidence during a qualified state
The source's open-circuit threshold description is at least 200 kilohms. Evaluation occurs in the driver-off state with powertrain relay voltage at least 11 volts, engine speed at least 400 rpm and diagnostic enablement. At least 20 failures in 25 samples establish the result; checks occur every 250 milliseconds.

An electrical interruption can be intermittent even if the circuit appears normal during a later inspection. The monitor's accumulated result should therefore be considered alongside the operating state and event history. Clearing the code without explaining the interruption supplies no evidence that the path remains reliable.

## Avoid converting circuit identity into a parts verdict
Determine the actual cylinder-6 control conductor and its connected load from the correct service information. Establish whether an open exists before attributing the fault to the solenoid itself. An interrupted connection, load or driver relationship must be distinguished by measured evidence, not by the cylinder number alone.

Verification should show a complete electrical path and successful evaluation under the applicable conditions after correction. The table offers no universal coil-resistance value, connector pinout or mechanical repair instruction. Its high-impedance definition should not be used as an instruction to measure resistance on powered circuitry.
