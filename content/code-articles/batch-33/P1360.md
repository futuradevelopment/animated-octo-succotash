# P1360: Ignition group-B control circuit open or held high

P1360 concerns ignition-control group B in GM's 2000 Car V8 group 1 table. The group contains cylinders 2, 3, 5 and 8. GM describes a circuit that is open or shorted toward 12 volts, with internal fault feedback used to make the diagnosis.

## The group label changes interpretation
Several cylinders sharing a control group is a different diagnostic structure from one code per coil. An apparent problem on one cylinder does not establish that P1360 is a direct cylinder-specific verdict. Conversely, a shared group-B concern should not be dismissed merely because one of its cylinders appears normal during a later observation.

The monitor requires Ignition 1 power and uses five failures in ten observations at a 250-millisecond interval. Its open-circuit check runs only at key-on while the PCM is in reset. The short-to-supply branch and the reset-window open branch therefore require different event context.

## Preserve timing and circuit identity
Identify group B from the appropriate service diagram, then determine whether the fault feedback corresponds to an open or a line held toward supply. Record the ignition state with the measurement; a running-engine result alone cannot validate the key-on open check.

After the established cause is corrected, verification should include the relevant powered and key-on behavior. The source does not identify a bad ignition module, coil assembly or controller simply from this DTC. Keep the historical 4.0L L47/4.6L LD8/L37 document scope attached to these group and timing details when comparing another GM application.
