# P3411: Cylinder-2 valve-control circuit remains low with driver off

P3411 is the cylinder-2 deactivation/gas-exchange valve control circuit-low diagnostic in GM's 2020 OBDG07 table. The controller identifies a voltage that remains low while the driver is off, consistent with a short toward ground. This electrical observation is separate from the cylinder's mechanical deactivation performance.

## An early-cycle switching check
The source describes turning the valve control on and off near the start of the driving cycle to check its operation. Its row lists the ECU in drive state, battery voltage above 10.9 and below 25.5 volts, and an engine-speed window from 80 to 255 rpm. The fault condition lasts 0.5 second.

These are source-specific enable criteria, not a universal instruction to test at road speed. In particular, a low reading while the driver is intentionally on is not the off-state failure this row describes. The table assigns this low-circuit branch a one-trip classification.
