# P2E69: Bank-1 adaptation falls below its deactivation limit

GM's 2020 OBDG07 P2E69 monitor evaluates excessive downward fuel correction on bank 1 during cylinder deactivation. It checks the multiplicative long-term adaptation term in the source's ZAS mode against a lower boundary of 0.769989. This is approximately a 0.77 factor, not a voltage or an air-fuel ratio.

## Rich-side correction needs stable evidence
The term being below unity represents a reduction in this multiplicative correction. The diagnostic identifies a sustained rich-side adaptation concern; it does not independently prove that an injector is leaking or that fuel pressure is excessive. Those possibilities need their own evidence.

The bank-1 adaptation mode must be active and its integrator stable. GM includes at least ten seconds of stability, along with checks involving reference speed/load and the behavior of short-term and additive adaptation terms. A brief negative short-term trim during a transient is not the same quantity or test.

## Preserve the state that produced the correction
Record the deactivation state and the exact long-term term used by the monitor. Compare the behavior with full-engine operation and with the other bank under equivalent conditions. A scanner's combined trim display may not expose the same multiplicative adaptation variable, so confirm the parameter definition before comparing numbers.

Verification should establish acceptable bank-1 adaptation through an enabled deactivation evaluation after the underlying cause is corrected. Clearing learned values alone does not demonstrate that the rich-side demand has disappeared. The document provides monitor criteria, not a universal fuel-system repair or component replacement instruction.
