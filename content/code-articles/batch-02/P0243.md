# P0243: Wastegate solenoid A open-circuit feedback while switched off

The cited GM P0243 implementation checks the low-side driver for turbocharger wastegate or boost solenoid A. It looks for open-circuit feedback while the output is off. This is an electrical actuator diagnosis, not a direct measurement of wastegate travel or boost regulation.

## The output state determines the interpretation

The controller compares off-state voltage with its own acceptable range. The source relates its open-circuit specification to impedance above 200 kΩ between output and controller ground, but this is not a universal resistance-test instruction.

The test needs powertrain-relay voltage of at least 11 volts, run/crank voltage above five volts and no engine cranking. Ten failures out of twelve samples, taken every 100 milliseconds, produce the documented Type A result.

## Actuator identity and paired codes

The source defines turbocharger A as the first unit in exhaust-flow order for a series layout, or the unit associated with Bank 1 in a parallel layout. That definition must be matched to the actual vehicle before choosing a connector.

It also notes that some controllers may set P0245 with P0243. Two codes therefore do not automatically prove two separate defects. Preserve their status and verify the applicable driver behavior. Successful confirmation requires an eligible off-state circuit test without open feedback; correct boost during one drive alone does not exercise the same electrical criterion.

## Source and applicability

This explanation is limited to the 2025 GM 25OBDG03A monitor on [page 223](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=223). Its thresholds are calibration examples, not universal specifications. Confirm the vehicle, engine and controller procedure before applying them.
