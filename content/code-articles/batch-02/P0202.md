# P0202: An electrical opening in the cylinder 2 injector path

The documented GM P0202 diagnostic addresses an open circuit in the cylinder 2 direct-injection output path. It is not a combustion-balance test. The controller uses its driver's voltage feedback to recognize an electrical load condition outside the acceptable range.

## Why on and off measurements differ

The low-side portion is checked with the output powered off, while a high-side alternative is checked with the output powered on. Those different states are part of the monitor design. Comparing a voltage captured in the wrong driver state with an assumed fixed limit can therefore lead to the wrong conclusion.

The published driver specification uses an open-circuit impedance boundary of at least 200 kΩ. It gives no universal pin-to-pin workshop method. Vehicle-specific circuit information remains necessary to identify the correct test points and controller architecture.

## Preserve evidence beyond the cylinder number

The monitor runs with battery voltage at or above 11 volts and appropriate P062B status. Its failure counter requires ten of twenty samples at a 100-millisecond interval. Record whether P0202 is isolated or accompanies wider injector-control faults, since a broader driver issue changes how the cylinder-specific feedback should be interpreted.

A valid repair confirmation is a completed electrical monitor with the cylinder 2 driver responding correctly. Smooth running alone does not reproduce both driver-state checks. Use the approved direct-injection service procedure for any electrical or fuel-system intervention.

## Source and applicability

This explanation is limited to the 2025 GM 25OBDG03A monitor on [page 211](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=211). Its thresholds are calibration examples, not universal specifications. Confirm the vehicle, engine and controller procedure before applying them.
