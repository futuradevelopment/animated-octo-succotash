# P0452: Fuel-tank pressure voltage below the electrical lower limit

P0452 detects a low out-of-range fuel-tank pressure signal in the cited GM vented ELCP system with a Fuel Tank Zone Module. The controller compares raw FTP voltage with 0.15 volts, equivalent to three percent of its reference scale.

## Electrical range before vacuum interpretation

The source identifies a normal voltage range from 0.5 to 4.5 volts. A value below 0.15 volts is therefore outside that intended electrical region. It should not be treated automatically as a valid measurement of tank pressure or as proof of an EVAP leak.

The diagnostic runs whenever the controller is awake, not only while the engine runs or the leak pump operates. It requires 640 failures in 800 samples at 12.5 milliseconds each. Thus, a fault recorded during an off-cycle wake-up can be relevant even if the current engine-running display is normal.

## Communication remains a prerequisite

The table excludes an active EVAP sensor communication fault. Valid communication carrying low voltage is a different evidence path from missing or invalid sensor information.

Preserve raw voltage, controller wake state and message status. Use the applicable circuit procedure to determine why the signal leaves its electrical range. The printed pressure conversions on this page contain awkward sign notation and are not used here as service specifications. Verification should show a completed low-range check with valid communication, not just an apparently reasonable converted pressure.

## Source and applicability

This explanation is limited to the 2025 GM 25OBDG03A monitor on [page 290](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=290). Its thresholds are calibration examples, not universal specifications. Confirm the vehicle, engine and controller procedure before applying them.
