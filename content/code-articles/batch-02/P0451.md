# P0451: Fuel-tank pressure compared with the leak-pump pressure sensor

P0451 is a fuel-tank pressure sensor performance code. The cited GM implementation compares the FTP sensor with the ELCP pressure sensor while the pump creates tank vacuum during a propulsion-off leak test. It is a two-sensor correlation check, not simply a voltage-range test.

## Agreement over a readable range

After an initial five-second delay, the controller averages the difference between the two readings. A difference above 1,021 Pa is the published failure boundary under the defined readable-range conditions. If both sensors indicate that vacuum has moved beyond the FTP sensor's readable range, the judgment uses the comparison accumulated before that point.

This means disagreement while one sensor appears saturated needs context. A single end-of-test value cannot replace the earlier averaged comparison, and matching readings outside the useful range do not independently validate the entire test.

## Follow the off-cycle test

The monitor runs during scheduled wake-up windows several hours after shutdown, with fuel level, temperature, voltage and communication restrictions. Refueling detection or key-up can abort the sequence. Pressure-circuit, pump, switch and reference-orifice faults can inhibit it.

Capture both sensor traces and the completion or abort reason. Do not assume P0451 identifies which sensor is wrong merely because its title names FTP. Verification requires a completed, eligible correlation test with acceptable agreement. The source does not supply a universal sensor replacement instruction or authorize pressure-system disassembly.

## Source and applicability

This explanation is limited to the 2025 GM 25OBDG03A monitor on [page 287](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=287), [page 288](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=288), [page 289](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=289).
