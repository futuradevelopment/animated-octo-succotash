# P0480: Fan 1 relay-control opening detected with the driver off

The cited GM P0480 monitor checks the cooling fan 1 relay-control low-side circuit for an open condition. Its heading explicitly excludes EREV, PHEV and HEV use for this row. It should not be assumed to describe every electrified vehicle carrying the same generic code.

## Relay feedback is separate from fan speed

The failure evidence is low voltage during the driver's off state, interpreted by this controller as an open circuit. The associated internal specification is impedance above 200 kΩ between signal and controller ground. It is not a direct measurement of fan rotation or a universal fan-motor resistance test.

The monitor requires powertrain-relay voltage above 11 volts and uses fifty failures out of sixty-three samples at 100-millisecond intervals. Its Type B reporting and sampled window explain why a fault may be stored even if the fan later operates.

## Interpret a paired code in context

The source notes that P0691 may also set on certain controllers. Two electrical labels need the correct driver procedure before being treated as separate defects. Record supply conditions and command state along with code status.

Verification should demonstrate a passing relay-driver circuit test, then the applicable system operation check. Seeing the fan turn once does not establish that its relay off-state feedback is correct. Use the approved cooling-system procedure and keep clear of an electrically controlled fan during any inspection.

## Source and applicability

This explanation is limited to the 2025 GM 25OBDG03A monitor on [page 305](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=305). Its thresholds are calibration examples, not universal specifications. Confirm the vehicle, engine and controller procedure before applying them.
