# P0366: Bank 1 camshaft Sensor B pulse-count plausibility

P0366 is the performance diagnostic for Bank 1 camshaft Sensor B in this GM section. The controller compares the number of cam pulses with crank-referenced observation windows. A waveform can be present and still fail because pulses are missing or additional edges are counted.

## Short and long windows answer different questions

At startup, a twelve-event medium-resolution crank window fails below four or above six cam pulses. A second method examines 100 engine cycles and fails below 398 or above 402 pulses. The startup test begins at crank region 3; its eligibility begins with starter engagement and can continue after the starter releases.

Both paths require crankshaft synchronization and no relevant crank-sensor fault. An unreliable crank reference changes the meaning of the apparent cam count and must be considered first.

## Evidence beyond a live angle display

Capture the number and placement of cam edges against the correct crank pattern using the vehicle's approved procedure. A scan value reporting a plausible cam angle does not prove that every required pulse was received throughout the monitor window.

The page does not determine whether signal integrity, the sensor or the target explains a failed count. Confirm the specific engine's expected pattern before assigning a mechanical cause. Verification should show passing counts in the affected startup or continuous window with the reference synchronized, rather than only clearing P0366 and checking for an immediate return.

## Source and applicability

This explanation is limited to the 2025 GM 25OBDG03A monitor on [page 275](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=275). Its thresholds are calibration examples, not universal specifications. Confirm the vehicle, engine and controller procedure before applying them.
