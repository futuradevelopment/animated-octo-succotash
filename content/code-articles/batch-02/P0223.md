# P0223: Throttle-position channel 2 above its allowed signal range

P0223 is a high-circuit diagnostic for TPS2 in the cited GM implementation. Although the generic title mentions throttle or pedal position, this row concerns the second throttle-position channel. Its failure boundary is above 91.80 percent of the reference-voltage scale.

## High position and high circuit are different

The controller is checking whether an electrical representation exceeds the permitted range. The code does not merely mean the throttle was requested to open widely. The source defines the scale relative to a five-volt reference and describes the fault as continuous or intermittent short-high behavior.

Run/crank voltage must exceed 6.41 volts, and the relevant fourth reference circuit must be free of a P06A3 error. Checking reference status is therefore necessary before interpreting a high TPS2 value as an isolated channel defect.

## Capture events at the right level

The diagnostic evaluates counts every 3.125 milliseconds and includes both intermittent and continuous failure paths. A normal reading after the event cannot rule out a short high that the controller already captured. Retain the stored event and accompanying throttle data before erasing them.

The next decision is whether the signal path, reference environment or controller-side interpretation explains the high reading, using the correct service procedure. Verification requires an eligible passing high-range test. The monitor page supplies no physical sensor location or automatic replacement instruction and should not be used to justify improvised electronic-throttle manipulation.

## Source and applicability

This explanation is limited to the 2025 GM 25OBDG03A monitor on [page 214](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=214). Its thresholds are calibration examples, not universal specifications. Confirm the vehicle, engine and controller procedure before applying them.
