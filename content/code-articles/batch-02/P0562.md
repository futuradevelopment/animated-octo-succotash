# P0562: Low system voltage measured while the engine is turning

P0562 is a system-voltage-low code. The cited GM engine-controller monitor detects battery-system voltage at or below nine volts while engine speed is at least 400 rpm. It also requires run/crank voltage above five volts and the diagnostic enabled.

## Preserve the operating state

Those prerequisites distinguish this test from an arbitrary battery reading with the vehicle asleep. A low measurement during the monitored running-speed state has a different context from voltage during initial starter engagement or a battery disconnected for service.

The controller counts 400 failures out of 500 samples at 12.5 milliseconds per sample. A brief present-day voltage check may miss the episode that filled that counter. Record fault timing and voltage history before deciding which electrical component needs attention.

## The code does not identify the source of the loss

The page confirms low system voltage but does not distinguish battery condition, charging behavior, a supply connection or the controller's measurement path. Use the applicable electrical diagnostic procedure to locate the reason for the low reading rather than selecting a battery or generator from the code alone.

This row is Type C, one-trip and explicitly has no emissions malfunction-lamp request. Lack of that lamp therefore does not mean the voltage monitor passed. Verification should show stable voltage during a completed eligible test and resolution of any related supply symptoms or faults.

## Source and applicability

This explanation is limited to the 2025 GM 25OBDG03A monitor on [page 331](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=331). Its thresholds are calibration examples, not universal specifications. Confirm the vehicle, engine and controller procedure before applying them.
