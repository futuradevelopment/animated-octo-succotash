# P02F0: Cylinder 3 injector feedback during sufficiently long pulses

P02F0 concerns cylinder 3 injector circuit range/performance. In the cited GM monitor, the key evidence is the voltage-feedback waveform used to estimate opening magnitude and closing time. The controller checks whether those estimates are available and plausible.

## The test is conditional on injector travel

The diagnostic requires a pulse width large enough for maximum pintle travel and stabilized coil behavior. It does not judge every tiny injection pulse by the same full-travel rule. Valid feedback data and the general diagnostic enable must also be present.

Failure can result from an undetected opening or closing event, or from a detected value exceeding its minimum or maximum calibration boundary. That distinction points the investigation toward the actual failed feedback feature rather than an assumed generic injector defect.

## Evidence for a focused retest

Preserve injection pulse width, feedback validity and the available opening/closing diagnostic results for cylinder 3. The source uses cylinder-event sampling and describes 50 to 100 samples with Type B reporting. Its numeric opening and closing limits are supplied elsewhere in supporting tables.

A continuity check cannot reproduce this dynamic rationality evaluation. Equally, the code does not by itself prove the quantity of fuel delivered is wrong. Verification should demonstrate eligible events and a completed passing feedback test under the applicable calibration. Any electrical capture or fuel-system intervention should follow the correct direct-injection service procedure.

## Source and applicability

This explanation is limited to the 2025 GM 25OBDG03A monitor on [page 239](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=239), [page 240](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=240). Its thresholds are calibration examples, not universal specifications. Confirm the vehicle, engine and controller procedure before applying them.
