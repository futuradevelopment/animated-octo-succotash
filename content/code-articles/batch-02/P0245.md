# P0245: Wastegate solenoid A low-side output held toward ground

P0245 identifies low-circuit behavior for wastegate or boost solenoid A in this GM monitor. The controller's low-side output diagnostic checks for a short to ground while the driver is off. It uses internal voltage feedback rather than directly measuring the actuator's mechanical position.

## Command state is essential

A low voltage cannot be judged without knowing whether the driver was commanded on or off. This particular short-ground diagnosis evaluates the off state, where the feedback should meet a controller-specific expectation. The published electrical specification references impedance below 0.5 Ω to controller ground; it is not a generic instruction to measure a powered circuit.

Powertrain-relay voltage must be at least 11 volts, run/crank voltage above five volts, and the engine must not be cranking. The monitor counts ten failures in twelve 100-millisecond samples.

## Interpret overlapping reports carefully

The page warns that P0243 may also set on certain controllers. It also notes architecture-dependent requirements for application software to drive the output. These qualifications mean a paired open/low report needs the correct controller procedure, not two assumed component failures.

Record command state, supply conditions and both code statuses. Confirm which actuator A denotes on the actual turbo layout. Verification should show the low-side feedback passes the applicable off-state test; it should not rely solely on an absence of boost symptoms.

## Source and applicability

This explanation is limited to the 2025 GM 25OBDG03A monitor on [page 224](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=224). Its thresholds are calibration examples, not universal specifications. Confirm the vehicle, engine and controller procedure before applying them.
