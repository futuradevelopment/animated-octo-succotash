# P0246: Wastegate solenoid A high feedback during an on command

For the cited GM system, P0246 is the high-circuit diagnostic of wastegate or boost solenoid A's low-side driver. The controller looks for short-to-power behavior while that driver is commanded on. The command state distinguishes this evaluation from an off-state continuity check.

## What the controller observes

Its internal voltage thresholds are designed around a short-to-power specification, listed as impedance below 0.5 Ω between the output and controller power. That describes the driver diagnostic's target fault; the page does not provide a safe standalone connector test or a universal expected voltage trace.

The test requires powertrain-relay voltage at least 11 volts and run/crank voltage above five volts, with the engine not cranking. The failure decision is ten of twelve observations at 100 milliseconds per sample. Some controller implementations require the application software to be driving the output.

## Separate electrical and mechanical questions

A command that produces high electrical feedback is not, by itself, evidence of a stuck wastegate mechanism. First establish whether the electrical command path behaves as expected. The source's definition of actuator A depends on series versus parallel turbo layout, so physical identification must come from the applicable vehicle documentation.

Following diagnosis, verify the eligible on-state test passes. A road test that never exercises this driver condition cannot conclusively verify the circuit. The monitor does not select a replacement solenoid or controller without further circuit evidence.

## Source and applicability

This explanation is limited to the 2025 GM 25OBDG03A monitor on [page 225](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=225). Its thresholds are calibration examples, not universal specifications. Confirm the vehicle, engine and controller procedure before applying them.
