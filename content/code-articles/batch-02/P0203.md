# P0203: Cylinder 3 injector continuity as seen by the controller

P0203 is the cylinder 3 injector-circuit code. In the cited GM direct-injection table, it specifically represents open-circuit feedback from the output-driver diagnostics. The controller can recognize that feedback on a low-side off-state check or a high-side on-state check.

## Interpret the electrical result narrowly

The page describes voltage thresholds tailored to the controller and an open-circuit specification corresponding to at least 200 kΩ. It does not establish injector spray quality or prove that the injector itself is the open element. Those are separate questions requiring the actual circuit procedure.

The monitor's ten-of-twenty failure decision, sampled every 100 milliseconds, also allows a fault to be recorded even if the present connection appears normal. Historical fault status and the conditions around its occurrence remain useful when the code is intermittent.

## What should accompany a retest

Confirm battery voltage is at least 11 volts and inspect the meaning of any P062B injector-control status before relying on a clean rerun. Identify cylinder 3 through the correct engine documentation; this source page does not establish its physical location for every vehicle covered by the larger file.

After diagnosis, seek a completed driver test without the open-circuit feedback. A successful engine start alone is not equivalent evidence. Any direct-injection circuit or fuel intervention should follow the vehicle-specific professional method, with no assumption that a generic resistance check is safe or sufficient.

## Source and applicability

This explanation is limited to the 2025 GM 25OBDG03A monitor on [page 212](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=212). Its thresholds are calibration examples, not universal specifications. Confirm the vehicle, engine and controller procedure before applying them.
