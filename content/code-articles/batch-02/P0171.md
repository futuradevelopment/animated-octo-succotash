# P0171: Lean fuel-trim evidence and the importance of purge correction

P0171 means the documented Bank 1 fuel-control monitor has accumulated evidence of a lean condition. The GM strategy uses filtered fuel-trim metrics centered on 1.0: a value above that center represents added fuel. This is a correction-based diagnosis, not a direct measurement of a leaking part.

## What crosses the limit

The cited calibration can fail when purge-adjusted long-term trim reaches 1.270 or the non-adjusted purge-on metric reaches 1.900, subject to its short-term criterion. These internal ratios should not be confused with raw percentage displays without confirming the scan-tool definition.

A decision also requires more than 40 seconds of data on the trip and at least 20 seconds in the current fuel-trim cell. Different operating cells may not all be used for diagnosis. Consequently, one transient positive-trim reading is weaker evidence than a persistent correction in a monitored cell.

## Narrowing the investigation

Compare purge-on and purge-adjusted information, operating load and the cell where the failure occurred. The distinction helps determine whether the apparent correction depends on vapor contribution. Review airflow, manifold-pressure, injector, oxygen-sensor and misfire faults because they can inhibit this monitor.

Verification should reproduce the relevant closed-loop operating region and allow enough data accumulation. Clearing learned trims removes useful evidence and does not establish that the underlying correction demand has disappeared. The table supports these diagnostic distinctions but does not identify a universal cause or replacement part.
