# P0261: Cylinder 1 injector low-side circuit checked in the off state

P0261 is the cylinder 1 injector circuit-low code. The cited GM direct-injection monitor interprets it as short-to-ground behavior on the low-side driver. It checks voltage feedback with the output powered off.

## Low feedback must be paired with command state

The controller-specific specification is at or below one volt between signal and controller ground during the relevant test. That does not make every low injector-control voltage a fault: an intentionally driven output has a different meaning. The off-state condition is central to this diagnosis.

The test requires battery voltage of at least 11 volts and acceptable P062B injector-control status. It counts ten failures among twenty samples, with 100 milliseconds between samples, and uses Type A, one-trip reporting.

## What the code does and does not isolate

This row identifies the cylinder 1 electrical path, not a measured fuel-flow shortfall. It cannot establish spray quality, injector clogging or the physical point where the circuit is pulled low. Confirm cylinder numbering and the applicable driver architecture before choosing a test location.

Preserve the driver command, related fault statuses and available feedback. Use the approved direct-injection circuit procedure to separate harness, connection, injector and controller possibilities. Verification should demonstrate that the off-state low-feedback monitor completes without failure. A smooth idle alone does not show that this electrical test passed, and improvised powered probing is inappropriate for the injection driver.

## Source and applicability

This explanation is limited to the 2025 GM 25OBDG03A monitor on [page 226](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=226). Its thresholds are calibration examples, not universal specifications. Confirm the vehicle, engine and controller procedure before applying them.
