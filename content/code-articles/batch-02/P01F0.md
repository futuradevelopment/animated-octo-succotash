# P01F0: Coolant falls below the monitor temperature after warming up

P01F0 concerns coolant temperature that drops below a diagnostic threshold after the engine has already warmed. The cited GM monitor requires engine outlet coolant to have exceeded 67°C at least once during the key cycle, then evaluates an unexpected fall below 56°C during qualifying operation.

## This is a warm-then-cool test

The failure window is 48 seconds out of 60. The controller also requires more than 30 seconds of runtime and more than one kilometre traveled, with ambient temperature above −9°C and ambient pressure above 55 kPa. A cold engine that has not yet reached the initial temperature is therefore outside this particular sequence.

The strategy accounts for heat input and operating state. Extended deceleration fuel cut-off and half-cylinder operation are among the conditions that affect eligibility. These restrictions matter when comparing a temperature dip during unusual operation with the stored fault.

## Build the temperature history

Record the warm-up peak, subsequent coolant trend, ambient conditions and engine operating state. A cooling-system conclusion is stronger when the temperature drop remains unexplained after these inputs are checked. Temperature-sensor, pump, valve and torque-estimation faults appear among the inhibitors, so related codes can change the diagnostic order.

The table does not identify a thermostat or pump replacement as the inevitable remedy. Verification should demonstrate the relevant warmed-up operating period without a qualifying relapse and with valid temperature inputs. A short cold-start check does not exercise the fault condition described here.

## Source and applicability

This explanation is limited to the 2025 GM 25OBDG03A monitor on [page 208](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=208), [page 209](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=209). Its thresholds are calibration examples, not universal specifications. Confirm the vehicle, engine and controller procedure before applying them.
