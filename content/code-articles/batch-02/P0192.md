# P0192: Low SENT data from fuel-rail pressure sensor 1

The cited P0192 monitor evaluates the first high-pressure fuel-rail sensor's SENT digital value. A raw value at or below 94 is treated as out of range low. The number describes the digital signal representation, not 94 kPa, 94 psi or an analog voltage specification.

## What the sample count adds

The controller accumulates failures across 500 samples and reports the low-range failure when 400 qualify. Samples occur every 6.25 milliseconds. The document identifies this as a continuous, Type A, one-trip diagnostic.

That distinguishes a sustained or frequently recurring low payload from a single isolated reading. It also means an ordinary scan tool's slower update rate may not display every event the controller counted. Preserve the fault record instead of assuming a current normal value disproves the stored condition.

## Signal validity before hydraulic conclusions

A circuit-low code is not sufficient proof that physical rail pressure is low. The useful next distinction is between an out-of-range digital measurement and a valid pressure measurement indicating an actual supply problem. Check the applicable SENT and pressure-sensor diagnostic procedure to establish that distinction.

The page does not provide a sensor location, pinout or complete enabling table. It cannot support a universal replacement recommendation. Verification should show valid sensor data and a completed low-range monitor without renewed failure accumulation. Any independent high-pressure measurement belongs within the vehicle-specific professional service method; do not open the fuel circuit to investigate a digital code.

## Source and applicability

This explanation is limited to the 2025 GM 25OBDG03A monitor on [page 207](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=207). Its thresholds are calibration examples, not universal specifications. Confirm the vehicle, engine and controller procedure before applying them.
