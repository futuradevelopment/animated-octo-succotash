# P0507: Idle speed remains higher than a stable commanded target

P0507 is the high-idle counterpart of the documented GM speed-control evaluation. Actual engine speed exceeds desired idle enough that the filtered speed error crosses the published boundary below −182 rpm. The negative sign belongs to this error definition; it is not a negative engine-speed reading.

## A requested fast idle is different

The monitor requires coolant above 60°C, more than thirty seconds of running and a commanded-rpm change below 25 rpm. Vehicle speed must be below two km/h, and recent gear or torque-converter changes delay eligibility. A deliberately elevated or rapidly changing target should not be mistaken for unexplained high idle.

It runs every 12.5 milliseconds and reaches a pass/fail report after ten seconds of the qualifying conditions. Off-vehicle service control must be inactive, and the engine must actually be idling rather than in auto-stop.

## Diagnose the excess relative to demand

Capture requested and actual rpm alongside throttle, airflow and related diagnostic status. Fuel-trim, purge-flow, misfire and several input faults can inhibit this check. A missing result while those faults are active is not proof that speed control is normal.

Use the applicable idle-system procedure to identify why actual speed stays high. The monitor does not specify a universal throttle-cleaning or adaptation remedy. Verification should show a completed passing high-idle test at a stable target, not only an rpm value that looks familiar.

## Source and applicability

This explanation is limited to the 2025 GM 25OBDG03A monitor on [page 318](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=318), [page 319](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=319), [page 320](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=320). Its thresholds are calibration examples, not universal specifications. Confirm the vehicle, engine and controller procedure before applying them.
