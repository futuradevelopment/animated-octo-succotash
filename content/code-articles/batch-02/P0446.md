# P0446: Vent restriction detected while purge operates normally

The generic P0446 title mentions the EVAP vent-control circuit. The cited GM implementation instead describes vent-system performance on a conventional ELCP vented system with a Fuel Tank Zone Module. It looks for restriction in the vent solenoid, filter, hose or canister.

## Vacuum rises despite an open vent command

The diagnostic runs with normal purge and the canister vent commanded open. It fails if tank vacuum exceeds 2,989 Pa for five seconds before purge volume exceeds six litres. The alternative passing pattern is enough accumulated purge volume before the excessive vacuum develops.

That relationship matters more than a valve command alone. An electrically accepted open command does not prove the vent path flows freely, while tank vacuum without purge-volume context does not reproduce the full decision.

## Check eligibility and pressure validity

The test is scheduled once per cold start and can abort after 1,400 seconds. Fuel level, startup temperatures, voltage and ambient pressure are restricted. Pressure-sensor, purge/vent circuit and module-communication faults are among the inhibitors.

Record the vent command, purge volume and tank-vacuum trend together. Use the applicable restriction-isolation procedure rather than assuming the vent solenoid itself is faulty. Verification should show the completed flow-versus-vacuum test passes with valid pressure information. The dictionary's circuit wording should not erase this documented performance interpretation or imply it applies identically to every P0446 vehicle.

## Source and applicability

This explanation is limited to the 2025 GM 25OBDG03A monitor on [page 286](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=286). Its thresholds are calibration examples, not universal specifications. Confirm the vehicle, engine and controller procedure before applying them.
