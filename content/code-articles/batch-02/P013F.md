# P013F: Why a rear sensor can fail the first lean-to-rich response

P013F is the initial lean-to-rich delay check for Bank 1 Sensor 2 in this GM strategy. The controller enriches fueling and asks whether the rear oxygen sensor leaves its lean indication soon enough. The documented failure combines voltage below 350 millivolts with accumulated test airflow above 360 grams.

## Delay and transition rate are different

This is the fifth test in a six-stage rear-sensor sequence. The following P013B evaluation examines slow response through a later voltage interval. P013F instead concerns reaching the initial threshold after enrichment begins. A trace that rises after a long pause may therefore contain relevant evidence even if its later rise looks quick.

The airflow-based allowance also means elapsed time alone is incomplete. A comparison between two runs needs the airflow history, not just the number of seconds before voltage changes.

## Conditions that affect the result

The source requires preceding tests to pass, heater resistance to be learned, and more than one cylinder to be fueled before enrichment is entered. During testing, commanded equivalence ratio and airflow must remain within specified limits; otherwise the procedure can abort. New-catalyst conditioning is another documented gate, with a feature the table says cannot be re-enabled in service.

Record test completion, commanded mixture, rear voltage and related diagnostic status. These data distinguish a valid failed response from a test that never reached its decision. Verification should establish that the completed initial-delay monitor passes; replacing a component or erasing codes alone does not demonstrate that outcome.

## Source and applicability

This explanation is limited to the 2025 GM 25OBDG03A monitor on [page 179](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=179), [page 180](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=180), [page 181](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=181).
