# P0234: Overboost relative to demand or basic boost pressure

P0234 means the documented boost-control strategy has detected excessive boost. The cited GM section has two diagnostic paths and explicitly excludes the mechanically driven supercharger case named in its note. It should not be treated as a universal boost-pressure limit.

## Which pressure comparison failed

With closed-loop pressure control active, the controller subtracts actual boost from desired boost. Excess actual pressure produces a negative deviation, which is compared with calibration tables dependent on engine speed, desired pressure and ambient pressure.

A second path operates with pressure control open loop. It compares actual pressure with basic boost plus a diagnostic offset. This path matters when the requested pressure is below the system's basic boost level: a desired-versus-actual comparison alone would not describe the complete strategy.

## Steady demand and valid sensing

The closed-loop path requires a restricted desired-pressure range and a limited pressure-demand rate of change. Both paths count 40 failures out of 50 samples at 100 milliseconds each. Sensor, bypass and pressure-actuator faults can inhibit the diagnosis, while manual device control must be inactive.

Record actual and desired pressure, ambient pressure and control mode together. Their relationship is more informative than peak boost alone. Check the applicable supporting calibration before judging a deviation numerically. Verification should reproduce the valid control mode and show a completed passing monitor; the code itself does not prove a failed turbocharger, wastegate or sensor.

## Source and applicability

This explanation is limited to the 2025 GM 25OBDG03A monitor on [page 215](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=215), [page 216](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=216), [page 217](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=217). Its thresholds are calibration examples, not universal specifications. Confirm the vehicle, engine and controller procedure before applying them.
