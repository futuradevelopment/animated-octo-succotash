# P018B: A pressure sensor that stays plausible but fails to respond

P018B is a fuel-pressure Sensor B range/performance diagnostic. This GM strategy specifically looks for a sensor response stuck within its normal operating range. It uses an intrusive test rather than waiting for the signal to cross a simple high or low electrical limit.

## Why a believable pressure is insufficient

The test can be triggered when fuel-pump duty has remained clamped at a minimum or maximum for at least five seconds, or when pressure-error variance is sufficiently small. It evaluates the pressure change during a test lasting approximately five to twelve seconds, depending on fuel flow. Passing intrusive tests are separated by at least 60 seconds.

Thus, an apparently reasonable static pressure may still fail to track a commanded change. The useful evidence is the relationship between pump behavior and pressure response, not just one pressure number.

## A threshold limitation

The table prints a pressure-change comparison of at least 30 kPa beside a strategy described as detecting a stuck sensor. The page does not clearly resolve whether that comparison is the required response or the failure expression. This article does not invert it or claim a verified pass/fail limit.

Review pump, reference-voltage, module-temperature and communication faults because they gate the test. Fuel-flow validity also matters. Verification should follow the applicable controller's complete intrusive procedure and confirmed threshold. Fuel-system pressure work belongs within the vehicle-specific professional procedure; this monitor summary does not authorize opening a pressurized circuit.

## Source and applicability

This explanation is limited to the 2025 GM 25OBDG03A monitor on [page 202](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=202), [page 203](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=203).
