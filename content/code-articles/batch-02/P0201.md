# P0201: Cylinder 1 injector open-circuit feedback from the driver

P0201 identifies the cylinder 1 injector circuit. The cited GM direct-injection monitor is an open-circuit test performed by the controller's output-driver diagnostics. It judges voltage feedback against controller-specific limits while the driver is in defined states.

## Two driver states can expose the fault

One path evaluates the low-side driver while its output is off. Another evaluates the high-side driver while the output is on. The page relates the controller's open-circuit specification to impedance of at least 200 kΩ, but it does not turn that internal criterion into a universal connector resistance test.

This is electrical evidence. A restricted injector or an incorrect delivered fuel quantity is not established simply because P0201 is present. The driver-state feedback must be investigated on the correctly identified cylinder 1 circuit.

## Conditions for a useful verification

Battery voltage must be at least 11 volts, and the P062B injector-control fault status must permit the test. The controller counts ten failures out of twenty samples at 100-millisecond intervals and uses Type A, one-trip reporting.

Save supply voltage and related driver codes before deciding whether the fault is isolated. Then use the applicable circuit procedure to distinguish injector, connection, harness and controller-side possibilities; the table does not rank them. Confirm that the output-driver monitor completes without renewed open-circuit feedback. Direct-injection fuel and driver circuits require the vehicle-specific service method rather than improvised powered probing.

## Source and applicability

This explanation is limited to the 2025 GM 25OBDG03A monitor on [page 210](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=210). Its thresholds are calibration examples, not universal specifications. Confirm the vehicle, engine and controller procedure before applying them.
