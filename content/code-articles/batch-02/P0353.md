# P0353: Cylinder 3 ignition-control circuit opening versus a weak spark

P0353 is an ignition-control circuit code. This GM monitor specifically checks cylinder 3's low-side ignition driver for an open condition, using feedback measured while the output is off. Its subject is the control circuit, not a direct test of the spark delivered across the plug gap.

## Supply and state define the test

The engine must be running and ignition voltage must exceed 11 volts. The controller counts 50 failures among 63 samples at 100-millisecond intervals. The published open-circuit driver specification is above 30 kΩ between signal and controller ground, with voltage limits implemented inside the controller.

A measurement taken during an on command or with inadequate supply is not equivalent to the documented off-state diagnosis. Retain command and voltage context when examining an intermittent event.

## Identify the monitored path correctly

The broad generic title may call this coil C, while the primary source calls it cylinder 3 ignition control. Neither label establishes physical placement across all engines. Confirm the engine-specific mapping before choosing a connector or interpreting another cylinder's waveform.

The appropriate diagnostic decision is whether the cylinder 3 control path is electrically open and where. The source does not establish a coil, connector, harness or controller failure by itself. After correction, verify the eligible off-state monitor passes. Smooth running alone does not test every aspect of the driver-feedback circuit.

## Source and applicability

This explanation is limited to the 2025 GM 25OBDG03A monitor on [page 273](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=273). Its thresholds are calibration examples, not universal specifications. Confirm the vehicle, engine and controller procedure before applying them.
