# P0459: Purge-solenoid high feedback during an active low-side command

P0459 identifies a high fault in the EVAP purge-control circuit. For the cited GM system, the controller checks the canister purge solenoid's low-side driver for a short to power when the output is on. It evaluates whether the electrical feedback matches the commanded state.

## This is not a purge-flow measurement

A driver-high result does not independently establish how much vapor reaches the intake or whether the valve seals mechanically. The circuit response must be evaluated first. The page defines a controller short-to-power specification corresponding to less than 0.5 Ω between output and controller power, without supplying a universal field-test procedure.

Powertrain-relay voltage must exceed 11 volts. The controller uses twenty failures out of twenty-five samples at 250-millisecond intervals and lists Type B, two-trip behavior. Command timing is therefore relevant when interpreting a stored event or comparing a captured voltage trace.

## Confirm the on-state response

Retain command, supply and feedback information together. A high value observed with the output off cannot simply be judged by this on-state criterion. Use the actual vehicle's circuit procedure to distinguish the affected connection, harness, actuator or driver path.

After correction, confirm the completed circuit-high monitor passes while its prerequisites are met. An EVAP leak-test pass alone does not reproduce this electrical test. The document does not justify replacing the purge valve solely because P0459 appears.

## Source and applicability

This explanation is limited to the 2025 GM 25OBDG03A monitor on [page 298](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=298). Its thresholds are calibration examples, not universal specifications. Confirm the vehicle, engine and controller procedure before applying them.
