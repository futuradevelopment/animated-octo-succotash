# P0178: Low flex-fuel sensor frequency received through the tank module

P0178 is a fuel-composition sensor circuit-low code. In the cited GM arrangement, the engine controller receives the ethanol sensor's frequency through a Fuel Tank Zone Module, or FTZM. The diagnostic treats a frequency below 45 hertz as out of range low or open.

## Frequency is the measured quantity

The source describes a nominal scale from 50 hertz at E0 to 150 hertz at E100, with a specified accuracy corresponding to five hertz. A frequency below the electrical limit is therefore not simply evidence of low ethanol content. It falls outside the documented composition measurement range.

The monitor requires ignition voltage above 11 volts and the configured FTZM/flex-fuel-sensor arrangement. It counts 50 failures out of 63 samples at 100-millisecond intervals and is Type A, one-trip.

## Separate sensor data from communication

FTZM reset and information-message faults inhibit the diagnosis. That matters when several codes coexist: an unavailable or invalid message is not equivalent to a valid message carrying an abnormally low frequency.

Preserve the raw frequency, module communication status and ignition voltage if the scan system exposes them. Determine which part of the data path is trustworthy before interpreting an ethanol percentage derived from the same signal. Verification should demonstrate valid communication and an in-range frequency during a completed monitor. The table does not establish that changing fuel or replacing the composition sensor alone will resolve the circuit fault.

## Source and applicability

This explanation is limited to the 2025 GM 25OBDG03A monitor on [page 196](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=196). Its thresholds are calibration examples, not universal specifications. Confirm the vehicle, engine and controller procedure before applying them.
