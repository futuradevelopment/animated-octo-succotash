# P013A: Slow rich-to-lean response after the rear sensor begins switching

P013A is the Bank 1 Sensor 2 rich-to-lean slow-response diagnostic in the cited GM strategy. It belongs to an ordered sequence of intrusive rear oxygen-sensor tests. The controller commands fuel cut-off and evaluates how the sensor voltage falls as air passes through the engine.

## Two ways the controller reaches a result

When the signal crosses the required voltage thresholds, the primary method normalizes its response to airflow and filters the result using an exponentially weighted moving average. The published failure limit is above 8 units and the repass limit is below 6 units. These are processed diagnostic units, not seconds measured from an ordinary graph.

If the transition is not completed, the alternative method evaluates accumulated airflow. Its relevant crossing is from 450 to 150 millivolts, with a 30-gram airflow threshold. Thus, simply seeing the voltage move eventually does not establish that the response was acceptable.

## Distinguishing slow from delayed

P013E, the initial-delay test, must pass before this later slow-response evaluation. That ordering is valuable: P013A focuses on the subsequent transition rather than merely waiting for the first response to fuel cut-off.

Review the earlier test results, learned heater-resistance validity and the rear-sensor trace alongside airflow. Misfire and fuel-trim faults are among the inhibitors. The diagnostic interpretation is to investigate response quality in a valid test sequence; the document does not prove that replacing the sensor is the correct repair. Verification requires a completed sequence and the processed monitor result, particularly after memory clearing changes its filtering behavior.

## Source and applicability

This explanation is limited to the 2025 GM 25OBDG03A monitor on [page 171](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=171), [page 172](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=172), [page 173](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG03A.pdf#page=173).
