# P1224: Jaguar AJ26 throttle position error and mechanical guard mode

P1224 is a throttle-control position error in Jaguar's 1997 AJ26 diagnostic summary. The source links the fault to a protective mechanical-guard response and a wide range of possible electrical or adaptation causes.

## A battery event can be relevant

Jaguar includes throttle adaptations not performed after battery disconnection, disconnected position sensing, resistive or open feedback wiring, relay and relay-supply faults, motor-drive circuit faults and throttle assembly failure. This variety means a position error is not proof that the throttle motor itself has failed.

The adaptation entry makes recent battery work useful history, but it does not establish that adaptation is the remedy on a particular vehicle. Circuit and operating evidence still have to support that conclusion.

## Understand the reduced-function state

The listed condition is ignition on for more than three minutes. With the first-trip red warning, Jaguar describes mechanical guard mode and inhibition of idle control, cruise control, traction/stability control and power limiting. The check-engine column lists two trips, so that warning's timing is not the same as the first protective response.

Treat unexpected throttle behavior or a red warning as requiring professional assessment. The summary explains controller behavior; it does not authorize bypassing the mechanical guard or forcing the throttle actuator. Establish the exact AJ26 system before applying these historical details.
