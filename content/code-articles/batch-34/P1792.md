# P1792: Jaguar ZF 4 HP 24 sport-mode indicator circuit

P1792 identifies a sport-mode indicator circuit failure in Jaguar's ZF 4 HP 24 E9/AJ16 summary. That definition conflicts with a generic catalog's ignition-supply wording, and it should not be transferred to other transmission families.

## An indicator fault is not proof of a shifting fault

Jaguar lists a failed sport indicator bulb, an open or shorted TCM-to-instrument-pack indicator circuit and instrument-pack failure. The shorts can involve ground or battery voltage. These possibilities concern the indication path, rather than directly establishing that sport-mode shift control is mechanically inoperative.

The monitoring condition is ignition on while operating the mode switch. A static glance at the indicator without changing the command does not represent that complete check.

## The row lists no limp-home response

The summary shows no check-engine activation, transmission warning or limp-home action for this entry. Those absences are useful for interpreting this specific indicator fault, but they do not guarantee that other simultaneous transmission faults are harmless.

Confirm the ZF 4 HP 24 E9 controller before using this definition. Professional diagnosis should distinguish an illumination problem from failure of the commanded mode using the appropriate wiring information. The source provides no instrument-cluster disassembly instructions or bulb specification, and P1792 alone does not justify replacing the TCM.
