# P0142: Volkswagen sensor-three signal coupling to heater switching

P0142 is the bank-one, sensor-three oxygen-sensor circuit entry in Volkswagen's 2014 CBTA/CBUA chart. The detailed criterion focuses on the signal's reaction when heater switching occurs.

## A switching-related change is the useful clue

The chart describes a voltage step above 2.00 volts at a heater-switching event and at least six instances of heater coupling. This is more specific than a generic statement that the oxygen sensor has an unusual reading. The relationship between heater activity and the signal change is part of what the monitor evaluates.

That relationship helps distinguish this entry from P0146, which assesses lack of signal activity using a different group of observations. It also differs from P0042, whose subject is the heater-control circuit itself.

## What the table does not diagnose

The switching criterion does not independently locate an internal sensor defect or a wiring fault. It supplies an observed interaction that must be checked under the proper vehicle-specific procedure. A single static voltage measurement may not capture the repeated switching behavior described by the row.

Keep the sensor-three location and the CBTA/CBUA chart scope in the diagnostic record. The source provides no connector layout, oscilloscope setup or replacement instruction. Professional evaluation should establish the origin of the coupling rather than replacing a sensor solely because its heater operation is mentioned.
