# P1641: Jaguar V12 fuel-pump relay-one circuits

P1641 identifies a malfunction associated with fuel-pump relay one in Jaguar's V12 Nippondenso summary. The manufacturer separates the relay's control wiring from its switched pump wiring.

## Two sides of the relay need different evidence

Jaguar lists relay failure, an open or short in the relay-to-ECM coil circuit, and an open or short in the relay-to-pump switched circuit. A valid relay command therefore does not, by itself, establish that the switched path can deliver power. Conversely, loss of pump operation does not prove that the control coil is at fault.

That distinction is more useful than treating the phrase “fuel pump primary circuit” as a pump replacement instruction. The source names the relay and both related wiring paths; it does not state that a pressure or flow failure has been measured.

## Scope and monitoring

The listed condition is ignition on for more than five seconds, with two consecutive qualifying trips for the check-engine warning. This is the relay-one entry in the V12 system, not a universal definition for other Jaguar engines or later pump-control modules.

Professional diagnosis should use the appropriate circuit diagram and fuel-system procedure. The summary does not authorize bridging relay contacts, applying external power to the pump or opening fuel connections. Establish which electrical path is abnormal before selecting a repair.
