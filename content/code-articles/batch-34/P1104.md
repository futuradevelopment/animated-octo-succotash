# P1104: Jaguar AJ26 MAF ground fault and substitute load calculation

P1104 identifies a mass airflow sensor ground malfunction in Jaguar's 1997 AJ26 engine-management summary. It is an electrical integrity complaint with consequences for how the controller calculates engine load.

## What the fallback changes

The listed monitor condition is ignition on for more than five seconds. When the associated amber warning is activated, Jaguar describes substitution of throttle angle for the airflow-based load measurement, a 3,000 rpm engine-speed limit, and inhibition of canister purge and EGR. Those are documented controller responses, not proof that each inhibited system has developed its own fault.

## Where the evidence points

The causes include an open or resistive reference-ground circuit, a ground circuit exposed to high voltage, an open airflow sensing circuit, and sensor failure. The word “ground” therefore should not restrict diagnosis to a single ground attachment: Jaguar also includes the signal path.

A useful diagnostic distinction is whether an implausible airflow reading is the original defect or a substituted value after the fault. Interpret live data alongside fault status and the documented fallback. Confirm the correct AJ26 wiring and circuit integrity before condemning the airflow sensor; this summary supplies neither a universal pinout nor a replacement decision.
