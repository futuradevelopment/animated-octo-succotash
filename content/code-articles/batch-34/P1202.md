# P1202: Jaguar AJ16 cylinder-two injector electrical fault

P1202 is the cylinder-two injector open-or-short fault in Jaguar's 1995 AJ16 engine-management summary. The source identifies an electrical path and a particular cylinder, without deciding which part of that path has failed.

## Connector, harness or injector

The possible causes are supplied through the P0201 cross-reference: the injector may be disconnected, its harness may be open or shorted, or the injector itself may have failed. A connection disturbed during prior work is therefore a supported possibility, but the code does not establish a repair history or prove that the connector is the cause.

Retain the cylinder-two identity when following the reference. P0201 supplies shared diagnostic mechanisms; it does not redirect P1202 toward cylinder one's injector. That distinction matters when notes or scanner descriptions are abbreviated.

## The shutdown interval is part of the evidence

Jaguar describes running the engine and then keeping the ignition off for more than two seconds. The table lists two consecutive qualifying trips for the check-engine warning. A running-engine observation alone does not reproduce the full documented condition.

Evaluate circuit continuity and injector behavior using the exact AJ16 workshop specifications. This manufacturer summary provides no resistance limit, pinout or instruction to substitute parts. An electrical injector code should not be converted into a diagnosis of fuel blockage without additional evidence.
