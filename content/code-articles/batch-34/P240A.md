# P240A: Volkswagen leak-detection pump heater open-circuit monitor

P240A identifies the evaporative-system leak-detection pump heater control circuit's open classification in Volkswagen's 2014 CBTA/CBUA chart. The heater is a distinct monitored circuit from the pump motor control.

## The table separates heater and pump faults

Volkswagen lists a high monitored signal voltage with a threshold range of 4.7–5.4 volts for this heater entry. Nearby P2400 concerns pump control rather than the heater, even though its diagnostic voltage range looks similar. Similar numbers do not make the two circuit identities interchangeable.

The chart's range reflects the published criterion; it does not identify one exact calibration threshold or state that 5 volts should be applied externally. A diagnostic signal voltage is not necessarily the component's normal power-supply value.

## What remains to be established

An open-circuit classification cannot locate the interruption by itself. The quick reference gives no wiring diagram, heater resistance, connector assignment or failed-component conclusion.

Professional diagnosis should identify the heater-control path and use the applicable electrical tests before selecting parts. Do not treat the code as proof of a vapor leak or a pump motor failure. This explanation applies to the documented CBTA/CBUA section, with configuration-specific verification still required before its values are used on an individual vehicle.
