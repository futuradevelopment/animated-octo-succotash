# P1397: Jaguar AJ26 B-bank VVT open-circuit classification

P1397 is the B-bank variable valve timing solenoid open-circuit classification in Jaguar's 1997 AJ26 summary. The diagnostic possibilities are wider than one visibly disconnected wire.

## An electrical classification, not an oil-flow diagnosis

Jaguar lists an open or resistive ECM-to-solenoid circuit, a circuit shorted to higher voltage and solenoid failure. The high-voltage possibility is particularly useful: the “open” description is the controller's classification and should not be treated as proof of one specific physical defect.

This row differs from the broader VVT malfunction entry, which includes oil supply and mechanical timing possibilities. P1397 directly points toward the electrical control path; it does not, by itself, establish inadequate oil pressure or a damaged timing drive.

## Inhibited VVT is part of the response

The source lists ignition on for more than five seconds as the monitoring condition. When the check-engine warning is activated on the second qualifying trip, VVT is inhibited. Absent VVT activity after that response should be interpreted alongside the fault status.

Use the precise AJ26 bank identification and circuit specifications before diagnosing the solenoid. The summary does not give a resistance limit, a connector pinout or permission to energize the actuator directly. Confirm the electrical defect before converting the stored classification into a parts decision.
