# P1204: Jaguar AJ16 cylinder-four injector shutdown monitor

P1204 reports an open-or-short fault for the fourth cylinder's injector in Jaguar's 1995 AJ16 diagnostic summary. The cylinder number is part of the fault identity and should not be replaced by a generic bank description.

## Why the event may appear after running

Jaguar lists a sequence of engine operation followed by more than two seconds with the ignition off. The adjacent P0204 entry instead uses a running-engine interval. These separate observation conditions mean that a fault recorded during shutdown is meaningful even if the injector appears active during a brief live-data inspection.

For possible causes, P1204 points to the P0201 row. That row identifies disconnection, an open or short in the harness, and injector failure. The reference shares electrical mechanisms while P1204 retains cylinder four as its target.

## Interpreting the result

The code cannot tell whether the defect lies inside the injector or between the injector and controller. Neither does it establish blocked fuel flow merely because an injector is named. Keep the recorded electrical fault separate from any additional combustion complaint until testing connects them.

The summary lists two consecutive qualifying trips before the check-engine warning. Use the correct AJ16 circuit specifications and include the documented shutdown condition when evaluating recurrence. The table does not authorize direct energizing or fuel-rail work as a shortcut to diagnosis.
