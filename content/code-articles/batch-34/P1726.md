# P1726: Jaguar 5 HP 24 engine-overspeed malfunction

P1726 is the engine-overspeed malfunction entry in Jaguar's 1997 5 HP 24 transmission summary. The fault can involve the transmission's interpretation of speed as well as mechanical operation.

## The source includes information faults

Jaguar lists an output-speed sensor problem, corrupted engine-speed data arriving from the ECM over CAN, and mechanical transmission failure. An overspeed description therefore does not establish that the driver actually over-revved the engine or that the transmission has mechanically slipped.

Comparing the recorded speed information and companion faults is useful for separating an implausible data relationship from a physical speed event. The summary does not provide the overspeed threshold, so a universal rpm limit should not be invented for this code.

## Understand the protective state

The row describes fourth-gear mechanical limp-home operation and engine-power limitation after detection. The check-engine warning requires two qualifying trips, while the amber gearbox-fault indication is immediate.

The published monitoring condition involves full-throttle acceleration during driving. That is a service-monitor description, not an instruction to reproduce an overspeed complaint on public roads. Professional diagnosis should determine whether any dynamic test is suitable and use the exact 5 HP 24 procedure. The code alone does not justify replacing the speed sensor, ECM or transmission.
