# P1720: Jaguar loss of both transmission and wheel-speed information

P1720 identifies loss of the transmission output-speed signal together with loss of CAN wheel-speed messages in Jaguar's 1997 5 HP 24 summary. It is a combined loss of two information paths, not simply a vehicle-speed display problem.

## The earlier code matters

Jaguar notes that P0721 is logged first. Its cause list then adds corrupted ABS/traction-control wheel-speed data, wheel-speed sensor failure or an ABS/traction-control fault. The sequence helps distinguish the original output-speed problem from the later loss of an alternative source of speed information.

An isolated transmission speed-sensor replacement may therefore leave the additional network or wheel-speed problem unresolved. That is an interpretation of the two-path description, not proof that two components failed independently.

## Protective behavior

The monitor is described during driving with ABS/traction control inactive. When the fault is detected, Jaguar specifies fourth-gear mechanical limp-home operation and engine-power limitation. The check-engine entry is two trips, with an immediate amber gearbox-fault indication.

Professional diagnosis should preserve the P0721 record and inspect the relevant ABS/traction-control module evidence as well as the TCM record. The summary does not provide a sensor waveform or a network isolation test. It also does not certify that a vehicle in the documented protective state is suitable for a road test.
