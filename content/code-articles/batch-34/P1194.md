# P1194: Jaguar downstream heater resistance and intermittent wiring

P1194 identifies a high-resistance fault affecting both downstream oxygen-sensor heaters in Jaguar's 1995 AJ16 summary. The row refers to P1188 for its possible causes.

## What the reference adds

P1188 includes an open or intermittently open ECM-to-heater connection, a wiring short to ground, excessive resistance or an open circuit in the heaters, and heater failure. For P1194 those mechanisms must be evaluated in the downstream heater path, rather than copied onto the upstream components named by the reference row.

The intermittent-open possibility deserves attention when the current reading looks normal. A normal measurement at one moment does not reproduce the electrical condition during a previous event. Fault status and evidence of wiring intermittency help decide whether further observation is needed before a part is condemned.

## Monitor timing is part of interpretation

The summary lists engine idle for more than 25 seconds and two consecutive qualifying trips for the check-engine warning. This differs from the longer low-idle interval used for the neighboring downstream open-circuit classification P1193.

The manufacturer summary supplies no numeric resistance threshold. Use the exact AJ16 circuit and test specifications to separate harness resistance from heater-element failure. The word “both” describes the monitored group; it is not evidence that two replacement sensors are automatically required.
