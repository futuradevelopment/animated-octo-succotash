# P1794: Jaguar AJ16 low ECM battery supply

P1794 identifies ECM battery-positive supply below 10.5 volts in Jaguar's 1996–1997 AJ16 summary. This row measures the engine controller's supply condition, which should be distinguished from a general battery-health verdict.

## The charging system and feed path both matter

Jaguar lists a loose generator drive belt, resistance or an open/ground short in the ECM supply circuit, battery malfunction and charging-system malfunction. These alternatives mean a low controller supply can arise from vehicle charging trouble or from the path delivering power to that controller.

The listed operating condition is engine speed above 1,600 rpm, with two consecutive qualifying trips for the warning. A static battery measurement with the engine stopped does not reproduce that described condition.

## Use the number in its proper role

The 10.5-volt value is the fault description's low-supply threshold. It is not a recommended charging voltage or a universal battery replacement limit. Further measurements must be interpreted using the applicable AJ16 electrical specifications and actual operating conditions.

Record companion supply-related faults and establish whether the voltage problem is widespread or localized to the ECM feed. The source does not provide a belt-adjustment procedure, charging-system repair decision or instruction to replace the battery automatically. Resolve the electrical evidence before interpreting controller behavior as an unrelated engine-component failure.
