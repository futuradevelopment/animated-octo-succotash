# P1745: Jaguar 5 HP 24 pressure-regulator-one electrical fault

P1745 identifies a circuit malfunction for pressure regulator one in Jaguar's 1997 5 HP 24 summary. The manufacturer includes wiring outside and inside the transmission, as well as the regulator itself.

## Circuit location changes the repair decision

The listed possibilities are an open or short in the transmission-to-TCM regulator-one circuit, an open or short in the internal regulator-one circuit, and regulator failure. A controller command does not establish that it reaches the internal component, and a stored electrical code does not decide which wiring segment is defective.

The code's regulator number must stay attached to the diagnosis. Neighboring codes identify other pressure regulators and should not be treated as interchangeable merely because they share a protective response.

## The monitor does not require a shift event

Jaguar lists ignition on as the monitoring condition. Upon detection, the TCM adopts fourth-gear mechanical limp-home operation and the ECM limits engine power. The check-engine entry is two trips, with an immediate gearbox-fault indication.

These are electrical fault and fallback details, not a measured hydraulic-pressure diagnosis. Professional testing should use the exact 5 HP 24 pinout and component specifications before considering internal work. The summary provides no resistance value, pressure setting or instruction to remove the valve body.
