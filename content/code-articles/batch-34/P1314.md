# P1314: Jaguar AJ16 catalyst-risk misfire in cylinders four through six

P1314 identifies catalyst-risk misfire in cylinders four, five and six in Jaguar's 1995 AJ16 summary. The source names this group B bank, distinguishing it from P1313's first three cylinders.

## Use the cylinder group without over-narrowing the cause

The group designation helps organize companion misfire evidence, but Jaguar's cause reference remains broad. It includes fuel quality and delivery, ignition secondary faults, low compression, broken valve springs and relevant crank-sensor, injector or ignition-primary circuit problems. The code does not decide which of those mechanisms is present.

Jaguar says random or cylinder-specific misfire codes accompany this classification. Those records can be more useful for locating the affected cylinder than treating all three cylinders as confirmed failures.

## Warning behavior is model-year specific

The row's monitor uses steady operation for more than two minutes. Despite a one-trip column entry, the page footnote states that P1314 itself does not light the check-engine lamp on 1995 vehicles. That exception should not be transferred to later systems without checking their summary.

The catalyst-damage wording describes the risk category of the misfire, not proof that a catalyst needs replacement. A vehicle with active misfire requires professional diagnosis; do not prolong running merely to complete the listed monitor. Preserve the distinction between the combustion cause and possible catalyst consequences.
