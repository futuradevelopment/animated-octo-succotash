# P1758: Jaguar combined pressure-regulator current fault

P1758 identifies incorrect combined current for pressure regulators two, three and five in Jaguar's 2000-onward 5 HP 24 summary. It evaluates a group of regulator circuits, rather than assigning the fault to one regulator alone.

## The aggregate measurement matters

Jaguar's row names the combined current and lists TCM failure as the possible cause. It does not provide a numeric current threshold or direct the reader to replace all three regulators. Treating the code as three independent solenoid failures would add a conclusion the source does not support.

The useful diagnostic distinction is between this combined-current monitor and a separate numbered-regulator circuit code. Companion individual-circuit faults may add context, but this summary does not specify a precedence rule or a complete pinpoint test.

## Monitoring and fallback

The service-monitor description involves shifting through all gears at least twice. On detection, Jaguar specifies fourth-gear mechanical limp-home operation and engine-power limitation. The warning entries are two trips for check engine and an immediate amber gearbox-fault indication.

Professional evaluation must confirm the correct model-year transmission controller and follow its full internal-fault procedure. The source does not give permission to apply external power to the regulators, a valve-body repair instruction or a guaranteed controller replacement remedy. It supplies the group-current identity and the documented response.
