# P1376: Jaguar cylinder-six ignition primary timing error

P1376 is the cylinder-six incorrect-spark-timing entry in Jaguar's 1995 AJ16 ignition diagnostic table. Its manufacturer wording should be preserved instead of assuming that every catalog's “early activation” label is the complete diagnostic definition.

## A narrowly stated cause reference

Jaguar points to P1371, whose possible causes are an ECM-to-ignition-coil primary-circuit short and coil failure. That reference transfers fault mechanisms to cylinder six; it does not redirect testing to cylinder one.

The same page separately lists P1366 as a cylinder-six primary-circuit malfunction. That broader row includes open or resistive wiring and crankshaft-sensor trouble, so the exact code changes what the source says was observed. Do not collapse both entries into a single presumed coil failure.

## What timing does and does not mean here

This is ignition primary-circuit timing. The summary does not identify a valve-timing fault, prescribe timing-chain work or provide an acceptable ignition waveform. Those would be unsupported conclusions from this row alone.

Monitoring is listed after more than one second of engine operation, with two consecutive qualifying trips for the warning. Further diagnosis should use the correct AJ16 circuit and professional ignition-test procedures. Preserve companion codes and distinguish an external short from internal coil trouble before choosing a repair.
