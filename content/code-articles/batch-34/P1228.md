# P1228: Jaguar mechanical-guard sensor high input

P1228 identifies a high-voltage mechanical-guard sensor circuit fault in Jaguar's 1997 AJ26 diagnostic summary. It is the high-side electrical entry in a group that also contains separate low-input and range/performance faults.

## The source narrows the electrical possibilities

Jaguar lists the sensor-to-ECM signal circuit shorted to a higher voltage and mechanical-guard sensor failure. A high reading is therefore not proof that the guard is physically at an extreme position. The sensing circuit may be delivering an invalid representation of that position.

The table does not provide a high-voltage threshold or a sensor replacement test. Use the applicable circuit and signal specifications to distinguish external voltage intrusion from a sensor defect. A generic pedal or throttle-position specification cannot automatically be transferred to this guard sensor.

## Cruise inhibition is a response

Monitoring is described with ignition on for more than five seconds. The first-trip amber warning inhibits cruise control, while the check-engine entry is two trips. This timing matters when the driver reports that cruise operation disappeared before a check-engine warning appeared.

The explanation is limited to the documented AJ26 system. Further testing should preserve the mechanical guard's safety function. A code describing its sensor does not justify bypassing the guard or forcing its actuator to restore throttle response.
