# P1315: Jaguar persistent misfire with an injector switched off

P1315 describes persistent misfire with one cylinder identified and its injector switched off in Jaguar's 1995 AJ16 summary. The injector shutdown is a controller response and must not automatically be mistaken for the original electrical fault.

## A disabled injector can be an effect

The manufacturer says individual cylinder-identification codes P0301 through P0306 accompany P1315. Use those records to establish the affected cylinder. Finding that its injector is not being driven does not, by itself, prove a failed injector driver when the source explicitly describes intentional shutdown.

The referenced causes include fuel contamination, injector flow problems, secondary ignition breakdown, inadequate fuel pressure, compression or valve-spring trouble and relevant sensor or circuit faults. P1315 therefore gives useful response information while leaving the underlying misfire diagnosis open.

## The historical warning exception

The monitoring condition is steady engine operation exceeding two minutes. The 1995 page footnote states that this code does not itself activate the check-engine lamp, despite the row's trip notation. Later applications require their own warning interpretation.

Do not repeatedly run a persistently misfiring engine simply to see whether the injector is re-enabled. Professional diagnosis should connect the companion cylinder evidence to the actual combustion fault and then verify recovery under the applicable procedure. The summary does not prescribe an injector replacement.
