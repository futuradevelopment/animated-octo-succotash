# P1398: Jaguar AJ26 B-bank VVT short-circuit fault

P1398 identifies a short-circuit fault in the B-bank variable valve timing solenoid circuit in Jaguar's 1997 AJ26 summary. Jaguar specifies a short to ground in the ECM-to-solenoid path or solenoid failure as the possibilities.

## Preserve the source's electrical direction

A dictionary may label this a high-input fault, but the manufacturer row describes the short-circuit condition above. Do not turn that catalog wording into a recommendation to search only for battery-voltage intrusion. The exact AJ26 circuit interpretation takes priority for this explanation.

The neighboring P1397 row includes open or resistive wiring and a short to higher voltage. The two entries therefore provide different electrical evidence even though both can stop normal VVT control.

## What happens after detection

Jaguar lists ignition on for more than five seconds as the monitor condition. VVT is inhibited when the check-engine warning is activated on the second qualifying trip. A later observation of no actuator activity may be part of that response and cannot independently prove mechanical seizure.

The summary does not supply a solenoid resistance or a complete pinpoint test. Use the correct bank and workshop wiring information to distinguish an external short from an internal solenoid defect. P1398 alone does not establish an oil-flow problem or justify timing-system disassembly.
