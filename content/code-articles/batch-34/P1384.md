# P1384: Jaguar AJ27 A-bank variable valve timing malfunction

P1384 identifies an A-bank variable valve timing malfunction in Jaguar's 1999 AJ27 summary. The row includes electrical drive faults, oil-flow faults and mechanical faults, so it is broader than a simple solenoid continuity code.

## The commanded fallback changes what data means

Jaguar describes setting the VVT pulse-width command to zero when the check-engine warning is activated on the second qualifying trip. The documented result is the intake camshaft in its fully retarded position. A retarded position observed after the fault may therefore reflect the controller's response, rather than independently proving a seized mechanism.

The cause list includes the PWM drive circuit, the solenoid ground circuit, the solenoid itself, VVT oil flow and VVT/camshaft mechanical failure. Distinguishing a valid command with an incorrect response from an invalid electrical drive is useful diagnostic reasoning based on those alternatives.

## Operating context

The listed monitor uses repeated acceleration and deceleration during a drive. It is not presented as a stationary resistance test. Any recurrence evaluation must follow the appropriate workshop procedure and account for the protective state.

The summary gives no universal oil-pressure value or cam-angle tolerance. Confirm the AJ27 application and bank before further testing. P1384 alone does not identify the solenoid as the replacement part or establish that the timing mechanism requires disassembly.
