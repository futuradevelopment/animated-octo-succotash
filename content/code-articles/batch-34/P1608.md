# P1608: Jaguar ZF 4 HP 24 internal controller timing error

P1608 identifies an internal timing error in the transmission controller in Jaguar's ZF 4 HP 24 E9/AJ16 summary. The timing in this row is controller operation, not engine valve timing or a gear-change duration measurement.

## The warning and fallback are separate facts

Jaguar lists ignition on as the monitoring condition and TCM failure as the possible cause. The row marks transmission-warning activation and limp-home operation, while the check-engine entry is two consecutive trips. The introductory notes explain that transmission warning timing can vary with other fault parameters.

That distinction prevents assuming that the absence of an immediate check-engine lamp means the transmission controller has accepted the fault as harmless. The summary gives no exact fallback gear for this entry, so borrowing a gear from the 5 HP 24 section would add unsupported detail.

## Keep the transmission family attached to the code

The source is specifically the ZF 4 HP 24 E9 system associated with AJ16 applications. The neighboring 4L80-E and later 5 HP 24 summaries use different diagnostic arrangements.

Obtain the appropriate controller test procedure before considering repair or replacement. This table does not provide a software update, circuit pinout or component-level watchdog repair. It supports an internal TCM timing diagnosis that still requires confirmation on the correctly identified vehicle system.
