# P1789: Jaguar 5 HP 24 ignition supply in the low-voltage band

P1789 identifies low ignition-switched supply voltage in Jaguar's 1997 5 HP 24 transmission summary. The published band is above 7 volts and below 9 volts, with a note that at least 7 volts is required for the code to be flagged.

## A missing code does not prove adequate voltage

That recording limitation matters. A more severe supply collapse may prevent this particular event from being recorded normally; absence of P1789 is not a complete charging-system test. The row's monitor condition is engine operation above 1,600 rpm.

Jaguar lists resistance or an intermittent short/open in the ignition-switched supply, intermittent battery failure and intermittent generator failure. The code therefore identifies the supply experienced by the controller, without proving that the battery itself is defective.

## Protective behavior can follow electrical trouble

The source describes fourth-gear mechanical limp-home operation and engine-power limitation when the fault is detected. The amber gearbox-fault indication is immediate, while the check-engine entry is two trips.

Professional diagnosis should distinguish a vehicle-wide supply problem from voltage loss in the controller's switched feed. The documented voltage band is specific to this summary and is not a normal charging target. Establish the actual electrical condition before interpreting protective transmission behavior as evidence of an internal mechanical failure.
