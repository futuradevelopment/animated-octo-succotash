# P1730: Jaguar 5 HP 24 gear-control fault after a ratio warning

P1730 identifies a gear-control malfunction affecting second, third or fourth gear in Jaguar's 1997 5 HP 24 summary. The manufacturer row differs from catalog wording that includes fifth gear, so the documented transmission scope matters.

## P1731 is the earlier stage

Jaguar says P1731 is logged first. Its neighboring row describes leaving the problem gear and attempting the shift again; if the inconsistency remains, P1730 or P1734 is logged. P1730 therefore follows a ratio-related problem that has survived the controller's retry behavior.

Possible causes include low transmission oil, input- or output-speed sensor trouble and mechanical transmission failure. Incorrect speed information can affect the ratio assessment, so the gear-control label alone is not proof of a failed clutch.

## Fifth gear is the fallback, not this row's faulty gear group

The source specifies electronic limp-home operation in fifth gear and engine-power limitation. Confusing that fallback gear with the second/third/fourth diagnostic scope would misread the table.

Monitoring involves repeated shifts through the gears, with two trips for check-engine activation and an immediate gearbox-fault indication. Professional evaluation should retain the earlier P1731 evidence and use the applicable speed-sensor and transmission procedures. The summary does not specify a fluid-filling method, ratio tolerance or internal repair.
