# P1700: Jaguar 5 HP 24 conflicting transmission fallback requests

P1700 means multiple transmission faults require conflicting TCM default actions in Jaguar's 2000-onward 5 HP 24 summary. It is a combined-fault result, so the other recorded codes are central to understanding it.

## Follow the underlying faults

Jaguar's possible-cause entry is that other DTCs are flagged. P1700 therefore does not name a single valve, sensor or clutch to replace. The next useful distinction is which individual faults requested incompatible responses and whether those faults share an underlying electrical or mechanical cause.

The source describes a fourth-gear mechanical limp-home state and engine-power limitation when the fault is detected. A catalog description suggesting that the transmission necessarily fails to neutral is not the behavior documented in this particular manufacturer row.

## Warning timing and application

The monitoring condition is driving the vehicle. The check-engine entry is two consecutive trips, while the amber gearbox-fault indication accompanies fault detection. The presence of a protective gear does not establish that continued driving is suitable.

Preserve the complete TCM record before clearing anything, because removing the companion faults discards the evidence needed to interpret this combined condition. Professional diagnosis must use the 5 HP 24 system and model-year scope. The summary is not a repair sequence and does not identify which of the conflicting faults should be repaired first.
