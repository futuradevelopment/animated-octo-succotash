# P1179: Jaguar AJ16 rich air-flow adaptation

P1179 describes rich air-flow adaptation in the 1995 Jaguar AJ16 diagnostic summary. The table's focus on idle pressure and airflow measurement gives this fault a narrower meaning than a generic “running rich” label.

## Evidence that separates the possibilities

Jaguar lists high fuel pressure at idle and an airflow-sensor signal biased high. It also lists an incorrect airflow sensor for the naturally aspirated engine. Confirming the installed sensor's suitability is therefore relevant evidence, especially after earlier parts work; the code itself is not proof that the existing sensor has failed electrically.

This is the air-flow adaptation row. P1177 is the separate fuel-flow adaptation fault, whose list of possibilities and monitoring conditions should not simply be substituted for P1179.

## Recheck the same operating behavior

The source specifies a normally warm engine, an idle period over three minutes, steady operation above 20 mph for over three minutes, and another idle period over three minutes. Two qualifying consecutive trips are listed for the warning. A short post-repair start does not cover that sequence.

The practical diagnostic question is whether idle fuel delivery is excessive or airflow information is causing an incorrect correction. Professional measurement under the applicable service procedure is needed to resolve that question. This historical AJ16 definition should not be transferred to another manufacturer's P1179 entry.
