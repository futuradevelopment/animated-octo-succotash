# P1748: Jaguar 5 HP 24 pressure-regulator-five circuit fault

P1748 is the pressure-regulator-five circuit malfunction in Jaguar's 1997 5 HP 24 diagnostic summary. It identifies a specific electrical control path rather than a general statement that transmission pressure is low.

## External and internal wiring remain separate possibilities

Jaguar lists an open or short between the transmission and TCM, an open or short in the transmission's internal regulator-five wiring, and regulator-five failure. The code alone cannot tell which of those locations contains the defect.

This matters before authorizing internal transmission work. A malfunction in the external path can produce the same circuit classification, while a normal external connection does not establish the condition of the internal harness or regulator. Use direct circuit evidence to distinguish them.

## What the protective response means

The monitor condition is ignition on. The source describes fourth-gear mechanical limp-home operation and engine-power limitation on detection. The amber gearbox-fault indication appears immediately; the check-engine entry is two qualifying trips.

The fallback is not a measurement of regulator-five hydraulic output. Neither does the absence of a road test prevent the listed electrical monitor from detecting a problem. Professional diagnosis should use the correct regulator identification and 5 HP 24 specifications. The summary supplies no valve-body disassembly steps or universal resistance threshold.
