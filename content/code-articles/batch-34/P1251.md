# P1251: Jaguar AJ26 throttle-position check after engine shutdown

P1251 identifies a throttle-position malfunction during an engine-off check in Jaguar's 1997 AJ26 diagnostic summary. Its timing is part of the meaning and can be lost in a scanner's short “throttle position” label.

## The source describes a shutdown observation

The listed sequence includes driving, decelerating to a stop, switching the ignition off for more than five seconds with the accelerator released, and then switching it on. A normal position reading during a brief running-engine inspection does not cover that complete condition.

Jaguar includes open or resistive position-sensor wiring and feedback circuits shorted to higher voltage. It also includes sensor failure, motor disconnection, open or shorted motor-drive wiring, motor failure and throttle assembly failure. Both feedback and actuation therefore matter to the position check.

## No lamp does not erase the diagnostic evidence

The row lists no check-engine warning, other warning or default action. A stored P1251 can still direct a technician toward the documented engine-off check; lamp behavior alone is not a substitute for understanding the recorded code.

Professional diagnosis should distinguish inaccurate feedback from an actuator that did not behave as expected. The summary provides no universal throttle-angle limit and no instruction to force the plate or motor. Confirm the correct AJ26 system before using these historical monitoring details.
