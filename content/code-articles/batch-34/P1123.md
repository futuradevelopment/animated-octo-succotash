# P1123: Jaguar pedal sensor A high signal

P1123 identifies excessive voltage on pedal-position circuit A in Jaguar's 1997 AJ26 engine-management summary. It concerns the accelerator demand signal, rather than a command to replace the complete throttle assembly.

## What separates this from P1122

The high-side row lists a signal wire shorted to a higher voltage and pedal sensor failure. The neighboring low-side diagnosis includes interrupted signal or supply paths, so the two codes should not be treated as interchangeable evidence. Establish whether the signal is actually high with the pedal at rest or whether the stored event was intermittent.

Jaguar specifies ignition on for more than five seconds as the monitoring condition. The table associates this row with an amber warning and message, but lists no independent default action. That describes this particular fault's entry; combined faults may require a different diagnostic interpretation.

## Interpreting a high reading

A consistently high pedal signal does not establish that the driver is pressing the pedal. Electrical contamination from another circuit can produce a misleading demand value. Confirm the A circuit and its wiring against the exact vehicle diagram before evaluating the sensor itself. The source does not provide a universal voltage limit or authorize bypassing the pedal's redundant safety checks. Keep this explanation confined to the documented AJ26 application.
