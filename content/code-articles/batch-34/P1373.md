# P1373: Jaguar cylinder-three ignition timing fault in the primary circuit

P1373 identifies incorrect spark timing associated with cylinder three's ignition primary circuit in Jaguar's 1995 AJ16 summary. A catalog may call it early activation, but the manufacturer row uses the broader incorrect-timing description.

## Timing evidence differs from missing activation

Jaguar directs this entry to P1371's causes: a short in the ECM-to-coil primary circuit or ignition-coil failure. The nearby P1363 primary-malfunction row has additional causes, including interrupted wiring and crank-sensor trouble. The two lists should not be silently combined into one diagnosis.

The source therefore supports investigating an incorrectly timed primary event. It does not establish a mechanical camshaft-timing problem merely because the word “timing” appears in the description. The monitored circuit and the cylinder identity remain essential.

## How to retain the useful distinction

Record whether the actual stored code is P1373 or P1363 and whether both are present. A generic ignition-fault label loses information that could affect the next electrical check. The summary itself provides no waveform or numerical timing tolerance.

The row lists more than one second of engine running and two consecutive qualifying trips for the check-engine warning. Use the exact AJ16 workshop tests to separate wiring and coil defects. Professional ignition equipment is needed; the code alone does not prove that cylinder three's coil must be replaced.
