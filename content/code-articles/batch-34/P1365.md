# P1365: Jaguar AJ16 cylinder-five ignition activation fault

P1365 concerns the cylinder-five ignition primary circuit in Jaguar's 1995 AJ16 diagnostic summary. The manufacturer's description is a primary-circuit malfunction, rather than proof of a worn spark plug or an internally failed coil.

## Follow the correct cause reference

Jaguar refers this row to P1361, which includes open or high-resistance wiring between the ECM and coil, a short to ground, crankshaft-sensor malfunction and coil failure. The cylinder-five identity remains unchanged when consulting the shared reference.

This gives two useful distinctions. First, the primary circuit is the control side of ignition, so a general combustion complaint does not establish its electrical condition. Second, a crank-position fault can affect the information used for ignition operation, making companion P0335 or P0336 evidence relevant before assigning blame to one coil.

## Preserve the observed fault type

P1375 is the separate timing-related entry for cylinder five in the same table. A scanner's abbreviated “ignition fault” label can hide that distinction. Record the full code and the controller that reported it.

The summary lists engine operation exceeding one second and two consecutive qualifying trips for warning activation. It does not provide primary-resistance limits or a parts-substitution procedure. Professional circuit diagnosis should establish whether the defect is in wiring, timing information or the coil before repair selection.
