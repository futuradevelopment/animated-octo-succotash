# P1205: Jaguar AJ16 number-five injector open or short

P1205 identifies the number-five injector circuit in Jaguar's 1995 AJ16 open-or-short fault group. Its shared cause reference should be read together with its own cylinder assignment and monitor condition.

## What the manufacturer reference actually transfers

Jaguar refers the reader to P0201 for possible causes: injector disconnection, an open or shorted harness, or injector failure. It transfers those mechanisms, not cylinder one's identity. The circuit that requires evaluation for P1205 remains the cylinder-five injector circuit.

This also limits the diagnosis. The summary does not specify whether an open is internal to the injector or external in a connection, and it does not identify a particular shorted wire. A replacement decision needs circuit evidence beyond the code description.

## Running and shutdown are different observations

The P1205 condition is engine operation followed by ignition off for more than two seconds. The companion running-engine injector entry, P0205, uses a different window. A normal observation while the engine is idling does not by itself eliminate a condition detected after shutdown.

Two consecutive qualifying trips are listed for the warning. Confirm the exact AJ16 application and cylinder numbering before further electrical testing. This code is not evidence of fuel leakage, and the summary supplies no injector-resistance limit or permission to open the fuel system without the relevant professional procedure.
