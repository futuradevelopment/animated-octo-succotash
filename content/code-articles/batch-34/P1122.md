# P1122: Jaguar pedal sensor A low signal

P1122 means the accelerator pedal position sensor's A circuit has a low-voltage fault in the 1997 Jaguar AJ26 diagnostic summary. The named circuit is pedal demand, which is distinct from the throttle plate position feedback.

## Low does not automatically mean a short

Jaguar includes an open or high-resistance sensor-to-ECM signal wire and a sensor supply fault among the possibilities. A low reading can therefore result from a circuit that cannot provide a valid signal, rather than only from a wire touching ground. Sensor failure remains another possibility, but the code does not distinguish those causes by itself.

The table describes checking after the ignition has been on for more than five seconds. It lists an amber warning/message response while showing no separate default action for this individual row. That limited statement should not be expanded into a promise of normal throttle operation if additional pedal or throttle faults are present.

## Keep the two positions separate

Record the A-channel signal and compare its behavior with the other available pedal channel using the correct diagnostic information. A throttle-angle reading alone does not establish that the pedal signal reaches the ECM. Use the AJ26 circuit identification when checking the supply and signal path; later Jaguar systems can organize pedal faults differently.
