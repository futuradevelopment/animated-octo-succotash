# P1198: Jaguar V12 fuel-level signal high

P1198 identifies a high-voltage fuel-level sensing fault in Jaguar's V12 Nippondenso engine-management summary. It describes the electrical information reaching the controller, not a measurement proving that the fuel tank is full.

## An open wire can produce the high classification

Jaguar lists an open sensing wire, a sensing wire shorted to battery voltage and fuel-level sensor failure. The open-circuit possibility matters because a high-input code does not invariably mean the sender is physically stuck at its maximum position. Establish whether the controller receives a valid signal before interpreting that signal as fuel quantity.

The neighboring P1199 row describes the low-voltage side and specifically includes a short to ground. Those two diagnostic paths should remain separate even if a general scanner groups them under one fuel-level description.

## Timing and application

The published monitoring condition is ignition on for more than one minute, with a one-trip warning entry. A brief key-on inspection does not cover that full interval.

Confirm the exact V12 system and its wiring before diagnosing the sender. The source does not provide a universal sender resistance, tank access procedure or replacement instruction. Fuel-tank access and electrical work around fuel vapors belong under the applicable professional workshop procedure. The same code on another Jaguar engine may require different information.
