# P1221: Jaguar AJ26 pedal sensor B plausibility fault

P1221 identifies a range or performance problem in pedal-position circuit B on the 1997 Jaguar AJ26 system. Its scope is the pedal demand signal, not the throttle plate's position sensor.

## More than a high-or-low label

Jaguar lists open, shorted or resistive signal wiring, a sensor supply fault, a reference-ground fault and pedal sensor failure. Unlike a simple high-side or low-side description, this range/performance entry does not isolate one voltage direction. The supply and ground context is therefore important when deciding whether the B signal is credible.

The source identifies circuit B separately from circuit A. Comparing the correct channels can be useful, but the summary does not provide a universal ratio between them or an acceptable voltage range. Those specifications must come from the applicable workshop information.

## Warning and monitoring context

The published condition is ignition on for more than five seconds. The row associates the fault with an amber warning and message, while listing no independent default action. That statement should not be extended to a vehicle with several simultaneous throttle or pedal faults.

Confirm that the scanner has retained the B-channel identity before evaluating wiring. A throttle-angle reading cannot substitute for evidence that the pedal-demand signal is valid. Use the exact AJ26 circuit information rather than applying a later Jaguar pedal definition by code number alone.
