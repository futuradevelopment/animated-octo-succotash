# P1203: Jaguar AJ16 cylinder-three injector circuit versus fuel flow

P1203 identifies an open or short in the cylinder-three injector circuit in Jaguar's 1995 AJ16 summary. It is more specific about the electrical circuit than a general misfire report.

## Do not confuse an electrical fault with a blocked nozzle

The referenced P0201 possibilities are a disconnected injector, open or shorted harness wiring and injector failure. The separate misfire table includes blocked or leaking injectors among a wider range of combustion causes. That difference is useful: evidence supporting a fuel-flow problem is not automatically evidence of an open injector circuit.

If cylinder-three misfire information accompanies P1203, the injector-circuit fault provides an additional lead, not a completed explanation of every symptom. The source expressly allows injector-circuit faults to appear alongside misfire codes.

## When Jaguar evaluates this entry

The P1203 monitoring condition includes running the engine followed by ignition off for more than two seconds. Two consecutive qualifying trips are listed for warning activation. This post-running interval differs from the ordinary running-engine injector entries in the same summary.

Confirm cylinder numbering and the correct circuit before testing. The manufacturer table does not give a universal resistance specification or identify whether the harness or injector is defective. A professional electrical diagnosis should establish that distinction before any fuel-system disassembly is considered.
