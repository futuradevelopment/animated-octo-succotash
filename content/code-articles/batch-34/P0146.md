# P0146: Volkswagen sensor-three no-activity monitor

P0146 identifies no detected activity from bank-one oxygen sensor three in Volkswagen's 2014 CBTA/CBUA chart. The source uses more than a momentary mid-range voltage to describe this condition.

## The observation has time and load context

Volkswagen lists a signal remaining between 0.40 and 0.60 volts for more than three seconds. The same row also includes a load-pulse voltage difference of at least 2.80 volts and an internal-resistance condition above 40 kilohms with exhaust temperature above 670°C.

These are monitor observations from the manufacturer table, not instructions to externally load or heat a sensor. They show why a single normal-looking voltage snapshot cannot reproduce the complete no-activity assessment.

## Distinguish no activity from heater coupling

The nearby P0142 row relates a signal step to heater switching, while P0146 concerns the inactive-signal classification. Different codes can therefore point to different behavior even though they name the same sensor location.
