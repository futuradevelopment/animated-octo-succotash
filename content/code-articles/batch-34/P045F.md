# P045F: Volkswagen CJAA low-pressure EGR stuck-closed monitor

P045F identifies the low-pressure EGR stuck-closed condition in Volkswagen's 2014 CJAA chart. The detailed row compares the requested EGR position with the position reported by the sensor.

## Commanded and actual position must be distinguished

Volkswagen lists a closed-state position below 12 percent in this comparison. The value is part of the documented monitor, not a universal normal EGR opening or an instruction to adjust the valve to that position.

A closed valve is not inherently faulty when closure is requested. The diagnostic meaning comes from the comparison with desired position. That is why the actual position alone cannot establish the stuck-closed condition without command context.

## Separate position failure from electrical range faults

The CJAA chart also contains distinct actuator and position-sensor circuit entries. P045F's performance result does not, by itself, prove an open wire or identify a mechanically seized valve. The reported position, actuator response and circuit evidence need to be interpreted together under the full workshop procedure.

The source identifies the low-pressure EGR path, so do not transfer this row directly to the separate high-pressure EGR actuator. It provides no cleaning procedure, actuator travel limit or replacement recommendation. Professional diagnosis should determine why desired and actual position disagree before selecting a repair.
