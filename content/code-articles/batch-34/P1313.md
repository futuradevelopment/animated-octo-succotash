# P1313: Jaguar AJ16 catalyst-risk misfire in cylinders one through three

P1313 reports a catalyst-damaging misfire classification for cylinders one through three in Jaguar's 1995 AJ16 summary. Jaguar calls this cylinder group A bank; the definition is specific to this engine-management system.

## Read the model-year footnote

Although the row contains a one-trip entry, the page explicitly says P1313 does not itself activate the check-engine lamp on 1995 vehicles. It also says associated random or individual-cylinder misfire codes will be recorded. Ignoring that footnote could produce an incorrect expectation about warning behavior.

The monitor condition is steady engine operation exceeding two minutes. The referenced misfire causes include contaminated fuel, blocked or leaking injectors, secondary ignition problems, low fuel pressure, compression loss, broken valve springs and crank-sensor or injector/coil circuit faults.

## Catalyst risk does not identify a failed catalyst

The code classifies the consequence of misfire. It does not prove that the catalyst is already defective or that replacing it will stop the misfire. Companion cylinder and circuit codes help distinguish the combustion fault from its potential exhaust-system effects.

Active misfire or rough running warrants professional assessment rather than deliberate continued running to reproduce a test. Preserve the companion codes and determine the combustion cause. This summary supplies neither a safe-driving judgment nor a single-component repair instruction.
