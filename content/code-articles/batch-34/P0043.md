# P0043: Volkswagen CBUA sensor-three heater voltage low

P0043 is the low-voltage classification for bank-one, sensor-three heater control in Volkswagen's 2014 CBUA chart. The applicable row lists heater voltage below 2.34 volts.

## Keep sensor position and circuit function together

The source explicitly marks this heater group CBUA only. It concerns the third oxygen sensor's heater, not the second sensor and not the third sensor's exhaust-mixture signal. That distinction matters because abbreviated scanner descriptions and other sections of the book can omit or confuse the location.

The adjacent P0042 entry occupies a different diagnostic voltage band, while P0044 represents the high-side classification above 3.59 volts. Those boundaries explain how this chart separates the recorded electrical conditions; they are not instructions to apply a particular voltage to the sensor.

## What the code leaves unresolved

A low monitored heater voltage does not identify the defective component. The quick-reference chart supplies neither a wiring fault tree nor an element-resistance limit, so it cannot decide between wiring, heater and control-side trouble on its own.

Use the exact CBUA circuit and workshop procedure to evaluate the recorded low condition. A mixture signal that appears plausible does not establish that its heater control is healthy. Conversely, P0043 alone is not evidence that the exhaust mixture is lean.
