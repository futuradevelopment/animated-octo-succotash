# U0298: A lost converter message does not by itself prove converter failure

Mazda U0298:00 in this PCM chapter identifies a communication error with the M Hybrid DC-DC converter lasting at least five seconds. The ignition must already have been on for 1.5 seconds. These enabling and timing details distinguish a monitored communication failure from an isolated momentary scan-tool connection issue.

## Operating history changes the documented response

The fault inhibits i-stop engine stopping. Before hybrid operation, the source describes the 12-volt battery supplying electrical devices. During or after hybrid operation, it describes inhibition of motor assistance. The converter communication record can therefore be relevant to several unavailable functions without proving several separate failures.

The diagnostic path names the sub relay and IG1 relay No.2, then the converter connector and power supply before the converter inspection. This is evidence that loss of required supply must be distinguished from an internal converter problem.

## A source limitation worth retaining

The page's headings and cause list use inconsistent bus terminology: the outline says CAN, while one cause entry says LIN. This article consequently does not assign a wiring topology from that inconsistency. The applicable vehicle wiring information must settle the physical communication path.

The verified facts are the communicating modules, duration, operating responses and diagnostic sequence. A qualified hybrid technician should preserve related PCM codes and event data, establish supply and communication evidence through the correct procedure, and verify recurrence. No energized converter probing or internal repair is supported by this overview.

Scope: Mazda3 01/2019 onward manual d9n1-1a-22d_ver10, in the controller and equipment variant named by the source. Confirm vehicle applicability before using its diagnostic path.
