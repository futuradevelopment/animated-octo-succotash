# B0079: The driver belt retractor has a monitored deployment circuit

The generic B0079 family is driver seat-belt pretensioner B deployment control. SAIC's SDM procedure identifies the driver pretensioner retractor. It separates shorts to ground (:11) or battery (:12), low (:1A) or high (:1B) deployment resistance, and incompatible configuration (:56).

## Mechanical belt movement is not the monitored quantity

A belt can pull out and retract while its pyrotechnic deployment circuit has an electrical fault. The code concerns that monitored circuit, not merely whether the belt feels smooth in ordinary use. Conversely, it does not prove that the retractor assembly is defective before wiring evidence is assessed.

For the electrical branch, the source starts with the SDM's reported driver-pretensioner circuit-resistance data. An abnormal result leads toward harness and connection assessment; the procedure retains a separate SDM branch. This diagnostic data is not an invitation to measure a pretensioner directly with a meter.

## Configuration has a different meaning

The :56 variant concerns whether the configured deployment circuit matches what is detected after power-on, with a six-second identification window. A configuration record should not be treated as interchangeable with an ordinary resistance fault.

The source illuminates the restraint warning and distinguishes current recovery from forty-cycle history aging. A qualified SRS technician must establish the correct component configuration and complete verification. Do not probe, bypass or disassemble the retractor from this overview; a cleared warning alone does not establish a repaired deployment path.

This interpretation is limited to the cited MG4 UK manual revision; it does not establish applicability to every MG4 build or another manufacturer.
