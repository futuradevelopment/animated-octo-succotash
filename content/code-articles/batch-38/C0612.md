# C0612: The right parking-brake motor supply-short monitor

In the cited SAIC IBS procedure, C0612:12 concerns the right parking-brake actuator's motor line being shorted to battery supply. The monitor is enabled when the parking brake is applied or released and includes a regular passive-test assessment.

## A circuit-high code is not a mechanical diagnosis

The detected condition is electrical. The source does not say that every C0612 means the right brake is seized or that the motor is internally shorted. Its cause list includes the related harness and connections, the actuator motor and the IBS controller.

That distinction is useful when reviewing a repair proposal. A complete diagnosis should show why the fault was assigned to the motor or circuit rather than merely repeating the code's component name. The right-hand location also matters; the manual documents a separate left-side code.

## Recovery differs from initial detection

The source describes normal actuator operation as part of recovery and a 40-failure-free-working-cycle history rule for the right-side group. Historical aging is not an instruction to keep operating a faulty parking brake until a code disappears.

The associated diagnostic procedure checks circuits and the motor before the software and IBS branches. These are workshop activities requiring the complete brake safety instructions. This article supplies no direct-power test, forced release or connector measurement. Verification should establish that the monitored electrical condition is absent and the required actuator behavior is normal under the approved procedure.

This interpretation is limited to the cited MG4 UK manual revision; it does not establish applicability to every MG4 build or another manufacturer.
