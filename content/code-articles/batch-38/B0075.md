# B0075: The right-rear pretensioner code is independent of buckle sensing

In SAIC's SDM procedure, B0075 is the second-row right seat-belt pretensioner retractor. It is a deployment-control fault family, distinct from a buckle switch that reports whether the passenger fastened the belt.

## Electrical and configuration variants need different interpretation

The source assigns :11 to a ground short, :12 to a supply short, :1A to low resistance, :1B to high resistance and :56 to incompatible configuration. The configuration check compares the expected deployment circuit with what is detected within six seconds after power-on.

For the resistance-related diagnosis, the procedure first reads the SDM's parameter for the second-row right pretensioner. A normal reported value and an abnormal one lead to different branches. This makes the controller's complete diagnostic evidence more informative than testing ordinary belt movement.

## Do not skip wiring because the title names a retractor

The source assesses the appropriate harness and connections before the retractor and the SDM. All remain possible causes until the procedure establishes the defect. The rear-right location should be preserved in the scan and repair record so evidence from another belt is not substituted.

Current recovery and forty-failure-free-cycle historical clearing are separate. A warning that disappears after a reset does not prove the original fault was corrected. A qualified SRS technician must perform the prescribed safety and completion procedures. The article does not authorize probing, opening or bypassing a pyrotechnic retractor.

This interpretation is limited to the cited MG4 UK manual revision; it does not establish applicability to every MG4 build or another manufacturer.
