# B0028: The right seat-side airbag has circuit and configuration checks

SAIC's B0028 page concerns the right seat-side airbag, described as mounted beneath the seat upholstery on the outside of the seat structure. The SDM monitors its deployment path for ground short, battery short, low resistance, high resistance and incompatible configuration.

## Separate the seat airbag from the curtain airbag

This component is the right seat-side airbag, not the overhead curtain. A general right-side restraint complaint should not cause their codes or service procedures to be interchanged. The :11, :12, :1A, :1B and :56 suffixes provide the next distinction within B0028 itself.

The source's electrical criteria assume normal system voltage and a normally functioning SDM. Its configuration check uses a six-second power-on identification window. The presence or absence of expected circuitry can therefore matter after seat or restraint work, but the code alone does not establish that such work caused the fault.

## Diagnosis remains a controlled SRS task

The manual evaluates the SDM-to-seat-airbag harness and connections before the airbag and SDM. Its resistance criteria describe the controller's assessment, not a safe meter test at the airbag connector. The table gives no normal clearing condition for the :56 variant.

A qualified technician should retain the exact suffix and verify both the identified defect and complete restraint-system status. This article provides no upholstery removal, connector probing or deployment-circuit bypass instructions. Normal seat adjustment or an extinguished lamp is not sufficient evidence that the monitored deployment path is correct.

This interpretation is limited to the cited MG4 UK manual revision; it does not establish applicability to every MG4 build or another manufacturer.
