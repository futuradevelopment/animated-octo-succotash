# B0054: Understanding the rear-center buckle circuit fault

B0054 represents the second-row-center seat-belt sensor family. The cited Mazda BCM procedure defines B0054:11 as a rear-center buckle-switch circuit malfunction. Its detection belongs to the electrical supply unit, which recognizes a short to ground on two consecutive occasions.

## The seating position is diagnostic information

The center input must not be substituted with either outboard rear buckle circuit. They have separate code identities even though the same ESU participates in their monitoring. A repair record should retain the exact seating position rather than saying only that a rear seat-belt sensor failed.

Mazda states the switch is removed during the relevant detection setup. This condition belongs to the workshop procedure and does not describe ordinary belt use. A reminder sounding with a seated passenger is consequently not enough to verify the electrical fault specified by :11.

## What must be separated

The procedure considers the ESU connector and a signal short before assessing the ESU by recurrence. The BCM is another possible cause in the later verification path. The source does not make the buckle mechanism the default replacement.

A qualified technician should preserve the complete scan, establish whether the code repeats under the correct diagnostic conditions and follow any required network assessment. Comparing other buckle-related codes can help describe the case, but this page does not prove a shared defect from simultaneous codes. Seat-belt components and adjacent restraint wiring must remain within the applicable factory handling procedure.

Scope: Mazda3 01/2019 onward manual d9n1-1a-22d_ver10, in the controller and equipment variant named by the source. Confirm vehicle applicability before using its diagnostic path.
