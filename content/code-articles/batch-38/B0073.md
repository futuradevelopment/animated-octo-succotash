# B0073: The second-row left pretensioner needs its own deployment-circuit diagnosis

SAIC's B0073 procedure identifies the second-row left pretensioner retractor. The SDM distinguishes a ground short (:11), battery short (:12), low resistance (:1A), high resistance (:1B) and incompatible configuration (:56). This code concerns deployment control, not the rear buckle reminder switch.

## Retain the seat position and the failure type

The left-rear retractor is a specific circuit. A front-belt or right-rear diagnostic result cannot substitute for it, even when similar codes share a common procedure. The source begins by reading the SDM's reported resistance information for this exact pretensioner.

That scan-data decision separates an abnormal deployment-loop finding from the later control-module branch. The source then considers the corresponding harness and connection before the retractor itself. It does not make retractor replacement the first conclusion from B0073.

## Why configuration can appear after component work

The :56 monitor compares configured equipment with the circuit detected in the first six seconds after power-on. The configuration must be correct for recovery; ordinary code clearing is not a substitute for establishing matching hardware and data.

The manual separates restoration of a normal circuit from historical clearing after forty failure-free power cycles. These are controller-status rules, not instructions to operate with an unresolved warning. A qualified restraint technician must follow the complete SRS procedure. Do not probe or bypass the pretensioner, and do not infer pyrotechnic readiness from normal mechanical belt retraction.

This interpretation is limited to the cited MG4 UK manual revision; it does not establish applicability to every MG4 build or another manufacturer.
