# C0040: The two brake-switch signals behind Mazda's plausibility check

Mazda C0040:64 is a plausibility fault for the brake switch in the DSC hydraulic unit/control module. The source distinguishes brake-switch No.1, received from the electrical supply unit, from No.2, received from the PCM through the body controller over CAN. The two paths should not be treated as interchangeable scan data.

## What makes the signal implausible

The monitor can detect a No.1 signal remaining on during an extended period above its speed threshold. It can also detect a signal that never turns on despite repeated decelerations, or that remains off when brake-fluid pressure reaches the specified level. These are relationships between signals and vehicle behavior, rather than proof of a broken switch contact from one static reading.

## A useful first distinction

The diagnostic path begins with the electrical supply unit's BRK_SW data. Whether that value changes correctly separates the switch-input side of the system from the later signal path to the DSC controller. Connector, supply and signal-circuit faults are among the supported possibilities; the switch itself is only one candidate.

A brake lamp that appears to work cannot by itself prove that every signal path used by this monitor is correct. Conversely, replacing the DSC unit before checking the upstream data would omit Mazda's first diagnostic decision. A qualified technician should perform the specified checks and controlled verification. The driving conditions in the factory monitor are not a recommendation to attempt a public-road fault-reproduction exercise.
