# U0305: Checksum and counter errors behind Mazda's diesel U0305

The generic U0305 title mentions cruise-control software incompatibility. Mazda's SKYACTIV-D page identifies incompatibility with the vehicle control module, or VCM, through checksum or message-counter errors in its CAN signals to the PCM. This is a defined message-integrity finding rather than an assertion that a cruise switch has failed.

## The monitor needs an enabled observation window

Mazda specifies ignition on and battery voltage of at least ten volts sustained for 1.5 seconds as preconditions. The malfunction determination period is five seconds, with a one-cycle diagnostic entry. Those conditions explain why the status of a previously stored fault and its current repeatability are separate questions.

A checksum error and a counter error describe received message content or progression. They should not automatically be rewritten as complete absence of communication. The physical line, module connections and either controller remain supported possibilities.

## Follow the evidence rather than the label

The workshop sequence retains event data, considers related service information and checks other PCM DTCs. The troubleshooting section then focuses on VCM and PCM connections before the prescribed repeatability and CAN assessment.

No universal software update or cruise-control replacement is specified by this page. The exact diesel controller, message-integrity finding and full code must guide diagnosis. The source supports a narrowly scoped explanation of a monitored data error; repair decisions still depend on the vehicle-specific network procedure and successful completion checks.
