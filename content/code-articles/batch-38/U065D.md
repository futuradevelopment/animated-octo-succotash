# U065D: A fresh-air duct communication fault on SKYACTIV-X

Mazda's SKYACTIV-X PCM procedure assigns U065D:00 to a sustained communication problem with the fresh-air duct valve position sensor. The fault requires an error lasting at least five seconds with battery voltage at or above eight volts. The code's general air-intake-actuator name therefore needs this application-specific sensor context.

## Power loss can look like lost communication

The source considers the fresh-air duct actuator connection, F12 supply protection, sensor supply and ground, the communication path, and the PCM connection. The component inspection comes after these checks. A missing message is consequently not proof that the valve mechanism is physically stuck.

The page also directs attention to other related PCM faults and to the stored event information. Those records can help determine whether this was an isolated communication problem or part of a broader electrical event.

## Do not infer a bus from inconsistent wording

The retrieved procedure calls the outline SENT communication, but other passages use CAN and LIN terminology. That inconsistency prevents a reliable bus-topology conclusion from this page alone. The exact vehicle wiring diagram and current Mazda service information must identify the physical signal before electrical testing.

The verified scope remains the fresh-air duct position-sensor communication monitor, its five-second duration and its supply-related diagnostic branches. This article does not turn the inconsistent labels into pin tests or a sensor replacement rule. A technician should resolve the documentation detail and perform the prescribed recurrence check before concluding that the sensor or PCM is defective.
