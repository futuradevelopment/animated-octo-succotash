# C060D: The left parking-brake motor circuit is being checked for a supply short

SAIC's IBS table defines C060D:12 as a left-actuator motor-line short to battery. The generic C060D title calls the circuit high. The important application detail is the left parking-brake actuator, with monitoring enabled when the electronic parking brake is applied or released.

## The detection paragraph is broader than the row title

The setting description says a regular passive test can identify a motor power-line short to ground or to KL30 supply. This wording is broader than the table's short-to-battery label. It would therefore be inaccurate to infer a measured high-voltage value or a specific pin solely from the code.

The source groups the code with other actuator faults and lists harness, motor and IBS causes. It does not establish that the left caliper is mechanically seized. An electrical circuit finding and a failure to move require separate evidence.

## What should narrow the repair decision

The shared diagnostic procedure evaluates circuit condition and the motor before software and IBS assessment. For this left-side variant, the recovery description involves an external ignition or diagnostic trigger rather than the right-side group's normal-operation history rule.

A qualified technician should preserve the :12 suffix and source wording, then follow the actual vehicle wiring and brake procedure. Manually supplying power, bridging connections or attempting a release is outside this explanation. Completion must confirm that the original monitored fault has been resolved, not merely that the warning was cleared.

This interpretation is limited to the cited MG4 UK manual revision; it does not establish applicability to every MG4 build or another manufacturer.
