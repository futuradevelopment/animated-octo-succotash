# U300E: When local ignition power disagrees with the BCM's message

Mazda's dash electrical supply unit stores U300E:13 when its local IG1 supply appears off while the BCM's CAN message says IG1 is on. The monitored condition is a disagreement between physical power and communicated state, not simply an ignition switch left in the wrong position.

## Two observations must be kept separate

A BCM reporting ignition on does not prove that the dash unit actually receives its required supply. Conversely, a missing local feed need not mean the BCM is transmitting false information. This distinction is the reason the source directs attention to the supply path.

The documented possibilities include IG1 relay No.2, the dash-unit connector, F47 protection, an open or ground short in the IG1 feed, and the dash unit itself. The procedure checks the relay and connection before following the fuse and supply-circuit branches.

## Interpreting a repair conclusion

A useful diagnosis should identify which side of the disagreement was abnormal and what evidence established it. Replacing a controller solely because it reports the code omits the external supply causes Mazda explicitly lists.

Completion involves checking whether the pending DTC returns; recurring faults also introduce CAN diagnosis and renewed assessment before a dash-unit replacement decision. This page's component identifiers apply to the documented Mazda3 equipment and must be checked against the vehicle configuration. It does not provide a universal fuse location or authorize bypassing a relay or increasing a fuse rating to restore power.
