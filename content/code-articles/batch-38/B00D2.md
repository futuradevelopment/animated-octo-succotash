# B00D2: The restraint warning indicator can have its own fault

The B00D2 family concerns restraint-system malfunction indicator 1. Mazda's specific B00D2:01 SAS page identifies an airbag-system warning-light malfunction. Its scope is the indication path; it does not identify a failed driver airbag, crash sensor or pretensioner.

## A warning device is also monitored equipment

An indicator is useful only if the system can communicate its condition to the driver. A fault associated with that warning device must therefore be distinguished from the separate restraint faults the device may be intended to display. The absence of a visible warning cannot be used to dismiss an electronically recorded indicator problem.

The source's possible-cause entry is the warning light itself, and its diagnostic instruction directs the workshop to the instrument cluster. It supplies no lamp-driver voltage limit, independent bulb test or deployment-circuit measurement for this code. Those missing details should not be invented from the generic title.

## Confirm the exact variant

This article covers :01 only. The surrounding Mazda manual contains other B00D2 variants, so a scan that omits the suffix is insufficient to apply this page's short procedure. The SAS module identity and the complete restraint-system scan remain important context.

Instrument-cluster work should follow the applicable vehicle procedure, including any required setup and verification. An indicator repair also does not establish that every other restraint fault has been corrected. A qualified technician should evaluate the full system record rather than interpreting a working or extinguished lamp as proof of complete SRS health.

Scope: Mazda3 01/2019 onward manual d9n1-1a-22d_ver10, in the controller and equipment variant named by the source. Confirm vehicle applicability before using its diagnostic path.
