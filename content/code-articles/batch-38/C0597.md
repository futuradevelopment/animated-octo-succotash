# C0597: Unexpected deceleration during adaptive-cruise brake startup

The generic C0597 title is brake-system control-module A performance. SAIC's IBS table calls the :92 variant an adaptive-cruise acceleration/deceleration out-of-range fault. Its setting statement is specifically about vehicle deceleration exceeding a threshold during ACC brake startup.

## A defined operating event matters

The monitor is listed as running once per cycle. It is therefore not described as a continuous electrical open-circuit check. A fault captured during the particular ACC transition cannot be explained adequately by a static sensor voltage alone.

The page does not publish the relevant deceleration threshold for C0597. Adjacent entries contain acceleration and yaw-sensor values, but those belong to different DTCs and should not be borrowed as this monitor's limits.

## Interpret the grouped procedure carefully

The source lists ACC and auto-hold as unavailable for C0597:92. Its broader section discusses integrated inertial sensors, calibration, mounting and IBS assessment, but it does not provide a unique failed-part conclusion for C0597. Those neighboring diagnostic branches must not be represented as proof that a yaw sensor caused this event.

A useful workshop record retains the :92 suffix, captured operating context and associated brake or stability faults. The next-cycle history statement is a status rule, not a cure. A qualified technician should use applicable current service information and controlled verification; this article does not instruct a driver to engage adaptive cruise to recreate an abnormal braking event.

This interpretation is limited to the cited MG4 UK manual revision; it does not establish applicability to every MG4 build or another manufacturer.
