# B0029: Right-curtain airbag faults include both circuit and configuration variants

SAIC's B0029 procedure concerns the right curtain airbag and its body-harness connection to the SDM. The family includes :11 for a ground short, :12 for a battery short, :1A for low resistance, :1B for high resistance and :56 for incompatible configuration.

## A high-resistance record is not the same as incorrect configuration

The configuration logic compares the expected deployment circuit with what the controller detects within six seconds after power-on. Electrical status can influence that comparison, so the presence of :56 does not justify simply changing coding until the warning disappears.

The normal electrical variants instead describe the deployment loop's condition. The manual includes defined uncertain and definite resistance regions, but those are controller diagnostic criteria rather than safe instructions for testing an airbag connector.

## What the diagnostic sequence preserves

The source considers the SDM-to-right-curtain harness and connections before the curtain assembly and SDM. A connector or harness fault therefore remains a supported alternative to an airbag fault. The component's position above the doors does not authorize trim removal without SRS precautions.

The manual gives electrical recovery and historical-aging rules but no ordinary clearing condition for :56 in this table. Do not promise that repeated ignition cycles will resolve a configuration fault. A qualified restraint technician must retain the full suffix, establish the actual cause and verify the complete system under the appropriate factory procedure.

This interpretation is limited to the cited MG4 UK manual revision; it does not establish applicability to every MG4 build or another manufacturer.
