# U0182: The front-lighting module depends on Mazda's equipment variant

Mazda's BCM records U0182:00 when it cannot receive CAN messages from the applicable front-lighting controller. The page names three possibilities: the adaptive front-lighting system controller, the auto-leveling controller or the adaptive LED-headlight controller. These are equipment alternatives, not proof that three modules have failed at once.

## Identify the installed lighting system first

The generic lost-front-lighting-module title cannot establish which of those systems the vehicle has. The exact equipment determines the sender, network relationship and service procedure. Searching for an adaptive LED controller on a differently equipped car would follow the wrong diagnostic target.

The source points to the CAN line between the fitted lighting module and the BCM. It does not provide a bulb failure test or say that a headlamp assembly is necessarily defective. Missing communication is different evidence from a lamp that receives commands but does not illuminate.

## The page's diagnostic boundary

Mazda refers this code to its multiplex communication diagnosis. No connector measurements, timeout duration or universal headlamp repair are supplied on the DTC page. The correct network procedure must establish why the expected messages are missing before component replacement is justified.

Preserve the full :00 code, reporting BCM identity, vehicle lighting specification and other communication records. Those details help a technician determine whether the loss is isolated or part of a broader network problem. They also prevent a front-lighting communication fault from being reduced to a speculative bulb or headlamp purchase.

Scope: Mazda3 01/2019 onward manual d9n1-1a-22d_ver10, in the controller and equipment variant named by the source. Confirm vehicle applicability before using its diagnostic path.
