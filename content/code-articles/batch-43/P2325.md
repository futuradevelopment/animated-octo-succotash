# P2325: Audi’s coil-I circuit-high threshold is expressed as a voltage range

Audi lists P2325 as ignition coil I primary control circuit high in the CEJA W12 chapter. The criterion is signal voltage above a threshold expressed as 5.1 to 7.0 V. The range belongs to the threshold; the document does not define a fixed seven-volt limit for every operating condition.

## Keep the control signal separate from the ignition output

This entry concerns the primary control circuit. Its single-digit voltage figures are not a secondary spark-voltage specification. They also do not establish a plug-gap problem or identify the strength of the spark produced by the coil.

The source omits the conditions selecting a threshold within the stated range, as well as a measurement terminal and persistence time. A technician therefore needs the corresponding workshop test before interpreting an external voltage reading. The summary alone cannot tell the reader which wire or component has caused the high assessment.

## Coil I must be identified for the actual engine

The letter is a circuit label, not a physical location supplied by this page. The same chapter has a broader coil-I circuit entry, P0359, with low signal-current or internal-check criteria. Those findings should not be substituted for P2325’s voltage-based high category.

Verification should confirm the correct channel and its prescribed electrical behavior under the relevant conditions. Replacing a coil based solely on its lettered name, or seeing a normal voltage while the fault is absent, does not by itself explain the recorded high-control event.
