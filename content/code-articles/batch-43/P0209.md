# P0209: Audi flags the cylinder-9 injector circuit through an internal check

Audi places P0209 in the CEJA W12 injector circuit/open group and identifies cylinder 9. Its malfunction criterion is an internal logic failure. That wording matters because the same engine chapter gives numerical current criteria for the neighboring injector circuit-low and circuit-high faults; those limits are not the published trigger for P0209.

## Do not manufacture an open-circuit measurement

The word “open” in the description does not supply a resistance value, connector location or test duration. An internal monitor has reported a circuit fault, but this summary does not identify the exact internal comparison that failed. It therefore cannot establish whether an external conductor, connection, injector or control stage is responsible.

Cylinder-9 P0285 uses current below 2.1 A, while P0286 describes a low-side short to battery positive with current above 14.70 A. These related entries help preserve the distinction between an internal circuit assessment and a specifically categorized current fault. Applying either numerical boundary directly to P0209 would erase that distinction.

## Keep the cylinder identity in the repair evidence

The actual CEJA cylinder numbering and connector assignment must come from matching workshop information. A generic picture of another engine is not adequate identification for cylinder 9. Record the exact DTC and related injector faults before a qualified technician follows the applicable circuit procedure. Verification should address that identified channel and the original monitor event; a normal idle alone does not demonstrate that the reported electrical condition has been resolved.
