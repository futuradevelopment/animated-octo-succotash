# P2333: Audi’s coil-L low category is triggered by a higher signal current

P2333 identifies ignition coil L primary control circuit low in the CEJA W12 reference. Audi specifies signal current above 24.0 mA. This is a useful example of why a DTC’s low/high label and the direction of its numerical monitor quantity must be read separately.

## Keep the greater-than sign intact

The criterion is not current below 24.0 mA. It is also not a published low-voltage boundary. The document does not describe the controller’s internal sensing arrangement, so the current-to-classification relationship should not be embellished with an invented driver-circuit explanation.

The adjacent coil-L high code, P2334, uses signal voltage above a threshold range of 5.1 to 7.0 V. Those two entries concern different quantities. Their figures cannot be combined into a normal current or voltage interval for testing a loose coil.

## The reference identifies a circuit, not a replacement part

The letter L needs to be matched to the actual CEJA circuit diagram. No physical position, connector terminal, persistence interval or direct-activation instruction appears in the summary. Nor is this a criterion for secondary spark energy.

A qualified technician should preserve the exact code and recorded context, then evaluate the appropriate primary-control path using the factory procedure. Verification must address the original current-based finding. Successful starting or an immediate absence of the DTC after clearing memory may leave the relevant monitor condition untested.
