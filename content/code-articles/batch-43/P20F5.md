# P20F5: Audi’s low SCR-adaptation value has conflicting consumption labels

Audi’s Q7 CNRB table places P20F5 at an SCR adaptation value of 0.51 or less and calls the associated condition reductant consumption too low. The generic catalog calls P20F5 consumption too high. That opposed wording is a real source-review issue, not permission to choose whichever direction fits an assumed repair.

## What is supported despite the naming conflict

The CNRB row supplies a numerical adaptation boundary. It does not identify a direct fuel- or reductant-consumption measurement, and it gives no distance, time or volume unit for 0.51. The value should therefore remain an adaptation criterion rather than being translated into a claimed quantity of fluid lost or injected.

The CPNA A8 reference uses high-consumption wording for P20F5 and a strict less-than-0.51 adaptation boundary. Even this apparently small difference matters: “less than” and “at or below” are not identical comparisons. Mixing the two sources would obscure both the label conflict and the exact application criterion.

## Obtain the applicable diagnostic definition

Before selecting a remedy, the technician needs the actual engine calibration and its full current procedure. The summary does not prove a leaking dosing injector, identify contamination, or prescribe a reductant-system adjustment. Those conclusions require additional evidence.

Verification should show that the correct application’s monitored adaptation behavior is understood and satisfactory after the cause is addressed. Erasing the record, replacing fluid or observing different tank usage cannot settle the source discrepancy by itself. The unresolved direction labels remain visible so this source-scoped explanation is not mistaken for a universal high- or low-consumption diagnosis.
