# P2162: Volkswagen compares the two transmission speed paths under load

In Volkswagen’s DQ381 0GC table, P2162 is a rationality check involving the shaft-1 and shaft-2 speed paths. The criterion describes a difference between output-shaft speeds and the input speeds of shafts 1 and 2, with a threshold above 150 rpm. This is a correlation monitor rather than a single-sensor high-voltage check.

## The relationship matters more than one isolated speed

The enabling columns require both input-shaft speeds above 100 rpm and an engaged-gear position other than neutral. A stationary or neutral-state comparison does not reproduce the complete published condition. The exact transmission relationships are needed to interpret which speed should agree with which calculated value.

The table does not supply a universal formula for comparing arbitrary scanner labels. In particular, engine rpm, input-shaft rpm and output-shaft rpm should not be assumed equal simply because all are displayed in revolutions per minute.

## Separate correlation from an absent signal

The same section distinguishes missing input-shaft signals and individual speed range faults. P2162 instead identifies disagreement between related information. A technician should retain those companion records and establish credible inputs before treating the correlation result as mechanical slippage.

Volkswagen lists a two-second monitoring time, continuous checks and two driving cycles for MIL operation. The summary does not isolate a failed sensor or prescribe a transmission replacement. Verification should demonstrate consistent speed relationships in the required engaged, rotating condition. A cleared memory or a normal stationary scan cannot independently satisfy that check.
