# P2831: Volkswagen has lost the position signal for the first/third shift fork

Volkswagen maps shift fork A to the 1–3 gear-shift module in its DQ381-7A/F 0GC transmission table. P2831 records absence of the position signal for more than one second. The code concerns missing feedback, not a direct finding that first or third gear is mechanically jammed.

## Separate missing data from data outside its range

The same channel has a distinct range/performance entry, P2832, evaluating PWM duty cycle and signal period. P2831’s published criterion is instead no signal. A duty-cycle reading cannot be assumed valid if the controller has lost the signal needed to calculate it.

The table lists two seconds as the monitoring-time entry, continuous checking and two driving cycles for MIL operation. The one-second absence boundary and the separate monitoring-time field should both be retained; neither should be silently substituted for the other.

## Use the fork mapping before inspecting components

The A designation refers to the manufacturer’s 1–3 shift module. It does not mean sensor A on every dual-clutch transmission has the same physical location. The exact 0GC wiring and service information are needed to identify the relevant path.

This row does not provide a connector test, adaptation instruction or replacement decision. A qualified diagnosis should establish whether usable position information is available and reconcile related circuit or movement faults. Verification requires restored feedback under the appropriate checks. A successful selection of another gear or a recently cleared DTC does not prove that the first/third position channel has recovered.
