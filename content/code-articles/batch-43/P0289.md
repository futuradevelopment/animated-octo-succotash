# P0289: Audi distinguishes a cylinder-10 injector high-current fault

The CEJA W12 reference gives P0289 two useful pieces of information: cylinder 10 is the affected injector circuit, and the high-fault description involves a short to battery positive on its low side. The accompanying threshold is signal current above 14.70 A.

## Do not substitute a voltage test for the stated current criterion

Although the fault description names battery positive, the numerical monitor quantity in this row is amperes. Treating 14.70 as a voltage threshold would be an incorrect reading of the source. The document also supplies no connector pin at which an equivalent external test could be performed.

P0288 is the related cylinder-10 low-current code, with a boundary below 2.1 A. That difference helps preserve the controller’s classification when reviewing a scan history. The table does not say that one code develops into the other or that their simultaneous presence establishes a specific failed part.

## Use fault isolation before making a repair decision

This brief entry points to an electrical path; it does not distinguish all possible locations within that path. The correct CEJA wiring information and the applicable guided checks are needed to determine how the controller output, connections and injector are evaluated.

No direct battery-feed or injector activation experiment is justified by this reference. Verification belongs within the appropriate professional procedure and must address the original cylinder-10 circuit event. Replacing a fuel-delivery component solely from the word “injector” would skip the electrical finding that makes P0289 specific.
