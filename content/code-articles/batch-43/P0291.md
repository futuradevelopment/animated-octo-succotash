# P0291: Audi checks the cylinder-11 injector for low signal current

Audi’s CEJA chapter lists P0291 for cylinder-11 injector circuit low and specifies signal current below 2.1 A. The cylinder designation and electrical unit are both essential. This row does not establish a fuel-pressure target, injector flow rate or combustion-quality limit.

## Distinguish a low-current finding from an internal circuit failure

The same cylinder has P0211, an injector circuit/open entry defined by an internal logic failure. P0291 provides a more explicit current boundary, but it still does not prove a particular physical break or failed component. The reference does not include a connector-by-connector fault-isolation tree.

The corresponding high entry, P0292, describes current above 14.70 A and a low-side short to battery positive. Those separate classifications should be preserved in the repair record rather than combined into an undifferentiated cylinder-11 injector complaint.

## Use the matching measurement method

A current threshold without the monitor timing and measurement arrangement is not a ready-made test instruction. The quick-reference page does not tell the reader to disconnect the injector, insert a meter or energize it externally. Those actions require the correct workshop procedure and suitable professional equipment.

Diagnosis should establish the actual cylinder-11 circuit and relate available measurements to the stored event. Following repair, the same channel must satisfy the relevant electrical checks and monitor conditions. A smooth-running engine at one moment is useful operating context but does not alone explain why the controller previously recorded insufficient injector-circuit current.
