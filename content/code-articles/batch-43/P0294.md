# P0294: Audi records low current on the cylinder-12 injector channel

P0294 is the CEJA W12 cylinder-12 injector circuit-low entry. Audi supplies a signal-current threshold below 2.1 A. The source therefore supports an electrical-current interpretation; the code title does not establish whether the injector is mechanically restricted or whether rail pressure is adequate.

## Keep the electrical result separate from its possible cause

A circuit can require further fault isolation even when the monitor category is clear. This summary does not provide a terminal location, a winding-resistance specification or a decision that the injector itself must be replaced. Its current boundary belongs to the controller’s monitoring arrangement.

For the same cylinder, P0212 records an internal circuit logic failure, while P0295 describes a low-side short to battery positive with current above 14.70 A. These neighboring entries demonstrate distinct diagnostic classifications. They should not be rewritten as three interchangeable names for a confirmed failed injector.

## Resolve the identified channel with appropriate service information

The physical cylinder-12 location and wiring must be confirmed for the CEJA application. Shared thresholds across the W12 injector table do not allow measurements from another cylinder to stand in for this circuit.

A qualified technician needs the matching test method and operating conditions to compare the stored fault with real electrical behavior. After a repair, verification should establish that cylinder 12 satisfies the relevant circuit checks. A later code-free scan without completion of the applicable monitor may leave the original low-current condition untested.
