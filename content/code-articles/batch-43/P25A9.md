# P25A9: Volkswagen’s piston-cooling open-circuit check runs with the actuator off

Volkswagen associates P25A9 with the piston-cooling oil control circuit and directs diagnosis toward nozzle-control valve N522. The 2021–2022 engine table describes an open-circuit check while the actuator is commanded off. That command state is part of the criterion, not an incidental detail.

## The open classification uses a voltage region

The lower boundary is shown as a calibrated range from 1.92 to 2.21 V, and the upper boundary as 2.85 to 3.25 V. The row uses greater-than-or-equal and less-than-or-equal comparisons respectively. It does not define one universal pair of limits for every calibration.

The table lists a two-second monitoring time, continuous checks and two driving cycles for MIL illumination. These are control-circuit observations, not measurements of oil pressure or nozzle flow.

## Keep the neighboring electrical categories separate

P25AA is a short-to-ground category below 1.85 V with the actuator also commanded off. P25AB instead uses a high signal above 3.3 V while commanded on. Those state-dependent checks show why one voltage reading without the commanded state cannot identify the correct branch.

The source does not provide a resistance limit or prove that the N522 winding is open. A professional needs the appropriate wiring and component procedure to isolate the reported condition. Verification should reproduce the required command state and acceptable circuit response; a cleared warning or normal engine oil level does not resolve an electrical open-circuit finding.
