# P0288: Audi’s cylinder-10 injector monitor reports insufficient current

Audi assigns P0288 to the cylinder-10 injector circuit-low monitor in the CEJA W12 chapter. It lists signal current below 2.1 A as the malfunction boundary. This is an electrical observation about a named injector channel; it is not a measured shortage of fuel in cylinder 10.

## A fault boundary is not a complete test specification

The quick-reference row does not state when in an injector command the current is assessed, how long the condition must persist, or where an external instrument should be connected. Those missing details prevent the number from being used as an improvised disconnected-component acceptance test.

Its neighboring cylinder-10 high entry, P0289, describes a low-side short to battery positive with current above 14.70 A. The two DTCs identify different electrical outcomes. Neither establishes a general current window that can be applied to any injector at any operating point.

## Establish the channel before comparing readings

The CEJA cylinder numbering and circuit diagram are necessary to associate a scan-tool label with the correct physical injector. A good reading from an adjacent injector would not resolve P0288, and the common numerical threshold across several cylinders does not make their identities interchangeable.

A qualified investigation should retain the stored code, identify the applicable electrical path and follow its factory diagnostic sequence. The source contains no direct component-replacement instruction. Verification should establish correct behavior of the cylinder-10 circuit under the relevant conditions, rather than infer success only from the absence of an immediate engine-running complaint.
