# P2259: Audi classifies a low control-B secondary-air signal

Audi’s CEJA chapter assigns P2259 to secondary-air injection system control B circuit low. The published criterion is a signal-voltage band of 0.0 to 3.26 V. That is the row’s monitored electrical quantity; it is not an airflow specification or a direct assessment of exhaust emissions.

## Preserve the band rather than inventing a switching rule

The source prints the two endpoints as a range. It does not provide a detection interval, control-command state or exact external measurement terminal. Accordingly, the range should not be turned into a rule that any low reading with the system intentionally inactive proves a defect.

The paired high code, P2260, is unusual because its row uses signal current from 0.60 to 2.40 A instead of voltage. The two rows therefore cannot be merged into one normal voltage window. Each describes its own monitor quantity and needs the corresponding service procedure.

## Control B is not a physical location

The letter identifies the manufacturer’s control category. This brief table does not establish a vehicle side, relay position or pump connector. Another CEJA entry, P0419, also concerns control B but uses a different voltage criterion, reinforcing the need to retain the exact DTC.

Qualified fault isolation should establish the correct electrical path and its operating state before interpreting readings. The source gives no direct replacement instruction. Verification needs the prescribed control-circuit checks, rather than assuming that audible pump operation alone resolves a low-signal fault.
