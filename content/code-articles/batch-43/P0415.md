# P0415: Audi identifies the secondary-air valve B circuit by current

P0415 is the secondary-air injection switching-valve B circuit entry in Audi’s CEUA/CGTA 4.0L reference table. The associated criterion is a signal-current range of 2.20–4.20 A. This is electrical monitoring of a named valve circuit, rather than a direct measurement of secondary-air flow.

## Keep current, voltage and airflow separate

The unit matters. An ampere value cannot be compared directly with a voltage obtained from a different test point, and the reference does not present the range as a specification for powering the valve on a bench. It gives neither the driver connection nor the complete enable conditions required to reproduce the monitor.

Likewise, a circuit fault does not prove that the pump has insufficient output or that an air passage is blocked. Those conclusions concern system operation and require their own evidence. Retaining the switching-valve B designation prevents an investigation from drifting toward a different valve simply because both belong to the secondary-air system.

## Use the row as a diagnostic starting point

A useful evaluation first confirms the engine and controller application, then matches the stored DTC with the proper actuator circuit and applicable service information. Related electrical records may help establish whether the event was isolated or part of a broader supply disturbance, but this chart does not assign a failure probability to any component.

No duration or replacement decision is supplied for P0415. The supported verification goal is to establish correct operation of the identified circuit under the manufacturer’s test conditions. A cleared warning or an audible pump alone does not demonstrate that the valve-B electrical monitor is satisfied.
