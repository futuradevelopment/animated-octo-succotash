# P2330: Audi uses a current threshold for coil-K circuit low

In Audi’s CEJA W12 chapter, P2330 identifies ignition coil K primary control circuit low. The malfunction criterion is signal current above 24.0 mA. The code’s “low” label should therefore not be translated into a statement that current is insufficient.

## Read the electrical quantity before selecting a test

The table does not provide a low-voltage value for this entry. It also does not explain the internal circuit relationship between the greater-than current threshold and the circuit-low classification. Inventing that relationship would exceed the information in the source.

Coil K has a separate high entry, P2331, whose criterion is signal voltage above a range of thresholds from 5.1 to 7.0 V. It also has the broader P0361 circuit/internal-check entry. These different categories should remain distinct when a technician reviews the fault history and chooses the applicable diagnostic branch.

## Do not treat the figure as a bench-energization instruction

The quick-reference page contains no connection method, measurement terminal or activation sequence. Matching CEJA service information is required to identify coil K and evaluate its primary control path. The threshold is not a specification for secondary spark current.

Repair verification should establish that the identified channel behaves correctly under the prescribed electrical checks. A coil replacement made without resolving the circuit evidence would not explain the excessive signal-current finding, and the lack of a visible running symptom does not by itself prove that the monitor is satisfied.
