# P0359: Audi offers two trigger paths for the coil-I circuit fault

Audi’s CEJA W12 entry for P0359 concerns ignition coil I and lists two alternative malfunction criteria: signal current below a threshold expressed as 0.25 to 2.0 mA, or a failed internal check. The word “or” is important. The code does not establish that both conditions occurred together.

## Retain the source’s threshold range

The table presents a range for the current threshold without explaining its selection conditions. It would be misleading to reduce that to a single universal 0.25 mA or 2.0 mA limit. This is also a signal-current quantity, not a specification for the coil’s high-voltage output or spark energy.

The factory title says primary/secondary circuit, while the general catalog emphasizes primary control/open. The source does not identify which internal check failed or prove that a secondary winding is defective. Its broader title must not become a component-level verdict.

## Match the circuit description to the correct coil

Coil I is the channel identifier supplied here. The quick-reference page does not locate it physically, identify a connector terminal or prescribe a coil-swap procedure. Those details need the actual CEJA repair information before testing.

A technician should preserve the full code and available fault context, then distinguish the appropriate electrical or internal-check branch. Verification must address the identified channel under the prescribed conditions. A visible spark or normal running at another moment would not independently explain the controller’s recorded circuit fault.
