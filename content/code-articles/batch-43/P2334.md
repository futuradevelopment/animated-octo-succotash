# P2334: Audi checks coil-L primary control for a high voltage condition

Audi lists P2334 for ignition coil L primary control circuit high in the CEJA W12 chapter. The signal-voltage malfunction threshold is expressed as greater than 5.1 to 7.0 V. The quick-reference source leaves the conditions selecting the exact boundary unspecified.

## Do not turn the endpoints into a normal range

The row does not say that operation between 5.1 and 7.0 V is acceptable. It represents a range of fault thresholds. Without the matching procedure’s measurement point and operating conditions, an external voltage reading cannot be confidently judged against either endpoint alone.

The preceding coil-L low entry, P2333, evaluates signal current above 24.0 mA. That difference matters when choosing a test: the low and high descriptions are not simply opposite voltage limits from one complete specification. P0362 is a broader circuit/internal-check category again.

## Establish coil-L identity and the control path

This page supplies a channel letter but no physical location or terminal assignment. Appropriate CEJA workshop information is needed before a professional makes circuit measurements. The source’s voltage criterion concerns primary control and cannot establish a secondary spark-output or spark-plug diagnosis.

Verification should connect the recorded high classification to the correct electrical channel and demonstrate proper behavior through the prescribed checks. Replacing an ignition component without explaining the control signal, or observing a normal reading while the fault is absent, would leave the original monitor event unresolved.
