# P284C: Volkswagen’s fourth/sixth fork exceeds its disengagement timeout

P284C is the DQ381 0GC shift-fork-D stuck code in Volkswagen’s table. The manufacturer maps D to the 4–6 actuator and describes a timeout in the disengage state greater than two seconds. This identifies a particular control transition rather than a general inability to select fourth or sixth gear.

## Read the state before interpreting the word stuck

The row expects disengagement. It does not publish a limit for engagement speed, a neutral-position offset or a sensor voltage. Importing a threshold from one of those other checks would change what P284C actually records.

The source lists two seconds as monitoring time, continuous checking and MIL operation after two driving cycles. The timing provides useful diagnostic context but cannot locate the cause within the actuator, position-feedback path or mechanism by itself.

## Correlate the D-channel evidence

The same module has P2840 for absent position information and P2848 for an incorrect neutral-position indication. A complete assessment should preserve these distinctions when companion faults are present. Missing or unreliable feedback must be reconciled before a timed-response fault is treated as proof of mechanical seizure.

A technician needs the matching 0GC procedure for command, position and movement checks. The table gives no instruction to force a shift or replace the complete gearbox. Verification should demonstrate the fourth/sixth actuator completing disengagement under the appropriate conditions and the relevant diagnostic judgment finishing. A cleared warning or operation of an unrelated fork is insufficient confirmation.
