# P2327: Audi’s coil-J circuit-low monitor watches excessive signal current

P2327 is the ignition coil J primary control circuit-low entry in Audi’s CEJA W12 table. Its criterion is signal current above 24.0 mA. The apparently opposing words are important: “low” describes the DTC category, while the quantity used to detect it is a current exceeding a threshold.

## Do not reverse the comparison to match the title

Changing the printed greater-than relation into “current below 24 mA” would misstate the manufacturer’s monitor. Nor does the code provide a direct low-voltage limit. The summary names current and gives no explanation of the internal sensing circuit that produces this classification.

The neighboring coil-J high entry, P2328, instead uses signal voltage above a threshold range of 5.1 to 7.0 V. The two criteria involve different electrical quantities. They cannot be combined into a single current range or used interchangeably as meter tests.

## Follow the identified primary-control path

The row does not give a coil-J physical location, terminal number, operating-state requirement or detection duration. Those details must be established through the correct CEJA service procedure. It is also not a test of secondary ignition voltage or spark-plug condition.

A qualified diagnosis should keep the exact code, channel and current direction intact when comparing the recorded event with circuit evidence. Verification must demonstrate correct primary-control behavior under the applicable checks; a normal engine start alone does not answer the current-based fault finding.
