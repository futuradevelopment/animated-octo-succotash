# P068B: Audi relay voltage fails to change at shutdown

Audi’s CBEA TDI chart links P068B to ECM power-relay de-energization that occurs too late. The accompanying criterion describes a stuck relay and no change in the circuit voltage. The important evidence is the missing transition, rather than a single voltage measurement taken with the ignition on.

## A shutdown event has a sequence

A supply can appear normal while the engine is running and still fail this monitor when the controller expects it to switch off. Conversely, voltage remaining present at an arbitrary moment after key-off is not enough to apply the code’s diagnosis: the reference does not specify the permitted after-run interval or the exact sampling point.

The relationship between the relay command and the observed circuit response is therefore central. A technician needs the matching power-distribution information and the controller’s shutdown strategy before deciding which component or circuit kept the voltage from changing. The phrase “relay stuck” in a summary chart should not bypass confirmation of the actual switching arrangement.

## Preserve the conditions of the event

The relevant service record should distinguish a late release from a supply that disappears too early or is absent altogether. Those are different behaviors even though a generic scan summary may call each a power-relay problem. The stored event and any observed shutdown behavior are more informative than a replacement performed solely because the relay name appears in the description.

This source contains no fixed number of seconds, terminal designation, or bench-test instruction for P068B. Verification must use the applicable repair procedure and demonstrate the expected voltage transition without assuming that an immediately successful restart proves the shutdown problem is gone.
