# P055C: Volkswagen checks an active high-pressure-switch indication after shutdown

P055C is labeled engine oil-pressure sensor/switch B low in Volkswagen’s 2021–2022 engine table. Its monitor description is a functional check of the high oil-pressure switch. The listed abnormal state is an active switch indication while the engine is not running.

## A switch indication is not a pressure measurement

The row does not publish a pressure in bar or kilopascals. It therefore cannot establish how much mechanical oil pressure exists or prove a low-pressure lubrication failure from the code alone. The “low” DTC title and the active-switch criterion describe different aspects of the control system.

The enabling conditions include oil temperature above -28°C and an engine-off interval whose calibrated threshold is shown from 15 to 120 seconds. The table gives a 0.5-second monitoring time, continuous checking and two driving cycles for MIL illumination. This is an engine-off plausibility assessment, so an active indication while the engine is intentionally running is not the same condition.

## Preserve the exact pressure channel

The component-diagnosis column references oil-pressure regulation valve N428. That referral should not be treated as proof that the valve itself caused the fault. Matching service information is needed to relate the switch indication, wiring and regulation system.

Verification should establish credible switch behavior through the applicable shutdown sequence and temperature conditions. A normal oil level or an erased warning cannot explain an implausibly active engine-off indication. Conversely, the monitor summary is not a substitute for appropriate mechanical pressure diagnosis if the actual vehicle shows a separate lubrication concern.
