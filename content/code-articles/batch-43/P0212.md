# P0212: Audi records an internal cylinder-12 injector-circuit fault

The CEJA W12 table lists P0212 as injector circuit/open for cylinder 12 and gives “internal logic failure” as the malfunction criterion. It does not define an injector resistance specification. The distinction is useful when a generic scan description appears more conclusive than the evidence actually provided by the manufacturer’s summary.

## An electrical classification does not establish fuel delivery

This row does not measure the quantity of fuel delivered or identify a blocked nozzle. Its evidence concerns the injector circuit as assessed by the controller. Mechanical fuel-delivery conclusions would need their own appropriate tests rather than being inferred from the circuit/open title.

Audi separately lists cylinder-12 low and high circuit entries on the next physical page. P0294 evaluates current below 2.1 A; P0295 describes a short to battery positive on the low side and current above 14.70 A. Those details show that the manufacturer distinguishes more specific electrical conditions from the internal logic category used for P0212.

## Preserve what the source does and does not say

The summary supplies no detection interval, driver-terminal assignment or replacement branch. A professional diagnosis needs the actual CEJA circuit information and correctly identified cylinder before moving from the code to measurements.

Repair verification should resolve the circuit condition on that channel under the applicable service procedure. A later absence of visible running symptoms does not explain an earlier internal fault, and another cylinder’s normal data cannot establish that cylinder 12 has passed its own monitor.
