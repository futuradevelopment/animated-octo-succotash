# P2845: Volkswagen detects movement away from commanded neutral on fork A

Volkswagen identifies shift fork A as the 1–3 actuator in the DQ381 0GC table. P2845 concerns an incorrect neutral-position indication. The published functional criterion is that the actuator leaves neutral by more than 3 mm while its nominal state is neutral.

## The commanded state gives the position error meaning

A displacement greater than three millimeters during an intentional gear engagement is not the same event. The monitor specifically expects neutral. The fault should therefore be interpreted with both the reported position and the control state, not with a position number alone.

The table lists a two-second monitoring time, continuous checks and two driving cycles for MIL operation. It does not define a mechanical adjustment clearance or instruct the reader to move the fork by three millimeters. The number belongs to a controller functional check.

## Distinguish neutral disagreement from signal loss

P2831 is the same module’s missing-position-signal entry. P2849 addresses a disengagement timeout. These categories preserve different evidence: no feedback, an unexpected neutral-state position, and failure to complete a state transition.

A professional diagnosis should establish credible A-channel position feedback before deciding what caused the neutral disagreement. The summary does not prove a bent fork or prescribe replacement of the mechatronic. Verification needs the first/third actuator to show the expected neutral behavior under the applicable service checks. An erased code or successful engagement of another gear does not address the specific nominal-neutral condition.
