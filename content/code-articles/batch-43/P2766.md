# P2766: Volkswagen rejects an implausibly high second input-shaft speed

Volkswagen’s DQ381-7A/F 0GC table associates P2766 with the input/turbine speed sensor B, identified as the shaft-2 speed channel. The published range-check criterion is a reported input-shaft speed above 12,000 rpm. It is not an engine-speed limit or a direct command to limit vehicle speed to a particular value.

## A reported overspeed is not proof of physical overspeed

The entry classifies the speed signal as outside its expected range. Determining whether the value reflects actual rotation, an incorrect signal or another diagnostic issue requires the matching transmission procedure. The number alone does not establish a failed bearing or damaged gear.

The same shaft has P2768 for loss of signal lasting more than one second. Those categories describe different evidence: a value outside range versus no usable signal. They should not be combined into one generic speed-sensor replacement instruction.

## Keep shaft identity and units intact

The table explicitly names shaft 2 and uses revolutions per minute. A comparison with engine rpm or another transmission shaft requires the appropriate operating relationship; their readings cannot simply be assumed equal.

Volkswagen lists a two-second monitoring time, continuous checks and two driving cycles for MIL operation. Verification should establish credible shaft-2 speed information under the prescribed conditions and reconcile any related signal-loss faults. A code-free scan while stationary does not demonstrate that the range monitor has been exercised through the original operating condition.
