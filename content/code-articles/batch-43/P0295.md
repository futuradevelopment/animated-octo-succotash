# P0295: Audi’s cylinder-12 injector high fault names a short to positive

Audi lists P0295 as the cylinder-12 injector circuit-high fault in its CEJA W12 chapter. The criterion describes a short to battery positive on the low side and signal current above 14.70 A. Both details narrow the interpretation beyond the generic words “injector circuit high.”

## High circuit does not mean high fuel quantity

The measured quantity in this row is electrical current. There is no fuel-flow, pressure or injection-duration criterion here. Concluding that cylinder 12 is over-fueling would require other evidence and would not follow from this monitor description alone.

The related P0294 entry uses current below 2.1 A. These high and low categories are fault boundaries, not the manufacturer’s complete statement of normal injector operation. The reference does not specify a waveform, a current-measurement interval or a permissible range for every operating state.

## Establish the low-side circuit before testing it

The source names the electrical side involved but supplies no connector pin or harness routing. Those details must come from the exact CEJA workshop information. A generic injector bench procedure cannot be inferred from the threshold, and direct energization is not instructed by this page.

Professional fault isolation should connect the reported cylinder-12 condition to the correct circuit and any related records. Verification must then demonstrate acceptable behavior under the applicable checks. A replacement injector or cleared memory, without evidence addressing the low-side electrical event, is not a complete explanation of P0295.
