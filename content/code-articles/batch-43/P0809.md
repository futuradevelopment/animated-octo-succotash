# P0809: Volkswagen separates missing clutch feedback from its supply prerequisites

In Volkswagen’s 2015 Jetta CNLA transmission table, P0809 is the intermittent clutch-position sensor A circuit entry. The manufacturer’s criterion is failure of the sensor-element signal, described as no signal. This is more specific than merely saying that the clutch position was occasionally wrong.

## The supply conditions are part of the check

The row requires the internal sensor supply of gearbox subsystem 1 and that of subsystem 2 each to exceed 4.5 V. These prerequisites help define when the missing-feedback assessment is valid. A loss of supply must not be silently treated as the same evidence as an absent signal with the listed supplies available.

The neighboring P0805 range check evaluates PWM duty-cycle boundaries. P0809 instead concerns signal loss, so its title does not authorize importing the duty-cycle limits as its own detection criterion.

## Missing feedback does not establish a stuck clutch

The same table has a separate clutch-position control-error entry for mismatch between actual and desired position. A no-signal fault cannot by itself prove that the mechanism failed to move. Qualified diagnosis needs the matching DQ200 electrical and position-data procedure before making that mechanical judgment.

Volkswagen lists 100 milliseconds of monitoring, continuous checking and two driving cycles for MIL operation. The summary gives no sensor-terminal assignment or mandatory replacement. Verification should demonstrate reliable feedback with the appropriate supplies present under the prescribed checks. Clearing the code or seeing the vehicle move does not independently establish that the intermittent signal-loss condition has been resolved.
