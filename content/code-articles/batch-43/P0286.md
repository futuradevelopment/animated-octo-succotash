# P0286: Audi’s cylinder-9 high-circuit fault refers to the injector low side

P0286 is the high-circuit fault for cylinder 9 in Audi’s CEJA W12 table. Its supporting row is more specific than the generic title: it describes a short to battery positive on the low side and signal current above 14.70 A. The term “high” therefore should not be paraphrased simply as excessive fuel pressure.

## Low side and circuit low are different terms

“Low side” identifies the part of the electrical control path described by the source. It does not mean this is a circuit-low DTC. That separate cylinder-9 entry is P0285 and uses current below 2.1 A. Confusing the two terms can send the investigation down the wrong electrical branch.

The summary does not provide a terminal number or explain the controller’s current-sensing method. Its wording supports a targeted circuit investigation, but it is not sufficient to identify a damaged harness section or prove that the injector itself is defective.

## Preserve the monitor context during diagnosis

The 14.70 A criterion is not a suggested bench-test current. A professional should use the correct CEJA wiring and service procedure, with the original fault record and related supply or injector codes available. The reference gives no instruction to energize the injector directly.

Verification requires the identified cylinder-9 control path to behave correctly under the prescribed test conditions. A cleared warning or a momentarily normal engine idle cannot substitute for explaining the reported low-side electrical fault.
