# P2019: Audi evaluates a low bank-2 intake-runner position signal

In Audi’s CGXC/CTUB 3.0L reference chapter, P2019 belongs to the bank-2 intake-manifold runner position-sensor/switch circuit. The listed monitor boundary is a signal below 0.2 V. This is evidence about the position-information circuit, not direct proof that a runner flap is physically stuck closed.

## Position feedback and mechanical movement are different checks

A low electrical input may prevent the controller from obtaining useful position information. It cannot, without additional application data, be translated into a particular flap angle or a mechanical end position. The reference does not publish a voltage-to-position relationship for this row.

The chart separately provides a bank-2 high-input entry above 4.8 V. Keeping those electrical categories distinct is useful when choosing the appropriate factory circuit test. A code describing runner movement or target-versus-actual position would involve different evidence, even though all such records may share the words “intake manifold.”

## Match bank 2 before evaluating the signal

The bank designation identifies the channel involved but does not locate it physically in this summary. The correct engine diagram is needed before interpreting a connector reading or selecting a component. A plausible bank-1 value cannot demonstrate that the bank-2 input was valid during the recorded fault.

Audi’s reference row contains no detection duration and no instruction to replace the manifold. A professional diagnosis should reconcile the recorded low signal with the correct circuit and operating data, then verify the behavior through the matching procedure. Erasing the fault or observing normal engine operation does not by itself resolve a position-feedback defect.
