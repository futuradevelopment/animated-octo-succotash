# P24D5: Audi’s EVAP pressure-sensor B circuit reaches its upper signal limit

P24D5 is the EVAP pressure-sensor/switch B circuit entry in the CHJA hybrid reference table. Audi lists a signal above 4.88 V. The criterion is electrical; the row does not convert that voltage into a fuel-tank pressure or an EVAP leak size.

## Three entries separate three kinds of evidence

The same group assigns P24D6 to a pressure-range check and P24D7 to an input below 0.20 V. That separation helps explain why P24D5 should not be treated as a generic instruction to find a leak. Its upper signal boundary differs from both a pressure plausibility failure and the opposite electrical condition.

The designation B must also be matched to the vehicle’s actual sensor circuit. The quick-reference table does not supply a mounting position, a connector number, or proof that an aftermarket scanner’s displayed pressure corresponds to the monitored B channel. Those are application details to establish before interpreting a measured value.

## Follow the circuit evidence without inventing a cause

A technician can use the recorded DTC to select the appropriate signal and supply checks in the matching service information. The source does not identify a particular failed sensor or rank wiring faults, and it states no duration for the upper-limit condition. It would therefore be premature to select a replacement part from this row alone.

Verification should explain the high electrical input and demonstrate correct behavior under the applicable test conditions. A successful leak test, by itself, would not prove that the pressure-signal circuit is healthy; equally, a plausible later pressure reading does not account for the original upper-limit event.
