# P0285: Audi identifies low current in the cylinder-9 injector circuit

In Audi’s CEJA W12 reference, P0285 is the cylinder-9 injector circuit-low entry. The specified quantity is signal current, with a boundary below 2.1 A. Reading “low” as low fuel pressure or low injector voltage would change the manufacturer’s stated criterion.

## Keep the current condition tied to its channel

The number belongs to the controller’s injector-circuit monitor. The reference does not supply the waveform, measurement point or operating interval needed to turn it into a standalone ammeter test. A technician needs the matching circuit procedure before attempting to reproduce the condition electrically.

Cylinder 9 also has a separate circuit/open entry, P0209, whose criterion is an internal logic failure. That means a low-current classification should not automatically be rewritten as proof of an open winding. The summary identifies the observed category but does not isolate the responsible component or connection.

## Compare related faults without merging their limits

P0286, the corresponding high entry, describes a low-side short to battery positive and current above 14.70 A. This is a different fault category, not the upper edge of a published normal operating range. The table does not promise that every reading between the two limits is healthy.

A useful diagnostic record preserves cylinder 9, the exact current-low DTC and any companion faults. Verification should follow the applicable injector electrical checks and monitor conditions. The threshold alone does not justify opening the fuel system or replacing the injector.
