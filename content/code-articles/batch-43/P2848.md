# P2848: Volkswagen detects an unexpected fourth/sixth position in neutral

P2848 belongs to shift fork D, identified by Volkswagen as the 4–6 actuator in the DQ381 0GC transmission. Its functional criterion is movement away from neutral greater than 3 mm while the nominal actuator state is neutral. This is a state-dependent position assessment.

## Position alone does not reproduce the monitor

An actuator is expected to travel when commanded to select a gear. P2848 instead evaluates the neutral state. The recorded control state must accompany the position information before a technician concludes that the manufacturer’s criterion was met.

The source gives a two-second monitoring time, continuous checks and two driving cycles for MIL illumination. It does not define the three-millimeter value as a mechanical service adjustment or publish a connector-level test for the D position sensor.

## Different D-channel faults describe different evidence

P2840 is the missing-position-signal entry. P284C is the stuck/disengagement-timeout entry. P2848 concerns incorrect neutral indication, so replacing one description with another can lead to an unsupported mechanical diagnosis.

The proper 0GC service procedure is needed to verify the position signal and actuator behavior together. The summary alone does not prove that a fork is bent or that the whole transmission must be replaced. Repair verification should establish the correct fourth/sixth actuator response in nominal neutral and completion of the relevant monitor. Normal operation of a different gear pair does not establish that this neutral-state check has passed.
