# P04B8: fuel-door position has entered an invalid signal band

P04B8 in the cited GM hybrid table means the fuel-fill door position signal lies in an invalid range. This is the door-position sensor or switch channel, not the separate lock-position feedback and not the driver's open-request switch.

## Invalid does not mean simply open or closed

The table uses a reported-position percentage and identifies a middle signal band as invalid. Separate neighboring diagnostics cover excessively low and high readings. A value inside the overall numerical display range can therefore be electrically implausible even though it is neither near zero nor at the maximum.

That percentage should not be interpreted as the physical angle of the fuel door. The summary describes the controller's reported signal and does not supply a conversion to hinge travel or a resistance specification for a handheld meter.

## Persistence matters

The published monitor requires six failing samples out of eight, sampled every half-second, and its diagnostic calibration must be enabled. A single unusual update is not the complete detection criterion. The table also describes a two-trip reporting classification for this row.

A useful diagnostic record preserves the signal channel name, the invalid-band behavior over time and any companion low/high or lock-position codes. Those details help distinguish an unstable reading from an actual door-state problem.

The summary establishes how the result is recognized, not which connector or switch has failed. Confirm the exact vehicle, circuit and service procedure before selecting parts.

## Application limits

Document-level 2015 GM diagnostic table; per-code vehicle/engine/calibration applicability not established.
