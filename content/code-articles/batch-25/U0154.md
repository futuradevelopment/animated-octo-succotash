# U0154: restraint controller times out waiting for occupant status

U0154 in the cited 2020 GM sensing-and-diagnostic-module table means the restraint controller did not receive the expected protected occupant-status message within its allowed time. The source identifies communication between the SDM and automatic occupant-sensing module.

## A communication result does not classify the occupant

The monitor evaluates message arrival, not the accuracy of a particular passenger-weight reading or the physical condition of a seat. Replacing a seat sensor solely from this timeout would skip identification of the communication failure.

The documented timeout is 2.5 seconds, monitored while the relevant CAN frames are being exchanged. That interval belongs to this implementation and should not be generalized to every vehicle reporting U0154.

## Check whether the network evaluation was enabled

The row requires a qualified supply voltage, enabled communication and diagnostic settings, and more than five seconds since specified reset or voltage events. It also excludes an active bus-related fault. A recent low-voltage event can therefore affect both communication and when the diagnostic begins evaluating it.

GM classifies the result as safety-related and non-MIL. An unlit check-engine lamp says nothing about restraint-system readiness. Follow the vehicle's restraint warning indications and qualified service procedure.

Preserve the reporting module, occupant-module communication status and preceding voltage/reset history before clearing data. Do not probe airbag deployment circuits or alter occupancy wiring to make a warning disappear; the summary provides no such repair method or assurance of protection.

## Application limits

Document-level 2020 GM diagnostic table; per-code vehicle/engine/calibration applicability not established.
