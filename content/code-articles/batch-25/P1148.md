# P1148: Maxima bank-1 closed-loop control has not become active

P1148 in the 2001 Nissan Maxima manual means bank 1 has not achieved the required closed-loop operation under the specified driving conditions. It is a one-trip diagnosis. The corresponding bank-2 result is P1168, so preserve the bank identification when reviewing scanner output.

## Investigate the feedback needed for closed loop

Nissan lists the front heated oxygen sensor, its circuit and its heater as possible causes. This points the investigation toward whether the controller receives usable mixture feedback, rather than directly diagnosing a rear sensor or catalyst.

The procedure first checks that the front-sensor signal can reach both a rich-side and lean-side value during a controlled warm-engine check. That evidence matters because a signal that cannot respond cannot support the expected closed-loop behavior. The manual then uses a separate idle and driving sequence to attempt to confirm the DTC.

## A function check and a confirmed fault differ

Nissan expressly notes that its general function check may not confirm the code. Passing a quick response check is useful evidence, but it does not demonstrate that the controller completed the required closed-loop monitor in service.

The document also warns that other front-sensor codes can appear during the confirmation sequence. Record those results instead of discarding them as unrelated. Its final direction is to the applicable front-sensor slow-response diagnostic procedure.

A repair record should identify bank 1, heater and circuit findings, the observed signal response and the actual confirmation outcome. This is more actionable than treating “closed loop” as a command to replace an oxygen sensor without testing its supporting circuit.

## Documented application
