# P0B81: sense-O readings must be distinguished from actual cell voltage

P0B81 identifies battery voltage-sense circuit O in the cited GM diagnostic summary. The result concerns the measurement path used to report cell voltage. It does not independently establish the actual electrochemical condition of the cell represented by that channel.

## A reported voltage is only as reliable as its sensing path

The shared strategy compares affected and adjacent readings. It also describes patterns produced by busbar sensing and loss of sensing-electronics power. These mechanisms can make displayed values misleading, including a group of zeros when the relevant sensing chip loses power.

A qualified technician should therefore establish whether the readings are credible before discussing cell repair or whole-pack replacement. The O label must be mapped through the correct vehicle pack documentation, not inferred from its alphabetical position.

## Keep the limitations of the summary visible

The source does not fully print one busbar-positive comparison. No missing value or connector-level test is guessed in this article. The shared diagnostic also depends on communication, balancing and internal-module conditions and a sustained evaluation window.

Retain the O reading, adjacent values and supporting faults before clearing data. That record helps distinguish a localized sensing problem from a shared measurement failure.

Physical testing belongs within the approved high-voltage service and isolation process. A final repair conclusion should include valid sensing data and monitor completion under the required conditions, rather than a code-free snapshot taken before evaluation resumed.
