# P0B13: fine and coarse battery-current measurements disagree

P0B13 in the cited GM hybrid summary checks agreement between fine and coarse battery-current measurements. It evaluates the absolute difference between the two readings, rather than simply testing whether either reading is above a single high-current limit.

## Compare the channels inside their shared range

The documented difference threshold is ten amperes. The enable logic requires one of the current readings to be within the stated fine-range window. This matters because comparing channels outside the intended overlapping range can misrepresent their expected agreement.

The check also excludes several current-sensor and communication faults. A channel already known to be electrically invalid cannot provide a trustworthy comparison. Record both signed readings from the same operating interval rather than compare unrelated snapshots.

## A disagreement does not identify the wrong sensor

The absolute difference shows that the channels do not agree sufficiently. It does not, by itself, establish which sensor is biased or whether a supporting circuit caused the difference. The applicable service procedure must resolve that question.

The printed inhibit list includes P0B13 itself. Its precise relationship to the monitor's active/latched status is not explained in this summary, so this draft does not invent a reset or re-enable instruction.

Preserve the paired readings, current-range condition and companion codes before clearing data. High-voltage battery-current diagnosis requires qualified equipment and isolation procedures; the correlation result is not a reason to probe live battery conductors.
