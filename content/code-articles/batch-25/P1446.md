# P1446: Altima vent restriction can imitate a valve stuck closed

P1446 in the 2001 Nissan Altima manual indicates that the EVAP vent remains closed under the specified driving conditions. The valve is normally open between diagnostic sealing operations, so persistent restriction is the problem being investigated.

## Inspect the whole air path

Nissan starts its diagnostic sequence with the rubber tube and water separator. An obstructed path can prevent venting even if the solenoid itself moves. That is why replacing the valve solely from the code title can leave the actual restriction untouched.

The procedure then examines the valve and its seal, followed by possible water saturation of the canister. Its water-saturation assessment has a model-specific method and threshold. Those details belong to the documented assembly; they should not be applied as a universal canister weight test.

## Pressure feedback is another branch

The pressure sensor and its circuit remain possible explanations. The manual directs attention to a disconnected sensor hose and water in its connector before referring to the sensor diagnosis. A false pressure report can change the controller's interpretation of vent behavior.

The confirmation routine is a driving test, so a valve that clicks during a stationary check has not necessarily satisfied the monitor. Record the obstruction inspection, evidence of water intrusion, valve response and pressure-signal findings separately. Then confirm the result under the applicable routine. Opening vapor-system connections and handling a potentially waterlogged canister are workshop tasks; the code does not justify bypassing the vent or leaving hoses disconnected.

## Documented application

2001 Nissan Altima, EC section. Exact engine, drivetrain and diagnostic applicability must be checked in the cited procedure.
