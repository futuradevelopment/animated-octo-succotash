# P04C6: high lock-position feedback can represent an open circuit

P04C6 in the cited GM hybrid diagnostic table is the high branch for fuel-fill door lock-position feedback. The strategy explicitly allows a short-to-battery condition or an open circuit. A high reading therefore does not uniquely establish that the signal wire is touching battery power.

## Do not equate high with locked

The threshold concerns an electrically invalid reported position. It is not simply the normal indication that the latch is locked. A visual check of the lock cannot replace evaluation of the feedback circuit.

This distinction is especially useful when a disconnected or intermittent connection produces a high reading. The summary identifies that possibility, but it does not locate the open or provide a connector pinout. The applicable vehicle circuit diagram is still necessary.

## Related monitors depend on this signal

Lock, unlock and unexpected-state performance checks exclude P04C6 among their supporting circuit faults. Their silence while this high-input fault remains active may reflect inhibited evaluation rather than correct mechanical operation.

The published detection window counts repeated failures, and the calibration must enable the diagnostic. A brief valid value after reconnecting something is not equivalent to a completed successful monitor.

Document the lock-position data, connector condition found by the proper inspection and any accompanying low or invalid-range code. A complete repair verification should establish stable valid feedback and then allow the relevant performance checks to run, rather than stopping when memory has been cleared.

## Application limits
