# P0115: Altima may display a substitute coolant temperature

P0115 in the 2001 Nissan Altima manual means coolant-temperature sensor voltage is excessively high or low. Nissan identifies the sensor and an open or short in its circuit as the principal diagnostic possibilities.

## The scanner can show a calculated fallback

The important complication is fail-safe operation. When this fault is detected, the ECM can substitute a temperature based on elapsed time after ignition-on or starting. CONSULT-II then displays that substituted value rather than the failed sensor's true temperature.

The documented progression begins at 40°C and reaches 80°C after approximately four minutes, with intermediate values dependent on time. These are fallback values, not proof that coolant actually warmed through those temperatures. A plausible-looking display therefore cannot be used alone to dismiss the circuit fault.

## Fan operation also changes

Nissan describes cooling-fan operation after key-off while the system is in this fail-safe mode. A fan that continues running may be consistent with the controller's protective strategy rather than an independent fan-relay diagnosis.

The sensor itself is a thermistor whose resistance decreases with temperature. The service procedure checks the circuit and temperature-dependent resistance to separate sensor failure from a connection problem. Use those independent findings to validate the displayed data.

After repair, confirm that the electrical fault no longer returns and that live temperature behavior is credible. Do not open a hot cooling system to verify a scanner value; physical cooling-system work requires the factory pressure and temperature precautions.
