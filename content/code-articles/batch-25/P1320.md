# P1320: Maxima ignition-signal diagnosis starts with companion sensor codes

P1320 in the 2001 Nissan Maxima manual means the expected ignition-primary signal is missing during cranking or running. Each coil's power transistor switches the primary circuit; that switching produces the secondary ignition voltage.

## Resolve timing-reference faults first

Nissan gives accompanying P0335, P0340, P1335 or P1336 priority. Those crankshaft/camshaft-related results can affect the information required for ignition operation. Ignoring them and replacing coils immediately would skip an explicit diagnostic dependency.

The possible causes include primary wiring, the coil's integrated power transistor, the condenser and the crankshaft reference sensor or its circuit. P1320 therefore does not uniquely identify a coil.

## Running and non-running engines take different paths

For an engine that runs, the procedure uses a controlled power-balance test to help locate a circuit that does not produce the expected momentary speed change. A no-start result instead leads into the shared ignition supply and relay path before individual coil checks.

Later branches distinguish coil supply, ground and control wiring from the coil/transistor inspection. A failed shared feed can affect several cylinders, so the number of apparent ignition symptoms should not be used as a count of failed coils.

Record the companion DTCs, whether the engine runs, the power-balance findings where applicable and the actual failed electrical test. Ignition circuits generate hazardous voltage; the article's diagnostic explanation is not an instruction to hold a coil, improvise a spark test or bypass a driver.
