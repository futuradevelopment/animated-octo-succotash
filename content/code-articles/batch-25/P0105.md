# P0105: Maxima uses an ambient-pressure sensor for this diagnosis

P0105 is commonly displayed as a manifold-absolute-pressure/barometric circuit code. In the 2001 Nissan Maxima procedure, the component measures ambient barometric pressure. That vehicle-specific distinction matters before a technician starts looking for an intake-manifold pressure fault.

## Pressure and voltage should move together

Nissan states that sensor voltage rises as ambient pressure rises. The monitor detects excessively high or low voltage from the sensor, with the harness/connectors and sensor listed as possible causes. It is an electrical plausibility result rather than a direct diagnosis of an intake leak.

The first diagnostic actions address engine grounds and water in the pressure-sensor connector. Those checks can reveal a supporting electrical problem before the sensor itself is condemned. The subsequent branches distinguish supply, ground and signal paths using the correct wiring diagram.

## Key-on confirmation has a specific purpose

The confirmation routine uses ignition-on monitoring with a prescribed wait. A throttle response or boost-pressure road test is not required simply to reproduce this particular electrical check. That is consistent with the sensor's ambient-pressure role in this application.

Document whether the connector was dry, whether the relevant ground and supply paths passed, and what pressure/voltage information the controller received. If the circuit appears normal during inspection, follow the document's component and intermittent-fault branches rather than declare an unsupported diagnosis. These details apply to the 2001 Maxima procedure; other vehicles using P0105 may have a different sensor arrangement.
