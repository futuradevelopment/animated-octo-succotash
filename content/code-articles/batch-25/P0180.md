# P0180: Maxima compares fuel temperature with other thermal inputs

P0180 on the documented 2001 Nissan Maxima concerns the fuel-tank temperature sensor. The ECM can detect an excessively high or low voltage, or a temperature signal that is implausible compared with coolant and intake-air temperature.

## This is a temperature signal, not fuel pressure

The sensor is a thermistor: resistance decreases as fuel temperature increases. The code does not directly measure fuel pressure or establish that the fuel pump cannot supply enough fuel. Confusing those functions can lead to replacing a much larger assembly without testing the actual fault.

Because the monitor uses other temperatures for comparison, retain the intake, coolant and fuel readings together. A single fuel-temperature screenshot omits information needed to understand a plausibility failure.

## The connector serves an assembly

Nissan's electrical procedure checks the circuit through the fuel-level-sensor and pump assembly connector. It distinguishes supply and ground continuity before the component inspection. The presence of a pump connection in that procedure is not evidence that the pump motor caused P0180.

The component test and replacement branch belong to the fuel-level-sensor unit described in this manual. Accessing that assembly involves fuel-system precautions and should be handled with the correct workshop procedure. The code is not a reason to heat or energize a fuel-tank component experimentally.

Record the circuit results and the thermal comparison, then use the applicable confirmation sequence. If the circuit and component pass, the manual retains an intermittent-fault branch rather than assigning a replacement solely from the stored number.
