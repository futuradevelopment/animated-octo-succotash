# P0645: A/C clutch-relay command disagrees with circuit feedback

P0645 in the cited 2010 GM table concerns the air-conditioning clutch-relay control circuit. The ECM detects disagreement between the commanded driver state and the actual control-circuit state. This is an electrical consistency check, not a direct test of cooling output.

## A requested clutch is not a verified clutch circuit

A scan tool can show that the controller requested operation while the circuit fails to follow. Conversely, a visible compressor symptom does not identify the relay-control path as its cause. The code narrows the investigation to the monitored electrical relationship, but does not isolate the relay, wiring or driver.

The summary does not provide a connector pinout, coil-resistance specification or a safe jumper method. Those belong to the matching vehicle service information.

## Reproduce the enabled operating state

The documented check requires the engine to be running above its minimum speed and run/crank voltage within the specified range. It then counts twenty failing samples out of twenty-five at quarter-second intervals. A key-on inspection with the engine stopped does not exercise that same monitor.

Record the relay command, the available feedback and supply condition together. This is a practical inference from the command-versus-state design: without both states, a snapshot cannot explain the disagreement.

After a demonstrated circuit defect is repaired, confirm that the monitor evaluates successfully. A cold vent temperature alone does not establish that the relay-control diagnostic has passed or that an intermittent electrical fault is gone.

## Application limits

Document-level 2010 GM diagnostic table; per-code vehicle/engine/calibration applicability not established.
