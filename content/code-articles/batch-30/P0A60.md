# P0A60: Maverick and Nautilus inverter-controller faults after startup

P0A60 is one of the inverter-controller faults in Ford SSM 54823 for certain Maverick and Nautilus vehicles. The described event includes limited power after startup and a Stop Safely Now or Service Vehicle Soon message. The combination of operating symptom, reporting module and build date is what makes this service message relevant.

## Confirm the controller identity and production boundary

Ford calls the unit the inverter system controller, abbreviated SOBDMC. FDRS may display it as secondary on-board control module C. Those two names can refer to the same reporting unit in this bulletin; they should not be mistaken for separate failed modules.

The production cutoff differs by vehicle. The Maverick boundary is December 5, 2025, while the Nautilus boundary is September 4, 2025. A vehicle’s model year alone cannot establish the match. Preserve the full scan and startup warning rather than assuming that a generic P0A60 description proves the entire case.

## What the bulletin actually directs

For vehicles exhibiting the stated conditions, Ford directs replacement of the inverter system controller using the model-specific Workshop Manual section. It does not instruct replacement of the drive motor simply because the code mentions motor-phase current. It also retains the normal authorization and warranty requirements for component replacement; a service message is not an automatic promise of coverage.

Follow a Stop Safely Now warning and arrange appropriate assistance. Inverter service is high-voltage work for trained personnel. This source supplies no safe owner-level current measurement or repair procedure, and vehicles outside the stated symptom or build boundaries need their applicable diagnostic path.

## Source and applicability

Ford SSM 54823: 2025–2026 Maverick built on or before December 5, 2025 and 2024–2026 Nautilus built on or before September 4, 2025, with the specified startup limited-power condition and SOBDMC faults.
