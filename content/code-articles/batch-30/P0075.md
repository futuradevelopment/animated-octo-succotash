# P0075: City Express faults after the specified wiring campaign repair

P0075 appearing after a particular wiring repair on a 2015 Chevrolet City Express has a different starting point from an unrelated valve-control complaint. GM PIP5302 describes a service-engine lamp, possible reduced power and possible misfire codes after work under Customer Satisfaction Program 15169A. The bulletin identifies incorrect wire selection or a poor splice as possible causes.

## Check what was connected before replacing what the code names

The first branch asks whether the campaign repair joined the correct wires. GM directs the technician back to the campaign’s step 8 and identifies the required wire gauges and colors there. This matters because a wire of a similar color is not necessarily the correct conductor in a harness containing multiple circuits.

If the wrong wires were joined, the bulletin requires restoring their original routing and repeating the repair with the correct conductors. If the selected wires were correct, the next investigation is the quality of the crimp connection. Those are different failure mechanisms and require different corrections.

## Link the scan to the repair history

Preserve P0075 and any camshaft-timing or misfire entries, and provide the date and repair-order reference for the campaign work. The combination can support inspecting a shared repair site before treating each code as a separate failed component. It does not prove that every City Express P0075 is splice-related.

A qualified technician should use the original campaign and wiring instructions for identification and repair. This article does not supply a standalone instruction to cut or join wires. After correcting a verified issue, the bulletin calls for clearing the faults and reevaluating operation so a continuing concern is not hidden by the recent repair explanation.
