# U023A: 2024 Mustang pre-collision warning after remote start

A 2024 Mustang that displays a pre-collision assistance warning after remote start may match Ford SSM 52077. In this case U023A is stored in the steering column control module, and Ford identifies a possible low-power-mode issue in image processing module A, or IPMA.

## The restart method separates two paths

The bulletin uses a normal push-button start as a diagnostic distinction. After the vehicle is shut down and restarted with the button, rather than remote start, the described temporary warning should disappear. If both the message and indicator are absent in that condition, Ford says IPMA replacement or reprogramming is unnecessary under this service message.

If the message or indicator remains after that restart, the bulletin directs ordinary Workshop Manual diagnosis. A persistent warning is therefore outside the reassuring branch of this particular procedure. Record the start method and whether the warning cleared; that information is more useful than a code screenshot without operating context.

## Keep the historical limitation visible

The November 2023 message said engineering was working on a later solution. Its projected availability date is not proof that a fix was subsequently released or installed on this vehicle. The workshop should check the applicable service information before deciding on any current software action.

While an assistance-unavailable warning is displayed, do not rely on the affected pre-collision function. The bulletin describes a way to distinguish its temporary remote-start condition from a continuing fault, not permission to disregard an ongoing warning or replace the camera module merely because U023A is present.

## Source and applicability

Ford SSM 52077, November 2023: 2024 Mustang with pre-collision assistance unavailable and U023A in the SCCM after remote start.
