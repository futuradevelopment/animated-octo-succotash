# C0030: Bronco Sport bearing seating in a two-day production window

C0030:07 on a 2022 Bronco Sport has a very narrow production-related case in Ford TSB 22-2413. The bulletin covers vehicles built on August 4 or 5, 2022 with an ABS warning. A left-front squeal may also occur. Ford identifies incomplete seating of the wheel bearing or snap ring as a possible cause of contact between the halfshaft and wheel knuckle.

## The inspection determines how much is replaced

The procedure requires professional access to the left-front hub assembly and assessment of bearing and snap-ring seating, including metallic particles on the bearing surface. When the specified seating problem and debris condition are found, Ford directs replacement of the knuckle, hub, bearing and halfshaft.

The alternative branch does not call for replacing that entire set. It specifies reinstallation with a new bearing and snap ring. This distinction is important: neither a code-only parts order nor a casual external inspection reproduces the bulletin’s decision. The actual inspection result must support the chosen repair scope.

## Verification belongs after mechanical correction

Ford includes a prescribed road verification after the work to clear the diagnostic condition. That is not a recommendation for an owner to drive through an ABS warning or wheel noise in an attempt to erase the code. Wheel-end assembly and braking-related service require the proper equipment and procedures.

Provide the workshop with the production date, complete suffix and any left-front noise. A vehicle outside the two-day build window needs its applicable diagnostic information. The code’s tone-wheel wording alone does not prove a defective sensor, and this source does not justify extending the component replacement package to every Bronco Sport.

## Source and applicability

Ford TSB 22-2413, December 7, 2022: 2022 Bronco Sport built August 4–5, 2022, with ABS warning and C0030:07 in the ABS module.
