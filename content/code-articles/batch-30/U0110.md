# U0110: Mazda PHEV no-READY condition with luggage-compartment corrosion

U0110 is one of the possible codes in a Mazda plug-in-hybrid case where the vehicle fails to enter READY in cold weather. The affected CX-70 and CX-90 PHEVs may start successfully on a later attempt, and multiple warning lamps or other codes can accompany the event. Intermittent recovery does not identify the cause by itself.

## How a connector can affect relay interpretation

Mazda describes water-related corrosion at connector 3002-411 in the luggage compartment. Leakage current between terminals can make the main relay appear to be on when it should not be. The battery ECU can then interpret the relay state incorrectly and prevent READY activation. That explanation is different from proving that a high-voltage relay is physically stuck or that the traction motor has failed.

The inspection result controls the repair. If corrosion is present, the bulletin specifies replacement of the connector using its harness-repair method and addition of protective urethane. If corrosion is absent, Mazda directs further MGSS diagnosis. The code and cold-weather symptom alone do not authorize replacing a clean connector or a battery ECU.

## What to record before service

Note the ambient conditions, whether the next start attempt worked, all displayed warnings and the full module scan. Confirmation must include successful READY operation and absence of returning faults after the repair.

This procedure involves specific connector identification, wire mapping and approved repair techniques. It should be performed by a technician familiar with the PHEV system. Do not use this article as an instruction to probe high-voltage circuits or bridge a relay; the documented issue concerns verified connector contamination and its effect on control information.
