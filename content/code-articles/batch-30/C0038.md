# C0038: Ford stripped-chassis ABS faults and subtle tone-ring damage

C0038 on the Ford stripped-chassis applications in SSM 50514 can be associated with damage to a rear wheel-speed sensor ring. Ford notes that even minimal damage may produce an ABS warning and a stored fault. An obvious missing section is therefore not the only kind of ring problem worth considering.

## A quick visual impression may be insufficient

The bulletin warns that small defects can be difficult to see. A ring that looks acceptable during a casual inspection has not necessarily been confirmed sound. At the same time, the code alone does not establish that the ring is damaged. A qualified technician must use the relevant ABS diagnostic procedure and inspection access to decide whether replacement is required.

This is a different decision from replacing the wheel-speed sensor simply because its name appears in the code description. The ring provides the target that the sensor reads, so the target’s physical condition belongs in the investigation of the described case.

## Protect the replacement during installation

Ford also requires inspection of the replacement service part before fitting it. It specifically warns that the ABS sensor must be removed during ring service to avoid damaging the new ring, and that the hub and rotor assembly need appropriate support during installation. A correct part damaged during assembly can leave a new source of the same complaint.

Provide the workshop with the chassis year, full ABS scan and any recent rear-hub work. The service operations involve heavy assemblies and braking-system components, so this article does not supply a home lifting or disassembly procedure. Its useful distinction is careful diagnosis of the original ring followed by protection and verification of the replacement.
