# U0237: Ford audio loss with DSP reset and programming

U0237 may accompany a Ford audio system that does not power on, has no speaker sound or loses communication with the audio control module or digital signal processor. Ford SSM 51778 also describes chimes coming through the instrument cluster instead of the normal audio path. These observations help distinguish the bulletin’s module-level concern from an isolated silent speaker.

## Match the individual production cutoff

The service message covers several models, but most have their own build-date boundary. The date for an F-150 is different from the dates for an Escape, Mach-E, Expedition or Explorer. A broad statement that all 2020–2022 Ford vehicles qualify would erase those restrictions. Confirm the VIN build record and retain the full module scan.

U0184 and U0238 may also be present. The bulletin’s list does not require all three codes, and a communication entry alone does not prove the speaker hardware is defective. Record whether the ACM, DSP or both are inaccessible when the audio fails.

## Recovery and programming are both specified

Ford directs a hard reset of the DSP through the prescribed 12-volt battery-disconnection procedure, followed by DSP reprogramming with the appropriate diagnostic tool. Temporary return of audio after power interruption is not the complete documented correction. The programming step addresses the service condition beyond immediate recovery.

Have a technician follow the correct battery and module procedures for the exact vehicle. This source does not recommend repeated owner resets, automatic DSP replacement or applying another model’s fuse instructions. If the symptoms or production range do not match, the vehicle needs its own audio and network diagnosis.

## Source and applicability

Ford SSM 51778: 2020–2021 Escape built on/before November 23, 2021; 2021 F-150 October 8, 2021; 2021 Mustang Mach-E March 29, 2021; 2021 Expedition August 5, 2021; 2021 Explorer September 27, 2021; and 2020–2022 Edge.
