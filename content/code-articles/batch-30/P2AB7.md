# P2AB7: EcoBoost wastegate faults and the separately serviced actuator kit

P2AB7 is one of the faults in Ford’s EcoBoost wastegate service message for a lack-of-power complaint and illuminated malfunction lamp. SSM 50485 identifies possible actuator failure, a missing retaining clip or damage to the actuator rod. It expressly notes that rod damage may not be visually obvious.

## Separate the electrical wording from the mechanical assembly

A code referring to wastegate position does not, by itself, identify which part of the actuation system failed. A missing clip and an inoperative actuator are different findings, even though both can interfere with the expected operation. The source directs ordinary diagnosis before deciding on the repair; it does not instruct replacing a part merely because P2AB7 appears.

The same caution applies to a rod that looks intact. The bulletin’s reference to non-visible damage means appearance alone is not a complete functional assessment. Record the full code set and lack-of-power behavior so the technician can evaluate the actuator and linkage in context.

## A service kit can avoid an unnecessary complete assembly

If diagnostic procedures lead toward turbocharger replacement for the described issue, Ford identifies a lower-level kit that supplies the actuator, rod and both retaining clips. All components supplied in that kit must be replaced together under the stated procedure. The bulletin does not support fitting only one convenient piece or treating every turbocharger fault as repairable with this kit.

A workshop should verify exact vehicle and engine applicability and current parts suitability. Turbocharger and exhaust components require appropriate cooling and service procedures. This source establishes an alternative after diagnosis, not a guaranteed cause, universal adjustment specification or automatic complete-turbo replacement.

## Source and applicability

Ford SSM 50485: 2019–2022 Edge/Nautilus, 2020–2022 Escape/Corsair and 2021–2022 Bronco Sport with applicable 2.0L or 2.3L EcoBoost configurations described in the bulletin.
