# C0072: brake fade is a combined thermal and deceleration finding

C0072 is a brake-fade finding in this GM implementation. The controller combines estimated rotor temperature, system compliance, boost pressure and measured deceleration. A high-temperature title therefore understates the monitor: it evaluates whether braking behavior fits a modeled fade condition.

## Temperature alone is not the complete test

The threshold column gives two simultaneous conditions: average estimated rotor temperature above 400°C and a brake-fade factor above 50 percent, present for 800 milliseconds. Evaluation requires active boost control, no active slip control and vehicle speed above 4 m/s. These are documented calibration criteria, not safe operating targets or instructions for a road test.

The narrative criteria cell still contains unfinished placeholder wording. Its neighboring threshold column is more specific, but the incomplete text remains a source limitation. The exact vehicle calibration must be confirmed before applying those numbers in service.

## What changes the investigation

Preserve the braking conditions and available thermal, pressure and deceleration records. A temperature estimate and a physical temperature measurement are different evidence; the code does not establish that a particular sensor directly measured the rotor at the stated value.

The source provides no automatic pad, rotor or hydraulic-unit replacement rule. A qualified brake technician needs to investigate the actual braking complaint and the inputs used by the fade model. Clearing C0072 or seeing a lower temperature estimate does not by itself demonstrate restored braking performance. Follow the vehicle's safety and recovery procedures rather than attempting to reproduce brake fade.

## Source and applicability

GM 2020 20OBDG07 electronic brake control module table. Only the cited monitor and calibration are described; per-code vehicle and engine applicability has not been established.
