# U0244: Expedition and Navigator running-board faults from DSM software

U0244 can accompany power running boards that intermittently fail to deploy or retract on the vehicles described by Ford SSM 55262. A running-board fault message may appear, while the driver seat module records C1008:15 or C1009:15. Other modules may store U0244 or U0208, and heated, cooled or memory-seat functions can also be affected.

## A shared controller explains the wider symptom pattern

Ford identifies possible software involvement in the driver seat module, or DSM. The presence of seat-related symptoms alongside running-board faults can therefore be relevant to this case. It does not mean that the seat hardware and both running boards independently failed. Confirm the factory equipment, 2027 model year and September 13, 2026 build cutoff before applying that explanation.

The bulletin distinguishes an offline DSM from the underlying software correction. It describes restoring communication through the specified seat-switch or power-reset procedure, but communication recovery alone is not the complete update. A technician should record which modules were inaccessible before the recovery step.

## Update delivery and repair completion

Ford describes automatic DSM software delivery when vehicle connectivity is enabled, with dealer programming available for customers who do not wait or do not use connectivity. The October 2026 bulletin’s later delivery target is a plan, not proof that a particular vehicle has already received the update.

The workshop correction uses the named DSM software-update application in FDRS. Ask for confirmation of installed software and restored running-board operation. Keep clear of moving boards during assessment. The source does not support replacing the running-board mechanisms solely from U0244 or applying this procedure to earlier Expedition or Navigator models.

## Source and applicability

Ford SSM 55262, October 2026: 2027 Expedition and Navigator equipped with power running boards, built on or before September 13, 2026.
