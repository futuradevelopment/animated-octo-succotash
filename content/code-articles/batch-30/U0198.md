# U0198: Mazda TCU communication alerts and the information the dealer needs

U0198 in Mazda service alert SA-012/26 concerns communication between the connectivity master unit, or CMU, and the emergency-call/telematics unit. The described complaint is a TCU malfunction message; connected-service activation passcodes or remote functions may also fail. The alert identifies U0198:00-03 in the CMU and B1252:87-8B in the TCU.

## Confirm the exact fault before using this procedure

The alert covers the listed Mazda model years, not every Mazda with a failed app command. Its first diagnostic boundary is whether the specified communication faults are stored. If they are absent, Mazda directs normal MGSS troubleshooting instead. Save the complete suffixes and reporting modules, because a generic reader’s shortened code loses useful context.

The procedure also asks the dealer to document customer electrical devices, including USB-connected equipment, dash cameras and similar accessories. Recording a device is not a finding that it caused the fault. Mazda separately instructs replacement of the navigation SD card when one is installed; vehicles without that card proceed to the next stage.

## Why log collection matters

The remaining path includes a connected-vehicle questionnaire, CMU logs, TCU logs and contact with Mazda technical assistance. The original alert says the concern was under investigation. It therefore supports an evidence-gathering and escalation process, not a guaranteed universal module replacement.

Bring a description of failed functions, accessory use and the timing of the warning. Avoid clearing codes before the workshop captures the information requested by the alert. Its March 2026 revision supersedes earlier notices, but does not establish the status of any later investigation or software release.

## Source and applicability

Mazda SA-012/26 revision published March 2026: 2024–2026 Mazda3, CX-30 and CX-90; 2025–2026 CX-70 and CX-50, including all variants identified by the alert.
