# U0232: Expedition and Navigator radar warnings need contamination and wiring checks

U0232 may appear in the IPMA during the Expedition and Navigator rear-radar condition described by Ford TSB 24-2180. The customer sees intermittent blind-spot or cross-traffic unavailable warnings even when no object is nearby, sometimes more noticeably in wet weather. Ford identifies both contamination behind the bumper and damage or corrosion at radar components as possible causes.

## A blocked sensor message does not establish a failed sensor

Mud, snow or ice between the radar and bumper cover can affect operation without requiring sensor replacement. Corrosion in a radar sensor connector is a different finding: the bulletin directs replacement of the affected sensor. Harness or connector damage has its own pigtail-repair branch.

The procedure also distinguishes communication codes from visible contamination. When the relevant communication fault remains unexplained by the inspection, Ford calls for the separate U0232/U0233 diagnostic procedure. Cleaning the bumper is not a substitute for diagnosing an unresolved communication circuit.

## Prevent the same exposure after repair

Sensors that are not replaced and the corresponding inside bumper surfaces are cleaned. The procedure then installs rear-wheel splash shields appropriate to the model and wheelbase. This makes inspection, any necessary component repair, cleaning and protection separate parts of the remedy.

Give the workshop the exact messages, weather conditions and full IPMA and side-obstacle-module scan. Do not rely on unavailable blind-spot or cross-traffic assistance while the warning is present. Bumper removal, wiring work and sensor service should follow Ford’s instructions. The bulletin does not support replacing both radar sensors simply because U0232 was retrieved.

## Source and applicability

Ford TSB 24-2180, June 5, 2024: North American 2022–2024 Expedition and Navigator with intermittent blind-spot or cross-traffic sensor-blocked warnings and no nearby object.
