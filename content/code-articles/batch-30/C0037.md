# C0037: Bronco and Ranger startup brake faults with specific suffixes

C0037 on a 2025 Bronco or Ranger needs its suffix and operating context preserved. Ford SSM 53875 specifically lists C0037:4A and C0037:64 among ABS entries accompanying brake-system warnings at startup. It is not a general declaration that every left-rear wheel-speed fault on these vehicles is software-related.

## The self-test result separates the paths

Ford’s service sequence clears the stored entries, cycles ignition, operates the parking brake and runs a self-test. If any faults return, the message directs normal Workshop Manual diagnosis. If they clear, the described concern may be due to ABS software, and replacing the ABS module or wheel-speed sensors would not resolve that case.

This distinction is easy to lose when looking only at a screenshot of C0037. A code that returns during the prescribed evaluation and a historical startup entry that does not return lead to different actions. The complete suffix, other ABS codes and exact warning timing should be recorded before any clearing procedure.

## A historical software notice is not proof of a completed fix

The June 2025 message described engineering work toward a later solution. It does not confirm that the vehicle has received any subsequent update. Ask the workshop to verify applicable current information and the installed software before drawing that conclusion.

Because the complaint involves brake warnings, have braking operation assessed rather than relying on the disappearance of an icon. Do not replace a sensor merely from the code title, and do not dismiss a persistent warning as the bulletin’s temporary software case. The evidence needed is the qualified technician’s result from the relevant procedure.

## Source and applicability

Ford SSM 53875, June 2025: 2025 Bronco and Ranger with startup brake warnings and C0037:4A or C0037:64 in the ABS module, optionally with the other listed faults.
