# C05A4: wheel-speed supply monitoring can involve an internal controller check

The cited GM C05A4 monitor checks the supply used for wheel-speed sensing inside the brake controller. Its electronics compare internal voltage measurements rather than identifying one wheel as the failed component. A generic scanner description should not turn this shared-supply finding into an automatic wheel-sensor replacement.

## The hardware design changes the check

The controller reads a wheel-speed supply voltage result supplied by its application-specific integrated circuit, or ASIC, over an internal serial interface. That result must show adequate voltage for wheel-speed operation. The table does not publish a numerical minimum that can be used as a universal external test limit.

For controllers without wheel-speed supply regulation, the strategy adds a plausibility comparison between two internal supply measurements. That extra comparison belongs to the specified hardware variant. It should not be assumed to exist in every controller that can report C05A4.

## Separate a supply finding from a missing wheel signal

The monitor requires the internal system to be initialized, and its listed evaluation time is 15 milliseconds. Those details distinguish it from a road-speed comparison that needs the vehicle moving. An absence of wheel-speed data during a supply fault does not independently prove that the wheel sensor caused the supply problem.

Record the reporting module, associated supply or communication faults and the actual controller configuration. The source lists a defective ASIC as an investigated condition, but supplies no external pinout or complete repair decision tree. Brake-system testing and any controller replacement must follow the matching service procedure; internal monitor names are not instructions to probe the module.

## Source and applicability

GM 2020 20OBDG07 electronic brake control module table. Only the cited monitor and calibration are described; per-code vehicle and engine applicability has not been established.
