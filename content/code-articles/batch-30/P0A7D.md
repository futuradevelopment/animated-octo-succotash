# P0A7D: Ford hybrid battery discharge does not automatically mean pack replacement

P0A7D in Ford SSM 49163 concerns low high-voltage battery charge on 2020 Escape and Explorer hybrids. Ford identifies extended non-use and a crank/no-start condition as possible backgrounds. The code describes a charge state; it does not, by itself, establish that the battery pack is irreversibly damaged.

## Charging suitability depends on more than total voltage

The bulletin directs the technician to the applicable service information to decide whether off-board charging is required. It warns that voltage limits in the referenced general service bulletin do not apply unchanged to these two 2020 models. Even Escape and Explorer use different total-pack-voltage windows in this specific procedure.

The decision also considers minimum battery voltage and voltage variation data. That distinction matters because a total voltage reading alone does not describe the condition of the lowest monitored portion or the spread between readings. Copying one model’s total-voltage threshold to the other would miss the bulletin’s explicit boundary.

## A rescue charge is a controlled service operation

When the scan values are within the specified limits and the pack is suitable, Ford directs the workshop to contact technical assistance for the appropriate high-voltage charger. This is not an instruction to connect an ordinary charger to the traction battery or improvise access to high-voltage terminals.

Provide the technician with storage duration, the sequence of no-start attempts and the complete fault record. The service assessment should distinguish a discharged but chargeable pack from a condition outside the permitted limits, and should also address the original no-start cause when applicable. The bulletin does not authorize pack replacement or charging solely from P0A7D.
