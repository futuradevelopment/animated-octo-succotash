# U3012: Ford startup brake warnings and a historical ABS communication case

U3012:68 in Ford SSM 53683 is associated with a specific startup event: a soft brake pedal, brake warning lamps or messages, and a communication concern within the ABS module. That combination is more informative than the shortened U3012 code alone. The bulletin does not diagnose every brake warning on these vehicles.

## Recovery and verified braking are separate steps

Ford describes allowing all modules to power down completely before restarting. It specifically notes that key-fob activity, opening doors or keeping the fob near the vehicle can prevent that shutdown. A quick ignition cycle therefore is not equivalent to the controlled power-down condition in the service message.

The procedure then requires verification of brake functionality and clearing the diagnostic entries. Disappearance of a warning by itself is not a substitute for verifying the brakes. Because the reported symptom includes a soft pedal, arrange professional assessment and do not use a public-road braking test to decide whether the vehicle has recovered.

## Do not turn an interim message into a permanent repair claim

The May 2025 document said ABS module replacement would not resolve this described condition and that engineering was developing a solution. Its projected release timing is historical information, not confirmation of a later update’s availability or installation.

Give the workshop the exact suffix, startup sequence, pedal symptom and complete warning list. Ask it to check the applicable current service information and confirm the vehicle’s actual braking condition. This bulletin supports avoiding an unsupported ABS-module replacement in its defined software case; it does not justify dismissing persistent low pedal travel, hydraulic faults or warnings outside that case.

## Source and applicability

Ford SSM 53683, May 2025: 2025 F-150, Expedition, Navigator, Bronco and Ranger with U3012:68 in the ABS module, soft pedal and brake warnings following startup.
