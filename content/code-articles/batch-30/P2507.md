# P2507: Mazda transport-mode starts can leave a low-voltage history code

P2507:00 can be a historical consequence of transport-mode operation in Mazda TSB 01-002/20. The bulletin describes starting the engine three or more times with short shutdown intervals while the 15-amp room fuse is removed. In that condition, P2507 may accompany P2610 after startup or be discovered later without an illuminated warning lamp.

## The P2507 year restriction is narrower than the headline list

Although the bulletin’s vehicle list begins earlier for some models, Mazda explicitly limits this P2507 situation to 2016 and later. Applying the earliest CX-5 or Mazda6 year in the heading to P2507 would overlook that restriction. The documented use case also concerns dealer-inventory vehicles, not an established explanation for every low-voltage fault in daily service.

The transport-mode odometer indication and room-fuse history help distinguish the case from an ongoing PCM power-supply problem. A stored code without a present warning is possible, so record its status and the accompanying P2610 rather than assuming a current electrical failure.

## The corrective sequence preserves a diagnostic boundary

Mazda instructs the technician to restore the correct room fuse with ignition off, carry out the specified engine-run and restart sequence, verify the warning lamp and clear the two recorded entries. Other remaining faults require their own MGSS or Workshop Manual diagnosis.

The bulletin does not call for PCM replacement or identify a universally defective battery. If the vehicle has no relevant transport or fuse history, or the power fault persists after the prescribed work, this narrow explanation is insufficient. Ask the workshop to evaluate the actual supply condition instead of repeatedly clearing a returning code.

## Source and applicability

Mazda TSB 01-002/20, January 23, 2020: its P2507 case applies only to 2016-and-newer vehicles within the listed CX-5, Mazda6, Mazda3, CX-3, MX-5 and CX-9 ranges; Mazda3 coverage ends at 2018 and the others at 2020.
