# P1153: Bank 2 front oxygen-sensor switching must meet the monitor criteria

P1153 applies to Bank 2 Sensor 1 in GM's group 07 oxygen-sensor monitoring table. The controller assesses both half-cycle switching and slope-time behavior. A signal that occasionally changes from rich to lean can still fail if too few transitions meet the required criteria.

## Bank identity and qualification matter

This is the front sensor on Bank 2 in the documented implementation, not an instruction to replace a downstream sensor or every sensor on the engine. Confirm the actual vehicle's bank and sensor identification before using the generic description to select a component.

The source uses a 70-second sample window after heater and engine-running prerequisites are met. Low fuel status, sensor circuit faults, misfire and several fuel or air-system faults affect whether the evaluation can run. A monitor that remains incomplete while one of those conditions exists has not demonstrated a good sensor.

## Separate observed switching from a completed pass

The source refers to supporting half-cycle thresholds and also evaluates slope-time switching. A short scan-tool trace may not cover the complete observation window or expose the controller's qualification logic.

Preserve companion faults and the operating state, then use the exact engine's service procedure to assess the response. The code alone does not identify the underlying cause of insufficient switching. Verification should establish a completed qualified monitor after correction. Erasing P1153 or observing a few normal-looking transitions cannot substitute for that result.
