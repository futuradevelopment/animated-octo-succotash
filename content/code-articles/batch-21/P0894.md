# P0894: Slip during a Neutral Locked Turbine request

The generic P0894 name is transmission component slipping. GM's 2009 Allison summary describes a narrower monitor: turbine slip during a Neutral Locked Turbine, or NLT, request from the engine controller. It must not be treated as a universal test of every clutch during ordinary gear changes.

## Counted events belong to the current ignition cycle

An event is accumulated when commanded or attained range is NLT and turbine speed exceeds 50 rpm for more than three seconds. The failure decision uses at least three such events in the current ignition cycle. The source explicitly subtracts events carried over from previous cycles when forming that count.

This distinction matters when examining a lifetime or accumulated counter. A nonzero historical count does not automatically equal the current-cycle failure criterion.

## The requested operating state is essential evidence

The controller must have the relevant NLT state and qualified power and engine conditions. Turbine rotation during a different range or request cannot simply be substituted into this monitor's rule.

Record the requested and attained state, turbine-speed evidence and event-count context through the proper transmission diagnostic equipment. The summary does not identify a specific worn clutch or authorize a rebuild solely from P0894. Verification should demonstrate correct behavior during the prescribed NLT assessment and completed monitoring. An ordinary shift that feels normal, or an erased code, may never exercise the condition that this particular check evaluates.
