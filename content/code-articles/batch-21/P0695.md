# P0695: Fan relay 3 low-side diagnostic can also indicate an open circuit

GM's group 12 P0695 entry concerns relay 3 in the cooling-fan control system. The output driver performs the electrical diagnosis. In this implementation, the low-direction classification includes both a short to ground and an open circuit, so the generic word low should not be treated as proof of one specific wiring defect.

## Verify that the configured channel exists

The published conditions require the fan power stage to be configured, together with the specified automatic-transmission and air-conditioning configuration. These conditions limit the source's applicability. The relay number cannot be used to identify a physical relay or connector location on a different vehicle.

The monitor runs with ignition on, the engine running and battery voltage above nine volts. Its failure interval is 3.2 seconds, with continuous checks at the stated sampling interval. An unqualified engine-off observation is not equivalent to that test.

## Driver diagnosis is separate from cooling performance

A circuit fault can prevent correct fan control, but P0695 does not directly measure cooling capacity or establish whether an engine is overheating. Preserve the fan command, related electrical faults and actual temperature evidence separately.

Use the vehicle-specific circuit procedure to distinguish an open path from a ground-related fault. Verification should establish valid driver feedback and the fan operation prescribed by service information. A fan that happens to spin briefly cannot alone demonstrate that relay channel 3 passes its electrical monitor.
