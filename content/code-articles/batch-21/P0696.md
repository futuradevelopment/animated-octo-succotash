# P0696: Fan relay 3 driver reports a high-circuit condition

P0696 is the short-to-battery classification for fan relay 3 in GM's group 12 table. The electronic output driver performs the diagnosis. A high-circuit report therefore needs to be interpreted through the driver's commanded state and feedback, rather than treated as direct evidence of excessive fan speed.

## Configuration limits the meaning of relay 3

The source requires the relevant fan power stage, automatic-transmission configuration and air-conditioning configuration. These qualifications prevent a relay number in this document from becoming a universal physical location. Confirm the actual vehicle's circuit identity before selecting a relay or connector to test.

The monitored condition must persist for 3.2 seconds. Ignition-on, running-engine and battery-voltage requirements also apply, and a controller communication fault can inhibit the test. A check made under different conditions may not reproduce the stored result.

## High circuit and low circuit are different classifications

The adjacent P0695 row covers ground-related or open conditions. Preserving the exact code helps keep those electrical paths separate. Neither entry measures coolant flow or proves that the engine's cooling requirement is being met.

Record the fan command, supply conditions and companion faults for the prescribed service procedure. Verification should include a completed driver check and the required fan-function assessment. Hearing a fan operate briefly, or clearing the memory, does not establish that the monitored relay channel behaves correctly in its relevant states.
