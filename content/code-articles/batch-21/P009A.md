# P009A: Ambient-temperature plausibility during warm operation

The generic P009A label mentions intake and ambient temperature correlation. GM's 2009 group 12 implementation is more specific: it checks measured ambient air temperature against a modeled ambient value during warm-start and normal driving conditions. That model-based interpretation matters when a cold parked comparison looks reasonable but the code returns on the road.

## Learning comes before judgment

The published difference threshold is 20.3°C. The model's learning conditions require relatively stable measured ambient temperature and at least 45 seconds after the learning conditions are met. A momentary reading immediately after startup does not reproduce that evaluation.

The operating window also includes vehicle speed, airflow, engine speed and warm coolant requirements. These are prerequisites for this calibration, not instructions to reproduce a fault at a particular road speed. Monitor-status and recorded operating data are the safer way to establish whether the comparison actually ran.

## Distinguish a plausible signal from an accurate one

The adjacent P0072 and P0073 checks address electrically extreme ambient-sensor readings. P009A can instead concern a signal that remains within its electrical range but disagrees with the model. Replacing a sensor merely because its name appears in the code would skip that distinction.

Review measured ambient temperature, the conditions under which it learned, and related temperature faults together. Verification should show successful completion under the applicable warm-operation conditions, not just agreement between two parked readings.
