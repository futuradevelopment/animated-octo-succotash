# P023A: Intercooler pump command and circuit feedback disagree

This GM P023A entry applies to the supercharger intercooler coolant-pump control circuit. The controller compares its requested output-driver state with the electrical state it observes. A disagreement is circuit evidence, rather than a direct measurement of coolant circulation through the charge-air cooler.

## What the monitor can establish

The published enable window includes ignition voltage between 11 and 18 volts. The distinction matters because a control circuit observed outside its documented operating conditions may not reproduce the recorded diagnostic result.

The table identifies an electrical-integrity check without specifying a particular failed component. A failed pump, its control path and controller feedback cannot be separated solely from this code. Likewise, a pump that appears to run does not automatically prove that its monitored control circuit behaves correctly in both commanded states.

## One printed counter needs clarification

The sampling entry contains an impossible-looking failure-count denominator. It is retained as an unresolved documentation issue, not rewritten into a guessed test duration. The meaningful supported conclusion is command-versus-feedback disagreement, not a universal number of seconds to wait.

Capture the command state, available feedback information and supply conditions from the failure record. Diagnosis should follow the applicable electrical procedure before assuming a mechanical cooling restriction. After any correction, verify completed circuit monitoring as well as the pump function required by that vehicle's service information.
