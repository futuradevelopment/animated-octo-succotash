# P0788: Timing-solenoid circuit stays high under an off command

P0788 checks the timing-solenoid electrical circuit in GM's GMDAT transmission summary. The table describes a high or open condition when the controller commands the monitored signal off but observes the on-state voltage instead.

## An open circuit can share the high classification

The word high does not prove that a wire is directly shorted to supply. The source explicitly includes an open circuit in this classification. The vehicle's actual driver and feedback arrangement determines how those faults appear, so the matching circuit procedure is essential.

The monitor waits after a solenoid-output change, requires its diagnostic state to be active and excludes emergency mode. It then counts three sustained failure events, each lasting half a second. These qualifications distinguish a repeated electrical disagreement from a normal switching transition.

## Preserve the off-command evidence

A useful record identifies the requested state and observed feedback at the failure. A high voltage without that command context cannot reproduce the diagnostic conclusion. The neighboring P0787 row concerns the opposite relationship: low feedback under an on command.

The summary does not identify a failed solenoid versus wiring or control electronics solely from P0788. After the prescribed diagnosis and correction, verify that feedback follows both relevant commanded states and that monitoring completes. A normal shift on one occasion or a cleared fault list does not establish that the original electrical disagreement is gone.
