# P040F: EGR temperature comparison after a long soak

P040F is a correlation check between two EGR temperature sensors in the 2009 Duramax summary. The stated purpose is to detect biased readings after an engine-off soak. That differs from testing either sensor for an electrically extreme signal while the exhaust is hot.

## Cooling history is part of the evidence

The documented conditions include more than five hours with the engine off, ignition on and intake temperature above −7°C. Separate low- and high-circuit faults for the two temperature sensors must be absent. Those prerequisites make the comparison meaningful; a hot restart or an active circuit fault does not reproduce the same test.

A diagnostic record should preserve both temperatures at the qualified restart and the soak history. Agreement later in operation cannot establish what the two readings were when this monitor evaluated them.

## The threshold direction remains unresolved

The table prints an absolute-difference expression with a less-than-or-equal-to sign, despite describing a bias-detection failure. This article does not silently reverse that sign or use the value as a repair limit. Exact calibration documentation is needed before judging a measured difference numerically.

The supported diagnostic distinction is correlation versus individual circuit integrity. Determine whether each signal is credible before deciding which, if either, is biased. Verification should confirm a completed qualified-soak comparison after repair, rather than only an absence of codes following a short warm restart.

## Source and applicability
