# P0418: Secondary-air pump control is an electrical test

The documented P0418 check concerns the electrical control of the secondary-air pump. In GM's group 07 table, it fails when the output-driver command and the observed control-circuit state disagree. It does not directly measure the volume of air delivered to the exhaust.

## Pump noise cannot settle the circuit diagnosis

A pump heard running at one moment may not reproduce the commanded state or electrical conditions present when the fault set. The monitor repeatedly compares feedback in its operating voltage window, rather than relying on an audible indication.

This calibration evaluates 50 failing observations in a 63-sample window at a 250-millisecond interval. System voltage must be above 10 and below 18 volts. Those details describe the controller's evaluation and should not be converted into an improvised relay-jumper test.

## Keep the valve and airflow questions distinct

The neighboring P0412 entry covers the secondary-air solenoid circuit. A valve-circuit code and a pump-circuit code identify different monitored channels even though both affect the same emissions system. Flow-related faults add another layer of evidence and cannot identify the pump's electrical defect by themselves.

Retain the pump command, available feedback and companion codes for the applicable service procedure. After correction, confirm a completed pump-circuit check and the functional verification specified for that vehicle. A cleared code list or a pump that runs briefly does not establish that the monitored control path is reliable.
