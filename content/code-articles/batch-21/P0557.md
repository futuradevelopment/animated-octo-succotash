# P0557: Low brake-booster pressure sensor voltage

The P0557 monitor in GM's group 02 table checks brake-booster pressure sensor voltage. Its published low threshold is below 0.195 volts, with a two-second detection interval. This is an electrical signal boundary for the documented application, not a direct statement about the actual booster pressure.

## Low voltage is not a complete brake diagnosis

A low reported signal can make pressure information unreliable. The code does not measure braking performance or establish whether assistance is adequate. If pedal effort or braking behavior changes, assessment must follow the vehicle's brake-system guidance and appropriate professional procedures.

The neighboring P0558 entry checks the opposite electrical direction, while P0556 evaluates pressure plausibility. Separating a low circuit signal from an in-range but implausible pressure reading prevents the wrong diagnostic path from being chosen.

## Establish applicability before electrical testing

The source explicitly restricts these rows to internal application groups. It does not supply a model-wide fitment list or a universal connector pinout. Use the exact vehicle wiring and service information rather than applying the printed threshold to any car carrying P0557.

Preserve the low-signal record and related faults before work. Verification should demonstrate a valid sensor signal, completed circuit monitoring and any brake-function checks required by the service procedure. An erased code, by itself, does not establish that either the signal or brake assistance has been restored.
