# P011B: Coolant and intake temperatures after an engine-off period

This P011B implementation checks whether coolant and intake-air temperature disagree after an engine-off period. GM's group 05 table evaluates the difference after 20 seconds of engine operation. Either coolant being more than 15°C above intake temperature or intake temperature being more than 15°C above coolant can meet the stated comparison.

## Heat history determines whether the test is meaningful

The listed prerequisites include more than 15 minutes powered down, adequate running during the previous driving cycle and a limited change in intake temperature after restart. The table also considers intake-temperature rise after the preceding shutdown. These conditions show why two temperatures taken at an arbitrary moment are not an equivalent test.

A block-heater start and a controller reset are specifically excluded. Both can disrupt the assumptions behind a thermal comparison. A monitor that has not completed after either event should not be counted as a successful repair check.

## What to compare before choosing a sensor

Preserve the power-up temperatures, the readings around the evaluation point and the preceding shutdown history. The code identifies a disagreement; it does not establish which sensor is inaccurate.

An electrical fault, unusual heat history and a plausible but biased temperature input require different investigations. Use the actual vehicle's service procedure to separate them. Verification should demonstrate that the qualified restart comparison ran and passed, rather than only showing matching readings after the engine has fully warmed.
