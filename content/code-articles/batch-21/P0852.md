# P0852: Park-neutral input remains asserted against transmission evidence

P0852 is the opposite-direction park/neutral comparison in GM's Duramax summary. Here, the engine controller's sensed switch input indicates Park or Neutral while the transmission controller's GMLAN message disagrees. That distinction separates it from P0851's transmission-reported Park or Neutral condition.

## The check is qualified by driving-state evidence

The published prerequisites include engine speed, vehicle speed, torque and pedal position, together with a bounded battery-voltage range. The monitor therefore uses more context than a stationary selector-position observation. The table requires battery voltage above 11 volts and below 18 volts during the comparison. These are diagnostic qualifications, not instructions to test an uncertain range circuit by driving under load.

The disagreement must persist for one second, with the relevant communication and opposite-direction faults absent. A companion communication failure can prevent a valid comparison rather than demonstrate that the position disagreement has disappeared.

## Compare both controller views

Preserve the engine controller's direct status and the transmission controller's reported status separately. A dashboard indication alone may not expose both paths. The code does not identify which switch, wiring path or interpretation is responsible.

Use the vehicle-specific service procedure to assess the range information safely. Verification should establish consistent status recognition and a completed monitor in the prescribed conditions. Simply clearing P0852 or obtaining one correct selector reading leaves the original two-controller disagreement unresolved.
