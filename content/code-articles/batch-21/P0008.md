# P0008: Bank 1 cam timing disagreement and learned alignment

P0008 addresses the relationship between the crankshaft and both camshafts on Bank 1. In GM's 2009 group 02 table, it sits beside the Bank 1 idler-sprocket description. The controller evaluates learned alignment rather than simply checking whether a cam sensor sends a signal.

## Why the two cam readings matter

The table separates this bank-level check from P0016 and P0017, which identify individual intake and exhaust cam relationships. Two cam offsets moving together therefore present a different diagnostic question from one isolated cam discrepancy. That distinction supports comparing the relevant learned values before selecting a component to inspect; it does not prove an idler or chain has failed.

The monitor also depends on an adaptation process. The listed conditions exclude active camshaft sensor and cam control circuit errors. A missing or invalid signal must be understood before treating its learned angle as reliable mechanical evidence.

## What a useful verification records

Record which bank is affected, the companion cam codes, learned alignment status and whether the alignment check actually completed. A code disappearing before adaptation finishes is weak evidence of correction.

The printed threshold cell does not clearly supply both directional limits for the paired cam criteria. Do not turn the visible value into a universal timing tolerance. Obtain the exact calibration's diagnostic procedure before judging a borderline angle or authorizing timing-system work.
