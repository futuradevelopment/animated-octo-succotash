# P012C: Low supercharger inlet-pressure signal with deadbands

P012C is the low-circuit member of the documented supercharger inlet absolute-pressure sensor checks. GM describes a continuous short-to-low condition or an open in the signal circuit or sensor. An open circuit therefore cannot be dismissed merely because the code says low.

## Electrical evidence comes before pressure interpretation

This sensor implementation has electrical deadbands. The controller treats readings outside the intended signal region as circuit evidence rather than as a trustworthy low-pressure measurement. Consequently, the code does not establish that actual inlet pressure fell to the displayed extreme.

The table uses 320 failing samples out of 400, sampled every 12.5 milliseconds. That accumulated pattern is more specific than a single low sample. Available failure records and signal history can help distinguish a sustained problem from an observation taken after the fault disappeared.

## Read the printed limit cautiously

The threshold cell gives a percentage of the five-volt range and a parenthetical voltage that do not match exactly. This article does not choose one as a universal test limit. Obtain the applicable sensor and calibration specifications before electrical testing.

The neighboring P012D check identifies the opposite electrical direction, while P012B concerns in-range performance. Preserving that distinction guides the next investigation. Confirm restored circuit plausibility and successful monitoring after repair; clearing the code alone does not verify the signal or its response.
