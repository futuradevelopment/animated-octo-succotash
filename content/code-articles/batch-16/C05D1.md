# C05D1: Supply 1 supports both pedal and motor-position sensing

C05D1 is the first sensor-supply circuit diagnosis in the cited brake controller. The supply powers one pedal-travel circuit and one motor-position circuit. This shared dependency matters when both kinds of feedback report faults around the same event.

## Defined range checks with excluded states

The documented low branch requires feedback below 4.85 V for 100 milliseconds. The high branch requires feedback above 5.15 V for 100 milliseconds. Both associated sensor circuits must be enabled.

The monitor excludes initialization, reinitialization, engine cranking, certain diagnostic-command states and shutdown. A voltage captured during one of those excluded periods cannot be treated automatically as an eligible C05D1 failure. Equally, a normal reading after startup may not explain a recorded event from a later operating period.

## Investigate the common supply before isolated conclusions

Preserve the supply-1 status alongside pedal and motor-position validity. The fault can direct attention to a shared electrical dependency, but the table does not prove whether a connected load, internal board or supply-generation fault is responsible. It does not instruct replacing both sensors.

The exact application wiring and service routine are needed for electrical isolation and measurement. Do not power the circuit externally or bypass the controller's supply protection. Verification should show the relevant range check completing in an enabled state and the required brake-system function assessment. Erasing the code or seeing one valid position value cannot establish that the shared supply is dependable or that braking assistance is restored.
