# C0579: Brake booster temperature channel B has a low electrical input

C0579 concerns temperature sensor channel B within the documented brake-booster electronics. The source describes a signal shorted toward ground and checks the second temperature channel's voltage. The low label refers to the input circuit, not a conclusion that the booster is too cold.

## Repetition matters

The published criterion is signal voltage below 0.03 V for five consecutive observations. The table lists 0.600 second and continuous monitoring with ignition on. It assigns a two-trip response. The summary does not define a universal sample interval that should be calculated by dividing the listed time by five.

This threshold is different from the adjacent temperature channel A limits. Substituting another channel's specification would be an incorrect comparison even though both channels serve the same brake-booster assembly.

## Keep channel identity and validity together

Record channel B's signal status, the relevant temperature reading and companion supply or sensor faults. A converted temperature value may be less useful than the raw range-fault information once the input has been rejected. The code does not independently distinguish a sensor element, internal circuit or wiring cause.

No connector pin or safe external test is supplied by the summary. Use the exact brake-system service procedure before electrical access, and do not bypass the sensor to imitate a valid reading. Verification requires the qualified channel-B range check and the prescribed brake-function assessment. Erasing C0579 or observing a warning disappear does not prove that temperature monitoring or braking capability has recovered.
