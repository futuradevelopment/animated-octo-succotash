# C05C2: Booster overtemperature code also covers torque-limited replenishment

C05C2 is labelled brake-booster motor overtemperature, but the cited monitor contains more than a temperature comparison. One branch reports a torque-limited replenishment failure, while others directly evaluate electronic-control-unit temperature.

## Read the branch before assuming heat

The replenishment branch requires motor torque limitation, unsuccessful replenishment and actual pressure below its target. Its description includes limitation due to high temperature or low-voltage/current constraints. Consequently, the generic overtemperature title does not establish that heat was the only limiting factor.

The direct temperature branches list ECU temperatures above 120°C and above 142°C, with the booster temperature sensors considered fault-free. These are internal monitor levels, not safe-touch limits or temperatures to reproduce during a workshop test.

## Preserve limitation and pressure evidence

Retrieve the specific failure state, torque-limitation reason, target pressure and actual replenishment response. A genuine thermal level and a pressure shortfall while torque is restricted lead to different diagnostic questions. The source does not identify a replacement motor, control unit or hydraulic component solely from this code.

Professional brake-system diagnosis must follow the exact service procedure and account for warning indications and any reduced assistance. Do not repeatedly operate the booster to force it into the temperature thresholds. Verification should address the original limiting condition and complete the prescribed pressure and braking checks. A cooler component or a cleared code alone does not demonstrate that replenishment and dependable assistance have recovered.
