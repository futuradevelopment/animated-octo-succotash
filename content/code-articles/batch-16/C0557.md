# C0557: Left-rear wheel-speed protocol does not match expectations

C0557 identifies an incorrect-component concern for the left-rear wheel-speed sensor. In the cited strategy, that conclusion comes from protocol characteristics. It is not a direct mechanical measurement of the sensor's dimensions or the tone wheel's air gap.

## Two documented protocol routes

For the VDA variants, a received protocol-bit value other than nine is the failure criterion. For the DF11i entry, the controller looks for a missing stop pulse. Both use a three-second qualification with ignition on and the relevant wheel-speed undervoltage tests complete.

This variant dependence matters when reviewing repair history. A sensor with the right connector can still provide the wrong message structure. However, a protocol fault does not by itself establish that an incompatible part was installed; electrical conditions and the exact receiver configuration still need verification.

## What to compare during diagnosis

Retain the sensor's application identification and the specific protocol result from the left-rear channel. Do not substitute a front-sensor stop-pulse rule without checking the documented variant. A plausible speed value is only part of the data and does not demonstrate that the identification test passed.

The source contains no approved interchange list or connector-probing instructions. Brake-system diagnosis must follow the matching service procedure. After correction, verify the relevant protocol check with its supply prerequisites satisfied and complete the prescribed functional checks. A code erase or a brief movement that produces wheel-speed pulses is not sufficient to confirm sensor compatibility or dependable ABS operation.
