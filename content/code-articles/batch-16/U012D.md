# U012D: A missing generator response on the documented LIN channel

U012D is the lost-generator-communication code in this application. The cited monitor names the LIN3 generator response frame, ECM_LIN3_LGEN_Rsp2_MSG, as the item that is not being received. That focuses the diagnosis on receipt of a particular response, rather than directly on alternator output voltage.

## Missing response versus a bus-off combination

The same page gives U1347 a combined LIN3 bus-off and generator-timeout description. The U012D row itself only states the missing-response condition. It should not be expanded automatically into proof that the entire LIN channel is bus-off or that the generator has stopped producing electrical output.

The table lists a two-trip response but leaves the timeout and detailed enabling conditions blank. Its sender/receiver wording also refers awkwardly to the engine ECU. These omissions are retained rather than borrowing a three-event qualification from neighbouring, different message rows.

## Preserve communication and charging evidence separately

Record the missing-frame status, the reporting module and any accompanying LIN channel or supply faults. Charging voltage and generator demand provide context, but normal output at one instant does not prove the expected response frame arrived. Similarly, absent data alone does not identify a failed regulator.

Use the exact LIN topology and diagnostic procedure to determine the intended sender, receiver and timeout. Do not inject signals or replace the generator from this summary. Verification requires the specified response to be received during an eligible communication check; a code erase or a battery-voltage reading is not equivalent to that result.
