# U043A: Damper-control information fails protected-message validation

U043A is associated with damper-control information in the cited brake-controller network checks. The receiver evaluates protected messages after they arrive and are unpacked. It is therefore different from a direct electrical or mechanical test of a suspension damper.

## Protection and rolling-count evidence

The source checks alive rolling count and protection-value errors and uses an eight-of-sixteen sliding-window threshold. Its fastest maturation is described as eight consecutive count-sequence errors. The fault reflects message validation, not a measured ride-height or damping-force limit.

The check is suppressed during start mode, specified five-second periods after power transitions or resets, supply recovery and bus-off or recovery states. These exclusions matter because network data may be temporarily unavailable or untrusted during a state transition without reproducing an eligible monitor event.

## Identify the rejected information

Retain the damper-control message identity, protection/freshness status and the storing module. A normal-looking suspension value on a generic scan screen cannot prove the underlying protected message was accepted. Likewise, the generic suspension-module-B name does not establish a universal module location.

The table lists possible issues at the source, sender, bus wiring or receiver rather than one confirmed cause. Use the application network diagnostic procedure to distinguish them. Verification requires valid protected information under eligible conditions and the prescribed chassis checks. Clearing U043A or noticing improved ride feel does not demonstrate that the receiver's integrity check passed or that all related control functions have recovered.
