# C0503: Excessive left-front wheel-speed signal current

C0503 is the left-front wheel-speed circuit-high diagnosis in this GM brake-controller table. The monitor looks at current in the signal line, not simply at an unusually high displayed wheel speed. That distinction prevents a signal-range complaint from being mistaken for this electrical fault.

## The published electrical boundary

The source flags signal current above 0.05 A for 0.120 second. It describes the condition as a short of the left-front signal line to battery. Ignition must be on, and wheel-speed sensor undervoltage checks must have completed without the listed faults.

Those supply prerequisites matter when several corners report problems. A channel may not be evaluated normally while a supply-related test is unresolved. Equally, a current-based high fault cannot be reconstructed from a wheel-speed trace alone, even if the trace contains a spike.

## Preserve the electrical classification

Record the left-front high-circuit status, companion supply results and event context. The code supports investigating the signal circuit's electrical condition; it does not independently prove whether the sensor, harness or controller connection caused the excessive current.

The 50 mA figure is a monitor threshold, not an instruction to insert a meter or apply external power to the wheel-speed line. Use the application's approved circuit test and brake-system precautions. After repair, confirm the electrical monitor and the specified functional checks. A clean code list immediately after clearing does not establish a completed pass or guarantee restored anti-lock and stability-control operation.
