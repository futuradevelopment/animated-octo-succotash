# C0594: Booster motor performance can mean travel, self-test or pressure mismatch

C0594 is a broad brake-booster motor performance code in the cited table. It covers failure to find a mechanical backward bound, a hardware self-test result and disagreement between expected pressure and pressure sensor 2. The specific failure word is needed to understand the event.

## Different observations share the code

One branch compares plunger travel with its defined length when checking the backward bound. Another performs a motor hardware test cyclically every twenty seconds while the motor is not actuated. These are different from a pressure-response test during operation.

The pressure branches compare calculated pressure with sensor 2. The fast branch combines measured pressure above 10 bar with a calculated-minus-measured difference above 40 bar; the slower branch also covers a large discrepancy in the opposite direction. These comparisons depend on valid measurement and model context.

## Ask what failed before replacing the motor

Preserve the travel state, self-test result or pressure comparison relevant to the recorded subtype. A failed idle hardware test cannot be explained solely by a low hydraulic response, and a pressure disagreement is not automatically proof of a damaged electric motor.

The summary supplies no procedure for driving the plunger against an end stop or testing the hydraulic circuit. Use the exact professional brake-system method. Verification should repeat the appropriate controlled monitor and the prescribed functional checks. Erasing the generic performance code without resolving its branch leaves the original evidence unexplained and cannot certify safe braking.
