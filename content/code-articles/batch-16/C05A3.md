# C05A3: Wheel-speed supply overvoltage and stuck-on switching checks

C05A3 is the wheel-speed sensor supply-high diagnosis in the GM 04B brake controller. It includes an overvoltage-warning path and a separate check of whether the high-side supply can actually switch off. Those observations affect a shared supply system, not necessarily one defective wheel sensor.

## Protection and self-test have different evidence

When the supply ASIC detects overvoltage for its debounce period, it sets a warning flag and disables the four high-side switches. The table lists fifty milliseconds for that diagnostic. A loss of several wheel-speed signals can therefore be a consequence of protective supply shutdown.

The second check commands a known supply configuration and expects the channels' open-circuit indications to respond. If a channel does not show the expected open-circuit result, the controller can identify a stuck-on high-side condition. That branch lists fifteen milliseconds and requires the ASIC to be initialized.

## Avoid treating every wheel fault independently

Preserve shared-supply warnings, switch-test status and individual wheel-sensor faults together. A common supply event can change several channels at once. The code does not by itself prove that all four sensors failed or identify which internal supply component needs replacement.

The published sequence describes an internal self-test, not instructions to disconnect sensors or switch their supplies manually. Use the application wiring and brake-system service procedure. Verification should address the actual overvoltage or switching branch and confirm the required functional checks. Clearing faults after a supply shutdown does not establish that ABS or stability-control operation has recovered.
