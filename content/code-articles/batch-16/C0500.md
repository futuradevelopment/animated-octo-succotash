# C0500: Very low current on the left-front wheel-speed signal

C0500 concerns the left-front wheel-speed sensor circuit. In the cited brake-controller strategy, the electrical decision is made from signal-line current. The source groups a ground-related fault and an interruption under this monitor, so the code does not uniquely identify an open wire.

## Current rather than wheel speed

The failure criterion is signal current below 0.0038 A, qualified for 0.120 second with ignition on. The monitor also requires the wheel-speed sensor tests to have completed without their undervoltage faults. These prerequisites separate the channel diagnosis from a broader supply problem that could make several signals unreliable.

A displayed zero wheel speed is consequently not the whole test. A stationary wheel can legitimately read zero, whereas this code's criterion concerns insufficient electrical signal current. Conversely, a stored intermittent circuit fault may remain relevant even when the wheel-speed value later looks normal.

## What evidence narrows the issue

Retain the left-front electrical status and the supply/undervoltage results for the wheel-speed system. Determine whether the controller classified an interruption or ground-related condition with any available enhanced detail. The published threshold does not provide a safe series-current measurement procedure or terminal identification.

Brake and stability-system diagnosis should use the exact wiring and service information. Do not infer that the sensor itself must be replaced before the circuit is tested. Verification requires an eligible electrical check and the prescribed functional confirmation; a code erase or a single plausible wheel-speed reading does not establish that ABS and stability functions are restored.
