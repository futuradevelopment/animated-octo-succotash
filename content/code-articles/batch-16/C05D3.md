# C05D3: Measured brake pressure is too high for the pedal-travel model

C05D3 is the driver-applied-pressure-higher-than-expected diagnosis. In the GM 04B strategy, the controller models expected simulator pressure from pedal position. It flags the case where measured pressure is substantially above that model, rather than judging pressure in isolation.

## The application rate matters

The criterion is modelled pressure lower than measured pressure minus 20 bar. The monitor excludes a fast brake application because that can produce unpredictably high pressure. A sharp pedal event is therefore not automatically comparable with the conditions under which this fault is evaluated.

The source lists a 500-millisecond maturation and an additional count value without explaining their relationship. It identifies a closed or blocked simulator valve, simulator problems and an in-range pressure-sensor failure as possibilities to investigate, not confirmed causes for every stored code.

## Compare pressure and travel together

Preserve measured pressure, pedal travel and brake-application context. A high pressure reading may be appropriate for one demand but inconsistent with another. Conversely, a plausible travel reading does not prove that the pressure signal is accurate.

The source does not provide a safe hydraulic test or authorize manipulating simulator valves. Diagnosis should follow the exact professional brake-system procedure. Verification needs the specified pressure-to-travel comparison in an eligible controlled event and the required functional checks. Clearing the fault or observing a different pressure during a fast pedal application does not establish resolution or certify safe braking.
