# C05D2: Pedal travel predicts more simulator pressure than is measured

C05D2 is labelled master-cylinder piston position higher than expected. The source explains the relationship behind that wording: the brake system models the pressure expected from driver pedal travel and compares it with measured simulator-circuit pressure. The monitor is concerned when the travel predicts substantially more pressure than the sensor reports.

## A mismatch between travel and pressure

The published criterion is modelled pressure more than 20 bar above measured pressure. Pedal travel must exceed the minimum point in the applicable lookup table. The row lists a 500-millisecond maturation while also showing a count goal whose exact relationship is not explained.

This is a relationship test, not a direct statement that the pedal-travel sensor is electrically high. An in-range pressure-sensor error can also create the mismatch. The source lists seal leakage to the reservoir among the possibilities, but does not establish which cause applies to an individual vehicle.

## Preserve both measurements

Retain pedal travel, measured pressure and the model-related diagnostic status. Looking only at travel or only at pressure can hide the discrepancy that caused the code. The applicable lookup calibration is necessary before applying the twenty-bar relationship to a particular test point.

Brake hydraulic diagnosis requires a controlled professional procedure. Do not attempt to reproduce the pressure mismatch by road testing or opening the system. Verification must establish the underlying cause and the specified travel-pressure relationship, followed by required braking checks. Code erasure alone cannot demonstrate hydraulic integrity or safe assistance.
