# P2471: High-range exhaust temperature sensor 4 input

P2471 identifies a high-range input for exhaust-gas temperature sensor 4. In the cited monitor, high is interpreted according to the sensor arrangement. An analogue channel is checked as resistance, while a digital thermocouple channel is checked using its reported temperature.

## Two representations of an electrical concern

The analogue branch lists resistance above 100 million ohms. The digital branch lists temperature above 1,289.85°C. These are separate raw-input criteria; the digital value alone is not proof that the exhaust physically reached that temperature. The source specifically frames the check as detecting electrical trouble, including module-to-probe harness issues in the digital arrangement.

The qualification is nineteen failed samples within twenty-five, at a 100-millisecond task interval. The row lists a one-trip response. Cranking, invalid supply, lost communication and an incomplete initialization delay can prevent an eligible evaluation.

## Use the right data before deciding cause

Determine whether the vehicle uses the analogue or digital arrangement and preserve the relevant raw signal and validity state. A high resistance limit must not be applied to a digital communications line, and a temperature-display anomaly does not identify a failed probe without the appropriate tests.

The summary gives monitor thresholds, not connector access or circuit-isolation instructions. Follow the application's service procedure and allow safe exhaust-system conditions before physical work. Verification should confirm that the correct range check completes with valid input after initialization. A cleared code or one normal temperature sample does not establish that the repeated-sample fault has been resolved.
