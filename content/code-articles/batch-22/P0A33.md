# P0A33: Motor-B circuit-high requires startup and warmup context

P0A33 concerns the high-voltage end of motor B's temperature-sensor input. GM describes a reading below −40°C, near a 5V signal. It is an electrical high fault with a cold indicated temperature, which is different from the motor-overtemperature code on the same page.

## A startup indication may be qualified later

The wakeup signal must be on. When the malfunction exists at the beginning of the trip, the source adds at least 1.5 minutes of accumulated motor warmup time above its stated torque threshold. That means the time when an abnormal value first appears and the time when the monitor reports failure need not coincide.

The failing-sample requirement is 900 counts within 1,800, using approximately 10.4ms intervals. The circuit-low counterpart uses a different window, so its delay should not be substituted here.

## Keep a useful timeline

Record whether motor B's cold indication was present immediately after startup, together with wakeup state and available warmup or torque information. A later screenshot without that context may be insufficient to reproduce the monitor logic.

The source does not establish which connection or component caused the high signal. Physical diagnosis requires the proper motor B service information, including permitted test access. Hybrid motor and inverter circuits must be handled by trained personnel following the applicable isolation and verification procedure, rather than by improvised live measurements.
