# P0218: Sustained transmission sump heat needs a temperature history

P0218 is a transmission-fluid overtemperature report in this GM hybrid table. The controller evaluates sump temperature, rather than declaring that the temperature sensor itself has failed. That distinction matters when a scanner expands the title to include the words “temperature sensor.”

## The duration is part of the finding

The documented failure requires a sump reading of at least 135°C for 300 seconds or longer. Before evaluation, the fluid-temperature signal must have remained within the specified operating range for ten seconds. A single hot sample does not reproduce that sustained condition.

The pass condition is different: the sump must fall to 130°C or below for five seconds. This separation between failure and recovery thresholds prevents a value hovering near one boundary from being interpreted as repeated complete recovery.

## What evidence is useful

Record the fluid-temperature trace, elapsed hot time and the operating conditions associated with the event. Compare the thermal history with sensor-circuit or plausibility faults before treating the displayed value as established physical temperature. P0634 on the same page concerns the TCM substrate, which is a different measurement.

The table does not identify a failed cooler, low fluid level or a replacement transmission. Those possibilities require the correct vehicle service procedure. Investigation around the hybrid transmission, including any high-voltage components, belongs with a suitably trained technician; this monitor summary supplies no safe access or disassembly procedure.
