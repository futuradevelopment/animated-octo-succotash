# P0A35: Motor-B thermal protection uses a separate reset boundary

P0A35 is the sustained overtemperature monitor for motor B in the cited GM hybrid system. Its prerequisites require an in-range motor-temperature signal and no active P0A31 plausibility fault. The thermal judgment therefore depends on accepting the measurement first.

## Initial heat and recovery are different decisions

The table gives an initial fault threshold above 200°C and a lower reset boundary around 135°C. The monitored temperature must initially exceed the higher limit and then remain above the recovery boundary through the specified sample decision.

The example uses 300 failing counts within 450 at approximately 10.4ms intervals. These are controller calibration values, not general temperature limits for unrelated motors or a target to reach during testing.

## Check whether the temperature was trustworthy

A useful event record includes motor B temperature over time, the input's validity status and associated plausibility or circuit-limit codes. P0A32 and P0A33 describe electrical extremes; their presence changes how confidently an extreme displayed temperature can be treated as physical heat.

The monitor does not identify a blocked cooling path, failed pump or damaged motor. Those causes need independent evidence from the correct vehicle service procedure. A qualified hybrid technician should assess the system and any restriction on further operation. Do not deliberately overheat the motor to reproduce the code or approach energized motor-control equipment using this summary as a repair instruction.
