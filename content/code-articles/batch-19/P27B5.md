# P27B5: Overspeed protection and ratio plausibility are different supervision branches

P27B5 concerns internal transmission gear-ratio control performance in Ford’s eight- and ten-speed automatic supervision list. Its detailed P0701_B1 representation refers to driveline-element overspeed protection, while P0701_64 concerns shaft-speed gear-ratio plausibility. Those are related control concerns, but they are not the same observation.

## Preserve the branch before judging a ratio

The monitor is continuous and uses the group’s general below-one-second registration period. The listed entries are non-MIL. An absence of a check-engine indication therefore cannot be treated as proof that the supervisory relationship remained valid.

A protection-related result should not automatically be converted into a failed gear-ratio calculation. Conversely, a ratio-plausibility disagreement does not by itself establish that an element actually reached a damaging speed. The subtype preserves which interpretation the controller recorded.

## Use contemporaneous speed and state evidence

A useful record includes the complete identifier, requested gear, available shaft speeds and relevant shift state. Comparing speeds from different moments can lose the relationship that a plausibility check assesses.

The summary does not give P27B5 a universal ratio tolerance, overspeed limit or repair. It also does not identify a particular clutch or gear as failed. The applicable transmission procedure is needed to establish whether the disagreement involves speed information, commanded state or controlled behavior, without importing a limit from another gearbox architecture.
