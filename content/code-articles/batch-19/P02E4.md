# P02E4: A diesel throttle jammed result requires active position control

P02E4 is the diesel intake-air-control stuck-open classification in Ford’s throttle-valve monitor. The documented test compares desired and actual throttle position while closed-loop position control is active. A valve observed in one static position is therefore not enough to recreate the monitored relationship.

## Eligibility separates a control error from an inactive valve

The monitor requires engine speed of at least 500 rpm, active closed-loop governing, no active adaptive learning and no jammed-valve fault already present on the actuator. The table lists 25 seconds to register a malfunction.

Its printed position-error criterion is outside a ±15% band: desired minus actual position greater than 15% or less than −15%. Although the code name says stuck open, the published comparison includes both directions of error. The article does not silently reduce that bidirectional rule to one assumed physical position.

## Keep command, feedback and learning state together

Preserve the requested position, measured position and whether the controller was learning or actively governing. A measurement obtained during adaptation or without closed-loop control does not establish the same eligible fault.

The source does not identify a particular obstruction, failed motor or sensor from the comparison alone. Vehicle-specific diagnosis must determine whether the position disagreement is credible and why it occurred. The 15% figure is a monitor comparison, not an instruction to force the valve to a particular opening or replace it without further evidence.
