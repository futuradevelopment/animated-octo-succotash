# P0544: An exhaust-temperature circuit code can be a model-rationality result

P0544 is listed in Ford’s gasoline exhaust-temperature monitor as the bank 1 sensor 1 circuit/rationality entry, paired with P0544_92. Separate low and high electrical faults use different representations. The word “circuit” alone therefore does not establish that the sensor is electrically open.

## The cold and running checks ask different questions

The offset check follows a six-hour engine-off soak and examines an early startup window. A reading above 250°C for two seconds in that cold-start context is different evidence from a hot operating sensor reaching the same temperature.

The running rationality check compares measured EGT with an inferred temperature, with a discrepancy exceeding 200°C for more than five seconds. It requires a defined operating window, inactive filter regeneration and closed-loop stoichiometric fueling. The summary lists a longer overall registration duration for inferred-model rationality than for the other checks.

## Preserve the actual monitor opportunity

Record soak duration, startup timing, regeneration state and whether the comparison was against another inferred quantity rather than an electrical rail. Applying the cold-offset criterion to a fully warmed exhaust would misinterpret the source.

The document does not identify the cause of a model disagreement or authorize replacing the sensor from P0544 alone. Verification needs the applicable sensor architecture and eligible conditions, with electrical low/high findings kept separate from temperature plausibility evidence.
