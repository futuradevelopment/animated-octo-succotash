# P2461: High pressure-sensor B voltage is not a filter-restriction measurement

P2461 is Ford’s high analog circuit classification for particulate-filter pressure sensor B. The detailed representation is P245E_17, and the published electrical criterion is sensor voltage above 4.73 V for five seconds. This reports an out-of-range signal, not direct proof that the filter itself is restricted.

## Keep the sensor circuit and filter function separate

The analog circuit-monitor group operates continuously and lists a five-second duration. Filter restriction monitoring elsewhere uses eligible exhaust flow, warm-up conditions and normalized pressure relationships. Those functional criteria cannot be replaced by the high-voltage code alone.

The source also distinguishes analog pressure sensing from SENT implementations. Confirming the architecture is necessary before using 4.73 V as a relevant threshold; it is not a universal digital data-line limit.

## Record channel identity and signal history

Preserve the complete code, sensor B mapping, signal level and any companion circuit or supply faults. A later plausible displayed pressure does not recreate a sustained electrical excursion recorded earlier.

The summary does not determine whether the high signal comes from wiring, the sensor or receiving electronics, and it does not provide a universal connector pin. The appropriate circuit procedure must localize the electrical issue before a pressure-based filter conclusion or component replacement is justified. The B label alone does not establish a universal physical bank assignment.
