# P2B63: An actuator-stuck report should not be reduced to an open circuit

P2B63 is the actuator-stuck classification in Ford’s auxiliary coolant pump B table, paired with P261A_71. It describes a reported operating condition rather than the separate electrical-open, low or high classifications in the same family. A pump that appears motionless needs that distinction preserved.

## A stuck classification does not identify the cause

The pump group runs continuously, lists a five-second monitoring duration and requires battery voltage of at least 11 V. A test performed outside that supply window does not recreate the documented eligible state.

The summary does not disclose the rotor-position, speed or current rule used to declare the actuator stuck. It therefore cannot support inventing a stall-current threshold or claiming a particular mechanical obstruction from P2B63 alone.

## Resolve the pump identity before locating hardware

There is a source-label inconsistency: the B heading and P261A detailed-code family identify pump B, but the P2B63 row describes pump A. No physical component assignment is inferred from that conflict. Match the vehicle’s complete code and cooling-loop documentation first.

Preserve requested pump operation, available operating feedback and companion supply, temperature or circuit faults. Those observations help distinguish a reported stuck condition from a failure to command the pump or a separate electrical problem. The source does not justify forcing the rotor, applying direct power or replacing an assumed pump solely from the generic stalled-actuator label.
