# U0600: Digital oil-pressure communication needs a SENT-specific interpretation

U0600 is the lost-communication classification for Ford’s SENT engine oil-pressure sensor A in the cited summary. The digital-sensor section lists it separately from P0520’s internal signal fault. Missing communication and a sensor that communicates an internal fault are different forms of evidence.

## Identify the architecture before applying limits

The SENT monitoring runs continuously, lists five seconds to register a malfunction and requires battery voltage above 11 V. These are the documented communication-check conditions. They do not turn the analog sensor’s neighboring low/high voltage thresholds into SENT data-line specifications.

The page separately assigns P0522 and P0523 to analog oil-pressure circuit limits. A meter reading interpreted only against those analog values would not establish whether the digital sensor’s communication is valid.

## Preserve signal availability and pressure uncertainty

Record the complete code, sensor architecture, battery state and whether any pressure information or internal fault report was available during the event. A displayed substitute or later plausible value does not reconstruct a period in which the controller reported lost communication.

This entry does not prove actual oil pressure is low or identify a mechanical lubrication failure. It also does not localize the communication loss to the sensor, wiring or controller. The applicable digital-signal and vehicle-specific diagnostic procedure is needed before drawing a repair conclusion or treating an unverified pressure display as trustworthy.
