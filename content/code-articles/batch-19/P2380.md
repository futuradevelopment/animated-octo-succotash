# P2380: EGR sensor internal status must be separated from lost communication

P2380 is Ford’s EGR sensor D circuit classification in the documented EGR pressure-sensing monitor. The source pairs it with P2380_01 and lists U0669 separately for sensor communication. An internal malfunction indication and inability to communicate are distinct evidence, even if either undermines an EGR flow calculation.

## The electrical check protects a later flow assessment

The sensor check runs continuously during EGR monitoring and lists five seconds to register a malfunction. The malfunction descriptions distinguish a sensor that indicates an internal fault from communication that is lost, corrupted or otherwise unavailable.

Ford’s EGR flow-monitor sequence requires the EGR pressure diagnostics, including P2380-related checks, to pass. A flow result obtained without trustworthy pressure information therefore cannot automatically be interpreted as though all its prerequisites were satisfied.

## Retain sensor health and communication separately

Record the complete identifier, internal sensor status, communication availability and any accompanying flow codes. A plausible pressure value at another time does not reconstruct an earlier internal fault report, and a response to communication does not prove the sensor’s internal status was healthy.

The source does not identify a failed EGR valve or an obstructed passage from P2380 alone. It also does not provide a universal connector test for the digital sensor. The appropriate procedure must establish the sensor’s reporting path before a mechanical EGR repair is selected.
