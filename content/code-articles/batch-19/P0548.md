# P0548: Bank2 low EGT voltage is an electrical threshold, not cold exhaust proof

P0548 is Ford’s low electrical-circuit classification for exhaust-temperature sensor 1 on bank 2. The source pairs it with P0547_16 and gives a sensor-voltage threshold below 0.089 V for five seconds. That is signal evidence; it is not a direct statement that the exhaust gas is colder than expected.

## Keep persistence separate from overall registration

The electrical entry window uses battery voltage between 11 and 16 V. The group description lists continuous electrical testing after sensor warm-up and a 30-second malfunction-registration duration, while the low-voltage criterion itself specifies five seconds. These two timing statements should not be collapsed into a promise of one universal warning-lamp delay.

The same page gives an additional inferred-temperature warm-up qualification specifically for the high-voltage check. Its numerical high-test requirements must not be silently treated as P0548’s low-voltage criterion.

## Verify the affected signal path

Preserve bank 2 sensor identity, supply voltage, the duration of the low reading and any separate rationality or communication findings. A normal temperature display later does not reconstruct the electrical state during the event.

The source does not localize the low signal to a sensor, wire or controller input. It supplies no connector pin or universal resistance test. The applicable circuit procedure is necessary before a repair decision, with electrical low evidence kept distinct from cold-soak or inferred-temperature plausibility checks.
