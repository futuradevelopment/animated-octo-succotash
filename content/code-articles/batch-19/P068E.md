# P068E: Cylinder8 glow-control low uses a different category from open load

P068E identifies the cylinder 8 glow-plug low-circuit classification in Ford’s V8 diesel example. It appears under short-to-ground checking. The cylinder identity is specific, while the source shares the same monitoring method across the listed glow channels; no unique cylinder 8 threshold is invented here.

## Heating mode is required for the documented result

The monitor operates while glow plugs are heating and lists approximately three seconds to register their malfunction. Separate reductant-heater faults in the same section use a different timing, so a heater-relay test should not be substituted for this glow-plug channel.

The low-circuit classification is also separate from an open-load result. A low-current observation alone cannot be assumed to explain a code whose reported electrical category is short to ground.

## Do not repair a disputed source caption by guessing

The continuation page presents current criteria beneath a short-to-battery caption even though it follows the short-to-ground panel. This article preserves that ambiguity and does not turn those values into a definitive P068E test limit.

Useful verification retains the heating request, cylinder 8 channel, driver classification and related supply or module faults. The actual V8 circuit procedure must establish the correct criteria and localize the problem. The summary does not provide a direct-power test, connector pin or automatic replacement decision for the glow plug or its controller.
