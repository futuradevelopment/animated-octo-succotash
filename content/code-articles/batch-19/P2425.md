# P2425: An EGR cooling-valve open-load report is an actuator-circuit finding

P2425 identifies an open-load condition in the EGR cooling-valve actuator circuit in Ford’s diesel summary. The monitor is electrical. It does not directly report whether exhaust gas has reached a target temperature or whether the cooler transfers enough heat.

## Preserve the open category and its timing

The source lists continuous execution and a two-second malfunction-registration duration, with the timing continuing onto the following page. P2426 and P2427 are separately listed for low and high short-circuit classifications. Those categories should not be merged into one generic cooling-valve failure.

A lack of expected cooling behavior may be relevant context, but it cannot by itself establish an open actuator load. Conversely, a later normal operating response does not recreate the circuit state that produced the stored fault.

## Establish the affected control path

Retain the full diagnostic identifier, requested actuator operation and available electrical status. The document distinguishes this EGR cooling-valve circuit from other engine-coolant bypass valves, so a similar valve label is insufficient to select the component or connector.

Ford does not publish an open-load resistance threshold, pinout or direct-power test for P2425 in this summary. It cannot localize an interruption to the valve winding, a connection, harness or controller output. The applicable vehicle circuit procedure is necessary before selecting a repair, rather than condemning the EGR cooler itself from an electrical actuator code.
