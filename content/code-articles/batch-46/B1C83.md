# B1C83: distinguishing a held-on defogger relay from a powered glass circuit

B1C83:14 concerns the rear-window defogger relay control circuit. The HVAC module activates that relay by grounding its coil after a valid request. On navigation-equipped cars, the front controls interface module first sends the request over the vehicle network.

## A persistent heater has several possible paths

The linked Fusion procedure addresses a defroster that does not shut off automatically. It begins by isolating the relay and glass power connection to look for unintended voltage on the grid supply. This separates a powered wire from a relay that remains energized or has faulty contacts.

The next checks examine relay-control activity and an unwanted ground on the control wire. The code's wording includes both an open and a ground short, so the observed symptom and electrical results still determine the useful branch.

## A published test condition needs clarification

Step O3 calls for an engine-running condition before a resistance measurement. That unusual instruction needs confirmation against a corrected workshop procedure and safe meter practice; it should not be copied into an improvised live-circuit resistance test. This article retains a review flag for that reason.

The final branch inspects HVAC module connectors and verifies whether the concern persists before replacement. Repair confirmation should establish that the glass heats when requested and that operation ends as intended. Clearing B1C83 without checking the actual relay and grid behavior would leave the original complaint unresolved.
