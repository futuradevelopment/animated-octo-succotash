# B272F: a passenger blower-feed fault with an important source qualification

Ford's B272F description should be read beyond its title. The Fusion procedure explicitly says this passenger-seat blower fault sets for an open circuit or short to voltage, although the short label says short to ground. Using only the label could send diagnosis down the wrong electrical path.

## Shared power can explain a two-blower shutdown

The cushion and backrest blowers share supply CHS08 and return RHS08, while their speed commands are separate. The monitor can disable passenger-seat outputs four to six seconds after the stated shared-circuit fault. A single-blower open is also covered when the seat-module supply is below 12 volts.

The factory sequence checks unwanted voltage and continuity before measuring each assembly. Its 4,000–10,000-ohm component check is polarity-sensitive: the manual warns that reversing meter leads can wrongly identify a good component as defective.

## Confirm the connector before measurement

There is an additional source limitation. Step AR2 names cushion connector C3300, while the passenger cushion is identified as C3303 elsewhere in the same procedure. That inconsistency requires verification against the applicable wiring diagram; it is not silently corrected here. Both the label conflict and connector reference remain under review. A technician must also follow seat-airbag depowering requirements and verify restored passenger climate operation after the electrical fault is resolved.
