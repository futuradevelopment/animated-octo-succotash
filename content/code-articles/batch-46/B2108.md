# B2108: when the trunk key switch keeps reporting an unlock request

The trunk key cylinder does more than release a mechanical latch on this Fusion. Its switch tells the Smart Junction Box that the luggage compartment is being unlocked with the key. B2108 identifies an abnormal condition in that input, including a grounded signal circuit or a switch that remains electrically closed.

## What the alarm actually does

Ford distinguishes temporary alarm inhibition for luggage compartment access from disarming the entire perimeter alarm. Opening the trunk with its key switch does not, by itself, establish that all protected openings are disarmed. That distinction matters when an owner describes an alarm sounding after a different door is opened.

The normally open switch grounds its signal during the unlock action. A persistent low signal can therefore resemble a continuing request even when the key has returned to rest.

## How the fault is separated

The procedure compares switch resistance in the operated and resting positions, then isolates the switch from the signal wire. Its component checks expect less than 5 ohms during unlock and more than 10,000 ohms at rest. Wiring checks distinguish continuity from an unwanted ground.

These are diagnostic measurements, not instructions to defeat the alarm. Correct switch behavior, intact wiring and sound SJB terminals must be established before a module is condemned. After repair, verify both legitimate trunk access and the intended alarm behavior at other openings.
