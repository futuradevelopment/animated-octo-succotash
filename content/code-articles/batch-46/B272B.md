# B272B: compare passenger backrest temperature with cushion behavior

The passenger backrest TED is the focus of B272B in Ford's Fusion climate-seat procedure. The controller watches its temperature while regulating blower operation. The diagnostic sequence distinguishes a local airflow or installation problem from a seat that shuts down because of an electrical fault.

## Use temperature behavior as evidence

The source gives separate sustained-temperature criteria: more than 70°C during cooling or more than 85°C during heating, each lasting longer than 34 seconds. A disconnected device or blocked duct is also listed. Those values describe the TED monitor and should not be interpreted as seat-surface safety limits.

Ford first inspects the backrest blower and filter, then compares passenger cushion and backrest temperature PIDs. If the backrest becomes disproportionately hotter while the seat still operates, the procedure turns to TED installation and the air-distribution manifold. If operation is disabled, thermistor and blower-circuit checks follow.

## Do not silently accept a conflicting replacement instruction

The final AN6 branch names a cushion TED replacement even though the heading, connectors and preceding tests concern the backrest. This apparent source conflict remains flagged for review. It is not used here to recommend replacing the cushion. The technician should verify the correct factory revision and component location before parts selection, while observing the required seat-airbag depowering and restoration procedures.
