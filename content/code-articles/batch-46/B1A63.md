# B1A63: why a right solar-sensor code can appear with two other climate codes

B1A63:15 is Right Solar Sensor: Circuit Short to Battery or Open in this Edge procedure. The HVAC module sees feedback voltage above its expected level, consistent with a short to voltage or an open sensor path. The relevant input measures sunlight, rather than engine temperature or refrigerant pressure.

## Three codes can originate from one circuit

Ford states that the module internally links the short-to-voltage diagnostics for the cabin-temperature input and the two solar-sensor feedback inputs. A voltage short on any one of those three circuits can set all three associated codes. Consequently, B1A63 appearing with B1A61 and B1A64 does not prove that three sensors have failed.

That shared diagnostic behavior is specific evidence from this model's procedure and should not be generalized to every Ford climate-control module.

## Check all linked feedback paths

The pinpoint test disconnects the HVAC connectors and cabin-temperature sensor as specified, then checks the three feedback conductors for unintended voltage. A positive result directs repair to the affected conductor. The right and left solar circuits retain separate identities despite their linked fault reporting.

If the voltage check passes, Ford inspects HVAC terminals, reconnects them correctly, clears codes and repeats the self-test before the final module decision. This sequence makes the whole three-code pattern important. Verification should establish which circuit or connection caused the event and confirm that the related codes do not return, rather than replacing the right sensor solely because its name appeared first.
