# B1304: the accessory-delay control circuit has a different fault from a blown window feed

B1304 identifies a short to voltage on the Fusion accessory-delay relay control path. This differs from the B1302 open-or-ground fault and from a break in the separate supply to the master window switch. Retaining the exact code prevents those conditions from being treated as equivalent.

## Confirm the normal delay conditions

Ford describes window accessory power continuing for ten minutes after the ignition is turned off, unless a front door opens sooner. The diagnosis begins by verifying the door-ajar and interior-light response because an incorrect door state can change the apparent delay behavior.

The relay is then component-tested, followed by checks of the appropriate circuit protection and the master-switch power path. A window complaint affecting several switches may reflect this shared supply arrangement rather than multiple failed motors.

## Match the test to the installed windows

The workshop page distinguishes master-switch connections according to whether the passenger front window has one-touch operation. It also marks one fuse reference as MKZ-only. The Fusion's equipment and wiring diagram therefore determine which details apply. Connector corrosion or poor seating is assessed before replacing the SJB. Record whether the fault affects ignition-on operation, the delayed period or both, and whether a front-door event changes it. Verify the repaired relay-controlled supply and actual window operation rather than treating a cleared B1304 as the only completion criterion.
