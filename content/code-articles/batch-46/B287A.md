# B287A: why losing all four tire sensors changes the diagnosis

B287A is the system-wide missing-response code for the tire pressure monitor. In this Ford application it applies when all four sensors are faulty, not responding or not providing data to the Smart Junction Box. B2872 covers the different case in which only one to three sensors are affected.

## Consider what the missing signals share

Four absent responses do not automatically prove that four sensor batteries failed together. The workshop procedure considers radio interference, sensors that are not installed or trained, SJB connections and the SJB itself. Aftermarket electronic equipment is specifically considered when investigating intermittent communication.

The SYSTEM FAULT data state is associated with no transmissions from all four sensors for 20 minutes above 32 km/h. The warning may flash for 70 seconds and then remain illuminated.

## Confirm hardware before the module decision

Ford's later branch inspects SJB connectors for corrosion and damaged or displaced terminals. If the concern remains, the procedure requires verifying that sensors are actually present in the wheels before selecting its module-replacement outcome. Tire dismounting and sensor inspection belong with suitable tire-service equipment.

A successful training session clears the warning initially, but operating verification still requires the subsequent monitoring interval described by Ford. Check actual tire pressures independently while communication is unavailable. The diagnostic outcome should identify why the receiver lost all four signals, rather than equating a cleared lamp with proof that every wheel is being monitored again.
