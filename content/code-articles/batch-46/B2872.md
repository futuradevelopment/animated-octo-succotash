# B2872: when the monitor loses one to three tire-sensor responses

B2872 is the partial-sensor fault in this Fusion TPMS. Ford uses it when one, two or three tire sensors are faulty, fail to respond or stop delivering data to the Smart Junction Box. A missing response is different from a sensor successfully reporting low tire pressure.

## Read the warning pattern and status

The described fault makes the indicator flash for 70 seconds before staying on. The message center may report TIRE PRESSURE SENSOR FAULT. Scan data provide the SENSOR FAULT state when the specified missing-transmission condition persists for 20 minutes above 32 km/h.

Actual tire pressures still need checking with a suitable gauge. Communication failure means the monitoring system cannot be relied upon to provide complete pressure information.

## Training success is not the whole verification

Ford checks horn operation because horn responses are used during sensor training. It also considers radio interference, missing sensors and untrained sensors before replacement. A spare tire cannot be trained as an additional road-wheel position in this procedure.

If initial training fails, the workshop sequence changes wheel position and the starting wheel before condemning individual sensors. Successfully training all four turns the warning off, but the SJB does not immediately complete its operating self-check; the manual calls for a subsequent 18–20-minute drive. That verification belongs after tire condition is made safe, and a briefly extinguished lamp alone should not be reported as a completed repair.
