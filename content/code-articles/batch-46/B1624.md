# B1624: keypad lighting has a diagnostic reference as well as an active supply

B1624 concerns illumination of the Fusion entry keypad, not necessarily the button-recognition circuits. Ford says it can indicate an open illumination feed, an open LED ground or a short to voltage on the output. The keypad can therefore need lighting diagnosis even when it still operates the locks.

## Do not confuse reference voltage with an active command

The source describes a constant diagnostic reference of 10 volts, with a one-volt tolerance, on the illumination circuit. A separate commanded-on test expects more than 11 volts. Those are different operating states; seeing the reference alone does not establish that the SJB can supply the requested illumination.

The procedure first confirms keypad operation, then separates always-on lighting from absent lighting. Illumination that survives SJB output disconnection points toward an unwanted voltage supply in the harness.

## Include the LED ground in the diagnosis

For a dark keypad with the appropriate feed, Ford checks its separate illumination ground before replacing the keypad. A working keypad input ground does not prove this LED return path is healthy. Connector and SJB checks follow the circuit tests. Record whether lighting never appears, remains on continuously or fails intermittently after a button press. The completed repair should be verified with the lighting command as well as normal keypad function, keeping those two observations distinct.
