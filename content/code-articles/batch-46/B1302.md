# B1302: delayed window power can fail at the relay-control stage

The Fusion's accessory-delay relay supplies the master window switch and can keep that function available briefly after ignition-off. Ford B1302 records an open or grounded relay-control circuit. It does not identify a particular window motor as defective.

## First establish whether the delay is being canceled normally

The cited system retains accessory operation for up to ten minutes after ignition-off, or until a front door opens. Ford begins by checking door-ajar behavior through the interior lights. A false door indication can therefore affect the complaint before relay wiring is considered.

The procedure then tests the relay and verifies the relevant circuit protection. It separates those checks from continuity of the supply path to the master window switch. A passing relay does not prove that the switch receives power through an intact harness.

## Equipment changes the switch connection

The source lists different master-switch connectors depending on front-window one-touch equipment. It also contains a fuse instruction specifically limited to MKZ; that detail should not be copied onto a Fusion indiscriminately. After the appropriate circuit checks, Ford inspects SJB connectors before the final module branch. Note whether windows work with ignition on but stop immediately after shutdown, and whether either front door changes that behavior. Those observations help distinguish normal delay termination from a relay-control failure.
