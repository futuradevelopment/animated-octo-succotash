# B1A16: why SYNC cannot detect the voice microphone

B1A16:01 is the accessory protocol interface module's microphone-detection fault. The APIM sets it during an on-demand self-test when it cannot detect the microphone. That is more specific than a general complaint that the vehicle did not understand a spoken command.

## Establish whether voice mode starts

Ford first checks whether pressing VOICE makes the audio system enter voice-recognition mode. If it does not, steering-wheel control diagnosis comes first. If it does, a recognized PHONE command helps separate basic microphone operation from navigation-system recognition issues.

The microphone path includes signal, return and shielding. The procedure checks continuity, isolation from ground, unintended voltage and the microphone supply before using a replacement microphone to isolate the component. A shield fault can therefore matter even when the visible microphone looks undamaged.

## Respect the test conditions

Ford warns that failing to disconnect the battery at the specified resistance-test stage can produce false readings. Its sequence also allows two minutes for APIM initialization after reconnecting the module. Measurements made under different conditions are not interchangeable with the published limits.

The source alternates APIM connector identifiers C3338 and C3342 in later steps. Those references need confirmation against the vehicle diagram, so this article retains a review flag. An APIM replacement is the end of a diagnostic branch after wiring, microphone and connector checks, rather than the first response to an unsuccessful voice command.
