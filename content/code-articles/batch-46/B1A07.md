# B1A07: following the navigation voice-guidance audio path

B1A07:01 is labelled Speaker #7: General Electrical Failure, but Ford defines the setting condition as a fault in the navigation audio circuits. That makes the installed sound system essential to diagnosis. The label does not establish that the seventh physical speaker should be replaced.

## Identify where guidance is supposed to play

With premium sound, voice guidance uses the front speakers. With Sony sound, it uses the instrument-panel speaker and a dedicated audio pair between the ACM and digital signal processing module. The source also includes THX-specific wiring for another equipment branch, which is excluded from this Ford discussion.

Ford first checks guidance volume and starts a route to establish whether the complaint is a setting issue. A speaker walk test then confirms the relevant speaker output.

## Separate guidance audio from ordinary playback

For the Sony path, the procedure looks for a fluctuating AC guidance signal at the DSP connection while the navigation repeat function is used. If that signal is missing, the dedicated conductors are checked for unwanted voltage, an open or a ground short.

This explains why music playback alone cannot prove that the separate navigation path is intact. The final branches inspect ACM or DSP connectors before component replacement. Verification should include an audible route instruction through the correct speaker location, with the original guidance-volume setting understood and the electrical fault rechecked after repair.
