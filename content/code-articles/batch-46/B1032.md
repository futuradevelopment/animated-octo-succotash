# B1032: following the passenger heated-seat request to its control module

An illuminated passenger heated-seat indicator does not prove that the seat module received a usable command. B1032 is the HVAC module's diagnostic for the right-front heater control connection. The published :12 suffix means short to battery, while :14 means short to ground or open.

## Compare the two seats first

Ford begins by checking whether the driver heater works. If neither seat operates, a broader shared-system investigation is appropriate. If the concern is isolated to the passenger side, the procedure follows indicator behavior, switch data and relevant module codes before moving into the seat circuits.

The command is a brief grounding action from the HVAC module. A correctly timed voltage change therefore carries different information from a static reading taken without pressing the control.

## Keep the source's side errors visible

The passenger procedure contains repair branches that unexpectedly call for a driver cushion mat, and it alternates HVAC connector numbers in adjacent control-circuit steps. Those discrepancies must be resolved against the actual vehicle diagram and component identity before repairs are selected. They are not grounds to replace the opposite seat's mat.

After the command circuit is verified, the remaining tests distinguish the passenger temperature sensor, cushion mat, backrest mat and heated seat module. These measurements require the stated restraint-system precautions around the seat airbags. Verification should include a normal warming cycle and a repeat self-test after all connectors and safety systems have been restored.
