# C1701: parking-sensor timing can fail without a broken power wire

C1701 takes the Fusion parking-aid diagnosis beyond simple circuit continuity. Ford associates it with an internally faulty left rear sensor or an incorrect attenuation time. The manual describes attenuation time as the sensor's time-related observation used in assessing an object.

## Check the surroundings before replacing a sensor

Other parking-aid faults take priority. With those resolved, the factory sequence addresses dirty or obstructed sensors, mounting and the system's azimuth and elevation checks. A sensor that is not flush in its bezel or is operating against an unintended nearby object can lead the investigation in the wrong direction.

For its controlled clear-area test, the cited procedure expects a distance PID of 255 centimetres and attenuation values between 0 and 16 milliseconds. These figures belong to that specific diagnostic setup. They are not a promise that every obstacle at that distance will be detected under all parking conditions.

## Separate a local reading problem from a module problem

If the checks remain unsatisfactory, Ford evaluates the suspect sensor before reaching the module-connector and replacement branch. A record showing which position reported the code, the observed distance PID and the circumstances of the warning is more informative than a base-code screenshot alone. The reverse-selection portion of testing requires controlled vehicle handling. Until repairs are confirmed, direct observation remains necessary regardless of whether the parking aid sounds.
