# B2008: the wiper status signal behind automatic headlamp operation

B2008 belongs to the signal that tells the Smart Junction Box the wipers are operating. It is not a direct diagnosis of a seized wiper motor. On the Fusion covered here, the SJB supplies a 12-volt reference to the wiper module and monitors whether that line is pulled low.

## Why the lighting setting matters

With the lighting control in AUTOLAMP and the wipers operating, the wiper module grounds the status signal. Ford describes a three-second delay before the SJB switches on the headlamps. Testing with a different lighting selection can therefore produce an observation that does not exercise this function.

An unwanted ground on the status circuit can imitate the electrical state used to report wiper operation. Conversely, moving wiper arms alone do not prove that the SJB receives their status correctly.

## Isolate the communication wire

The pinpoint procedure disconnects the wiper module and checks for more than 10 volts on the reference circuit. Isolated wiring checks then distinguish a continuous conductor, below 5 ohms, from adequate isolation to ground, above 10,000 ohms. These limits apply to the specified disconnected tests.

Connector damage and terminal contact are checked before assigning an internal SJB failure. Verification should include the actual wiper-activated headlamp sequence, not just clearing the code. Until the lighting behavior is confirmed, do not assume that wiper operation guarantees the headlamps are on.
