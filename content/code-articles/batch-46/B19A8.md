# B19A8: the backrest blower control can stop the whole driver climate seat

Ford B19A8 localizes a driver climate-seat shutdown to the backrest blower control path. The controller normally supplies the TED assembly with power and ground while separately commanding blower speed. A short to ground on that command circuit triggers the shutdown described in the Fusion manual.

## Test the wiring independently of the assembly

The procedure identifies VHS16 as the speed-control wire and RHS03 as the blower return. With the required connections isolated, it checks the control wire against chassis ground, then against the return conductor. Ford expects greater than 10,000 ohms for both isolation checks.

This sequence distinguishes a harness defect from an internal assembly problem. Replacing the backrest unit while its command wire remains grounded would leave the same triggering condition available. Conversely, a healthy-looking supply voltage does not establish that the speed signal is intact.

## Keep the fault identity intact

The neighboring B19A4 concerns voltage on the backrest control wire, while B19A7 points to the cushion's grounded command. They are different locations or electrical conditions, not alternative names for this code. When the specified harness checks pass, Ford's branch proceeds to the backrest TED. The service path intersects both front-seat airbag connections and requires controlled restraint depowering. Report whether the driver climate function stops immediately or after selection, then verify the corrected function through the factory repeat test.
