# P0047: the VGT driver reports the low-side circuit fault pattern

P0047 is the DC-motor VGT control-circuit-low diagnostic in this GM table. The source associates it with commands shorted to ground. Its direct malfunction evidence comes from hardware diagnostics reporting H-bridge switch current above 8 A while the driver is on.

## High current is the measured criterion

“Circuit low” does not mean the controller measured insufficient boost. It describes the electrical fault classification, while the table's criterion is excessive driver current. The powertrain-relay supply must be above 11 V, the engine must be out of cranking and the hardware status must be determinate.

The test accumulates 240 failures out of 300 samples in a 12.5-millisecond task and uses Type A, one-trip reporting. A momentary current observation without driver-state and failure-window context cannot reproduce the complete monitor decision.

## Distinguish electrical loading from position performance

Preserve the driver fault subtype and whether a position-tracking fault accompanies it. The electrical result changes how a later tracking complaint should be interpreted: tracking cannot be judged independently of an active driver fault. That is diagnostic reasoning, not proof of a short at a particular connector.

Verification requires the driver to operate without the reported overcurrent fault during enabled testing. Do not manually energize a suspect motor circuit from this summary. The applicable vehicle procedure must establish the fault path before an actuator or controller is condemned.
