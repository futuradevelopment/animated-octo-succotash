# P0204: injector 4 shows an open-load electrical pattern

P0204 is specifically an injector-4 open-circuit monitor in this GM ECM section. The controller interprets low current through the low-side driver during operation as an open-load condition. The threshold description associates that pattern with external-circuit impedance of at least 200 kΩ.

## An injection request is required

The test is calibration-enabled and requires key on, battery voltage above 11 V, the engine not cranking and at least one second of engine running. At least one injection pulse must be requested, along with the row's output-enable state. A no-current observation when no injection is requested is not the same evidence.

The monitor uses four failures out of eight samples at 100 milliseconds per sample, with Type A, one-trip reporting.

## Electrical continuity and fuel delivery are different

The code does not evaluate how much fuel injector 4 delivers or whether its spray pattern is correct. Those performance questions remain separate even if an electrical fault is present. Preserve the low-current status and commanded-pulse context before interpreting an apparent cylinder contribution problem.

Verification needs the driver to see the expected electrical response during eligible requested injection. The table's 200-kΩ figure is an internal diagnostic boundary, not a manual powered-circuit test instruction. Confirm cylinder numbering and follow the vehicle-specific professional electrical/fuel-system procedure before condemning the injector or controller.
