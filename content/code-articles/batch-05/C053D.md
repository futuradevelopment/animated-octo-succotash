# C053D: brake-pressure plausibility includes correlation and learned offset

C053D covers multiple brake-pressure plausibility checks in the cited brake-controller section. One set compares two boost-pressure channels with position-based information; another evaluates the correction needed to zero a pressure sensor. The generic Sensor A title does not uniquely identify the failing branch.

## Two useful distinctions

The channel-correlation rows compare the absolute difference between Boost Pressure 1 and 2 against 5 bar, with position information used to help identify the inconsistent signal. Those inputs must be valid and allowed to initialize. A start-up transient cannot simply be interpreted as a completed correlation failure.

Separate offset rows fail when the magnitude of the learned channel offset exceeds 10 bar. Learning requires appropriate vehicle motion, acceleration and no driver braking. A static pressure observation while parked is therefore not equivalent to this learned-offset check.

## Limits of the source

The adjacent model-based branches contain inconsistent deceleration wording, so this article does not use them as a complete diagnostic rule. It focuses on the clearly stated correlation and offset checks and retains a source-review flag.

Preserve the detailed failure branch and channel values before choosing a professional test. Verification must repeat the relevant enabled evaluation; clearing C053D does not demonstrate correct brake-pressure sensing. Brake-system warnings require suitable professional assessment, and these tables do not provide a safe pressure-test or disassembly procedure.
