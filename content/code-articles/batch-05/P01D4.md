# P01D4: cylinder 5 shows negative drift in the SQP test

P01D4 is the over-advanced cylinder-5 result in this GM small-quantity injection strategy. The narrative covers excessive negative drift in either energizing time or injection angle, so the title should not be narrowed to one direct mechanical timing measurement.

## What the diagnostic sequence does

The ECM performs controlled small injections during diesel fuel cut-off. Rail-pressure drop helps determine quantity behavior, while the pressure falling edge locates injection start. Crank-wheel speed converts that instant to angle, and multiple test injections support the final mean and correction.

SQP quantity/timing and learning enables, temperature conditions, speed conditions and fuel-level restrictions govern whether the test can run. The source lists Type B, two-trip reporting and completion-dependent sequencing.

## Interpret the signed result cautiously

The negative direction is explicit in the prose, but positive greater-than values in the threshold column lack an explained sign convention. A numeric signed correction limit cannot safely be reconstructed from that alone; it remains a source-review issue.

Preserve cylinder 5's specific quantity-versus-angle result and whether the sequence completed. An electrical continuity pass does not by itself verify this hydraulic-response-based performance monitor. Verification and any correction must follow the actual vehicle's professional SQP/fuel-system procedure, rather than manually adjusting timing or replacing an injector from the generic title.
