# U0422: the front camera rejects body-controller data

In this GM front-camera-module section, U0422 covers body-controller data validity, rolling-counter and protection failures. The source states that detection disables adaptive cruise control. That documented response should not be generalized to every module or vehicle that can store U0422.

## Two different failure patterns

One branch fails when a signal used for normal functionality remains marked invalid for five seconds. Examples include brake-pedal travel, system power mode and steering-wheel information. Other branches monitor the brake-pedal-switch and cruise-control-switch messages for incorrect or non-updating counters and protection calculations.

The latter branches use a sliding window of three missing or incorrect messages out of ten. They also require a five-second delay after communication enable and voltage within 9–16 V, with their calibration enables active. The five-second invalid-signal branch and sliding-window branch are different tests, not interchangeable descriptions of one timeout.

## Diagnostic interpretation

Determine whether the problem was an explicit invalidity flag or a counter/protection failure. That distinction guides attention toward the transmitted signal's status versus the integrity of its delivery. A currently available BCM does not resolve either question on its own.

Verification should show valid, updating information at the front camera while the relevant branch is enabled. Feature availability must be checked through the applicable driver-assistance procedure; absence of an emissions warning lamp is not evidence that adaptive cruise has recovered.
