# P0087: rail pressure falls below a calibrated absolute minimum

P0087 is an absolute low rail-pressure monitor in this GM ECM section. The threshold is not expressed as target pressure minus actual pressure. The row instead references a minimum-rail-pressure calibration with values in the 0–15 MPa range, so no single universal low-pressure number can be taken from this page.

## Conditions exclude several misleading situations

The engine must have run for at least 30 seconds, excluding cranking, and powertrain-relay voltage must be at least 11 V. Estimated pressure at the high-pressure-pump inlet must be valid and at least 400 kPa. Low-fuel, shutdown and injector-flow-test conditions also restrict monitoring.

Failure requires 121 counts out of 242 samples at 6.25 milliseconds per sample. The row lists Type A, one-trip reporting. These prerequisites make a low reading during startup different from the monitored running-engine result.

## Diagnostic interpretation

Preserve the rail-pressure reading, operating state and minimum-pressure calibration context. Also keep the inlet-pressure validity record: passing an inlet gate supports monitor eligibility but does not prove every supply-system component is healthy.

Verification means rail pressure satisfies the applicable absolute minimum during an enabled evaluation. Normal tracking of a temporarily low requested pressure is not, by itself, proof that this absolute-limit check passed. High-pressure fuel work requires professional vehicle-specific procedures; this summary cannot identify a failed pump or justify opening pressurized connections.
