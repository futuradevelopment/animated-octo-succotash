# P0193: fuel-rail pressure signal exceeds its electrical upper limit

P0193 checks the fuel-rail pressure sensor's electrical output, expressed as a percentage of its supply voltage. The criterion is output above 96 percent of supply. This is different from determining that actual rail pressure exceeded a commanded pressure or a mechanical safety limit.

## A proportional electrical check

Using a percentage makes the sensor supply part of the interpretation. Preserve the sensor output and its supply context rather than comparing a lone voltage with an assumed universal threshold. A high electrical signal alone does not identify whether the fault lies in the sensor, circuit or another dependency.

The row includes a twelve-consecutive-sample initialization delay and 6.25-millisecond sampling. Its failure paths list fifteen failures out of thirty samples and a continuous-failure alternative, with Type A, one-trip reporting.

## One source value needs review

A starter-duration alternative is printed as greater than 15,000 seconds, which is not a usable basis for reconstructing the intended starting logic. This article therefore does not prescribe that duration or silently convert its units. The clearly stated non-engaged-starter and voltage-related branches require vehicle-specific confirmation for a complete enable procedure.

Verification should establish an electrically valid pressure signal during the proper enabled evaluation. Do not open the fuel rail to investigate an electrical-range code; high-pressure testing requires the applicable professional procedure and cannot be inferred from this monitor summary.
