# C006A: the sensing module flags IMU data as invalid

C006A is a multi-axis accelerometer performance diagnostic inside the sensing and diagnostic module, or SDM, in this source. It covers invalid yaw, longitudinal-acceleration or lateral-acceleration data from either of two sensors, as well as failed correlation between the two IMUs.

## Nine checks share the code

Six listed conditions concern individual data validity. Three concern correlation of yaw, longitudinal and lateral data. For correlation, the table describes sample-by-sample comparison between separate IMUs and an Uncorrelated status. It does not publish the numerical tolerance, so a universal allowable sensor difference cannot be calculated from this page.

The monitor requires vehicle supply voltage between 9 and 16 V and its calibration enable active. The listed detection interval is within 0.050 second. Reporting is a safety-related non-MIL, emissions-neutral diagnostic.

## Consequences and useful evidence

The SDM marks IMU information Invalid or Uncorrelated after detection. The source states that consumers such as adaptive cruise disable based on those flags. That makes the originating validity/correlation detail useful evidence, beyond the shared C006A label.

Verification needs valid individual signals and acceptable correlation in an enabled SDM evaluation. Work involving the sensing module must follow the applicable restraint-system service precautions and professional procedure. This table is not authorization to open, reposition or electrically probe a safety module, nor does it establish a replacement decision.

## Source and applicability

[GM 04B, PDF page 2004](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG04B.pdf#page=2004). This explanation covers the documented 2025 monitor.
