# P01D1: cylinder 4 fails positive drift limits in SQP learning

P01D1 reports excessive positive drift on cylinder 4 during the documented small-quantity injection evaluation. The monitor accepts either an excessive energizing-time delta or an excessive start-angle delta as a failure path. The displayed limits are above 100 microseconds and above five degrees respectively.

## Timing and fuel amount are related but distinct

A controlled small injection during diesel fuel cut-off produces a pressure drop that the ECM uses to estimate quantity correction. The falling edge of that pressure trace supplies timing information, converted to crank angle using crank-wheel speed. Multiple injections establish the behavior in the small-quantity region.

The source requires SQP learning conditions and quantity or timing diagnosis enabled, with additional fuel-level, temperature and speed gates. It lists Type B, two-trip reporting and allows an interrupted diagnostic to resume.

## What changes the next decision

Keep the two possible correction paths separate. A cylinder-4 energizing-time drift result is not automatically evidence that the whole engine's mechanical timing is retarded. Likewise, the word injector does not establish an open or short in its electrical circuit.

Verification requires a completed eligible cylinder-4 SQP result. A normal resistance check or smooth idle addresses different evidence and cannot alone verify this monitor. The actual cause and any high-pressure fuel work should be handled through the vehicle-specific professional service procedure.
