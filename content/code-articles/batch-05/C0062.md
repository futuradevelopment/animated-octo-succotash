# C0062: longitudinal-acceleration status fails IMU checks

C0062 covers longitudinal-acceleration information from the high-performance IMU in this EOCM3 section. The diagnostic has a correlation branch and a validity branch. Correlation fails when its status is Unknown or Not Correlated; the other branch detects a primary or secondary longitudinal signal determined invalid.

## An invalid status is not a measured acceleration limit

The table does not define this fault as exceeding a particular forward or rearward acceleration. It evaluates the supplied status information, with relevant communication and failed-safety faults excluded by the enable logic. A message can therefore be present while its reported longitudinal information is unusable.

Common, signal and communication diagnostics must be enabled. The manufacturer's enable counter must be zero. Monitoring is continuous, and the documented fallback disables adaptive cruise and/or Super Cruise. The source labels the diagnostic safety-related, emissions-neutral special Type C.

## What the distinction changes

Preserve whether the correlation status was unacceptable or a particular primary/secondary signal was flagged invalid. Those observations can lead to different vehicle-specific tests. A plausible acceleration number on a scan display is insufficient if its accompanying validity or correlation status remains failed.

Verification should establish valid longitudinal information and acceptable correlation during an enabled evaluation, followed by the applicable feature-restoration checks. The source does not establish an IMU location or a calibration procedure, and it cannot justify replacement or recalibration from the generic C0062 label alone.
