# P0046: VGT position does not follow a steady command

P0046 evaluates position tracking of the DC-motor variable-geometry turbo actuator. The ECM compares commanded and measured position, using an absolute error greater than 10 percent as the malfunction criterion. The source describes obstruction during vane opening or closing as the behavior this monitor is intended to detect.

## Not every transition is evaluated

Closed-loop VGT control must be active and relevant position-sensor, actuator and control faults absent. The position command must be sufficiently steady for at least 0.50 second. The table also excludes cranking, cold-start strategy, anti-sticking operation and the mechanical-stop soft approach.

Failure requires 960 counts out of 1,200 samples in a 6.25-millisecond task. Powertrain-relay voltage must exceed 11 V, and temperature conditions also apply. This is a sustained eligible tracking result, not proof that any brief command-versus-position difference is abnormal.

## Diagnostic interpretation

Compare demand and feedback over time, including whether the command had settled. A discrepancy while the command changes rapidly is different evidence from one sustained under the monitor's steady-state rule. The code does not independently distinguish a mechanical obstruction from every possible control or feedback problem.

Verification should show acceptable tracking during enabled closed-loop operation. Completing an endpoint learning routine or observing normal boost alone does not reproduce this specific test. Follow vehicle-specific professional turbo testing before selecting an actuator or turbocharger replacement.
