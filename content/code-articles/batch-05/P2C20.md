# P2C20: rail pressure exceeds its catalyst warm-up target

P2C20 is the high-pressure counterpart of a cold-start rail-pressure tracking fault. In the documented ECM strategy, measured pressure exceeds desired pressure during catalyst warm-up. Because GM expresses error as desired minus measured pressure, this fault concerns a negative pressure error.

## Read the sign before interpreting the value

A high rail-pressure reading alone does not establish this monitor's failure: the requested pressure is essential. The table references supporting calibrations for the actual excessive-pressure threshold, so its page cannot support a universal MPa limit. It does state a 10-second failure requirement within 12.5 seconds of sampling and Type A, one-trip reporting.

Catalyst warm-up must be active; the engine must no longer be cranking. At least 11 V and low-side fuel pressure above 0.275 MPa are among the prerequisites. Active service commands and relevant circuit faults can also prevent evaluation.

## How to use the result

Compare the time-aligned pressure target, measured rail pressure and pump-control behavior during the qualifying start. This separates pressure overshoot relative to demand from a normal high target. Record related sensor and pump-circuit codes because the monitor assumes those signals and circuits are usable.

An eligible warm-up cycle without excessive negative pressure error is stronger verification than a fault-free warm idle. This code does not authorize opening pressurized lines or establish which component failed; high-pressure work belongs within the applicable professional service procedure.
