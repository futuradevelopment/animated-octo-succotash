# P020D: injector 4 current pull-in takes too little or too much time

P020D reports abnormal electrical pull-in timing on injector 4 in this GM table. The hardware measures the interval from pulse start until current reaches 20 A. The row rejects intervals below 50 microseconds or above 90 microseconds.

## This is not the open-circuit test

A pulse may exist yet have the wrong current-rise duration. That distinguishes P020D from an open-load finding based on insufficient current response. The source names injector and ECU faults as possible origins, without proving either in an individual case.

The monitor requires key on, battery voltage above 11 V and its calibration enable. Related electrical, controller and boost-voltage faults must be absent, and hardware must not report a dropped pulse. The table includes normal requested-injection and controlled reactivation paths.

## How to verify the finding

Ten failures out of twenty samples cause the listed result, with one sample each engine cycle and Type A, one-trip reporting. Keep the too-fast or too-slow direction and the pulse context; they are more useful than the code alone for professional waveform interpretation.

Verification must demonstrate acceptable injector-4 pull-in periods while monitoring is enabled. A continuity pass or ordinary engine operation does not establish that. Confirm cylinder numbering and follow the actual vehicle's safe driver-testing procedure before any injector or ECU replacement decision.
