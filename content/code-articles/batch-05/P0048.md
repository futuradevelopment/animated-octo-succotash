# P0048: the VGT motor driver detects the supply-short pattern

P0048 identifies the high-side VGT control-circuit fault classification in this GM implementation. The source describes commands shorted to the power supply, detected through hardware information when current through the H-bridge switches exceeds 8 A.

## Why the hardware classification matters

The current threshold is also used by another driver-fault direction, so current magnitude alone cannot identify whether a circuit is shorted toward supply or ground. Preserve the reported hardware fault category and use the actual circuit procedure. The code does not mean charge pressure itself is high.

The H-bridge must be on, the engine outside cranking and powertrain-relay voltage above 11 V. The hardware error status cannot be indeterminate. The ECM requires 240 failing counts out of 300 samples in its 12.5-millisecond task and lists Type A, one-trip reporting.

## Useful verification

Establish whether the supply-short classification recurs when the actuator is commanded under valid test conditions. An actuator that moves once may still experience intermittent electrical loading; conversely, no movement alone does not prove this fault pattern.

After a diagnosed repair, verify the hardware status and actuator operation during the eligible commanded state. The source gives no terminal assignments or method for bypassing the H-bridge. Vehicle-specific electrical isolation and professional turbo-control tests are necessary before identifying a motor, harness or controller as the failed part.
