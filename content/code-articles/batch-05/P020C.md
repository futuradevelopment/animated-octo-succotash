# P020C: injector 3 fails its electrical current-rise timing window

P020C is the cylinder-3 member of the shared injector pull-in monitor. The ECM checks the time for current to reach 20 A at the start of the pulse. The documented acceptable boundary is 50–90 microseconds; values below or above those limits produce the relevant failure samples.

## Fault prerequisites shape the result

Battery voltage must exceed 11 V and key-on and calibration conditions must be satisfied. The source excludes related injector-circuit, controller-status and boost-voltage faults. It also requires usable pulse information rather than a hardware-reported dropped pulse.

These restrictions matter diagnostically: an absent P020C while a basic injector-circuit fault is active is not proof that pull-in timing is good. The monitor may not be evaluating the same eligible condition.

## Evidence and verification

The decision is ten failures out of twenty samples, with one sample per engine cycle and Type A, one-trip reporting. Preserve whether injector 3's pull-in period is shorter or longer than permitted; the generic timing label hides that useful distinction.

A successful verification checks the current-rise duration during eligible pulses. Mechanical engine timing, rail-pressure tracking and injector resistance are separate observations and cannot individually prove this monitor passed. The source does not identify the failed part or provide a manual actuation method; use professional vehicle-specific injector-driver testing.
