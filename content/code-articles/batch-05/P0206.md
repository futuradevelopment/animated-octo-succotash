# P0206: injector 6 fails the low-current open-circuit check

P0206 identifies the injector-6 open-load monitor in this GM source. Low current through the low-side driver during operation indicates the fault pattern, and the threshold description defines the open circuit as an external impedance at or above 200 kΩ.

## Why an operating test differs from a parked snapshot

The controller requires at least one requested injection pulse, battery voltage above 11 V, key on, no cranking and engine runtime of at least one second. Its calibration and output-enable conditions must also be satisfied. A static circuit observation without that context cannot by itself recreate the monitor.

The failure window is four samples out of eight at 100 milliseconds each. The row uses Type A, one-trip reporting.

## Keep the finding electrical

P0206 does not directly test injector-6 flow, small-quantity calibration or fuel timing. A separate performance fault may coexist, but this result first establishes an abnormal electrical response to a command. It does not say where an open exists or whether the controller is responsible.

Verification should show reliable current response during eligible injection commands. Intermittent recovery after a restart is not the same as a completed pass. Apply the actual vehicle's cylinder-numbering and circuit-isolation procedure before any component decision; the source's impedance boundary is not permission to test an energized injector circuit or open the high-pressure fuel system.
