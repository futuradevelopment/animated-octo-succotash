# P0205: the injector 5 driver sees too little current for its command

P0205 checks injector 5's electrical integrity during operation in the cited GM table. The relevant failure pattern is low current through its low-side driver, classified as an open circuit. The document describes an open-load impedance of at least 200 kΩ at the controller's external circuit.

## The commanded state gives the reading meaning

Monitoring requires an injection pulse request, key on and the row's applicable output-enable state. Battery voltage must exceed 11 V, cranking must have ended and the engine must have been running for at least one second. An injector intentionally not commanded cannot be judged by assuming any zero-current value is this fault.

Four failed samples out of eight, at 100 milliseconds per sample, satisfy the listed accumulation. Reporting is Type A, one trip.

## What the result narrows

An open-load electrical result is different from injector-5 quantity drift or an incorrect pull-in time. Preserve related circuit codes and the requested-pulse information so those different findings are not merged into one generic injector diagnosis.

For verification, confirm a proper electrical response under the same eligible commanded conditions. Engine smoothness alone cannot prove continuity is reliable through the monitored interval. Use the vehicle's wiring, cylinder-numbering and safe isolation information; this source does not locate the open path or prove that replacing injector 5 will resolve it.
