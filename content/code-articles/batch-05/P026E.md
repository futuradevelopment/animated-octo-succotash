# P026E: cooler coolant pump current disagrees with its speed model

P026E monitors charge-air-cooler coolant-pump performance indirectly through electrical load. The ECM estimates expected pump AC phase current from actual pump speed, then compares it with measured phase current. An absolute current error above 5 A for more than five seconds is the stated deviation criterion.

## It is not a direct flow-meter reading

The source describes the result as a flow-deviation failure, but its evidence is current versus a speed-based expectation. The code alone cannot establish a measured coolant-flow rate, locate a restriction or prove that the impeller failed.

The pump hardware must be present, the diagnostic enabled and actual speed above 1,000 rpm. Current-range fault status, supply configuration, coolant-temperature gates and related communication faults also affect eligibility. The engine must not be cranking and the pump must be enabled.

## Two timing details are listed

The row includes the more-than-five-second deviation condition and a decision of sixteen failures out of twenty samples at one second per sample. Those describe separate parts of the evaluation and should not be collapsed into a single five-second timer. Reporting is Type A, one trip.

Preserve actual speed, expected/measured current and validity status together. Verification requires agreement during eligible pump operation, followed by any needed cooling-performance checks. The table provides no safe coolant-opening, pump-bypass or replacement procedure.
