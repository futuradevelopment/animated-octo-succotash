# P01D6: the cylinder 6 SQP calculation shows negative drift

P01D6 is the cylinder-6 over-advanced result in this GM injection-monitor family. The source explicitly describes excessive negative drift in energizing time or injection angle. It therefore cannot be reduced to a direct measurement that the entire engine's timing is advanced.

## Two estimates come from the test injection

During diesel fuel cut-off, a controlled small injection changes rail pressure. Drop magnitude supports the quantity correction, and the falling-edge location supports injection-start timing. Crank-wheel speed translates time to angle; multiple injections establish the drift estimate.

The algorithm requires the appropriate learning and diagnostic enables plus temperature, speed and fuel prerequisites. Its reporting is Type B, two trips. Completion status matters because an interrupted sequence can continue later.

## Threshold-sign limitation

The printed table uses positive greater-than thresholds without explaining how they apply to the negative drift described in the narrative. No signed numerical adjustment rule is asserted here; that convention requires corrected or vehicle-specific calibration information and remains source-review flagged.

Preserve the cylinder-6 quantity-versus-angle failure detail and verify it through a completed eligible SQP test. Smooth idle, an electrical resistance check or a clear code history cannot substitute for that result. High-pressure fuel diagnosis and any injector work require the applicable professional procedure rather than a repair inferred from the title.
