# P01D5: cylinder 6 shows excessive positive small-injection drift

P01D5 concerns injector 6 in GM's shared SQP strategy. It detects excessive positive drift in a quantity-related energizing-time correction or in injection-start angle. The row's limits are a calculated time delta above 100 microseconds or angular delta above five degrees.

## How a controlled pulse becomes a result

The ECM commands small known injections during diesel fuel cut-off. It uses the rail-pressure drop to infer quantity behavior and the pressure falling edge to locate injection start. Crank-wheel speed converts that timing to angle. Multiple pulses and stepped energizing times define the injector's behavior in the small-quantity region.

The test requires SQP learning and quantity/timing monitoring to be enabled, with the listed fuel-level, temperature and speed conditions satisfied. It uses Type B, two-trip reporting and can resume a previously interrupted evaluation.

## Cylinder-specific evidence is important

Keep injector 6's correction type and completion status. Comparing other cylinders' completed results can establish whether the observed pattern is isolated or shared, but cannot alone prove a common or individual failed part. A normal large-fueling event does not verify the small-quantity test.

Verification needs an eligible completed cylinder-6 evaluation with acceptable drift. The source gives no injector location, manual timing adjustment or high-pressure disassembly method. Use the actual vehicle's professional fuel-system procedure before deciding on repair or replacement.
