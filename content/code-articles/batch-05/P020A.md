# P020A: injector 1 current reaches its pull-in level at the wrong time

P020A is an electrical pull-in timing monitor for injector 1 in this GM ECM section. The controller measures how long the injection current takes to reach 20 A at the start of the pulse. A period below 50 microseconds or above 90 microseconds is outside the listed range.

## Timing here means current-rise timing

This is not the same as measuring fuel injection's crank-angle position. The source says an injector or ECU fault can produce the abnormal pull-in period, but it does not establish which component caused a particular event.

The monitor requires battery voltage above 11 V, key on and its calibration enable. Relevant injector-circuit, controller-status and boost-voltage faults must be absent. It also excludes dropped-pulse information and requires an appropriate requested or reactivation pulse.

## What to preserve

Keep whether the measured rise was too fast or too slow. Both directions share P020A but imply different waveform observations to evaluate with the proper service equipment. The decision uses ten failures out of twenty samples, with one sample each engine cycle and Type A, one-trip reporting.

Verification should show acceptable pull-in timing during eligible pulses. A normal resistance reading or smooth idle cannot alone establish that the current-rise window passed. Follow the vehicle-specific professional injector-driver procedure; this table provides no safe manual energizing or waveform-probing instructions.
