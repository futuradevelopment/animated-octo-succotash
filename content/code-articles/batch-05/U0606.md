# U0606: throttle sensor 1 SENT communication is invalid

Although the generic title mentions a throttle or pedal sensor, this GM ECM row specifically identifies throttle-position sensor 1 and its SENT communication circuit. U0606 can arise from electrical-state, pulse-timing, message-age or CRC failures; it is not limited to a sensor that sends no information at all.

## What the digital monitor examines

The table gives wave-state boundaries of 0.5 V and 4.1 V. It also checks pulse duration outside 0.125977–0.209991 milliseconds, message age reaching 3.125 milliseconds, or a failed signal CRC. These are digital-protocol checks, not an analog throttle-opening voltage specification.

Run/crank voltage must exceed 6.41 V. The failure logic lists accumulated and continuous count paths, sampled at 3.125 milliseconds per count, with Type A, one-trip reporting. That combination allows intermittent malformed communication to matter even when a present-time throttle reading looks plausible.

## Interpreting the evidence

Separate the communication problem from a position-correlation or mechanical throttle concern. Preserve whether sensor 1 alone lost valid information and whether related supply or sensor faults occurred simultaneously. The code does not establish a throttle-body replacement or a pedal fault.

Verification requires valid SENT data during enabled monitoring, including correct timing and integrity rather than just a changing scan value. Waveform and circuit evaluation should follow the vehicle-specific service method; this source does not supply connector identities or a direct probing procedure.
