# U0632: cooling fan 1 misses its LIN communication deadline

U0632 identifies missing cooling-fan-1 LIN communication in this ECM table. The expected CFM1_Rsp_2D_C02 reply must not remain absent for 2,500 milliseconds or more. This is a response-monitoring fault, not a direct comparison between commanded and actual fan speed.

## When the fan monitor runs

The LIN channel must be enabled, the LIN module initialized and fan 1 configured as present. The engine must be running, or cooling-fan operation must be enabled through a received CAN signal with the propulsion system active for at least one second. General power conditions must also have persisted for at least three seconds.

These conditions matter when a stationary fan is observed with the engine off: that observation alone does not reproduce the monitored state. The row lists a 500-millisecond communication loop and Type B, two-trip reporting.

## Diagnostic interpretation

Record whether the fan is expected to be communicating and whether other LIN devices are available. A physically rotating fan may still have a communication problem, and a nonrotating fan may simply lack a current operating request. Neither observation alone identifies the cause of U0632.

Verification should demonstrate valid replies during an eligible fan-monitoring state, with any separate speed or cooling-performance concern assessed independently. Use the actual vehicle's fan-control and power information; the source supplies no wire assignment, bypass procedure or basis for replacing fan 1 solely from this code.
