# C0019: left-rear outlet solenoid driver feedback is abnormal

C0019 corresponds to the left-rear dump or outlet solenoid driver in this GM brake-controller table. The monitor checks driver feedback while the solenoid is commanded on. Its listed fault coverage includes an internally shorted coil, a short to supply or ground, and faults in the driver electronics or circuit board.

## What the monitor actually observes

The electronic driver monitors drain voltage and filters the comparator feedback. The table gives a comparator threshold range of 2.375–2.625 V and 15-millisecond maturation. These are internal driver-monitor specifications, not instructions to apply those voltages to a brake connector.

The solenoid must be commanded on, the power switch on, and supply voltage between 7.5 and 18 V. A parked system with no applicable actuation request may not reproduce this test merely because a scan tool is connected.

## Distinguishing an open driver from a shorted coil

The source explains that the controller can distinguish these conditions using a test pulse on the faulted channel with other coil channels off. That is an internal diagnostic strategy, not a safe manual test to imitate. Preserve any detailed failure subtype to support the correct professional procedure.

Verification requires the relevant driver feedback to pass during authorized brake-system testing. The code does not uniquely condemn the hydraulic unit or valve coil. Brake warnings and abnormal braking need professional assessment; the summary provides no disassembly, manual energizing or safe-driving conclusion.
