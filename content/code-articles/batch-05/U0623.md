# U0623: the auxiliary water pump stops communicating on LIN

The generic U0623 title says coolant pump A. This GM ECM section identifies the device more specifically as the auxiliary electric water pump. The monitored AWP_Rsp_36_C05 response fails the communication test when absent for at least 2,500 milliseconds.

## Communication is not coolant-flow measurement

This row does not measure pump delivery, impeller condition or coolant circulation. A missing response explains what information the ECM lost, but does not prove why the pump stopped responding. Likewise, observing pump noise or movement does not establish that its required message is arriving.

The diagnostic assumes the LIN channel is enabled, its module is initialized and the slave is configured as present. Actuator or powertrain-relay conditions also form part of the enable logic. General power conditions must persist for at least three seconds. The source lists a 500-millisecond LIN loop and Type B, two-trip reporting.

## Evidence for verification

Compare the pump's intended powered state with its communication availability at the fault. If other LIN devices also disappear, investigate the shared path using the vehicle's topology; if only this response is lost, preserve that distinction for the pump-specific diagnosis.

A completed retest requires valid pump responses during eligible operation. Any separate overheating or circulation complaint needs its own cooling-system assessment. The communication summary does not establish a safe coolant-opening procedure, a pump location or a replacement decision.
