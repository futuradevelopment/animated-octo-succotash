# P006E: VGT motor supply monitoring reports undervoltage

P006E monitors whether the variable-geometry turbo DC motor is correctly supplied in this GM ECM row. The hardware diagnostic reports supply below its threshold. The table's threshold cell is printed as “<6 M,” an unclear unit/value presentation confirmed in the PDF, so this article does not turn it into an asserted 6-V test limit.

## The enable supply is separately defined

The powertrain-relay supply must exceed 11 V, the engine must be outside cranking and the hardware error status must be determinate. A supply prerequisite above 11 V and an internal low-supply failure criterion are different parts of the logic; they should not be treated as a contradiction or collapsed into one battery-voltage test.

Failure requires 240 counts out of 300 samples in a 12.5-millisecond task. Reporting is Type A, one trip.

## What the code can support

Preserve the actual motor-supply diagnostic and powertrain-relay information at the event. A normal battery reading at a later time cannot establish the monitored supply was available when required. Nor does this code establish a mechanical turbo failure.

Verification needs a valid enabled evaluation with no hardware undervoltage report. The exact failure threshold requires corrected vehicle-specific information before circuit testing. This article remains marked for source review because the OEM page does not provide a clear usable threshold unit.

## Source and applicability

[GM 04B, PDF page 1054](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG04B.pdf#page=1054). This explanation covers the documented 2025 monitor. Confirm the vehicle's module, engine and calibration before applying these criteria; the document's vehicle list does not establish this code's applicability to every listed model.
