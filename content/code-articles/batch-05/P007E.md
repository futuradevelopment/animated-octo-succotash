# P007E: charge-air temperature moves outside its dynamic limits

P007E detects intermittent behavior of the charge-air-cooler upstream air-temperature signal. Unlike a simple fixed-limit check, the documented algorithm calculates allowable upper and lower limits using the last good temperature, sensor range and filter response.

## Why a snapshot is insufficient

The source includes maximum and minimum sensor-reading references of 300°C and −40°C, but its failure description uses calculated limits. Those endpoints should not be presented as the only temperatures that can trigger the monitor. The previous valid reading and sequence of failed samples influence the allowable change.

Evaluation requires the engine not to be cranking, run/crank-relay voltage above 11 V and no electrical fault on this air-temperature input. Failure is 60 counts within 75 samples in a 100-millisecond task, with Type B, two-trip reporting.

## Follow the signal over time

A time trace that captures abrupt excursions and recovery is more useful than a normal reading after the event. Because a basic electrical fault inhibits this monitor, absence of P007E while such a fault is active does not prove the signal has become stable.

Verification should show realistic, continuous temperature evolution during an enabled window. The monitor does not identify a cooler efficiency problem or prove a particular connector failed. Use vehicle-specific circuit diagnosis to establish the cause before replacing a temperature sensor or charge-air assembly.
