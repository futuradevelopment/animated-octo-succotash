# P003A: VGT end-position learning exceeds its allowed windows

P003A checks the learned end positions of the variable-geometry turbo actuator in this GM ECM section. The controller evaluates the SENT position signal at fully closed and wide-open positions. The closed-position acceptable window is bounded by 72 and 89 percent of the 5-V-scaled signal; the wide-open window is bounded by 14 and 32 percent.

## A key-off learning result

The monitor operates at key-off after successful end-position learning. It has no debounce: the row says the DTC sets when the error is present. That makes it different from a running-engine boost response test or a sustained tracking-error monitor.

Learning eligibility includes coolant temperature from 45 to 130°C, outside temperature at least −20°C and battery voltage at least 9.5 V. Relevant temperature, VGT position-sensor, actuator and control faults must be absent. The row gives Type A, one-trip reporting.

## What the result does and does not show

Preserve which learned endpoint fell outside its window. An offset at the closed endpoint and one at the open endpoint need not have identical diagnostic implications. The normalized percentage is not a mechanical adjustment target or a direct instruction to reposition the actuator.

Verification must include a valid completed learning event with acceptable endpoint results. A normal boost reading while driving cannot prove this key-off check passed. Follow the vehicle-specific turbo service procedure before interpreting the failed learning as a component-replacement decision.
