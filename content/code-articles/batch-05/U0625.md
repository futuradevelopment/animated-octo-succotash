# U0625: fuel-rail pressure sensor communication stays low

U0625 has a specific electrical interpretation in this GM ECM row. The controller watches the fuel-rail pressure sensor's SENT signal and identifies a low-state communication failure when the line has four or fewer pulses and its state is low.

## The sensor's pressure value is not the criterion

The monitor does not compare actual rail pressure with a target. It evaluates pulse availability and the communication-line state. A missing valid pressure value can therefore be an information problem without establishing whether physical rail pressure is too high or too low.

The failure requirement is 400 failing samples out of 500, with 6.25 milliseconds per sample. The SENT communication diagnostic must be enabled; the row also specifies code-clear/device-control restrictions. It uses continuous monitoring and Type A, one-trip reporting.

## What changes the next check

Preserve whether the signal was continuously low and whether valid pulse traffic returned intermittently. Those observations distinguish this documented pattern from an otherwise present but implausible pressure reading. The DTC itself does not locate a short or determine whether a sensor, circuit or controller is responsible.

Verification needs restored valid SENT traffic through an enabled sampling window, not merely a plausible pressure display after a restart. Fuel-rail pressure systems require the applicable professional service methods; this communication monitor provides no justification for opening a pressurized connection to test an electrical diagnosis.
