# P020F: injector 6 has abnormal current pull-in duration

P020F concerns the electrical current rise of injector 6 in this GM implementation. The pull-in period is the time taken to reach 20 A at the beginning of an injection pulse. The source fails periods shorter than 50 microseconds or longer than 90 microseconds.

## Keep electrical timing separate from combustion timing

The generic “injection timing” title can suggest crank-angle timing, but that is not what this row measures. It evaluates a hardware-reported current-pulse interval and lists injector or ECU faults as possible origins without identifying the failed part.

Monitoring requires key on, battery voltage above 11 V and the calibration enabled. Injector-circuit, controller-status and boost-voltage fault restrictions apply, and dropped-pulse information must be absent. A qualifying requested or controlled reactivation pulse supplies the test event.

## What verifies a repair

The listed decision uses ten failures out of twenty samples, one sample per engine cycle, with Type A, one-trip reporting. Preserve whether the duration was too short or too long; both share the code but produce different waveform evidence.

Verification requires repeated acceptable injector-6 pull-in periods while the monitor is enabled. A normal pressure trace or current reading at only one instant cannot prove the rise-time check passed. Follow the vehicle-specific professional injector-driver procedure rather than manually energizing the injector or replacing it from the code name alone.
