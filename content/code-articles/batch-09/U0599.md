# U0599: DC/DC converter voltage and crank-status messages fail validation

U0599 concerns invalid data from DC/DC converter control module A. The selected ECM row monitors rolling counts and protection values associated with the converter actuator-voltage ADC value and crank-control terminal status. Each listed check uses eight failures out of 18 samples.

## Data integrity is not a measured converter-output fault

The frame must be present on the bus for this monitor. An invalid protection value or counter does not directly prove that the converter's output voltage is outside specification. A voltage number carried by a rejected message should not be used uncritically to diagnose power conversion.

The controller evaluates every 12.5 milliseconds after five seconds of qualified conditions. Battery voltage must be at least 11 volts, and no accessory-to-off transition may be pending. The table does not establish the electrical architecture or high-voltage applicability of every vehicle using the generic code.

## Separate communicated status from physical measurements

Identify whether the voltage-data field or crank-status field fails and compare its counter/protection status with actual system-state information obtained through the correct service procedure. A successful diagnostic connection to the converter does not establish that these operational fields pass at the ECM.

Verification requires accepted protected messages under the receiver's enable conditions. Any physical converter testing must follow the applicable electrical-safety procedure; this monitor table supplies no permission to probe high-voltage circuits. It does not identify a replacement converter or prove an internal power-stage failure.
