# U010E: reductant control module CAN messages exceed their timeouts

U010E records loss of communication with the reductant control module, called SCR in this ECM table. The receiver monitors a group of messages, including $0C3, $2C4–$2C8, $2CA–$2CC, $3AB and $5C7. Each listed message has a timeout exceeding 10,000 milliseconds.

## A timeout differs from invalid fluid data

The event concerns message arrival. It does not directly evaluate reductant quality, tank level or dosing effectiveness. A module may provide incorrect data while still communicating, but that is not the missing-message mechanism described here.

The diagnostic runs in a 12.5-millisecond loop after the listed enable conditions have held for at least five seconds. Full CAN communication and normal transmission are required. The applicable bus-off diagnostic must not be active, and a sensor-bus arrangement requires its relay on.

## Interpret the group of missing messages

Determine whether the entire reductant message group disappeared together or whether only a particular scheduled message is absent. The former can guide investigation toward a shared module supply or communication path; the latter requires attention to message configuration and receiver expectations. These are diagnostic inferences, not proof of a failed part.

Verification needs reception of the expected group beyond its timeout windows under normal enabled operation. A brief scan-tool connection to the module does not establish that all required periodic messages arrive. The source supplies no connector assignments or instruction to replace the reductant controller from U010E alone.

## Source and applicability

Documented 2025 GM gm-2025-04b monitor only; vehicle, engine and controller applicability must be established separately. [GM monitor table, PDF page 928](https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/2025/25OBDG04B.pdf#page=928).
