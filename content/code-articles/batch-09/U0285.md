# U0285: grille shutter B LIN response timeout and a disabled calibration

U0285 is lost communication with active grille air shutter module B. The documented mechanism watches the LIN response ACM2_Rsp and uses a timeout of at least 1,250 milliseconds. The communication task is listed as executing in a 500-millisecond loop.

## A critical applicability limitation

The same row prints the diagnostic enable as “Disabled.” Consequently, this source supports explaining the monitor design but does not prove that the supplied calibration actively sets U0285. Confirm the actual vehicle's configuration and software before applying the timing as an enabled diagnostic requirement.

The design also expects an initialized and enabled LIN channel, the slave configured as present and the relevant actuator or powertrain relay supply state. General power conditions must be satisfied for at least five seconds. An absent response while a slave is intentionally unpowered is different from a missing response during expected operation.

## Communication is not shutter movement

A shutter can report without achieving its commanded position; conversely, a displayed position may be stale when responses stop. Identify whether fresh ACM2_Rsp messages are arriving before interpreting position feedback or diagnosing a mechanical obstruction.

Verification must first establish that the applicable calibration enables this monitor, then confirm timely responses with the module powered and expected on the LIN channel. The cited table cannot justify a shutter replacement or a universal relearn procedure. Its disabled status is retained as an explicit source-review issue.
