# U064F: intake runner swirl-position SENT protocol failure

The cited U064F strategy names the swirl SENT position sensor for the intake manifold runner system. It fails on an internal position-message fault, or on message age above 6.25 milliseconds combined with specified protocol-state evidence.

## A changing interface can still carry unusable data

The age-qualified branches include steady-high status, steady-low status or receipt of a new message. The last branch explains why seeing interface activity is not sufficient: new traffic can exist without producing a fresh valid position value. The controller evaluates the protocol, not merely electrical switching.

The listed decision requires 480 failing counts out of 600 samples at a 6.25-millisecond task rate. Calibration must enable the test, the engine must be out of cranking and powertrain relay voltage must exceed 11 volts.

## Do not turn lost position data into a runner diagnosis

An apparent fixed runner position during U064F can reflect unavailable feedback. Establish valid decoded position before using the trace to infer a mechanically stuck intake runner. Conversely, normal-looking actuator movement does not establish that the SENT sensor reports correctly.

Verification requires the swirl-position stream to remain fresh and protocol-valid while its operation is exercised under the enabled conditions. The table does not provide a universal runner angle, actuator location or wiring repair. It also does not establish an intake-flow performance fault solely from the communication code.
