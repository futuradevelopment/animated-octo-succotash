# U0499: telematics positioning data fails protection validation

U0499 is invalid data from the telematics module. The cited EOCM3 row narrows the monitored content to redundant precise-positioning information: its burst, heading, latitude and longitude. The strategy checks rolling-counter, checksum or message-authentication violations.

## This is not simply loss of cellular service

The failure rule is six rejected messages out of ten for the listed positioning fields. The table also states an eight-second window within ten seconds based on the slowest transmission. It does not diagnose cellular signal strength, a navigation subscription or satellite reception quality directly.

Supply must be between 9 and 16 volts, a participating partial network must be active and more than five seconds must have elapsed after reset or a voltage-condition event. The source states that adaptive cruise and/or Super Cruise can be disabled upon detection.

## Position values and protection status both matter

A plausible map position does not establish that the protected position stream is accepted by the assistance controller. Preserve which positioning field fails and whether its counter/protection behavior changes around resets. Conversely, a wrong-looking map position without a protection violation is not the same diagnostic mechanism.

Verification needs accepted protected positioning messages at the EOCM3 over the qualified period. The table does not establish a failed antenna, mandate telematics-module replacement or prove that restoring communications alone restores every assisted-driving function. Those functions require the applicable professional checks.
