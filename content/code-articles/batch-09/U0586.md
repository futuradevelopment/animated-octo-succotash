# U0586: grille shutter B initialization-status data fails its counter check

The GM U0586 monitor watches ACM2_InitStatARC from active grille air shutter module B. It counts three failing observations out of ten samples when the received initialization-status message fails its rolling-count validation.

## A reply can arrive without being accepted

This monitor requires the message frame to be available on the bus. The failure is therefore different from the second shutter module providing no response at all. A repeated status packet with an unacceptable counter can leave a scan-tool value looking unchanged and believable even though the ECM rejects the stream.

Evaluation takes place in a 12.5-millisecond loop after five seconds of qualified power and transition conditions. Battery voltage must be at least 11 volts, with no pending accessory-to-off transition.

## Keep B separate from A

The explicit ACM2 identifier directs attention to the second shutter's status stream. Compare the affected counter with any module-A data only as context, not as a substitute for verifying module B. A normal-looking first shutter cannot demonstrate health of the second interface.

Confirmation requires the ECM to accept repeated initialization-status messages with proper counter behavior under enabled conditions. Shutter movement and its learned endpoints are separate evidence. The source does not support diagnosing physical obstruction, replacing the assembly or performing a universal relearn solely because U0586 was stored.
