# U0566: automatic-braking message rolling counter fails at the brake controller

U0566 has a broad generic image-processing-sensor-A invalid-data title. The selected EBCM row identifies a specific message check: LSCMB_AUTO_BRK_ARC. The brake controller examines the alive rolling counter in each newly received automatic-braking frame and reports a continuous-operation failure when that sequence is unacceptable.

## A counter failure is not a hydraulic brake diagnosis

The published sliding-window rule is four failures out of eight, with fastest maturation after four consecutive count-sequence errors. The monitored evidence is message progression. It does not directly establish brake pressure, pad condition or whether the vehicle actually executed an automatic-braking request.

The diagnostic is excluded during Start power mode, early power-mode transitions, the first five seconds after reset or voltage recovery and confirmed/recovering bus-off conditions. Supply must be within the stated operating range of 9 to 16 volts.

## Check the source and receiver together

The table explicitly allows problems at the signal source, message-source module, bus wiring or receiving module. That prevents the generic code title from uniquely identifying a failed camera or brake controller. Determine whether frames arrive with an incorrect counter sequence rather than disappear entirely.

Verification requires accepted counter progression at the EBCM under enabled conditions and the applicable professional brake/assistance-system checks. The emissions-neutral no-MIL classification does not establish braking safety. Testing a protected automatic-braking message should not be replaced with an improvised on-road activation attempt.
