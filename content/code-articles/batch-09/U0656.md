# U0656: variable-geometry turbo position feedback loses valid SENT data

U0656 has a broad turbocharger/supercharger boost-control-A position-sensor title. The cited GM monitor specifically identifies VGT position feedback over SENT. Its evidence concerns the feedback interface, not a direct failure to achieve requested boost pressure.

## Pulses and usable position are different

The monitor checks for a message error, or valid-message age above 6.25 milliseconds accompanied by steady-high/steady-low protocol status. The continuation also treats updated position counters with excessive valid-message age as a failing condition. Electrical activity therefore does not guarantee that the controller can use the reported VGT position.

The VGT SENT sensor must be configured as present. The engine must be out of cranking, relay voltage above 11 volts and the diagnostic enabled. Confirmation requires 480 failed samples out of 600 at a 6.25-millisecond task interval.

## Avoid a premature turbocharger conclusion

An apparent position that will not follow a command may be stale communication rather than a seized geometry mechanism. Establish valid feedback before attributing a simultaneous boost complaint to physical vane or actuator behavior. U0656 alone does not identify a failed turbocharger assembly.

Verification requires fresh accepted VGT positions throughout applicable operation and completion of the communication monitor. Boost-response checks may still be needed afterward, but they answer a separate question. The source provides no actuator pinout, probing voltage or mechanical adjustment procedure.
