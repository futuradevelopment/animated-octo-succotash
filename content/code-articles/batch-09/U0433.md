# U0433: long-range radar bursts arrive with invalid protection

U0433 monitors the long-range radar's six LRRODP protected bursts. The cited EOCM3 strategy detects rolling-counter, checksum or message-authentication violations. Each listed burst uses three failing messages out of four; the table describes a 0.03-second failure window within 0.04 seconds.

## Distinguish corrupt protection from absent bursts

The same radar stream can be supervised for arrival and for validity. U0433 concerns validity of received protected information. A radar that answers and sends bursts can therefore still produce this code, even though it would not be accurately described as entirely disconnected.

The monitor requires supply between 9 and 16 volts, an active participating partial network and more than five seconds after reset or voltage recovery. The source states that adaptive cruise is disabled upon detection.

## What to retain from the event

Determine whether a particular burst repeatedly fails its counter or protection check, or the whole stream develops simultaneous errors. That difference helps separate message-specific evidence from a broader network or synchronization event. Radar alignment and target detection are not measured by this integrity rule.

Verification should demonstrate the complete burst sequence passing its protection checks at the EOCM3 receiver after qualification. A plausible target list on a scan tool does not independently validate the protected stream. The code does not establish a failed radar assembly or prescribe calibration; any subsequent radar-function verification must follow the applicable professional procedure.
