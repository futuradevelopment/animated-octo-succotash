# U065A: right-front climate actuator timeout with an applicability caveat

U065A is lost communication with the right-front temperature actuator. The cited BCM monitor watches AHDA05_Rsp_PDU and gives a timeout of at least 11,500 milliseconds, with a 600-millisecond LIN execution loop.

## The configuration field changes the conclusion

Although the diagnostic and LIN channel are listed enabled, the slave-presence calibration is printed false. This source therefore does not prove that the right-front actuator is expected or monitored as present in the supplied configuration. Confirm the actual vehicle's equipment and calibration before treating an absent AHDA05 response as a component failure.

The design otherwise requires an initialized LIN module, startup/reset/voltage-recovery qualification and valid power-mode conditions. The continuation adds off-key and shutdown qualifications. Those conditions explain why a silent interface during shutdown is not automatically a failed actuator.

## Separate the proposed mechanism from applicability

When the applicable vehicle does configure the slave as present, the mechanism is a missing operational reply, not a direct measurement of the passenger-side air temperature. A comfort complaint alone cannot establish the timeout's cause or the actuator's mechanical state.

Verification first needs confirmed configuration, then repeated AHDA05 replies within the permitted interval during qualified operation. A healthy left-front response cannot verify the right-front stream. The unresolved presence flag remains a source-review limitation, and this table supplies no universal actuator replacement or relearn instruction.
