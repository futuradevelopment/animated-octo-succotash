# U0151: restraint-module messages missing at different rates

The selected ECM implementation of U0151 monitors messages from the restraints control module. Messages $039 and $0B9 have timeout limits above 10 seconds; $528 and $52A use limits above 450 milliseconds. The code therefore can originate from different missing periodic data, not only complete disappearance of the module.

## What the receiver actually knows

The ECM checks arrival while full CAN communication is requested, normal transmission is enabled and the relevant bus-off fault is absent. Its general conditions must hold for at least five seconds, and the diagnostic runs in a 12.5-millisecond loop.

This monitor is listed as emissions-neutral with no malfunction-indicator-lamp request. That classification is about emissions reporting, not an assurance that restraint functions are available. A check-engine lamp that remains off does not establish the condition of the restraint system.

## Keep communication and deployment circuits separate

Identify which message stopped arriving and compare the event with supply, reset and network faults across the vehicle. Missing module messages do not identify a particular airbag, pretensioner or impact sensor, and they do not justify probing deployment circuits. Restraint-system electrical work requires the vehicle's professional service precautions.

Verification should show the expected periodic messages returning within both the short and long timeout windows, followed by the applicable restraint-system checks. A successful one-time scan-tool response is useful but does not demonstrate that every monitored broadcast has resumed.
