# U0423: cluster status messages fail rolling-count or protection checks

U0423 is invalid data from the instrument panel cluster. In this EOCM3 table, the watched information includes confirmed speed-limit status, chime-system health, display status, lane-following chime-interface fault status and related operational information. The strategy checks rolling-counter, checksum or message-authentication protection violations.

## The dashboard can appear normal while its data is rejected

The listed fields use eight failed messages out of ten, with a stated eight-second window within ten seconds based on the slowest transmission. The monitor is about the integrity of information supplied to another controller; it is not simply a test that the cluster illuminates or displays a speed.

Supply must be between 9 and 16 volts, the participating partial network active and more than five seconds elapsed after reset or a voltage-condition event. The manufacturer enable counter must be zero.

## Narrow the rejected status

Identify which protected status or counter failed. Chime-health information and display-status information can have different functional implications even though both lead to U0423. A working speedometer does not verify these other messages.

Confirmation should show accepted counter/protection behavior for the affected cluster messages throughout the qualified observation period. The source does not establish that the display hardware is defective, require cluster replacement or identify a software remedy. Interpret any assistance-system effects using the reporting controller and exact message involved.
