# U0651: high-pressure EGR position sensor A SENT communication fails

The generic U0651 description names EGR sensor A. The cited GM strategy specifically assigns it to high-pressure EGR position feedback over SENT. This mapping is calibration-specific; the letter A should not be treated as a universal physical sensor-location guide.

## How the interface fails

The monitor recognizes valid-message age above 6.25 milliseconds with steady-low or steady-high protocol status. A message-error indication is another route. Its continuation also checks updated position counters while the age of the last valid position still exceeds the limit.

The SENT position sensor must be configured as present. The engine must be out of cranking, relay voltage above 11 volts and the diagnostic enabled. The decision accumulates 480 failures out of 600 samples at a 6.25-millisecond rate.

## Position availability precedes flow interpretation

A stationary reported high-pressure EGR position during the fault may be stale data rather than a stationary valve. Check message validity and age before interpreting the position as evidence of sticking or inadequate EGR flow. The source does not measure actual recirculated gas flow in this diagnosis.

Verification requires valid high-pressure EGR position updates under the monitor's qualified conditions, not just a responsive actuator command. If motion or flow remains abnormal after communication is restored, that is separate evidence. The table does not prove which sensor, harness or controller element caused the protocol problem.
