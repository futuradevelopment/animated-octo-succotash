# U053D: video-processing messages arrive with invalid protection

The cited U053D monitor evaluates information received from the video processing module. Its specific items are the surround-vision data burst and auxiliary-camera-1 and auxiliary-camera-2 connection status. The strategy checks rolling-counter, checksum or message-authentication violations.

## A connection-status value can itself be untrusted

The listed message checks use six failures out of ten. The table gives an eight-second window within ten seconds based on the slowest transmission. This means U053D can concern protection around a connection-status message; it does not necessarily prove that the camera described by that message is physically disconnected.

Supply must be between 9 and 16 volts, the relevant partial network active and more than five seconds elapsed after reset or voltage recovery. The manufacturer enable counter must be zero. The source states that adaptive cruise and/or Super Cruise can be disabled.

## Separate integrity from missing communication

If a message is absent, that is different evidence from a received message failing protection. Likewise, visible surround-view images do not show that the protected operational stream is accepted by the EOCM3. Identify the affected burst or auxiliary status before choosing further tests.

Verification should confirm valid counter/protection behavior for the implicated video-processing messages throughout the qualified window. The table provides no camera alignment result, image-quality threshold or automatic module-replacement instruction. Any remaining picture or assistance-system complaint requires separate functional evidence after communication integrity is restored.
