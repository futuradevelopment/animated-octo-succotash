# U0452: restraint-module data is received but fails integrity checks

U0452 is invalid data received from the restraints control module. The selected ECM row checks several message groups, including serial data 46 and 47, post-collision information and occupant-restraint information. The required frame is available on the bus; the problem is its validation.

## Different restraint messages use different count rules

Serial-data group 46 uses eight failures out of 18 samples, group 47 uses four out of ten, and the post-collision and occupant-restraint groups use three out of ten. The monitor runs in a 12.5-millisecond loop after the listed power and transition conditions have held for five seconds.

A present message with an incorrect rolling count or protection value is not the same as a silent restraint controller. Likewise, a protection failure in post-collision information does not prove that a collision occurred; it means that information cannot be accepted under the monitored rule.

## Maintain the safety boundary

Identify the rejected message and compare its integrity status with related reset or communication events. The code does not identify an airbag deployment circuit or authorize electrical probing of restraint components. Professional restraint service procedures apply.

The row is emissions-neutral with no MIL request, but that is not a statement about restraint availability. Verification requires the receiver to accept the affected protected messages and completion of the applicable restraint-system checks. A code clear or one successful scan-tool response does not establish that all monitored data is trustworthy.
