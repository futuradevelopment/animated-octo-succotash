# U0420: steering-wheel information fails a received-data check

In the selected ECM row, U0420 monitors the power steering control module's SWIP_ARC and StrgWhlInfo_Prtctd fields. Each uses eight failed observations out of 18 samples. The frame must be available on the bus for the documented validity check.

## Received does not mean trusted

A present steering-information message may fail rolling-count progression or its protection check. This is different from the steering module being completely silent, and different again from a measured steering angle being physically implausible. Preserve which type of evidence is present before choosing a diagnostic path.

The monitor executes in a 12.5-millisecond loop after five seconds of qualified conditions, including battery voltage at least 11 volts and no pending accessory-to-off transition. The source classifies this ECM result as emissions-neutral with no MIL request; that classification does not certify steering-assist availability.

## Verification must reach the receiver

Check that the reporting ECM accepts the steering message consistently, rather than relying solely on a scan tool's ability to contact the steering module. A message readable at one point can still fail the intended receiver's validation.

The table does not identify a failed steering rack, torque sensor or wiring segment. Professional steering diagnosis should establish the communication cause and perform the applicable functional checks after correction. Other controllers monitor more extensive steering torque and status fields under the same code, so these specific thresholds remain limited to the cited ECM implementation.
