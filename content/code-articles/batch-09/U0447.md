# U0447: gateway backup power-mode message fails validation

The selected U0447 implementation is a BCM check of gateway A, named CGM in the strategy. It watches BSPMP_ARC and BkupSysPwrMode_Prtctd, the rolling-count and protected backup-system-power-mode fields. Both use 15 failures out of 16 samples.

## A power-mode message is not the physical supply voltage

The monitor's subject is communicated backup power-mode information. A bad counter or protection result does not itself prove the vehicle's battery voltage was low, although voltage conditions separately qualify the test. Keeping message content and electrical supply evidence distinct prevents an unsupported battery diagnosis.

The BCM runs the check every 10 milliseconds, at least five seconds after startup, reset or voltage recovery. The required partial-network, RUN-mode and above-11-volt conditions must hold for three seconds.

## Identify the receiver and frame

Another ECM implementation checks the same generic code with different counts and additional serial data. Establish the reporting controller before applying a threshold. For this BCM row, confirm whether the backup power-mode counter or protected field is rejected despite the gateway communicating.

Verification requires valid progression and protection in that message under qualified operation. Merely communicating with gateway A does not prove its backup power-mode broadcast passes at the BCM. The source does not identify the gateway hardware as the cause or justify programming, replacement or power-mode reconfiguration without vehicle-specific diagnosis.
