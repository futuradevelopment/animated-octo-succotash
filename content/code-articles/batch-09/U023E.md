# U023E: right-front short-range radar data stops arriving

U023E is assigned to the right-front short-range radar in the cited EOCM3 monitor. The receiver expects six SRRRFODP protected bursts. A listed burst missing for more than 0.20 seconds meets its timeout comparison.

## Reception and sensing performance are separate

A radar can communicate without detecting an object, and that is not the failure mechanism described here. U023E concerns missing messages. Before investigating aiming or obstruction, determine whether the expected right-front operational stream is present and fresh.

The required operating context includes an active participating partial network, enabled diagnostic and supply between 9 and 16 volts. Transport mode and the initial five seconds after reset or voltage recovery are excluded. An active bus-off condition also inhibits this sender-timeout monitor. The documented default response disables adaptive cruise.

## Use side-specific evidence

Compare right-front burst reception with left-front and other network traffic during the same event. A shared interruption and a right-front-only dropout lead to different next checks, although neither pattern alone proves a particular component failure. Do not substitute a healthy opposite-side radar trace for this stream's verification.

Confirmation needs all required right-front bursts arriving within their timing limit under qualified conditions. A module that answers a scan-tool identification request can still fail an operational-message requirement. The table does not specify connector tests or establish that communication recovery alone completes any necessary radar calibration.
