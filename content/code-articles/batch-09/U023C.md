# U023C: video-processing status messages exceed their timeout

U023C concerns the video processing module in the cited GM row. The monitored information includes a surround-vision data burst and auxiliary-camera-1 and auxiliary-camera-2 connection-status signals. Each listed timeout exceeds 0.250 seconds.

## A status message can be missing while an image still exists

This monitor does not simply ask whether a screen displays a picture. It evaluates particular operational data and connection-status messages at the receiving controller. A visible camera image therefore does not establish that every monitored message is arriving correctly.

The receiver requires a participating partial network active, supply between 9 and 16 volts and the diagnostic enabled. Transport mode, early reset/voltage-recovery periods and active bus-off conditions are excluded. The table lists disabling adaptive cruise control as the default action on detection, demonstrating that video-processing information may serve another consumer beyond the display.

## Distinguish sender availability from image quality

Identify which of the three monitored items timed out. Missing auxiliary connection status is not, by itself, proof that the auxiliary camera optics or image sensor failed; the diagnosis is made at the message interface. Compare other receiver faults to determine whether the communication disruption was local or shared.

Verification should establish fresh receipt of the required burst and both connection-status signals under enabled conditions. Assess image quality separately if a complaint remains. The code supplies no universal camera replacement, calibration or wiring-location instruction.
