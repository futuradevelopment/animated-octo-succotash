# U059F: post-catalyst NOx sensor B information fails integrity checks

U059F evaluates data from NOx sensor B, with the cited signal names identifying the post-catalyst channel. The monitor covers oxygen and NOx information, sensor status, self-diagnostic feedback/results, error reports and component information. The relevant frames must be present on the bus.

## A self-diagnostic result also needs valid transport

The table checks protection around the sensor's own diagnostic feedback. Receiving a sensor-error-looking value is not enough to establish a physical sensor defect when the message itself fails integrity. Preserve the protection status before relying on its contents.

Most main measurement and diagnostic fields use eight failures out of 18 samples. Additional data and component-information checks in the continuation use three failures out of ten. Evaluation occurs every 12.5 milliseconds after five seconds of qualified conditions, including battery voltage at least 11 volts and no pending accessory-to-off transition.

## Do not use rejected data to condemn the catalyst

An apparently high downstream NOx number can influence an efficiency assessment, but U059F establishes that some sensor-B information is untrusted. Identify the affected field and restore reliable communication before drawing a catalyst-performance conclusion from that stream.

Verification requires valid counters, checksums and protection on the implicated post-catalyst messages. Communication availability alone is insufficient. The table does not prescribe sensor replacement, catalyst replacement or a software update, and it does not establish exact physical location beyond this calibration's post-catalyst signal designation.
