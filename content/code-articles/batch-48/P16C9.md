# P16C9: The heater-support pump does not reach minimum speed

## The low-speed category has its own qualification

P16C9 is the CRUA/DEJA heater-support pump entry for failure to reach minimum speed. The component is V488, and the engine controller relies on the output driver's diagnostic indication. Volkswagen lists three attempts over 11.5 seconds, followed by a two-driving-cycle malfunction-lamp schedule.

The row does not disclose the numerical minimum pump speed. It is therefore not a basis for inventing an RPM target or comparing V488 with a different auxiliary pump. It also differs from P16C7, whose strategy explicitly identifies a blocked pump and uses a different observation interval.

## Verify the circuit before interpreting limited performance

The linked V488 procedure establishes supply voltage and checks the control connection to the engine controller. A technician should keep those electrical prerequisites separate from the reported performance result. Pump noise, vibration or brief operation alone does not demonstrate that the required response was achieved.

Preserve related pump codes and the circumstances of the warning. Correct component identification is especially important when the vehicle has several electric coolant pumps with different jobs and connectors. Use the appropriate wiring and cooling-system information before selecting further checks.

Once the cause is addressed, the relevant confirmation is successful commanded operation without low-speed feedback. The 11.5-second monitor duration should not be treated as an instruction to apply external power to a suspect pump or as a universal test specification for other Volkswagen engines.
