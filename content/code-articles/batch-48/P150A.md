# P150A: The engine-off timer disagrees with the after-run timer

## The comparison depends on power after ignition-off

The Touareg Hybrid CGFA P150A row compares engine-off time from the instrument cluster with the engine controller's after-run time. A difference below negative eight seconds or above positive eight seconds triggers the comparison. The monitor runs with ignition on, active CAN communication and the controller's keep-alive period, using a two-second observation once per driving cycle.

This is a disagreement between time records. It does not directly establish that the instrument cluster or engine controller must be replaced, and it is unrelated to simply timing how long the starter motor cranks.

## Check the retained power and ground first

The diagnostic column expressly says that the engine controller needs battery power and ground while ignition is off for its timer to operate. It directs the technician to establish those inputs before any controller-replacement decision. That is a concrete reason to investigate the off-state supply, not just verify that the engine starts with ignition on.

Preserve related supply and communication faults and note recent power interruptions or module work as diagnostic context, without assuming they caused the error. The correct wiring diagram must establish which circuits retain power during shutdown.

After repair, verification should include the shutdown, keep-alive and subsequent ignition event involved in the comparison. Erasing P150A while leaving the vehicle continuously running cannot demonstrate that the two time records now agree.
