# P305E: Downstream intake temperature disagrees with startup references

## The monitor compares sensors at a defined event

P305E is the Touareg Hybrid CGFA downstream-throttle intake-temperature plausibility entry. The table compares the startup value with coolant temperature and with intake temperature after intercooler one. Its high and low branches use differences around positive and negative 26°C, after the temperature cross-check has completed.

This is a comparison between values at startup, not a general requirement that all temperatures remain equal while the engine operates. The row lists an event-based, once-per-driving-cycle check with no additional delay and a two-driving-cycle lamp schedule.

## Establish which value is trustworthy

A disagreement does not independently identify the downstream sensor as the failed part. The reference channels and the conditions at the comparison event also matter. The diagnostic destination is G42/G71, but a technician should preserve related coolant and intake-temperature faults before deciding which signal can serve as a reliable reference.

Use the installed vehicle's component and circuit information and retain the original startup data where available. Comparing readings after the engine has already warmed may not reproduce the relationship evaluated by this monitor.

Do not replace a sensor merely because two live values differ during normal heating. Confirmation should show that the intended startup cross-check completes with plausible relationships among the correct channels. A single acceptable downstream reading or an erased warning cannot establish that the reference comparison has passed.
