# U140E: Low voltage restricts reducing-agent controller functions

## Function restriction begins at its own threshold

Audi's U140E row describes reducing-agent control-module function restriction due to insufficient voltage. With ignition on, supply below nine volts for more than 500 milliseconds meets the listed criterion. The table gives continuous checking and a two-driving-cycle lamp schedule.

The neighboring low-supply fault U140C uses an 8.5-volt threshold. The difference is useful: this restriction entry is not merely a second name for the identical threshold. Within the cited calibration, the controller can qualify restricted operation at a voltage that has not reached the lower U140C boundary.

## Restore reliable supply rather than bypassing the restriction

The diagnostic destination is J880. A technician should preserve related voltage and emissions-system faults and evaluate the module's actual supply and ground path using the installed vehicle's wiring information. The code does not identify which individual dosing or heating function was affected from its title alone.

Do not attempt to override restricted operation or assume a fluid refill will clear an electrical supply problem. The restriction is diagnostic evidence about operating conditions, not proof that the metering unit or control module must be replaced.

After the cause is corrected, verification should demonstrate stable voltage and restoration of the functions required by the matching factory procedure. A cleared warning or an acceptable battery reading at another location does not establish that J880's supply remained adequate throughout the relevant monitoring event.
