# P16C8: Thermal feedback from the heater-support pump

## The monitored temperature concern belongs to the pump

Volkswagen's CRUA/DEJA P16C8 row concerns an overheated heater-support coolant pump V488. Its diagnostic evidence is the output driver's report. The qualification is three attempts over nine seconds, and the malfunction lamp is listed after two driving cycles.

No numerical coolant-temperature threshold is supplied in this row. It would be inaccurate to translate the code into a particular dashboard temperature or declare the engine overheated solely from P16C8. It is equally unsupported to assume that normal indicated engine temperature rules out the reported pump condition.

## Investigate the pump's power and operating environment

The factory V488 procedure checks the electrical supply and the connection to the engine controller. Those checks establish whether the pump can be controlled as intended before a component decision is made. The diagnostic record should also retain other V488 reports, because an isolated thermal indication differs from a collection of electrical and operating faults.

A technician needs the actual vehicle's coolant arrangement and pump identification. Opening a hot cooling system or repeatedly operating a pump that reports overheating can create additional risk and does not determine the original cause.

After the underlying problem is corrected, verification must address the pump's monitored operation. The nine-second entry describes the controller's qualification window, not a recommended manual test duration. Absence of a warning immediately after cooling down is not the same as demonstrating successful operation under the conditions that produced the fault.
