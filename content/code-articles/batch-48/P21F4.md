# P21F4: Bank-one multiport fueling reaches its lean correction limit

## The positive correction is the diagnostic event

In the CREC table, P21F4 concerns system-B multiport fueling on bank one away from idle. The adaptation value reaches at least positive twenty-five percent, with a twenty-second monitoring period. This is a learned mixture-correction limit, not an electrical resistance test of an individual injector.

Eligibility includes coolant above 70°C, a defined air and fuel-flow range, and engine speed between 704 and 4,960 RPM. The table also requires relatively stable speed and air mass. A cold idle reading cannot be treated as the same qualified part-load result.

## Look beyond the injector named by a shortened description

Audi's diagnostic column spans fuel delivery, pressure regulation, the B-circuit injectors, pressure feedback, intake-air leakage and the bank-one oxygen-sensor information. That breadth matters because the control correction does not identify a unique failed component.

The table continues onto the next page with purge-related qualifiers, some marked unavailable. This article retains a review flag for that incomplete detail instead of publishing a fabricated complete drive cycle. Preserve the original operating record and associated lean, pressure or sensor codes for a technician to evaluate.

Fuel-system testing must follow the relevant pressure and fire precautions. Do not assume an injector replacement is justified by the adaptation limit alone. Confirmation should show acceptable bank-one correction under the applicable multiport part-load conditions, with the underlying air, fuel and feedback evidence considered together.
