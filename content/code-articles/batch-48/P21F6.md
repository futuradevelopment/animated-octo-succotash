# P21F6: Bank-two lean adaptation needs bank-specific feedback

## Keep the bank assignment attached to the control result

P21F6 is the CREC bank-two system-B lean fault away from idle. The table sets the adaptation boundary at positive twenty-five percent or higher and observes it for twenty seconds. Its conditions include coolant above 70°C, specified air and fuel-flow ranges and relatively stable part-load operation.

This is a bank-specific control result. It should not be turned into a diagnosis of one injector solely from the positive correction, nor should bank-one sensor data be substituted without confirming what the diagnostic tool is displaying.

## Compare local and shared evidence

Audi's references include fuel delivery, pressure regulation, B-circuit injectors, pressure sensor G247 and intake-air leakage. For oxygen feedback, the row names the bank-two paths, including the pre-catalyst GX11 reference. That combination allows a technician to consider shared supply issues alongside evidence specific to the affected bank, without assuming either category is the cause.

The continued enable-condition text contains unavailable purge-related values. This article retains a source-review flag and does not fill those gaps with another calibration's parameters. Preserve the complete operating record before resetting learned corrections.

Fuel-system work requires the appropriate service precautions. After the actual cause is resolved, confirmation should show acceptable bank-two mixture correction under eligible multiport part-load conditions. A single idle fuel-trim snapshot is not equivalent to this monitor and cannot establish that the original bank-specific condition has cleared.
