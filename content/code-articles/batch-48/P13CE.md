# P13CE: Cylinder-one pressure feedback is electrically high

## A glow-plug code can concern pressure measurement

P13CE is the cylinder-one pressure-sensor electrical-fault entry in Volkswagen's CRUA/DEJA manual. The strategy identifies a signal above 3.17 volts while the engine runs. It observes forty revolutions and lists a two-driving-cycle lamp response. The diagnostic destination is pressure-sensing glow plug QX1.

That description is different from a conventional glow-plug heater-circuit diagnosis. Volkswagen explains that QX1 supplies combustion-pressure information used by the engine controller to adjust injection timing and air management. The code's pressure-signal threshold should not be applied as a heater-supply voltage or as a general compression-test limit.

## Verify the signal circuit before condemning the assembly

The QX1 procedure first establishes its approximately five-volt supply and the relevant wiring paths. A high feedback value can therefore require examination of the connector and circuit as well as the integrated sensor. The code alone does not distinguish an external wiring problem from an internal sensing fault.

Identify cylinder one and QX1 from the installed engine's documentation rather than assuming a physical location from a generic diagram. Preserve related pressure-sensor and supply codes, then have a technician follow the appropriate electrical checks without probing an energized circuit indiscriminately.

The forty-revolution qualification also means that a static key-on reading is only one part of the evidence. Final confirmation needs the engine-running monitor to operate successfully with the correct pressure-signal circuit restored.
