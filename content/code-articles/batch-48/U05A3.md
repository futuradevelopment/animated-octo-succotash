# U05A3: Reductant-quality data exceeds raw-message limits

## Raw values must not be relabeled as temperatures

For CRUA/DEJA engines, U05A3 concerns invalid data received from the reductant-quality module. Volkswagen's table specifies a raw temperature value above 251 or a raw concentration value above 241. The monitor begins more than fifteen seconds after key-on and observes the condition for ten seconds.

Those numbers are identified as raw values. They are not presented as 251°C or 241 percent reducing-agent concentration, and this article does not invent a conversion. Treating the encoded limits as physical measurements would produce a misleading diagnosis of the fluid or sensor.

## Distinguish invalid content from missing communication

The invalid-data category means the returned information has failed its plausibility or coding check. It should not be collapsed into the separate lost-communication code U02A2. The table directs further diagnosis to the appropriate communication-system information rather than providing a fluid-replacement instruction.

Preserve the exact code and any related quality-sensor or communication faults. A technician should identify the installed module, inspect the relevant data in the correct units and follow the factory network and circuit checks. A displayed concentration reading from another scan-tool field is not automatically the raw value used by this monitor.

The fifteen-second startup qualification and ten-second observation also matter during verification. A brief key cycle without recurrence is not enough to establish that valid messages remain available through the full eligible check. Fluid condition may require separate evidence; U05A3 alone does not prove contaminated reducing agent.
