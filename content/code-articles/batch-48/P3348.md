# P3348: Turbocharger actuator control has current and voltage branches

## Two electrical criteria can produce the circuit fault

P3348 concerns turbocharger control module one J724 in the Audi CPNA/CPNB table. With the engine-controller power stage on, the strategy lists a current-related diagnostic threshold in an 8–18-amp range or a voltage-related signal above 5 volts or below 3.4 volts. The monitor observes the condition for half a second.

These are power-stage diagnostic quantities. They should not be treated as boost-pressure limits, turbocharger shaft-speed specifications or instructions to externally load the actuator. The code's circuit classification does not by itself establish a damaged turbocharger rotating assembly.

## Preserve command and actuator feedback together

Audi directs diagnosis to the J724 component procedure. A technician should identify the installed control module and its wiring, retain related actuator and supply faults, and determine which electrical evidence accompanies the stored P3348 record.

A mechanism that can be moved or a pressure reading that looks reasonable does not establish that the control circuit is sound. Conversely, an electrical driver report is not sufficient evidence to replace the whole turbocharger without the appropriate tests.

Work around the actuator and exhaust hardware requires safe temperature and movement precautions. Do not force a control arm or bypass the controller to suppress the warning. After repair, verification should demonstrate normal electrical feedback during the applicable on-state command and no return of the original circuit monitor, with the actual calibration and hardware identified.
