# P305C: A very hot indicated intake temperature can be an electrical fault

## The displayed temperature is part of the circuit diagnosis

In the Touareg Hybrid CGFA table, P305C concerns intake-air temperature sensor two downstream of the throttle. The strategy labels a short-to-ground condition and sets the criterion at an indicated temperature above 128°C. The observation lasts 25.5 seconds, with a two-driving-cycle lamp schedule.

The row does not establish that the incoming air physically reached that temperature. A sensor-circuit fault can produce an extreme interpreted value, so the electrical classification must remain attached to the temperature reading. The diagnostic reference identifies G42 together with manifold-pressure sensor G71.

## Verify the correct temperature channel

A technician should preserve related temperature and pressure-sensor faults, confirm the installed combined sensing arrangement and follow its supply and wiring checks. An upstream air-temperature value can be reasonable while this downstream channel remains invalid; the two readings are not interchangeable.

Do not replace charge-air cooling hardware solely because P305C contains a high calculated temperature. Equally, do not dismiss the fault just because the engine feels cool. The relevant question is whether the identified sensor circuit supplies credible information.

The 25.5-second qualification should be considered during verification. A single normal value after a restart does not establish that an intermittent low-circuit condition has disappeared. Confirmation should demonstrate stable, believable downstream-throttle temperature feedback and absence of the original monitor result through the appropriate observation.
