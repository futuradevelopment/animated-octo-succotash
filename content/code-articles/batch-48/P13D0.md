# P13D0: Pressure plausibility includes more than a voltage range

## Several tests can lead to one plausibility code

P13D0 is the cylinder-one pressure-sensor plausibility entry in the CRUA/DEJA table. One branch evaluates a signal outside 0.33–3.09 volts. Other branches look for insufficient pressure variation, an offset outside ±7 bar, and disagreement exceeding ±10 bar between measured pressure and a calculation based on intake pressure and compression ratio.

These branches explain why a signal that lies between its electrical limits can still be implausible. A sensor can produce a plausible voltage yet fail to follow the pressure changes or reference relationship expected by the controller. The table gives a 1,000-revolution monitoring period for the grouped entry.

## Preserve the operating event and the source limits

The calculated-pressure comparison uses fuel-cut conditions with the throttle open and both EGR paths closed. Other checks use running conditions. A single stationary reading cannot represent every branch, so recording when the fault occurred is more informative than checking only whether a voltage is present.

The source prints temperature qualifiers with awkward “and/or” wording. This article does not convert that wording into a new universal test window; the exact current procedure must resolve eligibility before a technician reproduces the test.

The referenced component is QX1, the cylinder-one pressure-sensing glow plug. Its supply and harness checks remain relevant before a replacement decision. P13D0 does not, on its own, distinguish sensor drift, circuit trouble or a pressure relationship requiring further investigation.
