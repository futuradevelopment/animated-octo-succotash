# P16C0: The heater-support pump driver reports a positive short

## Read the fault at the driver level

P16C0 identifies a short-to-positive condition in the heater-support coolant-pump circuit in the CRUA/DEJA table. The relevant component is V488, and detection is based on the output driver's diagnostic signal. The observation period is 480 milliseconds; the listed malfunction-lamp response is two driving cycles.

That description does not provide a universal measured-voltage limit or establish where the unwanted connection lies. In particular, the presence of normal battery voltage on an intended supply wire is not, by itself, the fault described as a short to positive. The circuit's function and the driver's feedback must be considered together.

## Use the pump-specific wiring information

The factory V488 procedure starts with the pump's supply and its connection to the engine controller. Its wiring branches distinguish the harness and connector from the component decision. This prevents a high-level scan description from becoming an automatic instruction to replace the pump.

Preserve any other heater-support pump codes and inspect the relevant operating record. A technician should identify V488's supply and control paths on the installed engine before testing. Applying a diagram for another auxiliary pump can misidentify both the connector and the expected signal.

After the actual circuit issue is repaired, verify that the driver no longer reports the positive-short state during pump operation. The 480-millisecond monitor entry is not a safe manual powering time or a substitute for the complete diagnostic procedure.
