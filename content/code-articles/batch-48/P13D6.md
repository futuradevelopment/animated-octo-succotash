# P13D6: Cylinder-three pressure can fail a dynamic plausibility check

## A steady signal is not necessarily a valid signal

Volkswagen associates P13D6 with cylinder-three pressure sensing through QX3. The CRUA/DEJA table includes an electrical-range check outside 0.33–3.09 volts, but it also describes insufficient variation in measured pressure and errors in offset or calculated-pressure agreement. The grouped observation is 1,000 revolutions.

That combination prevents a common diagnostic shortcut: finding a voltage within range does not prove that the sensor follows combustion events. The controller can question the shape or reference relationship of the signal even when it is neither near ground nor near the upper supply limit.

## Resolve the cylinder references before using detailed limits

The row's heading and component destination identify cylinder three, while some detailed comparison text still says sensor one. That mismatch is retained as a source-review flag. The numerical comparison branches are not presented here as verified cylinder-three workshop limits until the correct factory revision confirms their assignment.

A useful investigation preserves the original P13D6 record and compares it with other pressure-sensor faults. The technician can then confirm QX3 identification, evaluate its circuit and inspect the relevant live pressure information with the vehicle's actual calibration in mind.

Avoid treating this code as a conventional glow-plug heating failure or proof of poor mechanical compression. The cited evidence concerns pressure feedback. Any mechanical conclusion requires additional testing, and repair confirmation must exercise the pressure-plausibility monitor under its applicable conditions.
