# P25E9: The supercharger clutch fails to transmit the requested drive

## The speed ratio is evaluated after engagement is requested

In the CREC table, P25E9 identifies the supercharger disconnect clutch as stuck open. The controller commands the clutch closed and looks for a supercharger-to-engine speed ratio below 2.20. Engine speed must exceed 550 RPM, with limited engine-speed change and an elapsed-time qualification. The monitoring period is half a second.

This is a functional comparison, not simply an electrical continuity check. A clutch can have an intact electrical path while failing to transmit the expected drive, so a normal static resistance measurement cannot alone prove that the commanded mechanical connection succeeds.

## Keep electrical and loaded-operation evidence together

The referenced N421 section explains that the magnetic clutch connects the belt pulley to the supercharger drive shaft when higher output is required. Its checking sequence examines the component, supply and controller wiring, and expressly notes that a failure can appear under loaded operation.

A technician should preserve the commanded clutch state and relevant speed data, identify the actual N421 circuit and follow the correct procedure. Do not approach a moving belt drive or force clutch engagement to imitate the monitor.

After repair, verification should demonstrate the intended drive relationship when engagement is requested. Audible operation or an erased code does not establish the required speed ratio, and the threshold belongs to this CREC calibration rather than every supercharger clutch.
