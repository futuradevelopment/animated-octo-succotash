# P190D: A charge-air pump fails to achieve its mechanical response

## Read the feedback as a mechanical-response complaint

P190D belongs to the V188 charge-air cooling pump in Volkswagen's CRUA/DEJA table. Its monitor description combines a blocked pump with failure to reach minimum speed. Detection comes from the output driver's diagnostic signal, with three attempts over 7.5 seconds. The listed lamp response requires two driving cycles.

The code therefore carries more information than a generic cooling-system warning. The controller has categorized the pump response as mechanically unsuccessful. However, the table does not publish a universal minimum pump speed, current draw or flow specification. Applying a number from another auxiliary pump would add unsupported precision.

## Separate the report from the replacement decision

The associated V188 checking sequence still establishes electrical supply and wiring integrity. It checks the supply, ground and controller connection before its component decision. That order is useful because a mechanical-sounding description is not permission to skip the circuit evidence.

Preserve companion codes, especially supply or driver faults, and identify which coolant circuit V188 serves on the actual vehicle. A workshop can then follow the relevant factory checks and inspect the circuit under safe, depressurized conditions. Do not assume that a quiet pump is seized or that audible operation proves adequate coolant circulation.

After the cause is addressed, the diagnostic record should show that the requested pump operation can occur without the blocked/minimum-speed feedback returning. The 7.5-second entry describes this monitor's observation, not a recommended duration for manually powering a suspect pump.
