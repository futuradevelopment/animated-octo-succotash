# P25D9: The cluster reports a low fault from fuel-level sensor C

## Separate the transported message from the sensor fault

In the CREC table, P25D9 is the low-circuit entry for fuel-level sensor C, described in the strategy as sensor three. The engine controller receives an instrument-cluster report of a short-to-ground failure or a signal-range failure. The observation period is half a second, with the malfunction lamp listed after two driving cycles.

The message travels through the communication system, but the reported fault concerns the fuel-level sensing path. It would be inaccurate to conclude that a CAN wire is shorted to ground simply because CAN appears in the strategy description.

## Follow the fault back to its reporting module

Audi directs the technician to the correct wiring information and makes the cluster decision conditional on the fuel-level sensor and its circuitry checking correctly. That order preserves an important distinction: a module reporting a fault is not automatically the component that caused it.

Keep the exact sensor designation, cluster faults and related fuel-level information available before clearing memory. The installed tank and sensor arrangement must be identified from the matching vehicle documentation; the table does not provide a universal resistance curve or physical location for sensor C.

After the actual problem is corrected, verification should establish both believable level information at the reporting module and absence of the returned low/range fault message. A gauge movement or successful communication scan by itself does not demonstrate that the specific sensor-channel condition has been resolved.
