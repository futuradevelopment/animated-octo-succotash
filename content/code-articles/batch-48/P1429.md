# P1429: The brake-vacuum pump circuit has an open-path signature

## An intermediate voltage can be a diagnostic fault

P1429 is the Touareg Hybrid CGFA brake-vacuum pump interruption entry. The strategy identifies an open circuit when the monitored signal lies within 4.40–5.60 volts. The engine must exceed 80 RPM, and the observation lasts half a second. The malfunction lamp is listed after two driving cycles.

This window is a controller diagnostic signature, not the pump's normal power-supply specification. It should not be used as an external voltage to apply to the pump or interpreted as evidence that brake-vacuum pressure itself is correct.

## Verify the path without bypassing brake control

Volkswagen directs diagnosis to the V192 vacuum pump and J569 brake-booster relay procedure. The exact vehicle wiring is needed to distinguish an interrupted harness or connector from the component and control paths. A code naming a pump does not automatically identify the pump as the failed part.

Preserve other brake and electrical faults before clearing memory. Do not bridge relay contacts or rely on a pump noise test as proof of safe assistance. The monitored electrical condition and actual brake-assistance function require appropriate professional verification.

A vehicle with reduced assistance or unusual pedal effort should not be driven merely to see whether P1429 returns. After repair, the correct running monitor must remain satisfied and the braking system must meet its service checks. Clearing fault memory alone establishes neither result.
