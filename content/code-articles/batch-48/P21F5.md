# P21F5: Bank-one multiport fueling reaches its rich correction limit

## Negative adaptation is the central observation

Audi's CREC P21F5 row concerns rich operation in system-B multiport fueling on bank one away from idle. The adaptation value reaches negative twenty-five percent or below for a twenty-second monitor. The fault therefore describes excessive corrective effort in mixture control, not a direct measurement of a leaking injector.

The table qualifies the check with warm coolant, a defined fuel-flow range, engine speed between 704 and 4,960 RPM and limited speed/air-mass variation. A transient reading while the engine is cold or accelerating rapidly does not represent the same diagnostic window.

## Investigate pressure, delivery and feedback together

The diagnostic column includes pressure regulation and residual pressure, the B-circuit injectors, G247 pressure feedback and bank-one oxygen-sensor information. These references support a structured investigation; they do not identify one component that must be replaced because the correction is negative.

The row continues onto another page and includes purge-related qualifiers whose values are unavailable in places. That incomplete detail remains flagged. The article does not create missing enable values or prescribe an unsupported universal confirmation drive.

Preserve the fault's operating record and related mixture or pressure codes. A technician should verify the physical fuel system and the reliability of the feedback under the correct conditions, using professional fuel-pressure precautions. Clearing adaptation can change the displayed correction without correcting the cause, so successful confirmation requires acceptable behavior after the relevant control learning and monitoring have occurred.
