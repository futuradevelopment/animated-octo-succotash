# P3081: Measured coolant temperature stays below the model

## The temperature difference is the monitored quantity

Volkswagen's Touareg Hybrid CGFA P3081 entry describes engine temperature too low. The strategy compares a reference-model temperature with measured coolant temperature and identifies a difference greater than 9.8 kelvin. The observation period is 10.5 seconds, with continuous monitoring and a two-driving-cycle lamp schedule.

The criterion is a temperature difference, not an absolute coolant target of 9.8°C. It also does not establish that the thermostat is defective from the code alone. The model-to-measurement relationship depends on both actual warming behavior and credible sensor information.

## Check the thermal control and the measurement

The diagnostic column directs the technician to consider a thermostat stuck open and to check coolant-temperature sensor G62. Those are separate diagnostic paths: one concerns physical temperature regulation, the other the information supplied to the controller.

Preserve related coolant-temperature and operating faults and use the matching factory procedure for the installed engine. Do not infer a universal warm-up time or thermostat-opening temperature from this single row; those specifications are not supplied here.

Cooling-system work requires safe temperature and pressure, and sensor testing requires the correct wiring information. After the cause is corrected, verification should show acceptable agreement between the model and measured coolant temperature under relevant operation. A gauge that appears normal or a warning cleared after a short idle does not demonstrate that this comparison has passed.
