# P1427: The hybrid brake-vacuum pump circuit reports excessive current

## The monitor concerns the pump circuit

In the Touareg Hybrid CGFA table, P1427 is a brake-vacuum pump circuit fault. The strategy describes a short-to-positive condition and identifies signal current above 2.2 amps. It is qualified with engine speed above 80 RPM and the electrical vacuum pump commanded on, then observed for half a second.

The code therefore does not directly report a measured brake-vacuum level or prove that the vacuum pump is mechanically worn out. It records an electrical condition in the monitored pump-control path. The table lists a two-driving-cycle malfunction-lamp response.

## Treat brake assistance and the electrical evidence separately

Volkswagen points diagnosis to vacuum pump V192 and its brake-booster relay J569 procedure. A technician should preserve related brake and electrical faults and use the installed vehicle's wiring and service information to distinguish supply, relay, harness and component evidence.

Do not energize the pump through improvised wiring or assume that hearing it run proves brake assistance is adequate. The 2.2-amp criterion belongs to this monitor; it is not a universal acceptable running-current specification for every vacuum pump.

Brake-assistance concerns require professional assessment. If braking assistance is reduced or pedal effort is abnormal, the vehicle should not be used for a diagnostic road experiment. After repair, confirmation must address the correct pump circuit and the braking system's required operation, rather than relying solely on an erased engine-controller code.
