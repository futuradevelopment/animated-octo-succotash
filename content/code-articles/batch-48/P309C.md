# P309C: The hybrid clutch controller reports a hardware or voltage fault

## Read the report behind the mechanical label

In the Touareg Hybrid CGFA table, P309C is labeled as a clutch-control mechanical fault. Its strategy, however, evaluates communication from the electric-clutch control module and lists an actuator hardware failure or actuator-voltage failure. The monitor observes the condition for 0.1 seconds and lists a two-driving-cycle lamp schedule.

This distinction matters because the title alone can suggest that clutch friction parts are physically damaged. The stated evidence is a reported actuator fault. The row does not publish a wear limit, pressure specification or a teardown decision.

## Preserve the clutch module's own evidence

Volkswagen refers the technician to the appropriate repair manual rather than providing a complete component test in this row. The useful next evidence is the full clutch-control diagnostic record, related supply or communication faults and the exact installed hybrid-system information.

A qualified hybrid technician should determine whether the report originates in electrical supply, actuator hardware or another condition documented by the matching procedure. Do not bypass clutch control, force hybrid-drive operation or perform improvised testing around high-voltage components.

Repair confirmation should show that the actuator no longer reports the underlying hardware or voltage condition and that the relevant hybrid functions satisfy their prescribed checks. Clearing P309C from the engine controller does not establish that the originating clutch-control fault has been corrected, and the word “mechanical” is not sufficient evidence to authorize component replacement.
