# P13D3: Cylinder-two plausibility has a copied label in the table

## What is securely identified

The CRUA/DEJA manual places P13D3 under cylinder-two pressure-sensor plausibility and directs diagnosis to glow plug QX2. Its electrical-range branch lists values below 0.33 volts or above 3.09 volts while the engine runs. The grouped monitor is shown with a 1,000-revolution period and a two-driving-cycle lamp response.

Additional branches compare pressure variation, offset and calculated pressure. Those are useful distinctions from a simple open- or short-circuit monitor: an apparently normal voltage is not automatically a believable combustion-pressure signal.

## Keep the source inconsistency visible

Within the cylinder-two row, the detailed dynamic-pressure and calculated-pressure text still refers to sensor one. The cylinder heading and QX2 reference therefore do not agree cleanly with every internal label. This article remains marked for source review rather than silently rewriting those lines as confirmed cylinder-two specifications.

A technician should identify the stored code, QX2 and the appropriate wiring using the matching factory information, then verify the intended cylinder assignment before applying the detailed pressure comparisons. Do not replace QX1 merely because a copied line mentions sensor one, or treat the inconsistency as evidence that two components must be changed.

The practical starting point remains the cylinder-two pressure-sensing circuit and its real operating data. Preserve companion codes and the conditions that produced the plausibility fault; confirmation requires the relevant running check, not just an erased warning or a normal-looking static voltage.
