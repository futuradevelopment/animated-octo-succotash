# U02A2: The reductant-quality sensor loses its SENT communication

## A communication label does not identify every network layer

Volkswagen assigns U02A2 to lost communication with the reductant-quality module in the CRUA/DEJA table. The strategy specifically identifies a SENT driver error on the quality-sensor communication path. It becomes eligible more than fifteen seconds after key-on and uses a fifty-second observation period.

That detail is useful because a generic U-code description can tempt a diagnosis of the entire vehicle CAN network. The cited row concerns the sensor's communication path and directs the technician to the appropriate reductant-quality repair information. It does not give a universal CAN resistance test or a module replacement decision.

## Verify that the sensor can send usable information

Preserve other quality-sensor, power-supply and network faults before clearing memory. A technician needs the installed sensor arrangement, its supply and its actual communication wiring to determine whether the missing information results from a local circuit issue or the sensor assembly.

Do not infer reducing-agent concentration, contamination or tank level from a missing-message condition. Those are physical properties that require their own evidence. Likewise, a sensor that temporarily appears in a scan-tool list has not necessarily maintained communication through the monitor's long qualification window.

After repair, allow the correct startup and observation conditions to occur and verify that the communication error does not return. The fifty-second entry describes monitoring behavior; it is not a waiting period that can substitute for identifying and correcting an intermittent electrical cause.
