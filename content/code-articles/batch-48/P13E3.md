# P13E3: Cylinder-four pressure timing is not a heater diagnosis

## Follow the pressure-sensing function of QX4

P13E3 is identified as the cylinder-four pressure-sensor circuit plausibility fault in Volkswagen's CRUA/DEJA manual. Its strategy evaluates pressure-derived top-dead-centre timing during fuel cut. Coolant must be above 59.96°C, and the row lists forty revolutions with a two-driving-cycle lamp response.

The diagnostic destination includes pressure-sensing glow plug QX4 and crankshaft speed sensor G28. Although the component is a glow plug, the monitored function here is pressure information relative to engine position. Replacing a heater component without investigating that relationship would skip the evidence described by the code.

## Keep the documentation mismatch unresolved until verified

The detailed threshold line is copied with sensor-one numbering despite the cylinder-four heading. This article remains source-review pending. It does not present the printed angular boundary as an independently confirmed cylinder-four calibration value or infer a physical cylinder location from the generic code.

A technician can still preserve the operating record, identify QX4 correctly and examine related pressure and crankshaft faults. The actual factory wiring and diagnostic revision should establish which pressure input the comparison uses before detailed measurements are interpreted.

There is no basis in this row alone to declare a stretched timing drive, damaged cylinder or failed engine controller. Confirmation requires the correct pressure channel and crank reference to remain coherent during the eligible fuel-cut event, not simply a warning-free restart.
