# P1004: Cylinder-one torque correction exceeds the allowed error

## The controller compares torque, not compression directly

In Volkswagen's CRUA/DEJA table, P1004 identifies excessive torque-control error for cylinder one. The monitor compares actual and requested torque against a limit that depends on engine speed and desired torque; the row describes errors beyond negative or positive thirty newton-metres. Its monitoring period is 120 revolutions.

Eligibility is specific: the engine must run in closed-loop control, coolant temperature must exceed 50°C, barometric pressure must exceed 750 hPa, and more than thirty seconds must have elapsed since starting. Regeneration must be inactive. A result collected during a cold start or active regeneration is not the same documented operating event.

## Follow the evidence across fuel delivery and speed sensing

The diagnostic column directs attention to the crankshaft speed sensor G28, restrictions in the fuel filter or lines, the injectors and fuel pressure. That range is important: a cylinder-one torque discrepancy is not itself proof of a defective injector, low compression or a damaged engine controller.

Preserve the operating data and check related faults before selecting tests. A professional can compare the speed-signal and fuel-delivery evidence using the correct engine procedure. The manual expressly treats fuel-pressure work as high-pressure service; loosening a line to see whether fuel arrives is not an appropriate check.

Confirmation should return to the warm, non-regenerating control conditions that enable the monitor. An engine that idles briefly after codes are cleared has not necessarily completed the 120-revolution torque comparison under eligible conditions.
