# P16C7: The heater-support pump is reported blocked

## A blocked classification is not a complete diagnosis

P16C7 is the V488 heater-support pump blocked entry in Volkswagen's CRUA/DEJA table. The output driver supplies the diagnostic indication, and the row qualifies it over three attempts lasting 7.5 seconds. The malfunction lamp is listed after two driving cycles.

The monitor description does not provide a minimum acceptable rotor speed or a winding-resistance specification. It should therefore not be converted into a generic pump test by borrowing values from a different component. The fault establishes a failed operating response, while the reason for that response still requires diagnosis.

## Confirm that the requested operation is electrically possible

The V488 checking sequence first establishes battery voltage at the pump connection and then examines its path to the engine controller. Wiring or connector problems remain part of the investigation even when the word “blocked” suggests a mechanical issue. The factory procedure reaches a component decision only after its preceding checks.

Record related dry-running, supply and driver codes and identify the exact pump on the installed engine. A technician can then distinguish the operating report from the circuit evidence using the proper diagram and service procedure. Do not try to free or energize the pump through an unidentified connector.

Following repair, a useful confirmation shows the pump meeting its commanded operation without another blocked report. A momentary sound from the pump or an erased warning does not establish that the monitored response is now correct.
