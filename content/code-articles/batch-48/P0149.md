# P0149: The CREC fuel-timing entry monitors injector boost voltage

## Read the manufacturer strategy behind the generic title

The Audi CREC table labels P0149 as a fuel-timing error, but describes its monitor as injection-valve supply voltage outside range. The low-voltage branches are around the thirty-volt boundary, and the high branch is above seventy-five volts. The table qualifies operation after the engine has run for at least 0.3 seconds and uses a 3.6-second monitoring period.

These are injector-driver boost-voltage values. They should not be compared directly with ordinary vehicle battery voltage or used as a general fuel-pressure specification. The code title alone does not justify a conclusion that mechanical valve timing is incorrect.

## Keep the diagnosis with the injector operating circuit

Audi directs the technician to its fuel-injector checking procedure. Preserve related injector, supply and engine-running faults so the electrical event can be interpreted in context. The correct control-module and injector information is essential before deciding which measurements are relevant.

The row prints both “below” and “at or below” thirty volts in adjacent low-voltage lines. This article avoids turning that duplicated boundary into a new precision test and retains a source-review flag for the wording. The broader supported distinction remains a driver supply outside its intended operating range.

Fuel-system and injector-driver work requires qualified service precautions. Do not apply external voltage to an injector or loosen a high-pressure connection to investigate this code. Confirmation should address the operating driver-voltage monitor, not just an erased timing-error label.
