# P30A2: Brake-pedal position disagrees with the brake-light switch

## The fault compares two descriptions of pedal state

Volkswagen's CGFA P30A2 row compares brake-pedal position sensor G100 with the brake-light switch. It lists the switch as inactive while pedal position is above 27 percent or below 2 percent. The observation lasts three seconds, with a two-driving-cycle lamp schedule.

The lower-position alternative is important to preserve exactly: the source does not explain why an inactive switch together with a value below 2 percent is implausible in this comparison. This article therefore retains a source-review flag and does not silently change the operator or present the row as a complete adjustment specification.

## Verify signals without bypassing a braking safeguard

The clear diagnostic subject is agreement between the pedal-position information and switch state. A technician should retain the original data, identify the installed G100 and switch arrangement, and consult the current factory procedure before interpreting the disputed branch.

The code alone does not establish that the switch requires adjustment, that the position sensor has failed or that the brake lights necessarily behave in a particular way. Those conclusions require separate observations and the correct component information.

Brake-related inconsistencies need professional assessment. Do not bridge the switch or alter pedal sensing to remove the warning. After the underlying cause and source interpretation are resolved, confirmation should show coherent pedal and switch information along with the braking system's prescribed functional checks, not merely cleared fault memory.
