# P16C6: Dry-running feedback is evaluated after electrical faults

## The enable conditions help order the investigation

For CRUA/DEJA engines, P16C6 records a dry-running report from heater-support pump V488. Volkswagen gives three attempts over six seconds and a two-driving-cycle lamp schedule. The fault information comes from the output driver's diagnostic signal.

The row also excludes the monitor when the listed electrical faults P16C1, P16C0 or P16BF are illuminating the malfunction lamp. Those codes concern an open circuit and shorts to positive or ground. Their presence is therefore useful context when deciding whether a dry-running observation can be interpreted cleanly.

## Distinguish the reported state from its cause

P16C6 does not publish a reservoir-level threshold or directly identify air, leakage or a defective pump. It reports how pump operation has been classified. The technician still needs the correct cooling-circuit information and the electrical checks described in the V488 procedure.

The referenced procedure establishes supply voltage and the controller connection before its component decision. Preserve all pump-related records before erasing memory, particularly the electrical exclusions named by this monitor. Repeatedly commanding a pump that reports dry operation is not a substitute for determining whether the circuit and coolant arrangement are correct.

Cooling-system inspection must be performed at safe temperature and pressure. Once the underlying condition is resolved, confirmation should allow the applicable dry-running monitor to operate without a report, with the disqualifying electrical faults also addressed.
