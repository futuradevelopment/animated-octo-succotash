# P13E1: A cylinder-two timing check with an unresolved sensor label

## Preserve the distinction between the heading and the copied detail

Volkswagen assigns P13E1 to the cylinder-two pressure-sensor circuit plausibility check. The CRUA/DEJA row describes pressure-derived top-dead-centre timing and points to QX2 together with crankshaft speed sensor G28. It specifies fuel-cut operation, coolant above 59.96°C and a forty-revolution observation period.

The numerical line, however, still labels the pressure input as sensor one. That conflicts with the cylinder-two heading and QX2 destination. This article remains marked for source review rather than treating a copied ±1.8-degree line as a fully verified cylinder-two workshop specification.

## Investigate the relationship, not just the component name

A pressure-timing plausibility result concerns the relationship between pressure feedback and the crankshaft reference. It cannot independently prove that QX2 is defective, that cylinder two has low compression or that the timing drive needs repair. The manual's inclusion of G28 makes the reference signal part of the investigation.

A technician should retain the complete code record and confirm the intended pressure channel with the current factory information for the installed engine. Related pressure and crankshaft faults can help establish which signal needs attention first.

Do not apply cylinder-one wiring details to QX2 because of the inconsistent line. Equally, clearing P13E1 while the vehicle remains stationary does not verify its warm fuel-cut comparison. Repair confirmation must address both the correct channel identification and the operating event that enables the monitor.
