# P029D: cylinder-1 injector leakage inferred from two failures

P029D detects a mechanical open condition of high-pressure injector 1 in the cited table. Its evidence combines cylinder-1 misfire activity with an existing minimum rail-pressure-control error.

## Neither condition alone is the whole monitor

The documented misfire counter must exceed 100, and the rail-pressure-control minimum error must be set. The test requires engine speed above 1,520 rpm and below 6,000 rpm, relative air charge below 100 percent, and no electrical high-pressure-injector failure. Statistical inhibition must also be inactive.

The table lists a five-second interval and two-trip reporting. These conditions explain why an isolated cylinder-1 misfire code or an isolated low-pressure observation is not equivalent to a completed P029D diagnosis.

## Mechanical interpretation with limits

The absence of an electrical injector fault is an enable condition, not proof that every aspect of the injector circuit is perfect. It allows this monitor to interpret the combined combustion and pressure evidence as a mechanical open/leak pattern.

Verification should address both the cylinder-specific misfire behavior and the pressure-control condition in an eligible operating window. A qualified technician should use the applicable high-pressure-fuel procedure to establish the physical cause. The table does not locate an external fuel leak or provide permission to loosen a pressurized connection.
