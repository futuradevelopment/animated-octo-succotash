# P00A9: bank-2 intake-temperature signal disagrees with its filter

P00A9 monitors an electrically implausible bank-2 manifold intake-temperature signal by comparing the local voltage with a filtered version. The table’s sensor-4 designation must be matched to the vehicle before applying the catalog’s sensor-2 label.

## More than an instantaneous jump

The absolute difference between local and filtered voltage must exceed 120 millivolts for at least 20 seconds in the documented logic. Ignition must be on, and the minimum and maximum signal-range errors must not already be debounced as active.

This means the intermittent/erratic label does not describe every brief fluctuation. It identifies a discrepancy sustained under the monitor’s timing and filtering rules. The range-error prerequisites also separate this test from the neighboring low- and high-voltage diagnostics.

## Capturing the useful information

A single temperature number is less informative than the voltage behavior over time and the status of the range checks. A signal that has returned to a stable level by the time it is viewed may no longer display the discrepancy that qualified earlier.

Two-trip reporting and additional inhibition conditions are listed. Verification should establish that the local/filtered comparison completes without excessive discrepancy while the range checks permit it to run. The source provides no frequency-of-failure estimate and does not prove that the sensor, connector, or controller must be replaced.
