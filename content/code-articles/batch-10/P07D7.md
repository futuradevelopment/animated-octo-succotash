# P07D7: eighth gear cannot be confirmed released

P07D7 identifies eighth gear stuck during repeated disengagement attempts in this transmission monitor. The controller expects movement toward neutral and checks whether the disengagement sequence can progress.

## Position faults can contribute

The cited row names shift-fork position sensor 5. An electrical or consistency fault on that channel during actuation can cause an attempt to fail. An alternative path is failure to satisfy the next disengagement-state transition before its calibrated timeout.

Eighth-gear disengagement must be requested and active, pressure must exceed 4.5 bar, and error or bypass application states must be absent. The fault confirms after at least four unsuccessful disengagement attempts.

## Release performance is the diagnostic subject

Successful engagement of eighth gear does not prove it can later disengage correctly. The stored result specifically concerns release, and a position-sensor problem can prevent reliable confirmation even when the mechanical situation has not been independently established.

Retain the requested neutral target, sensor-5 status, timeout state, and attempt history for the applicable professional diagnosis. Verification should demonstrate a correctly confirmed release through the proper service sequence. The table delegates exact state timeouts to attachments and does not justify inventing a universal delay, forcing more shifts, or selecting a gearbox component for replacement from this code alone.
