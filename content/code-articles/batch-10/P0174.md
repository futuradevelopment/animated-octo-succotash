# P0174: bank-2 lean result from lambda-controller correction

P0174 is a bank-2 lean-system diagnostic based here on the fast lambda controller’s mean correction. The table monitors positive deviation from a reference value of 1.0 rather than naming a particular air leak or fuel-system component.

## What exceeds the limit

The documented maximum deviation is above 0.23 for ten seconds. Upstream bank-2 closed-loop lambda control must be active and unrestricted under the listed conditions. Fuel-cutoff recovery and acceleration/deceleration protection states are part of the enable logic.

The 0.23 value belongs to this controller’s normalized correction quantity. It should not automatically be relabeled as an identical percentage on every scan tool’s short-term or long-term fuel-trim display.

## Using the record

Determine whether the recorded correction was produced during eligible closed-loop operation. A lean-looking value during fuel cutoff does not reproduce a monitor that excludes that state. Likewise, a later normal oxygen reading may coexist with substantial corrective action from the controller.

The source lists two-trip reporting. Verification should establish that bank-2 correction remains acceptable when the documented monitor runs, including its control-state prerequisites. The code supports a lean-system interpretation but does not identify the physical cause, rank likely failures, or establish a replacement decision without further vehicle-specific diagnosis.
