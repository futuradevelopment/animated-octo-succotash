# P02A1: cylinder-2 injector leakage evidence

P02A1 identifies a mechanical open/leak pattern for high-pressure injector 2 in the cited monitor. The controller combines a cylinder-specific combustion problem with a pressure-control problem instead of directly measuring flow through the injector.

## The combined trigger

Cylinder-2 misfire count must exceed 100 while the minimum rail-pressure-control error is set. The row gives a five-second interval. It requires engine speed between 1,520 and 6,000 rpm, relative air charge below 100 percent, no electrical injector failure, and no statistical inhibition.

Those requirements distinguish this mechanical assessment from the adjacent P0202 electrical-circuit checks. Passing an electrical continuity test would not, by itself, disprove a mechanically open injector; equally, an active electrical fault can prevent this mechanical monitor from being eligible.

## Evidence for confirmation

Retain cylinder-2 misfire data together with the pressure-control diagnostic state. Looking at either one alone loses the combination the controller uses. A smooth idle after the event is also outside the printed speed window and therefore does not recreate this monitor.

Two-trip reporting is specified. Verification should confirm that the combined failure pattern is absent when the appropriate monitor completes. A qualified technician must establish the physical cause using the correct high-pressure-fuel procedure; the code is not a direct leak-location measurement or permission to disconnect a pressurized component.
