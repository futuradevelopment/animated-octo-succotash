# P0175: bank-2 rich result at the lower correction limit

P0175 monitors the lower rationality limit of bank-2 fast lambda-control correction. The cited table compares the controller’s mean deviation from 1.0, corrected with its proportional component, against a negative threshold.

## A sustained control result

Failure occurs below −0.23 for ten seconds in the documented calibration. This is the lower-limit counterpart to the positive deviation used by the lean-system monitor, but the source specifically includes the proportional correction in its description.

Unrestricted bank-2 upstream closed-loop operation is required. The enable logic considers fuel cutoff, restoration of lambda control, and acceleration or deceleration protection. A transient value generated in an excluded mode should not be equated with a completed rich-system diagnostic.

## What the code can establish

The monitor tells you that the required correction exceeded its allowed lower boundary. It does not independently measure a leaking injector or identify excess pressure. Those are possible diagnostic subjects only if supported by further evidence and the correct service procedure.

Two-trip reporting is documented. For verification, retain the relevant correction data and confirm that the lower-limit monitor completed in eligible closed-loop operation. Clearing adaptation or fault memory alone would remove useful history without demonstrating that the underlying rich correction demand has been resolved.
