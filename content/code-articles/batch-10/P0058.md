# P0058: heater driver stays high when commanded on

P0058 is the circuit-high check for the bank-2 sensor-2 oxygen-sensor heater low-side driver. Its failure signature is high voltage while the driver is on, which the table interprets as a short to power.

## Commanded state separates the high and low tests

The source describes a power-path impedance of 0.5 ohm or less and a 0.1-second detection interval. Unlike the open and grounded branches, which examine the off state, this branch judges voltage during the on state. A voltage observation without the matching command state cannot establish whether it reproduces the failure.

The general power-stage diagnostic must be enabled. The continuation requires released heating and includes completed engine start, post-drive exclusion, and voltage and heat/dew-point conditions. These conditions govern whether the electrical test can meaningfully run.

## Confirmation after diagnosis

A downstream oxygen signal that responds normally does not independently prove the heater driver is electrically sound. The heater circuit and the sensing response are separate diagnostic subjects. Use the vehicle’s correct circuit procedure to determine the reason for the high-on-state feedback.

The row specifies two-trip reporting. Verification should include the circuit-high monitor completing successfully under its enabled heater conditions. The published short-to-power classification supports investigation of that electrical relationship, but it does not identify a specific wire, connector, sensor, or ECU as the failed item.
