# P0023: open-circuit monitoring of the bank-2 B cam actuator

P0023 checks the bank-2 B camshaft-position actuator’s low-side driver for an open circuit in the cited table. This is an electrical driver diagnosis, distinct from determining whether a commanded cam movement was fast enough.

## Driver state explains the measurement

The controller looks for low voltage while the driver is off, identifying an open-circuit condition. The source describes the open path as at least 200 kilohms between the ECU pin and load, with a one-second detection interval. That impedance is the monitor’s circuit classification, not an instruction to measure resistance on an energized system.

Ignition must be on, the ECU must be in drive state, engine speed must be at least 80 rpm, and battery voltage must fall between the documented 10.9- and 25.5-volt limits. Additional enable and inhibit conditions apply.

## Diagnostic consequence

Because the voltage judgment depends on the driver being off, a voltage reading taken during active drive cannot be substituted without understanding the circuit. Likewise, the code does not establish a camshaft timing error; it concerns the path that drives the actuator.

Two-trip reporting is specified. Verification should confirm that the driver’s open-circuit diagnostic runs and passes in the required state. The source supports investigation of the electrical path but does not select the actuator, wiring, or control module as the failed part.
