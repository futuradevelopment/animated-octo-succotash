# P0157: bank-2 downstream low signal and load-pulse evidence

P0157 checks the bank-2 downstream oxygen-sensor signal for a short-to-ground signature. This implementation combines a low unloaded output with the signal’s response to three load pulses.

## Suspicion is only the first stage

The controller first requires output below 0.058 volt while catalyst purge and deceleration fuel cutoff are inactive and battery voltage exceeds 10.7 volts. That suspicion condition must persist for at least three seconds.

It then evaluates the mean loaded-to-unloaded voltage difference for three pulses. A difference below 0.014648 volt for the documented five-second interval supports the ground-short result. The table’s separate zero-second time column does not eliminate these explicit qualification periods.

## Why this is more than a lean reading

Fuel cutoff can naturally alter exhaust oxygen, which is why it is excluded from the suspicion conditions. The subsequent load-pulse test adds electrical evidence beyond simply seeing a low sensor value. A low-voltage snapshot outside the enabled state is therefore not the complete P0157 test.

The source lists two-trip reporting and additional common prerequisites. Verification should show the relevant signal-range diagnostic passing after suspicion and load-testing conditions are available. These internally applied pulses are not a recommendation to connect an external load to an unidentified sensor circuit.
