# P014A: rich-to-lean delay at bank-2 oxygen sensor 2

P014A monitors rich-to-lean delay of the bank-2 downstream oxygen sensor. The source explicitly calls this a delay-response check, so its result should not be merged with the separate transition-response test associated with P013C.

## The calibrated timing quantity

The table specifies an EWMA-filtered delay above 0.75 second and an exhaust-flow-dependent correction ranging from 0.04 to 0.08 second. It lists two counts/EWMA with one-trip reporting. The raw delay, its flow correction, and the filtered value are related but different quantities.

Commanded bank-2 primary lambda must equal 1. Enable conditions also cover a running engine, vehicle and engine speed, load, integrated airflow, coolant temperature, and a period without a gear change. The test is intended for a defined operating window rather than every observed rich-to-lean voltage movement.

## What to compare

Retain the monitor’s delay result with the operating conditions that made it valid. An apparently quick full voltage transition does not necessarily resolve a delay before that transition begins, and the inverse observation may also be incomplete.

Verification should establish that the particular delay monitor completed and passed under its allowed conditions. The source defines a detection threshold but does not prove a failed sensing element or authorize applying the same timing limit to another bank, sensor position, or calibration.
