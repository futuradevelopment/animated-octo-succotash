# P014B: lean-to-rich delay after the flow correction

P014B evaluates delayed lean-to-rich response from bank-2 oxygen sensor 2 in the cited calibration. Its arithmetic-filtered result accounts for exhaust-flow correction before comparison with the failure threshold.

## Reading the two-page row

The first page gives a delay threshold above 0.75 second and shows the filtered-update calculation. The continuation supplies the exhaust-mass-flow correction range of zero to 0.04 second. It also shows engine-speed, flow, temperature, and gear-change constraints. The table lists two counts/EWMA and one-trip reporting.

The correction range is different from that documented for the rich-to-lean delay code P014A. Direction is therefore part of the calibration, not merely a descriptive word that can be ignored when comparing results.

## Diagnostic use

A raw lean-to-rich time should be distinguished from the corrected, accumulated timing result. A single clean transition after a fault does not necessarily reproduce the event history or qualified operating window used by the monitor.

Useful verification follows the applicable bank-2 downstream delay test and confirms that it ran with primary commanded lambda at 1 and its other prerequisites satisfied. It should also establish that the result being viewed belongs to the lean-to-rich delay diagnostic rather than the separate transition-response diagnostic. This table supplies no universal replacement rule or physical fault location.
