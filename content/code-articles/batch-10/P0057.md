# P0057: low-side heater circuit grounded on bank 2 sensor 2

P0057 checks for a circuit-low condition in the bank-2 sensor-2 oxygen-sensor heater driver. The documented electrical interpretation is a short to ground while the low-side driver is off.

## Low voltage alone is incomplete evidence

The table describes a ground-path impedance of 0.5 ohm or less and a 0.1-second detection interval. The off-state qualifier is essential: low voltage must be interpreted in the context of what the controller was commanding, rather than treated as a fault in every operating state.

This row also requires the general power-stage enable and released sensor heating. The enable sequence includes engine start completion, exclusion of post-drive operation, and voltage and thermal conditions. Dew-point and integrated-heat branches extend onto the next page.

## What distinguishes this code

P0056 is the adjacent open-circuit check for the same heater, while P0057 describes the low/grounded circuit branch. Shared sensor identity does not make their electrical interpretations interchangeable. Determine which diagnostic result is present before choosing the applicable circuit test.

Two-trip reporting is documented. Verification should demonstrate a passing circuit-low diagnostic after heater and power-stage release, not merely a cleared memory or a changing oxygen reading. The low-resistance description explains the monitor classification and is not permission to resistance-test a powered circuit or an unidentified pin.
