# P0333: knock sensor B high-circuit sampling

P0333 monitors a high filtered output from knock sensor 2, identified by the catalog as sensor B. The source interprets the condition as a short-to-battery pattern, not a command to change fuel grade or ignition timing.

## The test is periodic

Filtered output must exceed 4.7 volts while engine speed exceeds 500 rpm. Three events are required, with two-trip reporting. The diagnostic runs every 15 to 120 seconds according to engine speed, and the source specifies a 0.5 low-pass filter gain.

That schedule matters when assessing an intermittent complaint. A normal display between scheduled checks does not prove what the controller observed during the qualifying events, and three events cannot be assumed to occupy only three seconds.

## Distinguish neighboring diagnostics

The same page separately lists the sensor-2 low-output and open-load checks. The open-load assessment has different speed, load, duty-cycle, and event requirements. Passing one branch does not automatically verify all three electrical classifications.

Confirmation should establish that the high-circuit branch completed with acceptable filtered output and correct sensor identification. The source explains the electrical evidence but does not identify a wire, connector, or sensor as the proven cause, and its thresholds are not a substitute for the applicable circuit-testing procedure.
