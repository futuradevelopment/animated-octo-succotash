# P07E6: park-lock release retries and unintended engagement

P07E6 is a stuck-in-park diagnosis with two paths in the cited transmission table. One follows unsuccessful release attempts; the other watches for an unintended engagement indication after the lock was already open.

## Release must first be possible

The disengagement branch requires a release state, no request to engage Park, sufficient hydraulic availability, and system pressure of at least 15 bar. Electrical or consistency faults in the park-position sensor must be absent. The controller compares position with adapted hold/open references and counts release timeouts; three retries confirm the fault.

The second branch operates from the open state. An unintended engagement indication persisting for 250 milliseconds increments an open-fault counter, with three events required. These are different histories behind the same code.

## Verification needs the correct history

A lock that never completed release and one that appeared to re-engage after opening require different evidence. Preserve the park state, request, adapted position information, and counter type before interpreting the fault.

Verification must use a qualified transmission procedure that controls vehicle movement and confirms the relevant position/state transition. The source does not provide a manual-release instruction or establish that the parking lock is mechanically broken. Its timing and retry counts describe diagnostic behavior, not directions to repeatedly command or defeat the lock.
