# P00A6: bank-2 intake temperature plausibility and stuck checks

P00A6 combines cold-start cross-checking and a stuck-signal test for the bank-2 manifold intake-temperature sensor in this table. The source calls it sensor value 4, while the catalog uses intake air temperature sensor 2; those naming systems require vehicle-specific reconciliation.

## Two different ways a signal can be implausible

The cold-start branches compare the sensor’s temperature relationship with an average-temperature delta, using a 14.96°C error threshold. A separate stuck check evaluates the difference between maximum and minimum sensor temperature, with a threshold below 4.96°C and a lookup-table reference.

A reading can therefore be within its physical temperature range yet fail comparison with other temperatures. It can also look reasonable at one instant but vary too little during the stuck-check observation. Neither condition is equivalent to a simple open-circuit voltage limit.

## What to preserve for diagnosis

Record whether the failure belongs to cold-start comparison or insufficient variation. A later warm-engine temperature snapshot does not reproduce the cold-start relationship, and two readings taken too close together do not establish completion of the stuck test.

The source lists ignition-on and inhibition requirements and two-trip reporting, but does not provide a universal observation duration on this page. Verification should use the applicable completed plausibility test and its lookup conditions, rather than inventing a temperature-change target for every drive.
