# P0056: bank-2 downstream heater circuit continuity

P0056 is an open-circuit diagnostic for the bank-2 sensor-2 oxygen-sensor heater low-side driver. The controller evaluates electrical continuity under a defined driver state, not simply whether the downstream sensor became warm.

## The failure and its prerequisites

Low voltage during the driver’s off state is interpreted as an open circuit. The table gives an open-path classification of at least 200 kilohms between ECU pin and load and a 0.1-second detection interval. Two-trip reporting is listed.

The general power-stage diagnosis must be enabled. Engine-speed and supply-voltage conditions apply, and heater operation must be released. The continuation includes completed engine start, exclusion of post-drive state, and dew-point/heat-release logic with alternative enabling paths.

## Avoid confusing “not tested” with “passed”

An unheated sensor immediately after starting may reflect heater-release conditions rather than a failed continuity test. The detailed heat and dew-point branches explain why a convenient cold-idle observation may not recreate the original diagnostic opportunity.

A successful repair confirmation should establish that the open-circuit monitor was actually enabled and passed. The heater’s electrical result should also be distinguished from downstream oxygen-sensor response tests. This source identifies the failed check and its timing; it does not determine whether wiring, the heater load, or the driver is the physical cause on a specific vehicle.
