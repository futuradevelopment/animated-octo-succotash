# P0158: bank-2 downstream high signal with readiness checks

P0158 is a bank-2 downstream oxygen-sensor high-signal diagnostic. The source interprets its signal-range branch as a short to battery and requires sensor-readiness evidence before judging the voltage.

## The high criterion is conditional

The listed output threshold is above 1.201172 volts with setpoint lambda above 0.995. Detection time is 0.2 second, and reporting is two-trip. Battery voltage must exceed 10.7 volts, and the internal-resistance information must be valid after more than ten measurements.

The table also excludes a detected oxygen-sensor open circuit. Its continuation details expected readiness, protective-heating completion, and dew-point-related conditions. Thus a high value seen before the readiness logic qualifies cannot automatically be treated as the same completed diagnostic.

## A useful interpretation

The internal-resistance prerequisite shows that this is not an isolated voltage comparator. The controller first needs enough evidence that its sensor assessment is meaningful. It is also inappropriate to use the high threshold as a rich-mixture target; the row classifies it as electrical fault evidence.

Verification should confirm both valid readiness information and a passing high-signal monitor under the applicable service procedure. A later voltage below the threshold is helpful but does not prove that the diagnostic’s required measurements and enable sequence have completed.
