# C003F: wheel rotation directions disagree

C003F is a wheel-speed-sensor direction correlation fault in this brake-controller table. The monitor compares reported directions rather than simply asking whether each wheel produces a speed signal.

## What qualifies the disagreement

The monitored wheel’s direction must differ from at least two other wheels. The table requires ignition on, vehicle speed above 3.13 mph, and direction information available from at least three wheel-speed sensors. The disagreement is monitored continuously with a 20-second qualification time and Type B, two-trip reporting.

Those details matter when interpreting an intermittent record. A comparison cannot be evaluated as described when too few direction channels are available. Also, a speed value that appears reasonable does not prove its accompanying direction information is correct.

## Identifying the relevant evidence

Review the direction data and availability for all participating wheels at the time of the event. The code names a correlation failure; it does not, by itself, select a particular corner or establish a mechanical failure. A wheel that disagrees with the others is the subject of the monitor, but the reason for that disagreement still requires the applicable service checks.

Verification must cover valid direction reporting and a completed comparison above the enable speed for the necessary interval. Because this concerns brake-control inputs, reproduction and testing should follow a suitable professional procedure rather than an improvised road maneuver.

## Documentation and applicability

These details describe the monitor in GM’s 2025 25OBDG07A Part2 file. Its application-matrix filename does not match the download index, so model and engine applicability remain unresolved. Use the vehicle’s correct service information before applying these calibration details.
