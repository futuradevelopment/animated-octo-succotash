# P0638: bank-1 throttle duty-cycle demand exceeds its limit

P0638 checks the magnitude of bank-1 throttle-actuator duty cycle in this calibration. The failure threshold changes with measured battery voltage, so it is not simply a fixed throttle-position error.

## Voltage compensation changes the boundary

The threshold is the smaller of 95 percent and 80 percent multiplied by 13.5 volts divided by measured battery voltage. The table gives a 0.6001-second interval and one-trip reporting. For illustration, at 13.5 volts the formula yields an 80-percent boundary; that arithmetic is an explanation of the published formula, not a vehicle test result.

The power stage must be on, actual position valid, adaptation release inactive, and the controller in drive or post-drive state. The desired-position gradient is also constrained, separating this check from unrestricted rapid command changes.

## What a high duty result means

The monitor reports that control effort exceeded its calibrated range in an eligible state. It does not directly establish why that effort was required or prove the actuator is mechanically stuck.

Verification should compare duty magnitude with the voltage-adjusted threshold while confirming the enable conditions and monitor completion. A normal throttle angle alone can coexist with excessive control effort. Use the vehicle’s procedure to establish the cause before selecting a throttle-body or electrical repair.
