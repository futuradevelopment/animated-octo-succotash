# P0050: bank-2 upstream oxygen-sensor heater open circuit

P0050 identifies an open-circuit check of the bank-2 sensor-1 UEGO heater power stage in this table. It concerns heater control rather than the oxygen sensor’s measured air-fuel ratio.

## A state-dependent electrical check

The monitor interprets low voltage during the driver’s off state as an open load. The source classifies the open circuit with at least 200 kilohms between the ECU pin and load and lists a 0.5-second detection interval.

The heater power-stage diagnosis must be released, and a separate diagnosis-report release condition for bank 2 sensor 1 must be satisfied. Supply voltage is constrained by the documented 10.7- to 16.1-volt window and timing conditions. The table also references common enables and inhibiting faults.

## Why a warm sensor is not enough

A believable oxygen reading does not prove that this heater circuit passed its electrical check. Conversely, a heater fault is not direct evidence that the sensing cell is reporting the wrong mixture. Keep the heater-driver result separate from fuel-control or sensor-response observations.

The row specifies two-trip reporting. After the appropriate electrical diagnosis, verification should show completion of the bank-2 sensor-1 heater power-stage monitor while its release conditions are met. Measuring an unidentified sensor connector or replacing a sensor from the code alone is not justified by this summary.
