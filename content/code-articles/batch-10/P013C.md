# P013C: bank-2 downstream rich-to-lean transition time

P013C evaluates how slowly bank-2 oxygen sensor 2 transitions from rich to lean in this calibration. The test uses a corrected, filtered response time rather than simply timing any visible voltage change.

## Correction precedes the decision

The table subtracts an exhaust-mass-flow-dependent correction from the raw transition time before updating its filtered result. The listed correction range is 0.04 to 0.10 second, and the failure threshold for the resulting value is above 0.75 second. The row specifies two counts/EWMA and one-trip reporting.

Commanded primary air-fuel lambda must be 1, the engine must run, and vehicle and engine-speed conditions apply. The continuation adds flow stability and other operating-window requirements. A random throttle movement or an unqualified voltage transition is not the complete diagnostic test.

## What a comparison should preserve

Separate raw transition time from the corrected monitor result. Different exhaust flow can change the correction, so comparing two raw times without the associated flow may misrepresent whether the monitor should pass.

This is the rich-to-lean direction only. A prompt response in the opposite direction does not establish this result. Verification needs a completed eligible rich-to-lean monitor event; the table does not determine whether a slow result originates in the sensing element or some other condition affecting the observed transition.
