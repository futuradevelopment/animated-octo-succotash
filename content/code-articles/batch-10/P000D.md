# P000D: bank-2 exhaust camshaft response monitoring

P000D identifies slow response of the bank-2 exhaust camshaft in this documented monitor. It evaluates whether an enabled position adjustment reaches its target, not whether the exhaust cam is always advanced or always retarded.

## The particular failure pattern

The table combines a target not reached within more than one second with an angle criterion above three crankshaft degrees. It counts at least four events. Its enable logic names the bank-2 outlet-camshaft governor operating in closed loop, along with ignition, speed, oil-temperature, voltage, and diagnostic prerequisites.

Bank selection is consequential: the related P000B entry names bank 1, while this row explicitly names bank 2. A normal response observed on the opposite bank does not verify the monitored camshaft here, although it may provide useful comparison data under the vehicle’s procedure.

## From code to evidence

Retain the requested and observed angles through the adjustment interval. An eventual arrival at the target can coexist with a slow-response fault, because elapsed response time is part of the decision. A snapshot taken only after the movement finishes loses that distinction.

The source assigns two-trip reporting. Verification should show the bank-2 exhaust control completing eligible movements within its calibrated response criteria. The table does not determine the physical cause or provide a universal repair; its separate enable and inhibition references must be applied to the correct vehicle.
