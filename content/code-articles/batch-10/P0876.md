# P0876: limited-slip differential pressure-sensor consistency

P0876 checks limited-slip differential pressure-sensor consistency in this transmission implementation. That is the source’s explicit component mapping for the catalog’s pressure-sensor D range/performance label.

## Valve checks come before sensor judgment

The recovery routine first verifies the pressure-control valve and redundant shutdown valve independently of the pressure sensor. Once they are considered operational, it commands small and large pressure steps: 4 bar and 6 bar in the table.

A measured-to-target difference above 1 bar at either step qualifies with a 100-millisecond confirmation interval. The routine must be requested and eligible to run, and relevant drift or valve-stuck faults must not already have been detected during the diagnostic instance.

## Why an isolated pressure error is insufficient

The strategy intentionally establishes actuator behavior before attributing the failed pressure response to the sensor. Skipping those checks would remove the evidence that supports the sensor-specific conclusion. A pressure disagreement during an ordinary maneuver does not reproduce the controlled step test.

Verification should show a completed recovery routine with acceptable response to both steps and valid prerequisite results. Some enable labels retain unrelated “odd clutch” wording, so the correct service procedure remains essential. This monitor does not authorize manual pressure application, forced valve operation, or replacement of the differential assembly solely from the code.
