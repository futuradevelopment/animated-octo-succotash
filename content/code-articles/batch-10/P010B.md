# P010B: bank-2 airflow versus a calculated flow envelope

P010B checks bank-2 measured mass airflow against modeled limits in the cited calibration. It is a plausibility comparison, so a sensor can communicate valid data yet disagree with the engine’s modeled air path.

## Upper and lower branches

The upper branch compares measured flow with maximum modeled throttle-body flow divided by a tolerance factor of approximately 0.92. The lower branch compares it with minimum modeled flow using a factor of approximately 1.08. Both pages list ten-second qualification and one-trip reporting.

These are calculated boundaries, not fixed grams-per-second limits. A measured value that is acceptable at one operating point may be implausible at another because the modeled flow changes.

## Model validity matters

The engine must rotate forwards, the measured airflow signal must not be invalid, and the relevant compressor-to-throttle flow and throttle-flow calculations must be valid. Those prerequisites prevent the modeled comparison from being interpreted independently of its inputs.

For diagnosis, distinguish an upper-envelope failure from a lower-envelope failure and retain the modeled quantities alongside measured bank-2 airflow. The two pages contain inconsistent variable lettering in their formula annotations, so their physical maximum/minimum descriptions are more reliable than treating the letters as a complete calculation specification. Verification should show the appropriate plausibility branch completing successfully with valid inputs; the code alone does not prove a failed airflow sensor.
