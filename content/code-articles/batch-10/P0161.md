# P0161: downstream bank-2 heater performance inferred from resistance

P0161 checks bank-2 secondary oxygen-sensor heater performance using the sensor’s internal resistance. The documented threshold is calibration dependent, spanning 500 to 10,000 ohms through a referenced lookup table, with a six-second failure interval.

## Resistance needs a qualified measurement

The enable logic includes startup history, battery voltage, engine speed, modeled exhaust temperature, and validity of the internal-resistance measurement. The continuation requires more than ten resistance calculations and specifies signal-stability conditions. Heater control must have been on for at least 30 seconds in the shown branch.

These requirements mean an arbitrary resistance reading taken from a cold or disconnected sensor is not the same quantity as the controller’s qualified hot-sensor estimate. The 500-to-10,000-ohm span also cannot be collapsed into one universal pass/fail value.

## Separating performance from continuity

The table describes its calibrated threshold as the component-vendor requirement for heater performance at high mileage. It is a performance assessment, distinct from the separate heater-driver open or short-circuit checks.

Two-trip reporting is listed. Verification should establish valid resistance measurement and completion of the heater-performance test under the applicable thermal conditions. The source supports interpreting an inadequate heating result, but does not prove whether the cause is the heater element, its electrical supply, or another factor considered by the complete service procedure.
