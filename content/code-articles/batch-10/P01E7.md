# P01E7: coolant sensor 3 intermittent-connection monitoring

P01E7 is the intermittent-circuit monitor for coolant-temperature sensor 3 in the cited GM documentation. Its specific evidence is an accumulated loss-connection error, not merely an unusual temperature value.

## Qualification and eligibility

The table gives a loss-connection counter threshold of at least five seconds and separately lists 0.1 second in its detection-time column. These should not be reduced to a claim that any one-tenth-second interruption sets the code; the accumulated counter criterion remains part of the test.

Ignition must be on, the auxiliary coolant-temperature signal must be available, and the maximum signal-range error and maximum-healing states must be false. These qualifiers distinguish an intermittent assessment from a sustained high-range failure or its recovery state.

## What data helps

Retain signal availability and range-diagnostic status alongside the temperature history. A smooth value shown after reconnection does not establish that no interruption was counted earlier. Likewise, an unavailable signal cannot be assumed to satisfy the monitor’s availability prerequisite.

The source assigns two-trip reporting and common enable requirements. Verification should show the correct sensor-3 channel remains available and passes the intermittent monitor under those conditions. The table does not locate a loose connector or prove a failed sensor; it describes the connection-loss evidence that further vehicle-specific diagnosis must explain.

## Documentation and applicability

These details describe the monitor in GM’s 2025 25OBDG07A Part2 file. Its application-matrix filename does not match the download index, so model and engine applicability remain unresolved. Use the vehicle’s correct service information before applying these calibration details.
