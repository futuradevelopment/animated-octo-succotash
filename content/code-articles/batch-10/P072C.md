# P072C: first gear fails repeated disengagement attempts

P072C means first gear was diagnosed stuck during disengagement in the cited transmission monitor. The controller judges attempts to move the synchronizer toward neutral, rather than treating any commanded first-gear operation as a fault.

## Position evidence or a state timeout

A disengagement attempt can fail when shift-fork position sensor 1 develops an electrical or consistency fault during actuation toward neutral. Another route is failure to meet a state-transition condition before the calibrated disengagement timeout. The actual state-specific timeout is delegated to the source’s attachment tables.

The first-gear disengagement request and active state must be present, system pressure must exceed 4.5 bar, and the application must not be in error or bypass state. Fault confirmation requires at least four failed disengagement attempts.

## What “stuck” does and does not prove

A position-sensor fault can contribute to this result, so the label does not independently prove that the gear mechanism is physically jammed. Identify whether the failed attempts involved position validity or a timed-out state transition.

Verification should demonstrate successful eligible first-gear disengagement and trustworthy position information using the correct transmission procedure. The four-attempt rule describes the controller’s decision, not an instruction to force repeated shifts or disassemble the gearbox without further diagnosis.
