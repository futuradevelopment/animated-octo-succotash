# P0306: cylinder-6 misfire needs valid timing information

P0306 reports misfire attributed to cylinder 6 in the cited diagnostic. The controller’s count comparisons depend on valid calculated engine-position segment time, so the quality of the underlying timing information is part of monitor eligibility.

## Do not read a count without its frame

The first emissions path uses the initial 1,000 crankshaft revolutions after startup. It lists an engine-total misfire threshold above 120 and a cylinder-6 comparison using ten percent of that total. The subsequent emissions assessment also uses 1,000-revolution frames and a four-frame counter.

The shown enable logic excludes overrun/fuel cutoff and zero-torque detection and requires the startup combustion delay to have completed. Counts observed while those conditions are not satisfied do not demonstrate the same qualified assessment.

## What confirmation should establish

Preserve whether the event occurred during startup or later operation, together with the validity of timing data. A smooth engine impression alone does not establish that enough qualified revolutions have elapsed for the original path to complete.

The continuation introduces a separate weighted catalyst-damage check, while the main row lists two-trip reporting. Verification should confirm the relevant completed monitor result and cylinder-6 behavior. This is combustion attribution, not direct proof that the sixth coil, injector, or another named part requires replacement.
