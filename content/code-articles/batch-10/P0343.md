# P0343: bank-1 intake cam signal stops at a high level

P0343 detects loss of new signal edges from the bank-1 intake camshaft sensor while its signal remains high. The documented monitor uses crankshaft rotation to decide that expected camshaft information is missing.

## High level alone is not the complete fault

The table requires at least four crankshaft revolutions and a permanently high signal at transition into the no-signal state. Ignition must be on, a crankshaft signal with its gap must be detected, and reverse engine rotation must not be detected.

A high logic level can occur normally within a changing digital waveform. What makes this condition diagnostic is the absence of expected new edges over the qualified rotation interval. The four-revolution criterion also is not a fixed number of seconds.

## What to verify

Preserve crankshaft synchronization and camshaft-edge information together. If the crankshaft reference is not valid, an observation of a static cam signal does not reproduce the listed prerequisites. If edges return later, their presence alone does not erase the earlier missing-edge interval.

The source lists one-trip reporting plus common enables and inhibitors. Verification should show the bank-1 intake signal producing the required edges while the reference and rotation conditions are valid. The code does not by itself establish mechanical timing displacement or prove a defective sensor.
