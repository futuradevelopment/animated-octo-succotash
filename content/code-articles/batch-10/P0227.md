# P0227: bank-2 throttle sensor 1 voltage below range

P0227 checks the raw voltage of throttle-position sensor 1 on bank 2 in the cited monitor. Although the catalog uses the general throttle/pedal sensor C wording, the source explicitly identifies the throttle channel.

## A direct range check

The printed low threshold is below 0.195 volt, with a 0.14-second detection interval and one-trip reporting. The ECU may be in drive or post-drive state. The remaining enable logic involves throttle operating supply, safety fuel-cutoff requests, limp-home state, and inhibiting faults.

The post-drive alternative is useful context: this electrical check is not limited to an ordinary engine-running throttle movement. At the same time, a low reading captured in an unrelated power state does not automatically reproduce the listed diagnostic.

## Separate range from correlation

This code concerns one channel being below its raw electrical range. The neighboring P0226 monitor compares throttle and air-charge evidence for plausibility. A channel can return inside its voltage range without proving that the redundant position information agrees correctly.

Verification should establish that the bank-2 sensor-1 range test has completed, and then apply any relevant related checks from the proper service procedure. The threshold does not identify a physical connector pin or prove that the throttle actuator itself is defective; channel mapping and circuit diagnosis remain necessary.
