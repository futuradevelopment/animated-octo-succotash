# P0155: bank-2 upstream heater temperature performance

P0155 includes a startup heater-performance check based on the ceramic temperature of the bank-2 upstream oxygen sensor. The cited start-diagnosis branch fails when the ceramic remains below 735°C over the listed 70-second period.

## Heating release starts the meaningful test

Engine start must be finished and the bank-2 sensor-1 dew-point condition must permit heating. Supply voltage and coolant-start conditions also apply. The test is therefore not simply a timer that always starts at the instant the ignition is switched on.

The row further describes aborting and deactivating the start check for the remainder of the drive cycle if certain operating conditions are not satisfied for an accumulated interval. An incomplete check after an interruption must be distinguished from a successful heating result.

## Diagnosis and verification

This thermal-performance evidence differs from an open-circuit heater-driver code. It reports inadequate achieved temperature during the eligible test, without proving the physical cause. A heater can require separate electrical and performance assessments under the vehicle’s procedure.

Two-trip reporting is listed. Verification should show completion of the startup heating check after its release conditions are satisfied. The following page contains additional permanent and low-temperature branches with contradictory printed voltage inequalities; those branches are not converted here into workshop test limits.
