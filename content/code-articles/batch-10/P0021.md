# P0021: bank-2 intake camshaft target-error interpretation

P0021 is described as a bank-2 intake camshaft target error in the cited GM table. The catalog wording includes over-advanced timing, but this source’s monitor description is framed around failure to reach the commanded target.

## Do not reverse the printed comparison

The table combines a target not reached after more than one second with an absolute-deviation criterion printed as less than three crankshaft degrees. The neighboring slow-response family instead uses a greater-than comparison. These are distinct documented branches; replacing the printed sign with an assumption would erase that distinction.

The target-error row counts at least four events and requires the bank-2 intake governor to operate in closed loop. It references a specific intake-camshaft lookup table and separate operating-condition and inhibition tables.

## What can be concluded

The supported conclusion is that the target-position monitor failed in its eligible control mode. The row does not, on its own, establish a fixed mechanical timing offset or prove which component caused it. Preserve the actual target and observed position through the event rather than interpreting a later angle snapshot as the complete test.

Two-trip reporting is listed. Verification should use the applicable target-error diagnostic and confirm its completion. Because the printed less-than criterion can be surprising beside the generic title, confirm the exact calibration before treating this summary as a workshop acceptance limit.
