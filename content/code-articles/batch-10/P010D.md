# P010D: high SENT data from airflow sensor B

P010D is a high-range digital airflow-sensor check in this GM table. Although the catalog title says circuit high, the documented quantity is SENT data, not a universal analog signal voltage.

## The actual limit

The monitor fails for high-range SENT data above 16,375 over 1.5 seconds. Ignition must be on, supply and common enable conditions must be satisfied, and the sensor self-diagnosis and electrical-line diagnosis must not report errors.

Those prerequisites distinguish a validly transmitted out-of-range value from a sensor-internal or communication-line fault. A raw data value above the limit also must not be confused with a physical airflow reading in grams per second; the table gives it as a digital range criterion.

## Use the right kind of evidence

Identify whether diagnostic data exposes the raw SENT quantity or only a converted airflow parameter. A plausible converted display does not independently confirm the raw range test, and an analog-voltage specification from a different airflow sensor is not transferable.

The table lists two-trip reporting. It also contains an implausibly high printed upper battery-voltage bound, which is omitted here rather than presented as a valid vehicle specification. Verification should use the correct calibration’s digital range monitor and confirm that its self-test and line-test prerequisites permit it to complete.
