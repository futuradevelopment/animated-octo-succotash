# P016C: Elantra lean-control limits after very cold starts

## The cold-start pattern

Hyundai linked P016C to incorrect fuel-adaptation learning on certain 2021 Elantra 2.0 L vehicles. The documented trigger is starting at an ambient temperature of -10 °C or colder. If incorrect adaptations are learned, the engine can later stall while idling or coming to a stop. That sequence is more informative than interpreting the lean-control-limit label as proof that the fuel pump or oxygen sensor has failed.

## Separate the two software subjects

Campaign T6R revises fuel-learning logic for this condition. The same bulletin also discusses blower-motor control and a different code, P1CD0. Their presence in one software release does not mean P016C diagnoses the blower motor. For the lean complaint, record the initial ambient temperature, whether the problem followed that cold start and the subsequent idle behavior. Those details help compare the vehicle with the documented case.

## What a workshop should verify

The vehicle must match the CN7/CN7A, 2.0 L and campaign criteria; the bulletin provides a ROM-ID table for checking calibration applicability. Its correction is an ECM software update using the proper vehicle-specific event, followed by a full fault check and confirmation of normal operation. A later available update may supersede the historical event, so the 2021 document should not be treated as the latest calibration listing. If the vehicle already has the relevant correction or its symptoms do not fit, the source does not justify repeated reflashing or speculative parts replacement. Investigate the actual lean or stalling evidence through current service information.

## Source and applicability

Hyundai 21-01-043H campaign T6R concerns certain 2021 Elantra CN7/CN7A vehicles with the 2.0 L engine. Confirm campaign eligibility and ROM ID.
