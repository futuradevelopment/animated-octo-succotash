# U0594: Ram eTorque data fault with an incompletely seated MGU connector

## Why this communication fault can accompany a stall

U0594 identifies implausible data received from the hybrid control processor in this Ram bulletin. FCA traced the listed service-action condition to a motor generator unit connector that was not fully seated. The reported pattern includes loss of main 12-volt battery charging, warning lamps, and a stall after approximately 15–40 minutes of driving, followed by an inability to restart. That combination deserves more attention than an isolated historical network code.

## Evidence to collect before choosing a repair

Record the complete scan, charging warnings, time from starting to the fault, and whether restarting remains possible. The same bulletin lists resolver, motor-position, internal-control and hybrid-generator faults as possible companions. Their presence helps describe the event but does not establish eligibility by itself. Confirm the engine, production date and original RRT VIN record. FCA instructs technicians to address unrelated system faults before following this action; another Ram configuration needs its own diagnostic procedure.

## What the documented remedy actually involves

For an eligible listed VIN, the bulletin specifies replacing the MGU under the eTorque service procedure and clearing associated faults. It does not instruct the owner to push the connector back into place or replace a network module solely because U0594 is present. A vehicle already stalling or losing charging should be recovered for professional diagnosis. Ask for the charging and restart results after repair, and confirm current parts and campaign status because this 2021 action had a limited eligibility period.

## Source and applicability

FCA bulletin 08-014-21, January 2021: North American 2020–2021 Ram 1500 DT with 5.7 L EZL eTorque engine, built June 11–November 14, 2020. The historical RRT repair applied only to listed VINs.
