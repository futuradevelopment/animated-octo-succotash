# P0253: Compass and Meridian diesel metering fault from harness chafing

## A fuel-metering code can originate at the PCM bracket

P0253 is described here as a metering-unit electrical short to ground. The documented cause is PCM wiring rubbing against its mounting bracket, with possible no-start behavior and additional P0235 or P0521 faults. The combination of different affected circuits makes the harness condition particularly relevant before replacing an individual fuel-control part. This bulletin is limited to the specified diesel/manual-transmission installation.

## Length and damage lead to different decisions

The workshop measures the harness segment from the PCM connector to the channel edge shown in the original diagrams. A length over 250 mm or visible damage enters the repair branch. Actual harness damage calls for replacement; the procedure then specifies protective taping and correct routing through the secured clamp. If the harness is undamaged and within the specified length, the bulletin sends the technician to further diagnosis rather than prescribing an unnecessary harness replacement.

## Document the contact point and final routing

Ask for the measured length, a photograph of any abrasion and confirmation that the routing clamp is locked after repair. Covering exposed damaged wiring with tape alone is not the replacement branch in this document. Model-specific harness selection matters because Compass and Meridian use different listed assemblies. After repair, clear the relevant faults and confirm starting and circuit operation. If P0253 remains with the harness passing this inspection, proceed with the exact metering-circuit diagnostic procedure rather than assuming all fuel-system complaints share this bracket issue.

## Source and applicability

FCA bulletin 08-086-25, March 2025: 2024–2025 Jeep Compass M6 and Meridian H6, built April 1, 2024–February 5, 2025, with 2.0 L EBS diesel and DE1 C635 six-speed manual transmission. Verify market and VIN applicability from current service information.
