# C058E: Wagoneer brake-booster position fault and paired component replacement

## Identify the motor and subcode before interpreting the note

C058E-00 is brake-booster motor B position-sensor circuit range/performance in this service note. It appears alongside separate motor A phase and position-sensor faults. Those descriptions refer to different monitored functions, so the complete diagnostic scan matters when deciding whether this document fits the vehicle. The source does not say that all three faults must be present together.

## The paired replacement instruction is conditional

The important service instruction comes after diagnostic testing: if the tests establish a need to replace the brake-system control module, the note directs replacement of the electric booster as well. It is not a command to replace both merely because C058E is stored. Ask the workshop which failed test supports the module decision and whether the vehicle and booster part family match the current service information. The note also requests both relevant units for engineering analysis, supporting the importance of retaining the original failure evidence.

## Cleanliness is part of brake-system repair

The communication emphasizes manufacturer-approved brake fluid, clean filling equipment and clean gloves to prevent cross-contamination. It supplies no standalone sensor voltage, pinout, bleeding sequence or owner repair procedure. Those details must come from the exact vehicle service manual. A warning accompanied by altered brake assistance requires prompt professional assessment. This manuscript remains pending detailed fitment confirmation; its useful conclusion is the documented conditional replacement boundary, not a universal diagnosis of the booster.

## Source and applicability

Stellantis GPOP note 9100565, July 2025, lists model years 2022–2025 and platform WS. It concerns C058E-00; the note provides no complete VIN, equipment or market applicability table.
