# C003A: Jeep right-rear wheel-speed suffixes and water-intrusion scope

## Keep the failure suffix

C003A identifies the right-rear wheel-speed sensor in this Jeep bulletin, but the additional characters carry useful diagnostic information. Compass M1/M6 entries include current out of range, signal comparison failure and erratic signal. Renegade B1 uses general-electrical-failure or invalid-signal variants. Recording only C003A loses the distinction between those reported failure modes.

## This is a narrowly scoped sensor campaign

FCA attributes the documented condition to oxidation or water intrusion and specifies replacement of all four wheel-speed sensors for the qualifying case. That instruction must stay attached to the bulletin limits. It covers LATAM/APAC applications with different platform and build-date restrictions; the M1 application additionally excludes specified parking-assist or adaptive-cruise equipment. A model badge and an approximate year are insufficient to establish eligibility.

## Useful questions for diagnosis

Ask whether the complete right-rear code suffix matches the applicable platform, whether other wheel-speed faults are present, and whether the vehicle meets the market, equipment and build criteria. Outside those limits, this document does not replace normal electrical and signal diagnosis or justify changing every sensor. Within them, it explains why a repair may address all four sensors despite a single corner appearing in the scan. Following the proper brake-system service procedure and checking returning faults are essential parts of completion. The bulletin does not establish that an illuminated ABS or stability warning can be ignored, nor does it supply a universal wheel-speed-sensor resistance value.

## Source and applicability

FCA 05-003-21 applies to specified 2018–2020 Compass/Renegade platform, market, build and equipment combinations. Its LATAM/APAC scope excludes a blanket US-market claim.
