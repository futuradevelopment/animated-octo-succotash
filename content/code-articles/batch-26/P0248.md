# P0248: Hurricane wastegate performance fault and retaining-clip measurements

## A mechanical connection worth checking

P0248-00 denotes wastegate actuator B range/performance in this Ram case. The bulletin connects it with incorrectly installed wastegate-rod retaining clips, sometimes alongside P2B94-00 and weak turbo acceleration. A retention problem can therefore be relevant before an actuator or turbocharger assembly is condemned. The code still needs the full vehicle scan and symptom match; this production issue is not a universal explanation for wastegate performance faults.

## The two clip locations have different limits

The workshop inspection measures both actuator-side retaining clips and both housing-side clips after gaining access. The published metric replacement thresholds are greater than 13.70 mm on the actuator side and greater than 17.78 mm on the housing side. These are distinct positions, not interchangeable dimensions. The bulletin calls for replacement of clips exceeding their respective threshold and uses different clip types. A written measurement and location are more informative than an invoice saying only that the turbo linkage was checked.

## Resolve eligibility and verify the result

The source states a June 2024 production start, but its March 2025 written endpoint and MDH endpoint disagree. Have the dealer verify the applicable VIN and current revision instead of guessing which endpoint is intended. After correcting a qualifying clip problem, the technician reinstalls the intake tube and clears faults. Acceleration and returning codes should then be reassessed. If the clips meet specification or the vehicle is outside this scope, continue the proper wastegate diagnostic procedure rather than repeatedly replacing clips.

## Source and applicability

FCA bulletin 09-008-25, April 2025: 2025 Ram 1500 DT with 3.0 L EFC Hurricane high-output twin-turbo engine. The printed build-date endpoint conflicts with its MDH value; confirm VIN applicability before using this procedure.
