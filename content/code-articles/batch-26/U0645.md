# U0645: Ram active air-dam communication and connector condition

## The equipment and symptom must match

The bulletin concerns an inoperative active front air dam accompanied by U0645, lost communication with its module. The MXB equipment code and build cutoff are essential: a truck without the same active device cannot be diagnosed from this case merely because another network code appears.

## Connector damage is not skipped

Although the documented repair replaces the air dam, FCA first calls for inspection of the harness connector for corrosion or water intrusion and correction of any such findings. Installing a new assembly while leaving damaged contacts would omit a meaningful part of the repair. The code itself does not identify whether the connector or internal assembly caused the communication loss.

## Complete the replacement routine when available

The procedure includes the diagnostic-tool “Active Air Dam Replaced” function where supported, followed by fault clearing and appropriate verification. The service record should identify the connector findings, assembly work and routine completion. The historical document also discusses a warranty extension, but current individual eligibility must be checked by VIN and service history; the article does not promise coverage. If the code or inoperative condition remains, continue with current electrical diagnosis. The bulletin supplies no universal bus voltage or permission to bypass the air-dam controller to remove the warning.

## Source and applicability

FCA 23-029-22 is a historical case for specified 2019 Ram 1500 DT with MXB active air dam, built through January 21, 2019.
