# P0A3C: Niro plug-in inverter temperature warning after an OBC coolant leak

## Check coolant loss before assuming inverter failure

P0A3C means drive motor A inverter over-temperature in this Kia bulletin. A detached onboard-charger coolant inlet hose can produce this code, related inverter-cooling faults and hybrid-system or refill-coolant warnings. The important connection is shared cooling: a warning naming the drive inverter can originate from lost coolant at the charger hose. The source does not establish that the inverter itself needs replacement.

## The repair addresses hose retention

Kia specifies a spacer at the OBC inlet quick connection. The technician first confirms that the hose is fully installed, positions the spacer behind the connector’s identified notch and checks that the hose remains latched. The spacer can crack if spread excessively or mishandled. The bulletin explicitly cautions against fully disconnecting the hose during the retention check because coolant can escape. This is therefore not an invitation for an owner to tug at unfamiliar components underneath a hybrid vehicle.

## Coolant recovery and verification matter

When coolant has been lost, the prescribed repair includes topping up and bleeding the inverter cooling circuit under the Kia service procedure, checking the OBC connection for further leakage and verifying vehicle operation. Ask whether a leak was actually found, whether the spacer was fitted and whether bleeding was completed. An over-temperature or hybrid warning should be assessed before continued use. If the hose is secure or the production range does not match, the temperature fault still needs its own cooling-system diagnosis rather than an assumed spacer cure.

## Source and applicability

Kia bulletin ELE161 / service action SA365, December 2018: 2018–2019 Niro DE PHEV produced March 21–August 22, 2018. Applicability uses the PHEV introduction and affected VIN information, not a generic Niro model tag.
