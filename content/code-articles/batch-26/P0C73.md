# P0C73: Niro coolant-pump performance and the contamination decision

## The case is a sealing problem

For the specified 2019 Niro hybrids, Kia associates P0C73 with intermittent electrical connection caused by liquid entering an auxiliary electric water-pump case through a poorly sealed joint. P2600 may appear with it. The distinction matters: the bulletin supplies a pump-sealing and internal-condition investigation rather than assuming that a pump-performance code proves a failed controller.

## Partial repair versus complete assembly

The illustrated repair section separates simple contamination from severe contamination. Its lighter-condition branch uses cleaning, drying and new kit components, while its severe-condition branch calls for the complete pump assembly. However, the introductory note uses broader wording about contamination or corroded terminals. That difference needs current Kia service clarification; this article does not resolve it by inventing a corrosion limit or telling an owner to classify the damage unaided.

## What to retain in the repair record

Confirm the HEV/PHEV variant and production dates, then document the actual internal condition and the reason for the selected repair level. The bulletin’s model statement should be used rather than broad catalog associations that include Niro EV. Following repair, the source calls for correct coolant replenishment, leak checks and confirmation of operation. Replacing only the case without establishing that the remaining electrical parts are serviceable is not an evidence-based conclusion. Equally, P0C73 alone does not prove that every pump component is beyond repair. High-voltage vehicle work and coolant-system service require the applicable professional procedures.

## Source and applicability

Kia ELE211 revision 1 concerns 2019 Niro DE HEV/PHEV produced November 26, 2018–January 23, 2019. Confirm current service clarification of contamination wording before repair.
