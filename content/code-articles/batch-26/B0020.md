# B0020: Jeep and Chrysler left-seat airbag open-circuit repair case

## The suffix identifies the relevant fault

B0020-13 means an open circuit in the left front seat side-airbag deployment circuit in this bulletin. The corresponding right-side fault is B0028-13. An illuminated airbag warning lamp is the reported symptom. Preserve the complete suffix and module report: a generic scanner displaying only B0020 loses the circuit-open detail needed to match this repair case, and a different manufacturer may assign a different meaning.

## Why both seat harnesses appear on the repair order

FCA specifies front-seat jumper harnesses that bypass the seat-cushion inline connection. Its instructions call for both jumpers even when only one of the listed codes is stored. That explains why a properly supported repair can involve both seats for a left-side warning. Harness selection differs between Cherokee and Chrysler 200, and the published connector cavity locations differ too; matching the model is more useful than copying a wiring location from a similar-looking seat.

## Professional verification is part of the repair

Airbag circuits must not be tested with improvised jumpers, ordinary resistance probes across an inflator, or an owner attempt to bypass the warning. The bulletin requires restraint-system isolation, seat disassembly, replacement seat-back fasteners, secured harness routing and a restraint verification procedure. It also calls for occupant-classification configuration and a steering-system verification after reassembly. Ask for the initial scan and completed verification results. Clearing the lamp without locating the open circuit does not show that the restraint system has been restored.

## Source and applicability

FCA bulletin 10-001-20: 2014–2017 Jeep Cherokee KL and 2015–2017 Chrysler 200 UF with CJ1 front-seat side airbags, covering North America, EMEA, APAC and LATAM. This article concerns B0020-13 specifically.
