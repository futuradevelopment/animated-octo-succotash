# C0033: Right-front tone-wheel debris and the retest decision

## A signal problem can begin with debris

The STAR case links a right-front tone-wheel invalid-signal fault, C0033-29, with metal dust or flakes collecting around the hub and tone-wheel area. ABS or stability-control lamps may be intermittent or steady. The proposed investigation is therefore directed at the signal-producing area rather than assuming the wheel-speed sensor electronics have failed.

## Cleaning comes before the replacement branch

The documented repair removes debris during the proper suspension/hub service procedure, then reassesses the vehicle on a test drive. Hub-bearing replacement is the next branch only if the condition returns; the tone wheel is part of that assembly in this case. A code clear without the inspection and retest does not establish either branch.

## Keep the applicability and safety limits

The short case publication does not contain a full model or VIN table, so a technician must verify that the actual hub arrangement and service procedure match. It is not a universal instruction to remove every right-front knuckle for C0033. Record the complete suffix, debris findings and whether the same fault returned after the documented cleaning. Brake and stability-system warnings require appropriate assessment, and suspension disassembly is professional work with the correct support and reassembly specifications. The case supplies no generic sensor resistance or air-gap limit and does not authorize ignoring a returning ABS fault.

## Source and applicability

Stellantis STAR case S2523000051, released May 2025, documents C0033-29. The PDF does not independently provide a complete model/VIN application table.
