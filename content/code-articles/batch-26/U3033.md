# U3033: Grand Cherokee security-certificate faults and gateway updates

## No warning lamp does not mean no effect

The bulletin describes U3033-00 and U3033-01 as missing or invalid security-certificate faults that can remain active or stored and resist clearing. It explicitly says these faults do not illuminate the malfunction lamp. The associated gateway-software case can nevertheless affect connected services, Wi-Fi setup or diagnostic communication. Record the symptoms and exact suffix rather than relying on whether the check-engine light is on.

## Establish whether the update finished

FCA distinguishes an over-the-air update that completed from repeated unsuccessful events or a message directing the customer to service. In the latter situation, a manual gateway flash may be required. A download notification alone therefore does not demonstrate that the security gateway reached the intended software level. The repair includes an inspection decision when the applicable update is already present.

## Keep the vehicle boundary visible

This revision specifies a 2025 WL build interval and market coverage, plus an RSU VIN check. Vehicles outside those limits need their own applicable guidance. The workshop should verify the gateway’s actual software and communication state, address unrelated faults and confirm the affected connected functions afterward. The bulletin is a software-repair path; it does not instruct owners to bypass the gateway or install an arbitrary certificate. A code that remains after the correct documented update is evidence for further diagnosis, not automatic justification to replace every module that communicates through the gateway.

## Source and applicability

FCA 08-336-25 revision A covers specified 2025 Grand Cherokee/Grand Cherokee L WL vehicles built April 7–July 25, 2025, subject to the listed market and RSU conditions.
