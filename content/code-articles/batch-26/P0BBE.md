# P0BBE: Jeep 4xe battery-voltage variation and the XU1 repair case

## Pack-voltage variation needs qualified assessment

P0BBE-00 is hybrid battery pack voltage variation in this manufacturer communication. It is grouped with battery-balancing, unstable-voltage and state-of-health faults. Customers may see a charging-system service message with charging inhibited. The bulletin identifies a manufacturing defect and notes that affected packs may have cell-separator damage. This makes the complaint more significant than a routine request to clear a charging warning.

## The described remedy is pack replacement

For the specified condition and applicable vehicle, the repair is replacement of the high-voltage battery pack under the manufacturer procedure. The source does not provide an owner balancing method, a cell-level repair or permission to force charging through an inhibited system. High-voltage isolation and battery work are restricted to qualified technicians. Follow current vehicle warnings and obtain professional guidance before further charging; report any unusual heat, odor or other symptoms immediately to the repairer.

## Confirm the individual warranty and campaign record

The bulletin refers to XU1 and separate regional warranty documents. An owner should ask the dealer to verify the VIN, qualifying code, installed pack and current applicable coverage rather than assume that a search result guarantees free replacement. The cited document also instructs technicians to address unrelated faults separately. After a supported replacement, request confirmation of the specified cooling-system work, fault scan and restored charging operation. A different model, engine or battery complaint requires its own eligibility and diagnostic assessment.

## Source and applicability

FCA bulletin 08-072-26, February 2026: 2021–2025 Wrangler JL and 2022–2026 Grand Cherokee WL applications equipped with the 2.0 L ECX PHEV engine, subject to exact VIN, model and regional eligibility. The family heading alone does not establish that every listed nameplate has this powertrain.
