# P2110: Ram forced RPM limitation with throttle icing

## The limited-RPM code can be a protective result

In this documented cold-weather case, P2110 appears with throttle icing and may accompany P2072 or P2118. FCA also describes a possible no-start below freezing. The limited-RPM entry is therefore not sufficient evidence that the throttle motor itself has failed; ice and moisture in the intake arrangement are central to this case.

## The correction addresses more than the throttle surface

The repair includes drying the intake resonator, throttle area and PCV components, fitting revised make-up-air and air-cleaner parts, and an oil/filter change. It explicitly says throttle-body removal is unnecessary for the documented drying step. Merely clearing codes after the ice melts does not reproduce the preventive changes in the bulletin.

## Verify the case without inventing a threshold

The source’s printed Celsius and Fahrenheit figures do not agree, so use the actual below-freezing history and current clarified service guidance rather than a guessed cutoff. Confirm the DS/ERB application and inspect the relevant condition through the proper procedure. A workshop should record moisture or ice findings and the completed revised parts, then assess the original starting or throttle complaint. The bulletin does not establish that every P2110 is icing or authorize forcing the throttle open. Persistent faults need current electronic-throttle diagnosis.

## Source and applicability

FCA 09-023-23 concerns 2013–2023 Ram 1500 DS with the 3.6 L ERB engine. The document prints inconsistent Celsius/Fahrenheit values; no exact temperature threshold is adopted here.
