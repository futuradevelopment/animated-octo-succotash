# U0287: Compass transmission-pump communication and paired repair

## Use the accompanying operating complaint

The source requires a warning lamp and U0287-00, lost communication with transmission-fluid-pump module 1, together with disabled stop/start or a service-transmission message. That combination supports a specific electric-oil-pump case. A lone historical network entry on a differently equipped Compass is not the same evidence.

## The correction combines hardware and software

FCA identifies a faulty transmission electric oil pump and prescribes both pump replacement and a TCM software update. Replacing the pump without checking the software is not the complete bulletin repair, and a flash alone does not reproduce its hardware correction. The eight-speed 8F30 identification matters because another transmission can use a different pump and control arrangement.

## Verify the scope before condemning the pump

Confirm the engine, transmission and build cutoff, preserve the complete fault report and address other abnormalities through the appropriate diagnosis. A workshop should document the pump repair and final controller software, then reassess communication and the original stop/start or transmission warning. The bulletin does not provide a generic pump connector test or prove that every lost-communication code means a failed module. If the fault remains after the specified correction, current circuit and network diagnosis is still necessary. This article does not extend the repair to older Compass models or claim present warranty eligibility from the bulletin alone.

## Source and applicability

FCA 21-058-26 covers North American 2026 Compass MP built through February 11, 2026, with the 2.0 L EC1 engine and 8F30 DGD transmission.
