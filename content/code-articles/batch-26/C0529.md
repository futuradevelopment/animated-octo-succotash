# C0529: ProMaster steering-angle calibration before steering-gear replacement

## Missing calibration is not the same as failed hardware

FCA identifies C0529-00 in this note as a steering-angle-sensor module missing-calibration fault. Its first instruction is initialization before ordering a steering gear. The distinction matters after recent service: a controller without the required learned reference may report a fault even though replacing the assembly has not been shown necessary.

## Recent battery or gear work is relevant

The note specifically calls for the initialization following a battery disconnect or steering-gear replacement. Ask when the warning appeared relative to those events. A new gear does not eliminate the need for the calibration step, and a battery-related onset should be documented rather than omitted from the repair history.

## There is a second initialization route

If the stated manual initialization is unsuccessful, FCA directs use of the diagnostic tool’s advanced steering-angle initialization routine. That is a next step, not proof that the first failure condemned the steering gear. A workshop should use the complete applicable steering procedure, confirm successful initialization and reassess the code and steering operation. The short note does not provide a full electrical diagnostic tree or a guarantee that every C0529 is calibration-only. Persistent faults or abnormal steering behavior still require professional diagnosis under current service information.

## Source and applicability

FCA GPOP 9100119, dated January 6, 2023, concerns model years 2022–2023 VF and the specified electric steering gear. Original scanned page was visually checked.
