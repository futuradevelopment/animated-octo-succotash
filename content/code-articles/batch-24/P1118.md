# P1118: Volkswagen rear oxygen-sensor heater open circuit

Volkswagen P1118 corresponds to Ross-Tech's 17526/004376 entry for an open heater circuit at bank 1, sensor 2. Its target is the sensor's heating circuit. A rich or lean mixture diagnosis does not follow from this code, and it is not a direct judgment of catalyst efficiency.

## Identify the heater branch

The useful distinction is between the oxygen-sensing function and the electrical heater serving that sensor. Ross-Tech names two fault areas: the wiring or connectors associated with this heater, and the heater circuit itself. An open circuit can therefore require harness or connection work rather than replacement of the complete sensor assembly.

Before testing, match the bank and sensor designation to the vehicle's wiring information. The wiki does not supply connector cavities, a resistance limit or an applied-voltage procedure. Those details cannot be inferred from the five-character code.

## Establish what was actually repaired

A technician should document whether the interruption was in the external circuit or in the heater component, using the applicable electrical test. The code's wording narrows the investigation but does not identify the physical break.

After correction, reassess heater operation and the fault status under the conditions required by that controller. Clearing the stored entry alone provides no evidence that the heater circuit has become continuous again.
