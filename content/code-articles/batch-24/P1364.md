# P1364: Volkswagen cylinder 4 ignition open-circuit checks

Ross-Tech identifies Volkswagen P1364/17772 as an open circuit associated with cylinder 4 ignition. Possible symptoms include misfire, absent spark and an illuminated engine warning.

## The diagnostic branches

The reference names coils with output stages, fuses, and wiring or connectors to the engine controller. A confirmed external interruption requires a different repair from a demonstrated coil/output-stage defect. Use the correct ignition-system information to locate and evaluate the cylinder 4 branch.

Ross-Tech gives related cylinder codes the same electrical checks. The shared method does not make another cylinder's result evidence about this one.

## North American 2.0L documentation

The source refers Golf/Jetta/Bora 1J/9M vehicles with a 2.0L engine in the North American market to TSB 01-08-27/2010575. Check the original document and the vehicle's applicability before following that lead.

Keep the actual circuit finding and any applicable bulletin information together in the repair record. P1364 identifies where to investigate; it does not select a replacement part by itself.
