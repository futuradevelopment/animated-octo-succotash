# P1820: Volkswagen pressure valve 2 short to supply and connector condition

Volkswagen P1820/18228 concerns pressure-control valve 2 shorted to positive supply in Ross-Tech's reference. It identifies N216 and notes solenoid valve 5/N92 as an alternative name. Match that designation to the actual gearbox.

## Separate external and component findings

Ross-Tech lists a wiring short to supply and a defective valve as possibilities. Its checks cover the wiring, valve and relevant measuring values. A code naming the valve still requires evidence locating the electrical fault.

The short-to-positive direction distinguishes this entry from an open-or-grounded complaint on the same valve role. Preserve it in the diagnostic record.

## The connector inspection lead

The reference recommends checking for connector corrosion or water damage, particularly the 16-pin valve-body/harness connection on an 01V transmission. Apply that location only to a matching installation.

The procedure is shared across the pressure-valve family. P1820's distinct scope is valve 2/N216 or N92 and a short to positive supply.
