# P1392: Volkswagen bank 2 G163 camshaft sensor supply and signal paths

P1392/17800/005010 is the Volkswagen/VAG entry for bank 2 camshaft-position sensor G163 with an open or short-to-positive condition. Ross-Tech lists possible high-load power loss, elevated fuel consumption and poor emissions as accompanying symptoms. These do not prove mechanical camshaft timing failure.

## The source separates two wiring checks

One listed path is the wiring between the engine controller and G163. Another is the sensor's power-supply and ground wiring. That distinction matters: checking a signal wire alone does not establish that the sensor is receiving a sound supply and return path.

The remaining possibilities are the sensor itself and the engine controller. They are alternatives to evaluate, not a replacement sequence prescribed by the fault number.

## Resolve the actual electrical condition

The description groups an open path with a short to positive supply. Suitable engine-specific tests are required to determine which condition exists and where. The wiki supplies no common pinout, voltage threshold or waveform definition.

Record signal-path, supply/ground and component findings separately. A supported repair should explain why the affected G163 circuit now operates correctly, followed by reassessment of the original engine complaint. A temporary improvement in power or a cleared code cannot substitute for identifying and correcting the electrical defect.
