# P1247: Volkswagen G80 needle-lift sensor open or short to positive

P1247/17655/004679 concerns the G80 needle-lift sensor circuit being open or shorted to positive supply in Ross-Tech's VAG description. Its possible symptoms include rough running, excessive emissions and engine or pre-glow warning lights. This is a sensor-circuit entry, not a direct instruction to adjust injection timing.

## Separate the sensor path from the engine complaint

Ross-Tech names faulty fuses, wiring or connectors associated with G80, and the sensor itself. The electrical fault could therefore originate before the sensing component. A rough-running complaint does not prove that the injector pump or all injectors need replacement.

The wording groups two possible conditions: an open circuit and a short to supply. The scan code alone does not distinguish between them. The appropriate circuit tests and wiring information are needed to establish the condition actually present.

## Evidence needed before replacing G80

Identify the relevant fuse and circuit from the engine documentation, because the wiki does not give universal fuse positions or connector pins. Record the external-circuit findings separately from any sensor test result. A failed connection and an internally failed sensor are different repairs.

After correction, verify the G80 fault status and reassess the rough-running or warning-lamp symptoms. The important result is a demonstrated restoration of the affected sensing path, rather than a cleared code or an unrelated timing change.
