# P1213: Volkswagen cylinder 1 injector short-to-positive circuit fault

P1213/17621 identifies a short to positive supply in the cylinder 1 injector circuit, N30, in Ross-Tech's VAG reference. An engine warning lamp and poor performance are possible symptoms. The reported condition concerns the injector's electrical path.

## Fault areas for N30

Ross-Tech lists the injector fuse, harness, injector and engine controller as possible contributors. A wiring defect and an internally defective injector require different repairs, even though both can lead to an injector-related code.

## The supported checks

The reference calls for inspecting wiring to and from N30 and testing injector resistance using the engine's repair-manual specification. The wiring check evaluates the external path; the resistance check evaluates the component. Preserve those findings separately when deciding what needs repair.

This is the same electrical method Ross-Tech gives related injector-circuit codes. P1213's specific identifiers are cylinder 1, N30 and the short-to-positive condition; confirm their mapping on the actual engine.
