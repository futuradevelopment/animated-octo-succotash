# P1338: Volkswagen G40 camshaft sensor open or short to positive

Ross-Tech maps Volkswagen P1338/17746/004920 to camshaft-position sensor G40 with an open circuit or short to positive supply. The grouping is important: the code does not itself reveal which of those two electrical conditions exists.

## The sensor name does not establish the failed part

The documented possibilities are a defective G40 sensor or faulty wiring and connectors associated with it. Ross-Tech recommends checking those paths and camshaft adjustment. A mechanical adjustment check can form part of the investigation, but P1338 alone is not evidence that belt or chain timing must be repaired.

Keep an actual circuit finding separate from a timing-related observation. That avoids treating any camshaft code as permission to replace either the sensor or the timing system without supporting tests.

## Establish the applicable signal criteria

The page contains no universal terminal assignment, voltage range or waveform. Obtain those details for the particular engine and controller. An open wire and a connection to positive supply require different explanations even when the scanner reports them under the same code.

After correcting the verified defect, assess the G40 signal and whether the electrical fault returns. The useful result identifies the failed path and its restored operation; simply clearing the warning lamp cannot distinguish a successful repair from an intermittent problem that has not yet reappeared.
