# P1563: Volkswagen N146 lower control limit and warm-engine measurement context

P1563/17971/005475 is the Volkswagen/VAG N146 quantity-adjuster lower-limit entry in Ross-Tech's reference. The reported direction is the lower limit, while reduced power, jerking and a warning lamp are possible symptoms. It is not a standalone diagnosis that the pump must be replaced.

## Several fault types share the complaint

Ross-Tech identifies faulty fuses, N146 connections or wiring, an unadjusted quantity adjuster, and a blocked or defective adjuster. Electrical support, setup and physical movement are distinct diagnostic branches. The appropriate repair follows the demonstrated fault rather than the component name alone.

The page's pump-replacement option does not supply a test proving pump failure. Adjustment and pump work should use the correct engine-specific specialist procedure.

## Preserve the conditions attached to the ALH example

For the 1.9L ALH TDI, the source provides a 0.5–2.5 ohm N146 resistance range and a 1.45–2.0 volt reading in measuring block 001, field 3, once coolant is above 85°C. A cold reading is not equivalent to that stated warm-engine condition.

These are scoped reference values, not specifications for all Volkswagen diesel systems. Record the engine identity, test conditions and actual electrical or adjustment finding. After correction, confirm the relevant quantity-control behavior and revisit the running complaint; resetting the code alone cannot establish that the lower-limit condition is resolved.
