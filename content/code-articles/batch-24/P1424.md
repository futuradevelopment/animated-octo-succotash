# P1424: Volkswagen secondary-air bank 1 leak and the combination valve

Volkswagen P1424/17832/005156 is the secondary-air system bank 1 leak-detected entry in Ross-Tech's reference. It is narrower than a general complaint that secondary-air delivery is insufficient. The source identifies a leak path and a valve-related alternative.

## Inspect the delivery path, not just the pump

The documented possibilities are leaking piping between the secondary-air pump and the secondary-air valve, and a defective combination valve. The fact that the pump can operate does not establish that this downstream path is sealed or that the valve is functioning correctly.

The page directs checking that piping and replacing a combination valve only when it is faulty. It does not provide a universal leak-test pressure, valve location or replacement part number. Those details require the actual engine's procedure.

## Keep leak evidence separate from a low-flow code

Ross-Tech's P1423 entry describes insufficient flow and includes a broader set of pump-supply, vacuum and valve possibilities. P1424 should therefore retain its leak description in the repair record rather than being reduced to “secondary-air pump bad.”

After correcting an identified pipe or valve defect, confirm the relevant system behavior and fault status. A quieter pump, a reset warning lamp or a newly fitted valve alone does not establish that the documented leakage path has been resolved.
