# P1523: VAG implausible crash signal and the older Audi hazard-switch splice

P1523/17931/005411 identifies an implausible crash signal from the airbag controller in Ross-Tech's VAG reference. The entry lists wiring or connectors associated with airbag module J234 as a possible cause. The code does not establish that an airbag deployed or that crash protection is operating normally.

## A shared signal can involve more than the airbag module

For certain older Audi installations, Ross-Tech describes a discrete crash-signal wire shared with engine, airbag and central-locking or comfort modules. It advises checking whether the E3 hazard switch joins that circuit, often at a splice designated A125. A faulty switch can affect the signal on examples such as the 8N TT.

That is an Audi-specific diagnostic lead, not proof that every Volkswagen P1523 circuit contains the same switch or splice. The correct wiring diagram determines whether it applies.

## Preserve the multi-module evidence

The note mentions companion entries such as P1609, 01656 or 01366. Recording which modules report them can help a qualified technician locate the affected signal path. Their presence does not authorize resetting crash data or bypassing restraint functions.

Airbag-related circuits require the applicable safety and diagnostic procedures. The supported outcome is a verified signal-circuit diagnosis and post-repair checks, not simply clearing an engine-side warning while leaving the source of the crash signal unexplained.
