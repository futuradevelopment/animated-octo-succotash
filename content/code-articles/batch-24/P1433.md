# P1433: Volkswagen secondary-air relay J299 open circuit

Ross-Tech's Volkswagen/VAG description of P1433/17841/005171 is an open circuit associated with secondary-air pump relay J299. An engine warning lamp may accompany it. This identifies a relay-circuit fault, not an established failure of the pump motor.

## The relay is only one possible fault location

The source lists faulty fuses, wiring or connectors to and from J299, and the relay itself. These alternatives change the repair decision: restoring an interrupted external path is different from replacing a relay whose operation has actually been shown to be defective.

The wiki recommends checking those areas, but it does not identify fuse positions, relay pins or a universal electrical threshold. Match the circuit to the actual vehicle information before evaluating a measurement or ordering a component.

## Keep the electrical complaint distinct from airflow performance

Secondary-air systems can have delivery or valve problems, but P1433's stated condition is an open relay circuit. A test showing that air piping looks intact does not establish continuity in that circuit. Conversely, a repaired relay path does not automatically verify every secondary-air function.

Document the confirmed fuse, wiring, connector or relay finding, then assess the relevant electrical operation and whether this fault returns. A cleared warning lamp alone cannot distinguish a successful circuit repair from a condition that has not yet been monitored again.
