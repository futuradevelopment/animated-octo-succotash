# P1819: Volkswagen pressure valve 2 open or grounded: confirm N216/N92 mapping

Ross-Tech describes Volkswagen P1819/18227 as an open circuit or short to ground at pressure-control valve 2, N216. It notes that the same valve role may be called solenoid valve 5/N92 in the relevant transmission documentation.

## Establish the branch and defect

The listed possibilities are open or grounded wiring to the valve and a defective valve. The source recommends checking wiring, the valve and relevant measuring blocks. Resolve the transmission's N216/N92 mapping before attaching a test result to a component.

## Check the documented connection

Ross-Tech advises inspecting connectors for corrosion or water damage. On 01V transmissions, its specific lead is the 16-pin connection between the valve body and harness.

That inspection and the electrical checks are shared with related pressure-valve codes. P1819 identifies valve 2 and the open-or-grounded fault direction. Record whether the actual finding concerns the connection, the wiring path or a demonstrated valve defect; they imply different repairs.
