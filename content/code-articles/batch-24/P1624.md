# P1624: Volkswagen MIL request and the 09G connector fault example

Volkswagen P1624/18032/005668 means a malfunction-indicator request is active in Ross-Tech's description. Its diagnostic direction is to read transmission-controller fault memory. The engine-side entry is a messenger; it does not identify a failed engine sensor or transmission component by itself.

## Retrieve the transmission's own fault details

Preserve a full scan and the actual gearbox codes before selecting a repair. They provide more specific evidence than the warning request. Clearing P1624 alone can remove the reminder without addressing the condition that prompted it.

## A documented 09G case points to wiring, not an automatic overhaul

Volkswagen Technical Tip 01-15-06TT, revision 2042297/5 dated October 19, 2018, covers US-market 2015–2017 Jetta, Passat and Golf vehicles with the six-speed 09G automatic. It discusses erratic or harsh shifts with P1624 and possible P0776/P0796 entries.

For that exact application, the document identifies a possible broken wire at pin 6 of the T8 connector beneath the starter, serving the N216/N217 solenoid circuits. Its inspection target is the lilac/yellow wire. This is a concrete location-specific lead for a matching vehicle, not a pinout to transfer to other transmissions.

The prescribed sequence includes repair of a demonstrated wiring defect, basic settings and subsequent relearning before reassessing remaining shift concerns. The document explicitly identifies itself as a Tech Tip rather than a technical bulletin and does not authorize warranty claims. Confirm the vehicle and current procedure before applying it.
