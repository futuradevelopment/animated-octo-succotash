# P1171: Volkswagen throttle angle sensor 2 plausibility and shared supply checks

P1171/17579/004465 refers to an implausible signal from throttle-actuator angle sensor 2, G188, in Ross-Tech's VAG reference. This is throttle-body feedback, not the similarly named pedal-position sensing system. Confusing the two can send a diagnosis toward the wrong assembly.

## Read both angle channels

Although the heading identifies G188, the source lists both throttle-angle sensors G187/G188, their power supply, and their wiring or connectors as possible fault areas. A code naming sensor 2 therefore does not prove an isolated internal failure of that channel.

Ross-Tech suggests examining the corresponding measuring values, usually in block 062. “Usually” matters: confirm the measuring labels and controller documentation before treating that number as universal. The page does not publish the expected correlation curve or a voltage range.

## Distinguish supply trouble from angle disagreement

The useful investigation compares the available angle feedback with the condition of its supply and external connections. Evidence of a common feed or connector defect changes the repair decision compared with intact circuitry and a demonstrated internal sensing problem.

Record the supported finding, then check the applicable angle values and fault status again after correction. A throttle-body replacement should follow that evidence; P1171 alone is not a parts-order instruction or proof that adaptation will cure an electrical defect.
