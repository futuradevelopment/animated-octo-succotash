# P1337: Volkswagen G40 camshaft sensor circuit short to ground

P1337/17745/004919 identifies a short-to-ground condition associated with camshaft-position sensor G40 in Ross-Tech's VAG reference. An illuminated engine warning lamp is a possible symptom. The code's electrical description must be retained rather than replaced by a general assumption that the timing chain has stretched.

## Check the sensor and its external path

The source lists a faulty G40 sensor and faulty wiring or connectors to it as possible causes. Its checks cover those areas and also mention camshaft adjustment. That broader adjustment check does not mean the code by itself proves a mechanical timing defect.

The distinction changes the repair decision: an external circuit defect needs to be corrected even if the sensor is mechanically accessible and easy to replace. Conversely, an intact-looking connector is not a complete electrical test.

## Use engine-specific test information

The wiki gives no pin numbers, supply voltage or waveform criteria. The correct G40 circuit and expected behavior must be established from the engine documentation before testing or condemning a component.

Record the verified electrical finding separately from any camshaft-adjustment finding. After the relevant repair, reassess the affected signal and fault status. A cleared P1337 entry alone does not establish that the sensor circuit or the engine's timing-related behavior is now correct.
