# P1581: Volkswagen throttle basic setting not performed: check the prerequisites

P1581/17989/005505 is the J338 throttle-actuator basic-setting-not-performed entry in Ross-Tech's VAG reference. The page identifies both missing throttle-body alignment and a throttle malfunction as possibilities. Repeating an adaptation command is therefore not a diagnosis by itself.

## Distinguish an unfinished setup from a fault preventing completion

Ross-Tech recommends alignment, wiring or connector checks and evaluation of throttle measuring values. Its separate alignment guide describes the procedure as relearning the motorized throttle's positions and identifies preceding work, such as throttle removal or battery disconnection, that can make it relevant on applicable vehicles.

A mechanical or electrical defect can still prevent a valid result. The guide requires conditions including adequate battery voltage, appropriate temperature, an idle pedal position and a clean throttle body. These conditions should be matched to the vehicle's actual procedure rather than bypassed to force completion.

## The controller determines the procedure

Older controller families use numbered basic-setting groups, while UDS systems use named selections. The source specifically warns that UDS does not support the conventional group-number approach. This makes controller identification more useful than blindly entering a familiar number from another Volkswagen.

Confirm successful completion using the appropriate result indication, then reassess the original fault. If alignment fails, investigate the recorded prerequisites, circuit and throttle findings rather than assuming a new throttle body is automatically required.
