# P1571: Volkswagen left engine-mount N144 fault: confirm the electrical subtype

P1571/005489 is associated with the left engine-mount solenoid N144 in Ross-Tech's VAG reference. Reduced vibration damping is a possible symptom. The entry also notes that this type of fault can be stored without an EPC or engine warning lamp, so absence of those lamps does not establish normal mount operation.

## The component identity is clearer than the electrical subtype

The site's dictionary calls P1571 a short-to-positive fault. The Ross-Tech page names N144 but leaves its possible wiring conditions as an uncertain short or open circuit. That source does not independently settle the exact electrical subtype. Retain the full scan text and vehicle documentation rather than treating the broad wiki heading as confirmation of every dictionary word.

## Distinguish mount response from wiring condition

Ross-Tech recommends checking connections and the mount, with an output test as a way to assess operation. A suitable vehicle-specific procedure is required; the page provides no universal pinout, activation voltage or expected test response.

A failed response alone must still be interpreted alongside the circuit findings. The supported repair should identify a verified connection, wiring or mount defect and reassess the damping complaint afterward. Do not infer an internal mount failure solely from the absence of a warning lamp or from a code description whose electrical subtype remains unresolved.
