# P1528: Volkswagen N205 open circuit and reduced low-speed response

P1528/17936/005416 is Ross-Tech's Volkswagen/VAG description of an open circuit associated with bank 1 camshaft-timing adjustment valve N205. Reduced torque at lower engine speeds is a listed possible symptom. The fault text does not prove that a timing chain has stretched or that the complete adjuster assembly has failed.

## Find the interruption before choosing the component

The source lists faulty fuses, wiring or connectors to N205, and a defective valve. Its recommended checks cover each of those areas. An open external path and an internal component fault can produce different repair requirements even though both appear under the same valve-related code.

The wiki supplies no universal fuse location, connector terminal or resistance specification. Use the actual engine information to identify the circuit and decide whether a test result is acceptable.

## Tie the repair to both the circuit and complaint

Document where the interruption was established rather than recording only that an N205 valve was installed. If the fault was in the harness or supply path, that finding explains why replacing the valve alone would not address the cause.

Following correction, reassess the electrical fault status and relevant cam-adjustment behavior under the vehicle's procedure. The low-speed torque complaint should also be revisited, but its improvement alone cannot establish that the open-circuit condition is reliably resolved.
