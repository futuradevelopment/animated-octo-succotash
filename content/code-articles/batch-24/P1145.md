# P1145: Volkswagen G70 airflow signal short to positive supply

The Volkswagen/VAG interpretation of P1145/17553 is a G70 airflow-sensor circuit short to positive supply. Ross-Tech lists a warning lamp, excess exhaust smoke and reduced engine output among possible symptoms. These symptoms give context; they do not separate a damaged harness from a failed sensor.

## A directional circuit fault needs an electrical explanation

“Short to plus” is more specific than a general statement that airflow is implausible. Ross-Tech identifies fuse or harness issues as well as sensor failure, and points to the factory repair manual for circuit checks. Replacing the airflow sensor without establishing the condition of its external wiring can leave the reason for the code unresolved.

The source does not specify connector pins, a reference voltage or a resistance test. Those must come from the correct vehicle and engine documentation; the G70 designation alone is not a complete circuit specification.

## Keep the repair evidence tied to P1145

A useful diagnosis records whether the abnormal electrical condition is attributable to wiring, a connection or the sensor itself. General smoke or power complaints should not become the only basis for choosing a component.

After the confirmed defect is repaired, check the circuit outcome and whether the controller records the fault again under suitable operating conditions. The code's disappearance after clearing memory is only the start of that verification.
