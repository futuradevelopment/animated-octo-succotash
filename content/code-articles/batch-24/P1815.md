# P1815: Volkswagen pressure valve 1 short to positive and the 01V connector

P1815/18223 identifies pressure-control valve 1 with a short-to-positive condition in Ross-Tech's Volkswagen/VAG reference. The valve is called N215, with solenoid valve 4/N91 noted as an alternative designation.

## Retain the fault direction

The source lists wiring shorted to positive supply and a defective valve as possible causes. Its checks address the wiring, component and relevant measuring values. Confirm the actual transmission's component naming so the findings belong to the correct branch.

An external short and an internal valve defect are different repair outcomes. The fault's direction should remain in the record instead of being reduced to a general pressure-control complaint.

## Inspect the applicable connector

Ross-Tech recommends checking for corrosion or water damage and highlights the 16-pin valve-body/harness connection on 01V transmissions. This is a shared pressure-valve-family inspection lead, scoped to that gearbox.

For P1815, document the condition of that applicable connection and the specific valve 1 circuit before selecting the repair.
