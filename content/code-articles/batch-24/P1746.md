# P1746: Volkswagen transmission relay fault and low voltage at J743

P1746/18154/005958 is described by Ross-Tech as an electrical malfunction in transmission-solenoid power-relay control. A flashing or steady transmission-range display, warning lamp and transmission limp mode are possible symptoms. The title does not establish that a separately replaceable relay is the failed part.

## The source identifies J743 voltage as a key branch

The possible causes include low voltage at transmission controller J743 and an internal J743 problem. Ross-Tech recommends checking its wiring or connectors before considering repair or replacement. A verified external supply defect and an internal controller fault are different repair paths.

The diagnostic entry provides no universal supply threshold or connector pinout. The actual transmission configuration must determine the electrical test and acceptable values.

## The 02E example is specifically scoped

For the six-speed DSG/02E, the note points to low voltage at the mechatronic control module J743 and measuring block group 005. Confirm the controller's labels and procedure before treating that group number as applicable to a different gearbox.

Record the relevant voltage evidence and connection findings alongside the fault and symptom history. A transmission entering limp mode does not itself prove the mechatronic unit requires replacement. After correcting a supported defect, reassess the supply and controller behavior using the appropriate transmission procedure.
