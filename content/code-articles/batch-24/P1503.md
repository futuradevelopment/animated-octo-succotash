# P1503: Volkswagen alternator DF load signal and the BEW measuring-value note

Volkswagen P1503/17911 is an implausible or out-of-range alternator DF load-signal entry in Ross-Tech's reference. This concerns load information from the charging system; a battery-voltage reading alone does not establish whether that particular signal is correct.

## Separate alternator, regulator and external connections

The reference identifies the alternator, voltage regulator and wiring or connections as possible fault areas. It also calls attention to the fuse box in the battery-tray area, especially evidence of heat damage on an applicable installation. A damaged distribution connection needs its own assessment rather than being dismissed after fitting an alternator.

The source does not give a universal fuse-box location or establish that every Volkswagen uses the same arrangement. Match the installation to the vehicle.

## The numerical note applies to BEW engines

For the BEW engine, Ross-Tech refers to measuring block 16, field 1, and describes a typical idle reading around 15–30 percent. It says a zero reading can suggest a failed or incorrect alternator. These are bounded specialist observations, not a pass/fail rule for all engines or proof that zero always means alternator failure.

Record the correct measuring value, the installed alternator identity and the circuit findings. Then verify the DF-related result after the demonstrated defect is corrected, rather than considering improved battery voltage alone a completed P1503 repair.
