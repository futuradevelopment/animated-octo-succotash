# P1825: Volkswagen pressure valve 3 short-to-positive fault

Ross-Tech identifies Volkswagen P1825/18233 as a short-to-positive condition at pressure-control valve 3, N217. The page notes that some transmission documentation may use solenoid valve 6/N93 for that valve role.

## Keep the electrical direction specific

The listed possibilities are valve wiring shorted to supply and a defective valve. The source recommends examining the wiring, component and relevant measuring values. Confirm the actual gearbox's naming before interpreting the measurements.

A short-to-positive complaint should retain that description; it is more precise than a general shift-quality or pressure-control fault.

## Inspect the matching connection

Ross-Tech advises checking connectors for corrosion or water damage. On an 01V transmission, the highlighted location is the 16-pin connection between valve body and harness.

These checks are shared across the pressure-valve code family. For P1825, the evidence must concern valve 3/N217 or N93 and distinguish an external circuit defect from a demonstrated valve failure before a repair is chosen.
