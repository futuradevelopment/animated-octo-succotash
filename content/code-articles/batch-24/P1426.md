# P1426: Volkswagen N80 purge-valve open circuit and the J17 relay check

P1426/17834/005158 identifies an open circuit affecting the N80 EVAP purge valve in Ross-Tech's Volkswagen/VAG description. It is an electrical fault entry, not a direct report of a loose fuel cap or a measured vapor leak.

## Do not confuse valve operation with circuit continuity

The page lists N80 failure, faulty wiring or connectors associated with the valve, and fuel-pump relay J17 as possible causes. The electrical path therefore needs examination even when the valve is the component named by the code.

Ross-Tech recommends checking each of those areas but does not provide a common relay location, terminal assignment or circuit diagram. In particular, the J17 reference must be matched to the actual vehicle rather than used to assume a universal power-feed arrangement.

## What supports the repair decision?

A supported conclusion distinguishes an external interruption, a relevant supply or relay defect, and a verified internal valve fault. Replacing N80 because an EVAP code appeared does not establish which of those possibilities was responsible.

Use the applicable electrical procedure and reassess the circuit after the defect is corrected. If there are separate leak or mixture complaints, they require their own supporting evidence; this open-circuit code does not prove that all EVAP functions have been tested or that the fuel tank is leaking.
