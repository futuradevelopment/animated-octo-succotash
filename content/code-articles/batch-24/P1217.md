# P1217: Volkswagen cylinder 5 injector N83 short-to-positive diagnosis

P1217/17625 identifies a short-to-positive condition in cylinder 5 injector N83's circuit in Ross-Tech's VAG reference. The engine configuration must match that cylinder and component description. Poor running and an engine warning lamp are listed possible symptoms.

## The documented alternatives

Ross-Tech includes the injector fuse, harness, injector and engine controller in its cause list. It does not describe a fuel-flow test or establish nozzle contamination from this electrical code.

## Apply the shared injector checks to N83

The reference recommends inspecting the wiring to and from the injector and measuring injector resistance under the engine's repair-manual procedure. Keep the circuit finding distinct from the component measurement: a damaged external path and an internally defective injector are different outcomes.

Related cylinder codes use the same diagnostic method. The useful specificity here is cylinder 5/N83 and a short to positive supply, rather than a claim that cylinder 5 needs a different, undocumented repair.
