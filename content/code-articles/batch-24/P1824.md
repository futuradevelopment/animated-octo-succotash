# P1824: Volkswagen pressure valve 3 open or short to ground

P1824/18232 identifies pressure-control valve 3, N217, with an open circuit or short to ground in Ross-Tech's Volkswagen/VAG reference. The source also gives solenoid valve 6/N93 as an alternative designation.

## Confirm the valve mapping

The possible causes are open or grounded wiring to the valve and a defective valve. Ross-Tech recommends wiring and component checks together with relevant measuring blocks. Use the installed transmission's information to resolve the N217/N93 naming and evaluate the correct circuit.

## A concrete 01V inspection point

The source directs attention to connector corrosion or water damage. For the 01V gearbox, it highlights the 16-pin connection between the valve body and harness.

Related pressure-valve codes share this inspection lead and electrical method. P1824 applies them to valve 3 with an open-or-grounded condition. A damaged connection, a faulty wire and a verified valve defect are separate findings that should remain distinct when selecting the repair.
