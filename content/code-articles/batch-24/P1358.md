# P1358: Volkswagen cylinder 2 ignition circuit open: supply and coil evidence

P1358/17766 concerns cylinder 2 ignition with an open-circuit condition in Ross-Tech's Volkswagen/VAG reference. Misfire, no spark and a warning lamp are possible symptoms.

## Locate the electrical failure

The source identifies the coil/output-stage arrangement, fuses and wiring or connectors to the engine controller. A supply or connection defect is a documented alternative to a failed coil. Evaluate those paths against the actual engine's ignition-system information.

The same checks are used in Ross-Tech's adjacent cylinder entries. Keep cylinder 2's circuit findings separate when several ignition codes occur together.

## Check the 2.0L application note

Ross-Tech cites TSB 01-08-27/2010575 for North American Golf/Jetta/Bora 1J/9M vehicles with a 2.0L engine. That is a route to application-specific repair information. The original document and vehicle scope must be confirmed before using it to choose a repair; its presence on the wiki page does not prove an identical defect on an unrelated engine.
