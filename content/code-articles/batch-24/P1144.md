# P1144: Volkswagen G70 airflow circuit open or short to ground

P1144/17552 identifies an open circuit or short to ground affecting the G70 mass-airflow sensor in Ross-Tech's Volkswagen/VAG reference. Smoke, reduced output and an illuminated engine warning lamp are listed as possible symptoms, but none independently proves that the sensor element has failed.

## Establish whether the external circuit is intact

The source names both a fuse or harness problem and a failed G70 sensor. Those are different repair paths. An interrupted supply or damaged wire may require circuit repair even if a replacement airflow sensor can be physically installed without difficulty.

Ross-Tech directs fuse and harness checks to the factory repair manual and also recommends examining measuring values. The wiki does not give a common pinout or supply voltage for every G70 installation. Identify the engine and sensor circuit before applying any electrical specification.

## The TDI note changes the context

For TDI engines, the reference adds an EGR check. That is an application-specific diagnostic branch, not evidence that every petrol engine with P1144 has an EGR fault. It should be evaluated alongside the electrical findings and the relevant airflow values.

Document which circuit, sensor or applicable EGR finding explains the complaint, then reassess the affected values and fault status after repair. Smoke disappearing temporarily is not a substitute for resolving the reported electrical condition.
