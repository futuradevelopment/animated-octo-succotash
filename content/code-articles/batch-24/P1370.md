# P1370: Volkswagen cylinder 6 ignition open circuit and the J17 relay lead

Volkswagen P1370/17778/004976 describes an open circuit associated with cylinder 6 ignition. Ross-Tech specifically names ignition coil N324 in its possible causes and checks. The code does not alone establish an internal coil failure.

## A relay appears in this entry for a reason to investigate

Alongside N324 and its wiring or connectors, the reference lists fuel-pump relay J17. That additional branch is important because an investigation focused only on the coil would omit a documented possibility. However, the wiki does not provide the wiring diagram explaining how J17 relates to every installation.

Do not infer a universal shared feed, relay location or terminal number from the list. Confirm the actual vehicle circuit before deciding whether that relay is relevant and how to evaluate it.

## Distinguish component evidence from circuit evidence

A coil defect, a damaged connection and a relevant relay fault require different repairs. The source recommends checking all three areas but provides no common resistance specification or energized testing procedure.

The useful diagnostic result records what was demonstrated in the N324 branch and, where applicable, the J17 path. Reassess the fault after correction using the vehicle's procedure. An illuminated warning lamp is a possible symptom, while its disappearance after clearing memory does not by itself show that cylinder 6 ignition has been restored.
