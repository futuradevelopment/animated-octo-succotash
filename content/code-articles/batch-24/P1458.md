# P1458: Volkswagen G236 exhaust-temperature sensor short to ground

Volkswagen P1458/17866/005208 concerns exhaust-gas temperature sensor 2, G236, with a short-to-ground circuit condition in Ross-Tech's description. The entry lists a possible engine warning lamp. It does not report a specific excessive exhaust temperature or establish damage to the catalyst.

## Separate the sensing component from its wiring

The documented possible causes are faulty wiring or connectors associated with G236 and a defective sensor. A grounded or damaged external path can therefore need repair even when the sensor appears to be the obvious replacement target.

The source recommends evaluating both the circuit and component. It gives no shared pinout, resistance value or temperature-to-signal specification. Those details must match the actual exhaust-temperature system rather than being inferred from the sensor number.

## Keep the fault direction in the diagnosis

The short-to-ground wording is more specific than a general statement that the temperature is implausible. Preserve that electrical condition when discussing the fault with a technician. It helps distinguish the circuit investigation from unrelated concerns about exhaust heat or mechanical restriction.

After addressing a verified wire, connector or sensor defect, reassess the appropriate temperature information and fault status under the vehicle procedure. Erasing the code does not demonstrate that G236's signal path is restored, and a replacement sensor alone does not explain whether the original short was external.
