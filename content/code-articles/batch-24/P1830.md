# P1830: Volkswagen pressure valve 4 short to supply: N218 and N94 context

P1830/18238/006192 identifies pressure-control valve 4 shorted to positive supply in Ross-Tech's Volkswagen/VAG reference. The valve is named N218, with solenoid valve 7/N94 noted as an alternative designation.

## Identify the fault in the correct branch

Ross-Tech lists wiring shorted to positive supply and a defective valve as possible causes. Its checks cover the external circuit, component and relevant measuring values. Resolve the installed transmission's naming before interpreting a result as evidence about N218/N94.

## Connector condition can change the repair

The reference recommends looking for corrosion or water damage at connections. On an 01V transmission, it highlights the 16-pin connection between valve body and harness.

That inspection lead and electrical method are shared across the pressure-valve family. P1830's distinct scope is valve 4 and the short-to-positive direction. Record whether the supported defect concerns the connector, wiring or valve rather than treating every pressure-control entry as the same internal transmission failure.
