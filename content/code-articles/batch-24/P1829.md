# P1829: Volkswagen pressure valve 4 fault and the DSG mechatronic naming caveat

Volkswagen P1829/18237 concerns pressure-control valve 4 with an open circuit or short to ground. Ross-Tech names N218 and the alternative designation solenoid valve 7/N94. Its checks address valve wiring, the component and relevant measuring values.

## Why a separate valve listing may be absent

For some 02E DSG transmissions, Ross-Tech notes that N218/N94 is part of the mechatronic unit and may not be listed separately in the repair manual. A missing standalone listing therefore does not establish that the scan code is invalid. It also does not by itself prove that the entire mechatronic unit needs replacement.

## Keep the 01V note separate

The source's corrosion or water-damage check highlights a 16-pin valve-body/harness connector on 01V transmissions. That location must not be transferred automatically to an 02E.

The electrical method is shared with related pressure-valve entries. The transmission identity, valve-4 naming and actual open-or-grounded finding determine how it applies to P1829.
