# P1335: Volkswagen torque monitor 2 limit: preserve mixture evidence

P1335/17743 is the Volkswagen engine-torque monitor 2 control-limit fault in Ross-Tech's description. Reduced power and an engine warning lamp are possible symptoms. The name does not establish that a mechanical torque-producing component has failed or provide a measured torque value.

## Look at the inputs behind the complaint

Ross-Tech identifies intake leakage after the airflow sensor, intake-air and coolant-temperature sensing, G70 airflow measurement, and restricted air or fuel filters as possible contributors. These span physical airflow or delivery restrictions and inaccurate control information. They should not be collapsed into an automatic airflow-sensor replacement.

The location of the intake leak matters: air entering after the airflow measurement is specifically identified. That is a more useful investigation target than searching indiscriminately for any hose defect on the car.

## Save adaptation before clearing faults

The source explicitly recommends checking oxygen-control adaptation first. Its fuel-trim guide explains how learned correction can be lost when fault memory is cleared. Preserve the relevant values and their operating context while assessing the intake, sensor and filter findings.

The page does not publish the torque monitor's formula or threshold. A supported repair connects a verified input or physical-system defect to the complaint, then reassesses the related values and power behavior. A code reset alone cannot confirm that the control-limit problem is resolved.
