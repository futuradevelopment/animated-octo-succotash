# P1476: Volkswagen EVAP pump insufficient vacuum: test the supply separately

P1476/17884/005238 is the Volkswagen/VAG leak-detection pump malfunction or insufficient-vacuum entry in Ross-Tech's reference. It can illuminate the engine warning lamp. The code does not establish that the pump itself is defective.

## Three fault paths lead to different repairs

The source lists a leaking or faulty EVAP system, a defective leak-detection pump and insufficient vacuum supply to that pump. This last possibility is especially important: inadequate supply can prevent the pump system from operating as expected even when replacing the pump would not correct the upstream problem.

Ross-Tech recommends checking EVAP sealing, pump condition and vacuum supply at the pump separately. A single visual inspection of the canister does not answer all three questions.

## Keep measurements tied to the actual system

The entry gives no universal vacuum threshold, connection point or leak-test pressure. Use the procedure for the installed system before deciding that the supply is inadequate or the pump has failed. The title alone cannot provide those specifications.

A useful diagnosis states whether the demonstrated problem was a system leak, a vacuum-supply defect or pump failure. Following the appropriate repair, reassess the affected function and code status under the applicable monitoring conditions. Merely hearing the pump or clearing the warning does not establish that sufficient vacuum and proper EVAP sealing have both been restored.
