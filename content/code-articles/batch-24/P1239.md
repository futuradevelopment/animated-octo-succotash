# P1239: Volkswagen cylinder 3 injector N32 open-circuit fault

Volkswagen P1239/17647 identifies an open circuit affecting cylinder 3 injector N32 in Ross-Tech's reference. Poor performance and an engine warning lamp are possible symptoms.

## An open circuit has several possible locations

Ross-Tech lists the injector fuse, harness, injector and engine controller. Its recommended checks specifically cover the fuse, wiring to and from N32, and injector resistance under the repair-manual procedure. The external path and the injector therefore need separate findings.

## Distinguish it from P1215

The P1215 entry concerns the same N32 branch but describes a short to positive supply. Preserve that difference in the scan record; both codes should not become a single assumed injector failure.

The source uses a shared electrical method for related cylinders. For P1239, use the engine-specific N32 wiring and resistance information to establish where the interruption lies. A fuse or harness defect can support a repair without proving that the injector itself is defective.
