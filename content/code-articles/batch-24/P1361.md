# P1361: Volkswagen cylinder 3 ignition open circuit

Volkswagen P1361/17769 identifies cylinder 3 ignition with an open circuit in Ross-Tech's reference. A warning lamp, misfire or absent spark may accompany the fault.

## Distinguish the coil from its electrical support

Ross-Tech lists coils with output stages, faulty fuses, and wiring or connectors between the ignition arrangement and engine controller. These are separate possible failure locations. The circuit checks need to establish whether the problem is external to the coil or within the ignition component.

Use the installed engine's circuit information to attach the findings to cylinder 3. The source applies this shared method to related ignition-circuit codes rather than prescribing a different repair for each cylinder number.

## The bulletin lead has limits

For North American 2.0L Golf/Jetta/Bora 1J/9M applications, the page points to TSB 01-08-27 or 2010575. Confirm the original bulletin and matching vehicle before adopting its repair. The citation alone does not establish a coil failure on every P1361 vehicle.
