# P1355: Volkswagen cylinder 1 ignition open circuit and the 2.0L bulletin lead

Volkswagen P1355/17763 identifies an open circuit in cylinder 1 ignition in Ross-Tech's reference. Possible symptoms include misfire, absent spark and an engine warning lamp.

## The supported ignition checks

Ross-Tech lists coils with output stages, fuses, and wiring or connectors between the ignition arrangement and engine controller. The checks therefore need to distinguish a coil/output-stage defect from an interruption in its external circuit. Use the installed engine's ignition information to identify cylinder 1 and evaluate those paths.

## A scoped 2.0L bulletin lead

For North American Golf/Jetta/Bora 1J/9M vehicles with a 2.0L engine, the source points to TSB 01-08-27 or 2010575. Obtain the original bulletin and confirm the vehicle before applying its repair information.

Ross-Tech uses the same diagnostic framework for related cylinder ignition codes. P1355's distinct scope is cylinder 1 and an open circuit; the bulletin reference does not establish a universal coil replacement for every vehicle carrying this code.
