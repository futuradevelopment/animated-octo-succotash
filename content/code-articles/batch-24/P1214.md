# P1214: Volkswagen cylinder 2 injector N31 short to positive supply

Volkswagen P1214/17622 identifies cylinder 2 injector N31 with a short-to-positive circuit condition. Ross-Tech lists poor performance and an illuminated engine warning as possible symptoms. Neither symptom determines where the circuit fault is located.

## External circuit or injector?

The documented possibilities include an injector fuse, the wiring harness, the injector and the engine controller. The source does not rank those causes. A fault discovered in the N31 harness is therefore a meaningful repair finding, rather than an unrelated observation after an injector code appears.

## Checks that separate the possibilities

Ross-Tech recommends inspection of wiring to and from the injector and a resistance test using the applicable repair-manual procedure. Record the external-circuit finding alongside the component measurement and its correct engine-specific limit.

The same checks appear in adjacent injector entries. For this page they apply to cylinder 2/N31; findings from a different cylinder cannot establish the condition of this branch.
