# P1163: Volkswagen fuel-temperature sensor G81 open or short to supply

P1163/17571/004451 identifies an open or short-to-positive condition in the G81 fuel-temperature sensor circuit. This Volkswagen/VAG interpretation concerns temperature feedback, not a direct low-fuel-pressure measurement. Ross-Tech lists difficult starting, increased fuel consumption and excessive emissions as possible accompanying complaints.

## Resolve the electrical branch before chasing the symptom

The diagnostic entry names faulty G81 wiring or connectors and a defective sensor as separate possibilities. A starting complaint therefore does not justify immediately replacing a pump or injector. The recorded fault first needs an explanation in the temperature-sensing circuit.

An open circuit and a short to supply are alternative electrical conditions grouped under this code. The label does not tell the technician which one is present. Vehicle-specific circuit tests are needed to distinguish them; the page supplies neither connector cavities nor a universal temperature-versus-resistance specification.

## What a useful verification includes

Identify the actual G81 circuit from the engine documentation and retain the fault details before repair. The result should state whether the defect was found in a connection, wiring or the sensing component, rather than recording only that a new sensor was fitted.

After correction, confirm that the circuit fault does not recur and reassess the original starting or consumption complaint. Improvement in one symptom does not independently establish that the temperature feedback is reliable.
