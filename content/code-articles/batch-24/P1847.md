# P1847: Volkswagen ABS fault-memory referral and cross-module effects

P1847/18255/006215 refers the investigation to ABS-controller fault memory in Ross-Tech's Volkswagen/VAG reference. The listed cause is a stored fault in brake-electronics module J104; an engine warning lamp may accompany the referral.

## Use the more specific ABS evidence

Ross-Tech directs reading faults at address 03, Brake Electronics. Retain that record and the controller identity associated with P1847. The referring entry does not establish a wheel-speed sensor fault, a hydraulic defect or a particular network problem.

## A shared referral, not another failed component

The source explains that a module needing information from a faulty sender can record a related entry of its own. This can connect two fault records without proving that both controllers have failed.

P1653 is given the same referral meaning. Follow the actual J104 fault and vehicle symptoms to the applicable brake-system procedure. The useful outcome connects the originating evidence with the dependent module's complaint, rather than treating P1847 as an additional part to replace.
