# P1814: Volkswagen pressure valve 1 open or grounded: N215 and N91 naming

Volkswagen P1814/18222 describes an open circuit or short to ground at pressure-control valve 1 in Ross-Tech's reference. It names N215 and notes the alternative designation solenoid valve 4/N91.

## Resolve the valve identity and circuit condition

Confirm the installed transmission's naming before locating the component. Ross-Tech lists open or grounded valve wiring and a defective valve as possible causes. It recommends checking the wiring, valve and relevant measuring blocks; the grouped code does not establish which electrical condition is present.

## The 01V connector lead

The source advises checking connectors for corrosion or water damage. For an 01V transmission, it specifically highlights the 16-pin connection between the valve body and harness.

This inspection point and electrical method are shared across the pressure-valve family. P1814 applies them to valve 1/N215 or N91, with an open-or-grounded condition. A demonstrated connector defect and a verified internal valve problem support different repairs.
