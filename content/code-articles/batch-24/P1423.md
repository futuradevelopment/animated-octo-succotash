# P1423: Volkswagen secondary-air bank 1 low flow: distinguish air and vacuum paths

Ross-Tech identifies Volkswagen P1423/17831/005155 as insufficient secondary-air injection flow on bank 1. The engine warning lamp may illuminate. Low delivered flow is the reported outcome; it is not proof that the pump motor has failed.

## There are two different hose functions to assess

The reference lists leaking or collapsed vacuum hoses separately from leaks in the air piping between the pump and combination valve. These are different parts of the system. A sound large air hose does not establish that the control-vacuum path is intact, and the reverse is also true.

Other named possibilities include the pump fuse, secondary-air solenoids and combination valves. A useful diagnosis identifies which supply, control or air-delivery branch explains the low-flow condition.

## Check the housing detail only where it applies

Ross-Tech notes that some pumps have riveted plastic housing halves and that loose or missing rivets deserve inspection. This is a design-specific physical clue, not evidence that every pump should be repaired with improvised fasteners or that its motor is sound.

Use the appropriate vehicle procedure to establish the defect and acceptable repair. Afterward, assess secondary-air operation and the fault result under the controller's applicable conditions. Hearing a pump run alone does not establish that air reaches the intended path in sufficient quantity.
