# P1142: Volkswagen lower load-cross-check limit and the two throttle signal pairs

P1142/17550/004418 is the Volkswagen load-calculation cross-check lower-limit fault described by Ross-Tech. The word “lower” identifies the direction of the failed cross-check; the entry does not publish a numeric threshold or prove that the airflow sensor is the failed component.

## Pedal feedback and throttle feedback are different checks

The diagnostic note separately identifies pedal sensors G79 and G185, and throttle-actuator angle sensors G187 and G188. That distinction matters when interpreting a scan: a commanded pedal position and a reported throttle angle are not interchangeable measurements from the same component.

Ross-Tech also includes the G70 airflow sensor, intake-air faults or leakage, a dirty or defective throttle body, and the engine controller in its investigation. These alternatives explain why a throttle-related complaint needs more than a single sensor replacement guess.

## Preserve a coherent set of observations

Identify the engine and controller, then capture the relevant airflow, pedal and throttle values under the condition associated with the fault. A disagreement within one signal pair directs attention differently from an intact pair accompanied by an intake defect. This is diagnostic interpretation, not a prescribed Volkswagen test sequence.

The repair should address the verified defect and be followed by a fresh assessment of the cross-check and affected signals. The source does not support universal voltage limits, a particular replacement throttle body, or a guaranteed cure from cleaning alone.
