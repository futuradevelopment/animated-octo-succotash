# P1562: Volkswagen N146 quantity adjuster upper limit and the ALH-specific checks

Volkswagen P1562/17970/005474 identifies the N146 quantity adjuster reaching its upper limit in Ross-Tech's reference. Jerking, reduced power and an engine warning lamp are possible symptoms. The code does not by itself establish that the entire injection pump has failed.

## Separate electrical support from adjustment and movement

The documented possibilities include fuses, N146 wiring or connectors, incorrect adjustment and a blocked or faulty adjuster. Those categories require different evidence. A failed supply path is not corrected by changing a mechanical setting, and an adjustment complaint does not prove a wiring fault.

Ross-Tech recommends checking those paths before its pump-replacement option is considered. Pump adjustment or repair must follow the appropriate specialist procedure rather than an improvised response to the code.

## The numerical example is specifically for the 1.9L ALH

For that engine, the note gives N146 resistance of 0.5–2.5 ohms. It also specifies measuring block 001, field 3, at 1.45–2.0 volts with coolant above 85°C. The resistance check and warm-running measured value are different observations and must not be treated as interchangeable.

These values are not universal Volkswagen pump specifications. Confirm the engine and test conditions, document the actual fault found, and reassess the relevant control behavior after repair. A cleared upper-limit code alone does not establish correct adjuster operation.
