# P1527: Volkswagen N205 short-to-ground fault and low-speed torque

Volkswagen P1527/17935/005415 describes a short-to-ground condition in the bank 1 N205 camshaft-timing adjustment circuit. Ross-Tech lists reduced torque at lower engine speeds and an engine warning lamp as possible symptoms. A torque complaint does not independently prove that the adjuster is mechanically worn.

## Power supply is part of this code's checklist

The source identifies fuses, N205 wiring or connectors and the valve itself as possible fault areas. Its suggested work explicitly includes checking power supply to the valve. That matters because an external circuit problem can remain even if the actuator is replaced.

The entry does not publish a universal supply specification, pinout or resistance threshold. The actual engine's wiring and diagnostic instructions must determine how those checks are performed and assessed.

## Keep electrical and mechanical conclusions separate

P1527 reports a circuit condition. Evidence of a short, damaged connector or supply-path problem should be recorded distinctly from any mechanical finding obtained during a broader cam-adjustment investigation. The code alone does not establish that the entire timing assembly requires replacement.

Once the confirmed electrical defect is corrected, assess whether the code returns and whether the original low-speed response has improved. A temporary improvement in torque or an erased warning lamp is not a substitute for verifying the affected N205 circuit.
