# P1218: Volkswagen cylinder 6 injector N84 short to supply

Volkswagen P1218/17626 targets cylinder 6 injector N84 with a short-to-positive electrical condition. Ross-Tech lists an engine warning and poor performance as possible symptoms; the fault text itself concerns the circuit.

## Which part of the path is faulty?

The source identifies an injector fuse, the harness, the injector and the engine controller as possible contributors. A diagnostic record should retain which possibility was supported, particularly when several cylinder entries appear in the same scan.

## The source's electrical method

Ross-Tech recommends inspection of wiring to and from N84 and an injector resistance check against the applicable repair-manual specification. These checks distinguish evidence about the external circuit from evidence about the injector component.

The reference uses this method for the related injector-code family. P1218's identity remains cylinder 6/N84, short to positive supply; it should not be combined with an open-circuit entry under a generic injector-failure diagnosis.
